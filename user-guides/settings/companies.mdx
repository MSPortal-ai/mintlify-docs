Companies Settings is where MSP staff create client companies, edit their details, import them from your PSA and change many companies at once. Client users and roles live in **Settings > Users & Roles**.

## Before you start

- You need Companies settings access. Read access lets you view companies. Write access lets you add, edit and run bulk actions. Manage access is also needed to delete companies, turn **AI Home** on or off, and open **Blocked Companies** and **Hardware defaults**.
- Premium Training actions need the Training manage permission.
- To import companies, connect a supported PSA first. See [Import Companies](/user-guides/integrations/import-companies).
- Company users cannot use this page.

## Find a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Companies">
    Go to **Settings > Organization > Companies**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Use the column filters. **Company** searches by name. **Status**, **Training** (**Premium** or **Standard**) and **M365** (**Connected** or **Not Set**) are lists. **Integrations** and **Types** let you pick several values. The **Industry** column is hidden until you turn it on from the column menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check badges before editing">
    A **Parent** or **Child** badge shows the company hierarchy. **Imported from** shows the PSA that created the company. Similar names can be different companies, so check the badges first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each row has a **...** menu. With write access it shows **Edit Company**. With read access only, it shows **View Details & Users**.

## Create a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Company">
    Click **Add Company** in the page header. The **Add New Company** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter the required **Name**. Add **Contact Name**, **Contact Email**, **Contact Phone**, **Website**, **Industry**, **Company Size** and **Primary Domain** as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to the rest of the form">
    Below the contact fields, set the **Address**, **Active**, **Compliance Schedule**, **Business Review Schedule** and **Year-end Date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Company**. A message confirms the company was created. **Cancel** closes the form without saving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The top of the Add New Company form. The address, active state and schedule fields continue below, and Save Company is in the dialog footer.">
  <img src="/images/product/company-create-contact-fields.png" alt="Blank Add New Company form showing the required name and optional contact and website fields." width="1200" height="720" />
</Frame>

A contact email on a company does not create a portal user or send an invitation. The **Compliance Schedule** and **Business Review Schedule** fields set meeting frequency. They are separate from [automated compliance run schedules](/user-guides/settings/compliance-schedules).

## Edit a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company">
    In the list, open the row's **...** menu and click **Edit Company**. The company page opens on the **Overview** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a field">
    Point at a field and click the pencil icon. Change the value, then click the check mark to save, or the **X** to cancel. Each field saves on its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open another tab">
    Use the tabs at the top to work on the rest of the company. Each tab saves separately.

    | Tab | What you do there |
    |-----|-------------------|
    | **Overview** | Edit contact, address, industry, language, currency, time zone, size, parent company, types, schedules, year-end date, **Compliance Required** and custom fields. See the PSA, Microsoft 365 and device integration cards |
    | **Users** | View and manage this company's users. See [Change a Company user's permissions](/user-guides/settings/change-company-user-permissions) |
    | **Locations** | Add, edit, duplicate or delete physical locations, or import sites from your PSA |
    | **Defaults** | Set company defaults on the **Communication**, **Hardware**, **Software**, **Location**, **Import**, **Security** and **Tickets** sub-tabs. Click **Save Defaults** to save |
    | **Ticketing** | Set the default PSA routing for this company's tickets and choose which ticket templates are available to it |
    | **Team** | Assign MSP staff to this company's team roles |
    | **Notes** | Keep account notes and the QBR log |
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a value changes back after a sync, your PSA owns that field. Change it in the PSA.

### Add a location

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company menu">
    In the company list, open the row's **...** menu and click **Add Location**. You can also use **Add** on the **Locations** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the dialog">
    In **Create New Location**, enter the **Name** and the address, phone, email and notes you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create Location**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A parent company and a location are different things. A location is a physical site of one company.

## Import companies

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Import menu">
    On **Settings > Organization > Companies**, click **Import** in the page header. It shows only when a supported PSA is connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the provider">
    Click **Import from** and your provider's name. The import dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select and import">
    Pick the companies to import. You can link each one to an existing company or create a new one. Follow [Import Companies](/user-guides/integrations/import-companies).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Check [Integration Company Mapping](/user-guides/settings/integration-company-mapping) before you create a company that may already exist. Some selections create the company before the link is applied, and **Cancel** does not undo them.

## Run a bulk action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select companies">
    Tick the checkbox on each company. To include companies on other pages, choose all matching companies across pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Actions menu">
    Click **Actions (count)** in the page header. The count shows how many companies are selected, or **All N** for a cross-page selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the action and confirm">
    Pick an action from the table below. Check the dialog's company list before you click its apply button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Assign Teams to Selected** | Assigns or unassigns team roles for users across the selected companies. Changes apply immediately |
| **Set Parent Company** | Makes the selected companies children of another company. A company cannot be its own parent or the parent of its own ancestor. Choose **No parent company** to remove the relationship. Click **Update Parent** |
| **Manage Types** | Adds, removes or replaces company types. See [Manage company types](#manage-company-types) |
| **Set Language** | Sets a default language for the selected companies. **Clear Override** returns them to the tenant default. Click **Apply** |
| **Set Currency** | Sets a currency for the selected companies. **Clear Override** returns them to the tenant default. Click **Apply** |
| **Edit Custom Field** | Sets one custom field to the same value on every selected company. See [Set a custom field on many companies](#set-a-custom-field-on-many-companies) |
| **Set Meeting Schedules** | Sets **Business reviews** and **Compliance reviews** frequency. **Keep existing** leaves a schedule alone. **No schedule** removes it. Click **Apply schedules** |
| **Enable Premium Training** / **Disable Premium Training** | Turns Premium Training on or off at once. Needs the Training manage permission |
| **Enable AI Home** / **Disable AI Home** | Turns AI Home on or off at once. Needs manage access |
| **Delete Selected** | Permanently deletes the selected companies. Needs manage access |

**Enable and Disable Premium Training** and **Enable and Disable AI Home** apply as soon as you click them, with no confirmation dialog. Training availability, user overrides and enrollment are separate settings. See [Enable Training](/user-guides/training/enabling-training).

## Manage company types

Types group companies for filters, reports and ticket-template availability. A company can have several types. **Manual** types are created in MSPortal. **PSA-synced** types come from your PSA and show a tooltip, **Synced from** and the provider name.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the companies">
    Tick the companies. Use the column filters first to narrow the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Manage Types">
    Click **Actions (count)**, then **Manage Types**. For one company, use **Manage Types** in its row menu. The **Manage Company Types** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Under **Action**, pick one option.

    | Option | Result |
    |--------|--------|
    | **Add types (keep existing)** | Adds the types you pick to each company |
    | **Replace all types** | Replaces each company's manual types with the ones you pick. PSA-synced types stay |
    | **Remove types** | Removes the types you pick. The list shows only types on the selected companies |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick or create types">
    Pick existing types, or type a new name and click **Create type**. A new type is saved right away. Cancelling the dialog does not delete it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click **Apply**. A message says how many companies changed. Check the **Types** column.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Removed types stay removed. Your PSA sync does not add them back unless you add the type again.

## Set a custom field on many companies

Use **Edit Custom Field** to give one company custom field the same value on many companies. For example, set the same contract renewal date for a group of clients.

### Before you start

- You need write access to Companies settings, and your MSPortal billing must be active.
- Create the field first. Open **Settings > Customization > Fields**, choose **Companies**, and save the field. See [Custom Fields](/user-guides/settings/custom-fields).
- A **Dropdown**, **Multi-Select** or **Radio Buttons** field appears only after it has at least one saved option.

### Set the value

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the companies">
    In **Settings > Organization > Companies**, tick the checkbox beside each company. To include companies on other pages, select all matching companies across pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Edit Custom Field">
    Select **Actions (count)**, then **Edit Custom Field**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the selected companies">
    The **Selected Companies** list shows the first 30 names, then a **+N more** badge. Close the dialog and adjust your selection if a company is wrong.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the field and value">
    Choose a field under **Custom field**. Then enter the **Value**. The input matches the field type, such as a date picker for a date field or a list for a dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the value">
    Select **Apply**. A message confirms how many companies were updated, and the selection clears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Replace, clear and required fields

- **Apply replaces the existing value** on every selected company. It does not add to it. For a **Multi-Select** field, the companies end up with exactly the options you chose.
- **Clear Value removes the field's value** from every selected company. You do not need to enter a value first.
- **Required fields cannot be cleared.** The dialog disables **Clear Value** and says the field cannot be cleared. Apply still works.
- Apply updates all selected companies together. If it fails, no company changes.
- Clear Value works through the companies in batches. If it stops partway, the message says how many companies were cleared. Run Clear Value again to clear the rest.

To check the result, open the column menu and turn on the field's column. Custom field columns are hidden by default.

## Company users, access roles and team roles

| You want to | Open |
|-------------|------|
| Manage client users | **Settings > Users & Roles > Company Users**, or a company's **Users** tab |
| Define client access | **Settings > Users & Roles > Roles > Company Roles** |
| Define internal staff access | **Settings > Users & Roles > Roles > Tenant Roles** |
| Define account-team responsibilities | **Settings > Users & Roles > Team Roles** |

**Company Roles** control client access. **Team Roles** describe MSP responsibilities, such as account manager. Assigning a staff member to a team role can limit their company selector to the companies they are assigned. A team role does not give a client user any permission.

Every parent company has a **Parent Admin** team role. On the parent company's **Team** tab, assign a client user to it to give them every child company and a company selector. See [Give a parent-company user access to child companies](/user-guides/settings/parent-company-access). On a parent company's row menu, **Sync Roles to Children** copies the roles of the parent company's admin users to its child companies.

For permission changes, see [Change a Company user's permissions](/user-guides/settings/change-company-user-permissions).

### Auto-Mapping PSA Contact Types to Roles

If you use ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo, you can map PSA roles and contact types to your team roles. MSPortal then assigns people to a company's roles from your PSA data on each sync.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Roles">
    Go to **Settings > Users & Roles > Team Roles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a role">
    Click **Add Role** for a new role, or the edit icon on an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the PSA roles">
    In the PSA column (**CW Team Roles**, **Autotask Roles** or **Halo Roles**), pick the PSA roles that should map to this team role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the role">
    Click the **Save role** icon. The next sync applies the mapping.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Roles you do not map stay manually managed. A manual change to a mapped assignment can be overwritten by the next sync. See [Account teams and Team Roles](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/company-roles).

## Delete a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    In the company list, open the row's **...** menu and click **Delete Company**. This needs manage access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the warning">
    The dialog lists what is permanently deleted: company data and settings, compliance history, user associations, training records, tickets and locations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete Company**. Deleting cannot be undone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Do not delete a company to hide it or to fix an import. Change its status to inactive instead. A deleted company is blocked from being imported again. To allow it back, click **Blocked Companies** in the page header, select it and click **Unblock**. Unblocking lets it be imported again. It does not restore deleted data.

**Hardware defaults** in the page header sets the global hardware standards that every company inherits. It applies to the whole tenant, not to one company.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| A company is missing | Clear the list filters. Check **Status**, your access and your import mapping before you add a duplicate |
| The **Actions** menu is missing | Select at least one company. You need write access |
| **Delete Selected**, **Blocked Companies** or **AI Home** is missing | These need manage access |
| **Enable Premium Training** is missing | You need the Training manage permission |
| A client cannot sign in | Check the person's Company user record and Company role. A company contact email does not create a login |
| A parent-company user sees only their own company | Assign them the Parent Admin team role. See [Give a parent-company user access to child companies](/user-guides/settings/parent-company-access) |
| A bulk action hit more companies than expected | Check whether the selection covered one page or all matching companies. The **Actions** count shows which |
| **Edit Custom Field** says no company custom fields are defined | Create a field on the **Companies** tab in **Settings > Customization > Fields** |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where are Company Users now?">
    Open **Settings > Users & Roles > Company Users**, or the **Users** tab inside a company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Team Roles job labels for client users?">
    No. They define MSP account-team responsibilities. Assignments can change a staff member's company scope and the client team menu.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a company contact email create a portal login?">
    No. Add the person as a Company user and assign a Company role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the Compliance Schedule field create automated compliance runs?">
    No. Automated runs use **Settings > Compliance** with a run template and explicit targets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a bulk selection include companies that are off screen?">
    Yes. Choosing all matching companies includes every company that fits your filters, not only the visible page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does cancelling Manage Types undo a type I just created?">
    No. A new type is saved when you create it. **Apply** separately saves which companies have it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Replace remove PSA-synced types?">
    No. **Replace all types** changes manual types only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does saving an Overview field save the other tabs?">
    No. Each field and each tab saves on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will unblocking a company restore its deleted history?">
    No. It only lets the company be imported again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
