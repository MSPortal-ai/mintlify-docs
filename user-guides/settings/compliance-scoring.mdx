Compliance Scoring controls how much each check counts toward a company's compliance score and what percentage earns each letter grade. This page is for MSP staff. The settings apply to the whole tenant, not only the company selected in the top bar. This is separate from [Client Health scoring](/user-guides/settings/scoring) and from the fixed likelihood and impact matrix in the [risk register](/user-guides/compliance/risk-management).

## Before you start

- You need Compliance settings access to open the page. Saving and resetting need manage access.
- Write down the current values before you change them. Reset restores defaults and cannot be undone.

## Change the weights and grades

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Scoring">
    Go to **Settings > Operations > Compliance** and click **Scoring**. The page is titled **Compliance Scoring**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the scope weights">
    Under **Scope Weights**, set **Company Scope Weight**, **Location Scope Weight** and **Device Scope Weight**. Each accepts 0 to 10 in steps of 0.1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the priority weights">
    Under **Priority Weights**, set **High Priority Weight**, **Medium Priority Weight** and **Low Priority Weight**. Each accepts 0 to 5 in steps of 0.1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the criticality multiplier">
    Under **Criticality Multiplier**, enter a value from 0 to 5. The page shows the formula, `check_weight = scope_weight x (1 + criticality x multiplier) x priority_weight`. A value of 0 ignores criticality.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the grade thresholds">
    Under **Grade Thresholds**, set **Grade A Threshold (%)**, **Grade B Threshold (%)**, **Grade C Threshold (%)** and **Grade D Threshold (%)**. Each is a whole number from 1 to 100. Keep A above B, B above C and C above D. A score below the D threshold gets an F.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Grade Preview">
    **Grade Preview** shows sample percentages graded with your draft thresholds. It is not a live company score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes** in the page header. A toast says **Scoring configuration saved successfully**. The button stays disabled until you change something.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A zero criticality multiplier removes criticality from the weight. It does not mark a check as passed. Scoring changes do not edit check answers, evidence, integrations or risk likelihood and impact.

## Reset to defaults

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Reset to Defaults">
    Click **Reset to Defaults** in the page header. The **Reset Scoring Configuration?** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Reset**. The defaults are saved at once. A toast says **Scoring configuration reset to defaults**. You do not need to click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Setting | Default |
|---------|---------|
| Company, Location, Device scope weight | 3, 2, 1 |
| High, Medium, Low priority weight | 1.2, 1, 0.8 |
| Criticality multiplier | 1 |
| A, B, C, D thresholds | 90, 80, 70, 60 |

Reset does not restore your last custom settings. To go back to a custom model, enter the values you wrote down and save.

To throw away an unsaved edit, change the fields back or leave the page and open it again. Do not use **Reset to Defaults** for that.

## Check the result

Open Scoring again after a save or reset and confirm the values. Then look at the compliance scores that matter to you. Saving does not certify compliance or fix failed checks.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| **Save Changes** is disabled | There are no unsaved changes, or a save or reset is running |
| **Failed to save scoring configuration** | Check that each number is inside its range, then try again. Reopen the page to see the stored values |
| **Save Changes** or **Reset** does not work | You need manage access to Compliance settings |
| A number will not accept my value | Scope weights go to 10. Priority weights and the multiplier go to 5. Thresholds are 1 to 100 |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Compliance Scoring the Client Health model?">
    No. Client Health has its own settings page and model.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does editing a number save it?">
    No. Click **Save Changes**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Reset to Defaults to cancel an edit?">
    No. Reset saves the defaults at once. To discard an edit, leave the page and open it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I reset scoring for one company?">
    No. Scoring is tenant-wide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Grade Preview show?">
    How your draft thresholds grade fixed sample percentages. It does not show live scores.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change risk severity bands here?">
    No. Risk scoring uses a separate fixed matrix. See [Configure risk tracking and categories](/user-guides/settings/compliance-risk).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
