Settings is where MSP staff configure MSPortal. This page shows where each setting lives and links to its guide. Company users have their own account controls and cannot open MSP settings.

## Open Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Settings">
    Click **Settings** at the bottom of the main sidebar. The page opens on **User Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an area">
    Use the settings menu on the left. Related pages are grouped under **Organization**, **Operations**, **Customization**, **MSP Tools**, **Platform** and **Administration**. Click a group to open it, then click the page you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for a missing page">
    If a page you expect is missing, your role may not have access to it, or its feature may be off for your account. Ask an administrator to check your role's permissions. Primary admins can open every page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Know when a change saves

Settings pages do not all save the same way. Check each guide, and use this as a rule of thumb.

| Kind of control | How it saves |
| --- | --- |
| Forms with a header button, such as **Save Settings** or **Save Changes** | Nothing is stored until you click the button |
| Editors in a dialog, such as note templates | Click the dialog's **Save** or **Create** button |
| Switches and menu actions, such as turning an automation rule on or activating a receiving address | Save at once |
| Bulk actions on selected rows | Apply when you confirm the dialog. Some apply as soon as you click them |
| Imports and provider connections | Run when you start them and report a result |

Before a bulk action, check the selected rows and the dialog's list of what will change. A table filter changes what you see. It does not always limit a tenant-wide action.

## Check the result

Reopen the setting and confirm the saved value. Then test the feature as the role and company that matter. For example, saving an email template does not prove an email was delivered. If an action only partly worked, look at the current values before you repeat it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A settings page is missing | Ask an administrator to check your role's permission and the feature for your account |
| A save failed | Note the error message, reopen the page to see what was saved, and repeat only the part that did not save |
| A user sees unexpected content | Check their company scope, their role and the content's own visibility setting together |
| A bulk action changed more than expected | Check whether the selection covered one page or all matching rows |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do all settings save automatically?">
    No. Some need a Save button, some save as you change them, and imports and connections run when you start them. Follow the specific guide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a table filter limit every bulk action?">
    No. Check the dialog's scope and the selected rows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can another admin see more settings than I can?">
    The menu follows each role's permissions and your account's features. Compare the roles.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a saved setting prove the client workflow works?">
    No. Test the workflow as the role and company you care about.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
