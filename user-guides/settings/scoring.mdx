**Audience:** MSP staff. Company users cannot configure Client Health. These settings apply to the MSP's tenant; selecting one company does not make the configuration company-specific.

Open **Settings > MSP Tools > Scoring**. The page title is **Client Health**, and metrics live on its **Scoring** tab. The **Traffic Light** tab configures [Traffic Light Review](/user-guides/settings/traffic-light-settings). Your role needs Client Health settings read or manage access to open it, and **manage** access to save. Access to the Client Health dashboard is separate. Ask your MSP administrator to adjust permissions when needed.

This page includes or excludes metrics. It does not let you edit the model's base dimension weights, risk bands, evidence thresholds or minimum number of scored dimensions.

## Change which metrics count

1. Review the eight dimension cards and the enabled count. There are **25 metrics** in the current catalog.
2. Switch individual metrics on or off. These changes are an unsaved preview until you select **Save Changes** in the page header.
3. Review the percentage beside each enabled metric. It is the metric's share **within that dimension**, not its percentage of the overall health score. Remaining shares update when you exclude a metric.
4. Review each dimension's enabled count. A dimension switch is on whenever at least one of its metrics is enabled. Switching it off excludes all metrics in that card; switching it on enables all of them. It does not restore a previous partial selection.
5. Select **Save Changes** once and wait for the result. The button is disabled when there are no unsaved changes or while saving.
6. Review the resulting Client Health scorecard after the background refresh finishes. A saved configuration or a Refreshing message is not proof that recalculation has completed.

To discard an unsaved preview, return the switches to their saved positions, or leave and reopen the page. There is no Reset or Cancel button on this configuration page. Record the original choices before making extensive changes; do not rely on a navigation warning. To restore all metrics, enable each dimension and save deliberately.

## Understand shares and evidence

The displayed metric percentages are rounded to whole numbers and total 100% within an enabled dimension. They describe the selected configuration, while the published company score also depends on applicable, observed evidence and its coverage.

Excluding a metric does not turn its value into zero. The enabled metrics share the dimension's weight proportionally. If every metric in a dimension is excluded, that dimension does not contribute to the overall score. Available observations may still appear in company details for context, with excluded dimensions identified; missing observations are not guaranteed to appear.

Coverage is recalculated against enabled metrics. It can **increase or decrease** depending on whether the excluded metric had observed evidence. Excluding a dimension can also reduce the number of scored dimensions. The default model still requires at least **three scored dimensions** and **40% overall evidence coverage** for a numeric health score.

Enabling a metric does not create missing source data, connect an integration or guarantee a numeric score. Review data availability before excluding relevant metrics solely to remove N/A results.

## Saving and score publication

Save Changes stores the tenant configuration first, then attempts to start a Client Health refresh. If saving succeeds but the refresh cannot start, the saved choices remain. Reopen Scoring to confirm them, then use [Refresh Client Health scores](/user-guides/msp-tools/refresh-client-health) or allow the scheduled refresh to run.

The settings page does not track the background job through completion. The previously published scorecard remains visible until a complete replacement is published. Check a relevant company's **Last computed** date, dimension weights and evidence after the refresh. A failure to refresh does not mean the configuration save was rolled back.

Saving affects future scorecards; it does not rewrite previous publications. This page does not change source data, manual risk flags, company permissions or provider configuration. There is no per-company scoring override here.

If saving fails, reopen the page and inspect the stored choices before retrying. View access alone is insufficient to save, even if a switch can be changed in the current browser session.

## FAQs

  <Accordion title="Where do I configure Client Health metrics?">
    Open Settings > MSP Tools > Scoring. The page is titled Health Score Configuration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Company users change health scoring?">
    No. This is an MSP settings page. Ask the MSP to review scoring or access questions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the Company selector limit a scoring change to one company?">
    No. The configuration applies to the MSP tenant and affects its next published scorecards.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What permission lets me save scoring settings?">
    Client Health settings manage permission. Read access lets you open the configuration but does not authorize saving.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many metrics can I configure?">
    The current catalog has 25 metrics across eight dimensions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does switching a metric immediately save it?">
    No. It changes an unsaved preview. Select Save Changes to persist the configuration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Save Changes disabled?">
    There are no changes compared with the saved selection, or a save is in progress.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does a dimension switch do?">
    Off excludes every metric in that dimension; on enables every metric. It does not restore a prior partial selection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I discard unsaved scoring changes?">
    Return switches to their saved positions, or leave and reopen the page. There is no Reset or Cancel control on this page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I set my own risk bands or base dimension weights?">
    No. This page changes metric inclusion; it does not edit model risk bands, base dimension weights or minimum evidence requirements.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are the percentages beside metrics shares of the overall score?">
    No. They are rounded shares within that dimension and redistribute among enabled metrics.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does excluding a metric always improve evidence coverage?">
    No. Coverage is recalculated using enabled metrics and can rise or fall depending on which observed evidence remains.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can disabling dimensions create more Limited data results?">
    The default model still needs three scored dimensions and 40% overall coverage. Excluding dimensions can prevent a company from meeting that minimum.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will enabling a metric fill in missing data?">
    No. It still needs applicable source evidence; enabling it does not connect or sync providers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Save Changes mean the scorecard is already recalculated?">
    No. Saving persists the configuration and attempts to start a refresh. Wait for the replacement scorecard and check Last computed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The settings saved but the refresh failed to start. Were my settings lost?">
    Not necessarily. Saving and refresh submission are separate. Reopen Scoring to confirm the stored choices, then retry Refresh Scores from Client Health.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will changing scoring rewrite old results?">
    No. Saved settings affect future scorecards; previous publications remain unchanged.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does excluding a dimension delete its observed data or manual risk flags?">
    No. Exclusion changes scoring participation. Available observations may remain visible for context, and manual risk flags are separate.
  </Accordion>
