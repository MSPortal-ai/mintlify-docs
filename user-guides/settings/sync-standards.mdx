**Audience:** MSP staff managing Standards settings. This operation can affect all active companies in the MSP.

Open **Settings > MSP Tools > Standards** after [preparing the catalog](/user-guides/settings/standards). Review names, descriptions, prices and category Planner types before distributing them.

## Start a sync

1. Finish inline edits and wait for them to save. Resolve any save errors first.
2. Select **Sync All Companies**.
3. Review the number of catalog items and categories. The operation covers every active company; the top company selector is not a one-company rollout control for this action.
4. Under **Schedule new planner items**, choose **On a specific date** and pick a date, or choose **Send to Unscheduled**.
5. Decide whether to turn on **Hide from company users** for newly created Planner items.
6. Select the dialog's **Sync All Companies** to submit, or **Cancel** before submission to abandon these choices.
7. Wait for background processing, then review the matrix and linked Planner items. A started message confirms submission, not completion of every record.

The confirm button is disabled while **On a specific date** has no chosen date. A chosen date becomes both the start and due date for newly created items. Unscheduled leaves both dates empty. These dates are planning choices, not appointments or booked engineer time.

## New records and existing work

| Record | What the sync does |
| --- | --- |
| Missing company/standard tracking pair | Creates a Not Started tracking record for an active company. |
| Tracking record without a linked Planner item | Creates linked work, including for optional standards and categories without a Planner type. |
| Existing company deployment status | Keeps the recorded Standards status. |
| Existing linked Planner title, description and type | Refreshes these from the catalog, or from the standard's [Planner template](/user-guides/settings/standards#apply-a-planner-template), when they differ. |
| Existing linked Planner estimate | Refreshes from **Default Price** when the item has no cost rows. When it has cost rows, their total is the estimate. |
| Open item without the standard's current Planner template | Applies the template: business value, hours, probability, notes, cost rows and compliance links. Completed, Cancelled and archived items are skipped. |
| Existing linked Planner status | Reconciles it with Standards, while preserving Planner-only On Hold and Pending Approval states. |
| Existing Planner dates, Company visibility and public/private notes | The new-item schedule and visibility choices do not replace these existing values. Applying a Planner template writes its notes and can make an item internal. |

<Warning>
The dialog's statement that existing company statuses are preserved does not mean all Planner fields are untouched. A sync can replace a company-specific Planner title, description, estimate or type with the shared catalog values. When a standard has a Planner template, a sync can also replace the notes, business value, hours and probability on its open items.
</Warning>

A new Planner item's default estimate is not a purchase or approved quote. **Hide from company users** makes newly created work internal; leaving it off does not grant a Company user access to unrelated Planner work. Their permissions and company scope still apply.

## Verify the result

Open **MSP Tools > Standards**, check relevant company rows, then open their linked work. You can also use **Planner > List** and the **Standards** Source filter. Confirm names, statuses, dates, estimates and Company visibility before relying on the rollout.

If submission or processing fails, inspect what already exists before retrying: background work can make partial progress. Do not delete tracking or Planner records merely to force a retry. Report the workflow failure to the MSP administrator with the affected company and standard.

For Company-facing interpretation, share [Understand Standards items in Planner](/user-guides/planner/understand-standards-items).

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does saving a new standard distribute it automatically?">
    No. Catalog saving is separate from Sync All Companies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I sync only the company selected at the top?">
    Sync All Companies covers every active company in the MSP. The top company selector does not restrict this action to one company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the sync confirmation disabled?">
    On a specific date requires a chosen date. Pick one or select Send to Unscheduled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What dates do new Planner items receive?">
    The selected date becomes both start and due date, or Send to Unscheduled leaves both empty.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the schedule reschedule existing work?">
    No. These schedule choices apply to newly created Planner items.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Hide from company users hide existing work?">
    No. This sync option applies to newly created Planner items; review existing items separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can optional or untyped standards create work?">
    Yes. Sync can create tracking and linked Planner work for optional standards and categories without a Planner type.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does sync reset existing deployment statuses?">
    Existing company Standards statuses are preserved. Linked Planner statuses can be reconciled from them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which existing Planner fields can change?">
    The title, description, estimate, type and mapped status can refresh from Standards. When the standard has a Planner template, open items also get the template's fields. On Hold and Pending Approval are preserved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does sync replace Company notes?">
    Not on its own. Notes change only when a Planner template is applied to an open item, because the template's notes are written to it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a started message mean all companies finished?">
    No. It confirms submission to a background task. Review the matrix and linked work afterward.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I find the generated items?">
    Open Planner > List and choose Standards in the Source filter, or open linked work from the Standards matrix.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does syncing place an order or create a support ticket?">
    No. Standards creates planning and deployment tracking. Use the Home AI Assistant to submit a support ticket.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I do after a sync failure?">
    Inspect existing records before retrying because some work may already have completed; report affected companies and standards to the MSP administrator.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
