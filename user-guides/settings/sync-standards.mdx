Sync Standards creates a tracking record and a Planner item for every standard at every active company, and refreshes the Planner items that already exist. This page is for MSP staff who manage the Standards catalog.

## Before you start

- You need Manage access to Standards settings and an active billing status. Company administrator access does not grant this.
- The catalog needs at least one category. **Sync All Companies** is disabled while the catalog is empty.
- Review names, descriptions, default prices and category Planner types first. See [Manage the Standards catalog](/user-guides/settings/standards).
- The sync covers every active company. The company selector at the top of the page does not limit it to one company.

## Sync Standards to all companies

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > MSP Tools > Standards">
    Stay on the **Standards** tab. The sync saves any edits that are still pending before it starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sync All Companies">
    The **Sync Standards to All Companies** dialog opens. It shows how many items across how many categories will be synced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a schedule under Schedule new planner items">
    Pick **On a specific date** and choose a date with the date picker. The date becomes both the start date and the due date of each new Planner item. Or pick **Send to Unscheduled**. New items land in the Unscheduled lane with no start or due date.

    **Sync All Companies** in the dialog stays disabled until you pick a date or choose **Send to Unscheduled**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide whether to turn on Hide from company users">
    Turn it on to mark each new Planner item as internal-only, so company users do not see it in the client portal. Leave it off to let company users see new items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sync All Companies in the dialog">
    The dialog closes and a message says **Standards sync started. Company records will be updated in the background.** Click **Cancel** instead to leave without syncing. Your schedule and visibility choices are not remembered for the next sync.

    <Frame caption="Review the scope, schedule and visibility before you submit. Counts depend on your catalog.">
      <img src="/images/product/standards-sync-options.png" alt="Standards sync dialog with specific-date, Unscheduled and Hide from company users choices." width="1024" height="1420" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the background task to finish">
    The started message means the task was submitted, not that every record is done. Large MSPs can take a moment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check the result

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open MSP Tools > Standards in the sidebar">
    This is the company matrix. Check a few company rows and open their linked work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or open Planner > List and filter Source to Standards">
    Confirm the names, statuses, dates, estimates and visibility of the new items.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What a sync changes

| Record | What the sync does |
| --- | --- |
| A company and standard with no tracking record | Creates a **Not Started** tracking record for each active company where the standard applies. See [Choose which Standards apply](/user-guides/settings/standards-applicability). |
| A tracking record with no linked Planner item | Creates the Planner item, including for optional standards and for categories with no Planner type. |
| A standard that no longer applies to a company | Archives the Planner item the sync created. The sync restores an item it archived if the standard applies again. |
| Existing company deployment status | Kept as recorded. |
| Linked Planner title, description and type | Refreshed from the catalog, or from the standard's [Planner template](/user-guides/settings/standards#apply-a-planner-template) when it has one. |
| Linked Planner estimate | Set from **Default Price** when the item has no cost rows. When it has cost rows, their total is the estimate. |
| Open item without the standard's current Planner template | Applies the template: business value, hours, probability, notes, cost rows and compliance links. Completed, Cancelled and archived items are skipped. |
| Linked Planner status | Matched to the Standards status. Planner-only **On Hold** and **Pending Approval** states are kept. |
| Existing Planner dates and visibility | Not changed. The schedule and **Hide from company users** choices apply to new items only. |
| Existing public and private Planner notes | Not changed, unless a Planner template is applied to an open item. The template's notes are then written to it. |

<Warning>
The dialog says existing company statuses are not overwritten. That covers statuses only. A sync can replace a company-specific Planner title, description, estimate or type with the shared catalog values. When a standard has a Planner template, it can also replace the notes, business value, hours and probability on open items.
</Warning>

The default estimate on a new Planner item is a planning number. It is not a purchase or an approved quote.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Sync All Companies** is disabled. | The catalog has no categories, or a sync is already starting. Add a category and standard first. |
| The dialog's **Sync All Companies** button is disabled. | **On a specific date** is selected and no date is chosen. Pick a date or choose **Send to Unscheduled**. |
| The sync fails to start (**Failed to start sync** or an error message). | Check that you have Manage access to Standards settings and that billing is active. Then try again. |
| Some companies or standards have no new Planner item. | The sync may have only partly finished, or the standard does not apply to that company. Check the matrix, wait a few minutes, then run the sync again. Do not delete tracking or Planner records to force a retry. |
| Existing Planner items did not change. | Completed, Cancelled and archived items keep their template. Check the item's status. |
| Company users cannot see new items. | **Hide from company users** was on for that sync. Change visibility on the Planner items. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does saving a new standard distribute it automatically?">
    No. Saving the catalog and **Sync All Companies** are separate steps.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I sync only the company selected at the top?">
    No. The sync covers every active company in the MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the schedule reschedule existing work?">
    No. The date and the Unscheduled choice apply only to Planner items the sync creates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Hide from company users hide existing work?">
    No. It applies only to newly created Planner items. Change existing items in Planner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can optional standards or categories with no Planner type create work?">
    Yes. The sync creates tracking records and Planner items for them too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does sync reset existing deployment statuses?">
    No. Company Standards statuses are kept. Linked Planner statuses are matched to them, except On Hold and Pending Approval.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which existing Planner fields can change?">
    The title, description, estimate, type and status can refresh from Standards. When the standard has a Planner template, open items also get the template's fields.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does sync replace company notes?">
    Only when a Planner template is applied to an open item. Then the template's notes are written to it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do the generated items show up?">
    In **Planner > List** with the **Source** filter set to **Standards**, and from the linked work in the Standards matrix.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does syncing place an order or create a support ticket?">
    No. Standards creates planning and deployment tracking only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
