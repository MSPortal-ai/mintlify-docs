Use this page to find and set up everything reusable in client onboarding: templates, client pages, forms, checklists, client roles, portals and PSA defaults. It is for MSP staff. Company users follow the [client portal instructions](/user-guides/client-onboarding/open-your-onboarding).

## Before you start

- Client onboarding must be enabled for your tenant. If you do not see the settings, ask your account administrator to enable it.
- Viewing needs `read_settings_onboarding`. Creating and editing needs `write_settings_onboarding`. Archiving checklists and other administration needs `manage_settings_onboarding`.
- The **PSA projects** section appears only when a PSA integration (Halo PSA, ConnectWise or Autotask) is active.

## Find the onboarding settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Projects">
    In the unified experience, all reusable onboarding settings live here. The earlier experience uses **Settings > Client Onboarding** instead. Its tabs are **Templates**, **Portals**, **Client Roles**, **Pages**, **Forms**, **Checklists** and **PSA Projects**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Templates for the reusable libraries">
    The **Templates** tab holds **Templates**, **Checklists** and **Forms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Client Portals for portal setup">
    The **Client Portals** tab holds **Portals**, **Client roles**, **Pages** and **PSA projects**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In the unified experience, existing company and settings onboarding links redirect to these tabs.

To work on one client's portal, open **Projects** and select the **Onboarding projects** quick filter. Open the portal's project. These tabs and sections hold its work:

| Where | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** > **MSP Dashboard** | The portal overview |
| **Project Plan** > **Tasks** | Delivery and onboarding tasks |
| **Files & Forms** > **Documents** | Project files and client uploads |
| **Client Portal** | **Pages**, **Activity**, **Key dates** and **Portal settings** |

## Manage client roles

Client roles fill the pickers for onboarding contacts, client-owned task assignees, email recipients and client previews. They are separate from permission roles, which control access to the portal.

MSP-owned task assignees use Team Roles instead. Manage them under **Settings > Users & Roles > Team Roles**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the client roles list">
    Open the **Client roles** section (**Client Roles** tab in the earlier experience).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a role">
    Type a name in **Add a client role** and click **Add**. The role saves right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename a role">
    Click **Rename role**, edit the name, then click **Save**. Click **Cancel** to discard the rename.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder roles">
    Click **Move up** or **Move down**. The order saves right away and applies to every role picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove or restore a role">
    Click **Remove role**. The role moves to **Removed roles**. Click **Restore role** there to bring it back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Check the usage counts on a role before you rename or remove it. Removed roles stay on existing templates and portal contacts. They are hidden only from future assignments.

If an MSP task's **Assignee Role** picker shows only one role, add the missing roles in **Team Roles**, save them, then reopen the template. Client roles do not fill that picker. See [template role choices](/user-guides/client-onboarding/templates#assignee-role-choices).

## Build a reusable checklist

Checklists attach to tasks in the template builder. They appear as checkable sub-items during onboarding.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the checklists list">
    Open the **Checklists** section (**Checklists** tab in the earlier experience). Use **Search checklists...** to find an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new checklist">
    Click **New Checklist**. This opens an unsaved draft. Nothing is saved yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter a **Name**. Add a **Description** and **Tags** if you want. Tags are comma-separated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add items">
    Type an item in **Add an item...** and press Enter. Drag the handles to reorder items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import or generate items (optional)">
    Click **Import**, paste one item per line, then click **Import** again. Or click **AI Generate**, describe the checklist and click **Generate**. Both add items to the list for you to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the checklist">
    Click **Create** for a new checklist, or **Save** for an existing one. Empty item labels are dropped.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Other actions on a saved checklist:

- **Duplicate** makes a saved copy right away.
- **Archive** asks you to confirm. It needs `manage_settings_onboarding`. Archived checklists leave the checklist picker, and existing portals are not affected. Switch to the **Archived** view and click **Restore** to bring one back.
- A checklist synced from an integration is read-only. Click **Duplicate** to get an editable copy.

Editing a reusable checklist does not reset progress in portals that already use it.

## Set PSA defaults for portals

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open PSA projects">
    Open the **PSA projects** section (**PSA Projects** tab in the earlier experience).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the general options">
    Under **General**, set **Auto-create tickets**, **Status sync** and **Default billing status**. **Status sync** runs both ways every 10 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the provider defaults">
    Under **Provider Defaults**, pick the board or team, ticket type, priority, status and other defaults for each connected PSA. The lists come from your synced PSA data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set task status mappings">
    Under **Task status mappings**, pick the PSA status for each onboarding task status. The default is **Automatic match**, which uses the closest PSA status by name. For ConnectWise, choose the board first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set time entry defaults (ConnectWise only)">
    Under **Time entry defaults**, set **Default work role** and **Default work type**. MSPortal uses them when it logs onboarding task time to ConnectWise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. The button stays disabled until you change something.
  </Step>
</Steps>

These are defaults only. When you create or start a portal, check the project system and the ticket-creation option again.

## Which actions save right away

| Area | Save behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Templates | The header saves template details. Phase, item and link controls also save on their own. |
| Client roles | Add, reorder, remove and restore save right away. Rename uses **Save**. |
| Pages | **Add Page** and **Save Changes** in the page editor. Template links save separately. |
| Forms | **New** and field rows save right away. The header **Save** saves your edits. |
| Checklists | **New Checklist** opens a draft that needs **Create**. **Duplicate** saves right away. Edits need **Save**. |
| PSA projects | **Save Changes** |
| Portals | Creating, linking a project, activating and archiving are separate actions. |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The onboarding settings are missing | Ask your account administrator to enable client onboarding. Check that you have `read_settings_onboarding`. |
| The **PSA projects** section is missing | Connect Halo PSA, ConnectWise or Autotask in Integration Settings first. |
| A PSA selector is empty | Check the PSA connection and wait for the sync to finish. Pick from the synced list. |
| You cannot edit a checklist | It is synced from an integration. Click **Duplicate** and edit the copy. |
| A checklist is missing from the picker | It may be archived. Switch to the **Archived** view and click **Restore**. |
| The **Assignee Role** picker for an MSP task has one role | Add the missing roles in **Settings > Users & Roles > Team Roles**, then reopen the template. |
| A checklist import or AI result is gone after you leave | Imported and generated items are not saved until you click **Create** or **Save**. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are client roles the same as permission roles?">
    No. Client roles fill client-owned task assignees, contacts and email recipient pickers. MSP-owned task assignees use Team Roles. Permission roles control access to the app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Remove role remove it from existing portals?">
    No. It only hides the role from future assignments. Existing template and contact uses stay. **Restore role** makes it available again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does New Checklist save a checklist?">
    No. It opens a draft. Click **Create** to save it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the PSA projects section missing?">
    It appears only for an active Halo PSA, ConnectWise or Autotask integration. Native MSPortal project linking does not need a PSA.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does saving PSA defaults create tickets?">
    No. The defaults apply when you link a project or start a portal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
