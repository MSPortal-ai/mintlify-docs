A compliance schedule creates assessment runs for your companies on a repeating or one-time basis. This page is for MSP staff who manage Compliance settings. A schedule is tenant-wide. The top Company selector does not limit it. Its **Targets** decide which companies get runs.

## Before you start

- The Compliance feature must be available for your account.
- You need write access to Compliance settings to create, edit and pause schedules. Deleting needs manage access.
- Create an active run template first. Go to **Settings > Operations > Compliance**, open the **Runs** tab and build a template with the groups and checks you want. Only active templates appear in the schedule's template list. A template is the assessment package. A schedule decides when and for whom to create runs.

## Create a schedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Schedule tab">
    Go to **Settings > Operations > Compliance** and click **Schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Schedule">
    Click **Create Schedule** in the page header. The **Create Schedule** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter a **Schedule Name**, an optional description and the **Run Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the targets">
    Under **Targets**, pick one option. The form starts on **All companies**.

    | Option | Who gets runs |
    |--------|---------------|
    | **All companies** | Every active company in your tenant |
    | **Specific companies** | The companies you pick |
    | **Company groups** | Companies in the groups you pick |
    | **Industries** | Companies whose industry matches the ones you pick |

    For any option except **All companies**, pick at least one entry. The summary under the picker counts targets selected. In the groups and industries options it counts groups or industries, not companies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the cadence">
    Choose the **Frequency** and fill in the fields it shows (see the table below). The **Preview** box shows the dates. Changing the frequency resets its fields to defaults, so set the frequency first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the start date">
    Choose the **Start date**. For a one-time schedule, choose the **Run date**. The first run is created on or after that date. The form has no time-of-day or time-zone setting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Schedule**. A toast says **Schedule created successfully**. New schedules are active. **Cancel** closes the form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recurrence choices

| Frequency | What you set |
|-----------|--------------|
| **One-time** | **Run date** |
| **Weekly** | **Day of the week** and **Start date** |
| **Semi-monthly** | Two **Days of the month**. The second can be the last day |
| **Monthly** | A **Day of the month**, the last day, or a weekday such as the first Monday |
| **Quarterly** | **Starting month** and **Day pattern**. Repeats every three months |
| **Semi-annual** | **Starting month** and **Day pattern**. Repeats every six months |
| **Annual** | **Month** and **Day pattern** |

**Day pattern** is **On a specific day of the month** or **On a specific weekday**. Pick from the day choices the form offers.

## Check a schedule

The **Schedule** table shows each schedule's **Schedule Name**, **Run Template**, **Targets**, **Frequency**, **Starts**, **Last Run** and **Status**. Open **Edit** to check the template, targets and cadence. Runs are created in the background. After a run is created, open Compliance to see the runs and their checks.

## Edit, pause or delete a schedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit">
    Click **Edit** on the row, change the form and click **Update Schedule**. **Cancel** leaves it unchanged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pause or resume">
    Use the **Status** switch on the row. It saves at once and shows **Active** or **Paused**. Pausing stops future runs. It does not remove runs already created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    Click **Delete**, then **Delete Schedule** to confirm. Runs that have not executed yet are cancelled. Completed runs and their evidence stay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| **Create Schedule** is disabled or will not save | Check the **Schedule Name**, **Run Template** and that you picked at least one target |
| The template is missing from the list | Only active templates show. Activate it in the **Runs** tab |
| **No company groups yet** appears | Use **Specific companies** or **Industries**, or create a group first |
| **No industries on file** appears | Set the **Industry** field on your companies first |
| No runs appeared | Check that the schedule is **Active**, that its start date has passed, that the targets match active companies, and look in Compliance for new runs. Do not create a duplicate schedule |
| The switch or **Edit** is disabled | You need write access to Compliance settings. **Delete** needs manage access |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the Company selector restrict my schedule?">
    No. Schedules are tenant-wide. Choose the companies in **Targets**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does a new schedule target?">
    All active companies in your tenant. Change **Targets** before saving for a narrower schedule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Pausing need a Save?">
    No. The **Status** switch saves at once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Last Run mean all checks are done?">
    No. It shows when MSPortal last created a run. Checks are finished separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I pick an exact time or time zone?">
    No. The form offers dates and recurrence patterns only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting a schedule delete past assessments?">
    No. It stops future runs. Completed assessments stay.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
