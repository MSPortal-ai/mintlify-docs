This page is for MSP admins who control how projects look and who can see them. Open **Settings > Projects** to set client access, display fields, Kanban, status mappings, custom fields, project types, templates, forms, and client portals.

The unified page has three top-level tabs.

| Tab | Sections |
| --- | --- |
| **Project Settings** | **Display Configuration**, **Client project access**, **Task types**, **Kanban Board Settings**, **Status Mapping**, **Custom Fields**, **Project Types**, and **Upcoming projects** when available |
| **Templates** | **Templates**, **Checklists**, and **Forms** |
| **Client Portals** | **Portals**, **Client roles**, **Pages**, and **PSA projects** when a supported PSA is active |

The page opens on **Project Settings > Display Configuration**. Your role controls which sections you see. Saved links open the section they point to.

## Before you start

- You need project-settings read access to view these settings and write access to change them.
- The shared **Templates** and **Forms** libraries accept either the project-settings or the onboarding-settings permission.
- **Client Portals** and checklist administration need onboarding-settings access.
- PSA selectors (boards, lines of business, custom fields) depend on the integrations connected to your portal. See [Integrations](/user-guides/integrations/index).

<Warning>
The header **Save** button applies to **Client project access**, **Display Configuration**, **Kanban Board Settings**, **Status Mapping**, and **Upcoming projects**. Task types, custom fields, project types, templates, forms, and client portal sections save on their own.
</Warning>

## Choose what projects show

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Display Configuration">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Display Configuration**.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Desktop">
        <Frame caption="Branch preview: project configuration, reusable templates, and client portal settings have separate top-level tabs.">
          <img src="/images/product/projects-settings-display-desktop-preview.png" alt="Project Settings with Project Settings, Templates, and Client Portals groups above Display Configuration" width="2880" height="1920" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Phone">
        <Frame caption="Branch preview: the configuration sections wrap beneath the main settings tabs.">
          <img src="/images/product/projects-settings-display-mobile-preview.png" alt="Project Settings on a phone with grouped navigation, wrapped section controls, and Teams chat setting" width="780" height="1688" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn sections on or off">
    Under **Section Visibility**, switch the sections shown on the project detail page. The choices are Timeline, Progress, Hours, Financials, Details, PSA Phases, Project Tickets, Time Entries, and Project Summary.

    - **Project Tickets** needs **PSA Phases** on.
    - **Time Entries** needs **Project Tickets** on.
    - **PSA Phases** is locked on for Halo PSA and shows a **Required by Halo PSA** badge.
    - The ticket Kanban has its own switch under **Kanban Board Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the fields to show">
    Under **Field Visibility**, tick the fields shown on project cards and the project list. The choices are Project Name, Company, Status, Progress %, Start Date, Due Date, Estimated Hours, Actual Hours, Hours Variance, Summary, Project Lead, Timeline Comparison, and Last Changed. The **Card Preview** updates as you tick.

    <img src="/images/product/projects-settings-fields.png" alt="Project field visibility checkboxes alongside the complete built-in card preview" width="1756" height="850" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save** in the header. Display options only change what shows. They do not grant access to projects or replace role permissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Chat with the project manager in Teams

<Note>
Branch preview: this setting is part of the upcoming Projects update. The [contact guide](/user-guides/projects/manage-project-contacts#chat-with-the-project-manager-in-teams) shows the icon.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Display Configuration">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Display Configuration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the switch">
    Switch on **Chat with project manager in Teams**. It is off by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**. A small Teams icon now appears beside **Project Lead** in a project's **Overview > Client Dashboard > Contacts**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To hide the icon again, switch it off and select **Save**.

The icon uses the project manager's email address, which must match their Teams sign-in address. It stays hidden when no valid address is available. Clients still need access to the project, and Teams must let them chat with the manager.

## Control which projects clients see

This section controls which projects company users can see. MSP staff keep their own permitted project scope.

<img src="/images/product/projects-settings-access.png" alt="Client project access choices and active/inactive project health status controls" width="1900" height="1440" />

| Mode | What company users see |
| --- | --- |
| **All projects** | The projects otherwise available to each company user. |
| **Filtered projects** | Projects that match at least one selected project type, line of business, board, or specific project. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Client project access">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Client project access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Filtered projects">
    Select **Filtered projects** to show the selectors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the projects to include">
    Choose **Project types**, **Project boards** or **Lines of business**, and **Specific projects**. Use the search box to pick individual projects. Autotask uses lines of business instead of project boards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the access rules">
    Select **Save** in the header. Then check the result as a user in the client role and company.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Filtered projects with nothing selected shows company users no projects. A project hidden on its own project page stays hidden even when it matches a filter. Global search follows the same rules.
</Warning>

### Set project health status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Client project access">
    Stay on **Client project access** and scroll to **Project health status**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark each status">
    Mark each exact PSA status **Active** or **Inactive**. New PSA statuses start as active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Inactive statuses are left out of health KPIs and **Projects needing attention**. This does not change the project's status in your PSA, client access, or Kanban columns.

## Task types and individual task controls

<Note>Branch preview: the filterable Task types table requires the completed Projects update. The instructions below describe the intended post-rollout workflow.</Note>

### Set defaults for a task type

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Task types">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Task types**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the task type">
    Filter the list by **Task Name**, **Board**, **Internal only**, or **Include in scheduling**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flip the switches">
    Turn **Internal only** or **Include in scheduling** on or off for the type. Each switch saves at once. The header **Save** button is not used.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **Internal only** hides the task from company users everywhere in MSPortal.
- **Include in scheduling** shows the task in schedules, Gantt, and planning timelines.
- A type default applies to existing and future tasks of that type. Individual task overrides are kept.
- Excluded tasks keep their saved dependency links. The links stay inactive until scheduling is turned on again.
- To reverse a setting, flip the switch back. These controls do not change the PSA record.

### Override a single task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the task controls">
    In a project's **Phases** tab, select the **Visibility and scheduling** gear icon on the task row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the switches">
    Turn **Internal only** or **Include in scheduling** on or off. Changes save at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to the type default">
    Select **Use type default** to remove the override. The type default applies again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If no synced task types appear, you can still set controls on individual tasks. Only MSP staff see these controls.

## Set up Kanban

<img src="/images/product/projects-settings-kanban.png" alt="Kanban Board Settings with technician column modes, PSA groups, and specific technician selectors" width="1900" height="1560" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Kanban Board Settings">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Kanban Board Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the board">
    Switch on **Enable Kanban Board** to show the ticket board on eligible project workspaces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the technician columns">
    Under **Kanban technicians**, pick **Only technicians with tickets** or **Everyone in the roster**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the roster">
    Select **PSA groups** and **Specific technicians** to limit who can be assigned tickets. Leave both empty to allow every active PSA agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save** in the header.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Only technicians with tickets** | Shows Unassigned and the technicians who own project tickets. **Assign to…** opens a picker for another eligible technician. |
| **Everyone in the roster** | Shows every assignable technician, including those without tickets on the project. |
| **PSA groups** | Includes technicians in any selected role, department, team, or line of business. |
| **Specific technicians** | Also includes the selected technicians, whatever their group. |

ConnectWise projects stay limited to the ConnectWise project team. The roster settings can narrow that team further.

## Map statuses to columns

Configure **Project Statuses** and **Ticket Statuses** separately. Project Statuses group whole projects. Ticket Statuses group tickets inside the project Kanban.

<img src="/images/product/projects-settings-statuses.png" alt="Status Mapping with raw PSA status mode, unmapped statuses, and configurable project groups" width="1900" height="1600" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Status Mapping">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Status Mapping**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose raw statuses or groups">
    Switch on **Use Raw PSA Statuses** to show the original PSA names. To group statuses, leave it off and review the columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place unmapped statuses">
    Select **Assign** beside an unmapped status and pick a group, or drag the status into a group. **Smart Match** places the rest. **Generate with AI** drafts the groups. Review either result before you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review columns and defaults">
    Add a group with **Add custom category...**. Rename, reorder, or delete a group from its menu. Mark a status as the default for a column to set where a board move lands. A status belongs to one group at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the mappings">
    Check both **Project Statuses** and **Ticket Statuses**, then select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Check the **Unmapped Statuses** list before you save. Projects with an unmapped status are hidden from the dashboard. **Clear All** clears the current mapping, so review it before you save.
</Warning>

## Add PSA custom fields

Show ConnectWise, Halo, or Autotask custom fields on projects and project tickets, and choose who can see and edit them.

<img src="/images/product/projects-settings-custom-fields.png" alt="Custom Fields settings showing provider sync status and configured project fields" width="1900" height="1680" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Custom Fields">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Custom Fields**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check field definitions">
    Look at **Last synced**. Definitions refresh every day. After you change a field in your PSA, select **Sync now** and wait for the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a field">
    Under **Project fields** or **Project ticket fields**, select **Add field**, search the PSA fields, and add one. Open the added field to configure it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility and access">
    Turn on **Show on projects**. For project fields, turn on **List and filters** to add a column and a filter to the project list. Set **Who can view** and **Who can edit** to **Staff only** or **Selected roles**. Selected roles with no roles chosen gives no access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow edits if needed">
    Turn on **Allow edits** for a field that supports it. The edit is written back to the PSA. The person editing also needs view access to the field, project edit permission, and edit access to the field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Project fields appear on the project overview. Project ticket fields appear when a ticket is expanded on the **Phases** tab.

<Note>
Custom field changes save at once. Set role access before you turn on **Show on projects**.
</Note>

## Manage project types

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Project Types">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Project Types**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a type">
    Select **New project type**, enter a **Name**, switch **Onboarding** on or off, and select **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn onboarding on or off">
    Use the **Onboarding** switch in the table. It saves at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename or archive a type">
    Open **Project type actions** on a row. Select **Edit** to rename an MSPortal type, or **Archive** to take it out of the list used for new templates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore an archived type">
    Change the table's **Status** filter to **Archived**, open **Project type actions**, and select **Restore**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- PSA types come from your synced projects. Their names cannot be changed here.
- Onboarding types keep the client portal, dates, pages, and invitations when you pick them in a template.
- Any other type builds a plain project. The template hides the portal sections and shows a **Default status** field.
- A template with no type uses **Client onboarding (default)**.
- Existing records keep their saved type when you archive it.

## Set upcoming project rules

This section shows only when a PSA with sales opportunities is connected.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Upcoming projects">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Upcoming projects**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick opportunities">
    Turn on **Include sales opportunities**. Choose **Opportunity types** or enter phrases under **Opportunity name contains (optional)**, one per line. An opportunity matches a selected type or a phrase. Leave both empty to include every open opportunity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow by stage and status">
    Pick **Sales stages** and **Opportunity statuses** to narrow the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add planner items">
    Pick **Planner item statuses** to list planner items next to opportunities. Leave it empty to leave planner items out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save** in the header.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Upcoming Projects list and its schedule both use these rules.

## Create and manage templates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Templates">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Templates > Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a template">
    Select **New Template**, set the **Project type**, and select **Create Template**. If a PSA is connected, **Import from PSA** brings in an existing template instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the structure">
    Add phases, items, and actions. Each has its own editor and saves on its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the details">
    Select **Save Changes** to save the template's name and other details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use **Templates > Checklists** for reusable checklist definitions. This section needs onboarding-settings access.

3. To hide it from the pickers again, open it under **Active** and select **Archive**.

## Manage forms

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Forms">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Templates > Forms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maintain form definitions">
    Create, edit, archive, and restore the reusable forms your projects use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share a form with clients">
    Staff share each form before clients can use it. In a project's **Forms** tab, pick the form and switch on **Share this form with clients**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Staff and clients both fill forms from a project's **Forms** tab, including projects without an onboarding portal. See [Project Forms](/user-guides/projects/forms) for **Save progress**, **Submit**, sharing, and response links. Onboarding clients can also fill forms attached to portal tasks. See [Forms](/user-guides/client-onboarding/forms).

Responses to an archived form are read-only.

### Give roles access to forms

In the role editor, open **Projects** and set these permissions.

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **View forms and permitted responses** | See forms and the responses the role may view. |
| **Submit and edit form responses** | Fill, save, and edit responses. |
| **Share forms with clients** | Share a form with clients. This is a staff action. |

Plain Write access to Projects does not include **Submit and edit form responses** or **Share forms with clients**. Select them separately. Project and company access still apply. Give client roles the view and submit permissions they need.

## Set up client portals

This tab needs onboarding-settings access. It has **Portals**, **Client roles**, **Pages**, and **PSA projects** when a supported PSA is connected. Each section has its own controls and saves on its own.

These are reusable settings and the portal directory. To manage one portal, open its linked project and use **Overview > MSP Dashboard** and the **Client Portal** section.

## Save, leave, or reset

- The header **Save** applies your changes across Client project access, Display Configuration, Kanban Board Settings, Status Mapping, and Upcoming projects. You can switch between these sections, visit Templates or Client Portals, and return to the same unsaved changes.
- If you leave with unsaved changes, the app asks you to confirm.
- **Reset** puts the product defaults in the form. Nothing is saved until you select **Save**. Reset does not undo custom field changes, which save at once.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Save** is greyed out | Check that you have write access, that you changed something, and that a save is not already running. |
| Clients cannot find a project | Check the company scope, the client role's permissions, the access filters, and whether the project is hidden on its own page. |
| A project is missing from health KPIs | Its exact PSA status may be marked **Inactive** under **Project health status**. |
| A grouped board leaves out work | Map the item's PSA status under **Status Mapping**, or switch on **Use Raw PSA Statuses**. |
| A technician is missing from Kanban | Check that the person is an active PSA agent, the roster filters, and the ConnectWise project team. |
| A custom field is missing or read-only | Check **Last synced**, **Show on projects**, role access, **Allow edits**, and your project permissions. |
| A client cannot use a form | Check that the form is shared and that the client role has the forms permissions. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I change which projects my clients can see?">
    Open **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Client project access** and choose **Filtered projects**. Select **Save** in the header.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my custom field change save without pressing Save?">
    Custom Fields, Task types, Project Types, and Templates save on their own. The header **Save** does not apply to them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I turn off PSA Phases for Halo PSA?">
    No. Halo PSA projects need phases, so the switch is locked on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Internal only do?">
    It hides the task from company users everywhere in MSPortal. It does not change the task in your PSA.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find archived project types?">
    On **Project Types**, change the **Status** filter to **Archived**. Select **Restore** from **Project type actions** to bring one back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Reset delete my saved settings?">
    No. It only fills the form with defaults. Your saved settings stay until you select **Save**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
