Open **Settings > Planner** to manage the views the Planner board can draw. A view names a card style, a density and the fields a card shows. MSP views appear in the board's **Board view** menu for your team; client views are what your clients see.

## Before you start

- You need **Manage Planner** to open this page.
- Every portal starts with the built-in views: **Detailed**, **Compact**, **Dense**, **Sticky notes**, **Collection** and **Client view**. You can rename them, change them, or add your own.
- Each change saves as soon as you confirm it; there is no separate Save button.

## Add or edit a view

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Click **Add view**, or click a view's name. The row menu also offers **Edit** and **Duplicate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the view and choose its audience">
    **MSP** views are offered to your team on the board. **Clients** views are for client users. The last view of each audience cannot change audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a card style and density">
    **Cards** is the classic card. **Sticky notes** draws tinted notes by item type. **Collectible cards** draws trading cards with a rarity from business value; this style is available once collectible cards are turned on for your portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what cards show">
    Turn **Linked items**, **Status and value badges**, **Cost**, **Hours**, **Assignee** and **Description** on or off. The sample card on the right updates as you change settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign clients (client views)">
    Under **Clients**, choose the companies that see this view. Every other client sees the client default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create view** or **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set the defaults

Use a row's menu to **Set as MSP default** (what your team sees until they pick another view) or **Set as client default** (what clients see unless their company is assigned a view). The defaults show as pills on the row.

## Delete a view

Choose **Delete** from a row's menu. A default view, or the last view for its audience, cannot be deleted. Clients assigned to a deleted view see the client default.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A client sees the wrong view | Open the client view and check its **Clients** list; a company can be assigned to only one view. Otherwise the client default applies. |
| **Collectible cards** is greyed out | Collectible cards are not turned on for your portal yet; a view saved with that style draws classic cards until they are. |
| The board does not show a new view | Reload the Planner. Your browser remembers the last view you picked, so open **Board view** and choose the new one. |
