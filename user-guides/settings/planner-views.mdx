## Open Planner settings

You need **Manage Planner**, and Planner must be turned on for your portal. Open **Settings > Operations > Planner** (`/settings/planner`). The page title is **Planner**, and **Settings** is the default tab.

For now, the Settings card says **More planner settings will appear here.** There are no controls to save on this tab. With view customization off, **Views**, **Add view** and the board's **Manage views** link are hidden. A link ending in `?tab=views` still opens Settings while customization is off.

## Views preview prerequisites

The remaining instructions apply only when Planner view customization is enabled for your portal. The timeline otherwise keeps the original **Comfortable**, **Compact** and **Dense** options. Custom themes, sticky notes and client view assignments do not apply while customization is off; saved views and assignments are kept.

When available, open **Settings > Planner > Views**. **Board view > Manage views** also opens this tab directly (`/settings/planner?tab=views`); opening Planner from the settings menu still starts on Settings. On a phone, open the page's **Actions** menu to find **Add view** while on Views.

A view names a card style, a density, a board theme, the fields a card shows and the figures each column header shows. MSP views appear in the board's **Board view** menu for your team; client views are what your clients see.

- Built-in views are **Detailed**, **Compact**, **Dense**, **Sticky notes**, **Whiteboard**, **Collection** and **Client view**. You can rename them, change them, or add your own.
- Each change saves as soon as you confirm it; there is no separate page-level Save button.
- Collectible cards have their own separate availability setting, which is also off by default. Enabling Views alone does not enable collectible cards.

## Name the board columns

The **Settings** tab names the board's side columns in your own terms, such as **Backlog** for Unscheduled or **Delivered** for Completed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Settings tab">
    Go to **Settings > Planner**. The **Settings** tab opens first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the names">
    Under **Board columns**, type a name in **Unscheduled column**, **Completed column**, or both. Leave a field blank to keep the standard name, which appears as its placeholder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The board's side bars, panels and the **Show Completed column** option use the new names for your team and your clients. **Use standard names** puts both back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You need **Manage Planner** to change the names. PDF exports and planner analytics keep the standard names.

## Add or edit a view

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    On **Views**, click **Add view** (in **Actions** on a phone), or click a
    view's name. The row menu also offers **Edit** and **Duplicate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the view and choose its audience">
    **MSP** views are offered to your team on the board. **Clients** views are
    for client users. The last view of each audience cannot change audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a card style and density">
    **Cards** is the classic card. **Sticky notes** draws tinted notes.
    **Collectible cards** draws trading cards with a rarity from business value;
    this style is available once collectible cards are turned on for your
    portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a board theme">
    Under **Board**, choose **Standard** (the portal's own surfaces),
    **Whiteboard** (a dotted board with marker titles, notes held by magnets),
    **Corkboard** (cork with pushpins) or **Blueprint** (a drafting grid with
    white lines). The theme changes the board behind the cards, not the cards'
    content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a note colour (sticky notes)">
    For the sticky-note style, **Note colour by** decides a note's tint: **Item
    type** gives every type its own colour, **Business value** colours High
    pink, Medium yellow and Low green, and **Status** colours by status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what cards show">
    Turn **Linked items**, **Status and value badges**, **Cost**, **Hours**,
    **Assignee** and **Description** on or off. The preview on the right draws a
    sample column and item in this view and updates as you change settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the column header figures">
    Under **Show in column headers**, keep or hide **Item count**, **Hours** and
    **Budget** for each timeline column. Turn off hours and budget on a client
    view to keep pricing off the board your clients see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign clients (client views)">
    Under **Clients**, choose the companies that see this view. Every other
    client sees the client default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">Click **Create view** or **Save**.</Step>
</Steps>

## Set the defaults

Use a row's menu to **Set as MSP default** (what your team sees until they pick another view) or **Set as client default** (what clients see unless their company is assigned a view). The defaults show as pills on the row.

## Delete a view

Choose **Delete** from a row's menu. A default view, or the last view for its audience, cannot be deleted. Clients assigned to a deleted view see the client default. A built-in view you delete stays deleted; built-in views MSPortal adds later appear in your list on their own.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom                              | What to check                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Views** or **Add view** is missing | View customization is off by default. When enabled, select **Views**; on a phone, **Add view** is in **Actions**.                                                                           |
| Settings shows only a message        | **More planner settings will appear here.** is the current default content. The view editor has its own gated **Views** tab.                                                                |
| A client sees the wrong view         | First check that view customization is enabled. Then open the client view and check its **Clients** list; a company can be assigned to only one view. Otherwise the client default applies. |
| **Collectible cards** is greyed out  | Collectible cards are not turned on for your portal yet; a view saved with that style draws classic cards until they are.                                                                   |
| The board does not show a new view   | Reload the Planner. Your browser remembers the last view you picked, so open **Board view** and choose the new one.                                                                         |
| A column header shows no figures     | The view has every figure under **Show in column headers** turned off. Turn one on, or pick another view.                                                                                   |
