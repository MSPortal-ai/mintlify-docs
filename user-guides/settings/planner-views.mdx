**Settings > Planner** holds two things on one page: **Board columns**, which names the board's side columns, and **Views**, which decides how the board looks for your team and for your clients.

## Before you start

You need **Manage Planner**, and Planner must be turned on for your portal. Open **Settings > Operations > Planner** (`/settings/planner`), or choose **Manage views** from the board's **Board view** menu.

## Name the board columns

Name the board's side columns in your own terms, such as **Recommended** for Unscheduled or **Delivered** for Completed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the names">
    Under **Board columns**, type a name in **Unscheduled column**, **Completed column**, or both. Leave a field blank to keep the standard name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The board's side columns, panels and the **Show Completed column** option use the new names for your team and your clients. **Use standard names** puts both back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

PDF exports and planner analytics keep the standard names.

## What a view controls

A view names a card style, a density, a board theme, the fields each card shows and the figures each column header shows. **MSP** views appear in the board's **Board view** menu for your team. **Clients** views are what your client users see.

- **Card style**: **Cards** is the classic card with figures, status pills and linked records. **Sticky notes** draws tinted notes with the title in large type.
- **Density**: **Comfortable**, **Compact** or **Dense**.
- **Board**: **Standard** (the portal's own surfaces), **Whiteboard**, **Corkboard**, **Blueprint**, **Leather desk mat** or **Wood desk**. The theme changes the board behind the cards, not what the cards say.
- **Show on cards**: **Linked items**, **Status and value badges**, **Cost**, **Hours**, **Assignee** and **Description**.
- **Show in column headers**: **Item count**, **Hours** and **Budget**.

The built-in views are **Detailed**, **Compact**, **Dense**, **Sticky notes**, **Whiteboard** and **Client view**. You can rename and change them, or add your own, up to 20 views.

## Add or edit a view

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Click **Add view** in the page header, or click a view's name. A row's menu also offers **Edit** and **Duplicate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the view and choose its audience">
    Under **Audience**, choose **MSP** for your team or **Clients** for client users. The only view left for an audience cannot change audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a card style and a board">
    Choose **Cards** or **Sticky notes** under **Card style**, then a theme under **Board**. **Preview** on the right draws a sample column and item in this view and updates as you change settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a density and a note colour">
    Choose a **Density**. For sticky notes, **Note colour by** sets each note's tint: **Item type**, **Business value** or **Status**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what cards and column headers show">
    Turn fields on or off under **Show on cards** and **Show in column headers**. Turn off **Hours** and **Budget** in the column headers of a client view to keep pricing off the board your clients see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">Click **Create view** for a new view, or **Save**. The change applies as soon as it saves.</Step>
</Steps>

## Choose who sees a client view

A client view opens with **Who sees this view** above its card style. Assign it to any mix of:

- **Clients**: every user at the chosen companies.
- **Client roles**: every client user with the chosen roles.
- **People**: single client users. Search by name or email.

A client user sees the view assigned to them first, then the one assigned to their role, then the one assigned to their company. Anyone with no assignment sees the client default. Client users do not get a **Board view** menu; they see only their own view.

## Set the defaults

From a view's row menu, choose **Set as MSP default** (what your team sees until they pick their own) or **Set as client default** (what client users see when no view is assigned to them, their role or their company). The defaults show as **MSP default** and **Client default** on the row.

Each staff member can also pick their own default on the board: open **Board view**, choose a view, then choose its **Make … my default** item, such as **Make Whiteboard my default**. The board then opens on that view for them on any device. **Stop using as my default** clears it.

## Delete a view

Choose **Delete** from a row's menu. A default view, or the last view for its audience, cannot be deleted. Clients, roles and people assigned to a deleted view see their next view, or the client default. A built-in view you delete stays deleted.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom                                    | What to check                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Settings > Planner** or **Add view** is missing | You need **Manage Planner**, and Planner must be turned on for your portal.                                                                   |
| **Delete** is greyed out                   | The view is the MSP or client default, or the last view for its audience. Set another default or add another view first.                          |
| A client user sees the wrong view          | Open the client views and check **Who sees this view**. A person's own assignment wins over their role's, and a role's wins over their company's. |
| The board does not open on my default      | Open **Board view**, choose the view, then choose **Make … my default**. Reload the Planner after a teammate changes the views.                 |
| A column header shows no figures           | The view has every figure under **Show in column headers** turned off. Turn one on, or pick another view.                                          |
| "You can have up to 20 views"              | Delete a view you no longer use before you add or duplicate another.                                                                               |
