Use Training settings to choose which courses your clients can take, set the branding on training certificates, and add your own courses. This page is for MSP staff.

## Before you start

- To enable or disable courses and change certificate branding, you need Training settings **write** access and active billing.
- To use **Create Course**, you need the **Manage Training** permission and active billing. Training settings access alone does not give you this.
- Premium courses use the Bigger Brains catalog. They are enabled only for companies that have Bigger Brains turned on in company settings. See [Enable Training](/user-guides/training/enabling-training) and [Billing settings](/user-guides/settings/billing).
- If your organization uses the direct Bigger Brains experience, **Settings > Training** does not appear and the page opens the **Training** module instead.

## Enable or disable courses

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Training">
    The course table lists every available course with its **Enabled** switch, **Course Title**, **Type**, **Category**, **Tags**, **Duration** and **Enrollments**. **Type** shows **Included**, **Premium** or **Custom**.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/settings/settings-training-courses.png" alt="Training settings course table with Enabled switches, Type, Category, Duration and Enrollments" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the company scope">
    The company selector sets who the change applies to. With one company selected, the change applies to that company. With no company selected, it applies to your whole organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the course">
    Use the search box and the column filters for title, **Type**, category, tags, **Duration** and **Enabled**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the course on or off">
    Click the **Enabled** switch, or open the row menu and choose **Enable Course** or **Disable Course**. The change saves immediately and a toast confirms it. There is no Save button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: change several courses at once">
    Tick the checkbox on each row, then use the bulk menu to choose **Enable Selected** or **Disable Selected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Enabling a course makes it available. It does not enroll anyone. To assign courses, use [Learning Paths](/user-guides/training/learning-paths) or [Find and enroll](/user-guides/training/find-and-enroll).

Disabling a course does not delete enrollments. Check the learner's course access before you assume an enrolled learner can still launch it.

## Preview a course

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Training">
    Find the course in the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the row menu and choose Preview Course">
    The course opens in a new tab in preview mode. A staff preview does not prove that a company user has access. Check availability and Premium access too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage video questions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row menu for a video course">
    Only video courses show **Manage Questions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Manage Questions">
    The question editor opens in a new tab. Add your questions there, then open **Preview Course** to check the quiz.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose certificate branding

The **Certificate branding** card at the top of **Settings > Training** sets the name and logo printed on training certificates. It applies to all clients and ignores the company selector.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Training">
    Find the **Certificate branding** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a branding option">
    Choose **MSP branding** to show your organization's name and logo. Choose **Client branding** to show the learner's company name and logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    The button stays off until you change the selection. Wait for **Certificate branding saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With **Client branding**, a company without a logo shows its name instead. Learners who do not belong to a company keep MSP branding. The setting is read when a certificate is viewed, downloaded or shared, so it applies to certificates you already issued. With read-only access you see the current choice and no **Save** button.

## Create a custom course

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Training and click Create Course">
    The button is at the top right of the page. The **Add Training Course** panel opens.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/training-settings-create-course.png" alt="Create Course panel with the Basic Information fields" width="1102" height="584" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Basic Information">
    Enter a **Course Title** and a **Category**. Both are required. Add a **Description** if you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Course Type">
    Select **SCORM Package**, **Video Course** or **Document**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Course Settings">
    Optionally set **Duration (minutes)**, **Passing Score (%)**, **Max Attempts** and **Expires After (days)**. Turn on **Make this course mandatory for all users** if it must be completed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the content">
    Use the input for your course type.

    | Course type | Content |
    |---|---|
    | **SCORM Package** | One `.zip` file. SCORM 1.2 or SCORM 2004. |
    | **Video Course** | Under **Video Source**, choose **Upload file** and pick `.mp4`, `.webm` or `.mov` files, or choose **YouTube URL** and add one or more links. |
    | **Document** | A `.pdf`, `.doc`, `.docx`, `.ppt` or `.pptx` file. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Course">
    The course is created and its content uploads. **Cancel** closes the form without saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the new course">
    Find it in the table, make sure its **Enabled** switch is on, and open **Preview Course** to check the content before you assign learners.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The create form has no company field. If exactly one company is selected in the company selector when you click **Create Course**, the form says the course is for the selected company only, and the course is scoped to that company. With no company or several companies selected, the course is created for your whole organization.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Switches or the bulk menu are missing or disabled | You need Training settings write access and active billing. |
| **Create Course** is disabled | You need the **Manage Training** permission. |
| The **Certificate branding** card has no **Save** button | You have read-only access. |
| A course is missing from the table | Check the company selector and clear the table filters. |
| A learner cannot launch an enabled course | Check Premium access for the company and user, training permissions and the learner's assignment. |
| A toast says the content upload failed | The course record already exists. Find it in the table and upload its content again instead of creating a duplicate. |
| **Settings > Training** is missing | Your organization uses the direct Bigger Brains experience. Open **Training** from the main menu. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use client branding for only some companies?">
    No. Certificate branding is one organization-wide choice. With **Client branding**, each certificate uses its learner's company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does enabling a course enroll everyone?">
    No. Availability and enrollment are separate. Use learning paths or enrollment to assign learners.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do the Enabled switches wait for a Save click?">
    No. They save immediately for the company scope you selected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Training settings access alone create courses?">
    No. **Create Course** needs the **Manage Training** permission and active billing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a course exist after its upload fails?">
    Yes. The course is created first and its content uploads second. Find the course before you try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the company selector make a new course private to one company?">
    No. The create form does not use it. Review the course's availability after you create it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does disabling a course remove existing enrollments?">
    No, but do not rely on it as a cleanup step. Check the learner's actual access.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
