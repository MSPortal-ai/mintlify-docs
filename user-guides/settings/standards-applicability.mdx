**Audience:** MSP staff who manage the Standards catalog. Company platform
changes require Planner write access. Editing catalog rules requires Standards
settings management; selecting company fields also requires custom-fields read
access. Saving requires active billing.

Applicability answers “should this client have this standard?” It does not change
whether a tool is installed or whether a deployment has been completed.

## Match a standard to a device platform

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the company's platform">
    Open **MSP Tools > Standards**, expand **Company device platform**, and choose
    the company. Select **Only PCs**, **Only Macs**, or **PCs and Macs**, then
    select **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the standard's platform">
    Open **Settings > MSP Tools > Standards**, expand the category, and select
    the standard's name to open its editor. Under **Standards applicability**,
    choose its **Device platform** and select **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the matrix">
    Return to Standards and select the company. A Mac-only standard is N/A for a
    PC-only company, and a PC-only standard is N/A for a Mac-only company. Choosing
    **PCs and Macs** on either side permits both platforms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Rule-driven N/A cells cannot be edited and do not count as compliance gaps.
Their previous deployment answers are retained. If a later rule change makes a
standard applicable again, its saved answer becomes visible again.

## Match a standard to company field values

Use a company field with selectable options—for example, a service profile with
different client needs.

1. Open the standard's editor in **Settings > MSP Tools > Standards** and find
   **Standards applicability**.
2. Choose its **Company field**.
3. Under **Applies when the company has any of these values**, select one or more
   accepted values.
4. Select **Save**. Check a company with a matching value and one with a different
   value in the Standards matrix.


<Frame caption="Select the accepted company-field values.">
  <img src="/images/product/standards-company-field.png" alt="Managed Endpoint Protection applies to the Managed service company field value." width="3840" height="2160" />
</Frame>

A multiselect needs only one matching accepted value. A company with no value
keeps the standard applicable, so incomplete records cannot silently improve its
compliance percentage. Complete the company field to apply the intended rule.

Choose **All companies** and save to remove the field condition. If a field is
selected with no accepted values, Save is disabled. Changing the field clears
the previously selected values; choose values for the new field before saving.

## Understand scoring and service plans

Platform and company-field exclusions remove inapplicable checks from the
Standards compliance calculation and the corresponding Client Health scoring.
Client Health can reflect an earlier scoring run; compare scope and refresh time.

Service-plan membership is a separate setting in each standard's editor. An empty
plan selection means all plans. A standard outside a company's target plan is
de-emphasized with an explanatory dot, but plan membership currently does not
reduce the compliance denominator.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check |
| --- | --- |
| The company is missing from the picker | Check the app's company selector and your permitted company scope. |
| The field is missing | Use a company field with selectable options and check custom-fields read access. |
| A company with no field value still has the standard | This is intentional. Complete the field to apply the condition. |
| A cell is locked N/A | Review its device platform and company-field rule. Changing a deployment status cannot override the rule. |
| A higher score appears without new deployments | Excluding a required check changes the number of applicable checks. Review the exclusions alongside the percentage. |
