**Audience:** MSP staff who manage the Standards catalog. Editing a standard's conditions requires
Standards settings management; company field conditions also require
custom-fields read access. Saving requires active billing.

Applicability answers “should this client have this standard?” It does not change
whether a tool is installed or whether a deployment has been completed.

## Use inventory to choose applicable companies

Company computer platforms come from active device inventory. There is no manual
company-wide PC/Mac override. Configure the conditions on each standard instead.

- **Company computer platforms has any of PCs** includes PC-only and mixed companies.
- **Company computer platforms has any of Macs** includes Mac-only and mixed companies.
- To require both kinds, add separate PC and Mac conditions with **all conditions match**.
- **Company device types** checks whether the company has active devices of selected
  types, such as workstations, servers or network devices.
- **has none of** matches when none of the selected platforms or types are present,
  including when the company has no active inventory.

Computer platform conditions ignore Windows servers and inactive devices. A company
with no active computers does not match **has any of PCs** or **has any of Macs**.

Open **Settings > MSP Tools > Standards > Company Standards applicability** to
inspect company facts. Platform and device-type summaries are read-only. Industry
and editable company fields can still be changed with the corresponding permissions.
Use the app's company selector and table filters to narrow the companies shown.

## Add conditions to a standard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the standard">
    Open **Settings > MSP Tools > Standards**, expand the category, and select the
    standard's name to open its editor. Find the **Standards applicability** row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a condition">
    Select **Add condition**. Choose what to test, a comparison such as **is any of**,
    and the values. A new standard applies to every company until you add one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Combine conditions">
    With two or more conditions, choose whether the standard applies when
    **all conditions match** or when **any condition matches**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the matrix">
    A complete rule saves on its own a moment after your last change; there is no
    Save button. Return to Standards. Companies that don't match show the standard
    as a locked N/A.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Condition | Tests |
| --- | --- |
| **Company computer platforms** | Active PCs and Macs belonging to the company. Use **has any of** or **has none of**. |
| **Company device types** | Active devices of selected types belonging to the company. Use **has any of** or **has none of**. |
| **Industry** | The company's industry. |
| **Company type** | The company's PSA company type. |
| **Standards tier** | The tier mapped to the company's type. |
| **Company fields** | Any company custom field. Option fields use **is any of** or **is none of**. Text fields support **contains**, and number and date fields support greater, less, before and after comparisons. |

Existing saved platform and company-field rules remain available. Platform results
now follow active inventory: companies with no active computers can become N/A
where the earlier automatic fallback treated them as having PCs and Macs.

Rule-driven N/A cells cannot be edited and do not count as compliance gaps.
Their previous deployment answers are retained. If a later rule change makes a
standard applicable again, its saved answer becomes visible again.

## Missing values

For metadata conditions such as Industry or company fields, a company with no value
keeps the standard, so incomplete records
cannot silently improve its compliance percentage. To target companies with no
value, use **is empty** or **is not empty**. For example, **Industry is not empty**
excludes companies whose industry was never set.

Inventory conditions use presence instead: an empty inventory fails **has any of**
and satisfies **has none of**. A dash in a branch-preview summary does not establish
that the company has no devices.

Compliance checks use the same conditions, plus device conditions. See
[Compliance Settings](/user-guides/settings/compliance#choosing-which-companies-and-devices-get-a-check).

## Understand scoring and service plans

Condition exclusions remove inapplicable checks from the
Standards compliance calculation and the corresponding Client Health scoring.
Client Health can reflect an earlier scoring run; compare scope and refresh time.

Service-plan membership is a separate setting in each standard's editor. An empty
plan selection means all plans. A standard outside a company's target plan is
de-emphasized with an explanatory dot, but plan membership currently does not
reduce the compliance denominator.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check |
| --- | --- |
| The company is missing from the table | Check the app's company selector and your permitted company scope. |
| A company field is missing | Check custom-fields read access. User fields cannot be used in conditions. |
| A company with no value still has the standard | This is intentional. Complete the value, or use **is empty** or **is not empty**. |
| A cell is locked N/A | Review the standard's **Standards applicability** conditions. Changing a deployment status cannot override them. |
| The rule does not save | Finish or remove incomplete conditions, such as **is any of** with no values. The editor reminds you that the rule saves automatically once every condition is complete. |
| A higher score appears without new deployments | Excluding a required check changes the number of applicable checks. Review the exclusions alongside the percentage. |
