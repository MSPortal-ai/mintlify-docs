Billing settings show your MSPortal subscription, company usage, payment method, add-on charges and invoices. Payment and plan changes happen in a secure hosted billing portal that opens from this page. This page is for MSP staff who manage the account.

## Before you start

- Primary admins can always open **Settings > Administration > Billing**. Other staff need a role with manage access to billing settings.
- If your account is in read-only mode because of a billing problem, the Billing page stays open so you can fix it.
- Company users cannot see this page.

## Review your subscription and usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Settings > Administration > Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Usage Summary">
    The **Usage Summary** card shows **Billed monthly** or **Billed annually** and these numbers.

    | Number | What it means |
    |--------|---------------|
    | **Current Companies** | Companies in your account now |
    | **Billed Companies** | Monthly plans only. The highest company count this billing period |
    | **Contracted Companies** | Annual plans only. The company quantity on your plan |
    | **Days Until Billing** / **Days Until Renewal** | Days left in the current period |
    | **Trial Days Left** | Shown instead during a free trial |
    | **Bigger Brains Users** and **Billed BB Users** | Shown when Bigger Brains training is enabled for users |

    A **Pending sync** badge or a **Usage sync pending** note means your current company count differs from the billed count. Click **Sync billing status** at the top of the page. On an annual plan, **Company count exceeds contracted quantity** means you have more companies than your plan covers. Contact support to add companies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Subscription card">
    During a trial, the card shows **Access through** and the days remaining. On a paid plan it shows the **Next billing date** (or **Next renewal date** on an annual plan) and the **Estimated invoice** (or **Renewal amount**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the payment method">
    The **Payment method** card shows your default method, its expiry, and how many other methods are on file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you see **Billing Information Unavailable**, MSPortal could not reach the billing service. Click **Retry**. Missing numbers in that state do not mean you have no subscription.

## Set up billing or change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Settings > Administration > Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the matching button">
    Pick the button that fits your situation. Each one opens the hosted billing portal.

    | Button | When it shows |
    |--------|---------------|
    | **Choose a plan** | You have no active subscription |
    | **Set up billing** | No payment method is configured |
    | **Add payment method** | You have a subscription or trial but no payment method on file |
    | **Set up payment** | A subscription exists but is not active yet |
    | **Manage subscription** | You have an active subscription |
    | **Manage payment methods** | A payment method is on file |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the change in the portal">
    Review the details, change the plan or payment method, and confirm in the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to MSPortal and sync">
    Go back to **Settings > Administration > Billing** and click **Sync billing status**. A toast confirms **Billing status updated**. If it says the sync is still running, check again in a moment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Billing page with the Choose a plan and Set up billing buttons](/images/settings/settings-billing.png)

The portal confirms every plan or payment change. The Billing page has no Save button. If a window closes before you see a confirmation, reopen the portal and check the current state before you repeat the change.

## Download invoices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scroll to Billing history">
    The **Billing history** table lists **Invoice**, **Billing date**, **Status** and **Amount**. Status is **Paid**, **Open**, **Draft**, **Void** or **Uncollectible**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open or download an invoice">
    Click **View** to open the invoice, or **Download** to get the PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See older invoices">
    Click **View all invoices** to open the full history in the billing portal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Bigger Brains training charges appear in **Add-on Services** and are included in the estimated invoice.

## Restore access after a billing problem

When your subscription status is **Past due**, **Unpaid**, **Canceled** or **Incomplete**, your MSP team goes into read-only mode. Staff can view data but cannot create, edit or delete it. Your client users are not affected.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    A primary admin, or a user with billing access, opens **Settings > Administration > Billing**. This page stays available in read-only mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix the payment">
    Click **Manage payment methods** (or **Add payment method** or **Manage subscription**). Update the card or pay the outstanding invoice in the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync billing status">
    Return to Billing and click **Sync billing status**. Full access comes back once MSPortal sees an active subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| **Billing Information Unavailable** | Click **Retry**. If it keeps failing, contact support |
| **Billing account disconnected** | The billing account linked to your organization was deleted. Click **Contact support** to reconnect billing. Old invoices still show |
| The billed count looks stale | Click **Sync billing status** |
| The portal does not open | Try again. A toast says **Couldn't open the billing portal** if it fails |
| Staff see a read-only message | Your billing status is failed. Fix the payment as above |
| Invoice and usage numbers differ | Compare the billing period and the monthly high mark. Usage counts are not the invoice total |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I treat Current Companies as my invoice total?">
    No. Monthly plans bill the highest company count in the period, plus add-ons. Annual plans bill the contracted quantity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can my clients still work when my team cannot?">
    Billing restrictions apply only to MSP staff. Company users are never restricted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I change my billing email or business details?">
    See [Tenant settings](/user-guides/settings/tenant).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Bigger Brains Training missing from Add-on Services?">
    The card only shows when training is enabled for at least one user.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    Open **Manage subscription** to see what the portal offers for your account, or contact support at support@msportal.ai.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
