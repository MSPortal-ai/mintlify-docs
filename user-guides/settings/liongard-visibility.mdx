Use this page to choose which Liongard inspector types and systems appear on the **Documentation** page, and which Company permission groups can see them. It is for MSP staff.

Liongard content is hidden until you share it. With no rules, neither your team nor your clients see any Liongard content on the Documentation page.

## Before you start

- Your role needs access to Documentation settings (**manage_settings_documentation**).
- The Liongard integration must be active. The **Liongard** tab does not appear without it. See [Configure Liongard Integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-liongard-integration).
- Liongard must have imported its inspectors and systems. A system also needs to be linked to a company and not ignored.
- Company permission groups must exist. If the page shows **No company permission groups found**, create them in Companies settings first.

These rules work within each viewer's own tenant and company scope. They do not give a Company user access to other companies, set up compliance metric mappings, or change data in Liongard.

## Share an inspector type

Sharing an inspector type shares every eligible system of that type. Check how many systems it covers before you pick a broad group.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Documentation settings">
    Go to **Settings > Documentation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Liongard tab">
    Click **Liongard** in the tabs at the top. The page has two sections, **Inspectors** and **Individual Inspector Systems**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the inspector">
    In **Inspectors**, type in **Search inspectors...** to find the type. Search only changes which rows you see. Edits on rows hidden by your search are still saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose permission groups">
    On the inspector's row, click **Add groups** and tick the Company permission groups that should see it. Tick **All Permission Groups** to match every group (it shows as an **All Groups** chip). Leave the row empty to share it with no group. Click the **x** on a chip to remove it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Click **Save** in the **Inspectors** section. Chip changes are drafts until you save. A message reads **Visibility rules saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share an individual system

Use this when you want to expose one system, such as a single domain controller, without sharing the rest of its inspector type.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for the system">
    In **Individual Inspector Systems**, type a name in **Search systems by name...** and press Enter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the right system">
    Check the company shown next to each result. Click the **+** button on the system you want. It moves into the list below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose permission groups">
    On the system's row, click **Add groups** and tick the groups that should see it. A system with no group chosen is not shared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Click **Save** in **Individual Inspector Systems**. This section saves on its own, apart from **Inspectors**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove the type rule">
    In **Inspectors**, remove the groups from the inspector's row and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the system rule">
    In **Individual Inspector Systems**, remove the groups from the system's row and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check both">
    A system stays visible while either its inspector type rule or its own rule still grants access. Review both sections.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check who can see it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reopen Documentation">
    Go to **Documentation** and open the Liongard inspectors tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a Company user">
    A Company user sees an item only when a rule exists for its inspector type or system and the rule includes one of the user's groups (or **All Groups**). Check with an account in the group you shared with.
  </Step>
</Steps>

MSP staff need a rule to exist, but they skip the group match. If staff can see an item, that does not prove a client can.

**All Groups** removes the group match for that rule. It does not give anyone access to other companies.

The **Internal user access** selector above the tabs is a separate control. It saves the moment you change it, and it does not replace Liongard sharing rules. Its **Show all documentation** and **Show only shared documentation** options cover your team's view of documents, assets, configurations, and procedures.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **Liongard** tab is missing | Connect Liongard first. The tab shows only for an active integration. |
| An inspector is missing from the **Inspectors** list | Check that Liongard has synced and that the inspector is in use in your tenant. |
| A system is missing from the Documentation page | Confirm a type or system rule exists, the system is linked to a company, it is not ignored, and its device type is not set to auto-sync as a device. Then check the viewer's company scope. See [Integration Company Mapping](/user-guides/settings/integration-company-mapping). |
| A Company user cannot see an item staff can see | Check the user's permission groups against the rule. Add the right group instead of switching every rule to **All Groups**. |
| A system is still visible after you removed its rule | Its inspector type rule still shares it. Remove that rule too. |
| Saving fails with "This item changed in another session" | Another admin changed the rules. Reopen the page, review the saved rules, and make your change again. |
| **Save** is greyed out | There are no unsaved changes in that section. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does leaving the permission groups blank share the inspector with everyone?">
    No. An empty selection shares it with no one. Tick **All Permission Groups** if you want every group to match, then click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do group changes save right away?">
    No. Changes in **Inspectors** and **Individual Inspector Systems** are drafts until you click that section's **Save**. The **Internal user access** selector is the exception and saves immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does saving Inspectors also save Individual Inspector Systems?">
    No. Each section has its own **Save**. Save both when your change touches both.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does search limit what Save applies to?">
    No. Edits on rows hidden by search are saved too. Clear the search to review everything before you save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does All Groups show data across companies?">
    No. It only removes the group match. Each viewer still sees only systems in their own company scope.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Liongard sharing change anything in Liongard?">
    No. These rules only control what MSPortal shows.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
