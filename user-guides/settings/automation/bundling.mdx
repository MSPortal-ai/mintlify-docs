title: 'Bundling Automation Matches'
description: 'Group matching records by company and period so one rule creates one ticket, Planner item or digest per group instead of one per record.'

---

Bundling turns a rule that would act once per matching record into a rule that acts once per group. Forty devices with expiring warranties at one company become one ticket, one Planner item or one email that lists all forty. This page is for MSP staff who build automation rules.

## Before you start

- You need the **Automation Rules** write or manage permission to create or edit rules. Without it, you cannot create rules, and existing rules open in **View Only Mode**.
- Bundling is available on rules with a **Date Threshold** or **Scheduled** trigger. The checkbox does not appear for other triggers.
- Pick the actions first if you can. Bundling changes what **Open Ticket**, **Add to Planner**, **Send Email**, **Send Survey** and **In-App Alert** do (see the table below).

## Turn on bundling for a rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the rule">
    Go to **Settings > Platform > Automation Rules**. Click **Add Rule** for a new rule, or open the row menu (**...**) on an existing rule and choose **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the entity and trigger">
    Under **Trigger Configuration**, pick the **Entity** and a **Trigger** (**Date Threshold** or **Scheduled**). Fill in the trigger settings. For **Date Threshold**, choose the **Date Field**, **Days**, **Direction** and **Mode**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Combine matching records">
    Below the trigger settings, check **Combine matching records**. A **Bundle period** list appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the bundle period">
    Pick one of these options.

    | Option | One group per |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Per company** | Company |
    | **Per company and day** | Company and day of the record date |
    | **Per company and week** | Company and week of the record date |
    | **By month** | Company and month of the record date |
    | **By quarter** | Company and quarter of the record date |

    The day, week, month and quarter options only appear when the entity has a date field. A **Date Threshold** rule starts on **By month**. A **Scheduled** rule starts on **Per company**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the date field (Scheduled rules only)">
    On a **Scheduled** rule with a period other than **Per company**, a **Date Field** list appears. Choose the date that decides which period each record falls into. A **Date Threshold** rule uses its own **Date Field** automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the actions">
    Under **Actions**, click **Add** and choose the actions. In the text boxes, use the bundle variables described below. Click **Template Variables** to see them and click one to insert it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the rule">
    At the bottom of the **Setup** step, click **Run Test** in the **Test Rule** section to see the matching records. If the rule has an **Add to Planner** action, click **Preview bundles** to see the Planner items the rule would create, with their company group, period and due dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Next**, choose whether to turn on **Enable this rule**, then click **Create Rule** (or **Save Changes** on an existing rule). New rules start inactive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the first run">
    Open **Execution Log** from the Automation Rules page and look at the run. Confirm the number of tickets, Planner items or emails matches the number of groups you expected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Company grouping keeps clients separate. Period grouping uses the record date. Devices expiring in April and devices expiring in May at the same company form two groups, even if the rule runs on one day.

## What bundling does to each action

| Action | Bundled behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Open Ticket** | Creates one ticket per group, using the PSA settings in the action |
| **Add to Planner** | Creates one Planner item per group. The rule can keep that item current on later runs (see below) |
| **Send Email**, **Send Survey**, **In-App Alert** | Sends one message per recipient. Each recipient's message lists only the records that recipient is allowed to see, so counts can differ between recipients |
| **AI Transform** | Runs with the group as its context for the actions after it |
| **Update Field**, **Set Relative Date**, **Add team members**, **Remove team members** | Still run once for each matched record |

One bundled rule does not always mean one action in total. Companies, periods, recipients and action types each add to the count.

## Write templates for a group

Use these variables in titles, descriptions, subjects and bodies.

| Variable | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `{{entity_count}}` | Number of records in the group |
| `{{entity_list}}` | One line per record with its name and date, for example the device name followed by `expires 2026-04-05` |
| `{{group_period}}` | The period label, for example `April 2026` or `Q2 2026`. It is blank when the period is **Per company** |

Single-record variables such as `{{entity.serial_number}}` do not describe a group. `{{entity.company_name}}` still works.

Example title:

```text
Warranty review: {{entity_count}} devices expiring {{group_period}}
```

Example body:

```text
Review the following devices for renewal or replacement:

{{entity_list}}
```

## Planner dates and refreshes

The **Add to Planner** action offers **Task Title**, **Description**, **Planner Type**, **Business Value** and **Due Offset (days)**. It also has two switches for bundles.

- **Only create future-dated items** skips a Planner item when its calculated due date is today or earlier.
- **Use current period for past dates** puts the item in the current period when its due date has passed. Turning one switch on turns the other off.

For a bundled Planner item, the due date comes from the record date plus the trigger offset plus **Due Offset (days)**. It is not the day the rule runs. Click **Preview bundles** to see the calculated dates.

A rule keeps its Planner items current when every action in it can be bundled and one of them is **Add to Planner**. On each run, the rule updates the existing item for a group instead of creating a new one. It can replace the generated title, description, dates and Planner type, so do not put your own notes in those fields. When a run finishes without failures and matched every record, the rule archives Planner items for groups that no longer match.

Imported rule sets use this behavior. **Quarterly warranty planning** keeps one Planner item per company and warranty-expiry quarter. See [Example rules](/user-guides/settings/automation/examples).

## Failures and retries

If a group fails, for example because your PSA is briefly unavailable, the rule does not mark that group's records as processed. The next run matches them again and retries only the failed groups. Groups that succeeded are left alone.

A run can fail partway through. Earlier actions may already have sent emails or opened tickets. Check the results in **Execution Log** before you run the rule again.

A bundled rule reads up to 5,000 matching records per run. If more records match, a rule that keeps Planner items current stops with an error instead of syncing partial groups. Narrow the rule with **Scope** or **Conditions** and run it again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Combine matching records** is missing | Change the **Trigger** to **Date Threshold** or **Scheduled**. Other triggers cannot bundle |
| The **By month** and **By quarter** options are missing | The entity has no date field to group by. Choose **Per company** |
| **Preview bundles** is missing | Add an **Add to Planner** action. The button only shows for rules with one |
| A Scheduled rule will not save with a period | Choose the **Date Field** that appears next to **Bundle period** |
| The preview shows fewer records than **Run Test** | The preview uses a sample of the matches. The message at the top of the dialog says how many records it used |
| A Planner item disappeared from the active list | A completed refresh archives items for groups that no longer match. Check archived Planner items, the rule's **Scope** and its **Conditions** |
| Two recipients got different lists | Digests contain only the records each recipient is allowed to see |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I bundle a Scheduled rule?">
    Yes. Check **Combine matching records**, then choose **Per company** or a period and its **Date Field**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are only tickets and Planner items bundled?">
    No. Emails, surveys and in-app alerts are also bundled, per recipient. Field and date updates still run once per record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will each run create a new Planner item?">
    No, when the rule keeps its Planner items current. The rule updates the existing item for each group. Click **Preview bundles** to see what it will do.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit a Planner description that a rule created?">
    You can, but the next refresh can replace it. Keep your own notes elsewhere, or change the rule's **Description** template.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a failed bundle mean nothing happened?">
    No. Earlier actions in the run may have succeeded. Open the run in **Execution Log** to see which actions finished.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I group by location or device type?">
    No. Groups are always by company, and optionally by day, week, month or quarter of a date field.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
