Each example lists the settings exactly as they appear in the rule editor. Build one by following the steps, then change the names, templates and recipients to match your business. This page is for MSP staff who build automation rules.

## Before you start

- You need the **Automation Rules** write or manage permission. See [Rule wizard walkthrough](/user-guides/settings/automation/wizard) for every field.
- For every example, open **Settings > Platform > Automation Rules** and click **Add Rule**.
- Keep **Enable this rule** off on the **Review** step until **Run Test** shows the records you expect. Start with **Specific Companies** scope if you want to try a rule on one client first.

## Pick a pattern

| Pattern | Trigger | When to use |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Date-driven** | Date Threshold | Something should happen N days before or after a date, such as warranty end, meeting start, training due date or renewal date |
| **State-driven** | Scheduled plus Conditions | Records sitting in a state need action, such as resolved tickets, overdue goals or controls past due |

## Import a built-in rule set

MSPortal includes ready-made rule sets. Importing one copies it into your rules as a disabled rule that you can review and change.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Automation Rules">
    Go to **Settings > Platform > Automation Rules**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Import">
    Click **Import** in the page header. The **Import automation** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a rule set">
    Under **Choose a rule set**, pick one of the built-in rule sets.

    | Rule set | What it does | What you choose |
    |----------|--------------|-----------------|
    | **Survey response email** | Emails the members of a company role when a survey gets a response | A **Survey** (or **All surveys**) and a **Team role** |
    | **Quarterly warranty planning** | Keeps one Planner item per company and warranty-expiry quarter, updated as devices change | A **Planner item type** |
    | **SharePoint capacity planning** | Creates a Planner item when CIPP reports SharePoint storage over quota. Needs an active CIPP integration and mapped tenants | A **Planner item type** |
    | **Annual license renewal notice** | Emails each client's PSA contacts before annual license subscriptions renew. See [example 4](#4-annual-license-renewal-notice) | The **Contact types** to email |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the choices">
    Pick the values the rule set asks for. If a list is empty, use the link under it to create the survey, role or Planner type first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Import rule set">
    The rule is added to your list as a disabled rule. Open it, check the settings and templates, then turn it on when you are ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Import automation dialog with the Annual license renewal notice rule set and its contact types](/images/automation/license-renewal-import.png)

If a rule with the same name already exists, rename or delete it before you import again.

## Date-driven examples

### 1. Warranty expiration alert

Email the account manager 30 days before a device's warranty ends, and create a Planner task for the tech team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Basic Information">
    - **Rule Name**: Warranty 30-Day Alert
    - **Priority**: 50
    - **Scope**: All Companies
    - **Description**: Notify AM and open a Planner task 30 days before device warranty ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trigger Configuration">
    - **Entity**: Device
    - **Trigger**: Date Threshold
    - **Date Field**: Warranty End Date
    - **Days**: 30
    - **Direction**: Upcoming
    - **Mode**: Exactly 30 days

    The rule acts once for each device on the day its warranty end date is 30 days away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conditions">
    Click **Add**, then **+ Add condition**:

    - **Field**: Is Active, **Operator**: is true

    This skips retired devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action 1: Send Email">
    - **Send To**: Role Members, then pick the Account Manager role
    - **Subject**: `Warranty expiring: {{entity.name}} at {{entity.company_name}}`
    - **Body**:
      ```
      Device {{entity.name}} (Serial: {{entity.serial_number}}) at {{entity.company_name}}
      has a warranty ending on {{entity.warranty_end}}. Please reach out to the client
      to discuss renewal or replacement.
      ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action 2: Add to Planner">
    - **Task Title**: `Warranty renewal: {{entity.name}} ({{entity.company_name}})`
    - **Business Value**: Medium
    - **Due Offset (days)**: 30

    The Planner item is due 30 days after the rule runs, which is the warranty end date. It starts in **Not Started**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and save">
    Click **Run Test**, check the matching devices, click **Next**, then click **Create Rule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Want 60-day and 7-day reminders too? Click **Duplicate** in the row menu, open the copy, and change **Days** to 60 or 7. Each rule runs on its own.
</Tip>

### 2. Meeting agenda generation

One day before a quarterly business review, use AI to draft an agenda from the client's tickets, compliance status and goals, then send it to the attendees for approval.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Basic Information">
    - **Rule Name**: QBR Agenda Draft
    - **Scope**: All Companies
    - **Description**: AI-drafted agenda sent to QBR attendees 1 day before the meeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trigger Configuration">
    - **Entity**: Meeting
    - **Trigger**: Date Threshold
    - **Date Field**: Start Time
    - **Days**: 1
    - **Direction**: Upcoming
    - **Mode**: Exactly (the option reads **Exactly 1 days**)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conditions">
    Add one group with two conditions and leave the group on **AND**:

    - **Event Type** equals **Quarterly Business Review**
    - **Status** equals **Scheduled**

    This skips canceled and completed meetings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action 1: AI Transform">
    - **AI Template**: pick a meeting agenda template. For Meeting rules the list shows meeting templates.

    The result is stored in `{{ai_output}}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action 2: Send Email">
    - **Send To**: Meeting Attendees
    - **Subject**: `Agenda for tomorrow's QBR: {{entity.title}}`
    - **Body**:
      ```
      Hi team,

      Here is the proposed agenda for tomorrow's QBR with {{entity.company_name}}:

      {{ai_output}}

      See you in the meeting.
      ```

    Meeting emails always require approval, so the email waits for an approver in the **Pending Approvals** tab on the **Notifications** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and save">
    Click **Run Test**, click **Next**, then click **Create Rule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 3. Training due reminder

Remind each user 7 days before assigned training is due.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger Configuration">
    - **Entity**: Training
    - **Trigger**: Date Threshold
    - **Date Field**: Due Date
    - **Days**: 7
    - **Direction**: Upcoming
    - **Mode**: Exactly 7 days
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conditions">
    - **Status** is one of **Not Started**, **In Progress**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action: In-App Alert">
    - **Alert Type**: Warning
    - **Send To**: Assigned user (the user the training is assigned to)
    - **Title**: `Training due in 7 days: {{entity.name}}`
    - **Message**: `Please complete "{{entity.name}}" by {{entity.due_date}}.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name, test and save">
    Enter a **Rule Name** such as Training Due Reminder, click **Run Test**, click **Next**, then click **Create Rule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 4. Annual license renewal notice

Tell each client which annual subscriptions renew soon, while there is still time to change seat counts or cancel. The fastest way is the built-in rule set.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the import dialog">
    On **Settings > Platform > Automation Rules**, click **Import**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the rule set">
    Choose **Annual license renewal notice**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the contact types">
    Under **Contact types**, choose the PSA contact types to email. Billing and primary are selected by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import and review">
    Click **Import rule set**. The rule is imported disabled. Open it, read the email and turn it on when you are ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To build the same rule by hand, use these settings.

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Entity** | License Subscription (one row per Pax8, Sherweb, Adobe or Microsoft 365 subscription for a mapped company) |
| **Trigger** | Date Threshold, **Date Field** Renewal Date, **Days** 60, **Direction** Upcoming, **Mode** Within 60 days |
| **Conditions** | **Annual or Longer Commitment** is true, and **Is Active** is true |
| **Combine matching records** | **Per company**, so each company gets one email that lists all of its renewals |
| **Action** | Send Email to **Client Contacts (PSA)** with the contact types you chose |
| **Subject** | `Upcoming license renewals for {{entity.company_name}}` |
| **Body** | Includes `{{entity_list}}`, which lists one line per subscription with its renewal date |

Useful fields for narrowing the rule:

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Vendor Source** | Pax8, Sherweb, Adobe or Microsoft 365 |
| **Annual or Longer Commitment** | True for 1-year and multi-year terms. Microsoft 365 does not report its term, so its subscriptions never match true |
| **Commitment Term** | The term as the vendor reports it, such as `1-Year` or `Monthly` |
| **Auto-Renews** | Reported by Pax8 (false when a cancellation is pending) and Adobe |
| **Client Unit Price** and **Client Total Price** | Your sell price to the client. Partner cost is never available to rules |

Each subscription is notified once for each renewal date. When the next term's renewal date syncs, the rule acts again the following year.

## State-driven examples

These use a **Scheduled** trigger. The rule checks on a repeating schedule and acts on records in a given state. Leave **Deduplication** on **Once per entity** so each record is handled once.

### 5. CSAT survey for resolved tickets

Check every hour for resolved tickets and send each ticket's contact a satisfaction survey.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Basic Information">
    - **Rule Name**: CSAT Survey on Resolved Tickets
    - **Scope**: All Companies
    - **Description**: Send the CSAT survey for tickets that reach Resolved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trigger Configuration">
    - **Entity**: Ticket
    - **Trigger**: Scheduled
    - **Frequency**: Every Hour
    - **Deduplication**: Once per entity

    **Time** and **Timezone** do not appear for hourly rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conditions">
    - **Status** equals **Resolved**

    Each hour the rule finds tickets currently in Resolved. **Once per entity** makes sure a ticket gets only one survey.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action: Send Survey">
    - **Send To**: Ticket Contact
    - **Survey**: pick your active CSAT survey
    - **Invite note** (optional): a short thank-you message

    If the **Survey** list is empty, create and activate a survey first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and save">
    Click **Run Test**, click **Next**, then click **Create Rule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The rule checks the current status, not how long a ticket has been Resolved. A ticket gets its survey at the first hourly check after it reaches Resolved.
</Note>

### 6. Stale goal nudge

Once a week, alert account managers about in-progress goals under 50% progress whose target date is more than 60 days past.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger Configuration">
    - **Entity**: Goal
    - **Trigger**: Scheduled
    - **Frequency**: Weekly
    - **Day of the week**: Monday
    - **Time**: 09:00, and pick your **Timezone**
    - **Deduplication**: Once per entity
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conditions">
    Add one group with three conditions and leave it on **AND**:

    - **Status** equals **In Progress** (pick the status your team uses for goals in progress)
    - **Progress (%)** is less than **50**
    - **Target Date** is older than (days) **60**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action: In-App Alert">
    - **Alert Type**: Warning
    - **Send To**: Role Members, then pick the Account Manager role
    - **Title**: `Goal stalled: {{entity.name}}`
    - **Message**: `{{entity.name}} at {{entity.company_name}} has a target date over 60 days past and is below 50% progress.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name, test and save">
    Enter a **Rule Name** such as Stale Goal Nudge, click **Run Test**, click **Next**, then click **Create Rule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each stale goal triggers the alert once.

### 7. Overdue compliance control

Every day at 7 AM, create a Planner task for each compliance control that is past due.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger Configuration">
    - **Entity**: Compliance
    - **Trigger**: Scheduled
    - **Frequency**: Daily
    - **Time**: 07:00, and pick your **Timezone**
    - **Deduplication**: Once per entity
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conditions">
    Add one group and leave it on **AND**:

    - **Status** is not one of the statuses that mean a control is finished or not applicable
    - **Due Date** is older than (days) **0**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action: Add to Planner">
    - **Task Title**: `Remediate: {{entity.name}} ({{entity.company_name}})`
    - **Business Value**: High
    - **Due Offset (days)**: 14
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name, test and save">
    Enter a **Rule Name** such as Overdue Control Remediation, click **Run Test**, click **Next**, then click **Create Rule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Once per entity** stops the rule from creating the same task again tomorrow.

### 8. Meeting notes follow-up

Every evening, alert chosen users about meetings that are waiting for notes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger Configuration">
    - **Entity**: Meeting
    - **Trigger**: Scheduled
    - **Frequency**: Daily
    - **Time**: 18:00, and pick your **Timezone**
    - **Deduplication**: Once per entity
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conditions">
    Add one group and leave it on **AND**:

    - **Status** equals **Pending Notes**
    - **Has Meeting Agenda Items** is true
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action: In-App Alert">
    - **Alert Type**: Info
    - **Send To**: Specific Users, then pick the people who should add notes
    - **Title**: `Add meeting notes: {{entity.title}}`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name, test and save">
    Enter a **Rule Name** such as Meeting Notes Follow-Up, click **Run Test**, click **Next**, then click **Create Rule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The alert goes to the users you pick. It does not pick each meeting's organizer. It also does not limit matches to meetings that ended today.

## Tips for building your own

1. **Start with the AI Rule Designer.** Click the floating **AI Rule Designer** button, describe the outcome ("remind the account manager 60 days before warranty ends") and review the suggested changes.
2. **Always test.** Click **Run Test** at the bottom of **Setup**. It shows which records match under current data. Fix conditions before you save.
3. **Choose deduplication on purpose.** **Once per entity** acts on each record once. **Every run** repeats the action on every run.
4. **Layer rules.** Use several focused rules (60-day warning, 30-day warning, 7-day escalation) instead of one complex rule.
5. **Duplicate before changing.** The **Duplicate** row action creates a disabled copy named **Copy of** followed by the rule name. Edit and test the copy, turn it on, then turn off or delete the old rule.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| The **Role Members** list has no Account Manager | Create the team role first, then assign people to it for each company |
| The **Survey** list is empty | Create a survey and make it active. Only active surveys appear |
| **Status** has no values to pick | Finish the related sync, then reopen the list. Ticket statuses come from your PSA |
| The rule matches nothing in **Run Test** | Loosen the conditions or the **Days** window. Check the **Scope** |
| An email did not arrive | Open **Execution Log** from the page header. Meeting emails and any email with **Require Approval** wait in the **Pending Approvals** tab on the **Notifications** page |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does an hourly schedule mean one hour after a ticket is resolved?">
    No. It means the rule checks every hour. A **Status equals Resolved** condition finds tickets that are Resolved at that moment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I email the person who opened a ticket?">
    Use **Ticket Contact** in **Send To** for Ticket rules. It uses the primary contact on the ticket. **Client Contacts (PSA)** uses the contact types on the company's PSA record, which can be a different person.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a rule send to the assigned user automatically?">
    Yes for Planner, Training and User rules. Choose **Assigned user** in **Send To**. Other entities need **Specific Users** or **Role Members**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I set a custom field to mark a record as done?">
    No. **Update Field** only changes a fixed list of fields, and not for tickets or compliance controls. Use **Once per entity** to keep a rule from acting on a record twice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I safely adapt an imported rule?">
    Keep it disabled while you change the scope, recipients and templates. Click **Run Test**, then turn it on.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
