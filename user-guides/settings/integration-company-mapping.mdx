Use this page to match each MSPortal company to the company record it has in every connected tool. One grid shows every company on the rows and every connected tool on the columns. It is for MSP staff.

Linking a record is different from importing a company. Linking connects a record to a company you already have in MSPortal. Importing creates a new MSPortal company from a record in your PSA.

## Before you start

- You need to be MSP staff. Company users ask their IT provider to correct integration links.
- Your role needs **Integration Company Mapping** access to open the page. Linking, unlinking, applying suggestions, and ignoring or restoring records need write access. Integration settings access (read or write) also opens the page. With read-only access you can see the grid but cannot change it.
- Connect at least one integration first. Each connected tool adds a column. A tool with several connections can have one column per connection, labeled with the connection name.

<Warning>
Links save as soon as you pick a record or click **Unlink**. There is no page-wide Save button, and leaving the page does not undo anything. A new link can start a follow-up sync, so check the company and the record before you choose.
</Warning>

## Open the mapping grid

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Integrations">
    Click **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Map companies">
    The button is in the page header. The page that opens is titled **Integration Company Mapping**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the grid">
    The first column, **Company**, lists your MSPortal companies and stays in view as you scroll sideways. Each tool has its own column. Each cell shows the linked record, **Not linked**, or **Nothing left to link** when the tool has no unlinked records. The last column is **Microsoft 365**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On a wide grid, use **Columns** to hide columns you do not need. Use the **Company** column filter to find a company by name.

## Link a company to a tool

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the company and the tool">
    Find the company row, then the column for the tool. If the tool has several connections, check the connection name in the column header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the cell">
    Click the cell. A picker opens with a search box labeled **Search integration companies...**. An empty cell lists only records that are not linked yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the record">
    Search for the record and click it. The link saves right away and a message confirms it. The cell now shows the record name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Some records show a note that they are already linked to another company. Check that note before you choose one.

## Remove or fix a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the linked cell">
    Click the cell that shows the wrong record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Unlink">
    Under **Current**, click **Unlink** next to the record name. The company in the other tool is not deleted or changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the right record">
    Open the cell again and pick the correct record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Picking a different record in a cell that already has a link adds a second link. It does not replace the first one. A cell with extra links shows a **+1** (or **+2**, and so on) button. Click it to open **Multiple integration links**, then click **Unlink** next to each record you do not want.

## Find companies with missing links

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Blank links">
    Click **Blank links** above the grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what is missing">
    Pick **Any integration** to see companies missing at least one link. Or pick one or more tools to see companies missing a link in every tool you picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the filter">
    Choose **Clear filters** in the same menu to see every company again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use the company selector

The page follows the Company Selector in the top bar. With one company or an assigned set of companies selected, the grid shows only those companies. In Global View it shows every company. Changing the selector clears your **Blank links** choices.

## Review likely matches

**Review likely matches** looks at the unlinked records in every connected tool and suggests an MSPortal company for each. Nothing saves until you apply it. The button shows only if you have write access, and it needs at least one connected tool.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Review likely matches">
    The **Company mapping cleanup** card sits above the grid. Click **Review likely matches** in its **Recommended matches** section. If nothing is found, a message says no new matches were found.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the confidence slider">
    The slider is labeled **Auto-select by confidence**. It runs from 70 to 100 percent in steps of 5 and starts at 70. Suggestions at or above the slider value are checked for you. Moving the slider replaces your checks, so set it before you change individual rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each suggestion">
    Each row shows the tool record, the MSPortal company it would link to, and a confidence score. Click a row to check or uncheck it. Names that look alike can belong to different customers, so read every row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list if needed">
    Type in the search box to filter by tool record or company name. With a search active, the buttons change to **Select shown** and **Clear shown** and act on the rows you can see. Checked rows that the search hides stay checked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click **Apply** (the button shows the count, such as **Apply 12 Matches**). Every checked row is linked, including rows hidden by your search. Clear the search first to review the full selection. A message reports how many companies were linked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each link is saved on its own. If a message reports errors, some links may still have saved. Check the grid before you try again. Click **Cancel** to close the dialog without linking anything.

<Note>
The suggestions can include records from every connected tool, not only the companies shown in the grid. The Company Selector and **Blank links** do not limit them. Review every row before you apply.
</Note>

## Review unlinked records

Records that exist in your tools but are not linked to a company are listed in **Unlinked customer records**. Records that look like the same customer are grouped into one row, even when they come from different tools.

The **Company mapping cleanup** card appears above the grid while some records need attention or you have ignored groups. It shows the number of groups to review and, when you have ignored any, an **Ignored groups** count. When everything is linked, ignored groups still keep the card visible, and **Review unlinked records** opens on the **Ignored groups** tab so you can restore them. The card is hidden only when no records are unlinked and none are ignored.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Review unlinked records">
    The button is on the **Company mapping cleanup** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a tab">
    **To review** lists groups that need a decision. **Ignored groups** lists groups you set aside.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Use the search box (**Search companies or IDs...**), the integration filter, and the company type filter. Company types come from your PSA, so you can work on customers and skip vendors or prospects. A company type filter keeps the whole group visible, including records from other tools. Click **Clear Filters** to reset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a group to a company">
    In the group row, open **Link to company…** and pick an existing MSPortal company. For a group with several records, the picker reads **Link all 3 records to company…** (with the real count) and links them all. To link one record, click the arrow on the right of the row to expand the group, then use **Link to company…** on that record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ignore a group that should never be linked">
    For an internal test account or a vendor record, click **Ignore from cleanup**. Every record in the group leaves the **To review** list. Nothing is deleted in the other tool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore a group if you change your mind">
    Open **Ignored groups** and click **Restore**. The group returns to **To review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the dialog">
    Click **Close**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To handle several groups at once, tick their checkboxes. A bulk **Ignore from cleanup** (or **Restore** on the **Ignored groups** tab) button appears. The checkbox at the top of the list selects every group in the current view. Changing the search or a filter clears your selection.

Linking a record from **Ignored groups** restores it first and then links it. If you unlink it later, it returns to **To review**. Wait for the list to refresh before you link the next group. If a group link fails part way, the records that linked leave the list. Review what remains before you try again.

<Tip>
The **Your Companies** dashboard shows a banner titled **Likely customer matches to review** (or **Companies need mapping** when there are no suggestions yet). Its **Review likely matches** button opens this page. With suggestions available and write access, the likely matches dialog opens on its own.
</Tip>

## Import companies from your PSA

If you have write access and an active Halo, ConnectWise, Syncro, ConnectWise Asio, or Autotask connection, an **Import** menu appears above the grid. Pick **Import from** and the tool name to start an import. See [Import Companies](/user-guides/integrations/import-companies) for the full steps.

## Connect Microsoft 365 for a company

The **Microsoft 365** column tracks a cloud connection for each company. It is not a normal record match. It is hidden when you open the page for a single tool.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Microsoft 365 cell">
    Click the cell in the company row. It reads **Not connected**, the connected domain, or **Not applicable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to connect">
    Click **Sign in with Microsoft** to sign in now. If the company is already connected, the same item reads **Add Tenant**. Click **Copy link** to copy a connection link you can send to the client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the prompt">
    Read any compliance prompt that appears before you continue. Sign-in opens in a pop-up window, so allow pop-ups for MSPortal. The cell updates when sign-in finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a company that is not on Microsoft 365, open the cell and click **Mark as not on Microsoft 365**. The cell then reads **Not applicable** and the company stops counting as missing a Microsoft 365 connection. Open the cell and click **Restore** to undo it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The grid is empty | Check the Company Selector, the **Company** column filter, and **Blank links**. Click **Clear filters** in the **Blank links** menu. |
| A tool has no column | Confirm the tool is connected on **Settings > Integrations**. If you opened the page for one tool only, click **View all providers**. |
| The cell says **Nothing left to link** | The tool has no unlinked records. Run a sync from the integration settings if you expect new ones. |
| No records appear in the picker | Search by a different name. An empty cell lists only records that are not linked yet. |
| **Review likely matches** is missing or disabled | You need write access and at least one connected tool. |
| **Review likely matches** finds nothing | No unlinked record scored 70 percent or higher. Use **Review unlinked records** to link by hand. |
| A cell shows **+1** or more | The company has several links in that tool. Click the button and **Unlink** the wrong ones. |
| **Apply** reports errors | Some links may have saved. Check the grid, then try the rest. |
| The company is linked but its data is missing | Linking does not import data. Check the tool's sync, its device and record mapping, and its import settings. |
| The **Company mapping cleanup** card is gone | Every record is linked and you have no ignored groups. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to click Save after linking a company?">
    No. Picking a record or clicking **Unlink** saves right away. **Review likely matches** has its own **Apply** step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Unlink delete the company in my PSA or RMM?">
    No. It only removes the link in MSPortal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does picking a second record replace the first link?">
    No. It adds a second link. Unlink the one you do not want.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a search hide rows that Apply still linked?">
    Checked rows stay checked when a search hides them, and **Apply** links every checked row. Clear the search to review the whole selection first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I lower the confidence below 70 percent?">
    No. The slider starts at 70 percent and goes up to 100.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Ignore from cleanup delete anything?">
    No. It only removes the group from **To review**. Click **Restore** on the **Ignored groups** tab to bring it back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a linked company mean its devices are imported?">
    No. Company links, device mapping, import settings, and sync are separate. Check each one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I see the grid but not change it?">
    Your role has read access only. Ask an admin for write access to **Integration Company Mapping** or to integration settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
