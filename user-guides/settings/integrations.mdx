Use **Settings > Integrations** to connect your PSA, RMM, backup, security, documentation, and other tools to MSPortal. The page lists your connections under **Connected Services** and your available tools under **Add integration**. It is for MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need to be MSP staff. Company users ask their IT provider to manage connections.
- Your role needs integration settings access. With read access you can see connections. Resync, import, and **Rename** need write access. **Connect**, **Configure**, and **Disconnect** need manage access.
- A role that has only **Integration Company Mapping** access can open the page and the **Map companies** button, but sees connection status only.
- Have the credentials or admin consent for the tool ready. Each tool's own guide in the [Integrations guides](/user-guides/integrations/index) lists what it needs.
- Some tools are not enabled for every tenant. A tool can also be marked **Coming Soon** or **Beta**.

## Open Integrations settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click **Settings** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Integrations">
    The page opens with a search box, a **Category** filter, and a **Status** filter at the top. Below them is the **Connected Services** table, then the **Add integration** catalog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Connected Services** table has the columns **Integration**, **Category**, **Status**, **Last Sync**, and **Actions**. Click a row (or its **Show details** arrow) to see health and sync details.

Use the **Category** filter to narrow the page to tools such as PSA, RMM, Backup, Security, Documentation, or Cloud. Use the **Status** filter to show **Connected**, **Stopped**, **Error**, or **Disconnected** rows. Click **Clear filters** to reset both.

## Connect an integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the tool">
    Scroll to **Add integration**. Search for the tool or use the **Category** filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect** on the tool's card. For a tool you already connected that allows more than one connection, the button reads **Connect another**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the setup steps">
    Enter the credentials, give consent, and choose options as the setup page asks. Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace open Company setup pages. Teams has its own setup page. Not every tool has the same steps or buttons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the scope before you finish">
    Review the companies, record types, role defaults, and automatic-import choices. Click **Next** through the steps and **Complete Setup** on the last one. Some mapping controls save as soon as you change them, before you finish the wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Go back to **Connected Services**. Check the **Status** and **Last Sync** columns. Then open the matching company, device, or import page and confirm the records you expect are there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Run a PSA permission check

ConnectWise, Autotask, Halo PSA, and Syncro setup includes a **Permissions** step. It tests what your API user can read in your PSA before any sync is turned on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Permissions step">
    Connect the PSA first. The **Permissions** step runs after the connection step. If the PSA shows no results yet, click **Re-check**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    Each area, such as **Companies**, **Tickets**, **Invoices**, or **Product catalog**, shows **Granted**, **Not permitted**, **Not available**, **Could not verify**, or **Granted, but no data returned**. Missing permissions list the exact permission to grant in your PSA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix what is missing, then re-check">
    Grant the permission in your PSA, then click **Re-check**. Repeat until the **Required** permissions are granted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Area | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Required** | Companies, contacts and staff, tickets, and ticket reference data. You cannot complete setup until these pass. |
| **Optional** | Everything else. You can finish setup without them. The matching sync toggle stays locked, with the permission it needs, until the permission is granted. |
| Later changes | MSPortal re-checks permissions automatically. If your PSA stops allowing a sync later, MSPortal turns that sync off and notifies you. |
| **Could not verify** | The PSA was slow or unreachable. Nothing is switched off because of it. Click **Re-check** to try again. |

## Rename a connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the actions menu">
    In **Connected Services**, click the **...** button in the **Actions** column of the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Rename">
    The **Rename integration** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a nickname">
    Type a name in **Nickname**, such as the office or client the connection is for. Save it. Clear the field to go back to the default name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disconnect a connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the actions menu">
    In **Connected Services**, click the **...** button in the **Actions** column of the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Disconnect">
    A confirmation named **Disconnect** with the connection name opens. Read the name to be sure it is the right one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm to remove the connection and its stored credentials. Synced data for that connection may be removed. This cannot be undone. Cancel closes the dialog and changes nothing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resume a stopped integration

MSPortal stops automatic syncing after repeated connection errors. The row shows **Stopped** (or **Error**) in the **Status** column.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the details">
    Click the row in **Connected Services** to see the health details and the last error.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix the cause">
    Correct the problem the last error describes, such as expired credentials. You can reconnect with current credentials from the setup page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Reactivate integration">
    Syncing resumes shortly after.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | A connection is present. Check **Last Sync** and the imported records to confirm they are current. |
| **Syncing** | A sync is running now. |
| **Stopped** | Automatic syncing was paused, usually after repeated errors. |
| **Error** | The connection has a problem that needs attention. |
| **Disconnected** | No connection exists yet. Click **Setup** or **Connect**. |

## Manage a tool with several connections

Some tools allow more than one connection in the same tenant, such as separate Datto RMM accounts or a UniFi console per client. With two or more connections, **Connected Services** groups them under one row that shows a count such as "3 connections".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expand the group">
    Click the row or its arrow to show each connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work on one connection">
    Each connection has its own **Status**, **Last Sync**, and actions, including **Resync**, **Import Devices**, and **Configure**. Its **...** menu has **Rename** and **Disconnect** for that connection only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the group actions">
    The group row has actions for the whole tool, listed below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Add connection** | Starts setup for another connection to the same tool. |
| **Manage companies** (or **clients**, **customers**, **domains**, **environments**, **organizations**, **policies**, **sites**, **tenants**, depending on the tool) | Opens the company mapping grid for that tool only. |
| **Manage devices** | Opens the device type mappings for that tool. Shown for tools that sync devices. |

You can also add a connection from **Add integration**. Tools that allow several connections show **Connect another** once the first connection exists.

## Map companies

Use **Map companies** in the Integrations page header to link your MSPortal companies to their records in every connected tool. See [Integration Company Mapping](/user-guides/settings/integration-company-mapping) for the steps.

## Map device types for every connection at once

Some device-sync tools share one device type mapping across all their connections. You set the mapping once, and it applies to every current connection of that tool.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage devices">
    In **Connected Services**, find the tool's group row and click **Manage devices**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the summary">
    Three tiles show **Connections**, **Staged devices**, and **Types needing attention**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a device type">
    In the **Device type mappings** table, open **Maps to** on a row and choose an MSPortal device type. The mapping saves right away to every connection of that tool, and a message tells you how many connections it updated. Choose **None** to remove the mapping from every connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Auto-sync if you want it">
    Tick **Auto-sync** on a mapped row so new devices of that type import on future syncs. A type must be mapped first. The setting applies to every connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **External type** | The device type name as the tool reports it. |
| **Devices** | How many staged devices have that type, across all connections. |
| **Connections** | Coverage such as "2 of 3": how many connections found this type. |
| **Maps to** | The MSPortal device type it becomes. |
| **Auto-sync** | Whether devices of this type keep importing automatically. |

If connections disagree about a type, the row shows **Mixed mappings** and the **Auto-sync** checkbox looks partly filled. Picking a type at the tool level makes every connection match your choice.

### Override one connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the connection">
    In the **Connection details** card at the bottom of the workspace, click **Open devices** on the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the mapping">
    Change **Maps to** or the auto-sync setting for that type on this connection only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check coverage">
    Back in the tool workspace, the type shows **Mixed mappings**. Setting that type at the tool level later replaces the override on every connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Map unmapped types with AI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a connection's devices page">
    Click **Open devices** on the connection. The page needs unmapped external types for the button to show.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Map with AI">
    It matches your unmapped types to your device types. Only confident matches are filled in. Ambiguous types stay unmapped. The matches save as soon as they are made, and a message tells you how many types were matched. You need manage access for integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the mappings">
    Check each match in the table and change any you disagree with before you import devices or rely on auto-sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sync and resync a PSA

PSA tools can supply companies, contacts, staff, tickets, and other records. Which records sync, and how often, depends on the tool and on the options you chose in its setup. There is no single sync interval for all tools. Check the tool's own guide and its **Last Sync** column.

Click **Resync** on a connection to queue a sync for it. Wait for it to finish and check the records before you click it again. A PSA row also has **Sync PSA Data**, which refreshes PSA settings such as priorities, boards, statuses, and types.

## Set a default import role for one company

When PSA contacts are imported automatically, MSPortal gives each one a portal role. You can set a different fallback role for one company without changing the integration-wide default.

The role is picked in this order.

1. A matching contact-type rule from the integration setup, including Primary Contact and Important Contact rules. If several rules match, their configured order decides.
2. The company's **Default Import Role**, when no contact-type rule matches.
3. The integration's default import role, when neither of the above applies.

If no role applies, the automatic import skips the contact.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company">
    Go to **Settings > Companies** and click the company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Defaults tab">
    Click **Defaults** at the top of the company page, then the **Import** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Default Import Role">
    Open **Default Import Role**. **Use integration default** keeps the integration's role, and the text below the field shows which role and tool the company currently inherits. Choose a role to override it for this company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Defaults**. Future automatic imports use this role when no contact-type rule matches.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Company and integration defaults do not change the role of users who already exist. A contact-type rule can update the role of an already linked user during a later PSA sync, so check those rules before you change a user's role by hand.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A connection will not save | Check the credentials and the tool's required permissions. For a PSA, run the **Permissions** step and click **Re-check**. Confirm your PSA plan includes API access, and that no firewall blocks MSPortal. |
| The row says **Connected** but records are old or missing | Check **Last Sync**, click **Resync**, and open the tool's mapping and import pages. Company links, device type mappings, and import settings are separate. |
| The row says **Stopped** | Open the details, fix the last error, and click **Reactivate integration**. |
| A sync toggle is locked | The tool has not granted the permission shown next to it. Grant it in the tool and re-check. |
| You do not see **Connect** or **Configure** | You need manage access for integration settings. |
| You do not see **Rename** | You need write access for integration settings. |
| You do not see **Disconnect** | You need manage access for integration settings. |
| A documentation tool will not connect | Only one documentation tool can be connected at a time. Disconnect the other one first. |
| A tool says **Coming Soon** | It is not available in your account yet. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Connected mean every record is current?">
    No. Check **Last Sync**, the permissions, the mappings, and the records themselves.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does every integration use the same setup steps?">
    No. Follow the steps on the tool's own setup page and in its guide. Cloud Company setup and Teams have their own pages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect the same tool twice?">
    Only some tools allow it. Look for **Add connection** or **Connect another**, and give each connection its own nickname.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Manage devices change only one connection?">
    No. It saves to every connection of that tool. To change one connection, click **Open devices** on it. A later change at the tool level replaces that override.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is auto-sync the same as mapping a company?">
    No. Company links, device type mappings, import choices, and auto-sync are separate steps.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I leave setup to undo a company mapping?">
    No. Mapping changes can save as soon as you make them. Leaving the wizard does not undo them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will changing the default import role update existing users?">
    No. Company and integration defaults only apply to new automatic imports. A contact-type rule can update a linked user during a PSA sync.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Disconnect pause syncing?">
    No. It removes the connection and its stored credentials, and synced data may be removed. To stop and restart syncing, use the **Stopped** and **Reactivate integration** flow instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
