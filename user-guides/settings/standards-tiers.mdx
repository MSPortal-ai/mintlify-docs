Use this page to create your service plans (Standards tiers), tie each plan to PSA company types, choose which standards each plan expects, and rank the ways a standard can be deployed. It is for MSP staff. Company users can see the linked work but cannot change this setup.

## Before you start

- You need Standards settings **Manage** permission and an account in good billing standing. With **Read** permission you can view the page but not change it.
- Each tier is a service plan, such as Platinum, Gold, Silver or Bronze. The names and the order are your choice.
- Company types come from your PSA. A PSA integration with synced companies must exist before you can assign company types to a plan.
- The standards you want to plan around must exist. See [Manage the Standards catalog](/user-guides/settings/standards).

## Create and rank plans

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    Go to **Settings > MSP Tools > Standards** and click the **Standards tiers** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a tier">
    Click **Add tier**. A row named **New tier 1** (then 2, 3 and so on) is saved right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe it">
    In the row, type a **Name** (60 characters at most) and a **Description** (300 characters at most). Names must be different from each other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a color">
    Open the **Color** dropdown and choose one of Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Amber, Emerald, Sky, Violet, Rose or Slate. The color only helps you tell tiers apart. It does not set the rank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rank the plans">
    Use the up and down arrows at the end of the row to put the plans in order, strongest first. The first row is the strongest tier.
  </Step>
</Steps>

There is no Save button. Names and descriptions save shortly after you stop typing, and the arrows save when you click them.

## Assign company types to a plan

A company gets its plan from its PSA company type. If a company has several types, it gets the highest-ranked plan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    In the plan's row, click the **Company types** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the types">
    Tick the PSA company types that belong on this plan. A type already on another plan shows that plan in brackets, for example **Managed (now Gold)**. Ticking it moves it to this plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Close the list. The change saves when it closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To take a type off a plan, untick it and close the list. A type belongs to one plan at a time. The list is built from the types on your active, PSA-synced companies. This page does not create company types or change a company's types. If the dropdown is disabled, no company types are available yet.

## Choose which standards belong to a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the standard">
    On the **Standards** tab, open the category and click the standard's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the plans">
    Open the **Service plans** dropdown, tick the plans that expect this standard, and close the list. A **Plans updated** message confirms the save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For example:

| Plan | Standards expected |
| --- | --- |
| Gold | A, B and C |
| Silver | A, B and Y |
| Bronze | A |

A standard with no plans selected (**All plans**) is expected of every company. A company whose company type is on no plan is also expected to have every standard. The **Service plans** field only appears once you have created at least one tier.

A plan states what is expected. It does not install software or mark a company Deployed.

## Add deployment options to a standard

A deployment option is one way to meet a standard, such as a specific product or managed service. Each option is ranked by a tier.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the standard">
    On the **Standards** tab, expand the category and click the standard's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an option">
    Under **Deployment options**, click **Add option**. If the button is disabled, create a tier first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Type the implementation in **Option name**, for example a product name. Option names must be different within one standard. A new option starts as **New deployment option**, and later ones are numbered (**New deployment option 2**, and so on). Replace the placeholder with the real name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set its tier">
    Pick a **Standards tier** for the option. A new option starts on the first, strongest tier, so check this choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add detail">
    Optionally fill in **Configuration detail**, such as "Provided by us as a managed network".
  </Step>
</Steps>

Several options can use the same tier. Adding an option does not assign it to any company. To record which option a company uses, pick it from the company's status cell on the Standards matrix. See [Read the Standards matrix](/user-guides/msp-tools/read-standards-matrix).

## How the matrix uses tiers

- A deployed standard with no option selected shows **Deployed, not classified**, and the matrix counts it as unclassified.
- An option at or above the company's target tier meets the target.
- An option below the target tier stays Deployed and is counted as **below target**. Its tooltip reads **Below company target**. It does not drop back to Not Started.
- In a one-of group with several deployed alternatives, the strongest deployed tier is the one compared to the target.

## Change or delete tiers and options

Renaming a tier changes its label everywhere. Moving it changes its rank. Changing an option's tier changes how companies using that option are classified.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Delete an option">
    Click the trash icon (**Delete deployment option**) at the end of the option's row. It is deleted at once, with no confirmation. Companies that had selected it keep their Deployed status but lose the option, so they show as not classified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete a tier">
    Click the trash icon on the tier's row. In **Delete Standards tier?** click **Delete tier**. A tier that options still use cannot be deleted, so move or delete those options first. Deleting a tier removes its company type assignments, and no other plan takes their place.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add option** is disabled | Create at least one tier first. |
| **Service plans** is missing from a standard | Create at least one tier first. |
| The **Company types** dropdown is disabled | No active PSA-synced company has a company type yet. Sync your PSA, then reload. |
| "A Standards tier with this name already exists" | Give the tier a name no other tier uses. |
| A tier will not delete | Options still use it. Move those options to another tier or delete them. |
| A company type moved off a plan | A type belongs to one plan. Ticking it on another plan moved it. |
| A deployed standard shows **Deployed, not classified** | Pick a deployment option for that company on the Standards matrix. |
| New option started on the wrong tier | New options start on the strongest tier. Change it in the **Standards tier** dropdown. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I configure Standards tiers?">
    Go to Settings > MSP Tools > Standards and click the Standards tiers tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are tier names fixed to Gold, Silver and Bronze?">
    No. Choose any names and descriptions. Color options are Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Amber, Emerald, Sky, Violet, Rose and Slate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which tier is strongest?">
    The first one in the list. Use the up and down arrows to change the order. Color does not set the rank.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a new tier an unsaved draft?">
    No. Add tier saves a numbered row right away, and later edits save automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do the Company types come from?">
    From the company types on your active, PSA-synced companies. This page cannot create or change them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if a company has several company types?">
    It gets the highest-ranked plan among its types.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a plan install anything?">
    No. A plan only states which standards a company is expected to have.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What tier does a new option start with?">
    The first, strongest tier. Change it if that is wrong.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does creating an option assign it to companies?">
    No. You choose the option for each company on the Standards matrix.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a deployed standard be below its target?">
    Yes. It stays Deployed and shows as below target.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting an option ask for confirmation?">
    No. It is deleted at once. Companies that selected it keep their Deployed status without an option.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to company types when I delete a tier?">
    They are left with no plan. No other plan is chosen for them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
