Compliance Settings is where MSP staff build the checks, groups and run templates used to assess clients. The library is shared across all companies. Company users do not configure it.

## Before you start

- The Compliance feature must be available for your account.
- Open **Settings > Operations > Compliance**. The tabs you see depend on your permissions and features: **Checks**, **Playbooks**, **Metrics** (needs a Liongard integration), **Groups**, **Runs**, **Schedule**, **Scoring** and **Risk**.
- Permissions differ by action. **Add Group**, **New Check**, **Add New Template** and the **Schedule** controls need Compliance settings write access. Editing library content needs write access to Compliance. Deleting and approving needs manage access to Compliance. See [Permissions](#permissions).
- **Runs** here means reusable run templates, not the history of completed assessments. To run a template on a recurring basis, use [Schedule](/user-guides/settings/compliance-schedules).

The **Checks**, **Groups** and **Runs** tabs share a split-pane layout. The list is on the left and the detail is on the right. Click the panel icon at the top of the list to collapse it.

## Organize checks in folders

The **Checks** tab lists every check your tenant can use. Folders only organize this settings page. They do not change how checks behave in a run.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a folder">
    On the **Checks** tab, click the **New folder** icon at the top of the **Compliance checks** list. To nest a folder, open a folder's menu and click **New subfolder**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a name such as "Microsoft 365" or "CIS Controls v8" and click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add checks">
    Open the folder's menu and click **New check here**, or drag existing checks into the folder (see below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

A folder's menu also has **Rename** and **Delete**. Deleting a folder removes its subfolders. Checks inside move to **Uncategorized**, so no check is lost.

### Move checks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the checks">
    Click a check to open it on the right. Hold **Cmd** (macOS) or **Ctrl** (Windows) and click more checks to add them. Hold **Shift** and click to select a range. The list shows how many are selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag them to a folder">
    Drag any selected check onto a folder. The folder highlights while you hover. Drop to move every selected check at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or use the menu">
    For one check, open its **Check actions** menu and click **Move to folder**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To take a check out of a folder, drag it onto **Uncategorized** at the bottom of the list. If you drag a check that is not in your selection, only that check moves.

### Search and filter

Type in the search box to filter by check title. Folders with matches open automatically. Click the filter icon to filter by **Approval status**, **Priority**, **Criticality**, **Frequency**, **Status list** or **Compliance type**. Click **Clear all** to reset.

## Create a check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the new check form">
    Click **New Check** in the page header, or **New check here** in a folder's menu. A check made from a folder starts in that folder. Otherwise it starts in **Uncategorized**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the check">
    Enter the details, such as title, description, criticality, priority, frequency, status list and compliance type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the check. It appears in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To have AI draft many checks at once, click **Bulk Generate with AI** in the page header.

## Edit a check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the check">
    Click a check in the list. Its form opens on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the fields">
    Edit the fields (see the table below). Click **Improve with AI** to have AI suggest a better description, how-to-check, remediation and references from the title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** in the form header. A toast says **Check updated**. If you open another check first, MSPortal asks whether to discard your unsaved changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Section | Fields |
|---------|--------|
| Identity | **Title**, **Description**, **How to Check**, **Why It's Important** |
| Remediation | **Remediation**, **Remediation Impact**, **References**, **Default Value** |
| Assessment | **Criticality** (Minimal to Critical), **Priority** (High, Medium, Low), **Frequency** (Daily to Annually) |
| Classification | **Status list**, **Compliance type**, and **Default risk category** when risk tracking is on |
| Note templates by status | A note template per answer (see below) |
| Applies to | Conditions that decide which companies and devices get the check |
| Group assignments | The groups this check belongs to |

Changing the **Compliance type** can remove group assignments that no longer match when you save. Note-template links and group membership save separately and at once. Cancelling the main form does not undo them. If a save fails partway, such as on custom fields, reopen the check and look at what saved before you retry.

Checks have an approval status: **Draft**, **Pending review**, **Approved** or **Rejected**. Users with manage access can approve a check from its **Check actions** menu, or select several and click **Approve selected**.

### Link note templates to answers

Each check uses a **status list** that defines its answer choices, such as Pass, Fail and Needs review. You can link a note template to each answer. When an assessor picks that answer in a run, the template text is added to their public notes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a status list">
    In the check form, pick a **Status list**. The **Note templates by status** section appears. Save the check so the status list is stored.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a template for each answer">
    Choose a note template for each answer you want filled in. Leave the others on **No template**. Each choice saves at once. **Clear template** removes a link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assessor picks the answer">
    In a run, when the assessor picks an answer with a linked template, the template text is added under any existing public notes, after a blank line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only templates in the **Compliance Check Note** category appear. Create them in **Settings > Customization > Note Templates** first. See [Note Templates](/user-guides/settings/templates). The template is applied once per answer and template in a session, so switching between answers does not repeat it.

### Choose which companies and devices get a check

By default a check goes to every company, location or device a run covers. Add conditions under **Applies to** to narrow it. For example, send a FileVault check only to Macs, and a HIPAA check only to healthcare clients.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a condition">
    Scroll to **Applies to** and click **Add condition**. Choose the **Condition**, a **Comparison** such as **is any of**, and the **Value**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Combine conditions">
    With two or more conditions, choose whether the check applies when **all conditions match** or **any condition matches**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** in the check header. A toast says **Conditions saved**. New runs, and checks added to runs, use the conditions. Existing assignments stay as they are.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Condition | Tests |
|-----------|-------|
| **Company computer platforms** | The company's active computers: **PCs**, **Macs** or **PCs and Macs** |
| **Company device types** | The company's active device types, with **has any of** and **has none of** |
| **Industry**, **Company type**, **Standards tier** | The company's industry, PSA company type, or the Standards tier mapped to its company type |
| **Company fields** | Any company custom field. Dates use **is on**, **is after** and **is before** |
| **Operating system** | The device's OS family, such as **Windows**, **Windows Server**, **macOS** or **Linux** |
| **Device type**, **Manufacturer**, **RMM** | The device's type, manufacturer and source RMM |

Device conditions apply only to checks with the **Device** compliance type. Company, location and control checks use their company conditions and skip device conditions. You can add a limited number of conditions per check.

<Tip>
A company or device with no value for a condition still gets the check. To skip records with no value, use **is empty** or **is not empty**. For example, add **Operating system is not empty** to skip devices whose OS is unknown.
</Tip>

### Delete checks

Open a check's **Check actions** menu and click **Delete**, or select several checks and click **Delete selected**. Both need manage access. Deleting is permanent and removes the check from every group it was in.

## Build groups

Groups bundle related checks so you can add a whole set to a run at once. A check can be in any number of groups.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Groups tab">
    Go to **Settings > Operations > Compliance** and click **Groups**. The list on the left shows every group. Search by name, or filter by compliance type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Group">
    Click **Add Group** in the page header, or the **+** icon at the top of the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter a **Name** and an optional description. Choose a **Compliance type**. If you pick one, only checks of that type can be added. Choose **None (any type)** to allow any check. For a Device group, also pick **Device types**. Devices of those types are evaluated against the group's checks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**. The group appears in the list and opens on the right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/product/compliance-group-create-draft.png" alt="New group dialog with name, description and compliance type fields and Cancel/Create controls." width="960" height="856" />
</Frame>

If the device-type update fails after the group saves, reopen the group and check what saved before you retry.

### Add and remove checks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the checks">
    The group page has two columns, **Available checks** on the left and **Members** on the right. Each column has its own search box. Click a check to select it. Hold **Cmd** or **Ctrl** to add more, or **Shift** to select a range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag them across">
    Drag from **Available checks** to **Members** to add. Drag from **Members** to **Available checks** to remove. A toast says how many were added or removed. Each move saves at once, so wait for the toast before the next one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the group has a compliance type, only matching checks appear under **Available checks**. Clear the type to see all checks.

### Edit or delete a group

Use the **Edit group** and **Delete** buttons in the group header, or the menu on a list row. Deleting a group does not delete its checks. Deleting needs manage access.

## Build run templates

A run template bundles groups and individual checks into a reusable assessment package. A run made from the template assigns every check in each member group, plus each individual check.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Runs tab">
    Go to **Settings > Operations > Compliance** and click **Runs**. The list shows each template with its group and check counts. Filter by **All templates**, **Active only** or **Inactive only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add New Template">
    Click **Add New Template** in the page header, or the **+** icon at the top of the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    Enter a **Name** (for example, "Quarterly security review"), an optional **Description** and the **Active** switch. Inactive templates stay in this list but are left out of the schedule's template list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**. The template opens on the right with empty **Groups** and **Individual checks** tabs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="New template starts with Active on. Create saves the template. Add groups and checks afterward.">
  <img src="/images/product/compliance-run-template-create-draft.png" alt="New template dialog with name, description, Active switch and Cancel/Create controls." width="960" height="772" />
</Frame>

### Add groups and checks to a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Groups tab">
    On the template, click **Groups**. **Available groups** is on the left and **Groups in this template** on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag groups across">
    Drag groups into **Groups in this template** to add them. Every check in those groups runs whenever the template is used. Drag them back to remove them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add individual checks (optional)">
    Click **Individual checks** for one-off checks that are not in a group. They run in addition to the groups. The drag and drop works the same way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Group and check moves save at once. Cancelling the template's edit dialog does not undo them. Groups and individual checks are independent, so you can use either or both.

### Activate, edit or delete a template

- Use the **Active** switch in the template header, or **Activate** and **Deactivate** in the row menu. It saves at once. Turning a template off does not pause schedules that use it, so check your schedules separately.
- **Edit template** opens the name, description and active dialog.
- **Delete** removes the template. Runs already created from it are not affected. Deleting needs manage access.

<Warning>
Deleting a run template does not stop or change runs already created. It only prevents new runs from it.
</Warning>

## Permissions

| Action | Required permission |
|--------|---------------------|
| Open a tab | The matching Compliance settings read permission, and the Compliance feature |
| View checks, groups and templates | **read_compliance** |
| Create, edit, move, add and remove members | **write_compliance** |
| Delete checks, groups or templates, and approve checks | **manage_compliance** |

The page header buttons (**New Check**, **Bulk Generate with AI**, **Add Group**, **Add New Template**) and the **Schedule** controls need Compliance settings write access. Scoring and risk changes need Compliance settings manage access.

## Keyboard and mouse reference

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Open a check, group or template | Click the row |
| Select several | **Cmd/Ctrl + Click** more rows |
| Select a range | **Shift + Click** a second row |
| Clear a selection | Click **Clear**, or click a row without a modifier |
| Move items | Drag selected rows. Drop targets highlight |
| Collapse the list | Click the panel icon at the top of the list |

## Best practices

- **Start with folders.** Organize checks by framework (CIS, NIST, SOC 2) or domain (Microsoft 365, networking) before the library grows.
- **Use note templates for repeated findings.** Link a template to the Fail answer once instead of typing it every assessment.
- **Set a compliance type on groups.** It stops mismatched checks being added.
- **Build templates from groups.** They are easier to maintain than long lists of single checks.
- **Deactivate, do not delete.** Turn a template off with its **Active** switch to keep its history and keep pickers short.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| A tab is missing | You lack that tab's permission, or the feature is off. **Metrics** also needs a Liongard integration |
| **New Check**, **Add Group** or **Add New Template** is disabled | You need Compliance settings write access |
| I cannot delete or approve | These need manage access to Compliance |
| A check will not save | Read the error under **Couldn't save check**. If conditions are incomplete, finish or remove them first |
| A note template is missing from the list | Only templates in the **Compliance Check Note** category show |
| A check is missing from **Available checks** | The group has a compliance type that the check does not match. Clear the type or change the check's type |
| A template is missing from the schedule | The template is inactive. Turn on **Active** |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Runs show completed company assessments?">
    No. **Runs** here holds reusable templates. See completed assessments in the Compliance workspace.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does cancelling a check discard note-template link changes?">
    No. Each link saves at once, separately from the check's fields.
  </Accordion>

    No. Each drag between columns saves at once.
  </Accordion>

    No. Check your schedules separately. An inactive template is left out of the schedule's template list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing a group's type change its membership?">
    It can. Only checks of the matching type stay eligible, and a Device group also uses its device types. Check the members afterward.

    No. Create a run from the template, or set up a schedule that uses it.
  </Accordion>

    No. The checks stay in the library.
