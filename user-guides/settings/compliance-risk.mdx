Risk tracking turns compliance failures into a risk register with scoring, ownership and reporting. This page is for MSP staff who set it up. These settings apply to the whole tenant, not one company. [Risk Management](/user-guides/compliance/risk-management) explains the register and individual risks.

## Before you start

- The Compliance feature must be available for your account.
- Open **Settings > Operations > Compliance** and click the **Risk** tab. You need Compliance settings access to view it.
- Turning risk tracking on or off needs manage access. Adding, editing, reordering and archiving categories needs write or manage access.

## Turn on risk tracking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Risk tab">
    Go to **Settings > Operations > Compliance** and click **Risk**. The page is titled **Compliance risk**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category starter">
    Under **Choose a category starter**, click one card.

    | Starter | Categories it adds |
    |---------|--------------------|
    | **MSP risk domains** | A broad set: governance, cyber, privacy, operations, continuity, third parties, financial, reputation, physical and other |
    | **NIST CSF** | Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover and Other |
    | **Custom** | Other only. Add your own categories afterward |

    The starters are only category lists. They do not make you compliant with a framework.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Enable risk tracking">
    Click **Enable risk tracking** (or turn on the **Enable risk tracking** switch). The confirmation dialog shows the starter you picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Enable risk tracking** in the dialog. **Cancel** leaves risk tracking off. A toast says **Compliance risk tracking enabled**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    Check **Risk categories** on the page and the Risk workspace before you introduce the workflow to your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Enabling does more than flip a switch. It also creates the system **Risk Dashboard** and prepares risks from eligible compliance findings.

If you turn tracking back on later with the same starter, your categories stay as they are. If you pick a different starter, MSPortal adds the starter's missing categories and keeps all existing ones.

## Add or edit a category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Under **Risk categories**, click **Add category**. To change an existing category, open its **More category actions** menu and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the name and color">
    Enter a short **Category name** and choose a **Category color**. If you type a color, use a full six-digit hex value such as `#0d9488`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A toast confirms **Risk category created** or **Risk category updated**. **Cancel** discards your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reorder, archive or restore a category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reorder">
    Click **Move category up** or **Move category down** on a category. Each click saves at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive">
    Open the category's **More category actions** menu and click **Archive category**. It saves at once. An **Archived** badge appears. Existing risks that use the category keep it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore">
    Open the menu on an archived category and click **Restore category**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Scoring matrix** is a fixed reference. Every risk is scored as likelihood times impact on a 5 by 5 scale. The severity bands are **Low** 1 to 4, **Medium** 5 to 9, **High** 10 to 16 and **Critical** 17 to 25. Category colors and order do not change the bands.

## Turn off risk tracking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Risk tab">
    Go to **Settings > Operations > Compliance** and click **Risk**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off the switch">
    Turn off **Enable risk tracking**. It saves at once with no confirmation. A toast says **Compliance risk tracking disabled. Existing risk data has been preserved.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turning tracking off hides the feature. It does not delete your risks. Do not use the switch to preview a different starter. Check how it affects your team's register and reports first.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| The switch and **Enable risk tracking** button are disabled | You need manage access to Compliance settings |
| **Add category** or the category menu is missing | You need write or manage access |
| A category will not save | Check the name and that the color is a full six-digit hex value. Read the error message, reopen the list and try again |
| **Risk settings could not be updated** | Try again. If it keeps failing, contact support |
| A client cannot see a risk | Enabling tracking does not share risks. The risk must be marked visible to clients and approved by an MSP user. The client also needs Compliance access and the company must be in scope |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does picking a category starter turn on tracking?">
    No. Click **Enable risk tracking** and confirm in the dialog.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will a different starter replace my categories?">
    No. When you turn tracking back on with a different starter, MSPortal adds the missing starter categories and keeps the rest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does turning off risk tracking delete the register?">
    No. It hides the feature and keeps the data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do the arrows and Archive wait for a Save?">
    No. Reordering, archiving and restoring save at once. Name and color changes use the dialog's **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does archiving a category delete its risks?">
    No. Existing risks keep the category.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the scoring matrix?">
    No. It is a fixed 5 by 5 scale.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does enabling risk show every risk to clients?">
    No. A client sees a risk only when it is marked visible to clients and approved by an MSP user, and the client has Compliance access to that company.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
