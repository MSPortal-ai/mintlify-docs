This page shows Company users and MSP staff how to find a project in **Cards** or **List**, read its summary, and open it.

## Before you start

- You need Projects access and permission for **Cards** or **List**. Each view has its own permission.
- Company users see only the projects their MSP shares with them. **Dashboard** is an MSP-only view.
- MSP staff can search across every company they can access.

## Find and open a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your company">
    Use the company selector if it is available, then open **Projects**. **Global View** includes only companies you can access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Cards or List">
    Use **Cards** for summaries or **List** to compare rows. Your browser can remember a previous view. If a tab is missing, choose another available view or ask your MSP about its permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the project">
    Click the search (magnifier) icon and enter part of the project name, company name, or outcome. The state starts on **Open**. Choose **Closed** for closed projects, or **All** to search both. See [Filter and sort projects](/user-guides/projects/filter-projects) for dates, managers, status, and health.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open its workspace">
    Click the project card or table row to open its [workspace](/user-guides/projects/workspace). Use the page controls at the bottom to see more results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the project summary

| Field | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Status | The project status. Your MSP can map the original PSA status to a simpler label. |
| Health | The project's health classification. List shows the status when no health is set. |
| Hours progress | Hours used compared with the estimate, capped at 100%. A project with the Not Started status shows zero. |
| Timeline | Time elapsed between the start and due dates. |
| Lead | The MSPortal project lead when one is set, otherwise the synced PSA lead. |
| Next action | The next pending client action, with urgent and due-soon actions first. If there is none, the next dated milestone. |
| Milestones (List) | Completed visible milestones out of all visible milestones. |
| Last Change | The project's last update time from the PSA. |

The next action reads **Waiting on you** when the pending action is assigned to you, **Waiting on** a named person, or **Waiting on client**. Open the project to read the request before you respond.

Cards show only the fields your MSP turns on. A card shows up to four populated custom fields. List can show more.

## Compare projects in List

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a sort order">
    Use the **Sort by** menu, for example **Name (A-Z)**, **Last Changed**, or **Due Date (Soonest)**. Column headings do not sort the whole portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the columns">
    Click **Columns** above the table and tick the columns you want. MSP staff with the Project tracker can also turn on a column for each PSA project role, such as **Technical Lead** or **Account Manager**. Role columns start hidden.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter a column">
    Use the filter on a column heading. See [Filter List columns](/user-guides/projects/filter-projects#filter-list-columns).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The project is missing | Choose **All**, clear the search and other filters, and check the selected company. Clearing filters does not change **Open / Closed / All**, the view, or the sort order. |
| A date filter hides projects | The Date filter is remembered from your last visit. Click **Date** and then **Clear**. |
| The project is still missing | Ask your MSP to check the company mapping, project visibility, your role, and the PSA sync. MSP staff should check **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Client project access** and the project's own visibility. |
| Cards or List is missing | Each view has its own permission. Ask your MSP to review your role. |
| The page shows an error | Click **Retry**. An error does not mean the company has no projects. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can MSP staff see a project that I cannot?">
    Company scope, your role, Client project access settings, and each project's visibility limit what Company users see. Ask your MSP to review them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does 100% progress mean the project is finished?">
    No. The card's hours progress compares hours used with the estimate. Check the project status, milestones, and updates for completion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Dashboard missing from my Projects page?">
    Dashboard is MSP-only. Cards, List, Kanban, and Schedule each need their own view permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company administrator create a project here?">
    No. **New project** is for MSP staff with project write permission. Ask your MSP to scope the work, or start with the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) and review its proposed ticket before you confirm.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
