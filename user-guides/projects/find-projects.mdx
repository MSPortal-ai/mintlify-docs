Open **Projects** to find work shared with your company. MSP staff can use the same controls across the companies they can access.

## Before you start

You need Projects access and permission for **Cards** or **List**. Each view has its own permission. Company users see projects made available by their MSP; **Dashboard** is an MSP-only portfolio view. Ask your MSP to review missing access, including for Company administrators.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your company">
    Use the company selector if it is available, then open **Projects**. **Global View** includes only companies you can access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Cards or List">
    Use **Cards** for summaries or **List** to compare rows. Your browser can remember a previous view. If a tab is missing, choose another available view or ask your MSP about its permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the project">
    Enter part of its name, company name, or outcome in the search field. The initial state is **Open**. Choose **Closed** for closed projects, or **All** if you are unsure. Review the active filters; [filtering projects](/user-guides/projects/filter-projects) explains dates, managers, status, and health.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open its workspace">
    Select the project card or table row. Confirm the project and company before reviewing the [project workspace](/user-guides/projects/workspace). Use the page controls to see more results when the list spans several pages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the project summary

| Field | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Status | The displayed project status; your MSP may map the original PSA status to a simpler label. |
| Health | The project's health classification, where available. A missing health classification can cause List to display the project status instead. |
| Hours progress | Hours used relative to the estimate, capped at 100%. It is not a percentage of tasks completed. A displayed Not Started status keeps the card's hours progress at zero. |
| Timeline | Time elapsed between the start and due dates. It is separate from hours usage and task completion. |
| Lead | The MSPortal project lead when one is set, otherwise the synced PSA lead. |
| Next action | The next pending client action, prioritizing urgent actions and due dates; otherwise an upcoming visible milestone with a date. It is a summary, not the full action list. |
| Milestones in List | Completed visible milestones out of all visible milestones. |
| Last Change | The project's recorded PSA update time. It does not prove that every integration has just synchronized. |

**Waiting on you** identifies the selected pending action assigned to your user. **Waiting on** another person or **Waiting on client** describes that action's assignment. Open the workspace to read the request before responding.

Cards show only fields enabled by the MSP and available for that project. Custom fields must be enabled and shared with your role; a card shows up to four populated fields, while List can show additional columns. Hidden hours or an absent estimate are not evidence of zero effort.

## Compare projects in List

Use the page's sort selector, such as **Name (A-Z)**, **Last Changed**, or **Due Date (Soonest)**. Column headings do not sort the entire portfolio. Select **Columns** above the table to choose visible columns. Opening a row reads the project; it does not save a change.

When the Project tracker is enabled for your tenant, MSP staff can also add a column for each PSA project role, such as **Technical Lead** or **Account Manager**. Role names come from your PSA project teams, and each cell lists the people in that role. Role columns start hidden; turn them on in **Columns** and select **Save Changes**. To filter by role, use the filter panel instead.

The project row/card **…** menu may expose separate actions such as **Add to Meeting** or **Reschedule**. Those are workflow actions, not filters. Company users do not receive the MSP rescheduling control. Simply opening a project does not perform either action.

## If a project is missing

Choose **All**, clear the search and other filters, check the selected company, and try again. Clearing filters (press **Esc**, or **Reset** then **Apply Filters** in Filters) does not change **Open / Closed / All**, the selected view, or sort order. A date filter may be remembered from a previous visit.

If the project still does not appear, ask your MSP to check its company mapping, client visibility, role access, and source sync. MSP staff should also check **Settings > Projects > Client project access** and the individual project's visibility. Changing a Company user's role is an MSP task; Company administrators do not grant themselves access.

If the page shows a load error, use **Retry** when offered. An error or empty filtered result is not confirmation that the company has no projects.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can MSP staff see a project that I cannot?">
    Company scope, your role, Client project access settings, and individual project visibility can limit the Company view. Ask your MSP to review these after clearing filters and selecting All.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does 100% progress mean the project is finished?">
    The card's hours progress measures use of estimated hours and is capped at 100%. Check the actual project status, milestones, and delivery updates to establish completion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Dashboard missing from my Projects page?">
    Dashboard is MSP-only. Cards, List, Kanban, and Schedule each require their own view permission. Ask your MSP about a missing Company view.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company administrator create a new project here?">
    New project is an MSP staff action requiring project write permission. Ask your MSP to scope the work. For a support request, start with the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket), review its proposed ticket, and confirm before submission.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
