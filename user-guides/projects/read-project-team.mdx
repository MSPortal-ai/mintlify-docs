<Note>Branch preview: open **Team** or **Team & Contacts** in the grouped project workspace.</Note>

**Audience:** MSP staff with project access and the relevant portal permissions. Company users do not receive this management view; they can [read shared project contacts on Client Dashboard](/user-guides/projects/read-project-overview).

The tab has one **Project team** roster. Linked onboarding projects show **Client Contacts** beside it. ConnectWise assignments and portal task assignments appear together for people with a unique matching staff email. People without a matching portal account still appear with their ConnectWise assignments.

## Change roles beside a person

The roster has shared **Person**, **Portal task roles** and **ConnectWise project role** column headers. Both role controls sit side by side. The portal column appears on linked onboardings; the ConnectWise column appears on ConnectWise projects.

- **Portal task roles** controls the roles covered by that person's portal tasks. Select an unassigned role to assign its unfilled tasks. Deselect a role to unassign that person's tasks for that role. Choose **Apply** to save the selected portal roles. Other people's existing assignments are preserved. You need onboarding write permission.
- **ConnectWise project role** changes the role on an existing ConnectWise project-team assignment. Each assignment has its own selector. Changes save to ConnectWise immediately; select the previous role to restore it. You need project write permission and an active subscription. This write path is covered by automated provider-mocked tests; a live provider write has not been performed for this preview.

These roles do not grant portal access or change a user's permission role. MSP administrators manage [users and permissions](/user-guides/settings/users-roles) separately.

## Assign an unfilled portal role

Choose **Assign…** beside a role under **Unassigned roles**, then select an MSP staff member. That person appears in the roster with their task count and role selector. Removing their last role may remove them from the portal roster; their ConnectWise assignments remain visible when present.

The person picker on a portal staff row reassigns that person's portal tasks. Its remove option unassigns those tasks. It does not remove their ConnectWise team membership. Add or remove ConnectWise team memberships in ConnectWise.

## Client contacts

Use **Client Contacts** to manage portal invitations and client roles on linked onboardings. These contacts remain separate from internal staff assignments. See [Manage project contacts](/user-guides/projects/manage-project-contacts) for project summary contacts.

## Loading and unavailable data

The roster loads current ConnectWise assignments and can reuse recent results. Profile details come from synchronized member data. If one source fails, its error appears within the roster while available assignments from the other source remain visible. A loading error does not mean the team is empty.

On phones, contacts and the project team stack vertically. Each person's identity appears above their two role fields, which remain side by side.
