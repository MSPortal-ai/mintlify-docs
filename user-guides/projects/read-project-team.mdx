This page shows MSP staff how to see who is on a project and change their roles.

## Before you start

- You need MSP staff access to the project. Company users do not get this view. They [read shared project contacts on the Client Dashboard](/user-guides/projects/read-project-overview).
- The team page appears for ConnectWise projects and for projects with a linked onboarding portal.
- Changing a portal task role needs onboarding write permission. Changing a ConnectWise role needs project write permission and an active subscription.

## Open the team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project">
    Open **Projects** and click the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Team">
    Click **Team**, or **Team & Contacts** when the project has a client portal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page has one **Project team** roster. Projects with a client portal also show **Client Contacts** beside it. A person's ConnectWise and portal assignments appear together when their staff email matches. People without a matching portal account still show with their ConnectWise assignments.

## Change roles beside a person

The roster has shared **Person**, **Portal task roles** and **ConnectWise project role** column headers. Both role controls sit side by side. The portal column appears on linked onboardings; the ConnectWise column appears on ConnectWise projects.

- **Portal task roles** sets the roles that person covers in portal tasks. Tick an unassigned role to give that person its unfilled tasks. Untick a role to unassign their tasks for it. Click **Apply** to save. Other people's assignments stay as they are.
- **ConnectWise project role** changes the role on an existing ConnectWise team assignment. Each assignment has its own selector. The change saves to ConnectWise right away. Pick the old role to put it back.

These roles do not give portal access or change a user's permission role. Admins manage [users and permissions](/user-guides/settings/users-roles) separately.

## Assign an unfilled portal role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the role">
    Look under **Unassigned roles** for the role that has open tasks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign it">
    Click **Assign…** beside the role and pick an MSP staff member. They appear in the roster with their task count and role selector.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The person picker on a staff row moves that person's portal tasks to someone else. Its remove option unassigns those tasks. Neither changes their ConnectWise team membership. Add or remove ConnectWise team members in ConnectWise. If you remove someone's last portal role they can drop off the portal roster. Their ConnectWise assignments still show.

## Client contacts

Use **Client Contacts** to manage portal invitations and client roles on projects with a portal. They are separate from your staff assignments. For the contacts shown on the project summary, see [Manage project contacts](/user-guides/projects/manage-project-contacts).

On phones, contacts and the project team stack vertically, and each person's two role fields stay side by side.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Team** is missing | It shows only for MSP staff on ConnectWise projects or projects with a client portal. |
| The roster shows an error | The roster loads ConnectWise and portal data separately. If one fails its error shows, and the other source still lists people. An error does not mean the team is empty. |
| A role selector is disabled | You need onboarding write permission for portal roles, or project write permission and an active subscription for ConnectWise roles. |
| Someone is missing from the roster | Add them to the project team in ConnectWise, or assign them a portal role. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does changing a role here change what the person can do in MSPortal?">
    No. These are project roles only. Permission roles are managed under **Settings**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add someone to the ConnectWise team here?">
    No. Add and remove ConnectWise team members in ConnectWise.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
