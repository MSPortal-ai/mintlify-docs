This page shows Company users and MSP staff how to read a project's milestones. Company users see shared milestones on projects they can access. Your MSP manages milestone editing and permissions.

## Before you start

- You need access to the project.
- Internal milestones are hidden from Company users, so your list can be shorter than an MSP staff member's.

## Review milestones

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Milestones">
    Open **Projects**, click the project, then click **Project Plan** and **Milestones**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the list">
    The list on the left shows each milestone's title, its status (**Pending**, **In Progress**, **Complete**, or **Skipped**), and its planned date. Use **Search** above the list to find one by title, description, notes, or linked phase or ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a milestone">
    Click a milestone to read its description and any linked phase, ticket, or files on the right. MSP staff also see the actual completion date and internal notes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The milestone's planned date is the date the plan expects it. A **Complete** milestone can have an actual date that differs from the planned date. Milestone status and dates are kept separately from ticket and project completion.

For dated project tickets and rescheduling, use **Project Plan > Schedule** and the [project calendar guide](/user-guides/projects/calendar). For progress notes, open [Updates](/user-guides/projects/read-project-updates).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No milestones yet** | No milestones have been shared with you. Ask your MSP what has been shared. It does not mean the project has no work. |
| A milestone is missing | It may be internal, or your search is filtering the list. Clear **Search**. |
| A milestone has no date | The MSP has not set a planned date. |
| You need a date or status changed | Ask your MSP. Company users cannot edit milestones. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Complete mean the whole project is finished?">
    No. It marks that one milestone complete. Check the project and its remaining work separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the list order not follow the dates?">
    Your MSP sets the order by dragging milestones. Read the planned date on each row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change a milestone date or mark it complete?">
    No. Your MSP manages changes. Authorized MSP staff can use [Manage project milestones](/user-guides/projects/manage-project-milestones).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
