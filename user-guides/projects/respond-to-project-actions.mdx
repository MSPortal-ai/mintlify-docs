This page shows Company users and MSP staff how to answer a question, record a decision, or complete a request that is assigned to you in a project. You do not need permission to edit the whole project.

## Before you start

- You need access to the project.
- The item must be **Pending** and assigned to your signed-in user.
- Company users must also be able to see the shared item. The portal checks again when you submit.
- If an item is missing or assigned to the wrong person, ask your MSP to fix it. Company administrators cannot give themselves project access.

## Find the request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project">
    In **Projects**, [find the project](/user-guides/projects/find-projects) and click it. Check the company and project name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the list">
    Click **Collaboration**, then **Decisions** or **Action Items**. **Decisions** holds approvals, questions, access requests, scheduling confirmations, and decisions. **Action Items** holds work to complete. The **actions waiting** button in the page header opens **Decisions** first when decisions are pending, and **Action Items** otherwise. Its count covers both lists, not only your items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Above the list, click **Pending** (in **Action Items** it is **Open**) to see only unfinished items. Click **All** to see everything, including cancelled items. Each choice shows a count. Pending items come first, with urgent and earlier-due items at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Click the item to read its description, type, assignees, and due date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The counts apply to the list you are in, not to both lists together.

## Respond and confirm

| Request type | Button | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approval Required | **Approve** | Review the request and confirm you accept it. |
| Question | **Answer** | Enter **Your Answer**. A nonblank answer is required. |
| Decision Required | **Decide** | Enter **Your Decision** if needed; the dialog permits confirmation without text. |
| Access Needed | **Provide Access** | First arrange the required access through the agreed process, then confirm it has been provided. |
| Scheduling | **Schedule** | First arrange the requested scheduling, then confirm it is complete. |
| Action Item | **Complete** | Finish the requested work, then confirm completion. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your response dialog">
    Click the action button at the top of your assigned pending item, such as **Approve** or **Answer**. Read the title and description again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the response where offered">
    Questions need an answer. Decisions have optional response text. The other types ask you to confirm. There is no way to attach files or send credentials here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm or cancel">
    Click the dialog's action button to save. **Cancel** closes the dialog and clears your draft. Wait while **Submitting...** shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    The dialog closes and the project refreshes. The item shows **Done**. Click **Done** above the list to find it, then open it to see the response, the time, and who completed it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Provide Access** and **Schedule** record completion of the request. They do not grant permissions, exchange passwords, or create a calendar meeting. **Complete** also does not automatically finish a linked PSA ticket or the whole project.
</Warning>

## Shared assignments and corrections

When several people are assigned, any one of them can complete the item, and it is then complete for everyone. It is not a vote from each person. If someone else finished it first, check the result before you do anything else.

The response dialog has no **Reject** button. Do not approve something you do not accept. Contact your MSP to explain. You cannot reopen or change a completed response, so ask your MSP to correct it. For a new support issue, use the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket), review the proposed ticket, and confirm before you submit.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No action button on the item | The button shows only on a pending item assigned to your signed-in user. You can read items without being an assignee. Ask your MSP to check the assignment. |
| The submission failed | Read the error and keep your answer. Reload the project to check the item. It may already be resolved, or your access may have changed. A failed submit does not mean the work was recorded. |
| I cannot find my finished item | Click **Done** above the list. Finished items leave the **Pending** view. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is there no green response button?">
    It shows only on a pending item assigned to your signed-in user. Ask your MSP to check the assignment and your access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do all assigned people need to approve?">
    No. One person's response completes the item for everyone. Use separate items when each person must approve.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Provide Access create permissions, or does Schedule book a meeting?">
    No. Both only confirm that you already did the work. Arrange the access or scheduling with your MSP first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where did my response go after I submitted it?">
    Click **Done** instead of **Pending** or **Open**, then open the item. It shows the saved response and completion details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I reject, undo, or edit an approval?">
    No. The dialog has only the confirm button and **Cancel**. Ask your MSP about a disagreement or correction.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does completing a project action close its linked PSA ticket?">
    No. It records completion of the project item only. A linked ticket or phase keeps its own status.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
