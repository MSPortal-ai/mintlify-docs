**Projects > Dispatch** shows MSP staff who is working on what, by day, across every open project in the selected company scope. Technicians run down the side and working days run across the top. One toolbar holds the date range, the filters and the planning actions. Drag work onto a technician's day to book them in your PSA, and the booking is written right away. Native MSPortal projects keep a plan draft that you save with **Save**. The page replaces the Schedule workspace window. **Plan schedule**, **Plan in schedule workspace** and a task's **Reschedule** now open it.

You do not have to pick a project first. The page opens on every open project in your company scope. Narrow the **Projects** filter to one project when you want to plan it with Smart Schedule.

## Before you start

- You need MSP staff access with the Projects **Schedule** view permission, and the scheduling feature must be enabled for your portal. Without both, the **Dispatch** tab does not appear. Company users never see it.
- Booking, and changing dates or people, also needs project write permission. Without it, the board, the queue and the task editor are read-only, and Smart Schedule and **Schedule settings…** do not appear.
- The board follows the company selector. Pick a company, or **Global View**, before you open it. Only open projects load. When you arrive from a project or a task, that project is loaded whatever the selector says.
- Bookings and busy time come from ConnectWise schedule entries, Autotask service calls and Halo appointments.
- Booking needs **Manage** on the Projects permission or **All appointments** on the Calendar permission. If your role has neither, the board says **PSA bookings are hidden because your role needs Projects > Manage or Calendar > All appointments. Task dates and owners still show.** and does not book. See [Calendar visibility](/user-guides/ticketing/change-management#calendar-visibility).

## Use the toolbar

On a desktop everything sits in one row, and it wraps when the window is narrow. On a phone the toolbar wraps to two rows, and the four filters move into a **Filters** drawer. See [Filter on a phone](#filter-on-a-phone).

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Previous**, **Today**, **Next** | Move the board by the visible range. **Today** returns to the current week. The date range for the visible days sits beside the arrows. |
| **Week**, **2 weeks**, **6 weeks** | Set the visible range. The page starts on **Week**. **6 weeks** shows one column per week instead of one per day. |
| **Technicians**, **Companies**, **Projects**, **Phases** | Show only matching work. The board and the queue both follow them. The lists come from the projects in scope. |
| **Overdue (N)** | Appears when N projects are past their due date and still have unscheduled work. Click it to keep only overdue projects. |
| **Clear** | Appears once any filter is set and removes them all, including **Projects**. |
| Bookings status icon | A spinner while PSA bookings load. An amber warning icon appears if they are hidden for your role (**PSA bookings are hidden because your role needs Projects > Manage or Calendar > All appointments. Task dates and owners still show.**) or could not be loaded (**PSA bookings could not be loaded. Task dates and owners still show.**), and shows the reason. |
| **Status colours** | Opens the legend of ticket statuses in scope, most common first, with their colours. |

When you narrow **Projects** to exactly one project, that project becomes the one you are planning. The toolbar then adds **View**, **Smart Schedule** and a **More planner actions** menu (**...**) after the **Status colours** button. See [Plan one project](#plan-one-project).

The page remembers the range and the **Technicians**, **Companies**, **Phases** and **Overdue** filters for your portal in this browser. **Projects** always starts from the link you opened.

### Filter on a phone

On a phone the **Technicians**, **Companies**, **Projects** and **Phases** pickers are replaced by an icon button titled **Filters** at the end of the toolbar. A number on the button counts the options you have ticked. Tap it to open the **Filters** drawer. Each filter is a section with its own search, and the board and the queue update as you tick options. **Reset** clears the four filters, and **Apply Filters** closes the drawer. **Overdue (N)** stays in the toolbar and **Reset** leaves it as it is.


## Read the board

Each row is one technician in one PSA connection. The rows cover every task owner, everyone with a booking or busy time in the visible range, and the team of the project you are editing. A ConnectWise member who is listed in a ticket's Resources but has no portal login still gets a row, under their ConnectWise identifier. If two connected PSAs both have a member with the same number, each gets its own row. Dated work with nobody on it sits on an **Unassigned** row at the bottom.

Each column is one working day, or one week when the range is **6 weeks**. Today is highlighted and a heavier line marks each new week. Each cell lists that person's work for the day.

Every chip has two lines. The first is the task title. The second starts with the time for a timed booking, such as `9 AM – 1 PM`, then the company and the project, for example `Bluefin Dental · Clinic refresh`. When the project has no company, only the project name shows. Hover a chip to see the ticket number and title, the company and project, the status, and the source (hours for a task, or **Booked in ConnectWise** with the time or **All day** for a booking). Chips are coloured by ticket status, so a ticket still marked Need to Schedule stands out even once it is booked.

| Chip | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Task dates | A project task with a start and end date, on each working day it spans. The hover text shows the status and the hours. |
| Booking, with a calendar-check icon | A ConnectWise schedule entry, an Autotask service call or a Halo appointment on a project ticket or task, on each day it covers. The hover text reads **Booked in ConnectWise**, **Booked in Autotask** or **Booked in Halo PSA**, with **All day** for all-day bookings. A booking replaces the task's own date chip on the same days, so one day never shows the same work twice. |
| Booking with a spinner | The booking is being written to the PSA. The chip is dimmed and cannot be dragged until the PSA answers. |
| Dashed, faded booking with a clock icon | The PSA saved the booking, but MSPortal has not synced it yet. It is locked until the next sync brings it in. See [Unconfirmed bookings](#unconfirmed-bookings). |
| **Busy all day** or **Busy 2h** | That person's other PSA bookings, such as service tickets or internal activities, from the same PSA connection. No titles or details are shown. Hover for the number of bookings. On a **6 weeks** board it reads like **Busy 3 days**. |

If a person is listed in the ticket's ConnectWise Resources rather than as its Owner, the hover text says **Listed as a resource, not the owner**. A chip headed **Project outside the selected companies** is a booking on a project that is not loaded in this scope; it cannot be opened from here.

A cell shows three items. Click **+N more** to show the rest of that cell. On a **6 weeks** board each task or booking appears once per week, and a drop lands on the first working day of that week.

Below it, you set the dates, **Assigned person** and dependencies. The editor is the same as in the other views; see [Use the task editor](/user-guides/projects/plan-project-schedule#use-the-task-editor).

## Clear the Needs scheduling queue

**Needs scheduling** lists open work in scope that still has no date, no booking or no technician. It sits beside the board as a rail. Click **Hide queue** to collapse it to a narrow strip that shows only the count, and **Show queue** to bring it back.

<Frame caption="The queue collapsed to a narrow strip. It shows the count of 2 and the board takes the freed width.">
  <img src="/images/product/projects-dispatch-queue-collapsed-desktop.png" alt="Dispatch page with the Needs scheduling queue collapsed to a narrow strip showing the number 2, and the board widened to fill the page" width="1440" height="960" />
</Frame>

The queue is grouped by project and sorted by project due date, with undated projects last. Each group shows the project name, the number of tasks, the company, its due date or **No due date**, and **Overdue** in red when the project is past its due date.

Each row is one line with the task's status colour, the ticket number and title, and an icon for what is missing. A calendar icon with a cross means **No date**. A person icon with a cross means **No technician**. A row can show both. Hover a row for the status, the assigned person and the hours. Type in **Find work…** to filter the rows by task title, ticket number, project or company.

A PSA booking on or after today counts as both a date and a technician, so booked work leaves the queue even when the ticket's own dates and Owner are empty. When nothing is left, the queue reads **Every open task in this scope has a date and a technician.**

The **Technicians** filter keeps queue work assigned to those people, plus work with no technician. **Companies**, **Projects**, **Phases** and **Overdue (N)** narrow the queue the same way they narrow the board.

On a phone the queue sits above the board.


### Set real project dates in ConnectWise

The queue sorts by each project's Estimated End, which is its due date in MSPortal, and flags **Overdue** from it. When a project manager creates a project in ConnectWise, set a real Estimated Start and Estimated End instead of keeping the dates ConnectWise fills in. Placeholder dates push the project to the wrong place in the queue, and a project can show **Overdue** or **No due date** when it should not.

## Book technicians

On a ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo project, a booking is a real entry in your PSA, a ConnectWise schedule entry, an Autotask service call or a Halo appointment. Every booking change on the board is written to the PSA right away. It does not wait for **Save** and it is not part of the plan draft. Native MSPortal projects have no PSA, so they keep the draft (see [Date native project work by dragging](#date-native-project-work-by-dragging)).

You need project write permission, and either **Manage** on Projects or **All appointments** on the Calendar permission. Bookings can be all-day or timed, and can cover one day or several days or weeks.

### Book by dragging

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the work">
    Use a row in **Needs scheduling**, or a task chip that is already on the board. Completed tasks cannot be dragged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop it on a technician's day">
    Drop the work on that person's row, in the day you want. MSPortal books them all day on that day. The chip shows a spinner while the PSA saves it, then a calendar-check icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it if needed">
    Open the task and use its **Bookings** section for a timed booking, a different person or a longer span. See [Book from the task sheet](#book-from-the-task-sheet).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="After the drop, Install core switches sits on Priya Shah's Wednesday with a calendar-check icon and has left the queue.">
  <img src="/images/product/projects-dispatch-booking-created-desktop.png" alt="Dispatch page after dropping Install core switches on Priya Shah's Wednesday, showing the booking chip with a calendar-check icon and the Needs scheduling count down to 1" width="1440" height="960" />
</Frame>

Where a drop lands depends on who is already booked on the task.

- If that person is already booked on the task on that day, nothing changes.
- If that person has exactly one booking on the task, on other days, that booking moves to start on the day you dropped on. Dragging a task chip never gives one person a second booking on the same task.
- If that person has no booking on the task, or several, a new booking is made.

You can only book someone who is a member of the project's PSA connection. Dropping on the **Unassigned** row shows **Drop the work on a technician's row to book it.** Dropping on a row that is not linked to that PSA shows **{name} is not linked to a {provider} member, so they cannot be booked.** The person does not need to be on the project's team.

### Move or reassign a booking

Drag a booking chip (one with a calendar-check icon) to another day or another technician's row.

- Moving to another day shifts the booking by the number of days between the day you grabbed it and the day you dropped on. It keeps its length and its times, and a multi-day booking keeps its span.
- Moving to another technician's row hands the booking to that person.
- On a **6 weeks** board, a booking moves by whole weeks.

Dragging a task chip moves that person's single booking instead of adding a second one, as described above.

### Book from the task sheet

Open a ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo task. The **Bookings** section is the first part of the side sheet. It shows the number of bookings, **Add booking** at the top right, and the line **Changes save to ConnectWise right away.** (or Autotask, or Halo PSA). Each booking lists the person, then the dates and the time range, or **All day**. A task with no bookings reads **No one is booked on this task yet.** A completed task cannot take new bookings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add or edit">
    Click **Add booking**, or the pencil icon on an existing booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Choose the **Person** (the project team and anyone already booked on the task). Leave **All day** on for whole working days, or turn it off to set **Start time** and **End time**. Set **Start date** and **End date**. The end date can be days or weeks after the start. A new booking starts on the task's start date, or today if that date has passed, and starts with the task's owner chosen when that person can be booked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Click **Save**. The form stays open if the PSA refuses the booking, and shows the PSA's reason. **Choose a person to book.** appears when no person is chosen and **The end must be after the start.** when the end is not later. **Cancel** closes the form without writing anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete a booking">
    Click **Delete** on the booking. The row asks you to choose **Keep** or **Delete**. After **Delete**, **Undo** stays available for a short time before the booking is removed from the PSA.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Unconfirmed bookings

Sometimes the PSA saves a booking but MSPortal cannot confirm it yet. The chip stays dashed and faded with a clock icon, and the task sheet shows **Waiting for the next ConnectWise sync** (or Autotask, or Halo PSA) beside it. You cannot drag or edit it. **Do not book the person again.** The booking appears as a normal chip after the next sync, which runs every 30 minutes for ConnectWise.

### When a booking fails

A one-line notice appears under the toolbar and the board rolls the change back. If the PSA rejected the booking, the notice ends with the PSA's own reason. See [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting) for each notice.

## Date native project work by dragging

Native MSPortal projects have no PSA to book in, so a drag edits the plan draft. If your role cannot book on a ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo project (no write permission, or neither **Manage** on Projects nor **All appointments**), the board does not book either, and drags edit the draft the same way.

Drag a queue row or a chip onto a technician's day. The task starts on that day and keeps its duration in working days. You can drag work from any project you can edit.

- When the person is on the project team, the task is assigned to them.
- When they are not, the dates are set, the assigned person is left unchanged, and a one-line notice says so.
- Drop onto the **Unassigned** row to date the work and clear its person.

Dragging work from another project switches the page to that project, as long as the project you were editing has no unsaved changes. If it does, the drop is refused and the page says **Save or discard changes to {project} first**, naming the project you were editing.

Completed and anchored tasks cannot be dragged in the draft. Dragging also pauses while a Smart Schedule proposal is open, and a drop is refused while a date change in the task editor is waiting for **Apply to draft**.

## Save or discard changes

Only the plan draft waits for **Save**. That covers native project edits, task dates and people changed in the task editor, and Smart Schedule proposals. PSA bookings never wait. While you have draft changes, the right side of the toolbar shows **N unsaved changes**, **Discard** and **Save**. When the **Projects** filter is not on the project you edited, the count names it, for example `Server refresh: 3 unsaved changes`.

**Save** stays disabled, with a tooltip, while a task date change is waiting for **Apply to draft**, while a Smart Schedule proposal is open (**Accept or reject the proposal first**), or while schedule checks are blocking the save. Saving shows **Saving…**.

## What saving writes to ConnectWise

For a ConnectWise project, **Save** writes each changed task's dates to the project ticket's Schedule Start and Schedule End fields and its person to the ticket's Owner. Bookings are separate. They are written when you drag or use the **Bookings** section, and they do not need **Save**. MSPortal writes only the booking, not the ticket's Schedule Start, Schedule End or Owner.

Autotask and Halo projects sync dates and assigned people as described in [Save your plan](/user-guides/projects/plan-project-schedule#save-your-plan). Their bookings (Autotask service calls and Halo appointments) are written immediately, like ConnectWise schedule entries.

## Where ConnectWise dates and people come from

ConnectWise keeps a project ticket's planned dates, its people and its actual schedule in different places, and MSPortal shows each of them from its own source.

| In MSPortal | ConnectWise source | Who fills it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task start and end dates | The project ticket's Schedule Start and Schedule End fields | ConnectWise does not fill these itself. They are empty on every ConnectWise project ticket MSPortal has synced, in every ConnectWise portal. MSPortal writes them when you click **Save**. |
| Assigned person | The ticket's Owner field | Someone sets it by hand on the ticket. |
| Resources | The ticket's Resources field | ConnectWise fills it when a member is scheduled on the ticket. It can also be edited by hand. |
| Booking | A schedule entry on the ticket, made in its Resources & Meetings pod, the Dispatch Portal, **Schedule Me** or on this board | The dispatcher or technician. MSPortal syncs schedule entries every 30 minutes, and a booking made on this board is written to ConnectWise at once. |

Logging time on a ticket never sets its Owner, its Resources or its dates.

The board shows a schedule entry as a booking, and a booking on or after today takes the ticket out of **Needs scheduling**. To book work from MSPortal, drag the card onto the technician's day. The booking saves to ConnectWise right away, with no **Save** step. See [Book technicians](#book-technicians).

## Leave a task out of scheduling

**Include in scheduling** is an MSPortal-only switch on each task, under **Visibility and scheduling** in the task's details. It is on by default. Admins set the default per task type under **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Task types**, and a single task can override its type.

When it is off, the task is left out of this page, the project Gantt and Smart Schedule. The task itself, its bookings and its dependency links are kept, and they come back when you turn the switch on again. Nothing is written to your PSA either way. When a project has excluded tasks, **Schedule settings…** says how many, for example **3 tasks are excluded from scheduling.** See [Task types and individual task controls](/user-guides/settings/projects#task-types-and-individual-task-controls).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| The **Dispatch** tab is missing | You need MSP staff access with the Projects **Schedule** view permission, and the scheduling feature must be enabled for your portal. |
| Nothing can be dragged | You need project write permission, and the project must be a ConnectWise, Autotask, Halo or native project. Completed tasks never move. For a native project, or when the board cannot book, anchored tasks never move either, and an open Smart Schedule proposal pauses dragging. |
| A drop dates the task in the draft and does not book anyone | The board books only on ConnectWise, Autotask and Halo projects, and only when your role has project write permission and either **Manage** on Projects or **All appointments**. Otherwise the drop edits the plan draft, and you need **Save**. |
| **Save or discard changes to {project} first** | You dragged native-project work from a different project while the project you were editing still has unsaved changes. Click **Save** or **Discard** in the toolbar, then drag again. |
| **Drop the work on a technician's row to book it.** | You dropped on the **Unassigned** row. Drop on a named technician instead. |
| **{name} is not linked to a {provider} member, so they cannot be booked.** | That row is not a member of the project's PSA connection. Book someone who is a member of that PSA. |
| **{provider} rejected the booking.** | The PSA refused the write and the board rolled the change back. The PSA's own reason follows the notice. Fix what it names, then try again. |
| **Booked in {provider}. It will appear after the next sync, so don't book it again.** | The PSA saved the booking but MSPortal cannot show it yet. The chip stays dashed and locked. Wait for the next sync. |
| **{provider} did not confirm this booking. Wait for the next sync before booking it again.** | The PSA did not answer, so the booking may exist. The chip stays dashed and locked. Check again after the next sync before booking. |
| **This {provider} booking also covers other people, tickets or tasks. Move it in {provider}.** | An Autotask service call can cover several people, tickets or tasks. MSPortal will not move or reassign a shared call. Change it in Autotask. |
| **You do not have permission to change bookings in {provider}.** | Your role cannot write bookings. You need project write permission and an active subscription, and Company users cannot book. |
| **{provider} could not be reached. Try again.** | The PSA did not respond. Wait a moment and repeat the change. |
| **The booking changed in {provider}. The board now shows the latest version.** | Someone changed the booking in the PSA after the board loaded. Check the refreshed chip, then repeat your change if you still need it. |
| **The ticket or booking no longer exists in {provider}.** or **That person cannot be booked in {provider}.** | The ticket or booking was removed in the PSA, or that member is not bookable there. Reload the board. |
| **View**, **Smart Schedule** and the **...** menu are missing | They appear only when **Projects** is narrowed to exactly one project. Smart Schedule and **Schedule settings…** also need project write permission. |
| **PSA bookings are hidden because your role needs Projects > Manage or Calendar > All appointments. Task dates and owners still show.** | Ask an admin for a role with **Manage** on Projects or **All appointments** on the Calendar permission. See [Calendar visibility](/user-guides/ticketing/change-management#calendar-visibility). The board does not book until then. |
| **PSA bookings could not be loaded. Task dates and owners still show.** | Reload the page. If it persists, check the PSA connection under **Settings > Integrations**. |
| The **Bookings** section is missing from the task sheet | It appears only for ConnectWise, Autotask and Halo tasks, and only when you can book (project write permission, and **Manage** on Projects or **All appointments**). It is not shown for native projects. |
| **No technicians to show for these filters.** | Widen the range, click **Clear**, check the company selector, and check that the work has dates or a booking. |
| A technician is missing from the rows | Rows come from work in scope and the team of the project you are editing. There is no full staff roster on the board. Give them dated work, or open a task on their project so its team loads. |
| A chip is headed **Project outside the selected companies** | The booked ticket belongs to a project outside the loaded scope. Change the company selector. |
| A task sits under the wrong person | A chip follows the ticket's Owner, or its Resources when there is no Owner. Change **Assigned person** in the task editor, or fix Resources in ConnectWise. |
| **Showing the first 1,000 projects. Select companies to narrow the list.** | The page loads at most 1,000 open projects. Pick one or more companies in the company selector. |
| A project shows **Overdue** or **No due date** unexpectedly | The queue reads the project's Estimated End from ConnectWise. See [Set real project dates in ConnectWise](#set-real-project-dates-in-connectwise). |

    The list holds open projects in the selected company scope. Change the company selector, then reopen the page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I plan more than one project at once?">
    You can drag and edit work from any project, but Smart Schedule, the views and the schedule settings work on one project at a time. Narrow **Projects** to that project first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Company users open Dispatch?">
    No. Company users read shared dates in [Read a project schedule](/user-guides/projects/read-project-schedule).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does dragging write to ConnectWise?">
    Yes, and immediately. Dropping work on a technician's day creates a ConnectWise schedule entry for them, and dragging a booking moves it, with no **Save** step. Autotask service calls and Halo appointments work the same way. Native MSPortal projects have no PSA, so they keep the plan draft and **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Autotask service calls and Halo appointments on the board?">
    Yes. The board shows ConnectWise schedule entries, Autotask service calls and Halo appointments as bookings, and each PSA's other bookings as busy time. Busy time counts only within one PSA connection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I book someone for several days, or for certain hours?">
    Yes. Open the task and use **Add booking** in the **Bookings** section. Leave **All day** on for whole days, or turn it off to set start and end times. The end date can be days or weeks after the start.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I move or edit a booking?">
    A dashed, faded chip is waiting for the next sync, and a chip with a spinner is still being saved. Wait for them to finish. An Autotask service call that also covers other people, tickets or tasks cannot be moved here. Change it in Autotask.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
