This page shows Company users and MSP staff how to read a project's tickets as cards on **Project Plan > Board**. This is the ticket board inside one project. The **Projects > Kanban** view shows whole projects.

## Before you start

- Your role needs the project Kanban view permission, and your MSP must turn the board on in project settings.
- A project with no phases and at most one ticket uses the simpler Client Dashboard instead.
- Company users should ask their MSP about missing access. The MSP manages permissions.

## Find a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project's board">
    Open **Projects**, click the project, then click **Project Plan** and **Board**. Read the column headings before you judge where a card sits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search the cards">
    Type a ticket title, number, or owner's name in **Search tickets...**. For a number such as **#633**, search **633** without the hash. Search ignores capitals and covers only the tickets on this board, not descriptions or support tickets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check closed-ticket visibility">
    The eye button starts on **Show All**. Click it to switch to **Hidden**, which hides closed tickets. Click again to show them. It does not change any ticket's status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the matching cards">
    Each card shows the ticket number, summary, owner, and hours. In **Comfortable** or **Compact** density, cards also show status and priority badges. If a ticket seems missing, clear the search and set the eye button back to **Show All**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click a card to open its ticket details. What you see depends on your permissions and PSA. To report a new issue, start with the [Home AI Assistant ticket guide](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket).

## Group the same tickets differently

Click **Group by** and pick a layout.

| Group | What the columns represent |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | The MSP's configured status groups, using mappings from individual ticket statuses. A column heading can differ from the card's status badge. |
| **Member**, **Resource**, **Agent**, or **Technician** | The ticket owner. The label follows the portal's PSA terminology. Unassigned tickets appear separately. The MSP's roster configuration can affect the available columns. |
| **Priority** | Available priority values and the values on the project's tickets. Missing priority can appear as Unassigned. |

<img src="/images/product/project-ticket-group-options.png" alt="Project ticket Group by menu with Status, Resource, and Priority" width="260" height="204" />

This capture uses the label **Resource**. Your portal may use another owner label. Changing **Group by** only changes your view.

**Other** in Status view contains tickets that do not fit an available configured group. It is not a failure status. Ask the MSP to check status mappings if a ticket is unexpectedly there.

An empty column does not mean the project has no tickets.

## Choose card density and scroll

Click **Density** and choose **Comfortable**, **Compact**, or **Dense**. **Dense** uses smaller cards and hides the status and priority badges. Use **Comfortable** or **Compact** to see them.

<img src="/images/product/project-ticket-density-options.png" alt="Project ticket Density menu with Comfortable, Compact, and Dense" width="294" height="204" />

Density changes only how cards look. Your search, grouping, density, and eye-button choices apply to the current board view and may reset when you leave it.

Scroll sideways to see more columns. **Next** and **Previous** arrows appear at the edge when more columns are available. Long columns also scroll up and down.

## Understand counts and hours

Each column header shows the number of matching cards and their total actual hours. When a budget total exists, hours show as **actual/budget h**. The totals follow your search and the eye button.

Hours are not percent complete or remaining time. Missing hours count as zero, so a zero total does not prove nobody worked. A card leaves out its hours row when actual hours are unavailable.

## MSP editing boundaries

Company users use this board to read shared work. MSP staff with the right permissions can drag cards for supported ConnectWise, Autotask, and Halo tickets.

- Status changes need status-edit access.
- Owner and priority changes (ConnectWise and Autotask only) need project write access.
- A move can write back to your PSA.

For native MSPortal projects, use **Project Plan > Tasks > Edit ticket** to change status, priority, or assignee. The native board has no drag controls. For Halo or Syncro boards without them, change the work in your PSA.

The **Log time** icon on a card opens the time form. It records nothing until you save the form, and it needs permission to log time.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Board** is missing | Check project access, the Kanban view permission, and that your MSP turned the board on. Small projects use the Client Dashboard. |
| A ticket is missing | Clear **Search tickets...** and set the eye button to **Show All**. |
| A ticket shows under **Other** | Its status is not in a configured group. Ask your MSP to check the status mappings. |
| Counts or hours dropped | Totals follow your search and the eye button. Clear them before you compare. |
| Badges disappeared | **Dense** hides them. Switch to **Comfortable** or **Compact**. |
| I cannot drag a card | You need edit permission, and your PSA and grouping must support the change. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Kanban missing from my project?">
    Check project access, the Kanban view permission, and whether the MSP enabled the board. Projects with no phases and at most one ticket use Client Dashboard. Company users need the MSP to manage their permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I find a completed ticket?">
    Return the eye control to Show All and clear or adjust Search tickets. Hidden excludes closed tickets even when a search would otherwise match them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is an Open ticket under Not Started?">
    Status columns are configured groups. Several provider statuses can map to one group; read the individual badge for the ticket's supplied status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the board say Other?">
    The ticket's status does not fit an available group or mapping. Other is a grouping fallback, not a new PSA status or proof of a failed ticket.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does switching Resource or Priority change any tickets?">
    No. Group by changes the layout. An authorized MSP drag operation can change the underlying assignment or priority; switching the selector does not.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did the ticket counts and hours decrease?">
    Column totals sum the cards matching the current search and closed-ticket filter. Clear the search and return to Show All before comparing the whole board.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did the status and priority badges disappear?">
    Dense hides those badges. Choose Comfortable or Compact to show available badges again. Missing source values can also leave a badge absent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I move native project tickets on this board?">
    The current board does not enable native drag controls. MSP editors should use Phases and Edit ticket. Native work remains in MSPortal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I read cards but not drag them?">
    Reading and editing have separate permissions. Company readers cannot manage project work. MSP drag controls also depend on the provider, the selected grouping, loaded assignment options, and whether another operation is running.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does 0.0h mean no work has happened?">
    No. Zero can reflect missing or restricted hours, the current filters, or genuinely zero supplied hours. Ask the MSP for an appropriate progress summary rather than treating it as evidence of inactivity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
