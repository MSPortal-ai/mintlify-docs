**Projects > Schedule** shows dated work from your projects on a calendar. This page shows Company users and MSP staff how to move around it, change the grouping, and reschedule items. For what each item means, see [Read a project schedule](/user-guides/projects/read-project-schedule). For search and filters, see [Filter the project schedule](/user-guides/projects/filter-project-schedule).

## Before you start

- You need the **Schedule** view permission for Projects.
- The calendar shows only items that have dates. Work without dates, or work your PSA has not synced, does not appear.
- Company users see the milestones their MSP shares. MSP staff see project spans, phases, tasks, milestones, and Autotask service calls. A single project's own **Schedule** tab can show more dated work, as allowed by your MSP's client display settings.

## Move around the calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Schedule">
    Pick a company in the company selector, then open **Projects** and click **Schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the period">
    Click **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, or **Year**. Use the previous and next arrows to move one period, and click **Today** to jump back. You can also press the left and right arrow keys, and **T** for today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the grouping">
    Click **Projects**, **Tasks**, or **Start dates** in the toolbar. See [Group the schedule](#group-the-schedule).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the work">
    Click the search (magnifier) icon and type a project, company, or outcome. For more options, see [Filter the project schedule](/user-guides/projects/filter-project-schedule).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an item">
    Click an item. Projects, phases, and milestones open the project, on the tab that holds the item. Tasks and Autotask service calls open the ticket details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In Month view, each day lists its items as lines with a colored dot, and timed items show their start time first. When a day has more items than fit, click **+N more** to see the rest.

## Group the schedule

Click **Projects**, **Tasks**, or **Start dates** in the toolbar. **Projects** is the starting choice, and your browser remembers the one you pick.

| Grouping | What appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Projects** | One line per project for each run of consecutive days with scheduled work, for example "Office rollout · 20 items". Hover to see the first item titles. A project with no scheduled work in the period shows its date span. |
| **Tasks** | Every project span, phase, task, milestone, and service call on its own line. |
| **Start dates** | Each project once, on its start date. Tasks, phases, and end markers are left out. Upcoming opportunities still appear when you include them. |

**Projects** and **Start dates** lines are summaries, so they have no **Reschedule** option. Switch to **Tasks** to change one item's dates.

## Reschedule an item

MSP staff can change dates from the calendar. Company users cannot, so they should ask their MSP.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Tasks grouping">
    Click **Tasks** in the toolbar, so each item has its own line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the item menu">
    In Month or Week view, click the **...** menu on the item and choose **Reschedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the new dates">
    Check the item title. Set **New date range**, or **New milestone date** for a milestone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. Check the new date on the calendar and in the item's details. Click **Cancel** to leave the dates alone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What you can reschedule depends on the item and your permissions.

| Item | Who can reschedule it |
| --- | --- |
| Project, phase, or task from ConnectWise or Autotask | MSP staff with project write permission and an active subscription. The new dates are written to your PSA. |
| Project, phase, or task from Halo or a native MSPortal project | Only when the scheduling feature is enabled for your portal. Otherwise use the project's **Phases** or **Tasks** editor. |
| Milestone | MSP staff with project overlay-edit permission. This changes the milestone's planned date in MSPortal only. It does not move PSA tasks or complete the milestone. |
| Service call or upcoming opportunity | Not available from the calendar. |

When the scheduling feature is on, rescheduling a task opens the [Schedule workspace](/user-guides/projects/plan-project-schedule) instead of the date dialog.

## Review project teams and upcoming work (MSP staff)

Some ConnectWise portals have the **Project tracker** feature turned on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter by team">
    Click **Filters** and use **Project team member** and **Project team role** to find projects by who works on them. Use **Status** to narrow by project status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a project's team">
    Click a project's end marker on the calendar. Its title starts with **Project ends**. The **Project team** window shows the company, status, start and end dates, days until the end, and the people assigned. Click **Open project** for the full workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include upcoming work">
    In the filter bar, click **Upcoming** to see only upcoming work, or **All** to see it with open and closed projects. Use **Sales stage** in **Filters** to narrow it. You need planner read access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an upcoming item">
    Click an **Upcoming** item on the calendar to see its company, opportunity type, sales stage, status, sales rep, date, and estimated revenue. Click **Open item** to go to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Upcoming work shows open sales opportunities that match your rules, planner items in the statuses you choose, and projects marked upcoming. Only deals for companies already imported into MSPortal appear. An admin sets the rules under **Settings > Projects > Upcoming projects**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The calendar is empty | Check the date range, the company selector, the search, the **Staff** choice, **Hide items**, and the **Open / Closed / All** state. Items without a start date do not appear. |
| A day shows **+N more** | Click it to see the rest, or switch to Week view. |
| One project hides its tasks | The **Projects** grouping combines them. Switch to **Tasks**. |
| **Reschedule** is missing | Use the **Tasks** grouping. Then check that you are MSP staff, that you have the edit permission, and that your PSA supports date changes for that item type. |
| **Upcoming** or the team filters are missing | They need the Project tracker feature, a ConnectWise connection, MSP staff access, and for **Upcoming**, planner read access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I see more on the calendar than a Company user?">
    Company users see shared milestones. MSP staff also see project spans, phases, tasks, and service calls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a project date span mean a technician is booked?">
    No. A span shows planned dates. Only an Autotask service call is a timed booking.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does rescheduling a milestone change my PSA?">
    No. It changes the milestone's planned date in MSPortal only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
