This page shows Company users and MSP staff how to find work on the project calendar by searching, filtering, and hiding items. The controls only narrow items you can already read. For the difference between what Company users and MSP staff see, start with [Read a project schedule](/user-guides/projects/read-project-schedule).

## Before you start

- You need the **Schedule** view permission for Projects.
- **Projects > Schedule** (all projects) and a project's own **Project Plan > Schedule** tab have different toolbars. This page covers both.
- Search and filters apply to the items loaded for the period on screen, so move to the expected dates first.

## Find work in Projects > Schedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the dates">
    Click **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, or **Year** and use the arrows or **Today**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Click the search (magnifier) icon and type a project, company, or outcome. The icon turns teal while a search is active. Search does not look inside ticket descriptions or notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the project state and manager">
    Click **Open**, **Closed**, or **All** in the filter bar. MSP staff with the Project tracker also see **Upcoming**. Use **Project Manager** to pick one or more leads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick staff">
    Click **Staff** and tick the people. The calendar keeps items assigned to any ticked person. Milestones and many project and phase spans have no assignee, so they disappear when you pick a person. The **Staff** list comes from the loaded items, not from your full user directory. When the Project tracker is on, use **Project team member** in **Filters** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use more filters">
    Click **Filters** to filter by **Status**, **Health Status**, **Exclude company**, and other fields. Click **Apply Filters** to use your choices. See [Filter and sort projects](/user-guides/projects/filter-projects).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find work in a project's Schedule tab

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    Open the project and click **Project Plan**, then **Schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type in **Search events...**. It matches item titles, owners, and company names.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick staff">
    Click **Staff** to keep items assigned to the people you tick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open more filters">
    Click the **Filter** icon for **Project**, **Companies**, and **Assigned staff**, then click **Apply Filters**. Closing the panel with X keeps your choices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Hide or show all-day items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Filters panel">
    In **Projects > Schedule**, click **Filters**. In a project's Schedule tab, use **Hide items** in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn all-day items on or off">
    In **Projects > Schedule**, use the **Show all-day events** switch. In a project's Schedule tab, open **Hide items** and tick **Hide all-day events**. Project spans, phases, tasks, and milestones are all-day items. Timed service calls stay visible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This choice is saved in this browser and shared by both Schedule views.

## Hide items by title

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Hide items">
    In **Projects > Schedule**, click **Filters** and find **Hide items** near the top. In a project's Schedule tab, click **Hide items** in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the titles">
    Tick a title to hide it. The change applies right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show a title again">
    Open **Hide items** and untick the title. Hidden titles stay in the list even when they have no items in the current period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Hiding matches the title text, ignoring capitalization and surrounding spaces, so it can hide items with the same title in other projects. It does not delete work, change what clients see, or update your PSA. Hidden titles are saved in this browser and shared by both Schedule views.

## Show weekends

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Filters panel">
    Use **Week** view. In **Projects > Schedule**, click **Filters**. In a project's Schedule tab, click the **Filter** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on weekends">
    Turn on **Enable Saturday / Sunday**. Week view shows weekdays only until you do. Month view always shows the full week.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Save and reopen schedule filters

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the schedule">
    Choose the grouping, the calendar view, and your filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Filters**, then **Save Filter** (or **Saved filters**, then **Save New Filter**). Name it and click the check mark. Saving with an existing name replaces that filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reopen">
    Click **Filters**, then **Saved filters**, then the name. It replaces your current choices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A saved filter is private to your account and works on any device. It stores:

- The **Projects**, **Tasks**, or **Start dates** grouping
- The Day, Week, Month, or Year view
- The project state, search, health, manager, date, and custom-field filters
- Team roles, members, statuses, and sales stages
- All-day, weekend, and hidden-title choices

A filter saved from **Cards**, **List**, or **Kanban** has no calendar options, so opening it in Schedule keeps your current grouping and view. See [Filter and sort projects](/user-guides/projects/filter-projects#save-and-reopen-filters).

## What clearing does

| Control | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Pressing **Esc**, or **Reset** then **Apply Filters** in **Filters** | Clears the search and project filters. **Reset** alone keeps the search. |
| **Hide items**, all-day, and weekend choices | Kept. Change each one yourself. |
| The period and the Day, Week, Month, or Year view | Kept. Use the arrows, **Today**, or the view buttons. |

The schedule remembers its view in this browser. If you come back on the same day it can restore the date you were on. On a later day it opens on today but keeps your view.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The calendar is empty | Check the dates and the company selector. Clear the search and filters. Make sure all-day events are shown. Open **Hide items** and untick matching titles. Include weekends if the work falls on one. |
| Clearing filters did not bring an item back | Clearing keeps **Hide items** and the period. Untick the title in **Hide items** and move to the right dates. |
| Picking a staff member removed milestones | Staff filtering keeps only items with an assignee. Milestones and many spans have none. |
| A colleague is missing from **Staff** | The list comes from the loaded items' assignees. Check the period and the item assignments. |
| Hiding one title hid another project's item | **Hide items** matches by title, so items with the same title hide together. |
| The page shows a load error | Click **Retry** before treating an empty calendar as real. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Hide items remove work for my company?">
    No. It is a display choice in your browser. It does not delete anything or change what clients see.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I search ticket history here?">
    No. Search matches item titles and project, owner, and company text. Open the ticket for its history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Apply Filters to keep a selection?">
    Hide items, the all-day switch, and the weekend switch apply as you change them. Other filters in the drawer apply when you click **Apply Filters**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the calendar open on a different month sometimes?">
    The view is remembered, and the date resumes only on the same day. Click **Today** to jump back at any time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
