Use these controls in **Projects > Schedule** or a project's **Schedule** tab. They narrow dated items you are already allowed to read. For the difference between Company and MSP calendar contents, start with [Read a project schedule](/user-guides/projects/read-project-schedule).

**Audience:** Company users with project Schedule access and MSP staff.

## Find a scheduled item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the date range">
    Select Day, Week, Month, or Year and use the previous/next arrows. Search and selector options apply to loaded calendar items, so move to the expected date first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    In **Projects > Schedule**, select the magnifier icon and type a project, company, or outcome. The icon turns teal while a search is active. In a project's **Schedule** tab, enter an item title, owner, or company name in **Search events...**. Search does not look through every project ticket's description or note history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow by project, company, or staff">
    Open **Filter** for **Project**, **Companies**, and **Staff**. Project selects one project at a time. The toolbar also has a Staff selector. An empty selection leaves that filter unrestricted; multiple selected staff match an item associated with any selected person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and close the filter panel">
    Filter selections change the view immediately. **Apply Filters** closes the panel; closing it with X does not discard selections. Use **Reset** to clear ordinary filters when needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Restore all-day items

The calendar-off icon toggles **Hide all-day events** and **Show all-day events**. Hiding all-day events removes project spans, phases, tasks, and milestones from the view; timed service calls can remain. Choose **Show all-day events** to restore them.

This preference is saved in this browser and shared by the two Projects schedule views. **Reset** turns all-day items back on.

## Hide or restore items by title

In **Projects > Schedule**, open **Filter** and use **Hide items** at the top of the panel. In a project's **Schedule** tab, **Hide items** is in the toolbar. Select a title to hide it; reopen the selector and deselect the title to restore it. Selection applies immediately, without **Apply Filters**.

This hides every loaded item with that title, ignoring capitalization and surrounding spaces. It can affect matching titles in different projects. It does not delete work, change client visibility, or update the PSA.

Hidden titles persist in this browser and are shared between **Projects > Schedule** and individual project Schedule tabs. **Reset does not clear Hide items.** Previously hidden titles remain available in the selector even outside their original period, so you can restore them there.

## Show weekends

In **Week** view, open **Filter** and enable **Saturday / Sunday** in the Week section. Week view initially shows weekdays. This preference is shared with other calendar views in this browser; **Reset** returns Week to weekdays. Month view continues to show the full week.

## Save and reopen schedule filters

Open **Filter** and use **Saved filters** to keep a schedule setup under a name. A saved filter is private to your account and opens on any device.

A saved filter stores:

- The **Projects**, **Tasks**, or **Start dates** grouping
- The Month, Week, Day, or Year view
- The project state, search, health, project manager, date and custom field filters
- Team roles, members, statuses and upcoming stages
- Calendar display options, including all-day items, weekends and hidden titles

Opening a saved filter replaces the current selections. Saving with an existing name replaces that saved filter. Filters saved before grouping and view were stored keep your current grouping and view.

The same saved filters are available in **Cards**, **List** and **Kanban**. A filter saved from one of those tabs has no calendar display options, grouping or view, so opening it here keeps your current calendar setup. See [Filter and sort projects](/user-guides/projects/filter-projects#save-and-reopen-filters).

## What Reset does

| Control | Result of Reset |
| --- | --- |
| Search, Project, Companies, Staff | Cleared. |
| All-day visibility | All-day events shown again. |
| Week weekends | Disabled. |
| Hide items | Retained; unhide titles separately. |
| Selected period and Day/Week/Month/Year | Retained; use arrows, Today, or the view buttons. |

The Projects schedule remembers its view in this browser. Returning on the same calendar day can restore the date you were viewing; on a later day it returns to the current date while keeping the view choice. These preferences are separate from the main Calendar's selected date/view, though weekend visibility is shared.

## Empty-calendar checklist

1. Check the expected date and company scope.
2. Clear Search events and reset ordinary filters.
3. Confirm all-day events are shown.
4. Open Hide items and deselect matching titles.
5. In Week view, include weekends if the work falls there.
6. Check source start dates, unusually long spans, and the Company/MSP access differences in the [schedule reader](/user-guides/projects/read-project-schedule).

The Filter badge does not count every saved view choice. A zero badge does not prove there are no hidden titles. If loading failed, use the displayed retry control before treating the empty grid as a valid result.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why did Reset not bring my item back?">
    Reset preserves Hide items and the selected date/view. Unhide the title separately and navigate to the correct period. Also check sharing and the source start date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Hide items remove work for my company?">
    No. It is a browser display preference, not a deletion or client-sharing control. It hides matching titles in your Projects schedule views.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did hiding one title affect another project?">
    Hide items matches titles rather than individual record IDs. Items with the same title are hidden together, ignoring case and surrounding spaces.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did selecting Staff remove milestones?">
    Staff filtering keeps items associated with a selected person. Milestones and many project/phase spans have no staff assignment, so they do not match.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my colleague missing from Staff?">
    The list comes from loaded project-item assignments, not every employee or project-team member. Check the period and source assignments; do not create a portal user just to change this filter.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I search an entire project's ticket history here?">
    No. Search filters loaded calendar item titles and available project, owner, company, or location text. Open ticket details for their history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Apply Filters to save a selection?">
    Selections apply as you change them. Apply Filters closes the panel; X also keeps changes. These display controls do not update project records.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the calendar remember a different month sometimes?">
    The view persists, and the chosen date can resume during the same calendar day. On a later day the calendar resumes the current date instead. Use Today whenever you want to return immediately.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
