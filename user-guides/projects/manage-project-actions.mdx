This page shows MSP staff how to create requests, assign people, notify them, and close items in a project's **Decisions** and **Action Items** lists.

## Before you start

- You need project access and permission to edit project collaboration content (project overlay edit). General project-write or status-edit permission is not enough. Your subscription must also allow writes.
- Company users answer their own assigned items with [Respond to project decisions and action items](/user-guides/projects/respond-to-project-actions).
- Adding an item creates it at once, and edits save as you make them. There is no Save or Publish button. Write text that suits the project's audience before you add it.
- The editor has no Client Visible switch for a single item.

## Create a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the right list">
    Open the project, click **Collaboration**, then **Decisions** for a decision or request, or **Action Items** for work to complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the item">
    Click the **+** button at the top of the list and choose **Add Action** (in **Decisions**) or **Add Action Item** (in **Action Items**). If the list is empty, click the button in the empty state. The item is created as **Pending** and opens on the right. Click **All** above the list if a filter hides it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the work">
    Replace the default **Title** and write a clear **Description**. In **Decisions**, pick a **Type**: **Approval Required**, **Question**, **Access Needed**, **Scheduling**, or **Decision Required**. **Action Items** are always the Action Item type and have no **Type** picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date and owners">
    Pick a **Due Date** if you need one. In **Assigned To**, search for Company users or staff. The picker marks them **Client** or **Staff**. You can pick several people. Hidden user records are left out, and searching needs user-read access. Turn on **Mark as Urgent** to highlight pending work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the saved item">
    Wait for text to finish saving, then click another item and back to check the title, description, date, and assignees. If you see a save error or someone else changed the same item, check it before you continue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Text fields save after a short pause. **Type**, **Status**, **Due Date**, **Assigned To**, and **Mark as Urgent** save as you change them. A blank title does not replace the last saved title.

## Check Company visibility

Project access and the source update decide what Company users see. Assigning only staff does not make a request private on a client-visible project. A decision linked to an internal weekly update can stay hidden from Company users even when you pick a Company assignee.

Check **Updates** and the project's visibility before you notify a Company user. Do not rely on assignment, or on not notifying, to control the audience.

## Notify assignees

Assigning someone does not notify them. After the assignment saves, use the **Notifications** panel in the item.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the channels">
    Tick **In-App**, **Email**, or both. **In-App** starts ticked and **Email** starts off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check who gets it">
    Review the assignees and the item text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notify">
    Click **Notify Assignee** or **Notify Assignees**. The panel then shows the result and **Last notified**. Delivery can work for some people or channels and fail for others.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In-app notices need a portal login, and email needs an email address. Hidden Company content is not sent to Company assignees. The result shows what the application sent, not whether anyone read it. Clicking Notify again sends another notice, so check the result first. An email opens the project, and the person may need to open **Decisions** or **Action Items** to find the request.

If someone is missing from **Assigned To**, ask an admin to check that they are not hidden, that a Company user belongs to this project's company, and that your role can read users. A blank search can also come from a lookup error.

## Completion, cancellation, and removal

An assigned person completes an item with the action button at the top of the item (for example **Approve** or **Answer**). One assignee completing it resolves it for everyone. Questions need text. See the [response guide](/user-guides/projects/respond-to-project-actions).

You can also set **Status** to **Completed** or **Cancelled** yourself. That is an admin change, not proof that an assignee approved. A completed item shows **Response** and completion details. If you set a completed item back to **Pending**, it can keep its old response and completion details. Check them before you treat it as new.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find cancelled items">
    Click **All** above the list. **Done** shows completed items only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete an item">
    Click the item, click **Delete** at the top, and confirm. There is no undo. If you see an error, check the list to see what was actually saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decisions linked to weekly updates

**Updates > Decisions Needed** and the Decisions register are connected. New pending Decision Required or Approval Required items can be added to the latest existing update, or linked to an existing matching decision. Questions, access requests, scheduling requests, and Action Items do not automatically follow that same new-decision behavior.

Changing or resolving a linked decision can change or remove matching **Decisions Needed** text. Matching uses the decision title, so repeated titles in different updates change together. Check **Updates** after you rename, complete, cancel, or delete a linked decision. Give different decisions different titles.

These items live in MSPortal. A linked phase or ticket is only context. Editing a request does not edit or close the PSA record.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add Action** is missing | You need the project overlay-edit permission and an active subscription. Status-edit or project-write access alone is not enough. |
| I cannot find a new item | Click **All** above the list and clear **Search**. |
| Someone is missing from **Assigned To** | See the notes under **Notify assignees** above. |
| Assigning did not send anything | Assigning never notifies. Use **Notify Assignee** in the item. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Add Action open an unsaved draft?">
    No. It creates a pending item at once, and edits save as you type. Check the project and audience first. There is no Publish step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will assigning a user send an email?">
    No. Pick the channels and click **Notify Assignee** after the assignment saves. **Email** starts off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I edit project status but not add an action?">
    Editing decisions and action items has its own permission. Ask an admin to review your role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I keep an item private by assigning only staff?">
    No. Assignment does not control the audience. Check the project's visibility and any linked weekly update. There is no per-item visibility switch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a decision also appear in Updates?">
    Pending decisions and approvals stay in sync with **Decisions Needed** in a weekly update. Check both after you edit or resolve them, especially with repeated titles.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does marking an item Completed prove the client approved it?">
    No. An MSP editor can set **Status** directly. Check the saved response and completion details, and use the assigned person's response when you need their confirmation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
