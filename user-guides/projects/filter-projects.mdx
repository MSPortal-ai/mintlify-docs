This page shows how to narrow the project list by category, open or closed state, manager, date, status, and health, and how to save a filter for later. It is for Company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- The controls work in **Projects > Cards**, **List**, and the portfolio **Kanban**. They only narrow projects you can already read.
- **Schedule** and the MSP **Dashboard** share most of these filters. See [Filter the project schedule](/user-guides/projects/filter-project-schedule).
- The category dropdown, onboarding filters, and **Upcoming** state are MSP staff features.

## Choose projects or onboarding projects

<Note>
Branch preview: the category filter is part of the unified Projects update and appears when it is enabled for your portal.
</Note>

Open the category dropdown in the filter bar and pick one option.

- **All** includes both categories.
- **Projects** shows projects without a linked onboarding portal.
- **Onboarding projects** shows projects linked to a client onboarding portal. An onboarding project type alone does not include a project.

The selection filters your current view and follows the company selector. **Global View** includes linked onboarding projects across every company you can access. Selecting an onboarding category or criterion sets **Open / Closed / All** to **All**; you can choose **Open** or **Closed** afterwards. It does not open another page. Hover over a filter-bar control to see what it does.

## Follow up on onboarding

On the MSP **Dashboard**, Projects and Onboarding metrics share one summary card. Select an onboarding metric to open the regular **List** with its matching criterion:

| Summary metric | Projects shown |
| --- | --- |
| **Active onboardings** | Projects linked to active onboarding portals. |
| **Waiting on client** | Projects with open client tasks. The summary counts tasks; the list shows each matching project once. |
| **Never opened portal** | Projects whose portals have invited contacts who have never signed in. The summary counts contacts. |
| **Files received** | Projects still missing requested files. |

To filter by an onboarding criterion yourself:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the category dropdown">
    Click the category dropdown in the filter bar. You need onboarding-settings read access to see the submenu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Onboarding projects">
    Point at **Onboarding projects**. On a phone the submenu opens over the parent menu. With a keyboard, press the right arrow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a criterion">
    Choose **All**, **Active onboardings**, **Waiting on client**, **Never opened portal**, or **Outstanding files**. Other list filters can narrow the results further.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choosing **All** or **Projects** in the parent menu clears the criterion. Your category and criterion survive a reload. Choosing **All** in the onboarding submenu shows linked portals immediately in both **Cards** and **List**.

## Find open or closed work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the company and view">
    Pick the company in the company selector, then open **Projects** and choose **Cards**, **List**, or **Kanban**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Open, Closed, or All">
    Click **Open**, **Closed**, or **All** in the filter bar. **Open** is the starting choice. MSP staff with planner access and the Project tracker also see **Upcoming**, which lists sales opportunities expected to become projects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Click the search (magnifier) icon and type part of a project name, company name, or outcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a manager">
    Click **Project Manager** and tick one or more people. A project matches if it belongs to any of them. The choices come from the projects currently shown, so other filters can remove a manager from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the sort order">
    Click **Sort by** and pick **Name (A-Z)**, **Name (Z-A)**, **Last Changed**, **Due Date (Soonest)**, **Due Date (Furthest)**, or **Company (A-Z)**. Kanban keeps its configured columns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

These controls apply immediately and never change the project itself.

## Filter by activity or due date

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Date filter">
    Click **Date** in the filter bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the date to filter on">
    Under **Filter by**, choose **Last Activity** or **Due Date**. Last Activity is the project's last update time. Due Date is the project's due date, not a task's date.
    <img src="/images/product/projects-date-filter.png" alt="MSP Demo Date Filter popover with Last Activity and From and To date controls" width="576" height="508" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the range">
    Set **From**, **To**, or both. The list updates right away. Projects with no date in the range disappear, and **From** must not be later than **To**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear it when you are done">
    Click **Clear** in the popover to remove the dates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Apply several filters together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Filters">
    Click **Filters** in the filter bar, or press Ctrl+Shift+F (Cmd+Shift+F on Mac). The drawer lists **Status**, **Health Status**, **Project Manager**, and **Exclude company**, plus team, sales stage, and custom-field filters when your portal has them, and the **Date Filter**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your selections">
    Tick one or more values in a section. A project matches if it has any ticked value in that section. Different sections narrow the results together. **Status** lists the stored PSA statuses, which can differ from the labels on cards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or discard">
    Click **Apply Filters** to use your choices and close the drawer. Closing the drawer without applying discards them. **Reset** clears the drawer's choices, and you still click **Apply Filters** to use that. Reset does not change the search or **Open / Closed / All**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The number on the **Filters** button shows how many filters are applied. Open **Filters** to review them.

## Filter List columns

In **List**, each column heading has its own filter. Column filters narrow the rows already loaded by the toolbar and **Filters** choices, and every active filter must match.

| Column | Filter |
| --- | --- |
| Project, Next Action | Type part of the text shown in the column. |
| Company, Lead, Health, Outcome, role columns, custom fields | Search and tick one or more values. A row matches any ticked value. |
| Phases | Enter a minimum, a maximum, or both. |
| Due, Last Change | Choose a preset such as **Past due**, or a custom date range. |

The result count updates as you filter. Click **Clear Filters** above the table to remove every column filter. Column filters are not part of saved filters and reset when you leave the page. The health counts at the top ignore them.

## Save and reopen filters

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the filters">
    Choose the health, status, manager, date, company exclusion, team and custom-field filters you want, along with the project category, Open / Closed / All and any search text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save them">
    Click **Filters**, then **Save Filter** (or **Saved filters**, then **Save New Filter**). Enter a name and click the check mark. Saving with an existing name replaces that saved filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reopen them later">
    Click **Filters**, then **Saved filters**, then a name. The saved choices replace your current ones and the drawer closes. Click the trash icon beside a name to delete it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Saved filters are private to your account and open on any device. The same list appears in **Cards**, **List**, **Kanban**, and **Schedule**. A filter saved outside Schedule has no calendar options, so opening it in Schedule keeps your current grouping and calendar view. The sort order is not saved.

<img src="/images/product/projects-saved-filters-drawer.png" alt="Projects Filters drawer with the Saved filters list open, showing one saved filter and Save New Filter" width="1440" height="960" />

Custom fields appear in the drawer when your MSP turns them on for project lists and your role can view them. Picklist and Yes/No fields offer choices. Text, number, and date fields use a **Contains** box, which matches text and does not compare numbers or dates. Every custom-field filter must match.

## Understand health filters and totals

- **Needs Input** marks projects with pending client actions. A project can need input and also be **On Track**, **At Risk**, or **Blocked**.
- Ticking several health choices matches any of them.
- The health tiles count projects before the health filter is applied. They can overlap, so do not add them up.
- **Unscored** means the project has no due date and not enough hours data to score.
- To find **Completed** projects, set **Open / Closed / All** to **All** or **Closed** first.

Your MSP can exclude project statuses from health scoring or override a project's health.

## Clear filters and remembered choices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear everything at once">
    Press **Esc** while the Filters drawer is closed. This removes the search, health, status, manager, date, company exclusion, team, and custom-field filters and returns the category to **All**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or clear from the drawer">
    Click **Filters**, **Reset**, then **Apply Filters**. Reset leaves the search alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset the open state if needed">
    Change **Open / Closed / All** yourself. Clearing never changes it, the sort order, or the view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your browser remembers the view, sort order, and date filter. Manager choices are remembered per company scope. Other filters can stay on when you switch companies, so check them after you switch.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A project is missing | Set **Open / Closed / All** to **All**, press **Esc** to clear filters, and check the company selector. |
| Closed projects are missing after clearing | Clearing does not change **Open / Closed / All**. Choose **All** or **Closed**. |
| My drawer choices vanished | The drawer keeps drafts until you click **Apply Filters**. The **Date** and **Project Manager** controls in the filter bar apply at once. |
| The onboarding submenu is missing | You need onboarding-settings read access and the unified Projects update enabled. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why are closed projects still missing after clearing filters?">
    Clearing filters leaves Open / Closed / All unchanged. Choose All or Closed separately, then confirm the company scope and access with your MSP if the project is still absent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my filter changes disappear when I closed Filters?">
    The drawer holds draft changes until you click **Apply Filters**. The **Date** and manager controls in the filter bar apply immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do Needs Input and Blocked counts add up to the total projects?">
    No. A project can appear in both, and the health counts are calculated before the current health filter. Use the displayed project result count for the filtered list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I filter a custom number field by greater than a value?">
    No. The **Contains** box matches text only. The **Date** filter covers a project's Last Activity or Due Date.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
