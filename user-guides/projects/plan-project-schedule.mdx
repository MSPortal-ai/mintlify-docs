<Note>Destination paths below use the [grouped project navigation](/user-guides/projects/workspace#choose-a-section).</Note>

The schedule planner on **Projects > Dispatch** lets MSP staff plan one project's tasks as a draft. You can move and resize tasks, assign people, link dependent work, and ask **Smart Schedule** for a proposal. Nothing in the draft changes in MSPortal or your PSA until you click **Save**. Booking technicians on the board is separate and writes to your PSA right away (see [Book technicians](/user-guides/projects/dispatch-board#book-technicians)). Narrow the **Projects** filter to one project to plan it. For the cross-project board and the **Needs scheduling** queue on the same page, see [Dispatch project work](/user-guides/projects/dispatch-board).

## Before you start

- You need MSP staff access with the Projects **Schedule** view permission to open the page, and project write permission to change anything. Company users cannot open it. They read shared dates in [Read a project schedule](/user-guides/projects/read-project-schedule).
- The scheduling feature must be enabled for your portal. The **Dispatch** tab and **Plan schedule** do not appear without it.
- Saving needs an active MSPortal subscription.

| Project source | What saving does |
| --- | --- |
| ConnectWise, Autotask, Halo | Task start dates, end dates and assigned people sync to the PSA. Anchors, dependencies, durations, effort, skills and the working calendar stay in MSPortal. |
| Native MSPortal project | Everything saves in MSPortal. |
| Any other source | The planner is read-only and shows **This project provider does not support schedule writes.** |

Completed tasks appear dimmed and cannot be moved or edited.

## Open the planner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Projects > Dispatch">
    Open **Projects** and click **Dispatch**. Open the **Projects** filter and choose exactly one project. The toolbar then adds **View**, **Smart Schedule** and the **More planner actions** menu (**...**). Choosing a second project, or clearing the filter, returns the page to the cross-project board.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or start from a project">
    Open the project, click **Project Plan**, then **Schedule**, and click **Plan schedule** at the right end of the toolbar. The page opens with that project already in the **Projects** filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or start from a task">
    In either calendar, click a task's **...** menu and choose **Reschedule**. The page opens with that task's project in the **Projects** filter, the task open in the side sheet, and the board starting on the week of its start date. In the Gantt view, an item's **Plan in schedule workspace** action does the same.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While you have changes, the right side of the toolbar shows **N unsaved changes**, **Discard** and **Save**. A native project saves in MSPortal. For a ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo project, dates and assigned people sync to the PSA when you click **Save**, and the scheduling rules stay in MSPortal.

## Choose a view

Click **View** and choose **Board**, **Calendar**, **Timeline** or **Dependencies**. The **Needs scheduling** queue stays beside every view.

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Board** | Technicians down the side and working days across the top. See [Dispatch project work](/user-guides/projects/dispatch-board). |
| **Calendar** | One column per working day of the week, with a bar for each dated task. Each day header shows the team's booked hours against its capacity. **Team's other work** lists hours people have booked on appointments or other projects. |
| **Timeline** | A Gantt chart with one row per phase and task. Click a task row to open it. Drag a task's bar to change its dates. |
| **Dependencies** | Task boxes arranged left to right by dependency order, with arrows labeled by link type code, such as FS, and any lead or lag. |

Use **Previous**, **Next** and **Today** in the toolbar to change the week. When a week has no dated work, the Calendar view reads **No scheduled work for this project this week.** and offers buttons such as **2 later tasks** to jump to the nearest dated work. **Find work…** in the queue also filters the Calendar bars by title.

Task bars show the title, assigned person and remaining hours. An anchor icon and amber accent mark anchored tasks. A teal accent marks tasks on the critical path, where any delay pushes out the end of the linked work.

## Check the schedule settings

Click **...** and choose **Schedule settings…** to open a dialog for the project. It appears only when you can edit the project. It has three parts.

- **Working calendar** sets **Daily capacity (hours per person)**, **Holidays / non-working dates (YYYY-MM-DD, comma separated)** and the working weekdays. New plans start with Monday to Friday and 8 hours per day.
- **Project team** lists the roles under **Roles to schedule**. Tick a role to let Smart Schedule assign it. Each role shows how many people hold it, and the panel shows each person's hours **Booked this week**. For ConnectWise projects the team comes from the project team in ConnectWise. If it shows **This project has no active team members in ConnectWise**, add people to the team in ConnectWise and reload the page.
- **Schedule checks** lists the issues described in [Review schedule checks](#review-schedule-checks).

## Move and edit tasks

The **Needs scheduling** queue lists open work in the **Projects** filter that still has no date, no booking or no technician.

- **Move a task.** Drag its bar onto a day in Calendar. The task keeps its duration in working days.
- **Resize a task.** Drag the handle on the bar's last day to a later day to change its end date.
- **Schedule unscheduled work.** Drag a row from **Needs scheduling** onto its start day.
- **Edit a task.** Click its bar, queue row, Timeline row or Dependencies box.

Anchored and completed tasks cannot be dragged. Drag and selection pause while a Smart Schedule proposal is open.

These drags edit the draft. On the **Board** view, dragging work for a ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo project books a technician in the PSA right away instead. See [Book technicians](/user-guides/projects/dispatch-board#book-technicians).

### Use the task editor

The task editor opens in a side sheet. For a ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo task, a **Bookings** section sits above it, where you add, edit and delete bookings that save to the PSA right away. See [Book from the task sheet](/user-guides/projects/dispatch-board#book-from-the-task-sheet). Date fields in the editor wait for **Apply to draft**; the other fields update the draft as you change them.

| Field | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Anchor** checkbox | Keeps the task's current dates fixed. The task needs dates first. Clear it before changing those dates. Smart Schedule plans other work around anchors. |
| **Start date**, **End date** | Set new dates, then select **Apply to draft**. |
| **Move by days** | Shift the entered dates forward or back, up to 365 days. **Use working days** skips non-working days and holidays. |
| **Also move** | Choose **Only this task**, **All future tasks in this project** or **Dependent tasks**. |
| **Assigned person** | Pick an assignable person or **Unassigned**. |
| **Duration (working days)** | Sets how many working days Smart Schedule gives the task. |
| **Remaining work (hours)** | Sets the effort counted against daily capacity. It starts as budget hours minus actual hours. |
| **Required skills (comma separated)** | Limits Smart Schedule to people with every listed skill. |
| **Skills for this person (comma separated)** | Records skills for the assigned person. Appears when the task has an owner. |

If you change a date field without applying it, **Save** and **Smart Schedule** stay disabled and the tooltip reads **Apply the date change first, or close the task to discard it.**

### Link dependencies

Dependencies live on the later task. Select that task, then choose a **Predecessor**, a **Type** and a **Lead / lag (working days)** value, and select **Add dependency**.

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Finish → start** | This task starts no earlier than the working day after the predecessor finishes. |
| **Start → start** | This task starts no earlier than the predecessor's start. |
| **Finish → finish** | This task finishes no earlier than the predecessor's finish. |
| **Start → finish** | This task finishes no earlier than the predecessor's start. |

A positive lag adds working days and a negative value creates a lead. Use the remove control beside a listed dependency to delete it. MSPortal rejects a link that creates a loop with **These dependencies form a cycle. Remove a link before scheduling.**

## Use Smart Schedule

Smart Schedule proposes dates, and owners where needed, for open tasks that are not anchored. It uses durations, remaining hours, dependencies, anchors, required skills and daily capacity. It appears only when you can edit the project.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the planning settings">
    Open **Schedule settings…** to confirm **Daily capacity (hours per person)**, holidays, working weekdays and the **Roles to schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Smart Schedule">
    Click **Smart Schedule** in the toolbar. Set **Schedule from** and **Hours for unestimated work**. Tick **Suggest different owners to balance workload** to let Smart Schedule reassign tasks that already have owners. Unassigned tasks always get a proposed owner. When the project has more than one phase, tick **Run phases in order** to add a dependency from each phase to the next. A line at the bottom summarises how many tasks will be dated and for how many people.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the proposal">
    Click **Run Smart Schedule**. A line under the toolbar reads, for example, **3 changes proposed**. Click **Show changes** to list each changed task with its old and new dates and owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or reject it">
    Click **Accept** to copy the changes into your draft, or **Reject** to drop them. Accepting does not save. Review the result, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/product/projects-dispatch-smart-schedule-desktop.png" alt="Smart Schedule popover showing Schedule from, Hours for unestimated work, the owner and phase checkboxes, a summary line and the Run Smart Schedule button" width="2880" height="1920" />

If the plan already fits, the planner shows **The schedule already fits these constraints.** instead of a proposal.

Capacity counts this project's work, the same people's dated tasks in other open projects on the same PSA connection in the selected company scope, and accessible calendar appointments and busy blocks for staff linked to portal users. Unlinked calendars and unscheduled service work are not counted. Smart Schedule plans whole working days within one year of **Schedule from**; it does not book timed appointments.

**Run Smart Schedule** stays disabled, and the line at the bottom of the popover gives the reason, until assignable people and calendar availability load. If either fails, the line reads **Calendar availability or assignable people could not be loaded. Smart Schedule is unavailable until these reads succeed.**

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No available person matches the required role and skills. Check the scheduled roles, assignments and skills.** | Add the skill to a person, remove it from the task, or tick the role under **Roles to schedule** in **Schedule settings…**. |
| **There is not enough capacity before an anchor. Adjust the work, people, or project start.** | Move **Schedule from** earlier, reduce effort, or unpin the anchor. |
| **No available capacity was found within the scheduling horizon.** | Raise daily capacity, add people, or reduce remaining hours. |
| **A predecessor needs dates before this work can be scheduled.** | Give the predecessor dates or anchor it. |
| **The proposal conflicts with a dependency or resource capacity. Adjust the constraints.** | Review dependencies and capacity, then try again. |

## Review schedule checks

**Schedule settings…** lists the checks under **Schedule checks (N)**. Click one to close the dialog and open its task.

| Check | Blocks saving |
| --- | --- |
| **Fixed anchor conflict** | Yes, when your draft causes it |
| **Dependency dates conflict** | Yes, when your draft causes it |
| **Dates span more than ten years** | Yes |
| **Daily capacity exceeded** | No |
| **Past the project deadline** | No |
| **Needs scheduling** | No |
| **Falls outside working days** | No |
| **Required skills do not match** | No |
| **Outside its phase dates** | No |

Resolve the blocking checks before you save. **Save** then shows the tooltip **Fix N schedule checks before saving. See Schedule settings.** Conflicts that already exist on work you have not touched do not block saving. The other checks are warnings you can save with.

## Save your plan

For a ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo project, the sequence is **Smart Schedule → Accept → Save**. Saving updates the actual project tickets or tasks in your PSA with the changed start dates, end dates and assigned people. Generating or accepting a proposal alone does not write to the PSA.

For ConnectWise, the fields written are the project ticket's Schedule Start, Schedule End and Owner. See [Where ConnectWise dates and people come from](/user-guides/projects/dispatch-board#where-connectwise-dates-and-people-come-from).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The button reads **Saving…** while MSPortal writes each changed task. Stay on the page until it finishes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the result">
    A **Schedule saved** message confirms success. The planner then reloads the latest project data before you can edit again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recover from a failure">
    **The schedule could not be saved. Your draft is retained.** reports a failed save. Review the reloaded task dates and owners before retrying: an upstream date change can succeed before a later owner update fails. A message such as **3 tasks saved before a failure. Your remaining changes are retained.** means earlier tasks already synced. After the reload, your unsaved changes return on top of the latest data. Review them and save again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only one save can run for a project at a time. If another planner saved the project after you opened it, your save fails without writing. After the reload, your changes return on top of their saved schedule, so review them and save again.

## Protect, discard, print and export

**Discard** in the toolbar resets the draft to the last loaded schedule. Leaving the page, choosing a different project in the **Projects** filter, opening another project's work from the board, or using the browser Back button with unsaved changes asks **Discard this schedule draft?** Click **Keep editing** to stay, or **Discard changes** to leave. Closing the browser tab shows your browser's own leave warning.

In the **...** menu, **Print / save PDF** opens your browser's print dialog with a landscape table of tasks, start dates, end dates and assigned people. **Export XML** downloads `project-schedule.xml` with the task dates, owners and planning rules. Both use the tasks currently shown, including an open proposal.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **Dispatch** tab or **Plan schedule** is missing | Check that you are MSP staff with the Projects **Schedule** view permission and that the scheduling feature is enabled for your portal. |
| **Save** is disabled | Hover it for the reason. Apply any date fields you changed, accept or reject the open proposal, and fix any blocking schedule check. The **Save** button appears only while you have unsaved changes. |
| **Run Smart Schedule** is disabled | Read the line at the bottom of the popover. It waits for assignable people and calendar availability to load. Reload the page if loading fails. |
| **View**, **Smart Schedule** or the **...** menu is missing | They appear only when **Projects** is narrowed to exactly one project. **Smart Schedule** and **Schedule settings…** also need project write permission and a provider that accepts schedule writes. |
| The save failed | Your draft is kept. Review the reloaded dates and owners, then save again. |
| A project is missing from the **Projects** filter | The list shows open projects in the selected company scope. Change the company selector. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is the Dispatch tab or Plan schedule missing?">
    Both require an MSP staff account with the Projects **Schedule** view permission and the scheduling feature. Company users do not see them. Changing the schedule also needs project write permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do anchors, dependencies and skills appear in my PSA?">
    No. They are MSPortal scheduling rules. Only task dates and assigned people sync to ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a project missing from the Projects filter?">
    The list shows open projects in the current company scope. Change the company selector before opening the page. Closed projects and projects outside the scope do not load.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Save disabled?">
    Check for unapplied date fields, an open Smart Schedule proposal, or a blocking schedule check such as **Fixed anchor conflict** or **Dependency dates conflict**. Hover **Save** to see which applies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Smart Schedule book timed appointments?">
    No. It assigns whole working days to tasks in the draft. To book a technician in your PSA, including at set times, use the [Bookings section](/user-guides/projects/dispatch-board#book-from-the-task-sheet) of the task or drag work onto a technician's day on the board.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
