This page shows Company users and MSP staff how to read the weekly progress updates on a project. Company users read shared updates. MSP staff write and manage them.

## Before you start

- You need access to the project. Your MSP controls project visibility and permissions.
- Internal updates are hidden from Company users, so your list can be shorter than an MSP staff member's.

## Read an update

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Updates">
    Open **Projects**, click the project, then click **Collaboration** and **Updates**. The **Weekly Updates** list is on the left, newest reporting week first. The newest one has a **Latest** badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a week">
    Click a week in the list, for example **Week of Sep 1**. Under each week you see when it was posted and counts such as "3 done" and "1 risk". Use **Search** above the list to find a week by its summary text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the update">
    The update opens on the right. Read **Summary**, then **Completed This Week**, **Coming Up Next**, **Risks & Concerns**, and **Decisions Needed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on requests separately">
    A **Decisions Needed** bullet is a progress note, not a button. Open **Collaboration > Decisions** to see the related request. If a pending request is assigned to you, follow [Respond to project decisions and action items](/user-guides/projects/respond-to-project-actions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list is ordered by **Week Of**, the reporting week. Editing an older update does not move it up. The posted time is when the update was created. It is not a read receipt or last-edit time. Several updates can share the same week, so check each one.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No updates yet** | Your MSP has not shared an update with you. This does not mean no work has happened. |
| The week you expect is not first | Updates sort by reporting week, not by when they were edited. Click the week in the list. |
| A decision has no response button | A bullet does not assign anyone. Ask your MSP to check the related request in **Decisions**. |
| You need a correction | Contact your MSP. Company administrators do not edit updates. To open support, [start with the Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). |

For milestone dates and status, see [Read project milestones](/user-guides/projects/read-project-timeline). MSP authors can follow [Write and share project updates](/user-guides/projects/write-project-updates).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Latest mean this was the last update edited?">
    No. It marks the newest reporting week. Editing an older week does not change that.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I reply to an update?">
    No. Updates have no comment box. Use a request assigned to you in **Decisions** or **Action Items**, or contact your MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an empty Risks list mean the project has no risk?">
    No. It means no risks were recorded. Check the project status and ask your MSP about anything unclear.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see the MSP's internal updates?">
    No. Company users see only updates shared with clients. MSP staff can also see internal ones.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
