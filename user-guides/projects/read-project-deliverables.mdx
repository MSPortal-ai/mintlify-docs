This page shows Company users and MSP staff how to find the outputs an MSP has shared on a project and download their files. Your MSP manages deliverables, uploads, sharing, and permissions.

## Before you start

- You need access to the project.
- Company users see only shared deliverables and shared documents. MSP staff also see internal ones, so counts can differ.

## Find an output

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Deliverables">
    Open **Projects**, click the project, then click **Project Plan** and **Deliverables**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the list">
    Each deliverable in the list on the left shows a status (**Planned**, **In Progress**, **Review**, or **Complete**) and a due date when one is set. MSP staff also see an **Internal** badge on deliverables that are hidden from clients. Use **Search** to find one by title, description, or linked phase or ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the deliverable">
    Click a deliverable to read its description and find its **Documents** on the right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The order of the list is set by your MSP. A due date is context, not a sort order. A **Complete** status is the MSP's record. It is not a measure of hours, uploaded files, or formal approval.

## Download a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the document">
    Open the deliverable and look under **Documents** for the file name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Download">
    Click **Download** on the file. The portal asks for access to that file and opens it. Depending on the file and your browser, it downloads or opens in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Check your downloads or the new tab. If a link has expired, go back to the deliverable and click **Download** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New uploads use temporary download links that expire after about five minutes. Do not save one as a permanent link. Share the project location with other authorized people instead. Downloading a file does not approve it, mark the deliverable **Complete**, or tell the MSP you read it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No deliverables yet** | Ask your MSP whether any outputs are shared on this project. |
| A deliverable has no documents | The MSP must share each file as well as the deliverable. Some outputs have no file. |
| **Document not found** or an access error | The file may have been removed or unshared. Reopen the project and ask your MSP. |
| Nothing opens after **Download** | Check your browser's downloads and pop-up settings, then try again. |
| A document needs a correction | Contact your MSP. Company users cannot replace files. |

For a formal response, use a request assigned to you in [Decisions or Action Items](/user-guides/projects/respond-to-project-actions). For support, [start with the Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Complete mean I approved the deliverable?">
    No. It is the deliverable's recorded status. A formal request for your answer shows up in **Decisions** or **Action Items**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can my MSP see more than I can?">
    You see only shared deliverables and shared documents on projects you can access. Each deliverable and each document has its own visibility setting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I upload or replace a handover document?">
    No. The MSP manages documents. Contact them if you need to send a replacement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does downloading tell the MSP I read the file?">
    No. Download has no read receipt. Reply through the agreed process.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
