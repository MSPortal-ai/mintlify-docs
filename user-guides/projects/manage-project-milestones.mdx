This page shows MSP staff how to add milestones, set their dates and status, choose what clients see, and reorder them.

## Before you start

- You need permission to edit project collaboration content (project overlay edit).
- Company users [read shared milestones](/user-guides/projects/read-project-timeline). They cannot edit them.
- Milestones live in MSPortal. Changing one does not change a linked PSA ticket, phase schedule, or project status.

## Add a milestone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Milestones">
    Open **Projects**, click the project, then click **Project Plan** and **Milestones**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the milestone">
    Click the **+** button at the top of the list and choose **New Milestone**. If the list is empty, click **New Milestone** in the empty state. The milestone is saved at once as **Pending**, with **Client Visible** off, and opens on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe it">
    Replace **Title** with a clear outcome and add a **Description** if you want. Keep the title filled in. Text saves shortly after you stop typing. There is no Save button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the dates and status">
    Pick a **Planned Date** and a **Status**.

    <img src="/images/product/project-milestone-status-options.png" alt="MSP milestone Status menu: Pending, In Progress, Complete, and Skipped" width="728" height="268" />

    When it is finished, set the status to **Complete** and set **Actual Completion Date**. They are separate fields. Choosing **Complete** does not fill the date, and entering a date does not mark it complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add internal notes">
    Use **Notes (internal)** for staff-only notes. Company users never see them, even when **Client Visible** is on. Put anything clients should read in **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it when ready">
    Turn on **Client Visible** when the milestone is ready for Company users who can access the project. It saves at once. With it off, the whole milestone is hidden from Company users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link it to work">
    Under the editor fields, pick a **Linked phase**, **Linked ticket**, or **Linked files** if the milestone belongs with them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the **Mark as Milestone** shortcut on phases and tickets, see [phase and ticket action menus](/user-guides/projects/use-phase-ticket-actions).

## Edit, reorder, and remove milestones

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit">
    Click a milestone in the list and change any field. Changes save as you make them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder">
    Drag a milestone by its grip handle. Reordering is off while a search is active, and it never changes dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Skip">
    Set the status to **Skipped** when a milestone is no longer in the plan but should stay on record. It does not count as **Complete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    Click the milestone, click **Delete** at the top, and confirm. Check the list to make sure it is gone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Milestones** tab shows Company users only client-visible milestones. MSP staff also see internal ones.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **New Milestone** is missing | You need the project overlay-edit permission and an active subscription. |
| A new milestone did not appear | Clear **Search**. A new milestone is **Pending** and its title is **New Milestone**. |
| I cannot drag milestones | Clear **Search** first, and make sure you have edit permission. |
| Clients cannot see a milestone | Turn on **Client Visible**. They also need access to the project. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why did New Milestone create a record before I finished?">
    It saves the default **Pending** milestone at once. Finish its fields or delete it. Closing it does not discard it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Complete fill the completion date?">
    No. Set **Status** and **Actual Completion Date** separately. Reopening a milestone does not clear the date either.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Client Visible notify clients or give them access?">
    No. It shows the milestone inside a project they can already open. It sends nothing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I rely on Notes (internal) to hide text?">
    Yes. Internal notes are left out of what Company users receive, even when **Client Visible** is on.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
