**Projects** shows work across the companies you can access. It combines native MSPortal projects and synced PSA projects, with updates, milestones, decisions, action items, and deliverables. This page is for MSP staff and Company users.

## Before you start

- Open **Projects** in the left menu. Your role decides which views you see (**Dashboard**, **Cards**, **List**, **Kanban**, **Schedule**) and whether you can edit.
- Pick a company in the company selector to focus the portfolio, or choose **Global View** for every company you can access.
- Native projects live in MSPortal. PSA projects also depend on your connected PSA.
- Company users see only the projects and fields their MSP shares, set under **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Client project access**.

## Choose a portfolio view

| View | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** (MSP only) | Review portfolio health, the evidence behind each metric, and projects needing attention. |
| **Cards** | Scan project summaries, dates, hours, contacts, and the next action. |
| **List** | Compare projects in a table and choose the columns. |
| **Kanban** | See projects grouped by your configured PSA status columns. |
| **Schedule** | See dated projects and scheduled work on a calendar. |

## Find a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the company">
    Use the company selector at the top of the app, then open **Projects**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    In **Cards**, **List**, or **Kanban**, click the search (magnifier) icon and type a project, company, or outcome. Click **Open**, **Closed**, or **All**. **Open** is the starting choice. Use **Project Manager**, **Date**, or **Filters** to narrow further. With the unified update, a category dropdown also offers **All**, **Projects**, and **Onboarding projects**. Selecting onboarding sets the open state to **All** and shows linked portals within your company scope. You can then choose **Open** or **Closed** to narrow them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the project">
    Click a project card or list row to open its workspace. If you cannot find an older project, switch to **All**. Clearing filters does not change **Open / Closed / All**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Find and open a project](/user-guides/projects/find-projects) and [Filter and sort projects](/user-guides/projects/filter-projects) for dates, managers, health, saved filters, and onboarding filters.

The health filters are **Needs Input**, **Blocked**, **At Risk**, **On Track**, and **Unscored**. **Needs Input** marks pending client input. A project can have a separate health value too.

## Read portfolio health (MSP staff)

Open **Dashboard**. It shows three scores, each with the number of eligible projects.

- **Active schedule** covers open projects with due dates that are not overdue.
- **On-time delivery** covers projects completed on or before their due date in the last 12 months.
- **Effort adherence** covers projects at or below 110% of estimated hours.
- **Unscored** projects have no due date and not enough hours data to score.

Check **Projects needing attention** and any warning that source data is more than 14 days old. A metric with no eligible projects shows **No eligible projects**, which is not the same as healthy.

To choose which PSA statuses count toward health, open **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Client project access** and use **Project health status**.

With the unified update, the same card also has an **Onboarding** section. Each metric opens **List** filtered to that criterion. See [onboarding filters](/user-guides/projects/filter-projects#follow-up-on-onboarding).

## Move projects on the portfolio Kanban

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Kanban">
    Open **Projects** and choose **Kanban**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag a project">
    Drag a project card to another column. You need the project status-edit permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a status if asked">
    MSPortal uses the column's default status. If it needs more information, it asks you to choose a PSA status. Choose one and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The status change is written to your PSA. The board inside a single project (**Project Plan > Board**) groups tickets, not projects.

<Tip>
If a project is missing from a column, check the **Status Mapping** settings for unmapped PSA statuses, or turn on **Use Raw PSA Statuses**.
</Tip>

## Reassign tasks on the Resources board (MSP staff)

The Resources board groups project tasks across your portfolio by team member. It follows the same portfolio and company filters as the other views.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Resources">
    Open **Projects**, choose **Kanban**, and switch the **Kanban view** toggle from **Projects** to **Resources**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the work">
    Each team member has a column, and tasks without an owner sit in **Unassigned**. Every card shows its project and company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reassign a task">
    Drag the card to another team member's column, or click the card's **Assign Owner** button and pick a person. To choose someone without a column, drop the card on **Assign to…**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Reassignment writes back for MSPortal, ConnectWise, and Autotask project tasks. Tasks from other providers are read-only. A ConnectWise or Autotask task can only move to a member from the same integration; otherwise MSPortal asks you to use **Assign to…**.

## Create a native project (MSP staff)

Click **New project**, choose a template, and choose **MSPortal only** to keep the project in MSPortal. You need project write permission. Company users ask their MSP to start new work. See [Native Projects: Start to Finish](/user-guides/projects/native-projects).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No projects appear | Check the company selector, **Open / Closed / All**, the search, your filters, and the PSA project sync. Company users should also ask about client project access. |
| A view or edit control is missing | Ask an admin to check your role's project permissions. Some controls also need PSA support. |
| Fields or phases are missing | Check **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Display Configuration**, and whether the project has phases or tickets. |
| Health looks incomplete | Look at the eligible counts, **Unscored** projects, the data-freshness warning, and the active and inactive health statuses. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where should a Company user start?">
    Open **Projects**, choose **Cards** or **List**, search for the project, and click it. See [Find and open a project](/user-guides/projects/find-projects).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do Company users and MSP staff see different views?">
    **Dashboard** is for MSP staff only. Each of the other views has its own role permission. The MSP also sets which projects and fields clients see.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing a filter change the project?">
    No. Search, filters, sort, and view only change what you see. Your browser remembers some of them. Moving a status or rescheduling are separate actions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who changes a Company user's project permissions?">
    The MSP. See [Company user permissions (MSP)](/user-guides/settings/change-company-user-permissions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
