This page shows MSP staff how to write weekly updates, add progress and decision bullets, and control what clients see.

## Before you start

- You need permission to edit project collaboration content (project overlay edit).
- Company users [read shared updates](/user-guides/projects/read-project-updates). They cannot edit them.
- Updates live in MSPortal. Writing a bullet does not close a PSA ticket, schedule work, or send an email.

## Create an update

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Updates">
    Open **Projects**, click the project, then click **Collaboration** and **Updates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Update">
    Click the **+** button at the top of the **Weekly Updates** list and choose **Add Update**. If the list is empty, click **Add Update** in the empty state. The update is created at once and opens on the right. Closing it does not discard it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the reporting week">
    **Week Of** starts on the Monday of the current week. Click the date to change it. It is the reporting week, not a publish date. Several updates can use the same week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the summary">
    Type a short **Summary**. Text saves shortly after you stop typing. There is no Save or Publish button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add list entries">
    Under **Completed This Week**, **Coming Up Next**, **Risks & Concerns**, or **Decisions Needed**, type an entry and click the **+** next to it, or press **Enter**. Text left in the add field is not added to the list.

    <img src="/images/product/project-update-list-editor.png" alt="MSP Demo Completed This Week editor with a saved pilot workstation checklist entry and the adjacent add control" width="1094" height="256" />

    This synthetic example shows the MSP editor. Company users receive a reading view.

    Each added entry saves immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct a saved entry">
    Click the entry's text and edit it in place. The change saves when you press **Enter** or click away. Press **Shift+Enter** for a line break, or **Escape** to undo before it saves. Clearing all the text restores the original entry. Use the entry's delete control to remove it.

    <img src="/images/product/project-update-list-item-edit-preview.png" alt="Branch preview: MSP Demo Risks & Concerns entry being edited in place to show a verified outstanding balance, with its delete control beside it" width="1110" height="302" />

    Branch preview of the MSP editor using Demo sample text. On phones and tablets, the delete control stays visible; on desktop, it appears when you hover over or focus the entry. Editing a **Decisions Needed** entry also renames its pending request in **Decisions** and keeps that request's assignees, due date, and response. If the old title still appears in another update, MSPortal treats the edit as a new decision instead; check **Decisions** afterward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it when ready">
    Turn on **Client Visible** to share the update with Company users who can access the project. It saves at once and does not give anyone project access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the saved result">
    Click another week and then back, or reopen the project, to check the update. If you see an error, check what was saved before you repeat a change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Updates you add on the Updates tab start with **Client Visible** off. The **Create Update** shortcut in a phase or ticket menu starts shared. See [phase and ticket action menus](/user-guides/projects/use-phase-ticket-actions).

After an update is client-visible, every later edit is saved to the shared update. There is no draft copy or approval step. Turn **Client Visible** off before making private revisions, then review the whole update before you share it again. This does not take back copies people already read.

## Understand Decisions Needed

Adding a **Decisions Needed** bullet can create a linked pending item in **Decisions**. Open that register to set the request's details, due date, urgency, and assignees using [Manage project decisions and action items](/user-guides/projects/manage-project-actions).

The related request follows the source update's audience. Assignment does not automatically notify anyone. Use the request's explicit notification controls only when you intend to send a message.

Decision titles are matched across updates, ignoring letter case and extra spaces. Repeated titles can represent the same pending request. Removing a bullet or an update can remove a pending request that was generated from it; resolving or deleting a linked request can remove matching bullets from more than one update. Give distinct decisions clear, distinct titles and check **Decisions** after changing these bullets.

## Delete an update

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the update">
    Click the week in the **Weekly Updates** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete it">
    Click **Delete** at the top of the update and confirm. The update is removed from the project and cannot be undone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Decisions">
    If the update had **Decisions Needed** bullets, open **Collaboration > Decisions** and check the related requests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add Update** is missing | You need the project overlay-edit permission and an active subscription. |
| My typed bullet disappeared | Click the **+** or press **Enter** to add it. Text left in the add field is not saved. |
| A decision bullet changed in **Decisions** | Bullets and requests stay in sync. Check **Decisions** after you edit or delete a source update. |
| A shared update shows old text | Edits save as you make them. Check that the save finished and no error showed. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is Save or Publish?">
    There is no separate button. **Add Update** creates the update at once, **Summary** saves after a short pause, and the other controls save as you change them. **Client Visible** decides whether Company users can read it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Client Visible send the update to clients?">
    It shows the update in the portal. Adding, editing, or sharing an update does not email or notify clients.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my typed bullet disappear?">
    Click the **+** next to the field or press **Enter** to add it. Text left in the field is not saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I fix a wrong figure in an AI-drafted update?">
    Open the update, click the entry that has the figure, and edit it in place. Check the **Summary** too, since AI drafts can repeat a detail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Completed This Week update ticket status?">
    No. These are notes only. Change the ticket status in the ticket itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did removing a decision bullet change the Decisions tab?">
    Decision bullets and linked requests stay in sync. A pending request made from a bullet can disappear when you remove the bullet. Check **Decisions** after you change or delete an update.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the posted time change when I edit or share an update?">
    No. The posted time is when the update was created. It is not a publish time, last-edit time, or read receipt.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
