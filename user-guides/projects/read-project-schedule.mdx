This page shows Company users and MSP staff how to read the project calendar, and what each item stands for. Open **Projects > Schedule** for the calendar across projects. Open a project and click **Project Plan**, then **Schedule**, for that project's own calendar. The two views can show different items, especially for Company users.

## Before you start

- You need the **Schedule** view permission for Projects.
- Company users should ask their MSP about missing projects or fields. The MSP manages permissions and sharing.
- Seeing a milestone does not give you access to the tasks behind it.

## Choose the right schedule

| View | Company users | MSP staff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Projects > Schedule** | Shared milestones from projects your access permits. It does not provide the MSP's full project, phase, task, or service-call feed. | Available project spans, phases, tasks, milestones, and task-linked Autotask service calls in the selected company scope. |
| A project's **Project Plan > Schedule** tab | The accessible project's date span, shared milestones, and available phase/task dates when the MSP's client display settings allow them. | The project's supplied dated work, including available task-linked Autotask service calls. |

## Read an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the period">
    Click **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, or **Year**. Use the arrows to move one period, or **Today** to return to the current date. The calendar uses your portal's time zone and week start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find dated work">
    In **Projects > Schedule**, click **Projects**, **Tasks**, or **Start dates** first. See [Group the schedule](/user-guides/projects/calendar#group-the-schedule). Month view lists each day's items and ends with **+N more** when a day is full. In Week view, a long bar across the week is one date span, not a new booking each day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Click an item to open it. Where an item has a **...** menu, **View Details** does the same. The table below shows what each type opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img src="/images/product/project-schedule-view-modes.png" alt="Project Schedule view selector with Day, Week, Month, and Year" width="354" height="64" />

This capture shows the MSP view selector, with Month selected.

| Item | What it represents and opens |
| --- | --- |
| Project | The span from the project's start date to its due date. Opens the project's **Overview**. When the Project tracker is on, a project end marker opens the **Project team** window first. |
| Phase | The scheduled phase span. Opens **Project Plan > Tasks**. |
| Task | A project ticket's scheduled date span. Opens its ticket details, or **Project Plan > Tasks** when it has no PSA ticket. |
| Milestone | Its planned date, or its actual date when there is no planned date. Opens **Project Plan > Milestones**. |
| Autotask service call | A timed booking linked to a project task, for MSP staff. Opens the linked ticket details. |

<img src="/images/product/project-schedule-event-menu.png" alt="MSP Schedule item menu with View Details and Reschedule" width="256" height="150" />

This MSP menu includes **Reschedule** because this item and account support it. Company users do not get it. MSP staff can follow [Reschedule an item](/user-guides/projects/calendar#reschedule-an-item).

## Interpret dates and colors

Project, phase, and task spans include the end date. They show as all-day work even if the source has a time. A multi-day task is a planned date span, not a technician booked for every hour. Autotask service calls show as timed blocks.

Colors help tell items and owners apart. Task owners can have matching colored dots in **Staff**, and the colors can change as different people load. Completed or skipped items use a muted color. Check the item's details for its real status.

## Why work can be missing

A project, phase, or task needs a start date to appear. A due date alone is not enough. If its end is missing or earlier than its start, the display can fall back to the start day. A milestone needs a planned or actual date.

Very long planning spans are omitted: project start/end dates more than 400 days apart, or phase/task spans more than 120 days apart. Check the project's details for long-running plan items instead of assuming they were deleted.

The period, your access, and your filters also change what appears. Turning on all-day events can bring back a calendar that looks empty, and **Hide items** stays saved until you untick those titles. Follow [Filter the project schedule](/user-guides/projects/filter-project-schedule) before you report missing work.

Schedule shows synced project dates, not staff availability. It does not include meetings, appointments, or calendar blocks from the separate **Calendar** module.

## Use the Gantt view

When the scheduling feature is enabled for your portal, a project's **Schedule** tab has a **Calendar** and **Gantt** switch at the left of its toolbar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Gantt">
    Open the project, click **Project Plan**, then **Schedule**, and click **Gantt**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the rows">
    Each row is one **Item** with its **Start** and **End** dates and a bar across the period. Use the arrows and **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, or **Year** to change the scale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find an item">
    Type in **Search items...** to filter the rows. The chart reads **No items match your search.** when nothing matches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an item's actions">
    Open an item's actions menu to choose **Open ticket**, **Edit dates**, **Plan in schedule workspace**, **View in Phases**, or **View on Timeline**. Items with no date show **Set date**. You see only the actions your role and PSA allow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your browser remembers whether you last used Calendar or Gantt.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A project, phase, or task is missing | Check that it has a start date. A due date alone is not enough. Then check the period, your filters, and the long-span limits above. |
| A milestone is missing | It needs a planned or actual date. |
| Only milestones show in **Projects > Schedule** | Company users get shared milestones in that view. Open the project's own Schedule tab for more. |
| The calendar looks empty | Turn on all-day events and clear **Hide items**. See [Filter the project schedule](/user-guides/projects/filter-project-schedule). |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I see tasks inside a project but only milestones in Projects > Schedule?">
    The two views are fed differently. The all-projects calendar supplies shared milestones to Company users. A project's own Schedule tab can also show dates that your MSP's client display settings allow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an all-day task mean someone is booked all day?">
    No. It shows the task's planned dates. A timed Autotask service call is a separate booking.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a ticket with a due date missing?">
    Project tasks need a scheduled start date to appear. Check the visible period and filters too. A due date by itself does not create a calendar entry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which milestone date does Schedule use?">
    The planned date first, then the actual date when there is no planned date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does one project appear on many days?">
    Month view lists an item on every day its dates cover. It is one span, not repeated meetings. Use **Start dates** to see each project once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Schedule to see every meeting and appointment?">
    No. Use the separate **Calendar** area for meetings, appointments, and calendar blocks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a long-running phase or task absent?">
    Phase/task spans with start and end more than 120 days apart are omitted. Project spans have a 400-day limit. Read the project details for these longer planning items.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Company users reschedule work?">
    No. Company users read shared dates. Ask your MSP to change them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
