This page shows MSP staff how to track project outputs, keep their status and dates up to date, and choose what Company users see.

## Before you start

- You need permission to edit project collaboration content (project overlay edit).
- Company users [read shared deliverables and files](/user-guides/projects/read-project-deliverables). They cannot edit them.
- Deliverables live in MSPortal. Editing one does not change a PSA ticket, upload a file, or record a client's formal approval.

## Create an output

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Deliverables">
    Open **Projects**, click the project, then click **Project Plan** and **Deliverables**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the deliverable">
    Click the **+** button at the top of the list and choose **New Deliverable**. If the list is empty, click **New Deliverable** in the empty state. It is saved at once as **Planned**, with **Client Visible** off, and opens on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the output">
    Replace **Title** with a clear name and add a **Description** if you want. Text saves after a short pause. There is no Save button, and closing the editor does not discard the deliverable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set status and dates">
    Pick **Planned**, **In Progress**, **In Review**, or **Complete**.

    <img src="/images/product/project-deliverable-status-options.png" alt="MSP deliverable Status menu with Planned, In Progress, In Review and Complete" width="728" height="268" />

    Set **Due Date** and, when it is done, **Completion Date**. Status saves at once and dates save after a short pause. **Complete** and **Completion Date** are separate. Changing one does not fill or clear the other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the audience">
    Keep **Client Visible** off while you prepare internal content. Turn it on when the deliverable is ready for Company users who can access the project. This shares its title, description, and status. Each document needs its own **Share with client** switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the saved result">
    Click another deliverable and then back, or reopen the project, to check what saved. Clear **Search** if the deliverable is not listed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add files, see [Upload and share deliverable documents](/user-guides/projects/manage-deliverable-documents). A deliverable does not need a file to appear in the list.

## Reorder and delete

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reorder">
    Drag a deliverable by its grip handle. Reordering is off while a search is active. It changes the list order, not due dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    Click the deliverable, click **Delete** at the top, and confirm. This removes the deliverable and access to the documents attached to it. To remove a single file, use that file's **Delete** instead. Copies people already downloaded cannot be taken back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An **Internal** badge in the list marks a deliverable with **Client Visible** off. Turning **Client Visible** on does not notify anyone or give them project access. To stop sharing one document, turn off that file's **Share with client** switch.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **New Deliverable** is missing | You need the project overlay-edit permission and an active subscription. |
| I cannot find a new deliverable | Clear **Search**. A new deliverable is **Planned** and is titled **New Deliverable**. |
| I cannot drag deliverables | Clear **Search** and check that you have edit permission. |
| Clients cannot see a deliverable | Turn on **Client Visible**. They also need project access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel a new deliverable by closing the editor?">
    No. **New Deliverable** saves the default record at once. Finish it or delete it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is In Review a request for client approval?">
    No. It is only a status. Create a request in [Decisions](/user-guides/projects/manage-project-actions) when you need a recorded answer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Complete fill in the completion date?">
    No. Set **Status** and **Completion Date** separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will Client Visible share every attached file?">
    No. Each document also needs **Share with client** turned on, and new uploads start internal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting a deliverable delete its linked PSA ticket?">
    No. It removes only the output and access to the files attached to it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
