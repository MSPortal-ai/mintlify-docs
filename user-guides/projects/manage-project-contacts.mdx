This page shows MSP staff how to pick a client sponsor, keep a list of additional project contacts, and understand the synced project lead.

## Before you start

- You need permission to edit project collaboration content (project overlay edit).
- Company users can read contact details but cannot edit them. Their MSP manages users and permissions.
- A client sponsor must be an existing Company user for the project's company.

## Open the contacts form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project">
    Open **Projects** and click the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Client Dashboard">
    Click **Overview**, then **Client Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Contacts">
    Find **Contacts** and click **Edit Contacts**, or **Add contacts** when none are set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose the right contact type

| Contact | How it is maintained |
| --- | --- |
| **Project Lead (Your Team)** | Read-only in this form. The portal uses the synced project lead when available, with older stored details as a fallback. |
| **Client Sponsor** | Select an existing Company user belonging to the project's Company. |
| **Additional Contacts** | Enter useful contact details for vendors, owners, former providers, or other participants. This is a contact list, not a portal user list. |

## Update and save

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the project lead">
    Check the lead shown under **Project Lead (Your Team)**. It reads **Automatically synced** when details are available. You cannot type over it here. Fix a synced lead in your PSA. If it reads **No project lead was found in the synced project**, set the lead in your PSA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Client Sponsor">
    Click **Select a company user**, search by name or email, and choose the person. To clear the sponsor, open the picker and click the checked person again. If nobody shows up, ask your MSP administrator to check the Company user record. Do not create a duplicate user.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maintain Additional Contacts">
    Click **Add contact**, then fill in **Organization**, **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone**. You do not need every field, but an email must be valid. Blank rows are dropped when you save. Use **Remove contact** on a row to remove it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click **Save Changes** and wait for **Contacts updated**. Nothing is saved until you do. Reopen **Contacts** to check the sponsor and list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Closing the dialog without saving discards your edits. Removing an additional contact does not delete a Company user. Choosing a sponsor does not assign tasks, send a notification, or grant access. Contacts saved here do not update your PSA.

Everyone who can see the project sees this contact list, and there is no internal-only switch for a contact. Keep private notes somewhere else.

## Chat with the project manager in Teams

<Note>
Branch preview for the upcoming Projects update.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn the setting on">
    Open **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Display Configuration**, turn on **Chat with project manager in Teams**, and save. You need project settings write access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a project's contacts">
    In a project, open **Overview > Client Dashboard**. A small Teams icon appears beside **Project Lead** in **Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the chat">
    Click the icon to open a direct Teams chat with the manager. Opening it does not send a message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The manager's email must match their Teams sign-in address. When no valid email is available, the icon is hidden. If Teams cannot find the person, ask your MSP to check the address and the Teams external chat settings. Turn the setting off and save to hide the icon again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No Company users found | Search by name or email and check the project's Company. A failed search can also appear empty; close and reopen before asking the MSP administrator to investigate. |
| Sponsor is not in the list | The picker uses Company user records, not every MSP technician or external contact. Use Additional Contacts for someone who only needs to be listed. |
| A contact disappeared after saving | Completely blank rows are not stored. Reopen the form and check the saved result and any error. |
| A saved sponsor name is outdated | Reselect the correct user and save. The sponsor name and email are copied from that Company user when this form saves. |
| The client cannot open the portal | Contact details and portal access are separate. See [Company user permissions](/user-guides/settings/change-company-user-permissions). |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Add contact create a portal user or send an invitation?">
    No. It adds a project contact after Save Changes. User creation, access, and invitations are separate MSP workflows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I type a new Client Sponsor name?">
    Choose an existing Company user from the picker. Use Additional Contacts for a person who only needs to be listed, or ask the MSP administrator to manage their user record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not edit the project lead?">
    The lead is read-only here and uses synced project information where available. Change it in the appropriate project-management system.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I remove a contact or sponsor?">
    Remove an additional-contact row, or select the checked sponsor again to clear it, then Save Changes. This does not delete a user account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are contact changes saved immediately?">
    No. Select Save Changes. Closing the form without saving discards its edits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does choosing a sponsor assign decisions or notify them?">
    No. Use separate [project requests](/user-guides/projects/manage-project-actions) for assignments and explicit notification controls.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
