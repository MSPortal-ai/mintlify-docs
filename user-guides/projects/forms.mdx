This page shows MSP staff and clients how to fill a form on a project, save work in progress, submit it, and share forms with clients. Forms work on regular projects as well as projects linked to an onboarding portal.

The response table sits directly under the project navigation. **Fill a form** opens the form picker from its toolbar, and the form opens in a dialog so the response list keeps its space.

## Before you start

- You need access to the project and permission to view forms.
- The **Submit and edit form responses** permission lets you save entries.
- The **Share forms with clients** permission lets staff choose which forms clients can use.
- Clients see only forms shared with their project and their own responses. Sharing a form does not show them earlier internal responses.
- An admin builds and turns on forms under **Settings > Projects > Templates > Forms**. See [Build reusable forms](/user-guides/client-onboarding/forms).

## Fill and save a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Forms">
    Open **Projects**, click the project, then click **Files & Forms** and **Forms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a form">
    Click **Fill a form** in the response table toolbar and choose a form. Its fields open in a dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your progress">
    Click **Save progress** to keep unfinished answers. The entry shows **In progress**, and you can reopen it later. Typing alone does not save. You get an **Unsaved changes** prompt if you try to leave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit** when the required fields are done. Close the dialog and check the entry and its status in the response table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it later">
    Click **Edit** on a response to an active form to continue or correct it. If the form allows more than one entry, click **One More** to start another.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Using **Save progress** on a submitted response returns it to **In progress**. Click **Submit** again when your changes are done.

A form that allows one entry keeps one internal staff response and one response per client. A form that allows several entries creates a new response each time, so edit an existing response when you mean to correct it.

## Share or stop sharing a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In **Files & Forms > Forms**, click **Fill a form** and choose the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn sharing on">
    Turn on **Share this form with clients**. It saves at once and shows **Form sharing updated**. Clients with form access can fill the form and see their own responses. Internal responses stay private.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn sharing off">
    Turn the switch off to remove client access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Clients still need project access and the right form permissions. Hiding the project from clients also blocks their access to its forms. Staff can still remove sharing from an archived form.

## Read an archived form response

<Note>
Branch preview: reading archived responses comes with the Forms update.
</Note>

Click **View** next to a response to an archived form, or open its response link. You can read the saved answers. You cannot edit, add an entry, **Save progress**, or **Submit** while the form is archived. The form shows **This form is archived. Responses are read-only.**

Archiving does not give anyone more access. Staff still need the project and form permissions, and clients can read only their own responses on a form that is still shared.

## Copy, export, or link a response

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Export menu">
    Open a response and click **Export**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an action">
    Choose **Copy** to copy the answers, **Export CSV** to download them, **Copy form link** for a link to the form, or **Copy response link** for a link to the response.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Links need sign-in and the person's existing access. Copying a link does not share the form or send a message.

## Project responses and onboarding tasks

A project form response is separate from completing an onboarding task. Onboarding clients keep using the forms on their portal tasks. Staff with onboarding access can also see linked-portal responses in the response list, marked **Client portal** under **Source**.

Saving a response does not create or update a Hudu or IT Glue asset. To create one, use **Create asset** in the response's **Export** menu. See [Build reusable forms](/user-guides/client-onboarding/forms#mapping-fields-to-hudu-or-it-glue).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A form is missing | Check that the form is active, that it is shared with the project, and your permissions. |
| **Submit** fails | Check the required fields and any field errors, and your project access. Use **Save progress** if you are not ready. |
| A submitted entry says **In progress** | **Save progress** reopens a submission. Click **Submit** after you finish. |
| A copied link says access denied | Links do not give access. Ask your MSP to check project visibility, form sharing, and who owns the response. |
| An archived response is read-only | Click **View** to read it. Editing is off while the form is archived. |
