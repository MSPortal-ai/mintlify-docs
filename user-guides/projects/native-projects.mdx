This guide shows MSP staff how to run a project entirely in MSPortal, from a template through delivery and close-out. The project, phases, and tickets live in MSPortal, and no PSA records are created. Clients follow the parts you share in their branded portal.

The example is a **Client Workspace Launch** with two phases, three tickets, a decision, and an action item.

## Before you start

- You need an MSP staff account with project write permission and an active subscription. Building or changing templates needs project-settings permission.
- You need a company and a saved project template. If the wizard shows **No templates yet**, follow [Project Templates](/user-guides/projects/templates).
- Turn off **Enable Project Sync** in each connected PSA's sync settings under **Settings > Integrations**, and save. New projects use MSPortal only when no PSA project sync is set up. The PSA can stay connected for everything else.
- Check the company selector and [project display settings](/user-guides/settings/projects). Client users see only what their access allows.

## 1. Create the native project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wizard">
    Open **Projects** and click **New project**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the template">
    Under **Template**, pick your template. Each one shows its phase, ticket, and action counts. Pick a plain project type for this workflow. Onboarding templates add a client portal step and create a linked onboarding project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the company and dates">
    Choose the **Company**, enter the **Project name**, and pick a **Start date**. Check the suggested name before you keep it. Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the destination">
    Wait for the check of the company's PSA integration, and confirm the destination is **MSPortal only**. If it shows a PSA, close the wizard, turn off that PSA's **Enable Project Sync**, save, and open the wizard again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and create">
    Click **Next** and check the template, counts, company, project name, start date, and **Target**. Click **Create project** once. When it finishes, check the created counts and click **Open project**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
While PSA project sync is on, new projects are created in the PSA and need a valid company mapping. Turning sync off does not turn existing PSA projects into native ones. Existing PSA projects keep their provider, and supported edits need its project sync on.
</Note>

## 2. Explain the goal and client access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the details form">
    Click **Edit Project Details** in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the outcome">
    Enter **Project Goal**, **Project Description**, and **Current Phase**, then click **Save Changes**. The current-phase label does not move or complete the delivery phases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose client visibility">
    Tick **Hide from company users** to keep the project out of client views. Leaving it unticked does not override company scope, role permissions, or **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Client project access**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Also check the visibility of each update, milestone, decision, and deliverable. Keep internal notes internal and share only what the client should see.

## 3. Review and adjust the delivery plan

Open **Project Plan > Tasks**, expand each phase, and check the generated tickets, dates, hours, and assignments.

| Action | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| Add a phase | Click **Add phase**, enter its name, description, and optional start and end dates, then save. |
| Edit a phase | Open the phase's action menu, click **Edit phase**, change the fields, and save. |
| Add a ticket | Click **Add ticket**, enter its title and details, pick its **Phase**, pick an assignee and priority if needed, and save. |
| Edit a ticket | Open **Edit or delete ticket** and click **Edit ticket**. Check the phase, status, priority, budget hours, dates, and assignee, then save. |
| Remove unused work | Use **Delete phase** or **Delete ticket** and read the confirmation. Move or delete a phase's tickets before you delete the phase. Tickets with logged time cannot be deleted. |

Native ticket statuses are **Open**, **In Progress**, **Blocked**, and **Done**. Changing a project goal or posting a client update does not change ticket status.

Open **Project Plan > Board** and use **Group by** to read tickets by status, owner, or priority. The owner label follows your PSA's wording. The native board has no drag controls, so use **Project Plan > Tasks > Edit ticket** to change status, assignee, or priority. See [Read and filter project ticket Kanban](/user-guides/projects/read-ticket-kanban). Use **Project Plan > Schedule** to review dated work. See [Project Workspace](/user-guides/projects/workspace) and [Project Calendar](/user-guides/projects/calendar).

<Note>
If a native ticket shows in **Other**, open **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Status Mapping** and look under **Ticket Statuses**. Put **Done** in your completed column and **Blocked** in a blocked or on-hold column, then click **Save**. Keep any mappings your PSA projects still use.
</Note>

## 4. Record work and time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Log time">
    Open **Overview > MSP Dashboard** and click **Log time** in the time section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the entry">
    Pick a ticket or **Project (no ticket)**, set **Work date**, and enter **Duration** and **Notes**. Duration takes hours (`1.5`), hours and minutes (`1:30`), or minutes (`90m`). **Start / end** is an alternative. Check **Billable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**, then check the new entry and the hours total. Native time stays in MSPortal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Tenants without an active PSA integration also get **Start timer** in the project header. PSA-connected tenants do not see **Log time** or new native timers, including on native records made for onboarding. You can still stop and save a timer that is already running. A running timer is not a time entry until you stop it and save the entry.

Write clear notes. They help your team and feed AI summaries and project updates. Check the project and ticket before you save time.

## 5. Track milestones, decisions, and deliverables

- **Project Plan > Milestones** tracks milestone dates and completion.
- **Collaboration > Decisions** holds approvals, questions, scheduling requests, and other answers you need to move work forward.
- **Collaboration > Action Items** holds follow-up tasks apart from decisions and delivery tickets.
- **Project Plan > Deliverables** tracks outputs, due dates, completion, and files.

Create each item in its own list, or use a phase or ticket action to start a linked item. Editors save as you type. A new item is created at once, so fill in its title, dates, owner, and visibility in the same sitting.

In the example, record approval of the launch plan, prepare the handover guide, and track client acceptance. Completing an action item does not complete the project tickets.

## 6. Explain progress with AI and updates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Project AI">
    Click **Open Project AI** at the right edge of the project page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a draft">
    Click **Draft update**, or ask for a summary of current work, blockers, and next steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and apply">
    Check the reporting week, progress, next steps, risks, and client visibility on the proposal, then click **Apply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Updates">
    Open **Collaboration > Updates** and confirm the saved update. Text in the chat alone is not a saved update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also write updates yourself under **Collaboration > Updates**. A client-visible update shows in the project for people who have access. It does not send an email or a meeting invitation.

See [Project AI, MCP, and Client Reviews](/user-guides/projects/ai-and-client-reviews) for examples and connected workflows.

## 7. Review the project with the client

Use the project's progress, milestones, deliverables, and open decisions to prepare the conversation. Add project blocks to a QBR report or add the project to a meeting agenda. Confirm the report or meeting is scoped to the right company and inspect the actual content before sharing it.

Client access to a project, a report, and a meeting follows the permissions of each surface. Preparing a report or draft meeting does not itself send it to attendees.

## 8. Finish and close out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm with the client">
    Confirm the acceptance criteria and the final deliverables.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the work">
    Mark finished tickets **Done**. Resolve the remaining decisions and action items. Complete the milestones and deliverables.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish a final update">
    Check the time records, then post an update that explains the outcome, the handover, and any follow-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the project">
    Set the project's status to **Completed**. For example, ask **Project AI** to propose the status change, check the target project, and click **Apply**. This closes the project and records its completion date. Health and status are separate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it later">
    In **Projects**, click **Closed** or **All** to find completed work that no longer shows under **Open**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Keep the project as the delivery record. Closeout does not require deleting its tickets, time, decisions, or updates. Reuse the template for the next engagement and update the template deliberately when the process improves.

## Forms and your workspace

A native project can collect forms from staff and clients without an onboarding portal. Staff choose which forms to share, and client roles need form-view and submission permissions. See [Project Forms](/user-guides/projects/forms).
## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **New project** is missing | You need MSP staff access and project write permission. |
| No template can be picked | Create and save a template, and check that it is not archived. |
| The wizard shows a PSA destination | Turn off **Enable Project Sync** in the PSA integration settings, save, and reopen the wizard. Do this for every PSA that has project sync on. |
| A save fails | Read the message, and check the required fields, the date order, and your permissions. Check whether the record exists before you create it again. |
| Clients cannot see the project or an item | Check the company selector, their role, the project's visibility, the item's visibility, and display settings. |
| The AI described a draft but **Updates** is empty | Review the proposal and click **Apply**, then check **Collaboration > Updates**. |
| A completed project still shows | Check **Open / Closed / All**, your filters, and the project status. Completing a ticket does not close the project. |
