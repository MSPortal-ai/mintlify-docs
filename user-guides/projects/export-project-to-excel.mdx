**Audience:** MSP staff who can open the project. The **Export to Excel** button is not shown to Company users, because the workbook includes internal content such as hours, budget, and items that are not client visible.

Open **Projects**, select a project, and choose **Export to Excel** in the page header. MSPortal downloads an `.xlsx` workbook named after the project. The export works for PSA-synced and native MSPortal projects and changes nothing in MSPortal or your PSA.

## What the workbook contains

The workbook always has an **Overview** sheet. Every other sheet appears only when the project has rows for it.

| Sheet | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Company, source, status, health and health reason, current phase, project type, dates, percent complete, hours, budget and actual cost, customer PO, outcome, summary, details, contacts, project custom fields, and a linked list of the other sheets with their row counts. |
| **Phases** | WBS, phase, parent phase, status, scheduled and actual dates, deadline, scheduled, budget and actual hours, milestone flag, and task and open-task counts. |
| **Tasks** | WBS, PSA reference, task, phase, status, priority, owner, budget and actual hours, closed flag and date, scheduled dates, description, and task custom fields. |
| **Time Entries** | Date, member, task, phase, hours, billable option, work type, and notes, oldest first. |
| **Team** | ConnectWise project team assignments with role, work role, hours, and dates. |
| **Milestones** | Title, status, planned and actual dates, client visibility, linked phase or task, description, and notes. |
| **Updates** | Week, summary, done, next, risks, decisions needed, posted date and author, and client visibility. |
| **Decisions** and **Action Items** | Title, type, status, urgency, due date, assignees, description, response, completion, and linked phase or task. |
| **Deliverables** | Title, status, due and completed dates, client visibility, linked phase or task, description, and document names. |

## Work with the sheets

- Each data sheet has a frozen header row with filters, so you can sort and filter in Excel straight away.
- Hours and counts are numeric cells. **Phases**, **Tasks**, **Time Entries**, and **Team** end with a **Total** row.
- Phases and tasks follow WBS order.
- Documents are listed by name. The files themselves stay in MSPortal.
- The workbook uses your tenant's brand color and your current language. Custom fields follow your role's field access.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| The button is missing | You are signed in as a Company user. The export is for MSP staff. |
| A sheet is missing | The project has no rows for that part. **Team** appears only for ConnectWise projects with team assignments. |
| **Details** is blank on a PSA project | The workbook prints Details only when the project has written text there. Use **Summary** and **Outcome** for PSA-synced projects. |
