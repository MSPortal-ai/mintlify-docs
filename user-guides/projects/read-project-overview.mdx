Open **Projects**, select the project, and choose **Overview > Dashboard**. Company users see only information available to their account; the MSP manages project access and display settings.

## Read the MSP Dashboard

MSP staff use **MSP Dashboard** for operational work. On projects without an onboarding portal, it combines:

- Open and unassigned task counts, pending decisions and action items, and actual versus budget hours. The budget tile shows the percentage used when both values are available.
- Up to five open tasks, ordered by scheduled end date (or start date when no end is set), with undated work last. Rows include available owner, phase, priority, status, hours, and end date. Select **Unassigned open tasks** to narrow the preview, or **Open tasks** to restore it. **Schedule** opens the full plan when your role permits it.
- Up to five pending decisions and action items. Select a title to open its record or use the register buttons for the full lists.
- The latest weekly update, expanded description and deliverables preview, company, status, health, current phase, shared milestones, hours, budget, dates, and contacts. Project display settings still apply.

Linked onboarding projects retain their onboarding overview beneath the summary rail. Use **Overview > Team & Contacts** for onboarding invite contacts and assigned staff, and **Overview > Client Portal** for portal administration. Company users do not see these staff management areas.

## Read the Client Dashboard

Staff can switch to **Client Dashboard** within Dashboard. Company users receive the permitted client view without the staff switch. A recorded **0%** completion is shown; an unavailable percentage stays absent. Disabling dates also hides the next milestone's date.

## Read the summary

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the project and Company">
    Check the project name and Company before interpreting its status, health, dates, or contacts. Status and health are separate indicators; neither replaces the underlying work and updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the narrative">
    **Project Summary** contains the project's summary when available and enabled. **Project Outcome** contains the description maintained by the MSP. Use **Show more** to expand long text and **Show less** to collapse it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the latest Weekly Summary">
    Review **Completed**, **Coming Up**, **Risks & Concerns**, and **Decisions Needed** when present. The summary is selected by its **Week of** date, not its last edit time. Open [Updates](/user-guides/projects/read-project-updates) for other weeks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the relevant detail">
    The top information bar can show **Phase**, **Goal**, and the next shared pending or in-progress milestone. **What You're Getting** previews up to five deliverables; open [Deliverables](/user-guides/projects/read-project-deliverables) for the full available list and files. Respond to assigned requests in [Decisions or Action Items](/user-guides/projects/respond-to-project-actions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The next milestone can be overdue; “Next” does not guarantee a future date. A Phase label describes the current phase and is separate from the detailed plan in Phases. Summary cards do not approve work or change its status.

## Understand the different measures

| Measure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Tickets: Total, Open, Closed** | Counts of tickets supplied to this view. Its bar compares Closed with Total; this section appears when more than one ticket is available. |
| **Completion** | The project's recorded completion percentage, which can come from its connected project system. It is separate from ticket, milestone, and deliverable counts. |
| **Milestones** | Complete divided by shared milestones. Internal MSP milestones are excluded from this Client Dashboard measure. |
| **Hours: Logged, Billable, Estimated** | Recorded project hours and estimate when available. The bar compares Logged with Estimated; using the estimate does not mean the project is complete. |
| **Budget and Actual** | Available monetary values when enabled and shared. They are not an invoice or a payment confirmation. |

A missing measure can mean it is disabled, not shared, unavailable, or zero. A recorded completion of zero is shown. Ticket visibility and display settings can affect the available counts. Ask your MSP when figures disagree; do not assume all progress bars should match.

Some small projects show one ticket directly above the summary, including its status, owner, description, and available hours. Detailed time-entry cards live on the staff-only **MSP Dashboard**. Company users use the shared summary hours when enabled.

## Review dates and contacts

**Details** may show **Type**, **Budget Type**, **PO Number**, **Started**, **Target Finish** or **Overdue**, **Deadline**, and **Last Sync**. Deadline appears separately when it differs from Target Finish. These values depend on available project data and display settings. Last Sync describes a synchronization timestamp, not proof that every part of the workspace changed at that moment.

Under **Contacts**, review **Project Lead**, **Client Sponsor**, and **Additional Contact** entries. Select an email or phone link to open your device's email or calling application. The copy control beside a lead or sponsor copies the email address; it does not send a message.

<Note>
Branch preview: when your MSP enables the upcoming Teams chat setting, a small Teams icon beside **Project Lead** opens a direct chat with the manager. It appears only when a valid manager email is available. See [project contacts](/user-guides/projects/manage-project-contacts#chat-with-the-project-manager-in-teams).
</Note>

Custom fields may appear separately with the values your role can read. **Not set** means no displayed value, not that a requirement has been satisfied. Ask the MSP about missing fields, corrections, or access. MSP staff can [edit the project narrative](/user-guides/projects/edit-project-details) and [maintain contacts](/user-guides/projects/manage-project-contacts).

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why are there no weekly updates on Client Dashboard?">
    There may be no update available to you. Ask whether your MSP has written and shared one; an empty summary does not mean no project work has happened.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a recently edited update not the Weekly Summary?">
    Client Dashboard selects the update with the latest Week of date. Open Updates to read other reporting weeks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does 100% of estimated hours mean the project is finished?">
    No. That measures hours used against the estimate. Check project status, completion, deliverables, and outstanding requests separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can my MSP see more milestones than the Client Dashboard total?">
    Client Dashboard counts shared milestones. The MSP's **Project Plan > Milestones** view can include internal milestones as well.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are the rest of the deliverables?">
    What You're Getting previews at most five. Open Deliverables for the full list available to your account and any shared documents.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the project contacts or manage users?">
    Your MSP manages project contacts and user permissions. Being listed as a contact or sponsor does not itself grant portal access or editing rights.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does clicking a contact email send a portal message?">
    It opens your email application. The copy button only copies the address. Neither automatically submits a ticket; for support, [start with the Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
