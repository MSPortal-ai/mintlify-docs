This page shows Company users and MSP staff how to read a project's dashboard: the summary, progress, dates, and contacts.

## Before you start

- You need access to the project. Company users see only what their MSP shares. The MSP manages project access and display settings.
- Open **Projects**, click the project, and click **Overview**.

## Read the MSP Dashboard

MSP staff use **MSP Dashboard** for operational work. On projects without an onboarding portal, it combines:

- Counts of **Open tasks**, **Unassigned open tasks**, pending **Decisions**, and pending **Action Items**, plus **Actual hours** and **Budget hours**. The budget tile shows the percentage used when both values exist.
- Up to five open tasks, ordered by scheduled end date (or start date when there is no end), with undated work last. Rows show the owner, phase, priority, status, hours, and end date when known. Click **Unassigned open tasks** to narrow the list, or **Open tasks** to restore it. **Schedule** opens the full plan when your role allows it.
- Up to five pending decisions and action items. Click a title to open it, or click **Decisions** or **Action Items** for the full lists.
- The latest weekly update, expanded description and deliverables preview, company, status, health, current phase, shared milestones, hours, budget, dates, and contacts. Project display settings still apply.

Linked onboarding projects retain their onboarding overview beneath the summary rail. Use **Team & Contacts** for onboarding invite contacts and assigned staff, and **Client Portal** for portal administration. Company users do not see these staff management areas.

## Read the Client Dashboard

MSP staff click **Client Dashboard** next to **MSP Dashboard** to see what clients see. Company users get the client view without the switch. A recorded **0%** completion is shown, and an unavailable percentage stays blank. Turning dates off in project display settings also hides the next milestone's date.

## Read the summary

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the project and Company">
    Check the project name and company first. Status and health are separate indicators. Neither replaces the actual work and updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the narrative">
    **Project Summary** contains the project's summary when available and enabled. **Project Outcome** contains the description maintained by the MSP. Use **Show more** to expand long text and **Show less** to collapse it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the latest Weekly Summary">
    Review **Completed**, **Coming Up**, **Risks & Concerns**, and **Decisions Needed** when present. The summary is selected by its **Week of** date, not its last edit time. Open [Updates](/user-guides/projects/read-project-updates) for other weeks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the relevant detail">
    The summary panel on the right shows **Phase**, **Goal**, and the **Next** shared milestone that is pending or in progress. **What You're Getting** previews up to five deliverables. Open [Deliverables](/user-guides/projects/read-project-deliverables) for the full list and files. Answer requests assigned to you in [Decisions or Action Items](/user-guides/projects/respond-to-project-actions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Next** milestone can be overdue. The **Phase** label is separate from the detailed plan in **Project Plan > Tasks**.

## Understand the different measures

| Measure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Tickets: Total, Open, Closed** | Counts of tickets supplied to this view. Its bar compares Closed with Total; this section appears when more than one ticket is available. |
| **Completion** | The project's recorded completion percentage, which can come from its connected project system. It is separate from ticket, milestone, and deliverable counts. |
| **Milestones** | Complete divided by shared milestones. Internal MSP milestones are excluded from this Client Dashboard measure. |
| **Hours: Logged, Billable, Estimated** | Recorded project hours and estimate when available. The bar compares Logged with Estimated; using the estimate does not mean the project is complete. |
| **Budget and Actual** | Available monetary values when enabled and shared. They are not an invoice or a payment confirmation. |

A missing measure can mean it is turned off, not shared, unavailable, or zero. Ticket visibility and display settings change the counts. The progress bars do not have to match each other. Ask your MSP if figures disagree.

Small projects with one ticket show that ticket above the summary, with its status, owner, description, and hours. Detailed time entries are on the staff-only **MSP Dashboard**. Company users see the shared summary hours when they are turned on.

## Review dates and contacts

**Details** can show **Type**, **Budget Type**, **PO Number**, **Started**, **Target Finish** (or **Overdue**), **Deadline**, and **Last Sync**. **Deadline** appears when it differs from **Target Finish**. What shows depends on the project data and display settings. **Last Sync** is the time of the last PSA sync.

Under **Contacts**, you see **Project Lead**, **Client Sponsor**, and **Additional Contact** entries. Click an email or phone link to open your email or phone app. The copy button beside a lead or sponsor copies the email address and sends nothing.

<Note>
Branch preview: when your MSP enables the upcoming Teams chat setting, a small Teams icon beside **Project Lead** opens a direct chat with the manager. It appears only when a valid manager email is available. See [project contacts](/user-guides/projects/manage-project-contacts#chat-with-the-project-manager-in-teams).
</Note>

Custom fields show the values your role can read. **Not set** means there is no value yet. Ask your MSP about missing fields or corrections. MSP staff can [edit the project narrative](/user-guides/projects/edit-project-details) and [maintain contacts](/user-guides/projects/manage-project-contacts).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No weekly summary shows | No update has been shared with you yet. Ask your MSP. Open [Updates](/user-guides/projects/read-project-updates) to check. |
| A figure is missing | It may be turned off, not shared, or not available from the PSA. Ask your MSP. |
| The Teams icon is missing beside **Project Lead** | Your MSP has not turned the setting on, or the manager has no valid email. |
| You cannot see **MSP Dashboard** | It is for MSP staff only. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why are there no weekly updates on Client Dashboard?">
    Your MSP may not have written or shared one yet. An empty summary does not mean no work has happened.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a recently edited update not the Weekly Summary?">
    Client Dashboard selects the update with the latest Week of date. Open Updates to read other reporting weeks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does 100% of estimated hours mean the project is finished?">
    No. That measures hours used against the estimate. Check project status, completion, deliverables, and outstanding requests separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can my MSP see more milestones than the Client Dashboard total?">
    Client Dashboard counts shared milestones. The MSP's **Project Plan > Milestones** view can include internal milestones as well.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are the rest of the deliverables?">
    What You're Getting previews at most five. Open Deliverables for the full list available to your account and any shared documents.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the project contacts or manage users?">
    No. Your MSP manages contacts and permissions. Being listed as a contact or sponsor does not give you portal access or editing rights.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does clicking a contact email send a portal message?">
    No. It opens your email app. The copy button only copies the address. For support, [start with the Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
