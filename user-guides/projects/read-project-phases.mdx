This page shows Company users and MSP staff how to read a project's phases and tasks in **Project Plan > Tasks**. Company users read the plan their MSP shares. MSP staff use the same list and may have extra editing actions.

## Before you start

- You need access to the project.
- Hours and time details depend on what your MSP shares. Company users do not see detailed time history.
- A project with no phases and at most one ticket uses the simpler Client Dashboard instead of this list. MSP editors of native projects always see **Tasks**.

## Find the work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tasks">
    Open **Projects**, click the project, then click **Project Plan** and **Tasks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or filter">
    Type in **Search tasks...** to filter by task. MSP staff can use the **Owner** menu to show **All tasks**, **MSP tasks**, or **Client tasks**. Use the **Source** menu to show **All sources**, **PSA**, **MSPortal**, or **Both**: **Both** lists PSA tickets linked to portal tasks, plus every task in a phase that exists in both. When a project has more than one portal, pick it from the **Portal** menu in the same bar. On a wide screen, the **Task index** on the left jumps to a phase or task.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a phase">
    Click a phase to expand it. Each phase shows its status icon, name, source badges, how many tasks are done (for example "2/5 tasks"), its status, and a progress bar. A PSA phase and a portal phase with the same name show as one phase with both **PSA** and **MSPortal** badges. The flag icon links to the phase's milestone; hover it to see the milestone name. **Unphased Tickets**, when present, lists tickets with no phase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read a task row">
    Each row shows the title, a status badge, the ticket number, the owner, the end date (or start date), and hours as logged/budget. A struck-through title with a green check is a closed task. Staff may also see an **Internal** or **Not scheduled** badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the details">
    Click a task title to open its ticket details. Custom fields and, when your MSP turns on time entries, recent time entries show under the row.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand the numbers

| Measure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Phase progress bar and "x/y tasks" | Closed tasks divided by all tasks in the phase. It is not a measure of hours. |
| Task hours (logged/budget) | Actual hours and the budget when one is set. Going over budget does not close or block the task. |
| Phase status icon | Shows done when every task is closed, otherwise in progress or not started. |
| **Milestone** flag | The milestone linked to the phase, if there is one. Click it to open **Project Plan > Milestones**. |

Zero or missing hours do not mean no work happened. Hours may be unshared, unavailable, or not yet synced. Ask your MSP if totals look wrong.

## Read time details (MSP staff)

When time entries are turned on in project display settings, MSP staff see a task's entries under its row, with **Date**, **Person**, **Duration**, **Type**, **Notes**, and **Billable**. Click **Load more** when another page exists. **Check data** flags entries whose total is zero. Billable is not an invoice.

Company users do not get this detail. Ask your MSP for a shared summary.

## Control task visibility and scheduling (MSP staff)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the task's menu">
    Click **Ticket actions** on the task row and choose the **Visibility and scheduling** option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the controls">
    Turn on **Internal only** to hide the task from Company users everywhere in MSPortal. Turn off **Include in scheduling** to keep it off the [Dispatch board](/user-guides/projects/dispatch-board), out of its planner and Smart Schedule, and off the Gantt. The task, its bookings and its dependency links are kept and come back when you turn it on again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset to the default">
    Click **Use type default** to remove an individual override. Each change saves at once, and the same switch undoes it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Tasks use their task type's defaults unless you set an override. Admins set defaults in **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Task types**. See [Task controls](/user-guides/settings/projects#task-types-and-individual-task-controls).

For the actions on phase and ticket menus, see [Use phase and ticket action menus](/user-guides/projects/use-phase-ticket-actions).

## Find and assign unassigned tasks (MSP staff)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the unassigned tasks">
    On **Overview > MSP Dashboard**, click **Unassigned open tasks**. **Tasks** opens in the table view, filtered to open tasks with no owner. In the table you can also set the **Owner** filter to **Unassigned** yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the tasks">
    Tick the checkbox on each task, or tick the header checkbox to select every task the filters show. A task you cannot assign has a disabled checkbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign them">
    The table header shows how many tasks are selected and one picker for each kind of task in the selection:
    - **Assign staff** sets a staff member on your client portal tasks. Choose **Clear assignment** to remove it.
    - **Assign contacts** sets portal contacts on the client's own portal tasks. Pick contacts, or click **Select all**, then click **Assign to N tasks**. With no contact picked, the button reads **All contacts** and returns the tasks to the default, where every portal contact can see them.
    - **Assign owner** sets the owner of PSA tasks, including portal tasks that were sent to your PSA. It lists the people your PSA allows on the project and writes the change to your PSA.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Staff and contact assignment need permission to edit onboarding. Changing a PSA task's owner needs permission to edit projects, as on the Board.

An Autotask task needs a Work Type before it can be assigned. If one is missing, the message names it; set it in Autotask and try again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Tasks** is missing | The project may have no phases and one ticket or fewer, or your role lacks access. Ask your MSP to check access and display settings. |
| A phase will not expand | It may have no visible tasks. Phase status and task visibility are separate. |
| A task is missing | Clear **Search tasks...** and set **Source** to **All sources**. The task may be internal. |
| Hours show 0h | Hours may be unshared or unsynced. Ask your MSP. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a full progress bar mean the phase is finished?">
    The bar counts closed tasks. At 100% every task in the phase is closed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I open a support ticket from these rows?">
    No. These rows are existing project work. To ask for help, use the Home AI Assistant. See [Open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can Company users not see time notes?">
    Detailed time history is for MSP staff. A zero-entry result for a Company user reflects access, not missing work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are tasks from an old client portal not listed?">
    Tasks an archived client portal sent to your PSA are left out of the project, along with phases that held only those tasks. They stay in your PSA. Unarchive the portal to bring them back.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
