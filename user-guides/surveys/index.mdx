Use Surveys to collect feedback from clients with Net Promoter Score (NPS) surveys and custom questionnaires. This guide is for MSP staff who build, send and manage surveys. Company users who answer a survey should read [Complete a questionnaire](/user-guides/surveys/complete-a-survey) or [Respond to an NPS survey](/user-guides/surveys/respond-to-nps).

## Before you start

- Surveys must appear in your sidebar. That needs a role with **Surveys** read access.
- To create, edit or send a survey, you need **Surveys** write access. The **New Survey** button and the **Survey Groups** menu item appear only with write access.
- To delete a survey or a group, or to open **Configure** and **Activity Log**, you need **Surveys** manage access.
- You need at least one company or contact to send a survey to.
- Ask your MSP administrator to change roles in **Settings > Users & Roles**. The **Surveys** section of the role editor also has opt-in choices for **All surveys** or **Own surveys only**, **All responses** or **Own responses only**, **Edit submitted responses** and **View CSAT dashboards**.

## Find your way around

1. Click **Surveys** in the sidebar. The list of surveys opens.
2. Click the **Surveys Menu** button in the page header to jump to other areas.

| Menu item | What it opens | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The list of surveys | Everyone with Surveys access |
| **CSAT Dashboard** | Support satisfaction dashboard | Roles with **View CSAT dashboards**, when CSAT is available |
| **Survey Groups** | Recipient groups | Write access |
| **Activity Log** | Who created, sent, edited and deleted surveys | Manage access |
| **Configure** | Survey settings and email templates | Manage access |

## Create a survey

Every survey goes through a three-step wizard. The steps are **Create**, **Recipients** and **Send**. The footer shows **Step 1 of 3** and so on. Use **Next** and **Back** to move, or click a completed step at the top.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wizard">
    On the Surveys list, click **New Survey**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the survey type">
    Pick one tile.

    - **NPS Survey** (Recommended) measures loyalty with the Net Promoter Score (0-10 scale).
    - **General Survey** lets you build your own questions.
    - **CSAT Campaign** appears only when CSAT is available for your portal. It collects a rating per ticket through a snippet in your PSA's resolution email. Fill in **Campaign name** and click **Create Campaign**. The campaign skips the Recipients and Send steps and opens its CSAT dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the survey details">
    Enter a **Survey Title** (required), a **Description** and optional **Introduction Text**. The introduction appears at the top of the survey, above the title. Leave it blank to hide it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design the questions">
    Follow [Set up an NPS survey](#set-up-an-nps-survey) or [Set up a general survey](#set-up-a-general-survey) below, then click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose recipients">
    Follow [Choose recipients](#choose-recipients) below, then click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    Follow [Review, send or schedule](#review-send-or-schedule) below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Set up an NPS survey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the NPS question">
    Edit **NPS Question**. The default is "How likely are you to recommend us to a friend or colleague?" Recipients answer it on a 0-10 scale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a rating style">
    Under **Rating Style**, choose **Numeric Scale (0-10)**, **5 Stars**, **Satisfaction Faces** or **Happy Dogs**. Each tile shows a preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the thank-you message">
    Edit **Thank You Message**. Recipients see it after they submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: add review links">
    Expand **Review Link Prompts** and turn on **Show review links**. Choose a **Score threshold** and enter the **Review platform URLs** you want to show.

    - **Promoters only (9-10)** is the default and the safest choice.
    - **Passives & promoters (7-10)** and **All respondents (0-10)** also show the links to less happy clients.

    The platforms are Google Reviews, Yelp, Facebook Reviews, Trustpilot, G2, Capterra and one custom platform. Defaults for all new surveys live in [Change survey defaults](#change-survey-defaults).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Set up a general survey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a question">
    Click **Add Question**. A new question card opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the question">
    Enter the **Question Text** (required) and choose a **Type**.

    The types are **Short Text**, **Long Text**, **Single Choice**, **Multiple Choice**, **Rating**, **NPS Score**, **Yes/No**, **Date**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add options for choice questions">
    For **Single Choice** and **Multiple Choice**, type each option on its own line in **Options**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark required questions">
    Turn on **Required** to make respondents answer the question. Add **Help Text (Optional)** for extra guidance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder or remove questions">
    Use **Move up** and **Move down** to change the order. Click the arrow on a card to expand or collapse it. Click the trash icon to delete a question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Design questions with the AI Survey Designer

The designer works on the **Create** step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the designer">
    Click the sparkle tab on the right edge of the page. It is labeled **Open assistant**, or **AI Survey Designer** on portals without the shared assistant. The assistant opens in survey design mode and works on the survey in the wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you want">
    Type a request in plain language, or pick one of the prompt helpers under the input. For example, "Add questions about service quality and response times."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the suggested changes">
    Open **Review Survey Changes**. Accept all changes, accept or reject single changes, or refine your request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the changes">
    Apply the accepted changes. They update the draft in the wizard and nothing is sent. Use **Undo last changes** if you change your mind.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Choose recipients

On the **Recipients** step, pick one tile. The count at the top shows **N recipients**.

| Tile | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **All Clients** | Sends to all active client contacts with valid emails. You must tick **I understand that every contact will receive this email**. |
| **Survey Groups** | Choose one or more groups under **Select Survey Groups**. You can also add individual emails under **Add Individual Recipients (Optional)**. |
| **Companies** | Choose companies under **Select Companies**. The step shows how many contacts each company has. |
| **Manual Entry** | Add each person with **Add Recipient**. Pick an optional company and company user, or type an **Email** and an optional name. |
| **No Recipients** | Creates the survey without sending any invitations. Use it for onboarding templates, automations or public links, or add recipients later. |

### Review, send or schedule

On the **Send** step, the **Survey Summary** shows the title, type, question count and recipients.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the email subject">
    Edit **Email Subject** (required). The default is "We'd love your feedback."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an email template">
    Choose an **Email Template**, or choose **Create New Template**. Use **Preview template** to see the email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when to send">
    Under **Schedule**, pick one option.

    - **Send Now** sends the survey immediately.
    - **Schedule** sends it at the **Date** and **Time** you set.
    - **Recurring** repeats it at a **Frequency** of **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Annually**. Set an optional **End Date (Optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set reminders">
    Under **Reminders**, turn on **Automatic reminders** to nudge people who have not responded. Set **Days between reminders** and **Maximum reminders**. Reminders stop after a response, and reminders more than 7 days overdue are skipped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send, schedule or create">
    Click the main button in the footer. It reads **Send Survey** for Send Now, **Schedule Survey** for scheduled or recurring surveys, and **Create Survey** when there are no recipients. For Send Now, confirm **Send this survey now?** with **Send now**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To keep your work without sending, click **Save Draft** on any step. When you edit an existing survey, the button reads **Save** and saves your changes to that survey.

## Create a survey group

Survey groups let you reuse the same recipients. They need write access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Survey Groups">
    Open the **Surveys Menu** and click **Survey Groups**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a group">
    Click **Create Group**. The **Create Survey Group** dialog opens on the **Details** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter a **Group Name** (required) and a description. Pick a **Group Color**. Keep **Group is active** on so the group can be used in survey targeting. Choose **Tenant-Wide (available to all companies)** or select a company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: add rules">
    Open the **Rules** tab to add members automatically.

    1. Under **Rule Logic**, choose **Match ALL rules (AND)** or **Match ANY rule (OR)**.
    2. Click **Add Rule**. Pick **Include** or **Exclude**, then a field and a value.
    3. Click **Preview Matching Users** to see who matches.
    4. Turn on **Auto-Update Membership** to add and remove members automatically when the rules match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the group">
    Click **Create Group**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people by hand">
    Open the group again from its row menu with **Edit Group**. On the **Manual Members** tab, tick the people to include and click **Save Members**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
With auto-update on, members are added or removed based on the rules. Existing manual members may be removed if they do not match the rules.
</Warning>

To delete a group, open its row menu and choose **Delete**. That needs manage access and removes all members from the group.

## Run AI analysis

AI analysis summarizes the answers to a questionnaire. It needs at least one response.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the survey results">
    Open a general survey from the list. Use the **Analysis** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a date range">
    Optional. Use the start and end date pickers above the table to narrow the answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the analysis">
    Click **Run now**. Large surveys can take 30 to 60 seconds. The tab then shows **Overall Sentiment**, **Key Themes** with weight and evidence, **Follow-up Questions** and a **Word Map**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn a theme into a goal">
    Optional. Click **Create Goal** next to a theme to start a follow-up goal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When AI is enabled for your portal, the page header also has an **Analyze** menu. **Analyze all responses** runs the analysis and opens the **Analysis** tab. **Analyze selected respondents** opens the **Analysis** tab.

## Response notifications

Email your account team each time a questionnaire survey receives a response. The setup copies a built-in automation rule. You review and enable it in **Settings > Automation**.

The **Response notifications** button shows in a survey's page header when your role has write or manage access to **Automation Rules** settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the setup">
    Open a questionnaire's results page and click **Response notifications**. The **Import automation** dialog opens with the **Survey response email** rule set chosen and the current survey selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which surveys notify">
    Keep the selected **Survey**, or pick another active survey. Tick **All surveys** to send the notification for every current and future survey.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the team role">
    In **Team role**, pick the company role to email, such as vCIO. MSPortal emails the members assigned to that role for the company of each response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a fallback recipient">
    In **Fallback recipient**, choose **MSPortal team member** and select a person, or choose **Email address** and enter one address. A fallback is required. Only active staff with an email address are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import and enable the rule">
    Click **Import rule set**. MSPortal creates the rule disabled and opens it in the rule editor. Check the recipients, subject and body, then enable the rule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The rule is named **Survey response email notification**, followed by the survey title and the role name. Rules for **All surveys** leave out the survey title. If a rule with that name exists, the import stops. Rename or remove the old rule first.

The fallback receives the email only when no active member of the selected role has a valid email for the response's company, or when the response has no company. See [Send Email](/user-guides/settings/automation/wizard#action-types) for how fallbacks work in any rule.

You can also import the same rule set from **Settings > Automation** with the **Import** button.

## Change survey defaults

Open the **Surveys Menu** and click **Configure**. This needs manage access. The **Survey Settings** page has the tabs **NPS**, **CSAT** (when CSAT is available) and **Email Templates**.

### Set NPS defaults

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the NPS tab">
    The **Survey Behavior Defaults** card applies to new NPS surveys. You can override each setting per survey.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the behavior">
    Set **Automatic Survey Sending**, **Require Comment for Detractors** (scores 0-6), **Default Follow-up Delay (hours)** and **Default Thank You Message**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set default review links">
    In **Default Review Platform Links**, turn on **Show Review Links for High Scores**, set the **Default Score Threshold** and enter the platform URLs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Defaults** at the top right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Webhook Notifications** card is marked **Coming Soon** and does nothing yet.

### Turn on low NPS alerts

The **Low NPS Alerts** card emails people when an NPS response comes in at or below the detractor threshold. It has its own save button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable alerts">
    Turn on **Enable low-NPS alerts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the threshold">
    Under **Threshold (NPS score ≤)**, choose a score from 0 to 10. The default is **6 of 10 or below**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add recipients">
    Type addresses into **Notify emails (comma-separated)**, for example `alerts@msp.com, manager@msp.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save NPS Alerts**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each alert email names the company, the score, the respondent, the time submitted and the comment. It says "No comment left" when there is none.

### Create an email template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Email Templates tab">
    Click **Create Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the template">
    Enter the **Template Name**, **Subject Line** and **Email Content (HTML)**. Add an optional **Plain Text Version**. Click a variable on the right to insert it at your cursor, for example `{{recipient_name}}` or `{{survey_url}}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the template. Use **Preview**, **Duplicate** or delete on a template row later. Tick **Set as default template for survey emails** to make it the default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage surveys

Open a row's menu (three dots) on the Surveys list.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **View Results**, **View Analytics** or **View Dashboard** | Opens the results for that survey type |
| **Preview** | Shows the survey as a recipient sees it |
| **Recipients** | Opens **Survey Recipients** (see below) |
| **Edit Survey** (**Edit Settings** for CSAT) | Reopens the wizard with the survey loaded |
| **Activate** or **Deactivate** | Turns the survey on or off |
| **Delete** | Removes the survey permanently. Manage access only |

The list starts with the **Active** status filter. Change the **Status** filter to see inactive surveys.

### See and add recipients

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Recipients modal">
    From the survey's row menu, choose **Recipients**. The **Survey Recipients** modal opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter and search">
    Use the status filter to show **Completed**, **Sent**, **Expired** or **Pending** invitations. Search by name, email or company. Click a column header to sort.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy or resend a link">
    For invitations that are not completed, click the copy button to copy that person's survey link, or **Resend invitation** to email it again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a recipient">
    Click **Add recipient** and use **Manual**, **Search user** or **From group**. **Add & send** and **Add all & send** email the survey immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The table shows **Recipient**, **Company**, **Status**, **Score** and **Date**. In the same modal, the **Email template** field changes the template used for this survey's sends.

### Versions

Surveys with several versions are grouped in the list as v1, v2 and so on. Click the arrow beside the title to expand them. The latest version shows a green badge. The row menu on a group offers **Edit Latest Version** and **All Versions**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The survey did not send | Check that its status is **Active**, that the **Recipients** step had recipients and that you did not choose **No Recipients**. Check the **Recipients** modal for **Pending** invitations. |
| Recipients say they got no email | Check the addresses, ask them to check spam, and review **Settings > Email**. Use **Resend invitation** in the Recipients modal. |
| The **New Survey** button is missing | Your role has read access only. Ask your MSP administrator for Surveys write access. |
| **Configure** or **Activity Log** is missing | These need Surveys manage access. |
| AI analysis does not run | Check that the survey has responses and that the date range and company selection include them. |
| A group has the wrong members | Open **Edit Group**. Check the **Rules** tab, use **Preview Matching Users**, and check **Auto-Update Membership**. |
| No survey response emails arrive | The imported rule starts disabled. Enable it in **Settings > Automation**. Then check the survey, team role and fallback recipient. |
| A low NPS alert did not arrive | Check that **Enable low-NPS alerts** is on, the threshold covers the score and the recipient addresses are right. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does accepting AI survey changes send invitations?">
    No. Accepted changes update the wizard draft only. Nothing is sent until you use the button on the **Send** step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Save Draft start a scheduled survey?">
    No. **Save Draft** keeps your work without sending. Use **Schedule Survey** on the **Send** step to activate a scheduled or recurring send.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a scheduled survey not email right away?">
    Scheduled and recurring surveys are saved active and sent at the time you set. Only **Send Now** emails immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does editing a survey create a new version?">
    The wizard saves your edits to the same survey. Older version groups in the list come from earlier versions of that survey.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do survey group rules only add people?">
    No. With **Auto-Update Membership** on, people who no longer match can be removed, including people you added by hand.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Sent mean the recipient answered?">
    No. **Sent** means the invitation went out. **Completed** means the person submitted a response.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Create Goal do?">
    It starts a goal from an AI theme or from the answers you select. It does not change the survey.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are low NPS alerts the same as reminders?">
    No. Reminders go to people who have not responded. Low NPS alerts go to the internal addresses you list when a low score arrives.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
