Use this guide to rate a support experience from the email your IT provider sent you. It is for clients who receive a rating link in a ticket email. You do not need to sign in.

## Before you start

- Your provider adds rating choices to a support email, such as **Great**, **Okay** and **Not great**, or a star or number scale.
- Your rating is recorded only after you confirm it. Opening the email or the link does not record anything.
- A questionnaire from your provider is a different task. See [Complete a questionnaire](/user-guides/surveys/complete-a-survey) or [Respond to an NPS survey](/user-guides/surveys/respond-to-nps).

## Confirm your rating

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click a rating in the email">
    Click the rating that matches your experience. A page titled **Confirm your rating** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the rating">
    The page asks whether to share your rating with your provider, for example "Share 5/5 with Your MSP?" If it is not the rating you meant, go back to the email and click the right one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Click **Share feedback**. This is the step that records your rating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for the thank-you page">
    The page shows your rating and says it has been shared with your provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can stop here. A comment is optional.

## Add a comment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your comment">
    On the thank-you page, type in the box under **Want to tell us more? (optional)**. You can write up to 4,000 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether to allow a testimonial">
    Optionally tick the box that gives permission for your comment, name and company to be published as a testimonial. It starts unticked, and you can send a comment without it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the comment">
    Click **Send comment**. When you see **Comment saved. You can close this window.**, you are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You cannot edit a saved comment on this page. The comment link also expires after a while. If you need a correction, contact your provider.

## Answer a follow-up survey

After some ratings, the thank-you page shows **Continue with a short survey**. Click it to answer a few more questions. The follow-up survey has its own **Submit Survey** button. Your original rating is already recorded, even if you skip the survey.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Unable to record feedback** | The link is no longer valid. Ask your provider for a new rating link. |
| You clicked the wrong rating | Before you confirm, go back to the email and click the right one. After you confirm, contact your provider, because the thank-you page cannot change the rating. |
| The comment does not save | Check that it has text and is under 4,000 characters. The comment link may have expired or a comment may already be saved. |
| You do not see the follow-up survey | Your provider shows it only after certain ratings. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does opening the email record a rating?">
    No. Your rating is recorded when you click **Share feedback** on the confirmation page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to write a comment?">
    No. The rating counts without one. The comment saves separately with **Send comment**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Send comment also give permission to publish it?">
    No. Testimonial permission is its own box. Leave it unticked if you do not want your comment, name and company published.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my comment later?">
    No. This page has no edit option. Contact your provider about a correction.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a follow-up survey replace my rating?">
    No. Your rating is already recorded. The survey is a separate response.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my feedback post a public review?">
    No. If the page shows review links, they open other websites and you post there yourself. That is separate from your feedback.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
