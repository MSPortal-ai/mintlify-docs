Use this guide to answer a questionnaire your IT provider emailed you. It is for survey recipients. You do not need to sign in or have any portal permissions.

## Before you start

- Use the email addressed to you. The link identifies you and your company, so do not share it or use a colleague's link.
- Do not assume your answers are anonymous. Your provider can see who responded.
- Finish in one visit if you can. Answers are not saved to your account.

## Answer and submit the questionnaire

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation">
    Click the survey link in your provider's email. Read the survey title and the introduction at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the questions">
    Answer every question marked with an asterisk (*). Questions can ask for short or long text, a single choice, several choices, a rating, a number, a date, an email address, a phone number, or yes or no.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit Survey**. If a required question is missing an answer, the page shows **This question is required**. Fix it and click **Submit Survey** again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for the thank-you message">
    A **Thank You!** page means your response was sent. Wait for it before you close the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choosing an answer does not send it. Only **Submit Survey** sends your response.

## Save your progress on this device

If the page offers **Remember progress on this device**, turn it on to keep your answers in this browser. The page then shows **Progress saved** with the time. If you come back on the same browser, it says **Your previous progress has been restored**.

Click **Clear saved progress** to start over. Saved progress is not a submitted response. You still need to click **Submit Survey**, and the saved answers do not follow you to another device.

## Fix a link or submission problem

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Invalid survey invitation** or **Survey Unavailable** | Open the full link from the original email, or ask your provider for a new invitation. |
| **Survey Expired** | Ask your provider for a current invitation. |
| **You have already completed this survey** | The link takes only one response. Ask your provider if you need to correct an answer. |
| A required question is highlighted | Answer it and submit again. |
| **Failed to submit survey** or **An unexpected error occurred** | Check your connection and try again. If it still fails, tell your provider the question and the message. |
| You are not sure your answers were sent | Reopen the original link. If it says you already completed the survey, your response was received. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to sign in?">
    No. The emailed link opens the survey directly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I finish on another device?">
    No. Saved progress stays in the browser you used. Use one browser from start to finish.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my answers after I submit?">
    No. The survey has no edit option for recipients. Contact your provider to correct an answer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my response anonymous?">
    No, do not assume it is. Your invitation ties the response to you and your company. Some surveys are tied to a support ticket, and your feedback can then appear in that ticket's history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I submit again after a network error?">
    First reopen the original link. If it says you already completed the survey, do not submit again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a colleague's invitation?">
    No. Ask your provider for a separate link for each person.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
