Use this guide to answer a Net Promoter Score (NPS) survey from your IT provider. It is for survey recipients. You do not need to sign in or have any portal permissions.

## Before you start

- Use your own invitation link. It identifies you and your company, so do not assume your response is anonymous.
- The survey asks how likely you are to recommend your provider. Your provider chooses how the scale looks.

## Submit your feedback

  <img src="/images/product/nps-survey-preview.png" alt="Example NPS preview with a 0–10 recommendation scale and optional feedback field" width="2048" height="1132" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation">
    Click the survey link in your provider's email. Read the question and any introduction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a rating">
    Pick the number, star, face or dog that matches your experience. Numbers run from 0 (not at all likely) to 10 (extremely likely). Choosing a rating does not send anything, and you can change it before you submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a comment">
    Optional. Type in **Tell us more (optional)**. You can write up to 1,000 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer any extra questions">
    Your provider can show extra questions for certain scores. Answer the ones that appear. Required questions are marked with an asterisk (*). If you change your rating, check which questions are showing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit Feedback**. The button stays disabled until you choose a rating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the thank-you page">
    A **Thank You!** page means your feedback was recorded. Wait for it before you close the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you submit

The thank-you page can show a message from your provider and links to review sites. A review link opens another website. Your NPS response does not post a public review, so you only review there if you choose to.

Your invitation takes one response. The form has no edit option afterward. If you need to correct your feedback, contact your provider.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Submit Feedback** is disabled | Choose a rating. Wait for any current submission to finish. |
| **Please select a score** | Choose a rating, then submit again. |
| A required question is missing an answer | Answer the questions marked with an asterisk for your rating. |
| **This survey link is invalid or has expired** | Ask your provider for a current link. |
| **You have already completed this survey** | Ask your provider about a correction. Do not use someone else's link. |
| **Failed to submit response. Please try again.** | Check your connection and submit again. If it keeps failing, tell your provider. |
| You refreshed before you submitted | The form does not restore your answers. Choose your rating again and check every answer. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does clicking a number or star submit my rating?">
    No. You must click **Submit Feedback**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my rating before I send it?">
    Yes. Pick another rating. The extra questions can change, so check the ones that show.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to write a comment?">
    No. The comment is optional. Extra questions marked with an asterisk are required.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my feedback anonymous?">
    No, do not assume it is. Your invitation identifies you, and your provider can link the response to you and your company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit my response after the thank-you page?">
    No. Contact your provider to arrange a correction.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I answer from the Surveys page in the portal?">
    No. Use your invitation link. The Surveys page in the portal is for viewing survey information, not for answering.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
