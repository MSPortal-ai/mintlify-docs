Use this guide to find a survey and read its results. It is for MSP staff and for company users whose role includes Surveys access. To give your own feedback, use your [questionnaire invitation](/user-guides/surveys/complete-a-survey), [NPS invitation](/user-guides/surveys/respond-to-nps) or [support-rating link](/user-guides/surveys/rate-your-support).

## Before you start

- You need **Surveys** read access. Company users see only what their MSP makes available through their role and company scope. Ask your MSP to change access, because Company administrators cannot manage portal permissions.
- A role can limit you to **Own surveys only** or **Own responses only**. That is why a colleague may see more than you.
- To edit an answer, your role needs **Edit submitted responses**.

## Find the survey and its version

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Surveys">
    Click **Surveys** in the sidebar. If you can switch companies, check the company selection first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the survey">
    The table has the columns **Survey Title**, **Type**, **Version**, **Status**, **Responses**, **Response Rate** and **Created**. Use the column filters to narrow the list. It starts with the **Active** status filter, so change **Status** to find inactive surveys.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the version">
    If a survey has several versions, click the arrow beside the title to expand them. The row menu on the group also offers **All Versions** and **Expand All Versions**. Open the version you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the results">
    Click the title, or open the row's menu and choose the action for the survey type.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Survey type | Row menu action | What opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General questionnaire | **View Results** | **Questions**, **Respondents** and **Analysis** tabs |
| NPS | **View Analytics** | [NPS analytics](/user-guides/surveys/read-nps-analytics) |
| CSAT | **View Dashboard** | [CSAT dashboard](/user-guides/surveys/read-csat-feedback) |

The response count is the number of respondents, not the number of answers. One respondent answers many questions.

## Read answers by question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Questions tab">
    Each row is one question with its response count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand a question">
    Click the row to see every answer to that question, with the respondent and company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the answers">
    Click **Copy** to copy the question and its answers. The button changes to **Copied!**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Clicking an answer selects it, and the checkbox on a question row selects all its answers. Selecting does not change anything. Selected answers feed the **Add to Goals** and **Create Goals** actions, so review your selection before you use them.

## Read answers by respondent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Respondents tab">
    Each row is one person with their number of answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand a respondent">
    Click the row to read their questions and answers together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page through respondents">
    Use the page controls under the list to see more respondents. Click **Copy** on a row to copy that person's answers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A respondent shown as **Anonymous** may only be missing a display name. The invitation can still identify an email address and company.

## Filter the answers

Use the search box above the tabs to match a respondent name, email, company or answer text. Use the start and end date pickers to narrow the answer dates. Clear both to see everything.

Filters change which answers you see. A respondent can show fewer answers than they gave when search matches only some of them.

## Export the results

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your filters">
    Check the survey version, company, search text and dates. The export follows them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Open the **Page actions** menu in the header and click **Export**. The option appears once the survey has at least one response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the file">
    Open the downloaded CSV from your browser's downloads. It has one row per respondent, with **Respondent Name**, **Respondent Email**, **Company** and one column for each question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The export covers every respondent in the filtered set, including respondents on other pages, not only the rows you selected. A blank cell means the person did not answer, or an active filter hid the answer.

## Edit a text answer

This needs the **Edit submitted responses** permission. It applies to text answers only.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the answer">
    On the **Questions** or **Respondents** tab, find the answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit response">
    Click the pencil icon beside the answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Change the text and click **Save**. A **Response updated** message confirms it. Click **Cancel** to keep the original.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the analysis

The **Analysis** tab shows saved AI results, when you have run them. Check **Last analyzed** and compare the summary with the original answers, because a saved summary can be older than recent responses.

- **Overall Sentiment** shows the score.
- **Key Themes** lists themes with weight and evidence. **Create Goal** starts a goal from a theme.
- **Follow-up Questions** suggests questions to ask next.
- **Word Map** highlights repeated words. It does not rank problems.

To run or refresh the analysis, see [Run AI analysis](/user-guides/surveys/index#run-ai-analysis).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A survey is missing | Change the **Status** filter from **Active**, check the company selector and ask your MSP about your Surveys access. |
| You see fewer answers than a colleague | Role limits such as **Own responses only**, company scope and filters can differ. Do not assume the answers were deleted. |
| Export is missing | The survey needs at least one response. |
| Blank cells in the export | Clear the search and date filters, then export again. |
| No pencil on an answer | Your role lacks **Edit submitted responses**, or the answer is not text. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I find an inactive survey?">
    The list starts with an **Active** filter. Change **Status**, check the company selector and confirm that you have access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I compare survey versions?">
    Expand the arrow beside a grouped survey title, or open its menu and use **All Versions**. Open each version's results on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does selecting an answer create a goal?">
    No. Selecting only prepares answers. A goal is created only when you click **Add to Goals** or **Create Goals** and pick how to create it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the CSV contain only selected rows?">
    No. It contains the respondents left after the company, search and date filters, across all pages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Anonymous mean the response cannot be identified?">
    No. It can mean only that no display name was given.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the analysis not change when I search?">
    The saved analysis is a separate result. Check **Last analyzed** and compare it with the answers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
