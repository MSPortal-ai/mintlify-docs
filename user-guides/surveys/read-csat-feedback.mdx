Use this guide to read the CSAT dashboard and export its responses. CSAT is the satisfaction rating clients give after a support ticket. This guide is for MSP staff and for company users whose role includes CSAT access.

## Before you start

- Your role needs **Surveys** read access and **View CSAT dashboards**. An MSP administrator turns this on per role in **Settings > Users & Roles**, under **Surveys**. General survey access is not enough.
- CSAT must be available for your portal. That means built-in CSAT campaigns are turned on, or an integration that imports ratings (HaloPSA, Autotask or Simplesat) is active.
- Company users see only their permitted company. The **CSAT by Company** comparison table is for MSP staff.

## Open the CSAT dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Surveys">
    Click **Surveys** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the dashboard">
    Open the **Surveys Menu** in the page header and click **CSAT Dashboard**. You can also click the **CSAT** summary card on the Surveys list. For one campaign, open its row menu and choose **View Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check which dashboard you are on">
    The overall view is titled **CSAT** and covers every CSAT and CES campaign, native and synced. A campaign dashboard shows the campaign name and an **Overview** button to go back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the period">
    The summary says **Last N days**. Your MSP sets N under the dashboard display settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the numbers">
    Compare **CSAT**, **Responses**, **Satisfied**, **Dissatisfied** and **Companies**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the charts">
    **Trend** shows CSAT % over the period. **Score Distribution** counts **Satisfied**, **Neutral** and **Dissatisfied** ratings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the lists">
    **CSAT by Company** and **CSAT by Technician** rank each group by responses, CSAT and low scores. **Recent Responses** shows the newest ratings with their comments and ticket numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**CSAT** is the share of included responses rated satisfied. It is not the average star rating. MSPortal turns every rating into a 0 to 100 score so different scales compare fairly. A score of 80 or more is satisfied, 40 to below 80 is neutral, and below 40 is dissatisfied. Each response also shows its original rating and scale, such as 4/5.

Neutral responses count in **Responses**, so **Satisfied** plus **Dissatisfied** can be less than the total. A dash instead of a score means there is no data for that scope. Check the period, the company selection and your access.

The overall dashboard counts responses mapped to a company. Responses with no company appear as **No company mapped** in the company table. For a customer effort score (CES) campaign, open its own dashboard.

## Choose what the dashboard shows

Your MSP controls whether the dashboard shows written feedback, respondent details and the technician table. A user with manage access opens **Display settings** from the dashboard header to change them. The settings are **Lookback window**, **Recent responses to list**, **Show written feedback**, **Show respondent details** and **Show team member breakdown**.

These settings change only what the dashboards display. They do not stop an authorized CSV export from including names, emails and comments.

In **CSAT by Technician**, a technician is matched to a portal user by email. **No matching portal user** means no match was found. It does not mean a zero score.

## Check the time window before you compare numbers

| View | Which responses it uses |
| --- | --- |
| Summary, trend, company and technician figures | The dashboard period and the selected campaign and company |
| **Recent Responses** | The newest ratings, up to the **Recent responses to list** setting |
| Company response panel | Up to 50 newest responses for the company you selected |
| **Export CSV** | The last 90 days, for the selected campaign and company |

Counts can differ between these views. Compare the period, campaign, company and response limit before you treat a difference as missing feedback.

## Read one company's responses

This is for MSP staff.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company table">
    On the dashboard, find **CSAT by Company**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a company">
    Click a company row. A panel opens with that company's newest responses, up to 50, and a summary for the dashboard period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the panel">
    Close it to return to the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export responses

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Open the overall dashboard or the campaign dashboard you want to export.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company selection">
    If your account can change the company, choose it now. The export follows the selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export CSV">
    Click **Export CSV** in the page header. Your browser downloads a file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the file">
    Open the CSV and check the response dates and scope.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The export has a fixed set of columns. They include the rating, scale, normalized score, source, company, respondent name and email, ticket details, the assigned technician and the feedback text when there is any. It covers every matching record, not only the rows you can see. The default period is 90 days, even when the dashboard uses a different period.

If the download fails or stops, check your browser's download status before you use the file as a complete report.

## Import HaloPSA and Autotask satisfaction ratings

MSPortal can import HaloPSA native CSAT feedback and Autotask native survey results. They show in this dashboard, in custom dashboard widgets, in report blocks and in CSV exports. You can keep collecting feedback through the PSA's own survey. Simplesat is not required.

MSP staff set this up in the PSA integration:

1. Open the PSA integration's **Sync** settings and turn on **Import satisfaction ratings**. It is off by default.
2. Give the integration read access to satisfaction data in the PSA.
3. For Halo, choose the matching **Rating scale**. Autotask surveys are always rated 1 to 5.
4. Make sure companies are linked and related tickets are synchronized. Feedback appears in company-scoped results only after that.

Imported surveys are named **Halo PSA satisfaction** and **Autotask PSA satisfaction**. Autotask responses carry ratings only, because Autotask does not expose free-text answers.

Follow [Halo native CSAT setup](/user-guides/integrations/enable-halo-integration#import-halopsa-native-csat-for-dashboards-and-reports) or [Autotask native CSAT setup](/user-guides/integrations/enable-autotask-integration#import-autotask-native-csat-for-dashboards-and-reports) for the full steps. Company users should ask their MSP to check the import and CSAT access if responses are missing.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You can open Surveys but not CSAT | CSAT needs **View CSAT dashboards** on your role and CSAT must be available. Ask your MSP. |
| The dashboard shows dashes | Check the period, the company selection and that responses exist and are mapped to a company. |
| Imported ratings are missing | Check that **Import satisfaction ratings** is on, companies are linked and tickets are synced. |
| Recent Responses shows older dates | It lists the newest ratings and is not limited to the summary period. |
| The CSV has different dates than the dashboard | **Export CSV** defaults to 90 days. |
| Comments or names are hidden | Your MSP turned off **Show written feedback** or **Show respondent details**. |
| The company table is missing | It is an MSP view. Company users see their own company only. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is CSAT the average star rating?">
    No. It is the percentage of responses scored satisfied. Each response keeps its original rating and scale.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do Satisfied and Dissatisfied not add up to Responses?">
    Neutral responses also count in the total. **Score Distribution** shows all three groups.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Export CSV match the dashboard period?">
    No. It defaults to the last 90 days. Check the dates in the file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding respondent names remove them from the CSV?">
    No. Display settings do not change what an authorized export contains.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening feedback reply to the client or publish it?">
    No. Reading the dashboard does not send a reply, post a public review or publish a testimonial.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
