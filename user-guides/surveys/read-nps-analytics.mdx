Use this guide to read the results of an NPS survey. It is for MSP staff and for company users whose role includes Surveys access.

## Before you start

- Your role needs **Surveys** read access. Your MSP controls this in **Settings > Users & Roles**. Answering an NPS invitation does not give you access to results.
- The page follows the company selector. If you can change the company, the numbers reload for that selection.

## Open the analytics page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Surveys">
    Click **Surveys** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the NPS survey">
    The list starts with the **Active** status filter. Use the **Type** filter to show **NPS**, and change **Status** to see inactive surveys. If the survey has several versions, expand it with the arrow beside the title and choose the version you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the analytics">
    Click the survey title, or open the row's menu and choose **View Analytics**. The **NPS Analytics** page opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the summary

The top card shows the **NPS Score** with a status badge. The badge reads **Excellent** at 50 or above, **Good** from 0 to 49, and **Needs Improvement** below 0. The tiles next to it show these figures.

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Responses** | Number of responses included, with the number of invites sent |
| **Response Rate** | Responses divided by invites sent for this survey |
| **Avg Score** | Average of the 0 to 10 scores |
| **Recent Feedback** | Number of comments in the recent feedback list |

Below the tiles, three meters show **Promoters (9-10)**, **Passives (7-8)** and **Detractors (0-6)** with counts and percentages.

NPS is the percentage of promoters minus the percentage of detractors, rounded to a whole number. It runs from -100 to 100. Passives count in the total but are not added or subtracted. For example, six promoters, two passives and two detractors make an NPS of 40 (60% minus 20%).

## Read the charts and comments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the score distribution">
    **Score Distribution** shows how many responses landed on each score from 0 to 10.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the trend">
    **NPS Over Time** shows monthly NPS and response counts. It shows **Not enough data for trends** until there are responses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare companies">
    **NPS by Company** lists each company with its NPS and its promoter, passive and detractor counts. Expand a company row to see its respondents, scores, dates and feedback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read recent feedback">
    **Recent Feedback** lists up to 25 of the latest responses that include a comment. Responses without a comment still count in the score.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A small number of responses can swing the score. Look at **Responses**, the score distribution and the comments together, not just the color of the badge.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No analytics data available** | The survey could not load. Click **Back to Surveys** and open it again. |
| The score shows 0 | Check **Responses**. A score of 0 with no responses means nothing has come in yet. |
| Fewer comments than responses | **Recent Feedback** shows only responses with comments, up to 25. |
| The response rate looks wrong | Check the survey version and the company selector. The rate uses the invites counted for that scope. |
| A company is missing | Check the company selector and your Surveys access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is NPS the same as the average score?">
    No. NPS subtracts the detractor percentage from the promoter percentage. **Avg Score** is the average of the 0 to 10 scores.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do passives affect NPS?">
    Yes. They count in the total, which is the base for the promoter and detractor percentages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an NPS of 0 mean everyone gave 0?">
    No. It means promoters and detractors are equal in share. It also shows 0 when there are no responses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I see the feedback behind a company's score?">
    Expand the company row in **NPS by Company**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change a rating on this page?">
    No. This page has no option to edit an NPS response.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company users see every company's feedback?">
    No. Company users see only the companies their role and company scope allow. Ask your MSP if something is missing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
