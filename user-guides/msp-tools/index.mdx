Use **MSP Tools** to decide which client accounts need attention and investigate the evidence behind that decision. These tools support different questions and do not share one universal score, refresh or Save action.

## Before you start

Use an MSP staff account with access to the required tool. Client Health, Ticket Trends and Financial Reporting are MSP views. Access to one tool does not grant access to the others, their settings or their write actions. For Company-facing Standards behavior, use the separate Standards guides.

Confirm the relevant features are available for your tenant, then ask an MSP administrator to check your role under **Settings > Users & Roles** when a page or control is missing.

Connect the integrations you actually use and map external companies correctly. A connected tenant-wide integration does not establish that every client has current evidence. You do not need every integration to use MSP Tools.

## Start a client review

1. Choose the intended company scope in the global company selector.
2. Open **Client Health**, review risk and evidence coverage, and inspect a company's dimensions. **Limited data** means the score lacks sufficient evidence.
3. Use **Ticket Trends** for service-demand, resolution or backlog questions. Check the analysis date and underlying tickets before acting on generated recommendations.
4. Use **Standards** to investigate required-check deployment gaps. Status changes are separate write actions; filtering the matrix does not guarantee that a bulk action is restricted to the visible rows.
5. Use **Financial Reporting** to inspect the account's revenue and support evidence. Review classification and seat-profile setup when metrics show gaps.
6. Record reviewed follow-up work in Planner or your normal account-management process. Reading a score or generated recommendation does not assign work.

## Understand the evidence

| Source | Typical contribution |
| --- | --- |
| PSA | Synchronized tickets, invoice/revenue records, opportunities and projects, subject to provider coverage. |
| RMM/device integrations | Managed-device context, supported availability and patch evidence. |
| Meetings and surveys | Meeting recency and eligible survey/NPS responses. |
| Standards | Required-check deployment evidence. |
| Planner, Goals and Compliance | Eligible due-date, progress and operational evidence. |
| Quoter sources | Supported sales activity and outcomes where applicable. |

Client Health combines eligible evidence across Service Quality, Engagement, Financial, Sales, Projects, Standards, Environment and Operational dimensions. Missing or stale evidence reduces coverage; it must not be interpreted as healthy.

Financial Reporting grades describe supported operational economics. They are not accounting net-profit statements. Ticket Trends confidence and forecast signals likewise require checking their data coverage.

## Configure the relevant settings

| Task | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Review Client Health dimensions | **Settings > MSP Tools > Scoring**; see [Client Health scoring](/user-guides/msp-tools/understand-health-scoring). |
| Define required standards | [Standards settings](/user-guides/settings/standards). |
| Classify revenue and configure seat models | [Financial Reporting settings](/user-guides/settings/financial-reporting). |
| Correct company links | [Integration Company Mapping](/user-guides/settings/integration-company-mapping). |

Read each settings guide before saving. Header saves, immediate row changes and independently saved company models have different persistence boundaries.

## Freshness and refreshes

Client Health publishes a complete scorecard daily at **2:00 AM UTC**. **Refresh Scores** requests a new tenant-wide scorecard even when one company is selected. Wait for completion; the previous published scorecard remains visible during generation.

Ticket Trends prepares weekly aggregates on Monday. Its AI analysis is a separate saved result: check **Last analyzed**, history and the normal seven-day re-generation cooldown. A source sync does not automatically replace every generated analysis.

Other measurements follow their source sync and calculation schedules. A settings save or accepted background request is not proof that every dependent tool has finished updating.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A tool is missing | Staff identity, tenant feature availability and that tool's role permissions. |
| An expected client is missing | Global company scope, assigned-company access and integration mappings. |
| Scores or metrics are unavailable | Missing/stale source evidence, incomplete setup or an unfinished initial calculation. |
| Tools show different dates or counts | Their distinct source datasets, periods, snapshots and refresh schedules. |
| I can read but cannot change anything | Write, manage and settings permissions are separate from reader access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which MSP Tool should I open first?">
    Start with Client Health to prioritize accounts, then investigate service issues in Ticket Trends, deployment gaps in Standards or account economics in Financial Reporting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need every integration to use MSP Tools?">
    No. Each tool uses its supported available evidence. Confirm the sources needed for your question, their company mappings and freshness.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Limited data mean a company is healthy?">
    No. It means Client Health lacks enough evidence for a reliable score. Investigate the missing sources before interpreting the account's condition.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does selecting one company restrict Refresh Scores to that client?">
    No. Client Health refresh requests a tenant-wide scorecard. The selected company scope controls what you view.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does read access also let me run AI analysis or change settings?">
    No. Ticket Trends generation needs its write permission and enabled AI. Each tool's changes and settings have their own permission requirements.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will a single refresh update all four tools?">
    No. Source syncs, scorecard publication and generated Ticket Trends analyses have different refresh behavior. Check the relevant timestamps and completion state.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a poor Financial Reporting grade prove an accounting loss?">
    No. It summarizes supported operational economics and evidence. Review the metric definitions, classification and company seat model.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do recommendations automatically create follow-up work?">
    No. Review the evidence and explicitly create or assign work. Ticket Trends has a separate preview and Add selected to Planner action where available.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
