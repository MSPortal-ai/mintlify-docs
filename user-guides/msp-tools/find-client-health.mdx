This guide shows MSP staff how to find the companies that need attention in Client Health. Company portal users cannot open Client Health. They are sent to Home.

## Before you start

- You need Client Health read access (`read_customer_success`) and the Client Health feature turned on for your tenant.
- Client Health shows a published scorecard. It is not a live check of every device or service.
- A new tenant needs one completed score refresh before the dashboard appears. See [Refresh Client Health scores](/user-guides/msp-tools/refresh-client-health).

## Narrow the list to the companies you want

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Client Health">
    In the sidebar, open **MSP Tools > Client Health**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a company scope">
    Use the company selector at the top of the app to choose Global View, an assigned group or one company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by company">
    Use the **Company** filter in the table header to pick companies within that scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by company type or role">
    When they appear, use **Company type** and **Filter by Role** next to the table. Role choices filter companies by who holds that role. They do not change anyone's permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page through the results">
    The table loads 50 companies per page. Use the page controls under the table for more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Several values inside one filter can match. Different filters must all match. A combination with no matches shows an empty table.

Changing the company scope clears the **Company**, **Company type** and **Filter by Role** selections. Check the risk and **Limited data** filters afterward if the table looks empty.

## Filter by risk or Limited data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click a risk level">
    Click **Thriving**, **Healthy**, **At Risk** or **Critical** in the **Risk Levels** row. Click it again to clear it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or click Needs Attention">
    The **Needs Attention** card filters to **At Risk** and **Critical** together. Click it again to clear it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or click Limited data">
    The **Limited data** card finds companies without enough evidence for a score. Choosing it clears any risk filter. Choosing a risk level clears it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also use the **Risk Level** filter in the table header to pick several levels at once.

| Area | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Summary cards | Companies in your company, type and role scope, before the risk or Limited data filter. |
| Spotlight | The same scope. The risk and Limited data filters do not change it. |
| Table and count | The scope plus the current risk or Limited data filter. |
| Export CSV | The scope plus the current filters, up to 10,000 rows. |

A table filtered to Critical can show fewer companies than the summary. **Avg Health Score** averages only companies that have a numeric score. A company with limited evidence can still carry a manual risk flag, so a company can be counted under a risk level and under Limited data.

## Use Spotlight

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Spotlight">
    Click **Spotlight** to expand or collapse it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the two lists">
    Each list shows up to five companies.
    - **Needs Attention** lists the lowest numeric scores. Companies with limited data are left out. Use the **Limited data** card to find them.
    - **Most Improved** lists the biggest score gains since the previous published scorecard of the same scoring model. The number shown is the company's current score, not the size of the gain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a company">
    Click a company to open its health details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An empty **Most Improved** list means there is no earlier comparable score or no company improved.

## Read and arrange the table

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a company">
    Click a row to [read the company's health details](/user-guides/msp-tools/read-client-health-details).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hover a score">
    Hover a health or dimension score to see its supporting values. **N/A** means no score, not zero. **Manual** beside a risk level means someone set a risk flag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show or hide columns">
    Click **Columns**. **Trend**, **Flag Reason** and **CSAT Response Rate** are hidden until you turn them on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort">
    Click a sortable header, such as **Health Score** or a dimension. **Trend** and the CSAT columns cannot be sorted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A dimension column is missing when no company in scope has a score for it or the dimension is turned off in [scoring settings](/user-guides/settings/scoring). The CSAT columns also need Engagement to be on and CSAT responses to exist.

## Export the list to CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply your filters">
    Set the company scope and filters you want in the file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export CSV">
    The file includes every matching row across pages, up to **10,000 rows**. If more companies match, narrow the filters and export in smaller sets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the file">
    Check the row count before you share it. It can include internal scores and manual flag reasons.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A company is missing | Check the company selector, the **Company**, **Company type** and **Filter by Role** filters, and the risk and **Limited data** filters. |
| The page says **No published Customer Success generation is available yet** | Follow [Refresh Client Health scores](/user-guides/msp-tools/refresh-client-health). |
| A banner says the scorecard is stale | The source data is more than 36 hours old. Check integration sync health, then refresh. |
| A dimension column is missing | No company in scope has that score, or the dimension is off in scoring settings. |
| The table says **Failed to load dashboard data** | Reload the page. This is an error, not a healthy result. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can company portal users open Client Health?">
    No. It is an MSP view. Company users are sent to Home.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find companies needing attention?">
    Click **Needs Attention**, or filter the table by risk level. Use the **Limited data** card for companies with missing evidence.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the table count differ from the summary?">
    The risk and Limited data filters narrow the table. The summary covers the whole scope.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Spotlight follow the risk filter?">
    No. It follows the company scope only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Most Improved empty?">
    There may be no earlier comparable scorecard, or no company improved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Export CSV contain only the current page?">
    No. It exports all matching rows, up to 10,000.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
