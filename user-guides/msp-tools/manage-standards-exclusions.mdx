---
title: "Exclude or include companies in Standards"
description: "Keep a company out of Standards scoring without deleting the company or its Planner work."
icon: "eye-slash"
---

This guide shows MSP staff how to exclude a company from Standards scoring and how to bring it back. Excluding does not delete the company, its tickets, its devices or its Planner items.

## Before you start

- You need Standards read access, Planner Write permission and active billing.
- Excluding a company removes it from the deployment percentage and hides its matrix row by default.
- Excluding a parent company does not exclude its child companies. Exclude each one separately.

## Exclude a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Standards">
    In the sidebar, open **MSP Tools > Standards**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the company">
    Type the name in **Search companies...**. Check the exact row, especially when parent and child companies appear together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the company menu">
    Right-click the company name, or click the three-dot button beside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Exclude from Standards">
    The change saves right away. A toast reads that the company was excluded from Standards. There is no confirmation. To cancel, press **Escape** or click outside the menu first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the row is hidden">
    The row disappears from the matrix. Use **Show N excluded** to see it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Include a company again

<Steps>
  <Step title="Show excluded companies">
    Click **Show N excluded** above the matrix. The number is how many excluded companies are in the loaded scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the company">
    Excluded rows show an **Excluded** badge and a crossed-out name. Use **Search companies...** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Include in Standards">
    Right-click the company name, or click the three-dot button beside it, then click **Include in Standards**. A toast reads that the company was re-included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the matrix">
    The row returns to normal and counts toward the deployment percentage again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Including a company again keeps its existing statuses. It does not reset them to Not Started or mark them Deployed. **Hide N excluded** only hides the rows. It does not include anyone.

## What exclusion changes

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Search companies...** | Filters the rows and the summary for now. |
| **Show N excluded** and **Hide N excluded** | Show or hide excluded rows. |
| **Exclude from Standards** | Saves the exclusion for that company. |
| **Include in Standards** | Removes the saved exclusion. |
| **Mark N/A** on one standard | Changes one standard for one company. It does not exclude the company. |

- An excluded company does not count toward the Standards score, even while its row is shown.
- Column bulk actions skip excluded companies. Its own row menu still works while the row is shown.
- Exclusion does not change what company users can see in Planner, deactivate the company, or stop any integration.
- **Sync All Companies** still covers excluded companies, because it targets active companies. See [Sync Standards to companies](/user-guides/settings/sync-standards).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A company is missing from the matrix | Check the company selector, **Search companies...** and **Show N excluded**. |
| **Show N excluded** does not list the company | The company may be inactive, outside your company scope, or not assigned to you. |
| **Failed to update company exclusion** appears | The row comes back. Reload the matrix to see the saved state, check Planner Write permission and active billing, then try again. |
| Company users still see Standards work in Planner | Exclusion does not hide Planner items. Change item visibility in Planner or in the Standards sync settings. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does excluding delete the company or its Planner work?">
    No. It removes the company from Standards scoring and hides the row. All records stay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Show excluded put a company back in the score?">
    No. It only shows the rows. Use **Include in Standards** to remove the exclusion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does including a company reset its statuses?">
    No. The existing statuses come back as they were.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does excluding a parent exclude its children?">
    No. Manage each company on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will a column bulk update change excluded companies?">
    No. Column actions skip them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does exclusion stop the Standards sync for that company?">
    No. The sync still creates tracking rows for active companies.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
