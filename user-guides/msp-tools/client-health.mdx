Client Health gives your team a consistent way to review every client relationship. It combines service, engagement, financial, sales, project, standards, environment, and operational evidence into a health score from 0 to 100.

Unlike a simple checklist, Client Health does not treat missing data as healthy or unhealthy. A score appears only when MSPortal has enough current evidence to support it.

## Prerequisites

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **User type** | Client Health is an internal MSP view and is not available to company portal users. |
| **Permission** | Your role needs `read_customer_success`. |
| **Data sources** | Connected PSA, RMM, survey, project, standards, planner, goal, and compliance data can contribute evidence. You do not need every source, but broader coverage produces a more complete score. |
| **First published scorecard** | A new tenant or newly enabled environment needs one completed score refresh before the dashboard is available. |

Users with `write_customer_success` can also set or remove manual risk flags. Scoring configuration requires `read_settings_client_health` or `manage_settings_client_health`.

## Open and refresh Client Health

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Client Health">
    Open **MSP Tools > Client Health** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the company scope">
    Use the global Company selector to review all accessible companies, an assigned group, or a single company. Summary totals, Spotlight, table results and exports follow this company scope. Risk and Limited data filters then narrow the table/export, while summary and Spotlight remain scoped to companies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh when needed">
    Scores refresh automatically each day at **2:00 AM UTC**. To request a new tenant-wide scorecard, click **Refresh Scores** in the portfolio header and wait for the completion message. Selecting one company does not limit this refresh to that company.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
While a refresh is running, MSPortal keeps the last complete scorecard visible. An incomplete or failed refresh never replaces the published results.
</Note>

## Use the dashboard

### Portfolio summary

The summary area shows:

- **Needs Attention**: companies rated **At Risk** or **Critical**
- **Avg Data Coverage**: the average share of health dimensions that have data
- **Limited data**: companies without enough evidence for a numeric score, out of all companies in scope
- **Avg Health Score** across companies with a numeric score, and how many companies that is
- **Risk Levels**: the count for **Thriving**, **Healthy**, **At Risk**, and **Critical**, with a bar showing the mix of scored companies

Click **Needs Attention**, **Limited data** or a risk-level count to filter the company table. Click it again to clear that filter.

### Spotlight

Spotlight highlights two groups across the full selected scope:

- **Needs Attention** shows the companies with the most urgent current results, among companies with available numeric scores. Use the Limited data filter separately.
- **Most Improved** shows the strongest improvements compared with the previous published scorecard from the same model history.

<Info>
Client Health starts a clean history for the evidence-aware scorecard. Earlier legacy scores are not mixed into the trend or Most Improved comparison.
</Info>

### Company table

The table includes the company name, overall score, effective risk level, and available dimension scores. Use it to:

- Search or filter by company
- Filter by one or more risk levels
- Sort by supported score and dimension columns
- Show or hide columns
- Open a company detail page by clicking its row
- Export matching companies across pages, capped at 10,000 rows per request

The table respects both the global Company selector and table filters. A dimension column can be unavailable when the selected scope has no score for it or the dimension is disabled. If the dimension is unavailable for one company, that company shows **N/A**.

The optional **Trend** and **Flag Reason** columns can be enabled from the column visibility menu. A manually selected risk level is labeled **Manual** so it is not confused with the calculated result.

## Understand scores and data states

### Portfolio weights

The default model uses these base weights:

| Dimension | Base weight |
|-----------|------------:|
| Service Quality | 18% |
| Engagement | 18% |
| Financial | 13% |
| Sales | 8% |
| Projects | 10% |
| Standards | 13% |
| Environment | 10% |
| Operational | 10% |

Only enabled, applicable dimensions can contribute. When some evidence is unavailable, MSPortal uses the observed evidence and reduces that dimension's influence based on its coverage. Missing evidence does not automatically earn a neutral or positive result.

### Numeric score requirements

The default model shows a numeric health score only when both conditions are met:

- At least **3 dimensions** have a score
- Overall evidence coverage is at least **40%**

If either condition is not met, the company shows **Limited data** and does not receive a calculated risk level.

<Warning>
**Limited data is not a low score.** It means MSPortal cannot support a numeric result with the available evidence. Review the company's dimension coverage and connected data sources before drawing a conclusion.
</Warning>

### Risk levels

| Health score | Calculated risk level |
|-------------:|-----------------------|
| 80–100 | Thriving |
| 60 to below 80 | Healthy |
| 40 to below 60 | At Risk |
| 0 to below 40 | Critical |

A manual risk flag can replace the displayed risk level, but it never changes the calculated health score or its dimension contributions.

### What N/A and coverage mean

MSPortal evaluates each metric as observed, no samples, insufficient sample, source unavailable, not applicable, or excluded (switched off in scoring settings). In the interface, unavailable metrics and dimensions are represented by **N/A** or omitted when there is nothing useful to show.

A real zero can affect the score only when MSPortal has an observed denominator that proves the result was zero. For example, zero overdue items out of a real set of due items is evidence; having no due items is not the same thing.

## Review a company

Click any company row to open the detail page.

### Summary cards

The page can show:

- **Health Score** with the weighted dimension breakdown
- **Risk Level**, including the calculated level when a manual flag is active
- **Standards Compliance** when a required standards catalog applies

The overall score card is omitted when no numeric score exists; the Risk Level card can show N/A.

### Dimension Breakdown

Each available dimension shows:

- Its score and applied weight
- Evidence coverage and observed metric count
- Only the metrics MSPortal actually observed
- Values formatted with their correct unit, such as percent, days, currency, or hours per endpoint

A dimension whose metrics are all excluded in scoring settings can still appear when it has evidence, but it is labeled **Excluded from health score** and does not affect the overall result. Individually excluded metrics carry no scoring weight. Available values may remain listed for context; missing values are not guaranteed to appear.

### Health Score Trend

The 180-day chart uses published scorecards. Click **Show Dimensions** to add the individual dimension lines.

When the scoring model changes, the chart breaks the line at the model boundary instead of implying that unlike scores are directly comparable. New evidence-aware history begins with the first published scorecard; it is not reconstructed from legacy data. Metric inclusion changes within the same model do not necessarily create a chart break, so compare settings and evidence coverage as well as the line.

### Top Negative Drivers

Top Negative Drivers lists the dimensions responsible for the largest health-score loss. The displayed impacts are rounded score-point contributions. They are not monetary losses or proof of a cause, and can appear even when the company lacks enough dimensions for an overall numeric score.

## Set a manual risk flag

Use a manual flag when relationship context is not yet represented in the available data—for example, an executive escalation, a known renewal concern, or a positive strategic change.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company">
    From Client Health, click the company row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the flag dialog">
    Click **Flag Risk**. If a flag already exists, click **Edit Flag**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a risk level">
    Choose **Critical**, **At Risk**, **Healthy**, or **Thriving**. The dialog shows the calculated comparison when available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the reason">
    Enter a concise reason explaining why the relationship carries more or less risk than the score suggests, then click **Save Flag**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Review the reason through Edit Flag, the Flag Reason table column or the risk tooltip where available. Stored audit metadata is not a complete event-history viewer on this page. To return to the calculated risk level, edit the flag and click **Remove Flag**.

## How each dimension is calculated

Use [Understand Client Health scoring and evidence](/user-guides/msp-tools/understand-health-scoring) for the complete metric rules, source windows, provider coverage and worked weighting example.

| Dimension | Evidence to review |
| --- | --- |
| Service Quality | Eight complete ticket weeks, demand, resolution and backlog observations |
| Engagement | Past meetings, recent NPS and qualifying CSAT responses |
| Financial | Configured endpoint model, three complete months of profitability data, open and paid invoices |
| Sales | PSA opportunities and Quoter quotes; Quotes module provider support is broader than this scoring input |
| Projects | Synced PSA project dates and measured hours |
| Standards | Required checks and one-of groups, rather than a raw displayed-item count |
| Environment | Observed servers and recognized patch records from supported scoring sources |
| Operational | Planner due dates, goal progress and compliance completion evidence |

Missing samples do not earn healthy defaults. Source freshness, metric exclusions and evidence coverage affect which observations can contribute.

## Configure included metrics

Go to **Settings > MSP Tools > Scoring** to choose which metrics count toward Client Health. Each dimension lists its metrics with a switch; excluded metrics carry no weight, and their share is redistributed across the remaining metrics in the dimension after the next published refresh. A dimension with every metric excluded is left out of the overall score.

See [Health Score Configuration](/user-guides/settings/scoring) for permission and configuration details.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No published Customer Success generation is available yet">
    This appears when no complete scorecard has been published for your tenant. Click **Refresh Scores** and wait for the completion message. On a new deployment or newly enabled tenant, the first automatic refresh runs at 2:00 AM UTC. If the message remains after a completed refresh, contact MSPortal support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A company shows Limited data">
    Open the company and review its dimension coverage. A numeric score requires at least three scored dimensions and 40% overall coverage. Confirm that the relevant integrations are connected, company mappings are correct, and source data has synced recently.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A dimension shows N/A">
    The dimension may have no samples, an insufficient sample, an unavailable or stale source, or may not apply to the company. N/A is intentionally different from zero. Check the source integration, company mapping, and the dimension-specific prerequisites above.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The published scorecard is stale">
    The warning appears when the published scorecard has no data-through timestamp or that timestamp is more than 36 hours old. Review integration sync health, allow the source to update, and then click **Refresh Scores**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The risk level does not match the score band">
    Look for the **Manual** label. A manual risk flag replaces the displayed risk level without changing the score. Hover over the risk level or open the company to see the calculated level and flag reason.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A trend line has a gap">
    A gap marks a scoring-model revision. MSPortal separates the segments because scores from different model versions should not be treated as one continuous trend.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Best practices

- Review **Critical**, **At Risk**, and **Limited data** companies every week.
- Use **Top Negative Drivers** to choose the first area to investigate; confirm the observed metrics before taking action.
- Treat N/A as a data question, not a positive or negative outcome.
- Record a specific reason whenever you add a manual risk flag and remove it when the calculated result should take over again.
- Refresh after repairing an integration or company mapping so the next published scorecard can use the corrected evidence.
- Use the trend to evaluate interventions only within the same model segment.
