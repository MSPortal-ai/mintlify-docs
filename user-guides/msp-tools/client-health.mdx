Client Health gives your MSP team one place to review every client relationship. It combines service, engagement, financial, sales, project, standards, environment and operational evidence into a health score from 0 to 100. It is for MSP staff.

Client Health does not treat missing data as healthy or unhealthy. A score appears only when MSPortal has enough current evidence for it.

## Before you start

- Client Health is an MSP view. Company portal users cannot open it.
- Your role needs `read_customer_success`. Setting or removing a manual risk flag needs `write_customer_success`. Viewing scoring settings needs `read_settings_client_health`; changing them needs `manage_settings_client_health`.
- Connected PSA, RMM, survey, project, Standards, Planner, goal and compliance data can all add evidence. You do not need every source, but more coverage gives a fuller score.
- A new tenant needs one completed score refresh before the dashboard appears.

## Open and refresh Client Health

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Client Health">
    Open **MSP Tools > Client Health** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the company scope">
    Use the company selector at the top of the app to review Global View, an assigned group or one company. The summary, Spotlight, table and export all follow this scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh when needed">
    Scores refresh on their own every day at **2:00 AM UTC**. To request a new scorecard now, click **Refresh Scores** in the page header and wait for the **Refreshed N companies** message. The refresh covers your whole tenant, even when one company is selected. See [Refresh Client Health scores](/user-guides/msp-tools/refresh-client-health).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
While a refresh is running, MSPortal keeps the last complete scorecard visible. An incomplete or failed refresh never replaces the published results.
</Note>

## Use the dashboard

### Portfolio summary

The cards at the top show these numbers for your company scope.

- **Needs Attention**: companies rated **At Risk** or **Critical**.
- **Avg Data Coverage**: the average share of health dimensions that have data.
- **Limited data**: companies without enough evidence for a score, out of all companies in scope.
- **Avg Health Score**: the average across companies that have a score, with the count of those companies.
- **Risk Levels**: the count for **Thriving**, **Healthy**, **At Risk** and **Critical**, with a bar showing the mix.

Click **Needs Attention**, **Limited data** or a risk level to filter the table. Click it again to clear the filter. See [Find companies in Client Health](/user-guides/msp-tools/find-client-health) for filters, Spotlight and export.

### Spotlight and the company table

- **Spotlight** has two lists of up to five companies. **Needs Attention** shows the lowest scores. **Most Improved** shows the biggest gains since the previous published scorecard of the same scoring model.
- The table shows each company's score, risk level and dimension scores. Click **Columns** to add **Trend** and **Flag Reason**. A risk level marked **Manual** was set by a person.
- Click a row to open the company. Click **Export CSV** to download up to 10,000 matching rows.

A dimension column is missing when no company in scope has a score for it or the dimension is off in scoring settings. A company with no score for a dimension shows **N/A**.

## Understand scores and data states

### Dimension weights

The default model uses these base weights.

| Dimension | Base weight |
|-----------|------------:|
| Service Quality | 18% |
| Engagement | 18% |
| Financial | 13% |
| Sales | 8% |
| Projects | 10% |
| Standards | 13% |
| Environment | 10% |
| Operational | 10% |

Only enabled dimensions that apply to the company count. When some evidence is missing, MSPortal scores what it can see and lowers that dimension's influence in line with its coverage. Missing evidence never earns a neutral or positive result.

### When a company gets a score

The default model shows a numeric score only when both of these are true.

- At least **3 dimensions** have a score.
- Overall evidence coverage is at least **40%**.

Otherwise the company shows **Limited data** and has no calculated risk level.

<Warning>
**Limited data is not a low score.** MSPortal cannot support a number with the evidence it has. Check the company's dimension coverage and connected data sources before you draw a conclusion.
</Warning>

### Risk levels

| Health score | Risk level |
|-------------:|-----------------------|
| 80 to 100 | Thriving |
| 60 to below 80 | Healthy |
| 40 to below 60 | At Risk |
| 0 to below 40 | Critical |

A manual risk flag replaces the displayed risk level. It never changes the score.

### What N/A means

Each metric is observed, has no samples, has too small a sample, has an unavailable source, does not apply, or is excluded in scoring settings. The page shows **N/A** or leaves out what cannot be shown. A real zero counts only when MSPortal can see the set it came from. Zero overdue items out of a real list of due items is a zero. Having no due items is no sample.

## Review a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company">
    Click a company row in the table, or a company in **Spotlight**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary cards">
    **Health Score**, **Risk Level** and, when a required Standards catalog applies, **Standards Compliance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Dimension Breakdown">
    Each dimension shows its score, applied weight, evidence coverage and the metrics MSPortal observed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Top Negative Drivers">
    This lists the dimensions that cost the most points. The points are score impact, not money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Health Score Trend">
    Click **Show Dimensions** to add a line per dimension. The line breaks where the scoring model changed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Read a company's health details](/user-guides/msp-tools/read-client-health-details) for each part of the page.

## Set a manual risk flag

Use a flag when something about the relationship is not in the data, such as an executive escalation or a renewal concern. You need `write_customer_success`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company">
    From Client Health, click the company row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the flag dialog">
    In the risk panel, click **Flag Risk**. If a flag exists, click **Edit Flag**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a risk level">
    Choose **Critical**, **At Risk**, **Healthy** or **Thriving**. The dialog shows the calculated level for comparison.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the reason">
    Enter a short reason, then click **Save Flag**. A toast reads **Risk flag saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To go back to the calculated risk level, click **Edit Flag**, then **Remove Flag**. See [Manage manual risk flags](/user-guides/msp-tools/manage-client-risk-flags).

## What each dimension measures

[Understand Client Health scoring and evidence](/user-guides/msp-tools/understand-health-scoring) has the full rules.

| Dimension | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Service Quality | Eight complete ticket weeks: demand, resolution time and backlog |
| Engagement | Past meetings, recent NPS and CSAT responses |
| Financial | Three complete months of profitability data, the endpoint model, and open and paid invoices |
| Sales | PSA opportunities and Quoter quotes |
| Projects | Synced PSA project dates and hours |
| Standards | Required checks and one-of groups |
| Environment | Server availability and patch records from supported sources |
| Operational | Planner due dates, goal progress and compliance completion |

## Choose which metrics count

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Scoring settings">
    Go to **Settings > MSP Tools > Scoring**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch metrics on or off">
    Each dimension lists its metrics with a switch. An excluded metric carries no weight, and its share moves to the other metrics in that dimension after the next published refresh.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the scores">
    Click **Refresh Scores** on the Client Health page to publish the change now.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A dimension with every metric excluded leaves the overall score. See [Health Score Configuration](/user-guides/settings/scoring).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No published Customer Success generation is available yet** | No complete scorecard exists. Click **Refresh Scores** and wait for the message. On a new tenant, the first automatic refresh runs at 2:00 AM UTC. If it stays after a refresh, contact MSPortal support. |
| A company shows **Limited data** | Open it and check dimension coverage. A score needs three scored dimensions and 40% coverage. Check that integrations are connected, company mappings are right and data has synced. |
| A dimension shows **N/A** | It has no samples, too few samples, a stale source or does not apply. N/A is not zero. |
| A banner says the scorecard is stale | The source data is more than 36 hours old. Check integration sync health, then click **Refresh Scores**. |
| The risk level does not match the score | Look for **Manual**. A manual flag replaces the displayed level. Open the company to see the calculated level and the reason. |
| The trend line has a gap | The scoring model changed. Scores on each side are not comparable. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can company users see Client Health?">
    No. It is an MSP view.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Refresh Scores only refresh the selected company?">
    No. It refreshes your whole tenant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a manual flag change the health score?">
    No. It changes the displayed risk level only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I treat N/A as good or bad?">
    Neither. It is a question about data. Check the source integration and company mapping.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I compare trend points across a gap?">
    No. Compare only within one scoring model segment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
