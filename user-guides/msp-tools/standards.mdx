Standards (formerly Tool Stack) shows which of your recommended tools and practices each company has deployed. It is for MSP staff. Company users see the related work in Planner when you share it with them.

## Before you start

- You need an MSP staff account with Standards read access and the Client Health feature turned on for your tenant. If **Standards** is missing from the **MSP Tools** sidebar group, ask an administrator to check your role under **Settings > Users & Roles**.
- Changing statuses needs Planner Write permission and active billing. Defining categories and standards is a separate permission. See [Configure Standards](/user-guides/settings/standards).
- Define at least one category and standard first. Until you do, the page shows **No standards configured** with a link to **Settings > Standards**.

## Open the Standards matrix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Standards">
    In the sidebar, open **MSP Tools > Standards**. Companies are rows and standards are columns, grouped by category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the company scope">
    Use the company selector at the top of the app to limit which companies load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a company">
    Type in **Search companies...** at the top of the matrix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the cell">
    Each cell shows one status as a round mark. The key is in the table's header row, next to the company count. Hover a cell for details. Right-click it to change the status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compare billed revenue and white space

White space and Opportunity are available to MSP staff.

The **White space** tab summarizes standards linked to agreement SKUs for the companies in your selected scope:

| Summary | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Billed standards / month** | Existing monthly revenue supported by linked agreement SKUs. |
| **Estimated white space / month** | Estimated additional monthly value of applicable standards that are neither deployed nor billed. The line below counts missing standard deployments across clients. |
| **Deployed, not billed / month** | Estimated monthly value of standards marked deployed without linked billing evidence. The line below counts those deployments. |
| **Standards without a SKU** | Standards without a linked agreement SKU. Link a SKU before billing evidence can support their white-space estimate. |

For example, **$138,231.02 billed** and **$355,531.39 estimated white space** mean the first amount is already billed and the second is the estimated additional monthly opportunity. The deployment count is separate from either dollar amount.

### Drill into the numbers

Every count on these tabs leads to the rows behind it.

- On **White space**, click a standard to see its clients, or click its **Open** or **Not billed** count to see only those clients.
- On **Opportunity**, click a client to see its standards, or click its **Billed**, **Open** or **Deployed, not billed** count to see the standards behind that number.

The drill-down table has column filters for **State**, **SKU linked**, **Users**, **Monthly value** and **Planner**, plus **Export CSV**. A count opens the table already filtered; click **Clear Filters** to see every row. Hover a column header to read what it counts. **Not billed** means a client is marked deployed in the matrix but no linked agreement SKU is billed. Add the SKU to the client's agreement, or link the SKU they're billed under to the standard.

<Frame caption="MSPortal Demo: clicking a client's Deployed, not billed count on Opportunity opens its standards filtered to that state.">
  <img src="/images/product/standards-opportunity-drilldown.png" alt="Drill-down table for one client with State filtered to Not billed and SKU linked set to Yes, showing Acceptable Use with an Open in Planner link." width="2880" height="1920" />
</Frame>

Each row's **⋯** menu offers:

- **Open in Planner**: opens the client's planner item for that standard, which the Standards sync keeps.
- **Add to Meeting**: adds that planner item to the client's meeting agenda.
- **Open** followed by an agreement's name: opens the agreement whose SKU proves the standard is billed, when you can read agreements.

Select rows to **Export selected** or **Add to Meeting**. A meeting belongs to one client, so **Add to Meeting** works when every selected row is the same client's. Standards that aren't on Planner yet are left out; **Sync All Companies** in Standards settings creates their planner items.

### Export a client report

Select standards on **White space**, or clients on **Opportunity**, and choose **Actions > Export client report**. The report has one line per client and standard, with its state, users, monthly value (billed or estimated), billed SKUs, agreements and Planner item, ready to hand to your team. **Export CSV** on either tab downloads the summary table as shown. On **Opportunity**, a client's **⋯** menu also offers **Open client's Planner**.

<Frame caption="MSPortal Demo: Export client report from the White space actions menu.">
  <img src="/images/product/standards-white-space-actions.png" alt="White space tab with one standard selected and the Actions menu open, showing Export client report and Add to Meeting (one client at a time)." width="2880" height="1920" />

Switching between **Matrix**, **White space** and **Opportunity** keeps each tab's filters and table state. Changing the company selector resets the controls and loads the new scope. SKU links and deployment changes refresh the affected analysis.

On a phone, tap **Filters** to choose **Rows per page**, filter columns or sort them with the arrow buttons. Changes apply as you make them; tap **Done** to return to the table. The number on **Filters** shows how many column filters are active. Reopen the sheet and tap **Clear Filters** to remove them. The **Columns** icon sits beside the table's action button.

## How the matrix is scored

The matrix records **Deployed**, **In Progress**, **Not Started**, **N/A** and **Declined**. A deployed cell can also carry a deployment option and tier.

- Each **Required** standard counts as one check.
- A category marked **1 of N** counts as one check, met when any member is deployed.
- N/A and Declined standards drop out of the count.
- Optional standards are counted separately.

The same deployment percentage feeds the Standards dimension of [Client Health](/user-guides/msp-tools/client-health).

## Read monthly cost and margin

<Note>
**Cost** requires the Cost feature and the appropriate Standards and financial-reporting access.
</Note>

Open **Cost** to compare the monthly vendor cost of deployed standards with the monthly price charged to clients. **Clients not fully costed** counts clients with a deployed standard that lacks a vendor cost or the user/device count needed to calculate it.

The vendor-cost subtotal includes known costs. **Monthly margin** stays unknown while any included client has a cost gap. Each client's **Margin / month** becomes available once that client's deployed standards are fully costed. A dash is not a zero margin; check the missing cost or count before using the figures to make a pricing decision.

## Standards and Planner

Each standard can have a linked Planner item per company.

- Click a status cell with a linked item to open it in Planner.
- With Manage Planner, click a company name to open Planner List View filtered to that company's Standards items.
- Changing a status in the matrix updates the linked Planner item. See [Standards work in Planner](/user-guides/planner/understand-standards-items).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Standards** is missing from the sidebar | Ask an administrator to check your Standards read access and that the Client Health feature is on. |
| The page says **No standards configured** | Add categories and standards in [Settings > MSP Tools > Standards](/user-guides/settings/standards), then sync them to companies. |
| A company is missing | Check the company selector, the matrix search, and whether the company is excluded. Use **Show N excluded**. |
| Clicking a cell does nothing | The cell has no linked Planner item. Ask an administrator to run the Standards sync. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are Standards and Tool Stack different?">
    No. Tool Stack is the former name. Older links may still say Tool Stack.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do company users see Standards work?">
    In Planner, on items you share with them. They do not get the MSP Tools matrix.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Deployed mean the tool is running on every device?">
    No. It records the status your team set. Check live inventory and protection in your device and security tools.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does searching for a company limit a bulk update?">
    No. A standard column's **Mark all companies as** action covers every non-excluded company in the loaded scope, including rows hidden by the search.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
