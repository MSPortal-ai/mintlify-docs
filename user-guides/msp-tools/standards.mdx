Standards (formerly Tool Stack) shows which of your recommended tools and practices each company has deployed. It is for MSP staff. Company users see the related work in Planner when you share it with them.

## Before you start

- You need an MSP staff account with Standards read access and the Client Health feature turned on for your tenant. If **Standards** is missing from the **MSP Tools** sidebar group, ask an administrator to check your role under **Settings > Users & Roles**.
- Changing statuses needs Planner Write permission and active billing. Defining categories and standards is a separate permission. See [Configure Standards](/user-guides/settings/standards).
- Define at least one category and standard first. Until you do, the page shows **No standards configured** with a link to **Settings > Standards**.

## Open the Standards matrix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Standards">
    In the sidebar, open **MSP Tools > Standards**. Companies are rows and standards are columns, grouped by category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the company scope">
    Use the company selector at the top of the app to limit which companies load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a company">
    Type in **Search companies...** at the top of the matrix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the cell">
    Each cell shows one status. Hover a cell for details. Right-click it to change the status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the matrix is scored

The matrix records **Deployed**, **In Progress**, **Not Started**, **N/A** and **Declined**. A deployed cell can also carry a deployment option and tier.

- Each **Required** standard counts as one check.
- A category marked **1 of N** counts as one check, met when any member is deployed.
- N/A and Declined standards drop out of the count.
- Optional standards are counted separately.

The same deployment percentage feeds the Standards dimension of [Client Health](/user-guides/msp-tools/client-health).

## Standards and Planner

Each standard can have a linked Planner item per company.

- Click a status cell with a linked item to open it in Planner.
- Click a company name to open Planner List View filtered to that company's Standards items.
- Changing a status in the matrix updates the linked Planner item. See [Standards work in Planner](/user-guides/planner/understand-standards-items).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Standards** is missing from the sidebar | Ask an administrator to check your Standards read access and that the Client Health feature is on. |
| The page says **No standards configured** | Add categories and standards in [Settings > MSP Tools > Standards](/user-guides/settings/standards), then sync them to companies. |
| A company is missing | Check the company selector, the matrix search, and whether the company is excluded. Use **Show N excluded**. |
| Clicking a cell does nothing | The cell has no linked Planner item. Ask an administrator to run the Standards sync. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are Standards and Tool Stack different?">
    No. Tool Stack is the former name. Older links may still say Tool Stack.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do company users see Standards work?">
    In Planner, on items you share with them. They do not get the MSP Tools matrix.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Deployed mean the tool is running on every device?">
    No. It records the status your team set. Check live inventory and protection in your device and security tools.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does searching for a company limit a bulk update?">
    No. A standard column's **Mark all companies as** action covers every non-excluded company in the loaded scope, including rows hidden by the search.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
