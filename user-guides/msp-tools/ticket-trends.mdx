Ticket Trends turns weeks of PSA ticket history into a brief for service desk reviews and client meetings. It shows what changed, why it matters, what to do next and the evidence behind each point. It is for MSP staff.

## Before you start

- Company portal users cannot open Ticket Trends. Your role needs Ticket Trends read access.
- **AI Analysis** also needs Ticket Trends write access and AI turned on for your tenant.
- Creating Planner items needs Planner write or manage access and an eligible active subscription.
- Connect and map at least one PSA (ConnectWise, Halo, Syncro or Autotask), and let tickets sync. The page only sees what your PSA sync brings across.
- You need enough ticket history to fill the lookback you choose.

## Run an analysis

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ticket Trends">
    In the sidebar, open **MSP Tools > Ticket Trends**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the companies">
    Use the company selector at the top of the app. Pick Global View for the whole desk, or one client for meeting prep.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the lookback">
    Open **Weeks preset** and pick **Last 8 weeks**, **Last 12 weeks**, **Last 26 weeks** or **Last 52 weeks**. Use 12 weeks for monthly reviews and 26 or 52 for quarterly reviews. The page may open on a period that already has a saved analysis. Changing the scope or period loads the saved data. It does not start a new AI run.

    <img src="/images/product/ticket-trends-period-options.png" alt="Ticket Trends lookback menu showing 8, 12, 26 and 52 weeks." width="364" height="268" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click AI Analysis">
    The button reads **Re-run AI Analysis** when an analysis exists. The button shows **Analyzing...** and the run usually takes one to two minutes. A wide scope can take longer. The old result stays on screen until the new one is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Last analyzed">
    Read **Last analyzed** under the header to see how fresh the report is.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A successful analysis can be re-generated **once per week**. If the analysis is in an older format, the page asks you to re-run it. A failed run shows **Analysis failed. Please try running it again.** and you can retry. A new PSA sync does not remove the weekly limit.
</Note>

## Open an earlier analysis

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Analysis history">
    Click **Analysis history** and type in **Search analyses...** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an analysis">
    Choosing an older one also sets its lookback period. A line reads **Viewing saved analysis from** and the date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to the current one">
    Click **Latest analysis**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the brief and evidence

| Section | What to do with it |
| --- | --- |
| **Executive Summary** | Read what happened, the main concern and the suggested focus. The scope chips show the dates and companies covered. |
| **Key metrics** | Compare volume, resolution, backlog and recurring issues. The color is the report's reading of the trend. It does not prove an SLA breach. |
| **Recommended Actions** | Review each action's priority, impact, effort, suggested owner, why and next step. Reading it assigns nothing. |
| **What Changed** | Compare this period with the previous one. |
| **Top Risks and Opportunities** | Find issues to act on and improvements to try. |
| **Per-Client Talking Points** | Use as a draft for a client meeting. Check the facts and wording before you share. |
| **Forecast Outlook** and **Category Watchlist** | Read the forward-looking estimates and the categories worth watching. |
| **Identified Trends**, **Company Patterns**, **Category Breakdown** | Open these evidence sections to see the data behind the brief. |
| **Report Confidence** | Check confidence, data completeness and known gaps before you act. If completeness is low, narrow the scope or lengthen the lookback and re-run. |
| **Weekly Data**, **Company Metrics**, **Category Metrics** | Inspect the numbers. Each has a CSV download. |

Use the section navigation on the right to jump between sections. The page remembers which evidence sections you left open. A section is missing when the analysis has nothing for it.

Ticket links and drilldowns open the supporting tickets. While an analysis is running, drilldowns pause and a message reads **AI analysis is running**. Category counts in the report come from the saved analysis, but drilldowns show current tickets. A sync or category change since then can make the totals differ.

## Common reviews

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Service desk health review (weekly or monthly)">
    1. Open Ticket Trends with **Last 12 weeks** and Global View.
    2. Read **Executive Summary** and **Key metrics**.
    3. If a card shows a warning, open **Recommended Actions** and pick the top one or two for your next service desk meeting.
    4. Open the matching evidence section to see which companies, weeks or categories triggered it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Prepare for a client meeting">
    1. Select the client in the company selector.
    2. Choose **Last 26 weeks** or **Last 52 weeks**.
    3. Click **AI Analysis** or **Re-run AI Analysis** so the report covers that client.
    4. Read **Per-Client Talking Points** and **Forecast Outlook**.
    5. Click **Print / PDF** to save a copy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Spot recurring issues">
    1. Check the **Recurring Issues** card in **Key metrics**.
    2. Open **Identified Trends** to see the clusters, affected companies and sample tickets.
    3. Use a trend that hits several clients to plan a one-time fix, an automation or a knowledge base article.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Check category drift">
    1. Read **Category Watchlist**.
    2. Open **Category Breakdown** to confirm the numbers.
    3. Treat a category that keeps growing without a clear reason as a process or tooling gap.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Turn recommendations into Planner work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select one client">
    Pick a single company in the company selector. Global View cannot create company-specific Planner items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select recommendations">
    Check the recommendations you want in **Recommended Actions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Preview selected in Planner">
    This asks the AI for a preview. It creates nothing yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the preview">
    In **Planner Preview**, read each proposal's title, summary, **What**, **Where**, **Why it matters** and **How**. Select or clear proposals. The footer shows how many items will be created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add selected to Planner">
    This creates the items now. Click **Cancel** to leave without creating any.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Planner">
    Check the new items, then add dates, owners, costs and client visibility.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New items start **Not Started**, with no scheduled dates, and are hidden from clients. Adding the same recommendations again creates another set. If a request fails or you are unsure, check Planner before you try again.

## Choose which tickets Ticket Trends excludes

MSP administrators with **Manage Settings Ticket Trends** access can exclude values from every chart and analysis. The exclusions apply to your whole tenant, whatever company is selected.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Go to **Settings > MSP Tools > Ticket Trends**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick values to exclude">
    In **Boards**, **Types**, **Categories**, **Issue Types**, **Sub-Issue Types**, **Statuses**, **Sources** and **Priorities**, select the values to leave out. Remove a chip to include that value again. A counter shows how many values are excluded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save Changes">
    A toast reads **Saved. Rebuilding trend aggregates, this takes 1-2 minutes.** Click **Reset** before saving to go back to the last saved selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the report">
    After a couple of minutes, reopen Ticket Trends and check **Last analyzed**. Re-run the analysis if the old result still shows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Exclusions do not delete tickets or stop PSA sync. The counter counts excluded values, not tickets. The dropdowns are empty until a PSA has synced tickets.

## Export and share

- Click **Print / PDF** on the report to open the browser print dialog. Save as PDF from there. Check the pages before you share.
- Click the CSV icon next to **Weekly Data**, **Company Metrics** or **Category Metrics** to download that table. A CSV does not contain the whole brief or every ticket.
- For a one-image capture, add `?screenshot=1` to the Ticket Trends address. The page flattens so Edge or Chrome **Capture whole page** can capture it. Expand the evidence you want first.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Something looks missing or wrong | Check your PSA integration's sync settings first. Is ticket sync on? Are the boards, queues and statuses you need included? Is the integration syncing recently? See [Integration Company Mapping](/user-guides/settings/integration-company-mapping). |
| **AI Analysis** is missing | You need Ticket Trends write access and AI turned on. Read access alone cannot generate a report. |
| **Re-run AI Analysis** is disabled | An analysis is running, no data is available, or the weekly limit applies. Read the message beside the button. |
| An old report is showing | Check **Last analyzed**, and whether you picked an older analysis in **Analysis history**. |
| The analysis failed | Wait for the failure message, then run it again. |
| A company is missing | Check the company scope, the company mapping, the PSA sync coverage and the exclusions in settings. |
| A category is missing from the narrative | Small categories are left out of the narrative but still show in **Category Breakdown**. If it is missing there too, its tickets are not syncing. |
| Totals differ from the PSA | Compare period definitions, sync freshness, exclusions and saved versus current counts. |
| **Preview selected in Planner** is disabled | Select one client and at least one recommendation, and check you have Planner write access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can company users open Ticket Trends?">
    No. Share only reviewed output with clients.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does read access let me run the AI analysis?">
    No. Running it needs Ticket Trends write access and AI turned on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the company selector start a new analysis?">
    No. It loads the saved analysis for that scope. Click **AI Analysis** for a new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did the page open on a different lookback?">
    It prefers a period that already has a saved analysis. Pick the period you want.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I re-run after every sync?">
    A successful analysis can be re-generated once per week.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Planner Preview create items?">
    No. **Add selected to Planner** creates them. They start with no dates and hidden from clients.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add suggestions for all companies at once?">
    No. Select one client and at least one recommendation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Reset undo saved exclusions?">
    No. It restores the last saved selection in the editor. To undo a saved exclusion, change the selection and save again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Saved mean the report was rebuilt?">
    The settings saved. The rebuild runs in the background and takes a couple of minutes. If old results persist, ask your administrator to check.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
