Financial Reporting shows each client's revenue next to the support effort and endpoints behind it. Each company gets a letter grade so you can find your best and weakest accounts quickly. It is for MSP staff.

Financial Reporting lives in **MSP Tools > Client Success** as two tabs: **Financial** for the per-company table and **Revenue Trends** for monthly revenue history and a 12-month revenue forecast.

## Before you start

- You need Financial Reporting read access. Company portal users cannot open the page. Changing the reporting model needs separate settings permissions.
- Connect and map a supported PSA. Financial Reporting reads ConnectWise, Autotask and Halo PSA. See [Integration Company Mapping](/user-guides/settings/integration-company-mapping).
- Keep contracts or agreements, invoices or billing items, and time entries syncing in your PSA integration. Revenue comes from the first two and support hours from the third. Allow one full sync after you turn any of them on.
- Device or user metrics also need synced device or user records.
- Set your revenue categories first in [Financial Reporting settings](/user-guides/settings/financial-reporting). Without them the page cannot tell managed revenue from project revenue.
- [Where Financial Reporting data comes from](/msp-guides/msp-tools/financial-reporting-data-sources) explains each number.

## Compare companies

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Financial tab">
    In the sidebar, open **MSP Tools > Client Success**, then select the **Financial** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the company scope">
    Use the company selector at the top of the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a period">
    Open **Period** and pick a month, **Trailing 3 Months (Avg)**, **Year to Date** or **Current Month**. See the table below.

    <Frame caption="The Period menu. The named months move forward with the calendar.">
      <img src="/images/product/financial-reader-period.png" alt="Financial Reporting period menu with current month, trailing three-month average, year to date and six historical months" width="406" height="590" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the rows">
    Use the **Company** search, **Company type**, the **Grade** filter or the number ranges on the column headers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the abbreviations">
    Click **Glossary** for metric definitions. Hover a column heading or a grade for its explanation and score breakdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the evidence">
    Click a linked revenue, support hours or endpoint value to see the records behind it. Check the period, provider, totals and records before you act on a discrepancy. Close the detail to return to the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A dash or an ordinary zero may have no link. Seat-profile endpoints can open even at zero, to show what setup is missing.

### Periods

| Choice | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Current Month** | The current calendar month so far. |
| **Trailing 3 Months (Avg)** | The monthly average of the three most recent complete months. This is the default. |
| **Year to Date** | The monthly average from January through the current month. It is not the year-to-date total. |
| A named month | One of the six previous months. |

Revenue and support hours are monthly values for multi-month periods. A detail can show both **Period Total** and **Monthly Average**, so compare like with like. Endpoint counts are not a sum of every device or user seen across the months.

### Metrics

| Metric | What it is |
|---|---|
| **MRR** | Revenue classified as Monthly Recurring Revenue. |
| **ORR** | Other recurring revenue, such as separately classified recurring subscriptions. |
| **NRR** | Non-recurring revenue, such as project or one-time work. |
| **Support Hours** | Hours worked on tickets in the support boards, queues or teams you chose in settings. |
| Endpoints | Devices, users or both, following your reporting model. The column selector adds raw **Endpoints** and **Users** columns. |
| **RHEM** | Reactive hours per endpoint per month. |
| **EHR** | Managed revenue divided by support hours. It needs at least one support hour per month. |
| **AISP** | Managed revenue per endpoint. With a complete seat-profile model it is allocated monthly revenue per pricing seat. |

The cards above the table use totals for the whole company scope and period. They do not average the visible rows, and the grade and number filters do not change them.

### Grades

The grade combines three scores. Each score is 100, 80, 60, 40 or 20.

| Metric | Weight | 100 | 80 | 60 | 40 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RHEM** | 33% | under 0.33 | under 0.50 | under 0.75 | under 1.0 | 1.0 or more |
| **EHR** | 34% | 200 or more | 150 or more | 100 or more | 75 or more | under 75 |
| **AISP** | 33% | 200 or more | 150 or more | 100 or more | 75 or more | under 75 |

EHR and AISP amounts are in your tenant currency. When you set a **Target Seat Price**, AISP scores 100 at or above the target, then 80, 60 and 40 at 90%, 80% and 70% of it, and 20 below that.

| Grade | Score |
|---|---|
| **A** | 80 to 100 |
| **B** | 60 to 79 |
| **C** | 40 to 59 |
| **D** | 20 to 39 |
| **F** | under 20 |

A missing metric scores a neutral 60. The grade compares three revenue and support measures. It does not include every cost, so it is not net profit.

A dash means the metric is not available. EHR stays blank below one support hour per month.

**Needs setup** means the company's seat-profile model is incomplete, so grading is held back. Open the endpoint detail to see pricing seats, support endpoints, allocated revenue, targets, conflicts and gaps. Missing license seats, missing targets, revenue allocated to profiles without seats and unmapped managed revenue can all block grading.

Pricing seats and support endpoints can differ. An email-only profile can count for pricing and be left out of support endpoints. A person who matches several profiles counts in the earlier profile.

Financial grades feed the Financial dimension of [Client Health](/user-guides/msp-tools/client-health).

## Check Microsoft 365 licenses against devices

The Microsoft 365 license check compares each client's licensed users with its device count, so you can find clients with more devices than licensed users. It needs a Microsoft 365 or CIPP connection. MSPortal reads licenses from Microsoft 365 first, and from CIPP for clients without a Microsoft 365 connection.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the columns">
    On the **Financial** tab, click **Columns** and turn on the columns you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the difference">
    Sort or filter **Licensed users minus devices**. A red, negative number means the client has more devices than licensed users, so per-user billing may be short. A grey zero means they match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the figures">
    Hover a **Licensed users minus devices** value to see the licensed users, the device count and the license source.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **M365 Business Basic**, **M365 Business Standard**, **M365 Business Premium**, **M365 E1**, **M365 E3**, **M365 E5**, **M365 F1/F3** | Assigned licenses on each Microsoft 365 plan. |
| **Licensed users** | People on the plans chosen in [Financial Reporting settings](/user-guides/settings/financial-reporting#choose-the-plans-for-the-microsoft-365-license-check). Each person counts once, even with several plans. |
| **Licensed users minus devices** | Licensed users minus the client's devices. Green means more licensed users, red means more devices. |

The device count uses the **Managed Device Types** chosen in Financial Reporting settings. A dash means the client has no Microsoft 365 license data, or no managed device types are chosen. Hover the dash to see which. Add-ons such as Microsoft 365 E5 Security never count as licensed users.

## Review Revenue Trends

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Revenue Trends">
    On **MSP Tools > Client Success**, click the **Revenue Trends** tab and check the company scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the window">
    Open **Complete month window** and pick the last 3, 6, 12 or 24 complete months. The current month is left out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the comparison">
    Open **Trend comparison** and pick **Previous 2 months** or **Rolling 90-day average**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to group and draw it">
    Open **Group by** and pick **Classification**, **Line type**, **Subtype** or **Item**. Under **Chart style**, pick **Bars** or **Lines**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    Read **Total Revenue**, the completed month change, **Top Group** and **Peak Month**. Then compare the **Monthly breakdown** table under the chart.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click a legend item to hide or show a series in the chart. This does not reclassify revenue or remove it from the table.

**Previous 2 months** compares the latest complete month with the one before it. **Rolling 90-day average** compares the average of the latest three complete months with the three before them. It uses calendar months, not a daily window. A change may show **Not enough completed history** when there are too few months or the earlier period is zero.

Revenue Trends shows monthly revenue history. The **Financial** tab shows monthly-average metrics for the period you picked. A trend total for a window does not equal one company's monthly-average MRR.

## Forecast the next 12 months

The **Revenue forecast** section at the end of the **Revenue Trends** tab estimates revenue for the next 12 months. It adds three sources together:

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Baseline** | The average monthly revenue of your recent complete months, repeated for each forecast month. |
| **Planner** | Planned Planner work, weighted by its status. |
| **HubSpot** | Open HubSpot new-business deals, weighted by their deal stage probability. |
| **Total** | Baseline plus Planner plus HubSpot for the month. |

The footer row, **12-month forecast**, totals each column across the 12 months. These are planning estimates, not booked revenue.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the forecast">
    On **MSP Tools > Client Success**, click the **Revenue Trends** tab and scroll to **Revenue forecast** at the end. Use the arrow beside the title to collapse or expand the section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company scope">
    The forecast follows the company selector at the top of the app. HubSpot deals count for a company only when their HubSpot company is mapped to it. Deals for unmapped prospects appear only when you view all companies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the baseline">
    Open **Historical baseline** and pick **3 months** or **6 months**. The default is 6 months.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the Planner probabilities">
    Click **Assumptions**. Under **Planner probabilities**, set the percentage for **Not Started**, **Pending Approval**, **In Progress** and **On Hold** work, then click **Apply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    Compare each month's **Total**, then the **12-month forecast** row. On a phone, each month shows as a card with the same four amounts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How the forecast is built

- **Baseline** averages the revenue of the previous 3 or 6 complete months. Months with no revenue count as zero, so they lower the average. The current month is left out.
- **Planner** amounts follow each item's scheduled dates and billing frequency: one-time, monthly or annual. Each amount is weighted by the probability for the item's status. Completed and cancelled work is always left out, and recurring work that has already started is not added again.
- **HubSpot** counts open new-business deals once, in their expected close month, weighted by the probability of their current deal stage. Only deals in your tenant currency are included. Amounts are never converted or guessed.

The **Forecast assumptions** dialog shows how many Planner items and HubSpot deals were included. It also counts the ones left out because a date, amount, new-business classification or currency is missing or unsupported.

<Note>
Planner probabilities change only your current view. They are not saved, and they do not change the forecast for anyone else. Reloading the page restores the defaults: 25% for **Not Started**, 50% for **Pending Approval**, 90% for **In Progress** and 0% for **On Hold**.
</Note>

To include HubSpot deals, connect HubSpot and map its companies. See [Configure HubSpot Integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-hubspot-integration). The Planner column needs permission to read Planner.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **Financial** and **Revenue Trends** tabs are missing from **Client Success** | Ask an administrator to check your Financial Reporting read permission and that the Client Health feature is on. |
| The page says **No financial data available** | Connect ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo, set your revenue categories in settings, and wait for contracts and invoices to sync. |
| A company shows **Needs setup** | Open its endpoint detail and fix the seat-profile gaps in Financial Reporting settings. Allow time for recalculation. |
| EHR shows a dash although revenue exists | The company has less than one support hour per month. Check support hours and the support boards in settings. |
| Numbers differ from your PSA | Compare company mapping, period totals against monthly averages, revenue category rules, billing cadence and sync freshness. Open the linked values to see the records. |
| Revenue Trends omits this month | It uses complete months only. Use the **Financial** tab with **Current Month** for the month in progress. |
| **Baseline** and **Total** show a dash | There is no completed-month revenue to average. Planner and HubSpot estimates still show. Check your PSA sync and revenue categories. |
| The **HubSpot** column is all zero | Connect HubSpot in **Settings > Integrations** and wait for a sync. For one company, map its HubSpot company. Click **Assumptions** to see how many deals were included or left out. |
| The **Planner** column is all zero | Check that you can read Planner, and that the items have dates, amounts and an open status. Click **Assumptions** for the included and excluded counts. |
| The page says **The forecast could not be loaded** | Reload the page. Revenue history above the forecast stays available. |
| A settings change has not shown up | Saving starts a background recalculation. Wait a few minutes. Client Health updates on its own refresh. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Year to Date show my total revenue for the year?">
    No. It shows monthly averages from January through the current month. Use **Period Total** in the detail to reconcile totals.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Trailing 3 Months include this month?">
    No. It uses the three most recent complete months. **Current Month** is a separate choice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a D or F mean the company loses money?">
    No. The grade compares three configured measures and leaves out other costs. Check the data and settings first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do the cards differ from my filtered rows?">
    The cards cover the whole scope and period. Grade and number filters only change the table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can pricing seats differ from support endpoints?">
    Yes. Each seat profile decides whether its seats count toward support endpoints.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Rolling 90-day average use the last 90 days?">
    No. It compares the latest three complete months with the three before.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the Planner probabilities change the forecast for my team?">
    No. The probabilities apply only to your current view and reset when you reload the page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a HubSpot deal not appear for a company?">
    The deal's HubSpot company must be mapped to that MSPortal company. Unmapped prospects appear only when you view all companies. Deals that are not new business, have no amount or close date, or use another currency are left out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Licensed users count a person with two plans twice?">
    No. Each person counts once across the plans chosen in settings. Add-ons such as Copilot, Teams Phone or E5 Security never count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will a settings save update Client Health right away?">
    No. Financial recalculation and Client Health publication are separate. Click **Refresh Scores** on the **Health** tab after the financial data updates.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
