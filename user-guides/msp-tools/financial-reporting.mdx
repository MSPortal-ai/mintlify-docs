Use **MSP Tools > Financial Reporting** to compare company revenue with support effort and the configured device, user or seat counts. Start with **By Company** for individual accounts, then use **Revenue Trends** for changes across complete months.

## Before you start

This is an MSP staff tool. Your role needs Financial Reporting read access; Company portal users do not use this page. Changing the reporting model requires separate settings permissions.

Connect and map a supported PSA: **ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo PSA**. Check its relevant contract/agreement, billing/invoice and ticket activity syncs. Device or user metrics also need the corresponding synchronized records and reporting model. A connected provider alone does not prove that every company has usable evidence.

Use [Financial Reporting Settings](/user-guides/settings/financial-reporting) to review revenue classifications, support boards and endpoint choices before interpreting a grade.

## Compare companies

1. Open **Financial Reporting > By Company** and choose the intended company scope in the portal's company selector.
2. Choose a **Period**. Use the table's Company search, Company Type control, Grade filter or numeric ranges to narrow the rows.
3. Open **Glossary** for metric abbreviations. Hover a metric heading or grade for its explanation and scoring breakdown.
4. Click a linked revenue, support-hours or endpoint value to inspect its supporting records. A dash or ordinary zero may have no detail link; seat-profile endpoints can open even at zero to explain missing setup.
5. Review the detail's period, provider, totals and records before acting on a discrepancy. Close the detail to return to the table.

### Understand the period

<Frame caption="MSP example: the period menu. Named months advance with the calendar.">
  <img src="/images/product/financial-reader-period.png" alt="Financial Reporting period menu with current month, trailing three-month average, year to date and six historical months" width="406" height="590" />
</Frame>

| Choice | Meaning in By Company |
|---|---|
| **Current Month** | The current calendar month's available data; the month is still in progress. |
| **Trailing 3 Months (Avg)** | Monthly average across the three most recent complete calendar months, excluding the current month. This is the default. |
| **Year to Date** | Monthly average from January through the current month, including that incomplete month. It is not the year-to-date revenue total. |
| Named historical month | One of the six preceding months. |

Revenue and support hours are normalized to monthly values for multi-month periods. A detail can show both **Period Total** and **Monthly Average**; compare like with like. Endpoint counts are not a sum of every device or user seen across the selected months.

### Read the metrics

| Metric | What to check |
|---|---|
| **MRR** | Revenue classified as Monthly Recurring Revenue for the managed offering. |
| **ORR** | Other recurring revenue, such as separately classified recurring subscriptions. |
| **NRR** | Non-recurring revenue, such as project or one-time work. |
| **Support Hours** | Eligible synchronized support activity under the configured support-board rules. |
| Device, user or managed endpoints | The resolved reporting model. Additional raw **Endpoints** and **Users** columns are available in the column selector. |
| **RHEM** | Reactive support hours per endpoint per month. |
| **EHR** | Managed revenue divided by support hours. It requires at least one support hour per month. |
| **AISP** | Managed revenue per endpoint under ordinary models; allocated monthly revenue per pricing seat under a complete seat-profile model. |

The overview averages use eligible portfolio totals rather than a simple average of the visible page's ratios. Grade and numeric row filters do not make the overview cards a total of those filtered rows. Use the company scope and period, then inspect the table separately.

### Interpret grades and missing values

The displayed grade combines RHEM (33%), EHR (34%) and AISP (33%). Seat-price targets can change its seat-price component. A grade is a configured operational comparison; it does not calculate all labor, product, overhead or other costs and does not establish an account's net profit.

A dash means the metric is unavailable or not applicable. For example, EHR stays blank below one support hour per month and contributes a neutral score. Do not interpret missing hours as an exceptionally high hourly rate.

**Needs setup** means the company's seat-profile configuration is incomplete and grading is withheld. Open the endpoint detail to inspect pricing seats, support endpoints, allocated revenue, targets, conflicts and setup gaps. Missing license seats, missing targets, revenue allocated to profiles without seats, or unmapped managed revenue can prevent grading.

Pricing seats and support endpoints can differ: an email-only profile can contribute to pricing while being excluded from reactive-support endpoint counts. A person matching multiple profiles counts in the earlier profile; review the reported conflicts and profile order.

## Review Revenue Trends

1. Open **Revenue Trends** and confirm the company scope.
2. Choose the **Complete month window**. The current incomplete month is excluded.
3. Choose **Group by**: Classification, Line type, Subtype or Item.
4. Choose **Bars** or **Lines**. Use chart legend controls to hide or show a series for inspection; this does not reclassify revenue or remove it from the monthly breakdown.
5. Read **Total Revenue**, the completed-period change, **Top Group** and **Peak Month**, then compare the **Monthly breakdown** below the chart.

**Previous 2 months** compares the latest complete month with the preceding complete month. **Rolling 90-day average** compares the average of the latest three complete months with the preceding three complete months; it is a calendar-month approximation, not a daily rolling calculation. A missing percentage can mean insufficient completed history or a zero prior-period baseline.

Revenue Trends and By Company answer different questions. Trends shows monthly revenue history; By Company presents normalized company metrics and the current resolved endpoint model. Do not expect a Trends window total to equal one company's monthly-average MRR cell.

## Troubleshooting and FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Financial Reporting missing from my portal?">
    This is an MSP staff page. Ask your MSP administrator to check feature availability and your Financial Reporting read permission. Company portal access does not grant access to this tool.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Year to Date show my total revenue for the year?">
    No. By Company normalizes revenue and support hours to monthly averages across January through the current month. Use the detail's Period Total when reconciling totals, and account for the incomplete current month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Trailing 3 Months include this month?">
    No. It averages the three most recent complete calendar months. Current Month is a separate choice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Effective Hourly Rate a dash when revenue exists?">
    EHR requires at least one support hour per month. Below that threshold it is unavailable and receives a neutral score. Check support activity and board settings; a dash does not mean unlimited profitability.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a D or F prove the company is losing money?">
    No. The grade compares three configured revenue and support metrics. It does not include every cost needed to determine net profit. Inspect the underlying data and settings before drawing that conclusion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do the overview cards differ from my filtered rows?">
    They use eligible portfolio totals across the company scope, rather than a simple average of the current page. Grade and numeric row filters do not turn those cards into a summary of only the matching rows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a company show Needs setup?">
    Its seat-profile model is incomplete. Open the endpoint detail for missing seats or targets, revenue mapped to profiles without seats, or unmapped managed revenue. Correct the company model in Financial Reporting Settings and allow recalculation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can pricing seats differ from support endpoints?">
    Yes. Each seat profile separately controls whether its seats count toward support endpoints. An email-only profile can count for pricing while being excluded from support effort calculations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Revenue Trends omit the current month?">
    It compares complete calendar months to avoid comparing a partial month with a finished month. Use By Company > Current Month for the current month's available metrics.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Rolling 90-day average calculated from the last 90 individual days?">
    No. It compares the latest three complete calendar months with the preceding three complete months. It is a monthly approximation, and the percentage needs a positive previous baseline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do financial results disagree with my PSA?">
    Align company mapping, period totals versus monthly averages, revenue category precedence, billing cadence, support-board selection and source freshness. Open the linked metric details before changing settings. Saved configuration can precede completed background recalculation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will a settings save immediately update Client Health too?">
    Do not assume so. Financial Reporting recalculation and Client Health score publication are separate stages. Check the resulting financial data, then the published Client Health evidence and refresh state.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
