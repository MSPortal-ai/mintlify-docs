This guide shows MSP staff how to request a new Client Health scorecard. The refresh covers your whole tenant. Choosing one company in the company selector does not limit it.

## Before you start

- You need access to Client Health under **MSP Tools**.
- If source data looks missing or stale, check integration sync health and company mappings first. A refresh calculates scores from data MSPortal already has. It does not resync your integrations.
- Scores also refresh on their own every day at **2:00 AM UTC**.

## Request a new scorecard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Client Health">
    In the sidebar, open **MSP Tools > Client Health**. The refresh button is on the portfolio page. On a company's detail page, click the back arrow first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Refresh Scores">
    The button changes to **Refreshing...** while MSPortal checks the job every two seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the message">
    A success toast reads **Refreshed N companies**. A failure toast reads **Refresh failed**, or a more specific error.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    The page reloads with the new scorecard. Open a company and check its **Last computed** date and evidence coverage.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The last complete scorecard stays visible while a refresh runs. A failed run never publishes a partial scorecard. If you leave the button waiting for more than five minutes, the page stops watching the job, so reload the page and check the latest scorecard before you click again.

## Fix stale or missing evidence first

<Steps>
  <Step title="Repair the source">
    Reconnect the integration or correct the company mapping. See [Integration Company Mapping](/user-guides/settings/integration-company-mapping).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the data sync">
    Wait for the integration to bring in the new data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the scores">
    Click **Refresh Scores** so the next scorecard uses the corrected evidence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The stale banner shows when the scorecard's source data is more than 36 hours old. **Last computed** only says when the scores were calculated. It does not say the source feeds are fresh. A refresh does not remove manual risk flags, so a company can keep a displayed risk level that differs from the calculated one.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No published Customer Success generation is available yet** | No complete scorecard exists yet. Click **Refresh Scores**. If it keeps failing, contact MSPortal support. |
| **Failed to start refresh** | The refresh was not accepted. Wait a moment and try again. |
| **Failed to check refresh status** | The page lost track of the job. It may still be running. Reload and check the latest **Last computed** date before you try again. |
| **Refresh failed** | The old scorecard stays in place. Check integration health, then try again. |
| A company still shows **Limited data** after a refresh | The company still lacks the evidence the scoring model needs. See [Understand Client Health scoring and evidence](/user-guides/msp-tools/understand-health-scoring). |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Refresh Scores affect only the selected company?">
    No. It recalculates the scorecard for your whole tenant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When is the automatic refresh?">
    Every day at 2:00 AM UTC. It can take some time to finish.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Refresh Scores resync my integrations?">
    No. It uses the data already in MSPortal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to the old scorecard during a refresh?">
    It stays visible until the new one is published.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a refresh give every company a score?">
    No. A company still needs enough evidence to meet the scoring model's requirements.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Traffic Light statuses

Refreshing scores does not change manual Traffic Light statuses or Purple handling. Participating tenants use [Traffic Light Review](/user-guides/msp-tools/traffic-light-review) to change them and log reviews.
