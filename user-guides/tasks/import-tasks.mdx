Use this guide to create many tasks at once. You can import a list, add tasks from a ticket, device, report or other record, or turn a recorded meeting into tasks. This guide is for MSP staff. Review every task before it is created.

## Before you start

- You must be MSP staff.
- Imported tasks go on your personal list with no company. You can tag a company afterward.
- **From meeting** needs a connected meeting-notes tool and AI turned on for your portal.

## Import a list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Import">
    Go to **Tasks** and click **Import**, or click **Import tasks** in the side panel header.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste or upload your tasks">
    Paste one task per line, or click **Upload .csv or .txt** and choose a file up to 2 MB. Leading bullets, numbers, and checkbox markers are removed.

    For a CSV file, use these headers:

    ```csv
    title,description,due_date,priority
    Review restore evidence,Confirm the restore procedure,2026-10-01,high
    Update the recovery runbook,Record the agreed steps,2026-10-02,medium
    ```

    Use `YYYY-MM-DD` dates and `low`, `medium`, or `high` priorities. Quote fields that contain commas. A date or priority the import does not recognize is left out, so check the preview. Long titles and descriptions are shortened to their limits.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the preview">
    Under **Tasks to import**, untick rows to leave them out, remove unwanted rows, or use **Select All / Deselect All**. The count shows how many are selected.

    <Frame caption="CSV import preview.">
      <img src="/images/product/tasks-import.png" alt="CSV tasks preview showing titles, descriptions, dates, priorities and selection controls" width="2880" height="1920" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import">
    Click the import button, which reads **Import 5 tasks** for five selected rows. You can import up to 500 tasks at once. Nothing is created until you click it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tag a company (optional)">
    Open a task and choose a **Company** to share it with that company's team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add work from an existing record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Add to task list">
    On a supported list or record menu, choose **Add to task list**. To add several items, select them first and choose the same action from the bulk menu.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the proposed tasks">
    Check each task's title, **Company**, **Priority**, and **Due date**. A bulk addition creates one task per selected item. If the items belong to different companies, choose whether each task keeps its item's company or the whole batch uses one company.

    <Frame caption="Review linked tasks before adding them.">
      <img src="/images/product/tasks-bulk-add.png" alt="Add to task list dialog for selected portal items" width="2880" height="1920" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose sharing">
    Choose a company to share the task with its assigned team, or **Make private** to keep it to you and staff with task-list audit access.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the task">
    Click **Add task**, or **Add 3 tasks** when you selected three items. Each task keeps a link to its source. Creating or deleting the task does not change the source record.

    <Frame caption="Add a linked report from its row menu.">
      <img src="/images/product/tasks-report-entry.png" alt="Add to task list dialog opened for a report" width="2880" height="1920" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create tasks from a recorded meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click From meeting">
    On the Tasks page, or in the side panel (the video icon), click **From meeting**. The button shows only after your portal has connected a meeting-notes tool.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your account (first time)">
    If asked, paste your personal API key and click **Connect**. Create the key in your Fellow account under **User settings > Developer API**. If the tool is not connected for your portal, the dialog says so and an admin can connect it under **Settings > Integrations**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a meeting">
    Choose a meeting recorded in the last 14 days. Click **Create tasks**. AI reads its notes and transcript.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the suggestions">
    Edit titles, priorities, and due dates. Untick anything you do not want, then add the rest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Meeting action items

Action items assigned to staff in a meeting also appear in their task lists. If an item has no active staff assignee, its staff creator may own the task. Status, title, due date, supported priority changes, and staff reassignment stay in sync. Completing the task completes its meeting action item.

See [Add a meeting transcript](/user-guides/calendar/add-meeting-transcript) for the meeting workflow.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "That file is larger than 2 MB." | Split the file and import it in parts. |
| "No tasks found in this text." | Put one task per line, or check the CSV headers. |
| "You can import up to 500 tasks at a time." | Deselect some rows, then import the rest in a second pass. |
| A date or priority is blank after import | It was not in `YYYY-MM-DD` or `low`, `medium`, `high` form. Edit the task afterward. |
| **Add to task list** is missing | That list or menu does not support it yet. |
| **From meeting** is missing | Your portal has no connected meeting-notes tool. |
| "Fellow rejected this API key." | Create a new key and connect again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do imported tasks have a company?">
    No. They go on your personal list. Open a task to tag a company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a task change the original ticket or device?">
    No. The task only links to its source.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is anything created before I click Import?">
    No. You review every row first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
