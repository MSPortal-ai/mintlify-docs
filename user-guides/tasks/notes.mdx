Notes are a private scratchpad next to your task list. You can turn what you write, such as a call transcript, into suggested tasks. This guide is for MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You must be MSP staff.
- **Create tasks from notes** needs AI turned on for your portal.
- Notes are private to you. They are not part of task-list audit.

## Keep working notes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notes">
    Go to **Tasks > Notes** in the workspace, or click the **Notes** tab in the side panel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Type your notes">
    Type into the tab. It saves automatically after a short pause. The save indicator shows progress, or an error with a retry option. Switching tabs also saves anything pending.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add more tabs">
    Click **New notes tab**. You can have up to 20 tabs.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rename or delete a tab">
    Double-click a saved tab's name, or open its actions menu and choose **Rename**. The same menu has **Delete**, which asks you to confirm before it removes the tab and everything in it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Turn notes or a transcript into tasks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste the text">
    Paste the text into a notes tab. To use only part of it, select that passage. Otherwise the current tab is used.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Create tasks from notes">
    AI reads the text and suggests tasks. If the tab is empty, you are asked to write some notes first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the suggestions">
    Under **Review suggested tasks**, edit titles, priorities, and due dates. Untick anything you do not want.

    <Frame caption="AI suggestions from a synthetic operations-review transcript.">
      <img src="/images/product/tasks-ai-review.png" alt="Review suggested tasks with editable titles, priorities, dates and include checkboxes" width="2880" height="1920" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the tasks">
    Click **Add tasks** (the button shows the count). Only the ticked tasks are created, on your personal list. Nothing is created until you click it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Check dates and scope before you add the tasks. For a transcript attached to a meeting, use the meeting's [Transcript & AI step](/user-guides/calendar/add-meeting-transcript). There you can review generated action items and assign them to staff.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Couldn't save your notes." | Use the retry option in the save indicator. Your text stays in the tab. |
| "You can have up to 20 notes tabs." | Delete a tab you no longer need. |
| "Write some notes first." | The tab or selection is empty. Add text and try again. |
| "No tasks were found in these notes." | Add clearer action wording, such as who does what by when, and try again. |
| "Couldn't create tasks from these notes. Try again." | Try again. If AI is off for your portal, ask an admin to turn it on. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can other people see my notes?">
    No. Notes are private to you and are not included in task-list audit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does AI create tasks without my approval?">
    No. Suggestions stay in the review window until you click **Add tasks**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use just part of a tab?">
    Yes. Select the passage before you click **Create tasks from notes**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
