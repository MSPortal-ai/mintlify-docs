## Keep working notes

Open **Tasks → Notes** in the workspace or side panel. Type into a tab; it saves automatically after a short pause. The save indicator shows progress or a failure with a retry option. Switching tabs also saves pending edits.

Use **New notes tab** to add a tab, up to 20. Double-click a saved tab's name or open its actions menu to **Rename** it. The same menu offers **Delete**, with confirmation. Notes are private to their owner and are separate from task-list audit.

## Turn a transcript or notes into tasks

1. Paste the text into a notes tab. You can select a passage to use only that part; otherwise the current tab is used.
2. Choose **Create tasks from notes**. AI must be available for your tenant.
3. Review the suggested tasks. Edit titles, priorities and due dates, and untick anything you do not want.
4. Choose **Add tasks**. Only the selected tasks are created.

Suggestions do not create tasks until you approve them. Verify dates and scope before saving. For a transcript attached to a meeting, use the meeting's [Transcript & AI step](/user-guides/calendar/add-meeting-transcript), where generated action items can be reviewed and assigned to staff.

The Fellow recording picker is a separate integration workflow and has not been verified in this branch-preview guide.
