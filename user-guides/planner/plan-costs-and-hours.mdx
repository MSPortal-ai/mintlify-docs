Enter the effort, cost and likelihood for a Planner item. MSP staff set these estimates. Company users can read them.

## Before you start

- Changing estimates needs **Write Planner** or **Manage Planner**. Anyone with Planner read access can read them.
- Open the item from **List View** or the timeline. **Est. Hours**, **Qty** and **Costs**, and **Probability of Success** are on the left side of the dialog.
- Estimates are plans. They are not invoices, booked time or approved budgets.

## Add or change costs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Click the item's title in List View or on the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the quantity and unit amount">
    In a cost row, type the **Qty** and the unit amount under **Costs**. The row's amount is quantity times unit amount. When the quantity is not 1 and the amount is above zero, the total shows under the row.

    <Frame caption="Example: 3 at 250 one-time is 750, and 2 at 40 monthly is 80 per month. Save applies the estimate.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-cost-quantity-example.png" alt="Planner cost rows with quantity 3 at USD 250 one-time and quantity 2 at USD 40 monthly, showing totals 750 and 80." width="742" height="416" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the recurrence">
    Open the recurrence menu on the row and pick **One-time**, **Monthly** or **Annual**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more rows (optional)">
    Click **Add cost** for another row. An item can have up to 20 rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Cost changes stay in the form until you click **Save**. Reopen the item to confirm the stored estimate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Recurrence | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **One-time** | A single planned amount. |
| **Monthly** | An amount for each month. It is not already multiplied by twelve. |
| **Annual** | An amount for each year. |

Keep the cadences separate. A one-time 750 and a monthly 80 do not add up to 830 of anything.

## Remove a cost row

Click the trash button on the row. The last remaining row cannot be removed. To leave a zero estimate, clear its unit amount. A blank unit amount counts as zero. Do not use a quantity of zero to enter zero cost. A blank or zero quantity returns to 1 when you leave the field.

Some rows say **Managed from linked device standard prices**. Their fields and trash button are locked. Linking or unlinking a device can add or remove these rows. Do not add a manual row for the same cost, or it counts twice.

## Set hours and probability

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Est. Hours">
    Type the effort in **Est. Hours**. The field accepts quarter-hour steps such as 1.25. Clearing it saves zero. It does not create time entries or reserve a technician's time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Probability of Success">
    Drag the slider from 0% to 100% in steps of 5. A new item starts at 50%.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Changing probability does not change **Status** or **Business Value**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Probability feeds the expected-value and risk figures in Analytics. It is a planning estimate. It is not a promise and not a percent-complete measure. See [Understand Planner Analytics charts](/user-guides/planner/understand-analytics-charts).

## Change costs on several items

In List View, **Actions > Set Cost** replaces each selected item's cost rows with one row. Read [Update several Planner items](/user-guides/planner/update-multiple-items) before you use it.

## See how costs roll up

- The timeline column headers add up budget by due period. See [Read the Planner timeline](/user-guides/planner/read-timeline).
- Analytics sums one cost type at a time. See [Read and export Planner Analytics](/user-guides/planner/read-analytics).

Changing a chart filter never changes an item's cost rows.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The costs area shows an error | Click **Retry**. **Save** stays unavailable until costs load. Do not replace an estimate with zero because it failed to load. |
| The currency symbol looks wrong | The editor uses the company's currency. If it differs from what you see elsewhere, ask your MSP to check the company currency. The editor does not convert currencies. |
| A cost row is locked | It is managed from a linked device's standard price. Change the device standard instead. |
| You cannot add another row | The item already has 20 rows. |
| **Save** is grayed out | Wait for costs and custom fields to load, and check you have Write Planner. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I enter the unit price or the row total?">
    Enter the unit amount. The row amount is quantity times that amount.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can one item have one-time and monthly costs?">
    Yes. Use a separate row for each and choose a recurrence for each.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a Monthly amount already the annual total?">
    No. It is a monthly amount. The timeline can roll it into periods, and Analytics sums each cost type separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not remove the last cost row?">
    One row always remains. Clear its unit amount for a zero estimate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I enter zero cost?">
    Clear the unit amount or type 0. Leave the quantity at 1.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing a cost or hours field save immediately?">
    No. Click **Save** and reopen the item to confirm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Probability of Success measure completion?">
    No. It is the planning likelihood used in expected-value and risk figures.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
