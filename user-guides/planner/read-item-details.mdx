Open a Planner item to see its schedule, estimates, notes, linked records and files. This guide is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Planner read access. Your MSP controls which items company users can see.
- Reading an item never changes it. Closing the dialog without **Save** keeps everything as it was.
- Editing needs **Write Planner** or **Manage Planner**. See [Create and edit Planner items](/user-guides/planner/create-and-edit-items).

## Open an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the item">
    On the timeline, click the card's **title**. In List View, open **Planner Menu > List View** and click the item's title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the left side">
    The left side holds the schedule and estimates, then **Relationships** and **Custom Fields**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the right side">
    The right side holds **Description**, **Notes** and, for MSP staff, **Private Notes (Internal Only)**. Below them is the **Uploads** section for a saved item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll for more">
    Everything is in one dialog. Scroll inside it. There are no separate tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the dialog">
    Click the close button when you are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the plan

| Field | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** and company name | The work and the company it belongs to. |
| **Due Date** | The planned completion date. It decides where the card sits on the timeline. An item with no date appears in Unscheduled. |
| **Status** | Not Started, Pending Approval, In Progress, Completed, On Hold or Cancelled. |
| **Assignee** | The staff member who owns the item. Shown to MSP staff. |
| **Type** | The category your MSP set up. Types differ between MSPs. |
| **Business Value** | Low, Medium or High importance. It is separate from Status and Probability of Success. |
| **Est. Hours** | Estimated effort. These are not timesheet hours. |
| **Qty**, **Costs** and recurrence | Quantity, unit amount and whether each cost is One-time, Monthly or Annual. |
| **Probability of Success** | The planning probability used for expected value and risk figures. |

These are planning estimates. **Pending Approval** is a Planner status. It does not mean a quote, budget or purchase was approved. **Completed** describes the Planner item. It does not mean every linked ticket or project is closed.

See [Plan costs and hours](/user-guides/planner/plan-costs-and-hours) and [Read and export Planner Analytics](/user-guides/planner/read-analytics).

## Read the description and notes

- **Description** summarizes the initiative.
- **Notes** holds the shared explanation. Company users who can see the item can read it.
- **Private Notes (Internal Only)** is for MSP staff. Any MSP staff member with access can read it, not only its author. Company users never see this section.

Reading access does not allow editing. Fields can be turned off and **Save** can be unavailable. Ask your MSP if you need a correction.

## Read the relationships

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scroll to Relationships">
    **Relationships** lists the records that support the plan. They can include goals, compliance findings, Secure Score findings, devices, projects, opportunities and tickets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a record">
    Click a record name to open it. Some links open an MSPortal page and others open the connected provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Access to a Planner item does not give you access to the linked module or provider account. Linking a finding records a relationship. It does not fix the finding, finish a project or approve an opportunity. See [Open records linked to Planner work](/user-guides/planner/read-linked-records).

## Read custom fields and files

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Custom Fields">
    Click **Custom Fields** to expand it. The fields your MSP configured appear, and the item's Type can change which ones show.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Uploads">
    For a saved item, click **Uploads** to expand it. You see each file name with a **Download** control. A paperclip next to the title, when present, jumps to this section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Read-only users do not get upload or delete controls. See [Read and manage Planner files](/user-guides/planner/manage-files).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An item is missing | Check the company selector, your List View filters and **Archived**. Your MSP may have hidden the item from company users. |
| You cannot edit the item | You have read access only. Ask your MSP for Write Planner. |
| **Private Notes** or the assignee picker is missing | They are for MSP staff, so company users do not see them. |
| A linked record will not open | The linked module or provider needs its own access. Ask your MSP to check it. |
| **Custom Fields** is empty | Your MSP may not have set up fields for this type. If an error shows, reload the page. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can company users read the Notes?">
    Yes. Public Notes are part of an accessible item. Private Notes are for MSP staff only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Private Notes visible only to the author?">
    No. All MSP staff with access to the item can read them. Company users cannot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Pending Approval mean the quote or budget is approved?">
    No. It is the Planner item's status. Check the quote or budget for its own approval.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Completed mean linked tickets and projects are finished?">
    No. Open the linked records and check their own statuses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Est. Hours actual time entries?">
    No. They estimate the effort for the planned work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I download a file attached to an item?">
    Open the saved item, expand **Uploads** and click **Download** beside the file name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company users browse Planner templates?">
    No. Templates are an MSP catalog. Company users see only the items created from them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my MSP add the same work to several companies?">
    MSP staff can apply a template to many companies at once. Each company gets its own item. You see only items your MSP shares with you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
