Use a Planner item's **Relationships** section to open the goals, findings, devices, projects, opportunities and tickets behind the work. This guide is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need access to the Planner item. Linked modules and provider accounts can need their own access.
- To add or remove links, see [Link supporting records to Planner items](/user-guides/planner/link-supporting-records). That needs Write Planner or Manage Planner.

## Find the supporting records

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Click the item's title in List View, or open its card on the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to Relationships">
    Scroll inside the dialog to **Relationships**, on the left side.

    <Frame caption="A goal and a compliance finding linked to an item. Editing controls depend on your access.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-relationship-records.png" alt="Relationships section containing a strategic goal and a linked compliance finding." width="742" height="328" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a record">
    Click a record name. Links open in a separate tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the record, then return">
    Read the linked record's own details and status, then go back to the Planner item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Group | Where its links lead |
| --- | --- |
| **Strategic Goals** | The goal in MSPortal. |
| **Linked Findings** (compliance) | The compliance assignment in MSPortal. |
| **Linked Findings** (Secure Score) | The finding in MSPortal's Secure Score view. The **Open in Microsoft 365** icon opens the Microsoft action page. |
| **Linked Devices** | The device in MSPortal. |
| **Linked Projects** and **Linked Opportunities** | The connected provider, when a usable link exists. A name with no link is plain text. |
| **Linked Tickets** | The ticket in MSPortal. The **Open in PSA** icon opens the PSA when an external address is available. |
| **Cork Vulnerabilities**, **Cork Endpoints**, **Cork Compliance Events** | Labels and risk details only. These rows have no link to open. |

If the item is linked to a budget, the budget shows above the list. Not every item has every group. **No relationships defined.** means nothing is linked to the item. Related work can still exist elsewhere.

## Understand what a link means

A relationship explains why the work matters. It does not change the linked record.

- Linking a compliance assignment does not make it compliant.
- Linking a ticket does not close it.
- A Planner item marked **Completed** does not mean every linked record is finished.
- Ticket status shown beside a link belongs to the ticket, not the Planner item.

The cross button beside a link removes the link from the Planner item. It does not close or delete the linked record. Only staff with Write Planner see it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A project or opportunity shows as plain text | The provider does not supply a usable link here. Find the record in its own module or ask your MSP. |
| Nothing opened | Check your browser for a blocked pop-up. Links open in a new tab. |
| The provider asks you to sign in | Planner access does not include a provider account. Sign in, or ask your MSP for the details. |
| The destination says you have no access | Ask your MSP to check your company scope and access to that module. Do not change another company's scope to get around it. |
| The destination opens without the expected record | Tell your MSP the Planner item and the relationship you used. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find records related to a Planner item?">
    Open the item and scroll to **Relationships**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a Planner link give me access to another module?">
    No. The module or provider can need its own access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a project or opportunity plain text?">
    No usable provider link is available for that row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does clicking a ticket name open the PSA?">
    It opens the ticket in MSPortal. The **Open in PSA** icon opens the PSA when an address is available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I open the Microsoft action page for a Secure Score finding?">
    Click the **Open in Microsoft 365** icon. The name opens the MSPortal Secure Score view. Microsoft sign-in may be needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does linking a compliance finding fix it?">
    No. Remediation and answers are separate work in Compliance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does No relationships defined mean there is no related work?">
    It means nothing is linked to this item.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are the files for the plan?">
    Expand **Uploads** in the saved item. Files are separate from **Relationships**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
