A Planner item's **Type** says what kind of work it is. The same list also supplies budget categories. This guide is for company users and MSP staff. Your MSP manages the shared type list. Company administrators do not manage it.

## Before you start

- You need access to Planner or Budgets.
- Type names are set by your MSP. Examples such as Project, Hardware or Cloud Services are only examples. Your portal can use different names.
- MSP staff can change the list in [Manage Planner types and defaults](/user-guides/planner/manage-types).

## Read an item's type

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the work">
    Look in **Planner Menu > List View**, or on the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Click its title and read **Type** next to the other fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the status separately">
    **Status** reports progress. **Type** classifies the work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask if it looks wrong">
    Ask your MSP when the type is missing, unclear or does not fit the work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Field | What it describes |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | The category of planned work. |
| **Status** | Progress, such as Not Started or In Progress. |
| **Business Value** | High, Medium or Low importance. |
| **Cost recurrence** | Whether a cost is One-time, Monthly or Annual. |

A type called Project does not mean a PSA project is linked. Open **Relationships** to see linked records.

## Understand shared budget categories

Planner types also supply budget categories. Your MSP keeps one list for all companies, so choosing a company in the portal does not give you a separate list.

If your MSP renames a type, the new name shows in Planner and in budget forecasting. A new label does not mean the amount, due date, approval or status changed. Read the item and budget details.

Approved budget snapshots keep the category name recorded when they were approved, even after the live name changes. Ask your MSP which version you are reading before you treat an old label as an error.

## When fields or types change

Your MSP can change types, apply a template, import opportunities or set up custom fields for a type. The **Type** can decide which fields show under **Custom Fields**. A different set of fields does not mean you lost access.

If your MSP deletes a type, it can move the affected Planner items and editable budget lines to another type. Company users cannot create or delete types or change the default. Ask your MSP.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An item has no type or the wrong type | Ask your MSP to correct it. |
| A budget shows an older category name | It may be an approved snapshot. Ask your MSP. |
| Different items show different custom fields | The MSP can set fields per type. Check the item's **Type** and **Custom Fields**. |
| A category name changed | Your MSP renamed the type. Read the item and budget details for real changes. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is a Planner type the same as its status?">
    No. Type classifies the work. Status describes progress. Business Value and cost recurrence are separate too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the Project type create a project in the PSA?">
    No. A type is a label, not a linked record. Check **Relationships**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do Planner and Budgets show similar category names?">
    Planner types supply the shared budget category list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company administrator create a Planner type?">
    No. Ask your MSP to add or change the type.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Did a renamed type change my budget amount?">
    No. A new label alone does not change an amount. Review the budget with your MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does an approved budget still show an older category name?">
    Approved snapshots keep the name from approval time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the default type reclassify existing work?">
    No. The default applies to new items without a type only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
