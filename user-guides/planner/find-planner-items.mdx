Use List View to find a Planner item by title or compare many items in one table. This guide is for MSP staff with Manage Planner.

## Before you start

- You need **Manage Planner**. Without it, **Planner Menu** does not appear and List View opens the timeline. Use the [timeline](/user-guides/planner/read-timeline) instead.
- Open **Planner Menu > List View**. The table covers the companies in your company selector.

## Find and open an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the company selector">
    The list follows the company selection at the top of the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by title">
    Type part of the title in the **Filter...** box under the **Title** column.
    Results update after a short pause.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow with other columns (optional)">
    Use the filters under the other column headings. See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Click the item's title. Read-only users can read the details but not change
    them. See [Read a Planner item](/user-guides/planner/read-item-details).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Title filter searches titles only. It does not search descriptions, notes or linked records. Typing **high** here searches title text. Use the **Value** filter for High business value.

## Use the column filters

| Column       | How to narrow the list                                                                                        |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Title**    | Text contained in the title.                                                                                  |
| **Company**  | Choose one or more companies.                                                                                 |
| **Assignee** | Choose staff members or **Unassigned**. This is the item's assignee. Company users do not get the staff list. |
| **Status**   | Choose one status, such as Not Started, In Progress or Completed.                                             |
| **Value**    | Choose High, Medium or Low business value.                                                                    |
| **Type**     | Choose one item type.                                                                                         |
| **Source**   | Choose **Manual**, **Standards** or **PSA**.                                                                  |
| **Due Date** | Choose a preset, or **Not Set** for items with no due date.                                                   |
| **Hours**    | Enter a minimum, a maximum or both.                                                                           |

Filters work together. **Company** and **Assignee** accept several choices. **Status**, **Value**, **Type** and **Source** take one choice at a time. Filters search all matching items, not only the rows on the current page.

- **Standards** marks work created from your MSP's Standards (formerly called Tool Stack).
- **PSA** marks work that has a PSA-managed opportunity link. A manually linked opportunity does not make an item PSA-managed.

To clear the filters, click **Clear Filters**. If you arrived through a link that set a company or source filter, set that filter back to All to remove it.

### Use the Due Date filter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Due Date filter">
    Click the filter under the **Due Date** heading.

    <Frame caption="Due Date presets. Not Set finds work without a due date.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-list-due-date-options.png" alt="Due Date choices: All, Not Set, Past Due, Today, Tomorrow, Next Week and Next Month." width="256" height="460" />

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a preset">
    **Past Due** finds dates before today. It also finds Completed and Cancelled items, so combine it with **Status** to review unfinished work.
  </Step>

Filtering by date never changes an item's due date.

## Read pages and estimates

- The list shows 25 rows per page. Use **Rows per page** below the table to switch to 50 or 100, and the page controls to move between pages.
- Filtering, sorting, or changing the page size returns you to page 1. The **Source** column cannot be sorted.
- Scroll the table sideways to see **Hours** and **Cost**.
- **Cost** shows separate **One-time**, **Monthly** and **Annual** amounts in the company's currency. They are not added into one total.
- The row checkboxes select rows on the current page only. Changing page, filters or company clears the selection.

## Find archived work

Click **Archived** in the page header to show archived items only. Click it again to return to active work. Existing column filters still apply. Switching views returns you to page 1 and clears the selection.

An empty archived list can mean no accessible archived item matches your filters. It does not prove the item was deleted. See [Find archived Planner work](/user-guides/planner/find-archived-items).

## Use the row menu

Click the menu button at the end of a row.

| Option | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Archive** or **Restore** | Not offered for Standards items. |
| **Edit** | Opens the item. |
| **Save as template** | Creates a template from the item at once. |
| **Add to Meeting** | Adds the item to a meeting. |
| **Delete** | Turned off for Standards items. |

| Layout                     | What the PDF contains                                                                                  |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Planner view**           | Matching items as a timeline, one column per quarter, plus **Unscheduled** for items with no due date. |
| **List view**              | Matching items as a table, grouped by company when it covers several.                                  |
| **Planner and list views** | The timeline first, then the table starting on a new page.                                             |

**Current view** uses the filtered list across pages, not just the rows on the current page. A **year export** further limits dated items to those whose **Due Date** falls in that year and keeps matching items with no due date. Its planner layout uses that year's four quarters, Q1 through Q4. Stored start dates neither schedule items nor print in the PDF.

**With completed** includes completed work in a compact final **Completed** column on the planner and a separate **Completed** section in the list. Your other filters still apply: for example, a **Status** filter set to In Progress excludes completed work even when the checkbox is checked.

The PDF follows your company access, the company selector, column filters, sort and **Archived**. It prints up to 5,000 items. See [the timeline export guide](/user-guides/planner/read-timeline#how-dates-and-completed-work-appear) for the page layout and date rules.

List View has no **Export CSV** button. To export Planner data as CSV, open **Planner Menu > Analytics** and click **Export**. That export uses the Analytics filters, not the List View filters. See [Read and export Planner Analytics](/user-guides/planner/read-analytics).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An item is missing | Check the company selector, the column filters and **Archived**. |
| **Past Due** shows completed work | It filters by date only. Add a **Status** filter. |
| List View opens the timeline instead | You need Manage Planner. |
| Your selection disappeared | Changing page, filters or company clears it. Select the rows again. |
| An error appears above the table | Treat the list as incomplete. Reload the page or ask your MSP for help. |
| Completed work is missing from the PDF | Check **With completed**, then check the **Status** and other column filters. |
| A year PDF is missing an item | Check its **Due Date**, the selected year and column filters. A due-date filter can exclude undated work too. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does List View search descriptions or notes?">
    No. The Title filter searches titles only. The timeline search also checks
    descriptions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do filters search beyond the current page?">
    Yes. Filters search all matching items. The table shows one page at a time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are items without a due date?">
    Choose **Not Set** under **Due Date** here, or open **Unscheduled** on the
    timeline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is Assignee different from Filter by Role on the timeline?">
    **Assignee** is the person set on the item. The timeline role filter matches
    people who hold a role for a company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Archived show active and archived items together?">
    No. It shows archived items only. Click it again for active items.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Select all pick every page?">
    No. It selects the rows on the current page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are the costs annual totals?">
    No. One-time, Monthly and Annual amounts are shown separately and are
    planning estimates.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
