A Planner template holds reusable defaults for an initiative, such as a QBR preparation or a security assessment. This guide is for MSP staff. Company users never see the template catalog. They see only the items created from it.

A Planner template is different from a ticket template and from the text snippets you insert with **Add Template** in a notes box.

## Before you start

- You need **Manage Planner**. **Templates** appears in **Planner Menu** only with that permission.
- Templates are shared across all your MSP's companies. They have no company, due date, assignee, relationships, custom field values or **Uploads**. Set those on the real item.

## Create a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Templates">
    Choose **Planner Menu > Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Template">
    **New Template** is in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Click the title and type a clear name for the reusable plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the defaults">
    Choose **Status**, **Type** and **Business Value**. A new template starts at **Not Started** and **Medium**. Your MSP's Planner types supply the **Type** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter estimates">
    Fill in **Est. Hours**, the **Qty** and **Costs** rows and **Probability of Success**. A template can have up to 20 cost rows. Keep one-time, monthly and annual rows separate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the content">
    Add a **Description**, public **Notes** and **Private Notes (Internal Only)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set compliance auto-link and visibility">
    Pick **Compliance auto-link** checks if you want them, and review **Hide from company users** at the bottom. See the sections below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Reopen the template to check the stored defaults.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Templates keep each cost row's quantity, unit amount and recurrence. A template with 3 at 200 monthly still applies as 3 at 200 monthly.

Private Notes are MSP-wide internal notes, not author-only. Keep company-specific confidential details out of reusable content.

## Set the visibility default

**Hide from company users** stores a visibility default for the items you create from this template. A new template starts with the box unchecked. It does not give company users access to the template catalog.

When you apply a template, the **Hide from company users** field choice means "copy the template's visibility setting". It does not always hide the item. See [Apply a template to a Planner item](/user-guides/planner/apply-template-to-item).

## Set up compliance auto-link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Compliance auto-link">
    In the template editor, click the **Compliance auto-link** box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the checks">
    Choose the compliance checks whose company assignments should be linked when you apply the template.

    <Frame caption="Select checks whose existing company assignments should be linked.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-template-compliance-control.png" alt="Compliance auto-link picker and explanation in the Planner template editor." width="742" height="252" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    The setting stores the checks, not any company's findings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you apply the template, Planner links the newest matching assignment for each check in the target company. A company with no matching assignment gets no link for that check. Auto-link does not create an assignment or mark a check compliant.

## Find, edit or reuse a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the template">
    Type in the **Name** filter. The table also shows Description, Type, Default Status, Default Value, Hours, Cost and Created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Click the template's name, or open its menu and choose **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it and click Save">
    Closing without saving asks whether to discard your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Cost** column is a single summary. It does not show separate one-time, monthly and annual amounts, so open the template to read the cost rows. Editing a template does not change items already created from it.

## Save an item as a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the item">
    Open **Planner Menu > List View**, or the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Save as template">
    Open the item's menu (the row menu in List View, or the card menu on the timeline) and choose **Save as template**. It creates the template at once. No draft opens first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the template">
    Open **Templates** and check the new template.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The new template copies the item's reusable values. It sets **Status** to **Not Started** and checks **Hide from company users**. Review the notes, costs, compliance auto-link and visibility before you reuse it. Saving a template does not change or remove the original item.

## Delete templates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Open the template's menu and choose **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the confirmation">
    **Delete Template** says that items already created from it stay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete**, or **Cancel** to keep it. A short **Undo** button shows for a few seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To delete several templates, tick their rows and choose **Actions (N) > Delete Selected**. Check the count in the "Delete N templates?" dialog, then click the delete button.

Deleting a template removes it as a choice for future use. It does not delete items created from it.

## Apply templates to companies

Select template rows and choose **Actions (N) > Apply to Companies** to create new items. See [Create Planner items for multiple companies](/user-guides/planner/apply-templates-to-companies).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Templates** is missing from Planner Menu | You need Manage Planner. |
| **New Template** is missing, **Edit** and **Delete** are greyed out, and a template opens read-only | You have Write Planner but not Manage Planner. Template changes need Manage Planner. |
| "Failed to save template." appears | Check that the title is filled in and that you have Manage Planner, then try again. |
| The compliance picker is empty | Your MSP has no approved compliance checks to choose from. |
| An item you saved as a template is hidden from company users | **Save as template** checks **Hide from company users** on purpose. Clear it in the template if needed. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I create a reusable Planner template?">
    With Manage Planner, open **Planner Menu > Templates > New Template**, fill in the defaults and click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company users browse Planner templates?">
    No. The catalog is for MSP staff.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is this the same as a ticket template or a notes text template?">
    No. Planner templates supply planning fields. Ticket templates set up support requests. **Add Template** in notes inserts a text snippet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a template set the company, due date or assignee?">
    No. Set those on the item. In Apply to Companies you choose the companies and one due date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the Cost column show an annual total?">
    No. It is one summary figure. Open the template to see each cost row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does editing a template update existing items?">
    No. Existing items keep their values. Applying a template again replaces the fields you choose.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Save as template open a draft first?">
    No. It creates the template immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does compliance auto-link create missing compliance assignments?">
    No. It links existing assignments only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting a template remove the items created from it?">
    No. They stay as separate items.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
