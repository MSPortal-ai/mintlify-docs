Ask Planner AI to draft new Planner items or to change existing ones. It shows a proposal card first, and nothing is saved until you click **Apply**. This guide is for MSP staff. For read-only questions, see [Ask Planner AI about planned work](/user-guides/planner/ask-planner-ai).

## Before you start

- You need **Manage Planner**, and AI must be turned on for your MSP.
- Pick the company in the company selector first, or name the company in your message. Use company names, not IDs. If a name is ambiguous, the assistant asks which one you mean.
- Use the item editor for attachments, linked records, detailed cost rows and fractional hours. The AI proposal covers the main fields only.

## Draft one new item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Planner AI">
    On any Planner page, click the floating **Planner AI** button on the right edge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the request">
    Under **Try one of these**, choose **Draft a new item** to fill the message box, or type your own. A starter does not send until you press **Enter**.

    <Frame caption="This starter fills the message box. Review the request before you send it.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-ai-draft-prompt.png" alt="Planner AI Draft a new item shortcut." width="264" height="64" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the item">
    Give the company, a clear title, description, due date, status, business value, estimated hours, cost and recurrence. Say whether company users should see it. Use whole hours, because the proposal does not accept fractions.

    For example: "Draft a backup review for Acme Corporation, due 15 October, with Low business value, two hours, no cost and hidden from clients. Leave it Not Started for review."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the message">
    Press **Enter**. **Shift+Enter** starts a new line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review the proposal card

The assistant's reply and the proposal card are separate. A reply that says it drafted an item does not mean the item exists.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the card">
    Look under **Pending proposals: review and Apply to commit**, above the message box. A new item shows as **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each field">
    The card lists Title, Company, Type, Status, Business value, Cost, Cost type, Hours, Probability, dates, Description, notes and **Client visible**. A **Yes** under **Client visible** means company users can see the item. Scroll the card if it is long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix problems before you apply">
    Ask the assistant for a corrected draft, or open **Edit values (advanced)** to edit the proposal's JSON yourself. A mistake in the JSON shows "Edited JSON is not valid" and blocks **Apply**. Asking for a corrected draft is usually easier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Apply or Discard">
    **Apply** saves the item now. **Discard** removes the card and saves nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the saved item">
    After "Planner item created.", open **Planner Menu > List View**, find the item and reopen it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Ask the assistant to include important values, especially the company, client visibility and the cost's recurrence. If a field is missing from the card, the assistant may not have used the value you meant.

Applying a card is the save. There is no second **Save** in the chat. The card leaves **Pending proposals** after it applies, but the earlier reply can still say "drafted". If the result is unclear, search List View for the title before you ask again, because repeating a create request can make a second item.

Closing the assistant or clearing the conversation does not undo an applied change.

## Add several items at once

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the starter or paste a list">
    Choose **Bulk add items**, or type a list with a company for each item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Bulk create card">
    The card lists each item with a checkbox. Click **Select all** or **Deselect all**, or untick the rows you do not want. The card shows how many are selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    Click **Create N items**. A card can hold up to 50 items. If some fail, a message says how many were created and how many failed, and the card shows the details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you ask for a change to a pending batch, the new card replaces the old one. If you create items that were already created earlier in the same conversation, the card warns that applying again makes duplicates.

## Change an existing item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for the change">
    Choose **Move items between columns** or **Push due dates**, or describe the change. Include the company when item titles are similar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Update card">
    An **Update** card shows each changed field as the old value, an arrow and the new value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Apply">
    The item changes when you click **Apply**. You see "Planner item updated."
  </Step>
</Steps>

An update proposal can change status, due date, company, notes, costs, hours, business value, the archive flag and client visibility.

## Finish the item in Planner

After creating an item, use [Create and edit Planner items](/user-guides/planner/create-and-edit-items) to review visibility and add more fields. Use [Plan costs and hours](/user-guides/planner/plan-costs-and-hours) for detailed costs, [Link supporting records](/user-guides/planner/link-supporting-records) for relationships and [Read and manage Planner files](/user-guides/planner/manage-files) for attachments.

Creating a Planner item does not create a PSA opportunity, support ticket or project.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **Planner AI** button is missing | You need Manage Planner and AI turned on for your MSP. |
| A starter did not send | It filled the message box. Press **Enter** to send. |
| The assistant says it drafted an item but List View has nothing | Look for an unapplied card under **Pending proposals**. |
| **Apply** is not visible | Scroll the card. It sits at the bottom. |
| The company or date is wrong | Ask for a corrected card before you apply. |
| **Apply** shows an error | Fix the draft. Keep the title short, and use valid dates and whole hours. |
| You are not sure whether **Apply** worked | Search List View for the title before you create it again. |
| Company users cannot see the new item | Check **Client visible** in the card, or **Hide from company users** in the saved item, plus company scope and Planner access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can use Planner AI to create or change items?">
    MSP staff with Manage Planner, when AI is turned on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an AI draft create the item?">
    No. Click **Apply** on the card. Then confirm the item in List View.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to edit JSON?">
    No. **Edit values (advanced)** is optional. You can ask for a corrected draft instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I ask for fractional hours?">
    No. Use whole hours in AI proposals. Use the item editor for fractions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Discard delete a saved item?">
    No. It removes an unapplied card only. To delete an item, see [Archive, restore or delete Planner items](/user-guides/planner/archive-restore-delete).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does closing the assistant undo Apply?">
    No. An applied change is already saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I repeat a request when I am unsure it worked?">
    No. Search List View for the title first. Repeating can make a duplicate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the assistant still say drafted after I applied?">
    The earlier reply is not rewritten. Trust the saved item in List View.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a Planner item also create a ticket or PSA opportunity?">
    No. Those are separate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
