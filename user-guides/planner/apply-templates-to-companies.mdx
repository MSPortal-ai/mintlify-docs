Create the same Planner work for many companies in one step. Each selected template creates one new item in each selected company, so 2 templates for 3 companies make 6 new items. This guide is for MSP staff. Existing items are not changed. To change one existing item, use [Apply a template to a Planner item](/user-guides/planner/apply-template-to-item).

## Before you start

- You need **Manage Planner** for the target companies. Company users cannot use this action.
- Create the templates first. See [Create and manage Planner templates](/user-guides/planner/manage-templates).
- Keep each run to 500 template and company combinations or fewer.
- To link compliance checks, you also need access to read Compliance for the target companies.

## Create the items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Templates">
    Choose **Planner Menu > Templates** and find the templates with the **Name** filter. Review their costs, notes and visibility first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select templates and open the action">
    Tick the template rows, then click **Actions (N) > Apply to Companies**. The **Apply Templates to Companies** dialog opens. Confirm the names under **Selected Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the due date">
    Choose a **Due Date**. It starts 30 days ahead, and every new item in this run gets the same date.

    <Frame caption="One due date for all new items. This example shows October 6, 2026.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-bulk-template-due-date.png" alt="Required Due Date control and explanation that all Planner items will use that date." width="704" height="180" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select companies">
    Under **Select Companies**, search and tick each company. The list shows active companies. The count beside the title shows how many you picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the fields">
    Review **Fields to Apply**. See the next section. At least one field must stay ticked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the item count">
    Multiply selected templates by selected companies. That is how many items you will create.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Apply to N Companies">
    The button number is the company count, not the item count. Two templates and two companies show **Apply to 2 Companies** and create four items. This creates and saves the items. There is no separate **Save** step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    A message reports how many items were created. Open **Planner Menu > List View**, find the new titles and check each company's dates, costs, visibility and **Relationships**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Pick companies carefully

Typing in the search box keeps companies you already ticked. **Select All (N)** ticks every company in the current search results and keeps your earlier picks. Unticking it unticks only the companies in the current results. Clear the search at the end and check the selected count.

If a company is missing, clear the search and ask your MSP to check that the company is active and that you can manage Planner for it.

## Choose the fields to copy

All 13 fields start ticked. **None** clears them all except **Title**. **All** ticks them all again. The same choices apply to every item in the run.

<Frame caption="Bulk application includes both Cost Estimate and Cost Rows. Review the selection before you create the items.">
  <img src="/images/product/planner-bulk-template-fields.png" alt="The thirteen bulk-template fields, including costs, notes, visibility and compliance auto-link, all selected." width="820" height="872" />
</Frame>

| Field | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Always applied. The dialog says "Always applied. New items are created with the template title." |
| **Description**, **Type**, **Status**, **Business Value** | Copies the template's value. An unticked field gets the new-item default: no description, **Not Started** and **Medium**. |
| **Estimated Hours**, **Probability of Success** | Copies the template's estimate to each new item. It is not divided between companies. Unticked, hours are 0 and probability is 50%. |
| **Cost Estimate**, **Cost Rows (one-time / monthly / annual)** | Either one copies the template's cost rows, including quantity and unit amount. Ticking both does not duplicate rows. A template with no rows adds its legacy estimate as one One-time row. |
| **Public Notes**, **Private Notes** | Copies the notes. Private Notes stay MSP-only. |
| **Hide from company users** | Copies the template's visibility setting. **If you untick it, every new item is hidden from company users.** |
| **Compliance auto-link** | Links the newest matching assignment for each check, separately in each company. |

The company and due date come from the dialog. Assignees, uploaded files and custom field values are not copied. Other relationships are not set. Review the new items before company users see them. See [Plan costs and hours](/user-guides/planner/plan-costs-and-hours).

## Understand compliance results

Tick **Compliance auto-link** when you want each template's configured checks matched in each company.

A missing assignment does not stop the item from being created. You get a success message, and a separate error message says how many items were created without a matching link. That number counts items, not companies or checks.

For example, with one template and two companies you create two items. If only one company has the assignment, that item gets the link and the other item is created without it. Check **Relationships** on the affected item and arrange the compliance assignment separately.

## Cancel, retry and errors

- **Cancel** before you apply creates nothing. It resets the company choices, the field choices and the date.
- After a successful run, the dialog closes and the template selection clears.
- Deleting a template later does not remove the items it created.
- If an error or lost connection leaves the result unclear, check List View for the companies and titles before you start another run. A new run creates a new set with the same titles. It does not update earlier items. A compliance warning after success is not a reason to run the batch again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Apply to N Companies** is grayed out | Choose at least one company and one field, set a due date and wait for the company list to load. |
| A permission error appears | Ask your MSP to check that you have Manage Planner for the target companies, and Compliance read access if auto-link is ticked. |
| Fewer companies are selected than expected | Clear the search and review the ticked companies and the count. |
| New items are not on the timeline | Check the selected companies, the year, the filters and visibility. Use List View to find the titles. |
| A company user cannot see an item | Check **Hide from company users** on the item and the user's Planner access. |
| New items are hidden from company users | Either the **Hide from company users** field was unticked, or the template's own setting is hidden. Open each item and clear **Hide from company users**. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I create the same work for several companies?">
    Open **Planner Menu > Templates**, tick the templates and choose **Actions (N) > Apply to Companies**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many items do two templates and three companies create?">
    Six. The button shows the company count, not the item count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Apply to Companies update existing items with the same title?">
    No. It always creates new items.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to save each item afterward?">
    No. The run creates and saves them. Later edits need their own **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can each company get a different due date?">
    Not in one run. Use separate runs, or edit the items afterward.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the maximum run size?">
    500 template and company combinations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does ticking both Cost Estimate and Cost Rows double the costs?">
    No. Either one copies the template's cost rows once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does unticking Title leave items unnamed?">
    No. **Title** is always applied.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are new items visible to company users?">
    It depends on the **Hide from company users** field. Tick it to copy each template's visibility setting. Leave it unticked and new items start hidden.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are companies without a matching compliance assignment skipped?">
    No. Their items are created without that link, and a message counts those items.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company users run Apply to Companies?">
    No. It needs Manage Planner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting the templates undo the run?">
    No. The items stay.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
