Some Planner items come from sales opportunities in your MSP's PSA. This guide shows company users and MSP staff how to read those items and why their values can change.

## Before you start

- You need access to the Planner item. Your MSP controls company visibility, opportunity imports and integration settings.
- A visible Planner item can link to an opportunity page that you cannot open. Provider access is separate from Planner access.

## Read a PSA-linked item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Find it in **Planner Menu > List View** or on the timeline and click its title. In List View, the **Source** column shows **PSA** for PSA-managed items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the plan">
    Check the status, due date, description, hours and cost estimate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the opportunity">
    Look in **Relationships** under **Linked Opportunities**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it if a link is available">
    Click the name to open the opportunity in the provider, when you have access there. If there is no link, ask your MSP for the current details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A linked opportunity gives commercial context for the work. It does not prove the quote was accepted, an order was placed, the work was done or the amount was invoiced.

## Where the values come from

An MSP can import a synchronized Halo, ConnectWise or Autotask opportunity, or turn on automatic import. The item starts like this:

| Planner field | Comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Title | The opportunity summary. |
| Description | The opportunity details. |
| Due date | The opportunity's target date, or its deadline. |
| Cost | The opportunity value, as one One-time cost. |
| Status | The MSP's mapping of Open, Won and Lost to Planner statuses. |

A Won opportunity maps to **Completed** by default. The item can start as **Completed** even though no delivery work has happened. Read the description and confirm delivery with your MSP.

Later PSA syncs can refresh the fields your MSP chose, so a changed date or estimate can come from the opportunity and not from an edit in Planner. By default the refresh covers title, description, due date and cost estimate. Ask your MSP which fields the PSA manages when a value surprises you.

A manually linked opportunity does not always refresh Planner fields the way an imported item does. A relationship alone does not tell you which fields sync.

## Find the quote document

The opportunity link is not a quote file. For proposal documents use [Quotes](/user-guides/quotes/view-quotes) and the [Quoter, QuoteWerks and Salesbuildr document guide](/user-guides/quotes/download-quotes), or ask your MSP.

## If something looks wrong

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather the details">
    Note the Planner item title, the company and the field you question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your MSP">
    Ask whether the opportunity, an import status mapping or ongoing sync explains the value.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Company users do not repair PSA credentials or change the integration's mappings.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The opportunity name is plain text | No usable provider link exists for it. Ask your MSP. |
| The provider page will not open | Provider access is separate from Planner. Ask your MSP. |
| The due date or cost changed | A PSA sync probably refreshed it. Ask your MSP to check the source opportunity. |
| A new item says Completed | The MSP's mapping turned a Won opportunity into Completed. Confirm delivery separately. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why did an opportunity create a Planner item?">
    Your MSP can import synchronized PSA opportunities, or turn on automatic creation. Ask which applies to this item.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a linked opportunity mean the work is approved?">
    No. Check the quote or project with your MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can a new item say Completed?">
    Won opportunities map to Completed by default. That does not prove the work was done.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Planner access give me the PSA page?">
    No. Provider access is separate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is the quote document?">
    Use Quotes, or ask your MSP. The opportunity link is not a downloadable quote.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company administrator change import mappings?">
    No. Your MSP manages imports and integration mappings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
