Use the search box and the **Filters** drawer to narrow the Planner timeline. This guide is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Planner read access. Your MSP controls which items company users can see.
- Filters change only what you see. They never change an item's status, priority, dates or costs, and they never reveal hidden work.

## Search the timeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the timeline">
    Choose **Planner Menu > Timeline View** and check the company selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type in the search box">
    Type part of a title or description. Results update as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the year and Unscheduled">
    If you do not see the item, check the year and open **Unscheduled** for items with no due date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Typing exactly **high**, **medium** or **low** filters by **Business Value**. It does not search titles for those words. Any other text searches titles and descriptions only, not notes or linked records. Clear the box to remove the search.

## Filter by status, type and value

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Filters">
    Click the **Filters** button (sliders icon) in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose options">
    Tick options under **Status**, **Type** and **Business Value**. MSP staff also see **Company visibility** with **Visible to company users** and **Hidden from company users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Apply Filters">
    The drawer closes and the timeline shows the matching cards. Choices take effect as you tick them, so closing the drawer with the X keeps them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Options inside one section are alternatives. For example, **High** and **Medium** show items with either value. Different sections narrow the results together.

## Show finished work or recurring costs

Open the **Filters** drawer and use the switches at the top.

| Switch | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Show Completed & Cancelled** | Off by default, so those items are hidden. Turn it on to include them. Ticking **Completed** or **Cancelled** under **Status** turns it on for you. |
| **Project Recurring Costs** | Off by default. Turn it on to roll monthly and annual costs into every later period. It changes the cost figures only, not where cards appear. See [Read the Planner timeline](/user-guides/planner/read-timeline). |
| **Hide Optional Standards** | Appears only when your items include work from optional Standards. Turn it on to hide that work. |

## Filter by role

When your MSP has company roles set up, a **Filter by Role** menu appears in the toolbar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Filter by Role">
    Click **Filter by Role** in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick people">
    Choose one or more people. Each choice is a person in a role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The timeline then shows items for companies where a chosen person holds that role. It does not filter by the item's assignee. Role filters combine with the search box, the drawer filters and your company selector.

## Reset the filters

Click **Reset** in the **Filters** drawer. Reset clears the search box, role choices, status, type, business value, company visibility and **Hide Optional Standards**. It also returns the timeline to the current year in Quarterly view.

Reset keeps **Show Completed & Cancelled**, **Project Recurring Costs**, your view density and your company selection.

Changing the company selector clears the status, type, business value and company visibility choices.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Completed items are missing | Turn on **Show Completed & Cancelled** in **Filters**. |
| Searching for "high" finds nothing | The exact word **high** filters Business Value. Add more words to search titles. |
| Nothing matches Filter by Role | The role filter limits the timeline to those companies. Clear it, or check the other filters. |
| An item still seems missing | Clear the search and filters, check the year, open **Unscheduled**, then ask your MSP if the item was archived, hidden from company users or moved to another company. |
| The screen is empty and shows an error | Treat the result as incomplete. Reload the page or ask your MSP for help. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where are the Planner timeline filters?">
    Click the sliders button in the toolbar. The search box is in the same toolbar. **Filter by Role** appears when your MSP has role options.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do filters change my items?">
    No. They change which items you see. Changing an item needs the item editor and Write Planner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does closing the drawer discard my choices?">
    No. Choices apply as you make them. **Apply Filters** and the X both just close the drawer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Filter by Role show items assigned to that person?">
    No. It shows items for companies where that person holds the role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do filters apply to Unscheduled?">
    Yes. Its items and count follow the current filters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company users reveal work their MSP hid?">
    No. Filters do not override company access or an item's visibility.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Project Recurring Costs approve anything?">
    No. It only changes the displayed cost figures.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
