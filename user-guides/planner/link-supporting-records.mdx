Link the goals, compliance checks, Secure Score findings, devices, projects, opportunities and tickets that explain why a Planner item exists. This guide is for MSP staff. Company users read links in [Open records linked to Planner work](/user-guides/planner/read-linked-records).

## Before you start

- You need **Write Planner** or **Manage Planner**, plus access to the records you want to link.
- The item needs a **Company**. Pickers search that company's records.
- Relationships save with the item's **Save** button. Closing a picker does not save them.

## Add existing records

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Planner item">
    Click the item's title in List View or on the timeline, and confirm its company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Add menu">
    Scroll to **Relationships** on the left side and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a record type">
    Pick **Link to Goal**, **Link to Compliance Check**, **Link to Secure Score Finding**, **Link to Device**, **Link to Project**, **Link to Opportunity** or **Link to Ticket**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and select">
    Search the picker and click each record that belongs to the item. You can select more than one. The footer shows the selected count. Clicking a selected row again does not remove it or add a duplicate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Done">
    **Done** closes the picker. The records are now in the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review costs if you linked devices">
    Linked device standard prices can add or remove managed cost rows. Check the **Costs** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Click **Save** on the item, then reopen it to confirm the links.

    <Frame caption="Saved goal and compliance relationships. Use a row's remove button to take a link off before you save.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-relationship-records.png" alt="Planner Relationships section showing a linked goal and compliance assignment with remove controls." width="742" height="328" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Done** does not save the item. To abandon every unsaved change, close the item and choose **Discard**.

The device, project, opportunity and ticket pickers show a limited set of rows. Type a more specific name or number to find more. They have no next-page button.

## Link a compliance assignment

**Link to Compliance Check** opens **Select Compliance Assignment**. It selects an existing assignment for the item's company. It does not create a compliance check.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the picker">
    Click **Add > Link to Compliance Check**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by run (optional)">
    Open **Compliance run** and search by run name or date. Runs show newest first and the newest run with checks is marked **Latest**. Pick a run to see only its assignments. Click **Load more runs** for older runs, or clear the choice to return to **All runs**.

    <Frame caption="Search compliance runs by name or date. The date and Latest marker show below the name. Shown in MSPortal Demo.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-compliance-runs/desktop.png" alt="Compliance picker with a searchable run filter, run names and dates, and the newest run marked Latest." width="1280" height="900" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="The selected run keeps its date and Latest marker visible at phone width.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-compliance-runs/mobile.png" alt="Selected compliance run on a phone showing its date and Latest marker before the run name." width="390" height="844" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and filter">
    **Search** matches the title, company, device, location, answer text or run name. **Filter by answer** narrows the answers shown. **Filter by Planner association** offers **All**, **Unassociated** and **Linked to another Planner item**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each row">
    Read the run name and date, **Assigned To**, **Due Date**, answer or status and flag before selecting. The same check can have several assignments, so the title alone may not identify the right one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select, click Done and Save">
    Your selections stay when you change pages or filters. Click **Next** and **Previous** to move through pages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Unassociated** means the assignment has no saved Planner links. **Linked to another Planner item** means it is linked to a different item. You can still select it for this item. An unsaved choice in the current form does not yet count as a saved link.

To link checks automatically from a template, see [Create Planner items for multiple companies](/user-guides/planner/apply-templates-to-companies).

## Remove a relationship

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Open the Planner item and scroll to **Relationships**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the remove button">
    Click the cross beside the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    The link comes off this item. The goal, finding, device, project, opportunity or ticket itself is not deleted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To undo a removal before saving, close the item and choose **Discard**. Removing a device link can also change managed device-price rows, so review **Costs** first.

Cork Vulnerabilities, Cork Endpoints and Cork Compliance Events show information only and have no remove button.

## Create a PSA opportunity

**Add** links something that already exists. **Create** makes a new opportunity in your PSA.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the details first">
    Make sure the item's title, description, public **Notes**, due date and estimates are correct. They go to the PSA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Create menu">
    Click **Create** in **Relationships** and choose **Halo PSA Opportunity**, **ConnectWise Opportunity** or **Autotask Opportunity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    The opportunity is created in the PSA right away and added to the form. Click **Save** to keep the Planner link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Create** appears only when the company is linked to Halo, ConnectWise or Autotask and the item has no opportunity yet. Closing the Planner form does not undo an opportunity that was already created. If an error leaves the result unclear, check your PSA before you try again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A picker is empty | Check the item's company, clear the search and filters, wait for loading and check you can use that module. |
| A selected row will not deselect | Click **Done**, then use the relationship's remove button in the item. |
| A link disappeared after you closed the item | You closed without saving. **Done** alone does not save. |
| A new relationship has no link to click | Some provider records have no usable link here. Reopen after saving and look in the provider. |
| You cannot change the company | Remove the item's linked records first. Planner blocks the change while they exist. |
| **Create** is missing | The company needs a Halo, ConnectWise or Autotask link, and the item can have no opportunity yet. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I link an existing record to planned work?">
    Open the item, choose **Relationships > Add**, pick the type, select records, click **Done**, then **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Done save the relationships?">
    No. The item's **Save** button does.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I select several records at once?">
    Yes. The footer shows the selected count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I link checks from the latest compliance run?">
    Open **Compliance run** and choose the first run marked **Latest**. Confirm its name and date in the table, select the checks, click **Done** and save the item. The picker starts on **All runs**, so older assignments are still there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I select a compliance assignment that is linked elsewhere?">
    Yes. **Linked to another Planner item** only describes the assignment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does linking a compliance check create or fix it?">
    No. It links an existing assignment. Answers and fixes happen in Compliance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does removing a relationship delete the original record?">
    No. Only this item's link is removed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can linking a device change the planned costs?">
    Yes. Linked device standard prices can add or remove managed cost rows. Check **Costs** before you save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Discard undo a PSA opportunity I created?">
    No. Creation finishes separately in the PSA.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
