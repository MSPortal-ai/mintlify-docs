Planner shows proposed work on a timeline, grouped by due date. Each item carries estimates (hours, costs, probability) and links to supporting records such as goals, devices, tickets and PSA opportunities. MSP staff plan and manage the work. Company users read the work their MSP shares with them.

## Before you start

- Planner appears in the sidebar when it is turned on for your MSP and you have Planner read access.
- Company users see only items that their MSP has made visible to company users, within their company access.
- MSP staff permissions control what you can do:

| Permission | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Read Planner** | Open the timeline, List View, Analytics and item details. |
| **Write Planner** | Add and edit items, drag cards to a new period, archive and restore items, update several items at once, upload files. |
| **Manage Planner** | Everything in Write, plus delete items and files, manage templates, save an item as a template, import PSA opportunities and use Planner AI. |

## Timeline views

Cards appear in the column that contains their **due date**. Items without a due date are in **Unscheduled**. If expected work is missing, check the year, the company selector and your filters.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the timeline">
    Click **Planner** in the sidebar, or choose **Planner Menu > Timeline View**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a time scale">
    Choose **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Yearly** in the toolbar. The default is Quarterly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move between years">
    Use the year arrows in the toolbar. Click **Today** to return to the current period. **Today** does not change any item's date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Monthly view

Monthly shows one column for each month, January to December, for the selected year. It is not a day-by-day calendar.

## View density

**View density** changes how much detail each card shows. Hidden detail is still stored on the item.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the density menu">
    Click the **View density** button in the toolbar.

    <Frame caption="The View density menu offers Comfortable, Compact and Dense.">
      <img src="/images/planner/planner-density-dropdown.png" alt="View density menu on the Planner toolbar." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a level">
    **Comfortable** shows the most detail, including linked records. **Compact** hides the linked-record lists. **Dense** also hides the status and priority badges.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create Planner items

MSP staff with **Write Planner** or **Manage Planner** can add items from the timeline or from List View.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Item">
    On the timeline, click **Add Item** in the toolbar or inside a period column. In List View, click **Add Item** in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the item and click Save">
    Enter the title, company, due date, status, type, estimates and notes, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New items start hidden from company users. Clear **Hide from company users** before you save when the item is ready to share. [Create and edit Planner items](/user-guides/planner/create-and-edit-items) explains every field. [Update several Planner items](/user-guides/planner/update-multiple-items) covers bulk changes.

## Importing PSA Opportunities

MSP staff with **Manage Planner** and a connected **Halo**, **ConnectWise** or **Autotask** integration can turn synchronized PSA opportunities into Planner items.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Import">
    Click **Import** in the Planner page header. The button appears only when you have **Manage Planner** and one of those PSAs is connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select opportunities">
    Search by summary or ID, check the due date range, and tick the opportunities to bring in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map the statuses">
    Choose the Planner status for **Open**, **Won** and **Lost** opportunities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Import N opportunities">
    Each selected opportunity becomes a Planner item. Imported items are visible to company users by default, so review them afterward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

[Import PSA opportunities](/user-guides/planner/import-opportunities) covers every filter and mapping. [Understand PSA-linked Planner work](/user-guides/planner/understand-psa-linked-items) explains what company users see.

### Auto-import on PSA sync

Your PSA integration can create Planner items automatically and refresh linked items on every sync.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the PSA integration">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations** and open your PSA (Halo, ConnectWise or Autotask).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Opportunity Settings">
    Find the **Auto-import to Planner** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on auto-import">
    Turn on **Auto-import opportunities into the Planner**. Set **Minimum opportunity value** to skip small deals. Select **Opportunity types to auto-import** to limit the types. If you select none, all types are eligible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the fields to refresh">
    Under **Linked planner item refresh**, tick the Planner fields each sync updates. By default the sync updates Title, Description, Due date and Cost estimate. You can also refresh Public notes, Start date, Estimated hours, Probability, Status and Planner type. A field changes only when the PSA supplies a value for it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turning off **Auto-import opportunities into the Planner** stops new items from being created. Linked items keep refreshing the fields you ticked.

## Edit a Planner item

Click an item's title on the timeline or in List View to open it. Change fields, then click **Save**. Notes and fields save with **Save**. File uploads and deletions happen immediately. See [Read a Planner item](/user-guides/planner/read-item-details) and [Read and manage Planner files](/user-guides/planner/manage-files).

**Private Notes (Internal Only)** are for MSP staff. Company users never see them.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Planner is missing from the sidebar | Ask your MSP to turn on Planner and give you Planner read access. |
| An item is missing from the timeline | Check the year, the company selector, the search box and the **Filters** drawer. Open **Unscheduled** for items with no due date. |
| A company user cannot see an item | Open the item as MSP staff and check **Hide from company users**. |
| **Import** is missing | You need **Manage Planner** and a connected Halo, ConnectWise or Autotask integration. |
| **Templates** is missing from Planner Menu | You need **Manage Planner**. |
| The Planner AI button is missing | You need **Manage Planner** and AI turned on for your MSP. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a Planner item mean the work is approved or booked?">
    No. A Planner item is a plan with estimates. It is not a quote approval, a calendar booking or proof that the work is finished.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which date decides where a card appears?">
    The due date. A card sits in the column for the month, quarter or year that contains its due date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I drag a card to change its due date?">
    Yes, with Write Planner or Manage Planner. Drag the card to another column. Its due date becomes the first day of that period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see Private Notes?">
    MSP staff only. Company users do not see them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does saving an item create a PSA opportunity?">
    No. Only **Relationships > Create** creates an opportunity, and only when you choose it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
