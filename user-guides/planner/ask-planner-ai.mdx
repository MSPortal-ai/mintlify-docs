Planner AI answers questions about your Planner items, such as what is overdue or how work splits by status. This guide is for MSP staff. An answer does not save an item, approve work or book a technician.

## Before you start

- You need **Manage Planner**, and AI must be turned on for your MSP. Without both, the **Planner AI** button does not appear. Company users read the work shared with them in [Read a Planner item](/user-guides/planner/read-item-details).
- Planner AI follows your company selector. Pick the company first when you want an answer about one company.
- Planner AI can also propose changes, such as new items or date shifts. Those appear as cards you must apply. See [Draft or change Planner items with AI](/user-guides/planner/draft-item-with-ai).

## Ask a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a Planner page">
    Open the timeline, List View, Templates or Analytics. The assistant is available on all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the companies">
    Use the company selector at the top of the portal. With no company selected, the answer can cover the companies your account can access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Planner AI">
    Click the floating **Planner AI** button on the right edge of the page. The assistant opens in a side panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a starter or type your own question">
    Under **Try one of these**, choose **Find overdue items**, **Status breakdown** or **High-value work**, or type your own question. A starter fills the message box. It does not send.

    <Frame caption="Choose Find overdue items to fill the message box. Review it, then send.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-ai-analysis-prompt.png" alt="Planner AI Find overdue items shortcut." width="294" height="64" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the message">
    Press **Enter** to send. Press **Shift+Enter** for a new line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the answer">
    Read the companies, dates, statuses and totals in the answer before you use its figures.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Say the dates, statuses and archived items you need in the message. A timeline year, a table filter or a selected row does not carry into the question. When item titles are similar, include the company name.

Changing the company selection starts a fresh conversation. Click **Clear conversation** to start over at any time.

## Questions to try

Replace the dates and companies with your own.

| To do this | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| Find late work | "Which active Planner items are overdue? Give the full overdue count and a short list of examples." |
| Review statuses | "How many active items are in each status? Use the complete matched results, not just the displayed examples." |
| Review a due period | "What is due from 1 October through 31 December 2026 for the selected company?" |
| Find recently added work | "Which items were created from 1 September through 6 September 2026?" |
| Review business value | "Show High business value items. Tell me if the returned list is incomplete." |
| Review effort | "How many estimated hours are planned in this due-date period, broken down by company?" |
| Review costs | "Show planned costs by company, with one-time, monthly and annual amounts separately. Do not combine currencies." |
| Inspect an item | "For the selected company's server refresh item, summarize its status, due date, estimated hours and notes." |
| Include past work | "Include archived items when reviewing work created in this period." |

A request to move a status, shift a date or create work is an edit request. It produces a proposal card. Nothing changes until you click **Apply**.

## Know what the answer covers

### Company scope

The selected companies are the starting scope. Naming a company in your message does not change the company selector. Select it first when you need a complete answer for that company.

### Archived items

Archived items are left out unless you ask for them. This differs from the **Archived** button in List View, which shows archived work only. Say whether you need active work, archived work or both.

### Due dates and creation dates

Use **due** for the planned completion period. Use **created** or **added** for when items entered Planner. A due-date range leaves out items with no due date, so check **Unscheduled** on the timeline for those.

Overdue means the due date is before today and the status is not **Completed** or **Cancelled**. **On Hold** and **Pending Approval** items can be overdue. An item due today is not yet overdue. If another view differs, compare its date and status filters.

For financial-year questions, give an explicit start and end date. Companies can have different year-ends.

### Examples and complete results

A list of items is capped at 50 by default and can reach 200, so even a long list may not show every match. Counts and totals cover all matching items, beyond the examples. Ask for the full matched count and whether the list is incomplete. Do not count the examples yourself.

### Estimates and costs

Hours are planning estimates, not booked capacity or time worked. Costs are estimates, not invoices. Keep **one-time**, **monthly** and **annual** amounts apart and check each company's currency. Open the item to check its cost rows, files and linked records, because a short summary may not include every attachment or linked ticket. See [Plan costs and hours](/user-guides/planner/plan-costs-and-hours) and [Read and export Planner Analytics](/user-guides/planner/read-analytics).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **Planner AI** button is missing | You need Manage Planner and AI turned on for your MSP. Ask your administrator. |
| A starter did not send | Starters fill the message box. Review the text and press **Enter**. |
| The answer is about the wrong company | Select the company, then ask again. |
| AI and the table show different counts | Compare the company selection, archived items, statuses, dates and whether the list was complete. |
| An item is missing from the answer | Check its company, archived state and due date. Narrow the question or open it in List View. |
| Totals mix cost types or currencies | Ask for a breakdown by cost type and currency. |
| The assistant shows an error | Treat it as no answer, not as zero results. Try again, or use the normal Planner views. |
| You only wanted an answer but got a proposal card | Click **Discard** and ask again as a read-only question. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I ask AI about planned work?">
    Click the floating **Planner AI** button on any Planner page. You need Manage Planner and AI turned on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does asking a question change any Planner item?">
    No. Only an applied proposal card changes an item.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do the starters send immediately?">
    No. They fill the message box.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Planner AI use my table or Analytics filters?">
    No. State the statuses, dates and archived items in the question.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are archived items included?">
    Not unless you ask for them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does overdue mean here?">
    A due date before today and a status other than Completed or Cancelled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a due-date question miss an item?">
    The item has no due date. Check **Unscheduled** or List View.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are fewer items listed than the total?">
    The list is capped. Ask for the full matched count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are estimated hours a technician's booked workload?">
    No. They are planning estimates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an error mean there is no planned work?">
    No. Try again or use the normal Planner views.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
