Add a Planner item, fill in its plan, decide whether company users can see it, and save it. This guide is for MSP staff. Company users start with [Read a Planner item](/user-guides/planner/read-item-details).

## Before you start

- You need **Write Planner** or **Manage Planner**.
- Pick the company in the company selector first, or choose the company inside the item when you are in Global View.
- If you want to reuse a template, create it first. See [Create and manage Planner templates](/user-guides/planner/manage-templates).

## Create an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Item">
    In List View, click **Add Item** in the page header. On the timeline, click **Add Item** in the toolbar, or inside a period column to start with that period's first day as the due date. Opening the form does not create a record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the item">
    Click the title or the pencil beside it and type a clear title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the company">
    In Global View, pick the **Company**. Every item needs a company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule and classification">
    Set **Due Date**, **Status**, **Type** and **Business Value**. A new item starts with today's due date (or the period's first day), **Not Started** and **Medium**. **Type** starts with your MSP's default type, so check it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign an owner (optional)">
    Choose an **Assignee**. Members of the company's team are listed first, then the rest of your staff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter estimates">
    Fill in **Est. Hours**, the **Qty** and **Costs** rows and **Probability of Success**. See [Plan costs and hours](/user-guides/planner/plan-costs-and-hours).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the description and notes">
    Add a **Description** and **Notes**. Put MSP-only information in **Private Notes (Internal Only)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete custom fields">
    Click **Custom Fields** to expand it and fill in any required fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide who can see it">
    At the bottom of the dialog, clear **Hide from company users** if company users should see the item. See the next section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Wait for the dialog to close, then find the item and reopen it to confirm the saved plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make work visible to company users

The **Hide from company users** checkbox sits at the bottom of the dialog for MSP staff.

- **Checked** hides the item from company users. New items start checked.
- **Unchecked** lets company users with Planner access and company scope see the item after you save.

<Frame caption="Checked hides the item from company users. Save applies the change.">
  <img src="/images/product/planner-company-visibility-control.png" alt="Checked Hide from company users checkbox in the MSP Planner editor." width="368" height="42" />
</Frame>

The checkbox changes the form until you click **Save**. Making an item visible does not reveal Private Notes. All MSP staff with access can read Private Notes, not only their author, so review the public **Notes** before you share an item. On the timeline, hidden items show a small crossed-eye icon.

## Mention a teammate in notes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type @">
    In **Notes** or **Private Notes (Internal Only)**, type `@`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a person">
    Choose the staff member from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    New mentions notify the person after you save. The notification opens the item. Only MSP staff can send mentions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add notes with a template or AI

- Click **Add Template** above **Notes** or **Private Notes** to insert a saved text template.
- Click **AI Draft** above **Notes** to turn your **Private Notes** into client-facing text. **AI Draft** appears when AI is on for your MSP, and it is available only after you type something in **Private Notes**. The result is added to the end of **Notes**. Read and edit it before you save.

## Edit an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Click the item's title in List View or on the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the fields">
    Update what you need. Click the title or its pencil to rename the item. Pressing Enter in the title box finishes the rename but does not save the item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Wait for the dialog to close.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changing **Status** is separate from changing **Business Value** or **Probability of Success**. Planner statuses do not replace the approval steps in Quotes or Budgets.

An item created from Standards shows a **Standards** badge and has a locked title. Change the standard itself instead. See [Understand Standards work in Planner](/user-guides/planner/understand-standards-items).

To move an item to another company, remove its linked records first. Planner blocks the change while linked records exist.

## Close without saving

<Steps>
  <Step title="Close the dialog">
    Click the close button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to do with your changes">
    If you changed anything, Planner asks "Discard your unsaved changes to" followed by the item title. Choose **Keep editing** to return to the form, or **Discard** to close without saving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This covers unsaved fields only. It does not undo a file upload or a new PSA opportunity you already created.

## Add files and links

- Save a new item first, then reopen it and expand **Uploads**. Uploads and deletions happen immediately and do not wait for **Save**. See [Read and manage Planner files](/user-guides/planner/manage-files).
- Use **Relationships > Add** to link existing records. See [Link supporting records to Planner items](/user-guides/planner/link-supporting-records).
- **Relationships > Create** creates a PSA opportunity right away. Use it only when you mean to.
- To fill an item from a template, see [Apply a template to a Planner item](/user-guides/planner/apply-template-to-item).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Save** is grayed out | You need Write Planner. Costs and custom fields must also finish loading. Click **Retry** if a retry button shows. |
| "Please select a company before saving." | Choose a **Company** in Global View. |
| "Please fill in required custom fields" | Expand **Custom Fields** and complete the listed fields. |
| An error appears after **Save** | Keep the dialog open and read the message. Check whether the item already exists before you create it again. |
| The item saved but custom fields did not | Reopen the item and check **Custom Fields**. The item can save before its custom fields. |
| The item is missing after saving | Check the company selector, your filters, **Archived** and the visibility checkbox. |
| Company users cannot see the item | Clear **Hide from company users**, save, and check the user's Planner access and company scope. |
| **Uploads** is missing | Save the new item, then reopen it. |
| The title cannot be edited | The item comes from Standards, or you have read access only. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can MSP staff create a Planner item?">
    Click **Add Item** in List View or on the timeline. You need Write Planner or Manage Planner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening Add Item create a record?">
    No. The item exists only after you click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my new item hidden from company users?">
    New items start with **Hide from company users** checked. Clear it and click **Save** when the item is ready to share.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does clearing Hide from company users reveal Private Notes?">
    No. Private Notes stay MSP-only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I abandon unsaved edits?">
    Close the dialog and choose **Discard**. **Keep editing** returns to the form.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Discard undo an uploaded or deleted file?">
    No. File changes happen immediately and separately from **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Relationships > Create only prepare a link?">
    No. It creates a real opportunity in your PSA. Discarding the Planner form does not undo it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not rename an item?">
    Standards items have a locked title, and read-only users cannot rename items.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
