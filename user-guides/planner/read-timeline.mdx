Use the Planner timeline to see proposed work grouped by due date. This guide is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Planner read access. Company users see only items their MSP has made visible, within their company access.
- Dragging cards and adding items need **Write Planner** or **Manage Planner**.
- An item on the timeline is a plan. It does not mean the work is approved, booked on a technician's calendar or finished.

<Note>Branch preview: month, quarter and year headers use the same height when switching views, with no Current badge. Card lists scroll vertically within their columns. Sideways scrolling moves the timeline across periods; individual columns do not scroll sideways.</Note>

## Find work on the timeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the timeline">
    Click **Planner** in the sidebar. From another Planner page, choose **Planner Menu > Timeline View**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company selector">
    Look at the company selector at the top of the portal. Staff can view several permitted companies together or one company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a time scale">
    Click **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Yearly** in the toolbar. The default is Quarterly.

    <Frame caption="Branch preview: centered quarter labels sit above the item count, hours and budget. Hover or focus a quarter label to see its dates.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-quarterly-centered-desktop-preview.png" width="1280" height="853" alt="Planner timeline in Quarterly view." />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Branch preview: centered quarter headers on a phone.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-quarterly-centered-mobile-preview.png" width="780" height="1688" alt="Planner Quarterly view on a phone with centered headers." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the right period">
    Scroll sideways. Use the year arrows in the toolbar to change the year, and check the selected year. Hover or focus a quarter label to see its month range and year. Click **Today** to jump to the current period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an item">
    Click the item's **title**. The item opens in a dialog. Read-only users can read it but not change it.

    Branch preview: Comfortable titles use smaller text and show up to three lines. Hover over a shortened title to read its full name. Hovering over a linked finding underlines only that link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| View | What the columns mean |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly** | January to December of the selected year. It is not a day-by-day calendar. |
| **Quarterly** | Q1 to Q4 with the month range and year. Scroll to move across years. |
| **Yearly** | One column per year. The year arrows are turned off. Scroll to move between years. |

A card sits in the column that contains its **due date**. It is not a bar that spans a start and end date. The highlighted column is the current period. It is not an overdue or completion marker.

**Today** changes the period you are viewing. It does not change the time scale or any item's date.

The year arrows stop at 2024 and at ten years after the current year.

## Find items with no due date

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Unscheduled">
    Click **Unscheduled** on the right side of the timeline. The panel lists items with no due date and shows how many there are.

    Branch preview: the panel slides over the timeline while the columns keep their width and position. Scroll within the panel to reach the rest of your unscheduled work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the panel when you are done">
    Click the arrow in the panel header to reveal the timeline underneath.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The count follows your current filters, so a filter can hide unscheduled items too. [List View](/user-guides/planner/find-planner-items) is another way to find work.

## Move an item to another period

With **Write Planner** or **Manage Planner**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drag the card">
    Grab the handle on the left of the card and drop it in another column. The item's due date becomes the first day of that period, and a message confirms the move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move several cards at once (optional)">
    Hold **Ctrl** (or **Cmd** on Mac) and click each card's title to select it. Drag one selected card to move them all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove an item's due date (optional)">
    Drag the card onto the **Unscheduled** panel, or open the card's menu and choose **Remove from schedule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use the card menu

Hover over a card and click the menu button to see these options. Which ones appear depends on your permissions.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** or **View** | Opens the item. Read-only users see **View**. |
| **Delete** | Deletes the item. Needs **Manage Planner**. |
| **Save as template** | Creates a template from the item. Needs **Manage Planner**. |
| **Add to Meeting** | Adds the item to a meeting. |
| **Remove from schedule** | Clears the due date. |
| **Copy link** | Copies a link that opens this item for any teammate with access. |

## Change how much detail cards show

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open View density">
    Click the **View density** button in the toolbar.

    <Frame caption="Choose Comfortable, Compact or Dense.">
      <img src="/images/planner/planner-density-dropdown.png" alt="View density menu with Comfortable, Compact and Dense." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a level">
    Pick one of the options in the table below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Density | Card detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Comfortable** | Fullest detail, including linked records. |
| **Compact** | Smaller cards with status and priority badges. Linked-record lists are hidden. |
| **Dense** | Smallest cards. Badges and linked-record lists are hidden. |

Your browser remembers the year, time scale, density and recurring-cost setting. Search text and filter choices are not saved.

## Read the hours and budget figures

Each column header shows three numbers: **items**, **hours** and **budget**. They cover the cards in that column, after your filters. Hours and budget are planning estimates. They are not time entries or invoices.

By default the budget counts costs on items due in that period. To spread recurring costs forward, turn on **Project Recurring Costs** in the **Filters** drawer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Filters">
    Click the **Filters** button (sliders icon) in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Project Recurring Costs">
    Switch on **Project Recurring Costs**. Monthly and annual costs now roll into every later period they apply to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hover over a budget figure">
    The tooltip splits the budget into **Recurring**, **This Period** and **Total**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With projection on:

- Monthly costs add a full month, quarter or year of cost to each period from the due period onward.
- Annual costs add once in each period that contains the due month, from the due period onward.
- One-time costs stay in the period that contains the due date.

Projection uses full periods and does not prorate. A monthly cost of USD 100 due in February adds USD 300 to Q1 and USD 1,200 to that year. Projection adds cost to later columns. It does not add cards or hours there.

When companies use different currencies, hover over the budget figure to see each currency. Planner does not convert between them. The budget figure is an estimate from Planner costs. It is not a saved budget or actual spend.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An item is missing | Check the year, the company selector, the search box and the **Filters** drawer. Then open **Unscheduled**. |
| Completed items are missing | They are hidden by default. Turn on **Show Completed & Cancelled** in **Filters**. |
| The year arrows are grayed out | You are in Yearly view. Scroll sideways, or switch to Monthly or Quarterly. |
| A future column shows cost but no cards | **Project Recurring Costs** is on. Projection adds cost without cards. |
| Status badges or linked records disappeared | You are in Compact or Dense density. Switch to **Comfortable**. |
| You cannot drag cards | You need **Write Planner** or **Manage Planner**. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Monthly show individual days?">
    No. It shows one column for each month, January to December.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Yearly show twelve months?">
    No. Each column is a whole year. Use Monthly for month columns.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Today move my work items?">
    No. It only changes the period you are viewing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which date decides where a card appears?">
    Its due date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a card jump to the first of the month or quarter after I dragged it?">
    Dropping a card sets its due date to the first day of the column you dropped it in. Open the item to set an exact date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are the hours and budget figures actual time and spend?">
    No. They are Planner estimates for the filtered cards.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does recurring projection prorate the first quarter?">
    No. It uses full periods. A monthly cost of USD 100 due in February adds USD 300 to Q1.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I share one item with a teammate?">
    Open the card's menu and choose **Copy link**. The link opens the item for teammates who have access to it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
