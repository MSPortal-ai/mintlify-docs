Archive an item to set it aside, restore it to bring it back, or delete it for good. This guide is for MSP staff. Company users can [read accessible archived work](/user-guides/planner/find-archived-items).

## Before you start

- **Archive** and **Restore** need **Write Planner** or **Manage Planner**.
- **Delete** needs **Manage Planner**.
- Items created from Standards cannot be archived or restored from this menu, and their **Delete** option is turned off.

## Archive an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open List View">
    Choose **Planner Menu > List View**. Check the company selector and filters, and leave **Archived** off to see active items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    Click the menu button at the end of the item's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Archive">
    The item leaves the active list at once. There is no confirmation and no **Save** step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the result">
    Click **Archived** in the page header and find the item there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Archiving keeps the item's status, costs and other details.

## Restore an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Show archived items">
    Click **Archived** in the page header of List View.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    Click the menu button on the item's row.

    <Frame caption="Row menu for an archived item. Restore returns it to the active list. Delete is a separate action.">
      <img src="/images/product/planner-archived-item-actions.png" alt="Archived Planner item menu showing Restore and the separate Delete action." width="330" height="340" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Restore">
    The item leaves the archived list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the result">
    Click **Archived** again to return to the active list and find the item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Restore keeps the saved status. Restoring a Completed item does not restart it. Archive and restore do not change whether company users can see the item.

## Delete one item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the item">
    Find it in List View (active or archived), or on the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Open the item's menu and choose **Delete**. On the timeline, hover over the card first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the confirmation">
    **Delete Planner Item** names the item and says its costs and links are deleted too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete">
    Click **Cancel** to keep the item, or **Delete** to remove it. A short **Undo** button shows for about four seconds. After that the deletion is final.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the result">
    Check that the row is gone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Restore does not bring back a deleted item. Use **Archive** if you want to keep an item for later.

Deleting a Planner item does not delete a linked ticket, project, goal or provider record.

To delete several items, select their rows and use **Actions (N) > Delete Selected**. See [Update several Planner items](/user-guides/planner/update-multiple-items).

## Standards-linked items

Items created from Standards do not offer **Archive** or **Restore** in the row menu, and **Delete** is turned off. **Delete Selected** is missing when your selection includes one. Change the standard in Standards settings instead. See [Understand Standards work in Planner](/user-guides/planner/understand-standards-items).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Archive** is missing from the menu | You need Write Planner, and the item must not come from Standards. |
| **Delete** is missing or grayed out | You need Manage Planner. Standards items cannot be deleted. |
| An archived item does not show | Click **Archived** and check the other column filters. |
| An item vanished after you changed it | A filter may hide it. Check the filters and **Archived**. |
| An action failed | Read the message, reopen the list and try again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I archive a Planner item?">
    In List View, open the item's row menu and choose **Archive**. It applies immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Archive ask for confirmation?">
    No. Check the item first. You can restore it later.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does archiving mark an item Completed?">
    No. Archive and status are separate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I restore an archived item?">
    Click **Archived**, open the item's row menu and choose **Restore**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Restore change client visibility?">
    No. Company access and the item's visibility setting stay the same.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which permission deletes items?">
    Manage Planner. Archive and Restore need Write Planner or Manage Planner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I undo Delete with Restore?">
    No. Only the short **Undo** button right after you delete works. Restore is for archived items.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting a Planner item delete the linked provider ticket or project?">
    No. Manage those records in their own place.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I archive several items at once?">
    No. Archive and Restore are row actions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
