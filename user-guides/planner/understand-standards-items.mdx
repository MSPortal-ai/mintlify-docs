Your MSP can use Standards to track tools and practices such as backup, endpoint protection and acceptable-use policies. Standards can create a Planner item for each company and standard. This guide helps company users and MSP staff read that work. Standards was formerly called Tool Stack.

<Note>
Branch preview: automatic cleanup after a company is excluded from Standards awaits production rollout.
</Note>

When your MSP excludes your company from Standards, the generated Standards tasks are archived and disappear from the active Planner for both staff and company users. Manually created planning work stays visible. Re-inclusion can restore the same Standards tasks with their history, if their standards still apply and the archived tasks have not been changed.

## Before you start

- You need Planner read access. Company users see Standards items only when their MSP shares them, within the company's access.
- A Standards task in Planner does not mean you opened a support request or bought the product.
- Your MSP manages the Standards catalog, deployment choices and sharing.

## Find Standards work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open List View">
    Choose **Planner Menu > List View**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company selector">
    Select the company you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by source">
    Under **Source**, choose **Standards**. You can also search for the title your MSP gave you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Click its title to read the description, dates, status, notes and estimates. Standards items show a **Standards** badge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the item is missing, clear the other filters and ask your MSP to check sharing, company access and whether the item was archived. Older links and screens can still say **Tool Stack**. Current Planner labels say **Standards**.

## Hide optional Standards

Some standards are marked optional. When your items include optional-standard work, the timeline **Filters** drawer shows **Hide Optional Standards**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Filters">
    Click the **Filters** button on the timeline toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Hide Optional Standards">
    Planner hides items created from optional standards. An **Optional standards hidden** chip appears. Click the chip's close button to show them again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the status

A Standards item mirrors a deployment decision in Standards. The statuses line up like this:

| Standards status | Planner status |
| --- | --- |
| **Not Started** | **Not Started** |
| **In Progress** | **In Progress** |
| **Deployed** | **Completed** |
| **N/A** | **Cancelled** |
| **Declined** | **Cancelled** |

**Cancelled** does not tell you whether the standard did not apply (N/A) or was offered and declined. Ask your MSP which one was recorded. **Completed** reflects the recorded deployment. It does not prove current device health.

**On Hold** and **Pending Approval** are Planner-only statuses with no Standards equivalent. The Standards sync does not overwrite an item that your MSP set to one of them. If the Planner item and the deployment record disagree, ask your MSP to check both.

## Read dates and costs

A due date is a planning target, not a booked appointment. Some Standards work has no due date and sits in **Unscheduled**. Ask your MSP to confirm the deployment plan when a date is unclear.

The item can carry a default estimate from the MSP's catalog. Read the amount, currency and recurrence as shown. It is not a quote or a subscription price. For proposal documents use the [Quotes guide](/user-guides/quotes/download-quotes).

The Standards sync keeps each item's title, description, cost estimate and Planner type in step with the catalog. A standard can also apply a Planner template to its open items. If the same detail keeps changing across related tasks, ask your MSP to check the standard.

## Ask your MSP about an item

Send the company, the Planner title, the value you expected and the value you see. Useful questions:

- Is this standard required, optional or one of several alternatives?
- Was it marked N/A, declined or deployed?
- Is the estimate a planning amount or a quoted price?
- Who schedules the work, and what needs approval?
- Should company users see this item?

If you need support instead, use the [Home AI Assistant to open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). A Planner item is not a submitted ticket.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot find Standards work | Set **Source** to **Standards** in List View, check the company selector and ask your MSP about sharing. |
| You cannot rename or delete an item | Standards items have a locked title, and they cannot be archived or deleted from Planner. Ask your MSP to change the standard or, when the company should leave Standards entirely, [exclude the company from Standards](/user-guides/msp-tools/manage-standards-exclusions). |
| Cancelled is unclear | Ask whether it was N/A or Declined. |
| A task changed on its own | The Standards sync refreshed it from the catalog. Ask your MSP. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why did a Standards task appear in Planner?">
    Your MSP syncs Standards into company Planner work. It does not mean you submitted a request or bought the product.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do older links call this Tool Stack?">
    Tool Stack is the former name. Current labels say Standards.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can my MSP see an item that I cannot?">
    The MSP can hide generated work from company users. Ask your MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Cancelled mean we declined the standard?">
    Not always. Both N/A and Declined show as Cancelled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Completed prove every device is protected?">
    No. It reflects recorded deployment. Verify device health separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the due date a confirmed appointment?">
    No. It is a planning target and can be empty.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the cost a monthly subscription or an accepted quote?">
    No. Check the amount and recurrence, and use a real quote document for proposals.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company administrator manage the Standards catalog?">
    No. Your MSP manages it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a Standards Planner item a support ticket?">
    No. Open a ticket through the Home AI Assistant to request support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
