Archived items are planning work your MSP set aside. Use List View to read them. This guide is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Planner read access. Company users see only the archived work their MSP has made visible to them.
- Archived work is not on the timeline or in Analytics. List View is the place to find it.

## Open the archived list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open List View">
    Choose **Planner Menu > List View**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company selector and filters">
    The list follows your company selection and any column filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Archived">
    **Archived** is in the page header. While it is selected, the table shows archived items only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the item">
    Type in the **Filter...** box under **Title**, or use the other column filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Click its title to read the details. Your access decides which fields and actions you see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to active work">
    Click **Archived** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Switching the **Archived** button changes the list at once. There is no **Save** step. It also returns to page 1 and clears any selected rows. Column filters still apply, so check them if an item is missing.

## Understand the states

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Archived | Set aside from the active list. Staff with Write Planner can restore it. |
| Completed or Cancelled | A work status. Changing status does not move an item to Archived. |
| Hidden from company users | Company users cannot see it, even when they click **Archived**. |
| Deleted | Removed from Planner. **Archived** does not recover deleted items. |

Archiving keeps the item and its planning details. It does not mark the work Completed, cancel a provider project or close a linked ticket.

## If you cannot find the item

- Check both the active and archived lists.
- Check the company selector and the column filters.
- Ask your MSP whether the item was deleted, moved outside your access or hidden from company users.

If you need an archived item restored, ask your MSP. Restoring needs Write Planner or Manage Planner.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The archived list is empty | Check the company selector and filters, wait for loading and look for an error message. |
| An error shows above the table | Treat the list as incomplete. Reload the page or ask your MSP. |
| You cannot find a deleted item | Deleted items are gone. Ask your MSP. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find old Planner work?">
    In List View, click **Archived** and filter the **Title** column.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Archived show active and archived work together?">
    No. It shows archived items only. Click it again for active items.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Completed the same as Archived?">
    No. Completed is a status. A completed item can stay in the active list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Archived reveal an item hidden from company users?">
    No. Your company access and the item's visibility still apply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does clicking Archived change any item?">
    No. It only switches the view.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I recover a deleted item?">
    No. Restore works only for archived items.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
