Use this guide to find your alerts, open the record behind them and mark them read or unread. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- Every signed-in user can open Notifications through the bell in the top bar.
- The full page loads your 100 most recent notifications. It is not a full archive.
- Opening a related record still follows your access to that record.

## Find an alert

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the bell">
    Click the **Notifications** bell in the top bar. The panel lists your recent unread alerts. **Clear all** marks them all read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the full page">
    Click **View all notifications**. The **Notifications** tab opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Click **All** or **Unread** at the top. Use the **Type** filter to choose **All types**, **Info**, **Warning**, **Error**, **Success**, **Product Request** or **Change Approval**.

    The table also has filters on the **Notification**, **Source** and **Time** columns. Clear them if an alert seems to be missing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the alert">
    Read the title, source and time. Hover over cut-off text to see the full message. The **Source** column shows where it came from, such as **Automation**, **System**, **Manual** or **Planner mention**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the related record">
    Click the row. If the alert has a related record, the portal opens it. Clicking an unread row also marks it read. An alert with no related record stays on the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mark notifications read or unread

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mark one alert read">
    On an unread alert, click the checkmark labeled **Mark as read**. This does not open the related record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark one alert unread">
    On a read alert, click the dot icon labeled **Mark as unread** to bring it back to your unread list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark everything read">
    Click **Mark all read** in the page header. It marks all your unread notifications read, not only the rows you can see. The button appears only while you have unread alerts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changes save right away. There is no Save button. Read alerts stay under **All** and drop out of **Unread**. Marking an alert read does not delete the ticket, finish the task, fix the error or approve the action behind it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing appears | Click **All** and **All types**, clear column filters, and check for a loading error message. |
| A link will not open | The record may have changed, or your access to it changed. Tell your IT provider the alert title and time. |
| An older alert is missing | The page shows only your 100 most recent. Check the original module for the record, or ask your MSP. |
| An approval still needs attention | Open the **Pending Approvals** tab. Reading its alert does not send a decision. |
| **Mark all read** is missing | You have no unread notifications. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I mark a notification unread again?">
    Yes. Click **Mark as unread** on a read alert.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Mark all read affect only my filtered rows?">
    No. It marks all your unread notifications read, whatever the filters show. To clear just one, use the checkmark on that row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did an alert disappear after I opened it?">
    Opening an unread row marks it read, so it leaves the **Unread** view. Switch to **All** to find it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening an alert always take me somewhere?">
    No. Only alerts with a related record open another page, and your access to that page still applies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send a notification from this page?">
    No. Alerts come from the portal and from automation rules. Ask your MSP about the right way to send a message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does reading an error fix the problem?">
    No. Read status only marks the alert as seen. Look into the related workflow separately.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
