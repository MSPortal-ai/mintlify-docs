Use this guide to review an automation action that waits for approval, such as an email an automation rule wants to send. It is for assigned reviewers, usually MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You must be a tenant admin or an assigned approver for the rule's action. Your MSP assigns reviewers in the automation rule, by person or by role.
- **Approve** and **Reject** show only for tenant admins and assigned approvers. Anyone else sees a read-only view with the message "Only a tenant admin or an assigned approver can approve or reject this request." The row and bell panel show no decision buttons for those requests.
- A request stays open for a limited time. After that it shows **Expired** and cannot be reviewed.

<Warning>
Approving releases the action. An approved email can be sent right away. Check the recipients and the content first. The **Approve** and **Reject** buttons on a table row and in the bell panel submit your decision at once, with no confirmation and no notes.
</Warning>

## Review a pending request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pending Approvals">
    Click the **Notifications** bell in the top bar, then **View all approvals**. Or open **Notifications** and click the **Pending Approvals** tab. Choose **Pending** in the status filter to see only requests that wait for a decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    Click the row. The **Review Pending Approval** dialog shows the **Rule**, the **Type**, when it was **Requested** and when it **Expires**. For an email it also shows the **Recipients** and an **Email Preview**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the content if needed">
    Optional. Click **Edit** above the preview. Change the **Subject** or the **Body**, then click **Preview** to see the result. You cannot change the recipients here. Preview does not save anything, so your edits stay in the dialog until you approve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note">
    Optional. Type in **Review Notes (optional)** to explain your decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Click **Approve**, or **Approve with Changes** if you edited the content. Click **Reject** to stop this request. Wait for the confirmation message. **Cancel** closes the dialog without a decision and discards your edits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To decide faster, click **Approve** or **Reject** on the table row. Use that only when you already know the content is right.

## Check history and delivery status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a status">
    On the **Pending Approvals** tab, open the status filter and choose **All statuses**, **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected**. **Approved** also lists items that show **Sent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a past request">
    Click a row, or click **View**. The dialog opens in read-only mode with its saved review notes. An indicator shows when the content was changed before sending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Waiting for a decision |
| **Approved** | A reviewer approved it. The action runs next |
| **Sent** | The approved action has run. For an email, this does not show that the recipient read it |
| **Rejected** | A reviewer rejected it, so it is not released |
| **Expired** | The review window ended. It can no longer be reviewed |

A request that is already processed cannot be approved again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve** and **Reject** are missing, and the dialog says "Only a tenant admin or an assigned approver can approve or reject this request." | You are not an assigned reviewer. Ask your MSP who the assigned reviewer is. General portal access does not grant approvals. |
| The request expired or was already processed | Check its current status. Another reviewer may have acted. |
| It shows **Approved** but not **Sent** | Approval and sending are separate steps. Ask your MSP to check the automation's execution log before you create another request, so the action is not duplicated. |
| **Approve** is disabled | The edited content may be missing a required field, or another action is still running. |
| You see no requests | Choose **All statuses**, clear column filters and check the **Notifications** tab for the alert. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does opening a request approve it?">
    No. Only **Approve**, **Approve with Changes** and the row **Approve** button submit an approval.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the email recipients?">
    No. The dialog shows the recipients but edits only the subject and body. Ask the MSP to fix the rule if the recipients are wrong.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are my edits saved when I click Preview?">
    No. Preview only switches the view. Edits are sent when you approve. **Cancel** discards them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I undo an approval?">
    No. Approval can release the action at once. Contact your MSP quickly if it was a mistake.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does rejecting a request turn off the automation rule?">
    No. It rejects only this request. The MSP manages the rule in Automation settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a Company administrator approve every request?">
    No. Only tenant admins and assigned approvers can decide. Ask your MSP if you think you should be one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
