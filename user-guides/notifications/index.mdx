Notifications gather the alerts MSPortal sends you and the automation actions waiting for approval. This guide is for company users and MSP staff. Your notifications and the actions you can take depend on your role.

## Before you start

- Every signed-in user has a bell in the top bar. There is no sidebar item for Notifications.
- Reading an alert never approves, rejects or completes the work behind it.
- Approving an automation action needs you to be an assigned reviewer or a primary admin.

## Open Notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the bell">
    Click the **Notifications** bell in the top bar. A badge on the bell shows the combined number of unread alerts and pending approvals. A small panel lists your most recent unread alerts and has an **Approvals** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the full page">
    Click **View all notifications**. On the **Approvals** tab of the panel, the link reads **View all approvals**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a tab">
    The page has two tabs, **Notifications** and **Pending Approvals**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Read and manage notifications" icon="bell" href="/user-guides/notifications/read-notifications">
    Filter alerts, open related records, and mark items read or unread.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review automation approvals" icon="check-to-slot" href="/user-guides/notifications/review-approvals">
    Assigned reviewers inspect content, make a decision, and check its status.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What each tab does

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | Alerts of type Info, Warning, Error, Success, Product Request and Change Approval, with their source and time |
| **Pending Approvals** | Automation actions that wait for a decision, plus recent approved, rejected and sent history |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot find Notifications in the sidebar | Use the bell in the top bar. |
| The badge shows a number but the list looks empty | Check the **Unread** and type filters on the page, then click **All** and **All types**. |
| You cannot approve a request | Ask your MSP who the assigned reviewer is. See [Review automation approvals](/user-guides/notifications/review-approvals). |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can company users manage automation rules here?">
    No. Notifications is for reading alerts and, when you are an assigned reviewer, deciding on a pending action. The MSP sets up automation rules and reviewers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Mark all read approve pending requests?">
    No. It changes only read status. An approval needs its own decision.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Approved mean an email was sent?">
    No. **Approved** records the decision. The row changes to **Sent** when the action has run.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I change product update emails?">
    Go to **Settings > User Settings > Email Notifications**. That preference is separate from portal alerts. See [Manage your profile](/user-guides/account/manage-your-profile).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
