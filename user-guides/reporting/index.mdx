The Reporting module lets you build professional reports by combining data blocks from across MSPortal. Use it for QBR presentations, compliance summaries, budget forecasts and more. This guide is for MSP staff who create and share reports, and for users who read them.

## Before you start

- Click **Reporting** in the main navigation. Which tabs you see depends on your role.
- Reports are company-specific. Pick a company in the company selector before you create one.
- Creating reports needs **Write** or **Manage** access to Reporting. Scheduled, Approvals and Imports need **Manage**.
- Report defaults, [brand packages](/user-guides/reporting/brand-packages) and report types live in **Settings > Reports**, which needs **Manage**. From **Reporting**, open the page actions menu and choose **Page settings**.

| Tab | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| **Reports** | Everyone with Reporting access. Lists the reports for your company scope. |
| **Huntress** | Tenants with the Huntress integration. |
| **Security Awareness** | Tenants with a Phin, BSN or CyberHoot integration. |
| **Templates** | **Write** or **Manage**. |
| **Scheduled**, **Approvals**, **Imports** | **Manage**. |

## Read a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reporting">
    Click **Reporting** in the main navigation. The **Reports** tab opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the report">
    Use the table search and filters, or click a column heading to sort. The table shows **Report Title**, **Company**, **Creator**, **Created**, **Report Period**, **Blocks**, **Type** and **Status** (**Draft**, **Published**, **Generating** or **Failed**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Click the report title, or open the row's **...** menu and choose **View**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export or edit">
    Open the **Export** menu in the report header to download a PDF or print. Users with write access see **Edit Report**. On a phone, the header buttons are under **Actions**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export or print a report

The **Export** menu in the report header offers:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Export PDF** | Downloads a PDF of the report. MSPortal builds the file, so it is the same in every browser. Portrait and landscape pages keep their own orientation in one file, so a wide table such as the **Device Appendix** prints on a landscape page between portrait pages. |
| **Print** | Opens your browser's print dialog, for printing on paper. Choose the printer and options there. |
| **Word (.docx)** | Full reports only. Downloads the written report as a Word document. |

The menu stays disabled until every block has finished loading. Keep the page open while **Export PDF** prepares the file; your browser then downloads it. Some browsers cannot mix portrait and landscape pages in their print dialog and shrink the whole report instead, so use **Export PDF** for a report with landscape pages.

## Choose recipients for a scheduled report

On **Scheduled**, click **New Schedule** and choose a template and target companies. With a dynamic target such as **All active**, choose **One report per company** or **One combined report** under **Report shape**.

For separate company reports, the **Delivery** step offers **Roles and Contact Types**. Each report goes to the matching people for its company, with membership checked when it is sent. Use **Additional recipients (optional)** to select individual recipients.

Switching to **One combined report** clears selected roles and Contact Types and disables that picker. Select individual recipients for the combined report. Switching back to separate company reports enables the group picker again; select the groups you want to use.

The recipient picker offers contacts from the schedule's selected companies, and MSP staff. If you change the target companies, selected contacts from companies no longer in the schedule are removed. Searching keeps the recipients you already selected. For a dynamic target or a combined report, individual recipients must be MSP staff.

MSPortal checks the recipients when you click **Create Schedule** or **Update**, and when you resume a paused schedule. If a recipient belongs to another company, or a selected company has no recipient, an error explains what to fix. Correct the recipients or target companies and save again. A paused schedule with invalid recipients stays paused.

## Create a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the company">
    Choose a company in the company selector at the top of the portal. In **Global View** the builder shows **Select a Company** and asks you to pick one first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Report">
    On the **Reports** tab, click **New Report** and choose **Builder** for a block-by-block report, or **Full Report** for a generated, narrative-style report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the report details">
    In the builder, click **Report Settings**. Set the **Type**, **Description**, **Date Range** and display options. See below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add blocks">
    Search the **Blocks** list on the left and click a block to add it to the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange and configure">
    Drag blocks into place and use each block's gear to configure it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on Mac) or click **Save**. Wait for the status to read **Saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also start from a template on the **Templates** tab.

## Build and edit report blocks with AI

<Note>
  Branch preview: this section and its screenshots show the expanded AI block
  settings on `t3/ai-report-block-fields`, verified in MSPortal Demo. They
  describe pre-release behavior.
</Note>

Open the **Assistant** while you are in the report builder. Describe the blocks you want to add, or name an existing block and the settings you want to change. The assistant can configure the settings supported by that block, including its title, columns, filters, dates and display options. Available blocks and record choices follow your report's company scope, permissions and integrations.

### Create a filtered block

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the block and period">
    Include the block type, records, filters and columns. For example:

    > Add a Planner Table titled Upcoming Projects for Project items with statuses Not Started or Pending Approval, covering the full current and next quarter. Show title, status and type.

    **Current and next quarter** includes both complete calendar quarters. For a request made in October 2026, that is **October 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027**. Check the resolved dates in the proposal. A **Quarterly Roadmap** spanning those quarters uses separate blocks for Q4 2026 and Q1 2027 so each year shows only its requested quarter.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Configuration ready">
    Check the proposed block, title, types, statuses, columns and date range. For the example above, **Types** should contain **Project** and **Statuses** should contain **Not Started** and **Pending Approval**. A title such as “Upcoming Projects” does not itself filter the records.

    Choose **Discard** if the proposal is wrong, then refine your request. A chat response describing a change is still a proposal until you apply it.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply to draft">
    Choose **Apply to draft** after reviewing the settings. When adding blocks to a report that already has content, review **How would you like to apply?**: **Append to existing** keeps the current blocks; **Replace existing** clears them and uses the proposed layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result and save">
    Inspect the block on the canvas and open its gear to check its settings. Click **Save**, wait for **Saved**, then reload to confirm the result. Applied changes are eligible for the builder's normal autosave; sending the prompt alone does not change the report.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Choose records and filters

Ask for selectable records by name, such as a budget, project, goal, planner item, compliance run, Microsoft 365 license, imported document or published custom block. The assistant can look up available choices and ticket, user or device filter values within the report's scope. If a choice cannot be found or loaded, select it in the block's settings and review the result. Check the company, access and source data when an expected choice is missing.

Supported filters vary by block. For example, **Planner Table** supports type and status filters; use **Quarterly Roadmap** when you also need priority filtering. For date-aware blocks, specify whether to follow the report period, use fixed dates or use a rolling period, then check the proposed range.

New image uploads still require the image picker in the block's settings. Use it to upload and select the image, then continue editing the block.

## Set up the report in the builder

Click **Report Settings** under the title to open the panel on the right.

| Section | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| **Report Details** | **Type**, **Company** (in Global View), **Created By** and **Description**. For a parent company report you can also choose how child companies appear: parent only, merged, or grouped by company. |
| **Date Range** | A **Custom Range**, or **Quick Select** presets: Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month, This quarter, Last quarter, Quarter to date, This year, Last year, Year to date and All time. |
| **Display** | **Page** format (**Letter** or **A4**) and **Mode** (**Paged** or **Continuous**). **Show Grid** shows an alignment grid and **Show Cards** gives blocks card styling. |
| **Applied Blocks** | A list of the blocks in the report. Click one to jump to it. |

The title and version number are at the top of the builder. The version is a label you set. It does not give you a restore history. Use the undo and redo buttons for the current session. Report types come from your tenant. Manage them in **Settings > Reports** on the **Report types** tab.

Compliance content works best in **Continuous** mode. The builder warns you and offers **Switch**.

## Brand a full report

A **Full Report** title page and its presentation slides follow a brand package: a named set of colors, fonts, logo, cover image, background pattern and title page style. Create packages by hand or with **Build with AI** in **Settings > Reports > Brand packages**, then pick one under **Appearance & output > Visual theme > Brand package** when you create the report. See [Brand packages](/user-guides/reporting/brand-packages).

## Design a custom block

Use the block builder’s dedicated AI designer to create a reusable block. The builder's **Block and chart types** tab shows each type once. Select the **sparkle button** at the top right of a preview to add that type to your prompt. **Datasets** explains the available data, with sparkle buttons beside each available dataset to add precise sources. Existing prompt text is kept; send the request when it is ready. See [Design custom reporting blocks](/user-guides/reporting/custom-blocks).

## Add and arrange blocks

<Note>
The Blocks panel uses a searchable category dropdown with multiple selections. Custom Blocks appear after the built-in categories.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a block">
    The **Blocks** panel groups blocks by category. Use **Search blocks...** to find a block. Open the category dropdown showing **All blocks**, search for a category and select one or more. Deselect categories or use **Clear filters** to show everything again. **Custom Blocks**, when available, appear at the bottom and can be selected in the dropdown. On a phone, open the Blocks panel using the floating blocks button. Blocks only appear when you have the permission and, for integration blocks, the integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click the block. It lands on the current page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move and resize">
    Hover over the block. Drag the grip to move it and use the size button to change its size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure or remove it">
    Click the gear to edit the block's settings. Click **X** to remove it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage pages">
    Click **+ Add Page** to add a page. Each page's right margin holds its page controls: the trash icon deletes the page, and the up and down arrows move it. See [Reorder pages](#reorder-pages).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Reorder pages

Use the arrows in a page's right margin, below the trash icon and above the orientation button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Move the page">
    Click **Move page up** or **Move page down** (the up and down arrows). Hover over an arrow to see its full label, such as **Move page 3 up**. The page swaps places with its neighbor and the blocks on it move with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and save">
    Check the new order. To reverse a move, click the undo button in the builder header. Save when the order is right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- The up arrow is unavailable on the first page and the down arrow on the last page.
- The arrows appear only when there is another page to swap with.
- Pages that hold one linked block, such as a block split across pages, move together. Their arrows name every page that moves, such as **Move pages 3 to 6 up**. A move swaps the group with the page or group next to it, so linked parts never separate.

## Print every item in a Compliance Report block

The **Compliance Report** block shows summary tiles, a status breakdown and a list of compliance items. A long list does not fit one page, so the block ends with a "+N more items" footer. Split the block to print every item.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the block full size">
    Add **Compliance Report** from the **Compliance** group in the **Blocks** panel. Use the size button to make it at least 3 columns wide and 3 rows tall. Smaller sizes do not list items, so they cannot be split.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Split it">
    Hover over the block and click **Split across pages**, the scissors icon in the block toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the parts">
    The block stays in place with its summary tiles and the items its frame holds. MSPortal adds whole pages titled **Compliance Report (Part 2)**, **Compliance Report (Part 3)** and so on, which continue the item list. Continuation parts list items only and do not repeat the summary. A block with a custom title uses that title for its parts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** and wait for the status to read **Saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The parts form one linked block. Each part shows a link icon with its part number, such as **Linked block, part 2 of 3**. Change a setting on any part and every part updates. Rows move between parts to fit, and MSPortal adds or removes parts as needed. Removing or duplicating a part acts on the whole block. To edit parts separately, open a part's settings and click **Unlink parts**.

Changing a page's orientation after you split does not re-cut the parts. Check the last page before you print.

In **Continuous** mode, every part shows in one scrolling column. **Export PDF** and **Print** in the report's **Export** menu put one part per page, so the PDF holds every item. To move the parts, use the page arrows: linked pages move together. See [Reorder pages](#reorder-pages).

## Order sections in a Full Report

Open **Full Report**, choose the company and content sources, then expand **Advanced options** to find **Report sections**.

1. Keep a built-in section **Automatic**, make it **Required**, or mark it **Excluded**.
2. In **Planned outline**, drag a section by its grip or use the up and down arrows. The cover page stays first. For keyboard dragging, focus the grip, press Space, move with the arrow keys, then press Space again.
3. Click **Add custom section** to create a section for the client's priorities. Enter a **Section title** and **Writing goals**, such as questions to answer or business outcomes to assess, then click **Save**.
4. Use the pencil beside a custom section to revise its title or writing goals. Set it to **Excluded** to leave it out, and **Required** to include it again.
5. Click **Save Template** to reuse the section choices, order, custom titles and writing goals. Editing an existing template uses the same **Save Template** button.

Custom sections use the selected report sources. Writing goals guide the report; they do not supply missing evidence. A section can be omitted when the sources contain nothing relevant. Scheduled reports use the saved template choices too.

## Halo Problems in QBRs

For a company linked to an active Halo integration, **Full Report** offers a **Problems** content source. Choose **Unresolved and period activity**, **Current unresolved problems**, or **Resolved in report period** in its options. **Detailed** includes problem descriptions, status, priority and known-error status when Halo supplies them.

In **Builder**, search for **Problems** in the block library. Small blocks show unresolved and resolution metrics; larger blocks add problem records and a **+N more problems** count when the selected size cannot show every record. Use the shared block settings for its title, header and date subtitle.

**Unresolved problems** and **Problems** describe the current snapshot. **Opened** and **Resolved** follow the report's date range. An old unresolved problem can therefore appear in this quarter's QBR. Generic child tickets do not establish incident impact, so missing incident links are not shown as zero.

The source and block need ticket read access, linked Halo companies and synced Problems. Halo identifies these records through its **ITIL Ticket Type** classification, including custom-named problem ticket types.

## Save, share and export

| Task | How |
| --- | --- |
| Save | Press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on Mac) or click **Save**. Eligible changes also autosave after a short delay. The badge reads **Saving**, **Saved** or **Unsaved**. |
| Close | Click **Close**. If you have unsaved changes the builder asks you to keep editing or discard. |
| Share a link | On the report row, open **... > Share** to copy the report link. The recipient must already have access to the report and its company. |
| Publish | On the row, open **... > Publish**. **Unpublish** reverses it. |
| Duplicate | On the row, open **... > Duplicate**. |
| Make a template | On the row, open **... > Make Template**. |
| Add to a meeting | On the row, open **... > Add to Meeting**. |
| Bundle | Tick several reports and choose **Bundle** from the actions menu. |
| Delete | On the row, open **... > Delete** and confirm. |

Adding content to a new report can create it before you click **Save**, so check the report list. A new template needs one manual save before autosave starts.

### Client Benchmark

Compares a client's health score, patch coverage and backup success with all your active clients. Add it to a report or a dashboard from the **Data** category, or search for **Client Benchmark**. It shows current figures and does not follow the report's date range.

| Metric | What it measures | Data it needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Health score** | The client's latest [Client Health](/user-guides/msp-tools/client-health) score. | Client Health read access and a published scorecard. |
| **Patch coverage** | Patched devices as a share of the devices that report patch status. | Device read access and patch data from your RMM. |
| **Backup success** | Protected items with a current good backup as a share of protected items. | Backup read access and a connected backup integration. |

The block shows only the metrics your role can read. All three figures run from 0 to 100, and higher is better.

| Scope | What the block shows |
| --- | --- |
| One client | Each metric as a bar beside the median and the **Middle half of clients**, with **Ahead of N% of clients**. |
| Several clients, such as no company selected or a parent company | The **Median of all clients** for each metric, and a ranked list of clients for one metric. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the block">
    In the builder, search for **Client Benchmark** and click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the metrics">
    Click the gear. Under **Metrics**, tick the metrics to show. At least one stays on. Changes save as you make them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the ranking">
    When the block covers several clients, pick a metric in **Rank clients by** and choose **Lowest first** or **Highest first** under **Ranking order**. **Lowest first** puts the clients that need attention at the top.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Comparisons need at least 5 clients with data. With fewer, the block shows **Not enough clients to compare**. Patch coverage and backup success rank a client only when it has at least 5 devices or 5 protected items. A smaller client shows **Not ranked: fewer than 5 devices** or **Not ranked: fewer than 5 protected items**.

When a client user views the block in a shared report, it compares their own company with all the companies you support and never names another company.

## Email domain security in reports

This feature is **off by default**. An internal administrator enables **Email domain security checks** in Features for a tenant or a personal pilot. Until enabled, the email-security column, **Check now**, report block and **Domain check** choice are hidden. Turning it off stops new checks and hides stored DNS results without deleting them. Saved report and dashboard blocks also stop rendering cached domain-check results while this feature is off. Reenable it to show the stored results again.

Use the **Email Domain Security** block to show DMARC enforcement, passing and failing domain counts, and individual domain results. No security integration is required. The narrative **Security** source is also available when company domains exist and your role can read security data, even before the first check completes. Existing security integrations still make the source available.

Select **Email domain checks** in the security provider filters to include the stored domain posture. Results describe the latest measurement, not a history over the reporting period. An unknown DKIM result is not evidence of a failed configuration. Request a new measurement from **Documentation > Domains > … > Check now**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot see **Templates** or **Imports** | Those tabs need **Write** or **Manage** access to Reporting. Provider tabs also need the integration. |
| A block does not load or is empty | Check the company has data for the date range, the integration is connected and synced, then reload the page. |
| **Save** reads **Select Company**, or the builder shows **Select a Company** | Choose a company in the company selector, or in **Report Settings** if it offers one. |
| The **Export** menu is disabled | Wait for the blocks to finish loading. A failed chart also keeps export disabled; correct its data or loading error before exporting. |
| **Export PDF** shows **Failed to export PDF** | Reload the report, wait for every block to load and try again. **Print** still works from the same menu. |
| A landscape page prints shrunk or portrait | Use **Export PDF** rather than **Print**. Some browsers' print dialogs cannot mix page orientations. |
| **Last user** shows a dash | Your RMM has not reported a logged-in user for that device. Check the device in the RMM and wait for the next sync. |
| An exported block is missing rows | Check its filters, date range, row limit and page space. Use **Split across pages** for long lists. |
| A new template did not autosave | Save it manually once. Autosave works after that. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a block save a new report?">
    It can. Autosave starts once the report has content and a company. Check the save status and the report list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the version number an undo history?">
    No. It is a label. Use undo and redo for the current session and keep a copy before large changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the date range rewrite AI narrative?">
    No. Data blocks follow the date range. Generated text does not change on its own, so regenerate it and check it matches the period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a shared link give a client access?">
    No. The recipient needs access to the report and its company. A downloaded PDF is a separate copy, so choose who receives it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I check before sharing a PDF?">
    Wait for the blocks to load, confirm the company and period, look at every page for clipping or empty data, and review the narrative and branding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I upload the logo?">
    Your tenant logo comes from your tenant settings. Check the **Company Logo** block and the exported PDF to confirm it appears. A [brand package](/user-guides/reporting/brand-packages) can set its own logo for full report title pages and slides.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
