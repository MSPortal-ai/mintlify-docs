The **Planner Report** is a ready-made MSPortal template. It opens in the report builder with three parts already laid out on landscape pages:

1. A **cover page** with the client's name, the report title, your company name, the date and your logo.
2. The **planner board**: quarter columns of item cards, six columns to a row, with unscheduled items first and completed items last.
3. The **planner table**: one list of items, or a section per client, with category, description, assignee, due date, costs and priority / status.

The board and table use the same layout, type size and item order as the **Export** PDF on [/planner](/user-guides/planner/read-timeline). Unlike that export, the report can carry a cover page, be saved as a template, shared and scheduled.

## Before you start

- MSPortal turns on cover pages and MSPortal templates for each organization. If **MSPortal templates** is missing from the **Templates** tab, contact MSPortal support.
- You need **Read** access to the **Planner** for the board and table to show items, and **Write** access to **Reporting** to create a report.
- Adding the template to your own template list needs **Manage** access to **Reporting**.
- The cover uses your default [brand package](/user-guides/reporting/brand-packages) when you have one. Without one it uses a soft gray background and your reporting logo from [Tenant settings](/user-guides/settings/tenant).
- Select one company in the company selector. The report prints that client's plan.

## Create a planner report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open MSPortal templates">
    In **Reporting**, open the **Templates** tab and choose **MSPortal templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the report">
    On **Planner Report**, choose **Create report**. The builder opens with the cover, board and table. The board and table add as many pages as the client's plan fills.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and export">
    Choose **Save**, then open the report and choose **Print/Export PDF**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also start from **New Report > Full Report**: open **Load from template** and pick **Planner Report** under **MSPortal templates**. It opens in the report builder.

## Keep it as your own template

To change the template once and reuse it, or to schedule it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the template">
    In **Templates > MSPortal templates**, choose **Add to my templates** on **Planner Report**. It appears in your template list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit it">
    Open the template's actions menu and choose **Edit Template**. Change the cover or the planner blocks, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use or schedule it">
    Choose **Generate Report** to create a report from it, or **Schedule** to send it on a schedule. A scheduled report for several clients gives each client only the pages its plan fills.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customize the cover page

Click the cover in the builder and open its settings. Every look option can override the brand package for this cover only.

| Section | Option | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Content** | **Title** | The large title. Leave it empty to use the report's title. |
| | **Subtitle** | A second line under the title, such as "IT Planner". |
| | **Note** | A short line such as a tagline or meeting name. |
| **Details** | **Show on the cover** | Pick any of **Client name**, **Prepared for**, **Your company name**, **Report creator**, **Date**, **Report period**, **Your logo**, **Client logo** and **Confidential notice**. |
| | **Date** | **The day it is viewed or exported**, **A date I choose** (then pick the **Cover date**), or **The report's end date**. |
| | **Date format** | For example, Thursday, July 9, 2026, July 9, 2026, or July 2026. |
| **Look** | **Brand package** | **Default** follows your default package, so the cover changes when you change the default. Pick a package to keep this cover on it, or **No brand package**. |
| | **Layout** | **Follow the brand package** uses the package's title page style, as a Full Report title page does. **Card over background** (a white card centered on the background), **Full background**, **Color band**, **Side panel** or **White page**. |
| | **Alignment** | **Centered** or **Left**. |
| | **Background** | **Brand package colors**, **Soft gray texture**, **Solid color**, **Gradient**, **Image** or **White**. Colors take a hex value or the color picker. |
| | **Image** | Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB. **Shade over the image** keeps the text readable: **Light** for dark text, **Dark** for white text. |
| | **Pattern** | **Follow the brand package**, **Circles**, **Dots**, **Grid**, **Diagonal lines**, **Arcs** or **None**. |
| | **Accent** | The brand package's accent, or a **Custom color** for the rule under the title and the band edge. |

Your logo comes from the brand package when it has one, otherwise from your reporting logo. The client logo comes from the company's logo in MSPortal.

## Add a cover page to any report

The **Cover Page** block works in every builder report. In the builder, open the block list, find **Cover Page** under **Branding**, and drag it onto an empty page. It fills the whole page. Set the page to landscape or portrait from the page's orientation control.

## Match the planner PDF in your own report

The **Planner Board** and **Account Plan** blocks are the board and table from the planner PDF. You can add them to any report. These settings make them print as the export does:

| Block | Setting | For the PDF's look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Both | **Text size** | **Planner PDF size**, on a landscape page. **Compact** fits more into a smaller block. |
| Both | **Descriptions** | **Full**, or **Short (one paragraph)** like the export's short descriptions. |
| Both | **Include unscheduled items** | On, so items without a due date print first. |
| **Planner Board** | **Completed items in their own column** | On, for a final **Completed** column. |
| **Account Plan** | **Sections** | **List, by company (as the planner PDF)**. **One per period** titles a section per quarter, month or year instead. |
| **Account Plan** | **Completed items in their own sections** | On, for **Completed** sections at the end. |

A long board or table continues across pages on its own: each page holds as many whole cards or rows as fit, and a long column carries on at the top of the next page under the same column titles.

A description too long for one page, such as a pasted list of findings, ends with "…" on the board after about 420 characters and in the table after about 1,400. The PDF export prints the rest on the next page instead. Choose **Short (one paragraph)** under **Descriptions** to keep every card brief.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **MSPortal templates** is missing | The feature is not turned on for your organization yet, so contact MSPortal support. With it on, you need **Write** access to **Reporting**, and the Planner Report also needs **Read** access to the **Planner**. |
| **Add to my templates** is missing | Adding templates needs **Manage** access to **Reporting**. **Create report** still works. |
| The board and table are empty | Select a company with planner items. Items with a status of **Cancelled** are left out, as in the export. |
| The cover shows "Client name" | No company is selected in the builder. Pick one in the company selector. A report for a client prints its name. |
| The cover has no logo | Upload a reporting logo in [Tenant settings](/user-guides/settings/tenant) or set a logo on the brand package. |
| A description ends with "…" | It is longer than a page holds. Shorten it on the planner item, or choose **Descriptions: Short (one paragraph)**. |
| The cover does not use my brand colors | Check the default in **Settings > Reports > Brand packages**, or choose a package under **Brand package** on the cover. |
