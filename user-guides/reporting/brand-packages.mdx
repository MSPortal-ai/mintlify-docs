A brand package is a named look for client-facing reports. It sets the colors, fonts, logo, cover image, background pattern and title page style used on a **Full Report** title page and on its presentation slides (cover, section and closing slides, and the tint behind content slides).

Manage brand packages in **Settings > Reports** on the **Brand packages** tab. You can also open the page from **Reporting** through the page actions menu (**Page settings**).

## Before you start

- Managing brand packages needs **Manage** access to Reporting.
- Your organization can keep up to 12 brand packages. Delete one to add another.
- **Build with AI** needs AI turned on for your organization and enough AI credits.
- Brand packages style **Full Reports** and the presentations built from them. Block-based reports from the builder use the **Company Logo**, **Custom Image**, **Heading** and **Text** blocks for branding instead. See [Reporting](/user-guides/reporting/index#content-blocks).

## Create a brand package

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Brand packages tab">
    Go to **Settings > Reports** and choose **Brand packages**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a package">
    Choose **New package**. The editor opens with your tenant brand color filled in. You can also open a card's actions menu and choose **Duplicate** to start from an existing package.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and set the look">
    Enter a **Name**, then set the options below. The **Preview** shows a report title page and presentation slides as you change them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Choose **Save**. Nothing is stored until you save, and **Cancel** discards the draft.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Group | Option | What it controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Colors** | **Primary** | Title page and cover slide background, behind white text. It must be dark enough for white text to read. |
| | **Secondary** | Second color of the title page and slide gradient. |
| | **Accent** | Rules, highlights and chart accents. |
| | **Slide background** | Light tint behind content slides. It must be light enough for dark text to read. |
| **Fonts** | **Heading font**, **Body font** | **App default**, **Modern sans (Helvetica)**, **Humanist sans (Segoe, Trebuchet)**, **Geometric sans (Futura, Avenir)**, **Classic serif (Georgia)**, **Elegant serif (Palatino)** or **Slab serif (Rockwell)**. |
| **Layout** | **Title page style** | **Full color**, **Banner** or **Minimal**. |
| | **Background pattern** | **Circles**, **Dots**, **Grid**, **Diagonal lines** or **None**, drawn over the title page and cover slides. |
| **Images** | **Logo** | Replaces your reporting logo on title pages and slides. PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG, up to 2 MB. Leave it empty to use your reporting logo. |
| | **Cover image** | Shown behind the title page and cover slide, tinted with the primary color. PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 8 MB. |

Enter colors as hex values such as `#0d9488`, or use the color picker.

## Build a brand package with AI

AI can propose a package from your existing brand material, such as a brand guide, a past report or a screenshot of your website.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Build with AI">
    On the **Brand packages** tab, choose **Build with AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your sources">
    Drop in up to four PDF, Word, PNG, JPG or WebP files, 20 MB or smaller each. Leave **Use our logo** on to include your reporting logo. You can build from the logo alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the package">
    Choose **Build package**. AI reads the files and proposes colors, fonts and a layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    The proposal opens in the editor as a new package. Check every option against your brand and the preview, change what you need, then choose **Save**. The card shows **Built with AI**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The uploaded files are deleted once the package is built. If a color from your documents is hard to read, MSPortal adjusts it to a readable shade, so compare the colors with your brand guide before saving.

## Choose the default package

The first package you save becomes the **Default**. New full reports start with the default package selected.

To change it, open another package's actions menu and choose **Make default**. To stop preselecting a package, choose **Remove as default** on the current default. New reports then start with **None (your brand colors)**. These changes save immediately.

## Use a brand package in a full report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a full report">
    In **Reporting**, choose **New Report > Full Report**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Appearance & output">
    In the report options, expand **Appearance & output**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the package">
    Under **Visual theme**, pick a **Brand package**. The default package is already selected. Choose **None (your brand colors)** to use your tenant brand colors and reporting logo instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the report if needed">
    The package sets the report's cover style and accent color. You can still change **Accent color** for this report only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Manage brand packages** under the picker opens **Settings > Reports** for people who manage reporting.

When the report is generated, MSPortal copies the package's look into the report. Editing or deleting the package later does not change reports that already exist, and a locked report keeps the look it was published with. Presentations built from the report use the same look.

## Edit or delete a package

Open a package card's actions menu:

- **Edit** opens the editor. Choose **Save** to keep your changes. Reports created earlier keep their original look.
- **Duplicate** opens a copy named "(copy)" in the editor. It is saved only when you choose **Save**.
- **Delete** asks you to confirm, then removes the package. Reports already created with it keep their look. If it was the default, new reports start with **None (your brand colors)**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **New package** and **Build with AI** are disabled | You have 12 packages. Delete one to add another. |
| **White text is hard to read on this color. Choose a darker one.** | **Primary** and **Secondary** sit behind white text. Pick a darker shade. |
| **Slide text is hard to read on this color. Choose a lighter one.** | **Slide background** sits behind dark text. Pick a lighter tint. |
| **Build package** is disabled | Add at least one file, or turn on **Use our logo**. The logo option needs a reporting or tenant logo in [Tenant settings](/user-guides/settings/tenant). |
| **AI is turned off for your organization.** | Turn AI on in tenant settings, or create the package by hand. |
| **There are not enough AI credits to build a brand package.** | Add AI credits, or create the package by hand. |
| **The files could not be read. Use PDF, Word or image files.** | Try a different export of the document, such as a PDF or a PNG screenshot. |
| An existing report did not pick up package changes | This is expected. Reports keep the look they were created with. Create a new full report to use the updated package. |
| The **Brand package** picker shows only **None (your brand colors)** | No packages exist yet. Someone with **Manage** access to Reporting can create one. |
