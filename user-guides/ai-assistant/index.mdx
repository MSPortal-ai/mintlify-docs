**Audience:** MSP staff. Company users should start with [Use the Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ai-assistant/use-home-assistant).

Use the assistant on **Home** to ask a question, prepare a support request, or find product instructions. Where the shared assistant drawer is available, it can also help from the page you are working on. A page-specific editor may have its own draft and Save controls.

## Everyday use

Read [Use the Home AI Assistant](/msp-guides/ai-assistant/use-home-assistant) for sending a question, shortcuts, product-guide citations, review cards, and recent conversations. Ticket requests start on Home; **Tickets > New Ticket** is the manual alternative.

The assistant works within the signed-in user's access and selected company scope. Ask it to identify the target record and inspect the proposed values before applying a change. A conversational “yes” does not apply a central-assistant review card, and an editor draft can still require a separate Save.

## Guide questions versus record questions

Ask “How do Company users open a ticket? Cite the product guide” for public product instructions. Ask about a particular ticket only when you intend the assistant to read that ticket. Guides do not grant permissions or prove that a provider-specific control is available to every user.

## External AI tools

Use [MCP Server](/user-guides/mcp-server/index) for external AI clients and [MCP Tools Reference](/user-guides/mcp-server/tools-reference) for available capabilities. Their connection and approval flows are separate from the portal's Home controls.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do Company administrators manage portal users through AI?">
    No. The MSP manages Company user roles and access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a proposal mean the action succeeded?">
    No. Review and apply the card, then check the result. Follow any page editor's separate Save controls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where should staff learn the Home controls?">
    Use the MSP Home assistant guide linked above. It explains sending questions, reviewing changes, and recent conversations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
