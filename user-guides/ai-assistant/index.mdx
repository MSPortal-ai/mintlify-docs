The AI Assistant answers questions in plain language, finds records, prepares support tickets and proposes changes for you to review. This overview is for MSP staff. Company users should start with [Use the Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ai-assistant/use-home-assistant).

## Before you start

- AI must be turned on for your portal. If you see no assistant, ask your MSP administrator.
- The assistant works with your own access and the company you have selected. It cannot show or change anything your role cannot.
- Keep passwords, recovery codes and API keys out of the chat.

## Ask the assistant a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assistant">
    On **Home**, when your portal uses the AI Home, the assistant is the message box in the middle of the page. On other pages, click the sparkle tab on the right edge of the screen, labeled **Open assistant**. The **Assistant** drawer opens. **Open on home** in its header switches to the full Home view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the company">
    Check the company selector first. The assistant reads and proposes changes for the companies you have selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your question and send it">
    Describe what you need and press **Enter**, or click **Send message**. Press **Shift+Enter** for a new line. A shortcut such as **Find my Account Manager** sends its question at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before anything changes">
    When the assistant proposes a change, it shows a **Review change** card. Click **Apply change** only if the target record and values are right. Click **Discard** if not. Typing "yes" in the chat does not apply a card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the full walkthrough, including shortcuts, tickets and recent conversations, read [Use the Home AI Assistant](/msp-guides/ai-assistant/use-home-assistant). Ticket requests start on Home. **Tickets > New Ticket** is the manual way.

On a page with its own editor, such as the survey designer, the assistant prepares a draft. Save it from the page when you are happy with it.

## Ask about the product or about a record

Ask "How do Company users open a ticket? Cite the product guide" for product instructions. Ask about one ticket only when you want the assistant to read that ticket. A guide describes a feature. It does not give you access to it.

<Note>
Branch preview: portal how-to and configuration questions are kept separate from support-ticket requests. The web assistant and Chrome extension consult the product guides and answer these questions in the conversation. You can still explicitly ask the extension to open a support ticket, subject to your permissions.
</Note>

## Connect an external AI tool

To use Claude, Cursor, Codex or another AI client with MSPortal, follow [MCP Server](/user-guides/mcp-server/index). See the [MCP Tools Reference](/user-guides/mcp-server/tools-reference) for what it can do. That connection is separate from the Home assistant.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No assistant is visible | AI may be off for your portal. Ask your MSP administrator. |
| The assistant shows the wrong company's data | Change the company selector, then start a **New conversation**. |
| A review card says it expired | Ask the assistant to prepare the change again. |
| A page draft is ready but not saved | Save it with the page's own Save button. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do Company administrators manage portal users through AI?">
    No. The MSP manages Company user roles and access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a proposal mean the action succeeded?">
    No. Click **Apply change**, then check the result. On a page editor, use the page's Save button too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I learn the Home controls?">
    In [Use the Home AI Assistant](/msp-guides/ai-assistant/use-home-assistant).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
