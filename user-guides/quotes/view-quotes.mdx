Use **Quotes** in the sidebar to find the **Quoter**, **QuoteWerks**, and **Salesbuildr** quotes your MSP shares with you. This page is for company users and MSP staff with quote access.

## Before you start

- You need Quotes read access (the **Quotes** resource with **Read** access in the role editor).
- Your MSP must have a quoting provider connected, and your company must be mapped.
- Your role can limit which quotes you see. See [Control company quote access](/user-guides/quotes/manage-company-access).
- Quotes show after the sync. Syncs run every four hours.

## Find a quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Quotes">
    Click **Quotes** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the company">
    Use the company selector in the top bar. Changing the company resets the filters and the page. Company users see only their own company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Type in the filter boxes under the **Number**, **Name**, or **Contact** headings. Open the **Stage** filter to pick a stage. Open the **Created** filter to pick a range such as **This Month** or **Last Quarter**. The filters work together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show more columns">
    Click **Columns**, turn on **Owner** or **Monthly**, and click **Save Changes**. **One-Time** shows only when some quote has a one-time amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the quote">
    Confirm the **Number**, **Name**, **Company**, **Stage**, and **Contact**. The contact is the quote's billing contact. The owner is a different person.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list shows the newest quotes first, 20 per page. Use the page controls under the table to see more. You can sort by **Number**, **Name**, **Company**, **Stage**, **Monthly**, **One-Time**, or **Created**. A stage is the status synced from the provider.

## Open the quote details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the quote">
    Use the steps above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the details">
    Click the **Number**, or click anywhere else on the row. The quote detail page opens in MSPortal. See [Read a quote's details](/user-guides/quotes/read-quote-details).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **...** menu and the **Name** cell do not open the detail page. The **Name** is plain text.

## Open the provider page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the quote">
    Use the steps above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Click the **...** menu on the row and choose **Open in Quoter**, **Open in QuoteWerks**, or **Open in Salesbuildr**. The page opens in a new tab. On the detail page, the provider link is **Open quote**.

    <img src="/images/product/quote-reader-actions.png" alt="Quote row menu showing Open in QuoteWerks and Add to Meeting" width="374" height="148" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the quote">
    Read the terms on the provider page. Any acceptance, signature, or payment step happens there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the menu has no **Open in** action, the quote has no synced provider link. Ask your MSP for the link. The **Number** and the row still open the detail page.

For documents and PDFs, see [Download quote documents](/user-guides/quotes/download-quotes).

## Add a quote to a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    Click the **...** menu on the quote row and choose **Add to Meeting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the meeting">
    In the **Add to Meeting Agenda** dialog, open **Select Meeting** and pick the meeting. Check its company and date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add to Agenda">
    Click **Add to Agenda**. A message reads **Added to meeting agenda**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This adds the quote to the agenda. It does not create a meeting and does not approve the quote. If the dialog says no upcoming meetings were found, create a meeting first.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Quotes** is missing | Your role lacks Quotes read access, or no quoting provider is connected. Ask your MSP. |
| A quote is missing | Click **Clear Filters** and check the company selector. Then ask your MSP to check your role access, the company mapping, and the sync. |
| A colleague sees different quotes | Roles can limit users to **Mine only** or **My site**, or to certain stages. Ask your MSP. |
| The row has no **Open in** action | The quote has no synced provider link. You can still read it on the detail page. Ask your MSP for the link. |
| The provider link fails | MSPortal sign-in does not give you access to the provider page. Ask your MSP for the quote. |
| The stage looks old | The stage is the last synced value. Check the provider page and wait for the next sync. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I click the quote name to open details?">
    No. Click the **Number** or the row. The **Name** is plain text. Use **Open in** on the **...** menu for the provider page. See [Read a quote's details](/user-guides/quotes/read-quote-details) for the detail page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Mine only hide a quote I own?">
    **Mine only** matches the quote's billing contact to your full name. It does not match the quote owner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does All quotes show every customer's quotes?">
    No. Your company access still applies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I approve a quote?">
    On the provider page. MSPortal has no approve action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export every matching quote to CSV?">
    Yes. **Export CSV** covers every quote that matches your filters, not only the page you are viewing. See [Download quote documents](/user-guides/quotes/download-quotes).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
