Use the **Quotes** row in a company role to control which quotes the users with that role can see. This page is for MSP staff. Company administrators ask their MSP to change quote access.

## Before you start

- You need permission to edit roles in **Settings > Users & Roles**.
- Editing a role changes access for every user who has it. If only one person needs a different policy, duplicate the role and [assign the new role to that user](/user-guides/settings/change-company-user-permissions).
- The quote controls appear only on company roles, and only when the role has **Read** or **Manage** access to **Quotes**.
- Access never goes beyond the user's company access. These settings only narrow it.

## Set quote visibility and statuses

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company role">
    Go to **Settings > Users & Roles > Roles**. Under **Company Roles**, find the role and open its editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow reading Quotes">
    In the permission matrix, find **Quotes** and turn on **Read**. The **Visibility** and **Statuses** controls appear under the **Quotes** row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Visibility">
    Open **Visibility** and pick **All quotes**, **Mine only**, or **My site**. See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Statuses">
    Leave **Statuses** empty to show **All statuses**, or pick the stages this role may see. A restricted role does not see a quote whose stage is blank or not in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and check">
    Review the rest of the role, then click **Update Role**. Reopen the role to confirm the saved values. Then check the Quotes list as a user with that role. If the user still sees old access, ask them to sign out and back in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Visibility | Which quotes the user can see |
| --- | --- |
| **All quotes** | Every quote in the user's company access, within the allowed statuses. |
| **Mine only** | Quotes where the billing contact's full name matches the user's full name. This is not the quote owner. |
| **My site** | Quotes for the user, and for other portal users at the same company and location. Without a company or location, it works like **Mine only**. |

The same rules apply to the quote detail page and the Salesbuildr PDF.

## Find out why a quote is missing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the user's role">
    Open the user and confirm which company role they have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Quotes access">
    Open the role and confirm **Quotes** has **Read** access, and see which **Visibility** and **Statuses** are set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the names">
    For **Mine only** and **My site**, compare the user's full name with the quote's billing contact name. A spelling difference stops the match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the stage">
    If **Statuses** is set, check that the quote's stage is in the list. Use the stage names the provider uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company">
    Check that the quote is mapped to the user's company. See [Troubleshoot missing Quoter quotes and company links](/user-guides/quotes/troubleshoot-quoter-quotes).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Visibility** and **Statuses** are missing | Open a company role, and turn on **Read** for **Quotes**. |
| The statuses list is empty | You see **No quote statuses found** until quotes have synced from a provider. |
| A user still sees old quotes | Ask them to sign out and back in. |
| A user lost every quote after you picked statuses | None of their quotes has a stage in the list. Check the stage names. |
| Saving shows an error | Reopen the role and check each setting before you try again. Part of a role update may already have saved. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Mine only use the quote owner?">
    No. It compares the billing contact's full name with the user's full name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the user has no location?">
    **My site** then works like **Mine only**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does All quotes show other companies' quotes?">
    No. Company access still applies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does clearing Statuses do?">
    It allows every status. An empty list does not hide all quotes. To remove quote access, turn off **Read** for **Quotes**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing Visibility save right away?">
    No. Click **Update Role** to save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change one person without changing everyone on the role?">
    Give that person a different role, or create a new one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
