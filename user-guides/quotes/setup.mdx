Connect **Quoter** through **Settings > Integrations** to bring its quotes and line items into MSPortal. The wizard has two steps: **Connect** and **Complete**. Company association happens during quote synchronization; there is no manual **Map Companies** step in this wizard.

**Audience:** MSP staff with permission to manage integrations. Company users ask their MSP to connect Quoter, repair synchronization, or change quote access.

## Before you start

Have your Quoter **Client ID**, **Client Secret**, and **Quoter URL Name** ready. Use credentials for the intended Quoter account. The app points to **Account > API Keys** in Quoter; ask your Quoter administrator if you cannot obtain the credentials.

For an address such as `https://example.quoter.com`, the URL name is just `example`. Do not paste the complete URL, `.quoter.com`, or a quote's path into this field. The form accepts letters, numbers, and hyphens and saves the name in lowercase.

You need permission to manage integrations (**manage_settings_integrations**). Reading Quotes does not let you save credentials, change the URL name, sync, or delete the connection.

## Connect the account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Quoter">
    Open **Settings > Integrations** and search for **Quoter**. Under **Add integration**, select **Connect**. To manage a connection that already exists, open that connection's **Manage** or **Configure** action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the three fields">
    In **Connect**, enter **Client ID**, **Client Secret**, and **Quoter URL Name**. All three must contain a value before **Test & Connect** becomes available.

    Keep credentials out of tickets, chat messages, and screenshots. The connected view does not display the saved Client Secret.

    <Frame caption="The three blank fields on the Connect step.">
      <img src="/images/product/quoter-connection-fields.png" alt="Blank Client ID, Client Secret and Quoter URL Name fields in Quoter setup." width="892" height="476" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and save the connection">
    Select **Test & Connect** and wait for the result. This authenticates with Quoter and saves the connection; it is not a test-only button. The URL name is saved as part of the same user workflow.

    If an error appears after authentication, return to the integration list and check whether the connection already exists before retrying. Connection and URL-name saving can fail separately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Complete">
    After connecting, select **Next** or the available **Complete** step. There is no company-mapping screen between these steps.

    <Frame caption="The wizard has two steps. Complete stays locked until you connect.">
      <img src="/images/product/quoter-setup-steps.png" alt="Quoter setup has Connect and Complete steps; Complete is locked before connecting." width="620" height="64" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Start the quote import

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Complete">
    Open the **Complete** step for the connected account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sync Quotes">
    Click **Sync Quotes**. Wait for **Sync started**. This means the request started, not that every record has imported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Quotes">
    Click **View Quotes** when it appears, or open **Quotes** from the sidebar later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a known quote">
    Compare a known quote's company, number, revision, line items, and totals with Quoter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Complete Setup">
    Click **Complete Setup** to leave the wizard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Complete Setup** finishes the wizard; it does not itself start a quote sync. Use **Sync Quotes** explicitly. A connected or ready message also does not prove that every quote is assigned to the right company.
</Note>

Active Quoter connections are scheduled to sync quotes every four hours. This is a background schedule, not an immediate delivery guarantee. Connection errors, provider responses, and task failures can delay updates. There is no separate client-mapping import you need to complete in the current Quoter wizard.

## Change the Quoter quote-link address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Connect step">
    For an existing connection, open **Connect**. The connected view shows **Quoter URL Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the name">
    Replace the name with the account subdomain only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save URL Name">
    Click **Save URL Name** and wait for **Saved**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a link">
    Open a known quote and check its **Open in Quoter** link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This button saves the URL name directly. **Next** and **Complete Setup** do not save an unsaved edit to that field. The URL name supplies the account portion of quote links; it does not change the quote's company association or portal permissions. A quote also needs its synchronized Quoter identifier to produce a link.

## Replace credentials

From the connected **Connect** view, choose **Reconnect with Different Credentials**. The form returns to blank credential and URL-name fields. Enter the intended account's credentials and URL name, then select **Test & Connect**.

Opening that form alone does not disconnect the saved account. Before submitting, check that you are updating the intended connection. If more than one Quoter connection is listed, ask MSPortal support which one you are changing before you submit.

After a successful reconnect, review the saved URL name and run **Sync Quotes**. Check actual quote results rather than assuming that a success message also verifies company matching or every downstream import.

## Remove Quoter from MSPortal

For an existing connection, use **Delete** in the wizard footer. Read the confirmation. Choose **Cancel** to keep the connection, or **Delete Integration** to confirm removal.

<Warning>
Confirmed deletion removes the MSPortal connection and its synchronized Quoter quotes, line items, and legacy client data. Reconnecting later requires credentials and another import. This action does not delete the original quotes in Quoter.
</Warning>

Deletion is separate from pausing updates or closing the wizard. Do not delete a connection merely to troubleshoot an incorrect URL name or one missing quote.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Test & Connect is unavailable | Enter nonblank Client ID, Client Secret, and Quoter URL Name. |
| Authentication fails | Confirm the intended Quoter account and its API credentials; ask its administrator to check API access. |
| A connection error appeared after submitting | Reopen the integration list before retrying; connection saving may have succeeded before the URL name failed. |
| Complete says clients are mapped, but a quote has no company | Verify the actual quote. The current wizard does not provide a manual mapping step. See the troubleshooting guide below. |
| Sync started, but Quotes is still empty | Allow the background run to finish, check connection status, and review company scope and quote-access filters. |
| Open in Quoter is missing or opens the wrong account | Check the saved URL name and the quote's synchronized identifier. |
| An older quote did not change after a company correction | A normal sync can skip unchanged older quotes. Ask for a targeted import investigation; repeating Sync Quotes does not guarantee a full historical reimport. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company administrator set up Quoter?">
    No. MSP staff manage the integration. Company users ask their MSP for quote access or help with missing records.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I enter my Quoter API credentials?">
    Open Settings > Integrations, find Quoter, and open setup. Enter Client ID and Client Secret in Connect, along with Quoter URL Name. Do not put them in the AI Assistant or a support ticket.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What belongs in Quoter URL Name?">
    Only the account subdomain: use example for example.quoter.com. Do not include the protocol, domain suffix, or quote path. The saved name uses lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Test & Connect a harmless connection test?">
    It authenticates and saves credentials and the connection when successful. Treat it as a save operation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not select Next before connecting?">
    The wizard needs a saved connection before you can advance normally. Complete all three fields, select Test & Connect, and wait for a successful result.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are Map Companies and Quick Match in the Quoter wizard?">
    They are not steps in the current Quoter wizard. It has Connect and Complete. Company association is resolved during quote synchronization from an available linked opportunity or the billing organization name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a ready message prove my companies are matched?">
    No. Check a sample of imported quotes and their actual companies. The message does not validate every association.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Complete Setup start the initial quote import?">
    No. Select Sync Quotes in the Complete step. Complete Setup leaves the wizard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Sync started mean all quotes have imported?">
    No. It indicates a started request. Check Quotes after the background work completes and investigate missing records separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often are Quoter quotes synchronized?">
    Active connections are scheduled every four hours. Errors or provider delays can prevent a particular update from appearing on that schedule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to wait for a daily client sync?">
    No separate client-mapping import is required in the current Quoter wizard. Use Sync Quotes and verify the resulting company associations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Sync Quotes force a full historical import?">
    No. Normal sync can be incremental and process changed quotes only. Ask support to investigate older missing records or associations that remain stale.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I correct a Quoter link without replacing credentials?">
    Open the existing connection's Connect step, change Quoter URL Name, and select Save URL Name. Verify a known quote link afterward.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Next save a changed URL name?">
    No. In the connected view, use Save URL Name and wait for Saved. Advancing or completing the wizard is not a substitute.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the URL name move quotes to another company?">
    No. It affects the account address used in Quoter links. Company association and quote visibility are separate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Reconnect with Different Credentials immediately disconnect Quoter?">
    No. Opening the blank reconnect form alone does not change the saved connection. Submitting Test & Connect is the operation that saves replacement credentials.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I retrieve the saved Client Secret from this page?">
    The connected view does not show it. Obtain authorized credentials from your Quoter administrator when a replacement is needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect a second Quoter account independently?">
    Confirm that workflow with MSPortal support before submitting another account's credentials, especially if multiple Quoter connections are listed. Do not assume that opening another setup form guarantees a separate saved connection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will deleting the integration delete my original Quoter proposals?">
    It removes the MSPortal connection and synchronized Quoter data. It does not delete the original provider proposals. Cancel keeps the connection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does connecting Quoter give every Company user access to its quotes?">
    No. Quotes still need the correct company association, and the user's role and quote visibility rules must allow access. Your MSP manages these permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does this connection create or accept quotes?">
    It imports quotes and line items for the portal. Creating, revising, publishing, and accepting a proposal take place through the appropriate Quoter workflow, not through this setup form.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
