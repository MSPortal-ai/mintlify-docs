Use this guide to get a quote document, or to export the quotes table. This page is for company users and MSP staff. MSPortal links to the quote on the provider's page. Salesbuildr quotes also have a **Download PDF** action in MSPortal.

## Before you start

- You need Quotes read access (the **Quotes** resource with **Read** access in the role editor).
- Provider links and PDFs appear only when the provider supplied them for that quote.
- MSPortal sign-in does not give you access to the provider's page. Ask your MSP for a copy if the page will not open.

## Choose the right route

| Provider | Where to open the quote | How to get a document |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quoter** | **... > Open in Quoter**, or **Open quote** on the detail page. | Use the document options on the Quoter page. |
| **QuoteWerks** | **... > Open in QuoteWerks**, or **Open quote** on the detail page. This can open a QuoteValet page. | Use the document options on that page. |
| **Salesbuildr** | **... > Open in Salesbuildr**, or **Open quote** on the detail page. | Click **Download PDF** in MSPortal, or use the Salesbuildr page. |

On the quote detail page, the provider link is labeled **Open quote**.

## Open a Quoter document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the quote">
    Click **Quotes** in the sidebar. Check the company and number, then choose **... > Open in Quoter**. The **Number** opens the quote's detail page in MSPortal, where **Open quote** goes to Quoter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review it">
    The Quoter page opens in a new tab. Check the number, revision, line items, and terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your copy">
    Use any document or download option on that page. If there is none, ask your MSP for a copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If **Open in Quoter** is missing, ask your MSP to check the Quoter URL name and the synced quote link.

## Open a QuoteWerks or QuoteValet document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the quote">
    Click **Quotes** in the sidebar, then choose **... > Open in QuoteWerks** on the quote's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the proposal">
    The link can open a QuoteValet page. The menu still says **Open in QuoteWerks**. Read the proposal and its terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your copy">
    Use the document options on that page. If there are none, ask your MSP for a copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

MSP staff: check the synced quote link and the QuoteValet setting. See [Configure QuoteWerks Integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-quotewerks-integration). Do not build or guess a customer link.

## Download a Salesbuildr PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the quote">
    Click **Quotes** in the sidebar and find the Salesbuildr quote. On the detail page you can use the **Download PDF** button instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Download PDF">
    Click the **...** menu on the row and choose **Download PDF**. The label changes to **Preparing PDF...** while MSPortal asks Salesbuildr for the file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the file">
    The PDF downloads, or it opens from a Salesbuildr link in a new tab. If your browser blocks the new tab, it opens in the current tab. Check the number, revision, and company before you share it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The PDF is fetched from Salesbuildr each time, so it matches the current quote.

## Export the quotes table to CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the company and filters">
    Click **Quotes**, pick the company in the top bar, and set your filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Skip the paging">
    The export covers every quote that matches your company and filters, across all pages. You do not need to go to each page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the columns you need">
    Click **Columns**, turn on the fields, and click **Save Changes**. The CSV has the columns that are visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export CSV">
    Click **Export CSV** above the table. A progress toast reads **Exporting N of M...** while MSPortal loads the quotes in batches, then **Exported N rows**. The browser downloads `quotes`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the file">
    Open the file and check the rows before you use it. If a batch fails, MSPortal shows **The export could not finish. Try again.** and downloads no file, so you never get a partial export.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The CSV is table data. It is not the proposal or a document you can accept. Dates and amounts can look different from the screen.

## Troubleshooting

| Message or problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Open in Quoter** is missing | Ask your MSP to check the Quoter URL name and the synced quote link. |
| **Open in QuoteWerks** is missing | Ask your MSP to check the customer link or the QuoteValet setting. |
| The provider page has no download | Ask your MSP for a copy. |
| **Salesbuildr has no PDF for this quote yet. Generate the PDF in Salesbuildr, then try again.** | Ask your MSP to generate the PDF in Salesbuildr, then try again. |
| **Salesbuildr is busy right now. Try downloading this PDF again in a moment.** | Wait a moment and try again. |
| **Salesbuildr rejected the saved API key. Ask an administrator to reconnect the Salesbuildr integration.** | Ask an MSP administrator to reconnect the integration. |
| **This quote is no longer available.** | Go back to the list and ask your MSP to check the quote and your access. |
| **Salesbuildr could not return this PDF. Try again in a few minutes.** | Try again later, or ask your MSP for a copy. |
| **The quote PDF could not be downloaded. Please try again.** | Try again. If it keeps failing, ask your MSP. |
| **Download PDF** is missing | It is for Salesbuildr quotes only. Use the provider link for Quoter and QuoteWerks. |
| A column is missing from the CSV | Show the column with **Columns > Save Changes**, then export again. |
| **The export could not finish. Try again.** | No file was downloaded. Click **Export CSV** again. If it keeps failing, narrow the filters and ask your MSP. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does MSPortal support Quoter and QuoteWerks as well as Salesbuildr?">
    Yes. Quoter and QuoteWerks use the provider link. Salesbuildr also has **Download PDF**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Open in QuoteWerks open QuoteValet?">
    QuoteValet can be the customer page for a QuoteWerks quote. Check the number and company before you go on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Export CSV include all pages?">
    Yes. It exports every quote that matches your company and filters, not only the page you see. It fails instead of downloading a partial file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export Quoter and QuoteWerks rows too?">
    Yes. The export covers the matching rows for every provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the CSV a quote I can accept?">
    No. Use the provider's document and its acceptance process.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Preparing PDF mean the download worked?">
    No. Wait for the file or a message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company user fix the Salesbuildr connection?">
    No. Your MSP manages the connection and its credentials.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
