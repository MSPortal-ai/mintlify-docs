Use the quote detail page to see the pricing and line items MSPortal imported from Quoter, QuoteWerks, or Salesbuildr. This page is for company users and MSP staff. Open it from the Quotes list by clicking a quote's **Number** or its row.

## Before you start

- You need Quotes read access (the **Quotes** resource with **Read** access in the role editor).
- A direct link does not bypass your access. Your company, role visibility, and allowed statuses still apply.
- The page is read only. You cannot change prices, quantities, optional items, or the stage.

## Read a quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the quote">
    Click **Quotes** in the sidebar, then click the quote's **Number** or its row. The page title is **Quote Details**. Use the back arrow to return to **Quotes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the header">
    Read the quote name, the stage badge, and the number, with **Rev** and the revision when there is one. A **Flagged** or **Draft** badge can also show. Check the company, owner, and created date. The last date reads **Won** for a won quote or **Expires** for the rest. Tags and **Internal Notes** show when the quote has them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Pricing Breakdown">
    Each card shows **Subtotal**, **Discount**, **Tax**, and **Total** for one billing period. The periods are **Monthly**, **Annual**, **One-Time**, **Upfront**, **Quarterly**, and **Semi-Annual**. A card is hidden when its total is missing or zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Line Items">
    The table shows **Name**, **SKU**, **Qty**, **Unit Price**, **Total**, **Billing**, and **Optional**. A line marked **Selected** is an optional line the provider records as chosen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the provider quote">
    Click **Open quote** to see the current quote and its terms on the provider's page. Salesbuildr quotes also have **Download PDF**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the amounts

- Keep recurring and one-time amounts apart. Do not add a monthly and an annual total together without checking the quote terms.
- A missing card does not mean the quote costs nothing. Check the provider quote.
- **Selected** shows a state imported from the provider. You cannot change it here.
- Use the provider's document to confirm the currency, billing periods, and payable amount.

## Get a document

| Provider | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Quoter** | Click **Open quote** and use the document options on the Quoter page. |
| **QuoteWerks** | Click **Open quote**. The link can open a QuoteValet page. |
| **Salesbuildr** | Click **Open quote**, or click **Download PDF** when a PDF is available. |

See [Download quote documents](/user-guides/quotes/download-quotes) for each provider.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The page shows not found | The quote no longer exists, or it is outside your company, role visibility, or allowed statuses. Ask your MSP. |
| You are sent to the home page | Your role lacks Quotes read access. |
| You see **Line items are not available for this quote.** | The sync did not import line items. Open the provider quote or ask your MSP. |
| A pricing card is missing | Its total is zero or missing. Check the provider quote. |
| **Open quote** is missing | The quote has no synced provider link. Ask your MSP. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change a quantity or pick an optional item here?">
    No. Use the provider's process, or ask your MSP for a revised quote.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an empty line item table mean the quote is empty?">
    No. The sync may not have imported the line items. Check the provider quote.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Selected accept an optional item for me?">
    No. It shows the state the provider sent. Reading this page changes nothing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is this the latest revision?">
    Compare the revision with the provider's document. MSPortal can be a few hours behind a provider change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
