Use **Quotes** to see the quotes your MSP syncs from **Quoter**, **QuoteWerks**, and **Salesbuildr**. Company users review the quotes they can access, read their details and open the provider page. MSP staff connect the providers and set company access. You create, price, and publish quotes in the quoting system, not in MSPortal.

## Before you start

- You need Quotes read access. In the role editor this is the **Quotes** resource with **Read** access.
- Your MSP must have Quoter, QuoteWerks, or Salesbuildr connected in **Settings > Integrations**. **Quotes** appears in the sidebar only when one of them is connected.
- Quotes appear after a sync. Quote syncs for all three providers run every four hours, so a brand-new quote can take a few hours to show.
- Your role can limit which quotes you see. See [Control company quote access](/user-guides/quotes/manage-company-access).

## Find a quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Quotes in the sidebar">
    The list shows the newest quotes first, 20 per page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the company">
    Use the company selector in the top bar. With one company selected, the **Company** column is hidden.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Use the filters under the **Number**, **Name**, **Company**, **Stage**, **Contact**, and **Created** column headings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the quote">
    Click the **Number** or the row to read the quote's details in MSPortal. To open the provider page, click the **...** menu on the row and choose **Open in** and the provider name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Find and open a quote](/user-guides/quotes/view-quotes) for every step, including **Add to Meeting**.

## Supported providers

| Provider | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Quoter** | Find synced quotes and open the Quoter proposal. |
| **QuoteWerks** | Find synced quotes and open the customer link, which can be a QuoteValet page. |
| **Salesbuildr** | Find synced quotes, open the proposal, and click **Download PDF** in MSPortal. |

Prices and line items come from the provider. A missing link or **Download PDF** button does not mean a provider is unsupported. See [Download quote documents](/user-guides/quotes/download-quotes).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Quotes** is missing from the sidebar | Your role lacks Quotes read access, or no quoting provider is connected. Ask your MSP. |
| The list is empty | Click **Clear Filters** and check the company selector. Then ask your MSP to check the company mapping and the sync. |
| A quote is missing | Your role can limit you to your own quotes or certain stages. Ask your MSP. |
| A quote has no **Open in** action | The quote has no synced provider link. You can still read it on the detail page. Ask your MSP. |
| A new quote is not there | Wait for the next sync, then ask your MSP. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which quoting systems does Quotes support?">
    Quoter, QuoteWerks, and Salesbuildr. Only Salesbuildr has the built-in **Download PDF** action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find a quote from my IT provider?">
    Open **Quotes**, find the quote, and click its **Number** to read it in MSPortal. To go to the provider's page, use the **...** menu and choose **Open in** the provider name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I create or change a quote here?">
    No. The list is read only. Ask your MSP to create or revise the quote in its quoting system.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I give myself access to more quotes?">
    No. Your MSP sets quote access in your role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening a quote accept it?">
    No. Acceptance happens on the provider's page, if it offers one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
