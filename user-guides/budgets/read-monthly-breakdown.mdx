Use the **Monthly Breakdown** tab to see when each budget line is scheduled to cost money, month by month. This guide is for MSP staff and for company users whose MSP has given them budget access.

## Before you start

- You need budget read access.
- To change an amount you also need budget edit access, and the budget must be Draft or Pending Approval. See [Adjust a budget amount for one month](/user-guides/budgets/edit-monthly-overrides).

## Open the monthly schedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the budget">
    Click **Budgets**, click **...** on the budget's row, then click **View**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Monthly Breakdown">
    Click the **Monthly Breakdown** tab in **Budget Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read each line">
    The **Line Item** column on the left stays in place while you scroll. Under each description you see the recurrence: **One-time**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Annual** or **Every N months**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll across the months">
    Scroll the table sideways to read **Jan** through **Dec** and **Year Total**. The columns always run January to December. They do not shift to your fiscal-year start month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the subtotals and totals">
    Each category ends with a subtotal row named after the category, such as **Software subtotal**. At the bottom, **Monthly Total** adds all lines for each month, and **Running Total** adds up the months so far.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check line status on Overview">
    The schedule has no status column. Click **Overview** to see each line's status, quantity, unit price and start month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This tab is a plan for spending. It does not record payments or match invoices. Use **Actuals** for invoices.

## How charges are scheduled

Each charge is quantity times unit price. A line's first charge lands in its start month. If no start month is set, the schedule uses January.

| Recurrence | Scheduled charge |
| --- | --- |
| **One-time** | Once, in the start month. |
| **Monthly** | Every month from the start month. |
| **Quarterly** | Every three months from the start month. |
| **Annual** | Once, in the start month. |
| **Custom** | Every N months from the start month. |

A line with a term stops charging when the term ends. A 100 USD monthly line that starts in March with a three-month term shows 100 USD in March, April and May. Lines you add in the editor have no term setting. Terms come from Excel and previous-year imports. A saved month override replaces that month's calculated amount, even when the override is zero.

## Totals and rounding

- **Year Total** adds the twelve months for a line or a category.
- **Monthly Total** adds every line's amount for that month.
- **Running Total** is the cumulative planned spend through that month. It is not money left in the budget.
- Month cells round to whole currency units. Totals keep cents, so the cells can add up slightly differently than the total.
- A dash means no planned charge that month.
- The schedule includes every line, even rejected ones. It is not limited to approved lines.

An overridden cell shows in blue bold type. Hover it to see the calculated amount.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The tab says to add line items | The budget has no lines, or the schedule did not load. Check **Overview**, then reload the page. |
| **Year Total** differs from the **Overview** total | **Overview** counts each line once. This tab applies recurrence, start month, term and overrides. |
| You cannot click a month cell | You need budget edit access and a Draft or Pending Approval budget. Approved and Archived budgets are locked. |
| The cells do not add up to the total | Cells round to whole units. Totals keep cents. |
| A line's charges stop early | The line has a term from an import. Ask an editor to check it. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see a budget's costs by month?">
    Open the budget with **View**, then click **Monthly Breakdown**. Scroll sideways for all twelve months and **Year Total**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Monthly Breakdown show only approved lines?">
    No. It includes every line status, including rejected lines. Check **Overview** for each line's status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Running Total the remaining budget?">
    No. It is cumulative planned spend through that month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the schedule start in my fiscal-year start month?">
    No. The columns always run January to December.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does entering zero remove an override?">
    No. Zero replaces the calculated charge with zero. To go back to the calculation, clear the field and leave it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I print this tab?">
    Yes. Open **...** in the page header and click **Print** while this tab is open. It prints on a landscape letter page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
