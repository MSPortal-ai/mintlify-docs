Approving a budget turns it into the fixed baseline MSPortal uses to compare planned costs with customer invoice spend. Approval also gives every line item a unique reference, so projects, purchases, licenses, labor and other costs can be traced back to the approved plan. This guide is for MSP staff whose role has the **manage_budgets** permission. Other readers can review the plan and ask their MSP to approve it.

<Warning>
Approval is permanent. You cannot edit an approved budget or return it to Draft. Review the budget carefully before you confirm.
</Warning>

## Before you start

- The budget is **Draft** or **Pending Approval**, and it has no more than 500 lines.
- You have the **manage_budgets** permission. Without it, **Approved** is missing from the **Status** list.
- The budget has a company, fiscal year, currency and a complete set of lines.
- Quantities, prices, recurrence, categories, line statuses and linked projects or products are final.
- A supported PSA integration with invoice sync is on, if you plan to track actual spend.

## Approve a budget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the budget">
    Click **...** then **Edit** on the budget's row. Check the company, fiscal year, currency, period type, lines and totals. Rejected lines stay visible after approval. They are left out of the approved amount and cannot receive actual spend. Draft and Pending lines count toward the baseline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review matching links">
    For a project cost, open the Planner item and check that it links to the right PSA project under **Relationships** (**Link to Project**). For a recurring license or service cost, check the product link. MSPortal records the matching links it finds at the moment of approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status to Approved">
    Click **Budget Details** to expand it if it is collapsed. Change **Status** to **Approved**. The **Lock Budget After Approval?** warning opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the lock">
    Read the warning and click **Approve Budget**. Click **Cancel** to back out.

    <Frame caption="The warning opens before you save. After you confirm it, you still need to click Save.">
      <img src="/images/product/budget-approval-warning.png" alt="Lock Budget After Approval warning explaining permanent locking, with Cancel and Approve Budget buttons." width="1024" height="644" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the approval">
    Click **Save**. The budget locks only when the save succeeds. If **Your changes were saved, but the budget could not be approved. Reload the page and try again.** appears, reload and approve again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Actuals">
    Open the budget with **View** and click the **Actuals** tab. The approved period and the line references are ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What approval locks

| Budget data | After approval |
|-------------|----------------|
| Title, company, fiscal year, currency and notes | Read-only |
| Quantities, prices, recurrence and annualized costs | Read-only |
| Categories and line statuses | Read-only |
| Linked projects and products used for matching | Captured in the baseline |
| Line order and which lines exist | Read-only |
| Delete budget | Not allowed. Archive it instead. |
| Duplicate, print and export | Allowed |
| Actuals review and exception resolution | Allowed with the right permissions |

The Actuals period is fixed at approval. It runs from January 1 to December 31 of the fiscal year. Choosing **Quarterly** or **Monthly** as the period type does not shorten it.

<Note>
Budgets approved before actuals tracking existed keep their locked plan but have no references or matching links. Use manual allocations for them, or duplicate the budget and approve the copy to get references for future billing.
</Note>

## Use budget references in your PSA

Each approved line gets a short reference such as `BR-26-1A2B3C4D5E`. MSPortal looks for it in synced invoice, purchase order, project, contract and related details. It is an internal reference. It is not purchase-order approval or three-way matching. Only details your PSA integration imports can be searched. There is no custom-field mapping screen for references, so check that your chosen field reaches the synced invoice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the reference">
    Open **Budget Details > Actuals**. In **Line outcomes**, click the value in the **Reference** column to copy it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to the PSA">
    Paste it into the PSA field your team uses for that cost, such as a project PO, customer PO, invoice reference or a custom field that flows to the invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it with the billed work">
    Make the reference part of your project, purchasing or billing routine so it ends up on the synced invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    After the invoice sync finishes, open **Actuals** and confirm the event matched the right line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use one budget reference per invoice event. If one event holds more than one reference, MSPortal cannot pick an exact match.
</Tip>

## How automatic matching works

MSPortal records the identifiers on each line when you approve. It then matches actual spend in this order.

1. Exact budget reference
2. Linked project
3. Linked product
4. Product identity, such as SKU or product name

An exact reference wins over the other methods. If the best method points to more than one line, the event goes to **Unmatched exceptions**. A reference that does not name an eligible line in this budget also goes there. It does not fall back to a project or product match.

## Archive a superseded budget

Archiving retires an approved budget and keeps its baseline and history. Only the planned baseline is frozen. Actuals still change when invoices sync or when authorized users change reconciliation decisions, even on an archived budget.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the approved budget">
    Click **...** then **Edit**. The budget is read-only and a banner reads **Read-only - budget approved**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Archive">
    Click **Archive** in the banner. You need the **manage_budgets** permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The dialog asks **Archive Budget Title?** and says the budget becomes read-only and shows as Archived. Click **Archive**. The status changes to **Archived** and the Actuals history stays.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Replace an approved budget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Duplicate it">
    Click **...** then **Duplicate** on the approved budget's row. The Draft copy is created right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review every line">
    Copied lines start **Pending**, including lines that were Rejected in the original. Check each line's status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the copy">
    Follow the steps above on the copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive the original">
    Archive the old budget so both baselines stay for audit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prepare a line for matching

- **Project cost.** Open the Planner item, link the correct PSA project under **Relationships**, and add the item to the budget before approval. After approval, copy the reference into a PSA field your integration imports.
- **License or service cost.** Add it from the product catalog when you can. Check the product link, SKU, name, quantity, price and recurrence before approval. MSPortal uses the product link or identity when the invoice line has no reference.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| **Approved** is missing from **Status** | You need **manage_budgets**, and the budget must be Draft or Pending Approval. |
| Approval fails with a validation error | Complete the company, fiscal year, currency, categories and line data, then try again. |
| The budget cannot be edited after approval | That is expected. Duplicate it for an editable copy. |
| A line has no reference | An older locked budget has none. Use manual allocation. For a new budget, reload and confirm the save finished. |
| **Your changes were saved, but the budget could not be approved.** | Reload, check the saved budget, and approve again. A saved Draft is not a locked baseline. |
| A project or product link was wrong at approval | Duplicate and correct the copy. The original baseline does not change. |
| **Archive** is missing | The budget must be Approved, and you need **manage_budgets**. |

## Best practices

- Treat approval as the handoff from planning to measuring.
- Set line statuses before approval. Only Rejected lines are left out of the baseline.
- Link projects and products before approval so MSPortal records stable matching keys.
- Add the generated reference to the PSA early in the purchase or project workflow.
- Pick one PSA field for budget references and train billing staff to keep it on invoices.
- Duplicate and replace approved budgets. Do not delete old baselines.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I approve a budget?">
    Open the budget with **Edit**, expand **Budget Details**, set **Status** to **Approved**, click **Approve Budget** in the warning, then click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Approve Budget in the warning save the approval?">
    No. It only sets the status in the editor. You must click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company administrator approve budgets?">
    Only if their MSP gives them **manage_budgets**. The company administrator label alone does not.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I unlock an approved budget or change a rejected line?">
    No. Duplicate the budget, fix the Draft copy and approve it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Monthly or Quarterly limit the Actuals dates?">
    No. Actuals always uses January 1 to December 31 of the fiscal year.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I approve a budget with more than 500 lines?">
    No. Approval allows at most 500 lines. Remove lines or split the plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if someone changed the budget while I was approving it?">
    Reload, review the latest saved budget, and approve again only after you check it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does archiving freeze the Actuals?">
    No. The planned baseline stays fixed. Invoices and reconciliation decisions can still change Actuals.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does copying a reference send it to the PSA?">
    No. It only copies the code to your clipboard. Add it in your PSA yourself.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
