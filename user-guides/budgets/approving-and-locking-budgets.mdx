title: 'Approve and Lock a Budget'
description: 'Finalize a budget as an immutable baseline and prepare its line items for actual-spend matching'
icon: 'lock'
---

Approving a budget turns it into the fixed baseline MSPortal uses to compare planned costs with customer invoice spend. Approval also gives every line item a unique reference, so projects, purchases, licenses, labor and other costs can be traced back to the approved plan. Locking a budget from the editor needs the **manage_budgets** permission. Deciding individual lines needs the **Approve budget items** permission, described in [Approve or reject budget lines](#approve-or-reject-budget-lines).

Approval is the client's decision. A typical budget goes through three steps:

1. **Submit for review** (optional): colleagues at the MSP check the draft.
2. **Submit for approval**: the budget goes to the people at the client who decide it.
3. **Sign off**: a client approver approves the budget, line by line or all at once, and signs it with their typed name.

<Warning>
Approval is permanent. You cannot edit an approved budget or return it to Draft. Review the budget carefully before it is approved.
</Warning>

## Submit a budget for review

Ask colleagues at the MSP to check a draft before it goes to the client. The budget stays a Draft and you can keep editing it. Clients never see the review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the budget">
    Open the Draft budget in View mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit for review">
    Click **Submit for review** in the **Approval** row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the reviewers">
    Under **Reviewers**, choose one or more MSP staff members. The list shows staff who can see budgets. Optionally add a message saying what they should check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the request">
    Click **Send for review**. Each reviewer receives an email and an in-app notification. The **Approval** row shows who asked, when, and who the review is waiting on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Reviewers open the budget, click **Review** in the **Approval** row and answer **Looks good** or **Needs changes**, optionally with a note. The person who asked receives an in-app notification, and each answer appears in the **Approval** row. A reviewer can change their answer until the budget is sent to the client.

Click **Change reviewers** to ask different people (this clears the answers so far), or **Cancel review** to end the review. Submitting the budget for approval also ends the review; its answers stay in the history.

## Submit a budget for approval

Send the budget to the people at the client who decide it. Approvers are always client users: people at the budget's company, or at a parent company that manages it, who hold the **Approve budget items** permission. A parent company admin limited to selected child companies can approve only for those companies. MSP staff are not approvers; ask them for a review instead. MSPortal never sends the request to someone who cannot approve it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the budget">
    Open the Draft budget in View mode. The **Approval** row in the budget summary shows that it has not been submitted yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit for approval">
    Click **Submit for approval** in the **Approval** row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the approvers">
    In the **People at** list, which names the client, choose one or more approvers. Under **Company role**, optionally choose a role: everyone at the client who holds that role and can approve budget items is asked too. Each role shows how many people would be asked; a role nobody can approve with is unavailable. People in the role who cannot approve budget items are not asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a message">
    Optionally add a message for the approvers, such as a deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the request">
    Click **Submit for approval**. The budget moves to **Pending Approval**, lines still in Draft move to Pending, and each approver receives an email and an in-app notification linking to the budget. The **Approval** row shows who submitted the budget, when, and who it is waiting on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Decide each line

Approvers open the budget from the email or notification and use each line's status menu to approve or reject it, or **Approve all pending** to approve every pending line at once. Approvers who cannot edit budgets can decide lines only while the budget is Pending Approval.

### Change approvers or withdraw

While the budget is Pending Approval, budget editors can:

- Click **Change approvers** to choose different people or a different role. Everyone chosen receives the request again.
- Click **Withdraw** to return the budget to Draft. The approvers receive an in-app notice that the budget no longer needs their review. Submit it again when it is ready.

<Note>
A budget set to Pending Approval from the budget editor records who submitted it, but no approvers are notified. Click **Choose approvers** in the **Approval** row to send the request.
</Note>

### Sign off the budget

A client approver signs off the budget to approve and lock it. They can decide each line first, or sign off straight away to approve the whole budget: lines still pending are approved by the sign-off.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the lines">
    The **Approval** row shows how many lines are decided. Reject any line the client does not want; lines left pending are approved when you sign off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign off">
    Click **Sign off** in the **Approval** row. The dialog shows the approved and rejected lines with their totals, how many pending lines the sign-off approves, and the budget's fingerprint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree and type your name">
    Read the statement, tick the box to agree, and type your full name. Your typed name is your signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    Click **Sign off budget**. The budget becomes **Approved** and locked. The submitter and the other approvers receive an email and an in-app notification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Approvers the request was sent to can sign off. If the request named nobody, any client approver can. MSP staff cannot sign off, and you cannot sign off while viewing MSPortal as another user.

The sign-off records:

- Your typed name, your account name and email
- The date and time, your IP address and browser
- The statement you agreed to, including how many pending lines it approved
- The budget's **fingerprint**: a SHA-256 hash of the budget exactly as you approved it

If anyone changes the budget while you are reviewing it, the fingerprint no longer matches and the sign-off is refused. Review the updated budget and sign again.

The exported PDF of a signed-off budget ends with a **Sign-off certificate** page listing these details. The signer's email and IP address appear only in PDFs exported by MSP staff.

### Review the approval history

Click **History** in the **Approval** row to see every step, newest first: internal reviews and their answers (MSP staff only), submission, approval requests, each line approved or rejected, edits that returned a line to Pending, sign-off, approval and archiving. Each entry shows who acted and when. Names stay in the history after a user is deleted.

Each approved or rejected line also shows who decided it and when, below its status.

<Note>
Changing anything that defines an approved or rejected line, such as its quantity, price, recurrence or category, returns it to **Pending** so it is decided again. Editing only its notes does not.
</Note>

## Before you lock a budget from the editor

- The budget is **Draft** or **Pending Approval**, and it has no more than 500 lines.
- You have the **manage_budgets** permission. Without it, **Approved** is missing from the **Status** list.
- The budget has a company, fiscal year, currency and a complete set of lines.
- Quantities, prices, recurrence, categories, line statuses and linked projects or products are final.
- A supported PSA integration with invoice sync is on, if you plan to track actual spend.

## Approve or reject budget lines

Each line has its own status: **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Draft**. Changing a line's status needs the **Approve budget items** permission. In **Settings > Users & Roles > Roles**, it sits under **Budgets** in the role editor. **Manage** access to Budgets includes it. **Write** access does not, so a role can edit budgets without deciding lines, or decide lines without editing budgets.

| Your access | Where you can decide lines | Statuses you can set |
|-------------|----------------------------|----------------------|
| **Approve budget items** without Write | Budgets in **Pending Approval** | Approved, Rejected, Pending |
| **Approve budget items** with Write or Manage | Budgets in **Draft** or **Pending Approval** | Approved, Rejected, Pending, Draft |

Approved and Archived budgets are locked, so nobody can change their line statuses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the budget">
    In **Budgets**, click **...** then **View** on the budget's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change a line">
    On the **Overview** tab, open the **...** menu next to a line's status and choose **Approve**, **Reject**, **Mark as pending** or **Mark as draft**. The menu lists only the statuses you can set. The change saves at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve every pending line">
    To approve all Pending lines together, click **Approve all pending** above the line table. It shows the number of Pending lines and appears only when there are some. The confirmation shows how many lines and what total will be approved. Click **Approve _N_ items** to confirm. You can still change individual lines until the budget is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the totals">
    The summary shows **Total**, **Approved**, **Pending** and **Rejected**. **Pending** includes Draft lines.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Approving every line does not approve or lock the budget. To lock it, a client approver [signs it off](#sign-off-the-budget), or someone with **manage_budgets** follows [Approve a budget](#approve-a-budget). You can also change line statuses in the budget editor's **Approval** column, or for several lines at once with **Update Statuses** in the Budgets list row menu. See [Add and edit budget line items](/user-guides/budgets/edit-line-items#change-a-lines-approval-status).

## Approve a budget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the budget">
    Click **...** then **Edit** on the budget's row. Check the company, fiscal year, currency, period type, lines and totals. Rejected lines stay visible after approval. They are left out of the approved amount and cannot receive actual spend. Draft and Pending lines count toward the baseline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review matching links">
    For a project cost, open the Planner item and check that it links to the right PSA project under **Relationships** (**Link to Project**). For a recurring license or service cost, check the product link. MSPortal records the matching links it finds at the moment of approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status to Approved">
    Click **Settings** at the top right. Change **Status** to **Approved**. The **Lock Budget After Approval?** warning opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the lock">
    Read the warning and click **Approve Budget**. Click **Cancel** to back out.

    <Frame caption="The warning opens before you save. After you confirm it, you still need to click Save.">
      <img src="/images/product/budget-approval-warning.png" alt="Lock Budget After Approval warning explaining permanent locking, with Cancel and Approve Budget buttons." width="1024" height="644" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the approval">
    Click **Save**. The budget locks only when the save succeeds. If **Your changes were saved, but the budget could not be approved. Reload the page and try again.** appears, reload and approve again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Actuals">
    Open the budget with **View** and click the **Actuals** tab. The approved period and the line references are ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What approval locks

| Budget data | After approval |
|-------------|----------------|
| Title, company, fiscal year, currency and notes | Read-only |
| Quantities, prices, recurrence and annualized costs | Read-only |
| Categories and line statuses | Read-only |
| Linked projects and products used for matching | Captured in the baseline |
| Line order and which lines exist | Read-only |
| Delete budget | Not allowed. Archive it instead. |
| Duplicate, print and export | Allowed |
| Actuals review and exception resolution | Allowed with the right permissions |

The Actuals period is fixed at approval. It runs from January 1 to December 31 of the fiscal year. Choosing **Quarterly** or **Monthly** as the period type does not shorten it.

<Note>
Budgets approved before actuals tracking existed keep their locked plan but have no references or matching links. Use manual allocations for them, or duplicate the budget and approve the copy to get references for future billing.
</Note>

## Use budget references in your PSA

Each approved line gets a short reference such as `BR-26-1A2B3C4D5E`. MSPortal looks for it in synced invoice, purchase order, project, contract and related details. It is an internal reference. It is not purchase-order approval or three-way matching. Only details your PSA integration imports can be searched. There is no custom-field mapping screen for references, so check that your chosen field reaches the synced invoice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the reference">
    Open **Budget Details > Actuals**. In **Line outcomes**, click the value in the **Reference** column to copy it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to the PSA">
    Paste it into the PSA field your team uses for that cost, such as a project PO, customer PO, invoice reference or a custom field that flows to the invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it with the billed work">
    Make the reference part of your project, purchasing or billing routine so it ends up on the synced invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    After the invoice sync finishes, open **Actuals** and confirm the event matched the right line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use one budget reference per invoice event. If one event holds more than one reference, MSPortal cannot pick an exact match.
</Tip>

## How automatic matching works

MSPortal records the identifiers on each line when you approve. It then matches actual spend in this order.

1. Exact budget reference
2. Linked project
3. Linked product
4. Product identity, such as SKU or product name

An exact reference wins over the other methods. If the best method points to more than one line, the event goes to **Unmatched exceptions**. A reference that does not name an eligible line in this budget also goes there. It does not fall back to a project or product match.

## Archive a superseded budget

Archiving retires an approved budget and keeps its baseline and history. Only the planned baseline is frozen. Actuals still change when invoices sync or when authorized users change reconciliation decisions, even on an archived budget.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the approved budget">
    Click **...** then **Edit**. The budget is read-only and a banner reads **Read-only - budget approved**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Archive">
    Click **Archive** in the banner. You need the **manage_budgets** permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The dialog asks **Archive Budget Title?** and says the budget becomes read-only and shows as Archived. Click **Archive**. The status changes to **Archived** and the Actuals history stays.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Replace an approved budget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Duplicate it">
    Click **...** then **Duplicate** on the approved budget's row. The Draft copy is created right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review every line">
    Copied lines start **Pending**, including lines that were Rejected in the original. Check each line's status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the copy">
    Follow the steps above on the copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive the original">
    Archive the old budget so both baselines stay for audit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prepare a line for matching

- **Project cost.** Open the Planner item, link the correct PSA project under **Relationships**, and add the item to the budget before approval. After approval, copy the reference into a PSA field your integration imports.
- **License or service cost.** Add it from the product catalog when you can. Check the product link, SKU, name, quantity, price and recurrence before approval. MSPortal uses the product link or identity when the invoice line has no reference.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| **Approved** is missing from **Status** | You need **manage_budgets**, and the budget must be Draft or Pending Approval. |
| **Submit for approval** or **Submit for review** is missing | Your role must be able to edit budgets, and the budget must be Draft. Only MSP staff see **Submit for review**. |
| A person is missing from the approver list | Only client users who are active, can sign in, hold **Approve budget items**, and belong to this company or a parent company that manages it are listed. MSP staff are never approvers. Ask them for a review instead. |
| A colleague is missing from the reviewer list | Only MSP staff who are active, can sign in and can see budgets are listed. You cannot review your own request. |
| **Nobody you chose can approve budget items** | Nobody you chose can approve for this company. Grant **Approve budget items** to the people who should approve. |
| A company role is unavailable, or **Nobody at ... who holds this role can approve budget items** | Nobody holding that role at this company can approve budget items. Choose another role, or grant **Approve budget items** to the role's members. |
| **This budget changed since you opened it** | Someone saved the budget after you opened it. The page reloads. Review the change and submit again. |
| **Sign off** is unavailable | The budget has no lines, or you are viewing MSPortal as another user. |
| **Sign off** is missing | Only client approvers the request was sent to can sign off. MSP staff cannot. |
| **This budget changed while you were reviewing it** | The fingerprint changed. Review the updated budget and sign again. |
| A line has no status menu | You need **Approve budget items**. Without Write access, you can decide lines only while the budget is Pending Approval. |
| **Mark as draft** is missing | Only people who can also edit budgets can return a line to Draft. |
| Approval fails with a validation error | Complete the company, fiscal year, currency, categories and line data, then try again. |
| The budget cannot be edited after approval | That is expected. Duplicate it for an editable copy. |
| A line has no reference | An older locked budget has none. Use manual allocation. For a new budget, reload and confirm the save finished. |
| **Your changes were saved, but the budget could not be approved.** | Reload, check the saved budget, and approve again. A saved Draft is not a locked baseline. |
| A project or product link was wrong at approval | Duplicate and correct the copy. The original baseline does not change. |
| **Archive** is missing | The budget must be Approved, and you need **manage_budgets**. |

## Best practices

- Treat approval as the handoff from planning to measuring.
- Set line statuses before approval. Only Rejected lines are left out of the baseline.
- Link projects and products before approval so MSPortal records stable matching keys.
- Add the generated reference to the PSA early in the purchase or project workflow.
- Pick one PSA field for budget references and train billing staff to keep it on invoices.
- Duplicate and replace approved budgets. Do not delete old baselines.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I approve a budget?">
    Open the budget with **Edit**, click **Settings**, set **Status** to **Approved**, click **Approve Budget** in the warning, then click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Approve Budget in the warning save the approval?">
    No. It only sets the status in the editor. You must click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company administrator approve budgets?">
    The company administrator label alone does not grant approval. To decide lines or sign off a budget sent to them, their MSP gives their role **Approve budget items**. To lock the whole budget from the editor, they need **manage_budgets**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a sign-off legally binding?">
    A sign-off records the signer's typed name, account, time, IP address, browser and the exact budget they approved, identified by its fingerprint. Whether that meets the requirements of a particular contract or jurisdiction is for you and your client to decide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who receives the approval request?">
    Everyone at the client chosen by name, plus everyone at the client who holds the chosen company role and can approve budget items. The person who submits the budget is not notified about their own request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can MSP staff approve a budget for the client?">
    Not through a sign-off: approval requests and sign-offs are for client users. Staff who manage budgets can still set a budget to **Approved** in the editor, for example after the client approved it outside MSPortal. That is recorded as staff approval, not a client sign-off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the client approve the whole budget at once?">
    Yes. An approver can sign off without deciding each line: every line still pending is approved by the sign-off. Reject any line the client does not want first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the client see our internal review?">
    No. The review, its answers and its history entries are visible to MSP staff only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does approving every line lock the budget?">
    No. Line statuses and the budget status are separate. Only a client sign-off, or setting the budget's **Status** to **Approved**, locks it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I unlock an approved budget or change a rejected line?">
    No. Duplicate the budget, fix the Draft copy and approve it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Monthly or Quarterly limit the Actuals dates?">
    No. Actuals always uses January 1 to December 31 of the fiscal year.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I approve a budget with more than 500 lines?">
    No. Approval allows at most 500 lines. Remove lines or split the plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if someone changed the budget while I was approving it?">
    Reload, review the latest saved budget, and approve again only after you check it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does archiving freeze the Actuals?">
    No. The planned baseline stays fixed. Invoices and reconciliation decisions can still change Actuals.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does copying a reference send it to the PSA?">
    No. It only copies the code to your clipboard. Add it in your PSA yourself.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
