Use these steps to delete Draft or Pending Approval budgets you no longer need. Deleting removes the budget and all its lines. After the delete runs, it cannot be undone. This guide is for MSP staff and for company users whose MSP has given them budget management access.

## Before you start

- You need the **manage_budgets** permission. Without it the row **Delete** item, the row checkboxes and the editor **Delete** button are all missing. Write access alone is not enough.
- Only **Draft** and **Pending Approval** budgets can be deleted. Approved and Archived budgets are locked and cannot be deleted. To retire an Approved budget, use the archive step in [Approve and lock a budget](/user-guides/budgets/approving-and-locking-budgets).
- If you need a copy first, [download the budget](/user-guides/budgets/export-and-print-budgets).

## Delete one budget from the list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the budget">
    Click **Budgets** and check the company, title, fiscal year and status of the budget you want to remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    Click **...** at the end of its row, then click **Delete**. This opens a confirmation and does not delete yet. It selects only that row, so any rows you ticked before are cleared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the confirmation">
    The dialog asks **Delete 1 budget?** and lists the budget's title. It says the budget and its line items are deleted permanently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete budget**, or click **Cancel** to keep it. A **Deleted 1 budget** message appears and the row leaves the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete several budgets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tick the budgets">
    Tick the checkbox on each Draft or Pending Approval row you want to delete. Check only unwanted budgets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the actions menu">
    Click the menu button above the table. It shows the selected count and reads **Actions (count)**, for example **Actions (2)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete Selected">
    Click **Delete Selected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the list">
    The dialog asks **Delete N budgets?** and names up to five of them, followed by **and N more**. If a title is wrong, click **Cancel** and fix the ticks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete N budgets**. Check the list afterward to make sure the right budgets are gone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Cancel closes the dialog but keeps your ticks. Untick unwanted rows before you start another batch. Changing the company in the top bar clears the selection.

## Delete from the budget editor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the budget">
    Click **...** on the row, then **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete">
    Click **Delete** in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the warning">
    A confirmation opens beside the button. It reads **Are you sure?** and says it will permanently delete the budget, with its title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete Budget**. The confirmation changes to **Deleted** with an **Undo** button and a short countdown. Click **Undo** to keep the budget. When the countdown ends, the budget is deleted and you return to the Budgets list. You do not need to save first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Delete** in the editor is disabled when the budget is locked.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Delete** item or checkboxes | Your role lacks **manage_budgets**. Ask your MSP admin. |
| An error appears and nothing is deleted | The batch may hold an Approved or Archived budget. Untick it and try again. |
| **N budgets were not deleted. They may already be gone or you may not have access to them.** | Refresh the list. Ask your MSP if a budget is still there. |
| **Delete** is disabled in the editor | The budget is Approved or Archived. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which budgets can I delete?">
    Draft and Pending Approval budgets, with **manage_budgets**. Approved and Archived budgets cannot be deleted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I undo a deletion?">
    Only during the short countdown after you confirm a delete in the editor. Deleting from the list runs at once. After that the budget and its lines are gone for good.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Cancel clear my ticked rows?">
    No. Untick them yourself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I use the row Delete while other rows are ticked?">
    The row Delete selects that one row only, then asks you to confirm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Delete shown for an Approved budget?">
    The menu item shows for anyone with **manage_budgets**. The database still refuses to delete a locked budget.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
