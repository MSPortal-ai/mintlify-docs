Use the Budgets list to find a budget and open it. This guide is for MSP staff and for company users whose MSP has given them budget access.

## Before you start

- You need the **read_budgets** permission, or **write_budgets** or **manage_budgets**, which include it. The **Budgets** item is missing from the sidebar without it. Ask your MSP admin to change your role.
- MSP staff can pick a company in the top bar or use **Global View** to see every company they can access.
- Company users see only their own company's budgets.

## Find a budget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Budgets">
    Click **Budgets** in the sidebar. With one company selected, the page title reads **Budgets: Company Name**. In Global View it reads **Budgets (Global View)** and the table adds a **Company** column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by title">
    Type part of the name in the **Filter...** field under the **Budget Title** column heading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by year or status">
    Use the **Year** filter to pick a fiscal year. Use the **Status** filter to pick **Draft**, **Pending Approval**, **Approved** or **Archived**. The filters combine.

    <Frame caption="The Status filter only changes which budgets the table shows. It does not change any budget's status.">
      <img src="/images/product/budget-status-options.png" alt="Budget Status options: All, Draft, Pending Approval, Approved and Archived." width="312" height="332" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow by company in Global View">
    Use the **Company** column filter. If you click a company name in a row instead, the top-bar company selection changes to that company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the filters">
    Click **Clear Filters** to remove every table filter. This does not change the company selected in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page through the results">
    The table shows 20 budgets per page. Use **Previous** and **Next** below the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open a budget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    Click the **...** button at the end of the budget's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose View">
    Click **View**. The **Budget Details** page opens on the **Overview** tab. Opening a budget never approves it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Clicking the budget title works differently. If you can edit budgets, the title opens the editor. If you have read-only access, it opens **Budget Details**.

## Read the amounts in the list

| Column | What it adds up |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Line items with Pending status |
| **Rejected** | Line items with Rejected status |
| **Approved** | Line items with Approved status |
| **Total** | **Approved** plus **Pending**. Rejected lines are left out. |

The **Total** on the Overview tab includes rejected lines, so the two totals can differ. Neither is the yearly cost of recurring items. Use [Monthly Breakdown](/user-guides/budgets/read-monthly-breakdown) for that.

The budget's status (**Status** column) and each line's status are separate. An Approved budget can still hold Rejected or Pending lines.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A budget you expect is missing | Click **Clear Filters**, then check the top-bar company and the **Year** filter. |
| **Budgets** is missing from the sidebar | Your role has no budget permission. Ask your MSP admin. |
| The title opens an editor, not the reader | Your role can edit budgets. Use **...** then **View** for **Budget Details**. |
| The list **Total** is lower than the Overview **Total** | The list leaves out rejected lines. The Overview includes them. |
| Amounts look wrong across companies | Each budget uses its own currency. Check the currency before you compare. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I filter by year and status together?">
    Yes. The **Budget Title**, **Year**, **Status** and **Company** filters all apply at once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Clear Filters change my selected company?">
    No. It clears table filters only. Change the company in the top bar separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does filtering to Approved approve a budget?">
    No. The filter only changes which rows you see. Approving is a separate step that needs the **manage_budgets** permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where does a company user see budgets?">
    Open **Budgets** in the sidebar, then use **...** then **View** on the budget row. If the module or a budget is missing, ask your MSP.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
