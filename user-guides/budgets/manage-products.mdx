Use the budget product catalog to keep reusable names, prices and billing recurrence for the costs you add to budgets. Catalog changes save right away. Adding a product to a budget is a separate step. This guide is for MSP staff, and for company users whose MSP has given them budget write access.

## Before you start

- You need the **write_budgets** or **manage_budgets** permission.
- To make a company-specific product, select that company in the top bar first.
- Open **Budgets > Budgets Menu > Products**. See [Find and understand budget products](/user-guides/budgets/read-product-catalog).

## Create a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the company">
    Look at the top-bar company selector. With one company selected, the form shows **Assign to Company Name only**. Global View does not show it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Product">
    Click **New Product** at the top right of the **Products** page. The **New Product** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can use it">
    Tick **Assign to Company Name only** to make a product for that one company. Leave it clear to make a shared product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Type a **Name**. It is required. Enter the **Unit Price**, zero or more. A blank price saves as zero. Add a **SKU**, **Vendor** and **Description** if you want. Pick a **Category**, or **Uncategorized**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the recurrence">
    Pick **One-time**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Annual** or **Custom** in **Recurrence**. New products start as **One-time**. For **Custom**, type **Interval (months)**, a whole number from 1 to 60. For example, 6 means every six months. The price is the amount for one billing occurrence.

    <img src="/images/product/budget-product-recurrence.png" alt="Product recurrence menu: One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual and Custom." width="456" height="332" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the product">
    Click **Create**. The dialog closes and the product appears in the table. **Create** stays unavailable until the name is filled in and any custom interval is valid.

    <img src="/images/product/budget-product-example.png" alt="New Product form with an illustrative managed backup service, a monthly price of 120 and annualized cost of 1,440." width="1024" height="1012" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the annualized cost

A recurring product shows **Annualized cost:** under the fields. Monthly multiplies the price by 12, Quarterly by 4, Annual by 1, and Custom by 12 divided by the interval. One-time products show no estimate.

This is only an estimate. For an interval that does not divide 12 evenly, it is not a count of bills in one year. A budget line's real schedule comes from its start month, term and overrides. Check the line's billing after you add the product. See [Add and edit budget line items](/user-guides/budgets/edit-line-items).

## Edit a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    Find the product, click **...** at the end of its row, then click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the fields">
    In the **Edit Product** dialog you can change **Name**, **SKU**, **Category**, **Vendor**, **Unit Price**, **Recurrence** and **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The dialog closes and the table refreshes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The edit form has no **Product Group**, currency, setup fee or company field. To set a **Product Group**, use [Bulk Edit](/user-guides/budgets/bulk-edit-products). Selecting another company does not move an existing product to it.

Edits change the catalog record only. They do not change prices already copied into budget lines, and they do not change the product in your provider.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Create** or **Save** is unavailable | Fill in the **Name**. For **Custom** recurrence, enter an interval from 1 to 60. Wait if a save is running. |
| **Failed to create product** or **Failed to update product** | Read the message, check the values, and try again. |
| The product has a zero price | A blank **Unit Price** saves as zero. Edit the product to set it. |
| A recurring service shows One-time | Choose its **Recurrence**. New products start as One-time. |
| You cannot find **Assign to Company Name only** | Select one company in the top bar, then open **New Product** again. It is not in **Edit Product**. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I make a product for one company?">
    Select the company in the top bar, click **New Product**, and tick **Assign to Company Name only**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I set a semiannual price?">
    Pick **Custom** recurrence and type 6 in **Interval (months)**. The price is for each six-month period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change a product's company later?">
    No. The edit form has no company field. Ask your MSP admin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does saving a product update budgets or my provider?">
    No. It changes the catalog record only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
