Use the **Overview** tab of **Budget Details** to read one budget: its details, its cost lines grouped by category, and its totals. This guide is for MSP staff and for company users whose MSP has given them budget access.

## Before you start

- You need budget read access. Your MSP admin sets this in your role.
- The **Actuals** tab only appears if your role can also view invoices.
- Controls such as **Budgets Menu** and **Section order** only appear if your role can edit budgets.

## Review a budget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the budget">
    [Find the budget](/user-guides/budgets/find-budgets), click **...** on its row, then click **View**. **Budget Details** opens on **Overview**.

    <Frame caption="Budget Details opens on Overview. Actuals needs invoice access as well as budget access.">
      <img src="/images/product/budget-view-tabs.png" alt="Budget Details tabs: Overview, Monthly Breakdown and Actuals." width="690" height="86" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the details">
    The page header shows the budget title and its status. Below it, read **Company**, **Fiscal Year**, **Period Type**, **Currency** and **Author**. **Author** shows a dash when nobody entered one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the notes">
    If the budget has notes, they appear above the details. Notes are not private. They also print in the PDF and Excel exports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the three totals">
    Read **Total**, **Approved** and **Pending** in the summary bar. **Total** adds every line, including rejected lines. **Pending** adds lines with Pending or Draft status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the lines by category">
    Each category has a heading, its lines, and a subtotal. Every line shows **Description**, **Billing**, **Qty**, **Unit Price**, **Total** and **Status**. **Total** is quantity times unit price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the billing schedule">
    **Billing** shows the recurrence (One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual or Every N months) and the start month, such as **Starts Mar**. Open **Monthly Breakdown** to see when each charge falls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a linked Planner item or goal">
    Some lines show **Linked to:** and a name under the description. Click the name to open that Planner item or goal in a new tab. You set these links in the budget editor with the **Link to Planner Item or Goal** list.

    <img src="/images/product/budget-view-linked-to-preview.png" alt="Budget IT Infrastructure 2026 with Linked to entries for a Planner item and two goals under their line descriptions." width="1440" height="960" />

    The Planner icon marks a Planner item. The target icon marks a goal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose another view

| Tab | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Read details, categories, quantities, prices and line statuses. |
| **Monthly Breakdown** | See scheduled costs for January to December, monthly totals and saved overrides. |
| **Actuals** | Compare an approved budget with synced customer invoice spend. |

Opening a budget always starts on **Overview**. Switching tabs does not save or approve anything.

## Understand totals and statuses

- A line's **Total** is quantity times unit price for one charge. A monthly line with quantity 10 at 20 USD shows 200 USD. Twelve monthly charges come to 2,400 USD, which you see in **Monthly Breakdown**.
- An **Approved** or **Archived** budget is locked. Its lines can still carry different statuses, such as Rejected.
- **Overview** has no controls to approve or edit a line. Staff change line statuses in the budget editor. See [Approve and lock a budget](/user-guides/budgets/approving-and-locking-budgets).

## Print or export the budget

Click the **...** button in the page header. It lists **Help**, then **Print**, **Export PDF** and **Export Excel**. Users who can edit a Draft or Pending Approval budget with more than one category also see **Section order**. See [Download or print a budget](/user-guides/budgets/export-and-print-budgets) and [Arrange budget sections](/user-guides/budgets/arrange-budget-sections).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **Actuals** tab is missing | Your role cannot view invoices. Ask your MSP admin. |
| **Actuals** says to approve the budget first | Actuals only work on an Approved budget. |
| **Total** is higher than the list's **Total** | **Overview** counts rejected lines. The list does not. |
| **Total** looks lower than the yearly cost | **Total** counts one charge per line. Open **Monthly Breakdown**. |
| A **Linked to:** name is missing | The line has no link, or the linked item is not available to you. |
| The page sends you back to the list | The budget is outside your company scope, or your access changed. Ask your MSP. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are budget notes private?">
    No. Notes show on **Budget Details** and print in the PDF and Excel exports. Do not put MSP-only comments there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the Overview Total add up recurring charges for the year?">
    No. It adds quantity times unit price for each line once. **Monthly Breakdown** applies the schedule across the year.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I approve a line from Overview?">
    No. Overview only shows line statuses. Staff with the right permission change them in the editor.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can an Approved budget contain rejected lines?">
    The budget status and each line status are separate. Read the line statuses instead of assuming every line is approved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Export Excel on the Actuals tab export the reconciliation table?">
    No. Print and the exports always produce the budget document, whichever tab is open.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I export a budget?">
    Click **...** then **View** on the list row, then use the **...** button in the page header. The list row's **Export** item also downloads a PDF.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
