---
title: 'Adjust a budget amount for one month'
description: 'Set, review or clear individual monthly overrides without changing the line’s price or recurrence.'
icon: 'calendar-pen'
---

A monthly override replaces the calculated amount for one line in one month. Use it when a planned charge differs from the normal schedule. It does not change the line's quantity, unit price, recurrence or start month. It does not record a payment or edit an invoice.

## Before you start

- You need budget edit access (the **write_budgets** or **manage_budgets** permission). Company users get this only if their MSP grants it.
- The budget must be **Draft** or **Pending Approval**. Approved and Archived budgets are locked.

## Set an amount for one month

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Monthly Breakdown">
    Click **Budgets**, click **...** on the budget's row, then click **View**. Select the **Monthly Breakdown** tab.

    <Frame caption="Monthly Breakdown sits between Overview and Actuals.">
      <img src="/images/product/budget-view-tabs.png" alt="Budget Details tabs with Overview, Monthly Breakdown and Actuals." width="690" height="86" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the line and month">
    Scroll sideways to the month you want. Check the line description in the left column. If amounts are plain text instead of buttons, you cannot edit this budget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the amount">
    Click the amount, or the dash if the month has no charge. The cell becomes an input.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the new amount">
    Enter a number that is zero or higher, with no currency symbol or thousands separator. For example, type **1250.50**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave the cell to save">
    Press **Enter**, press **Tab**, or click outside the cell. The change saves right away and a **Saved.** message appears. There is no page-level **Save** for overrides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the totals">
    The line's **Year Total**, the category subtotal, **Monthly Total** and **Running Total** update. The overridden cell turns blue and bold. Hover over it to see **Override (computed: ...)** with the calculated amount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An override can add a charge to a month that had none. It changes only that month. For an ongoing change, edit the line's [billing settings](/user-guides/budgets/edit-line-items) instead.

## Cancel an unsaved edit

Press **Escape** while the input is open. The cell goes back to its saved amount. Clicking away or pressing **Tab** saves a changed value, and Escape cannot undo an amount that already saved.

## Clear an override

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the overridden cell">
    Click the blue cell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Empty the input">
    Delete every character so the input is blank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave the cell">
    Press **Enter** or **Tab**, or click outside. Wait for **Saved.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the calculation returned">
    The calculated amount shows again and the blue styling goes away. The amount comes from the line's current quantity, unit price, recurrence, start month and term. It can be zero if no charge falls in that month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Clearing one month does not clear other months. Typing the calculated amount saves it as an override. Only an empty field removes the override.

## Set a month to zero

Type **0** and leave the cell. The zero replaces the calculated charge and is saved as an override. A saved zero opens as **0** the next time. Tabbing out without changing it keeps the zero. The month displays as a dash, so look for the blue styling to tell a zero override from a month with no charge.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Month amounts are plain text | The budget is Approved or Archived, or your role cannot edit budgets. Ask your MSP admin. |
| An error appears when you leave the cell | Read the message, reload the budget and check the stored amount before you try again. |
| The cell shows a whole number but you typed cents | Cells round to whole currency units. The input and totals keep cents. Open the cell to see the exact value, then press **Escape**. |
| A negative amount is not accepted | Overrides must be zero or higher. |
| The Excel export does not show the override | The standard PDF and Excel documents have no monthly schedule. Use the Monthly Breakdown tab or print it. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When does an override save?">
    When you press **Enter** or **Tab**, or click out of the cell. Wait for **Saved.**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is zero the same as clearing the cell?">
    No. Zero replaces the calculated charge with zero. Clearing removes the override and restores the calculated charge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening a saved zero change it?">
    No. The input opens as 0, and leaving it unchanged keeps the zero.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I replace a positive override with zero?">
    Yes. Type 0 and leave the cell.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an override change the product price or other months?">
    No. It replaces one month's amount for one line.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does editing a month approve a rejected line?">
    No. The schedule includes every line. A line's status changes only in the budget editor.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I adjust an Approved or Archived budget?">
    No. Duplicate the budget to make a new Draft, then adjust the copy. See [Duplicate a budget](/user-guides/budgets/duplicate-budget).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
