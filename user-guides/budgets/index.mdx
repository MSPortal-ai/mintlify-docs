Budgets brings together planned costs, recurring services, approvals and forecasts for each company. MSP staff build and maintain the plans. Company users read the budgets their MSP gives them access to. Open **Budgets** in the sidebar to start.

## Who can do what

Your MSP admin sets these permissions in your role.

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **read_budgets** | See the Budgets list, read budgets, export and print. The **Actuals** tab also needs permission to view invoices. |
| **write_budgets** | Create, edit and duplicate budgets, import costs, change monthly amounts, and maintain the product catalog. Shows **Budgets Menu**, and opens **Forecast**. |
| **manage_budgets** | Everything above, plus approve, archive and delete budgets, delete saved lines, delete several budgets, and save or sync the forecast. |

Company users cannot grant themselves budget permissions. Ask your MSP if a button or tab is missing.

## The Budgets Menu

People with edit access see **Budgets Menu** at the top right of the Budgets pages.

| Menu item | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **All Budgets** | The list of budgets for the selected company, or every company in Global View. |
| **Products** | The reusable product catalog. |
| **Forecast** | The forecast worksheet for one company. |

There is no templates page. To start from an earlier plan, [duplicate a budget](/user-guides/budgets/duplicate-budget) or use an [import](#import-microsoft-365-licensing).

## Budget and line statuses

A budget has one status. Each line also has its own status.

| Budget status | Meaning | Editable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Being prepared | Yes |
| **Pending Approval** | Waiting for review | Yes |
| **Approved** | Locked as the baseline | No |
| **Archived** | Retired, still kept as a locked baseline | No |

| Line status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Being prepared |
| **Pending** | Waiting for approval |
| **Approved** | Accepted |
| **Rejected** | Declined. It is left out of the list **Total** and the Actuals baseline. |

An Approved budget can still hold Rejected or Pending lines. Exporting or printing never approves a budget.

## Approve and compare actual spend

- [Approve and lock a budget](/user-guides/budgets/approving-and-locking-budgets): the permanent baseline lock.
- [Track budget Actuals](/user-guides/budgets/tracking-budget-actuals): compare the baseline with synced customer invoices.
- [Resolve Actuals exceptions](/user-guides/budgets/resolving-actuals-exceptions): allocate unmatched spend.

Actuals needs an Approved or Archived budget and permission to view invoices. To change an Approved budget, duplicate it, edit the copy, approve the copy and archive the original.

## Maintain reusable products

Open **Budgets Menu > Products**.

- [Find and understand products](/user-guides/budgets/read-product-catalog)
- [Create and edit products](/user-guides/budgets/manage-products)
- [Bulk edit and delete products](/user-guides/budgets/bulk-edit-products)
- [Create products from Planner](/user-guides/budgets/import-products-from-planner)
- [Import and link integration products](/user-guides/budgets/import-products-from-integrations), for MSP staff

Catalog changes save right away. They do not add lines to a budget or change prices already copied into one.

## Forecast future costs

Select one company and open **Budgets Menu > Forecast**.

- [Read and adjust a company budget forecast](/user-guides/budgets/read-budget-forecast)
- [Sync a forecast from budgets or Planner](/user-guides/budgets/sync-budget-forecast)

The forecast is separate from budget approval and Actuals. Sync changes the worksheet. **Save** keeps the forecast rules.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which guide should a company user start with?">
    Start with [Find and open a budget](/user-guides/budgets/find-budgets), then [Read the budget overview](/user-guides/budgets/read-budget) and [Read Monthly Breakdown](/user-guides/budgets/read-monthly-breakdown).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do budget totals differ between views?">
    The list leaves out rejected lines. Overview counts every line once. Monthly Breakdown applies the billing schedule. Actuals uses the locked yearly cost of every line that is not rejected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does importing save changes automatically?">
    Importing into a budget needs **Save**. Importing into the product catalog saves at once. A forecast sync needs its own **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company administrator give themselves budget permissions?">
    No. The MSP manages permissions. Ask your MSP if an action or tab is missing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are budget templates available?">
    No. Duplicate a budget or use an import to start from earlier work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does downloading or printing approve a budget?">
    No. Approval is a separate step that locks the baseline.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
