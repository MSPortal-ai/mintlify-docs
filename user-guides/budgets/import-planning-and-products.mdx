Use **Planner Items**, **Strategic Goals**, and **Products & Services** in an editable budget to add proposed costs. Select an item's **+** control, review the resulting budget rows, and choose **Save**.

**Audience:** MSP staff and Company users whose MSP has granted budget editing access. The available company records and catalog choices depend on access and synchronized data.

## Find the source item

On a wide screen (1280 pixels or wider) the three source panels form a column beside Budget Line Items, below the year and month filter. Each panel header shows a chevron, the panel name and a count. Select a header to close or reopen that panel.

All three panels start open and share the column's height equally. Close one and the other two take its space; close two and the last fills the column. Each list scrolls inside its own panel, and the search box sits at the top of each open panel.

On a narrower screen, expand the corresponding panel above the line-item table instead.

- Use each panel's **Search** to narrow by title or product details.
- Planner Items excludes archived work and items marked Completed or Cancelled. An existing linked budget cost can remain after its source is no longer offered.
- The shared **Filter** for **Planner Items · Strategic Goals** defaults to the budget's fiscal year and all months. Choose **All** years to include work outside that year or without a matching year. Select a Month to narrow dated work; **Reset** restores the budget year and all months.
- The planning date filter changes the available source lists. It does not change the budget's fiscal year, products or rows already added to the plan.
- The **Show all tenant products** checkbox at the top of **Products & Services** is selected by default. Clear it to list only the company's own products. Selecting it lists products across the MSP's own tenant; it does not expose another tenant's catalog. Choose a product applicable to the target company.

## Add a Planner item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the planned work">
    Locate it in Planner Items using the year/month filter and search. Check its due date, status and estimated cost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add its costs">
    Choose the item's **+** control, labeled **Add to budget**. A Planner item with several positive cost components can add several budget lines. Review all of them, not just the first row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check billing and notes">
    The rows carry the Planner link, category and available cost details. The billing start month follows the Planner due date, falling back to an available start date or January. Review quantities, unit prices and recurrence, especially for incomplete estimates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the budget">
    Choose **Save** and check the saved plan and Monthly Breakdown. Adding to a budget does not mark the Planner work complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a strategic goal

Find the goal and select **+**. It adds a linked line with quantity **1**, unit price **0**, One-time recurrence and a January start. Enter the actual planned cost and billing schedule before saving. A goal's priority does not supply a price or approve the cost.

## Add a catalog product

Find the product in **Products & Services**, check its name, SKU, vendor and price, and select **+**. The main row starts with quantity **1**, the catalog unit price and default billing recurrence. Set the quantity you plan to buy and the correct Start month.

A product with a positive setup fee also adds a separate One-time setup row. Review that row separately; it starts with quantity 1 and a January start. Do not assume changing the main row's quantity automatically updates its setup row.

A matching existing budget category is used when possible. Review uncategorized rows, missing billing defaults and zero prices before saving. Catalog price changes do not automatically update costs already added to a budget.

<Note>
These **+** controls can add another copy when used again. They do not share Excel's description-based duplicate blocking or the licensing import's Already in budget check. Review the line table before selecting the same source again.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Planner item or goal is missing | Correct company, access, year/month filter and panel search; for Planner items, also check archive and completion status. Try All years and all months when the source has no matching date. |
| A goal adds a zero-price row | Enter the intended cost; the goal import does not estimate a price. |
| One click adds several rows | Review the Planner's separate cost components or the product's setup fee. |
| Product Save fails | Confirm the selected product applies to this company and that your access is still valid. |
| Duplicate costs appear | Remove unwanted proposed rows before Save; clicking + again can add another copy. |
| Source changed after importing | Review and update the budget separately. The imported values are a snapshot. |
| Imported row disappeared on navigation | Add to budget is not Save. Save the budget and wait for confirmation. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I add a Planner item to a budget?">
    Open an editable company budget, find the item in Planner Items, and choose its Add to budget plus control. Review the added costs and Save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do I only see some Planner items or goals?">
    The builder defaults those source lists to the budget's fiscal year. Check the shared year/month filter, panel search, company and permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the planning filter change my budget year?">
    No. It only changes which Planner items and goals are offered. Change Fiscal Year in Budget Details when you intend to change the budget itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did one Planner item add multiple budget lines?">
    A Planner item can contain several positive cost components. The importer creates separate lines so their prices and billing recurrence can be reviewed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does importing a goal create a zero-cost line?">
    A goal provides a link and title, not an estimated price. Enter the cost, quantity and billing schedule before saving.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a product add a separate setup fee?">
    A catalog product with a positive setup fee creates a separate One-time row. Review its quantity and start month as well as the main service row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can clicking Add to budget twice create duplicates?">
    Yes. Planner, goal and product plus controls can add another copy. Review the current line table before adding the same source again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a product place an order or change its catalog price?">
    No. It adds proposed budget costs. Save records the budget; it does not purchase the product or update the catalog price.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
