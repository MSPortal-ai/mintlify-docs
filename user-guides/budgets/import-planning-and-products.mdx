Use the **Planner Items**, **Strategic Goals** and **Products & Services** panels in the budget editor to add proposed costs. Click the **+** (**Add to budget**) button on an item, review the new rows, and click **Save**. This guide is for MSP staff and for company users whose MSP has given them budget edit access.

## Before you start

- You need budget edit access (the **write_budgets** or **manage_budgets** permission).
- The budget must be Draft or Pending Approval and must belong to a company.
- Planner items, goals and catalog products must already exist. See [Manage products](/user-guides/budgets/manage-products).

## Find the source item

On a wide screen the three panels sit to the left of **Budget Line Items**. On a narrow screen they are collapsed sections above the table, named **Planner Items**, **Strategic Goals** and **Current Software & Services**.

- Each panel has a **Search...** box.
- The **Planner Items · Strategic Goals** box at the top has a **Filter** button. It opens **Fiscal Year** and **Month** lists, and a **Reset** button. By default it shows the budget's fiscal year and all months. Choose **All** to include other years or items with no date.
- This filter only changes which planner items and goals the panels list. It does not change the budget's fiscal year or the rows already in the budget.
- Planner Items does not list items with status Completed or Cancelled.
- In **Products & Services**, the **all** checkbox is checked by default and shows tenant-wide products and this company's products. Clear it to see only this company's products.

## Add a Planner item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the item">
    Use the filter and search. Check the item's due date, status and estimated cost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **+** (**Add to budget**) on the item. A Planner item with several positive cost lines adds one budget line per cost, so check all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the rows">
    The rows link back to the Planner item and use its category and cost details. The notes start with **Imported from Planner:** and the item's title. The start month follows the item's due date, then its start date, then January. Check quantity, unit price and recurrence, mainly where the estimate is incomplete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Adding a row to a budget does not mark the Planner item done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a strategic goal

Find the goal and click **+**. It adds a line linked to the goal with quantity 1, unit price 0, One-time recurrence and a January start. Type the real cost and billing schedule before you save. A goal has no price of its own.

## Add a catalog product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the product">
    In **Products & Services**, check the product's name, SKU, vendor, category and unit price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **+**. The row starts with quantity 1, the catalog unit price and the product's default recurrence, starting in January. Set the real quantity and **Start month**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the setup line">
    A product with a setup fee also adds a separate One-time row named **Product name setup**. Its quantity is 1. Changing the main row's quantity does not change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The row takes the budget category with the same name as the product's category when there is one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Later catalog price changes do not update rows you already added.

<Note>
Clicking **+** again adds another copy. These buttons do not check for duplicates. Review the line table before you add the same item twice.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A Planner item or goal is missing | Check the company, the **Filter** year and month, and the search box. Choose **All** to see undated items. A Completed or Cancelled Planner item is not listed. |
| A goal added a zero-price row | Type the cost. Goals have no price. |
| One click added several rows | The Planner item has several cost lines, or the product has a setup fee. |
| A product is missing | Check the **all** checkbox, the search box and which company the product belongs to. |
| Duplicate rows appeared | Delete the extra rows before you save. |
| The rows vanished after you left the page | **Add to budget** does not save. Click **Save**. |
| The source item changed later | The rows are a snapshot. Update them in the editor. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the Filter change my budget year?">
    No. Change **Fiscal Year** under **Budget Details** for that.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did one Planner item add several lines?">
    Each positive cost on the item becomes its own line, so you can review each price and recurrence.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a product place an order?">
    No. It only adds a proposed budget line.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
