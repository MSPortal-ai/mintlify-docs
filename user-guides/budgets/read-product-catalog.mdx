Budget products are reusable starting values for budget line items. This guide shows you where the catalog is and how to read it. Opening the catalog does not order anything, change a subscription or add costs to a budget. It is for MSP staff and for company users whose MSP has given them budget edit access.

## Before you start

- **Budgets Menu** only appears for people who can edit budgets (**write_budgets** or **manage_budgets**).
- Changing the catalog (create, edit, delete, import) needs **write_budgets** or **manage_budgets**.
- Importing from integrations is for MSP staff only. Company users cannot use it.

## Open the catalog

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Budgets">
    Click **Budgets** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Click **Budgets Menu**, then click **Products**. The page is titled **Products** with the subtitle **Manage catalog items for budgeting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company">
    Look at the company selector in the top bar. With one company selected, the table shows that company's products and shared products. In Global View it shows products from every company you can access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the pages">
    The table shows 20 products per page, newest changes first. Use the page controls under the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The table has no **Company** column and no shared-only toggle. If you are not sure who a product belongs to, ask your MSP before you change it. A shared product can be used in many company budgets.

## Read the table

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The product's name. Similar names can be separate products. |
| **SKU** | An optional stock identifier. |
| **Category** | The budget category used when you add the product to a budget. |
| **Unit Price** | The price for one unit and one billing occurrence. |
| **Recurrence** | One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, or **Every N months** for Custom. |
| **Vendor** and **Product Group** | The supplier and an optional group. They can be blank. |
| **Created By** | The user, an integration import (for example **Autotask Import**), or **System**. |

The price shows a dollar sign. The table does not show a currency code and does not convert prices. Check the currency when you add a product to a budget.

## Find a missing product

The Products table has no working search, column filters, sorting or CSV export. Use the company selector and the page controls. Search exists inside the import dialogs and in the budget editor's **Products & Services** panel.

Check the company, the other pages, and whether the product was actually imported. A product you can see in the **Import from Integrations** dialog is not saved to the catalog until you import it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Budgets Menu** is missing | Your role cannot edit budgets. Ask your MSP admin. |
| A product is missing | Check the company selector and the other pages. |
| The list is empty after an error | Reload the page. An empty list after a failed load does not mean there are no products. |
| A provider product is not in Products | Import it from **Import from Integrations**. MSP staff only. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a company user maintain catalog products?">
    Only with budget write access from their MSP. Browsing the integration source list is limited to MSP staff.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do I see shared products in a company view?">
    A company view includes products for that company and shared products. There is no shared-only toggle.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I search or sort the product table?">
    No. Use the pages. Search works in the import dialogs and the budget editor panel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a catalog product appear in a budget by itself?">
    No. Add it from the **Products & Services** panel in the budget editor, review the line, and click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does editing a product price change existing budgets?">
    No. Budget lines keep the numbers copied when they were added.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
