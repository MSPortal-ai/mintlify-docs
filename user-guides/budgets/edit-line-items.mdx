Use the **Budget Line Items** table in the budget editor to enter planned costs, set how often they bill, and mark each line Pending, Approved, Rejected or Draft. This guide is for MSP staff and for company users whose MSP has given them budget write access.

## Before you start

- You need the **write_budgets** or **manage_budgets** permission. Deleting a saved line needs **manage_budgets**.
- The budget must be Draft or Pending Approval. Approved and Archived budgets are locked.
- Read-only users can [read the budget](/user-guides/budgets/read-budget) and ask their MSP for changes.

## Add a manual cost

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an editable budget">
    Click **Create New Budget**, or click **...** then **Edit** on a Draft or Pending Approval budget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Item">
    Click **Add Item** above the table. A new row opens for editing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the cost">
    Type a clear name in the **Description** box. Optionally open the **Link to Planner Item or Goal** list and pick one, and add **Notes (optional)**. Choose **None** in the list to leave it unlinked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category">
    Pick a category in the **Category** column, or leave it on **None**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set quantity and price">
    Type the **Qty** as a whole number above zero, and the **Unit Price** for one unit. **Line Total** updates to quantity times unit price. For example, five workstations at 1,200 USD give 6,000 USD. A unit price of zero shows as blank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the billing schedule">
    In the **Billing** column, choose the recurrence and a start month. For **Custom**, type the number of months in **Every N months**, from 1 to 60. Use **One-time** for a single purchase.

    <img src="/images/product/budget-line-recurrence-options.png" alt="Budget line Recurrence menu offering One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual and Custom." width="258" height="334" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the row">
    Click the check mark (**Confirm line item edits**) in the **Actions** column. Press the down arrow key to move to the next row, or to add a new row after the last one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** and wait for **Budget saved successfully.** Then check the budget and **Monthly Breakdown**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Billing choices

| Recurrence | Planned charges |
| --- | --- |
| **One-time** | Once, in the start month |
| **Monthly** | Every month from the start month |
| **Quarterly** | Every three months from the start month |
| **Annual** | Once, in the start month |
| **Custom** | Every N months from the start month, N from 1 to 60 |

**Line Total** is quantity times unit price for one charge. It does not add up the charges across the year. Ten licenses at 20 USD show 200 USD. A full year of monthly charges is 2,400 USD, which you see in [Monthly Breakdown](/user-guides/budgets/read-monthly-breakdown). Changing the budget's Period Type does not change a line's billing.

## Edit a line

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the line menu">
    Click the pencil button in the line's **Actions** column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit">
    Click **Edit**. The row opens with all its fields. Or double-click a description, category, billing, quantity or unit price cell to change only that field. Press **Enter** to finish a cell and **Escape** to cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the check mark, then click **Save** at the top of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The same menu has **Move Up** and **Move Down** to reorder the lines in the table, and **Delete**.

## Remove a line

Click the pencil button then **Delete**, or click the trash icon while the row is open. Then click **Save**. Removing a line you have not saved just drops it. Deleting a saved line needs **manage_budgets**, and the **Delete** item is missing without it.

## Remove a link

Open the row, open **Link to Planner Item or Goal**, choose **None**, and click **Save**. The Planner item or goal itself is not deleted.

## Change a line's Approval status

The **Approval** column holds a list with **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected** and **Draft**. This is the line's own status. It is separate from the budget's **Status**.

<Warning>
On a line that is already saved, choosing a value saves it at once and shows **Status updated**. You do not need to click **Save**. On a new line you have not saved, the value is stored with the first **Save**.
</Warning>

### Change several line statuses at once

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Update Statuses">
    In the Budgets list, click **...** on the budget's row, then click **Update Statuses**. On an Approved budget the item reads **Update Statuses (Locked)** and is unavailable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the statuses">
    The **Update Item Statuses** dialog lists every line with its quantity and total. Pick **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Draft** for each one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. A message such as **Updated 3 items** appears. This saves right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Approving a line does not approve or lock the whole budget. Once the budget is Approved or Archived, line statuses cannot change. See [Approve and lock a budget](/user-guides/budgets/approving-and-locking-budgets).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add Item** or **Edit** is unavailable | The budget is locked or your role cannot edit it. |
| The total did not change | Click out of the number box, check the value, then **Save**. |
| A line disappeared after you left the page | The budget was not saved. Click **Save** before you leave. |
| **Delete** is missing for a saved line | You need **manage_budgets**. Ask your MSP admin. |
| **Failed to update status** | Someone changed or locked the budget. Reload it and try again. |
| Monthly totals differ from **Line Total** | Check the recurrence, start month, term and monthly overrides. |
| A Planner item or goal is not in the link list | It belongs to another company, is not available to your role, or (for Planner items) is Completed or Cancelled. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I add a cost that is not in the product catalog?">
    Click **Add Item**, type the cost and billing, and click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the check mark save the budget?">
    No. It only closes the row. Click **Save** to store your changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I budget a quarterly service starting in April?">
    Set **Recurrence** to **Quarterly** and the start month to **Apr**, then **Save**. **Monthly Breakdown** shows April, July and October.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does rejecting a line remove it?">
    No. The line stays in the budget. A rejected line is left out of the Budgets list **Total** but counts in the Overview **Total** and in **Monthly Breakdown**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change billing after the budget is approved?">
    No. Duplicate the budget and change the Draft copy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do line edits change my PSA or order anything?">
    No. They change the MSPortal budget only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
