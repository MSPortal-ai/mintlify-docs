Use **Section order** to choose which category group comes first in a budget's **Overview** and in newly exported PDF and Excel documents. This guide is for MSP staff and for company users whose MSP has given them budget edit access.

## Before you start

- You need budget edit access (the **write_budgets** or **manage_budgets** permission).
- The budget must be **Draft** or **Pending Approval**.
- The budget must have at least two category sections. A section groups lines by category. Lines with no category sit under **Uncategorized**.

## Choose and save the order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the budget">
    Click **Budgets**, click **...** on the budget's row, then click **View**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Section order">
    Click the **...** button in the page header, then click **Section order**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move the categories">
    Click the up or down arrow on a row to move it, or drag the handle on the left. The first row cannot move up and the last row cannot move down. Each row shows the category, its item count and its amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the order">
    Click **Save order**. The dialog closes when the order is saved.

    <Frame caption="Save order stores the arrangement right away. You do not need to save again in the budget editor.">
      <img src="/images/product/budget-section-save-controls.png" alt="Save order button in the budget Section order dialog." width="202" height="80" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Overview">
    The category groups on **Overview** now follow your order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **Cancel** to close the dialog without saving. To change a saved order later, open **Section order** again, move the rows and click **Save order**.

## Where the order applies

| Place | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Category groups follow the saved order. |
| **Export PDF** and **Export Excel** | New downloads use the saved order. Files you already downloaded do not change. |
| **Monthly Breakdown** | Keeps its own category order. |
| Other budgets | Keep their own order. The setting belongs to this one budget. |

Section order moves whole groups. It does not move lines inside a group, change a line's category, or change any price, quantity, recurrence or status.

The amount on each row is quantity times unit price for every line in that category, including rejected lines. It is not a yearly projection.

A category added after you saved the order appears after the ordered ones. Open **Section order** again to place it. A category with no lines is not listed.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Section order** is missing from the **...** menu | Check that you opened **View**, the budget is Draft or Pending Approval, it has two or more sections, and your role can edit budgets. |
| Saving fails | Keep the dialog open, read the error and try again. Reload **Overview** to confirm the saved order. |
| The PDF still shows the old order | Download the PDF again. Existing files do not change. |
| A line sits in the wrong group | Change its category in the [budget editor](/user-guides/budgets/edit-line-items). |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I change the order of budget categories?">
    Open the budget with **View**, click **...** in the page header, then click **Section order**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company user arrange sections?">
    Only if their MSP grants budget edit access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Cancel undo an order I saved earlier?">
    No. Cancel only drops the changes you have not saved yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does this change every budget for the company?">
    No. The saved order belongs to one budget.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Uncategorized mean?">
    It groups lines that have no category. Assign a category in the budget editor if they belong elsewhere.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
