Use this guide to create a budget for one company, or to change a Draft or Pending Approval budget. It is for MSP staff and for company users whose MSP has given them budget write access. Company users with read access can [read the budget](/user-guides/budgets/read-budget) and ask their MSP to make changes.

## Before you start

- You need the **write_budgets** or **manage_budgets** permission. Approving a budget, and deleting saved lines or budgets, need **manage_budgets**. Changing a line's **Approval** status needs **Approve budget items**, which **Manage** includes and **Write** does not.
- A budget belongs to one company.
- Only Draft and Pending Approval budgets can be edited. Approved and Archived budgets are locked. To change one, [duplicate it](/user-guides/budgets/duplicate-budget).
- Choose the currency before you add costs. Changing it later does not convert any prices.

<Note>The editor uses the standard back arrow beside its title. On a phone, open **Actions** for Import, Export and Save. The arrow returns to the Budgets list.</Note>

## Create a budget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Budgets">
    Pick the company in the top bar if you want, then click **Budgets** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create New Budget">
    Click **Create New Budget** at the top right. If you are in Global View or have several companies selected, the page asks you to choose the company. Pick it in the **Company** list. This does not change the company selected in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the budget settings">
    The new budget opens the **Budget settings** panel, ready for you to type the title. Click **Done** when you finish, or click **Save** in the panel to save the budget now. The settings stay with the budget until you save it.

    - **Budget Title**: 2 to 120 characters.
    - **Author**: starts with your name. Type a name and press Enter, Tab or a comma to add it as a tag. Click **×** on a tag to remove it. MSP staff see matching people from their MSP while typing; you can also type any other name. A budget takes up to 5 authors of up to 48 characters each. The Budgets list, the budget page and exports show the authors as one list separated by commas.
    - **Status**: leave it on **Draft** while you build the plan.
    - **Fiscal Year**: starts on the current year. The list runs from two years back to seven years ahead.
    - **Period Type**: **Annual**, **Quarterly** or **Monthly**.
    - **Currency**: starts from the company's or your MSP's country, and falls back to USD.
    - **Notes**: up to 2,000 characters. Notes appear on the budget page and in PDF and Excel exports, and the helper text under the box says so. Write them for every reader.

    <Frame caption="Branch preview on the Demo tenant: a new budget's settings, with the signed-in person already added as the author.">
      <img src="/images/product/budget-builder-details.png" alt="Budget settings panel with an empty Budget Title, an Author tag for the signed-in person, and Draft status." width="461" height="242" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the costs">
    Click **Add Item** to type a cost, or add costs from the panels and the **Import** menu. See [Add and edit budget line items](/user-guides/budgets/edit-line-items).

    **Budget Line Items** fills the rest of the window and scrolls inside its own area. On a screen 1280 pixels or wider, the column beside it has **Planner**, **Goals** and **Products** tabs, and the open tab fills the column. See [Add Planner, goal and product costs](/user-guides/budgets/import-planning-and-products).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** at the top right, or press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on Mac). **Budget saved successfully.** appears and the new budget's editor opens, with the settings panel closed. Later saves update this budget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the monthly plan">
    Open the budget with **View** and read **Monthly Breakdown**. A line's **Line Total** is quantity times unit price for one charge. It is not the yearly cost of a recurring line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Typing a title, adding a row, or clicking a row's check mark does not save the budget. Click **Save** before you leave the page.

## Edit a saved budget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Click the budget's title in the Budgets list, or click **...** then **Edit**. The page shows the budget's title, with its fiscal year, period type, currency and status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Click **Settings** at the top right to change the title, author, status, fiscal year, period type, currency or notes. On a phone, **Settings** is in the **...** menu at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change what you need">
    Edit the settings, or edit lines with the pencil menu and **Edit**.

    <Frame caption="Branch preview on the Demo tenant: a saved budget with two authors.">
      <img src="/images/product/budget-settings-authors-saved.png" alt="Budget settings Author field with two author tags, each with a remove button." width="450" height="59" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. You stay on the page and a **Last saved** time appears. If you click **Save** in the **Budget settings** panel, the panel closes once the budget saves. If the save fails, the panel stays open and shows the error.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If someone else changed the budget while you were editing, the save is refused and an error appears. Reload the budget, compare it with your changes, and enter again the ones you still need.

## What saves when

| Action | When it saves |
| --- | --- |
| Title, author, fiscal year, period type, currency, notes | When you click **Save** |
| Add, edit, move or remove a line | When you click **Save**. Deleting a saved line needs **manage_budgets**. |
| Click the row check mark | It only closes the row editor. Click **Save** to store the row. |
| Change the **Approval** of a line already saved | Right away, with a **Status updated** message |
| Change the **Approval** of a new unsaved line | With the first **Save** |
| Change a month amount on **Monthly Breakdown** | When you leave the cell. See [Adjust a budget amount for one month](/user-guides/budgets/edit-monthly-overrides). |
| Set the budget **Status** to Approved | After you confirm the warning and click **Save**. This locks the budget for good. See [Approve and lock a budget](/user-guides/budgets/approving-and-locking-budgets). |

## Bring in existing costs

Use the **Import** menu for [Excel workbooks](/user-guides/budgets/import-excel), [earlier-year recurring services](/user-guides/budgets/import-previous-year) or [Microsoft 365 licensing](/user-guides/budgets/import-microsoft-365-licensing). Use the side panels for [Planner items, goals and products](/user-guides/budgets/import-planning-and-products). Each one adds rows to review. Click **Save** to keep them.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Create New Budget** is missing | Your role lacks budget write access. Ask your MSP admin. |
| Opening **Edit** sends you to the reader | Your role cannot edit budgets. |
| **Save** is disabled | Make a change first. It is also disabled while saving and on locked budgets. |
| Save shows an error | Read the message and check the title, currency, line values and links. A failed save stores nothing. |
| The budget is read-only | It is Approved or Archived. Duplicate it to get an editable copy. |
| **Export** does nothing in a new budget | Save the budget first. Exports use the saved budget. |
| An export misses recent edits | Save, then export again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can one budget cover several companies?">
    No. A budget belongs to one company. Create a budget for each company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company user create or edit budgets?">
    Only if their MSP gives them budget write access. Company users cannot grant it to themselves.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Period Type change each line's billing schedule?">
    No. Period Type labels the budget. Each line has its own **Recurrence** and **Start month**. Monthly Breakdown always runs January to December.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the currency convert my prices?">
    No. The numbers stay the same. Check existing and imported prices after you change it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Pending Approval approve the budget?">
    No. It is another editable status. Only the **Approved** status, set by someone with **manage_budgets**, locks the budget.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a line's status change when I did not click Save?">
    The **Approval** list on a saved line saves at once. Other edits wait for **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Export save my changes?">
    No. Save first, then export.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
