Use this page to find files, documents, folders, and links your MSP has organized in the Documentation Hub. It is for company users and MSP staff.

The **Documentation Hub**, also called the resource hub, is a page of resources your MSP arranges. Its title, sections, and contents are theirs to choose. Your portal might call it **Important Info**, show it as **Documentation > Hub**, or use it in place of Overview.

## Before you start

- You need Documentation read access.
- Your MSP must turn the hub on. If you see no hub, ask them where resources are kept.
- MSP staff can add resources. See [Add files and links to the Documentation Hub](/user-guides/documentation/add-files-and-links-to-hub). Company users should ask their MSP to add a resource.

## Find a resource

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the hub">
    Use the hub entry your MSP provides, or open **Documentation** and click the **Hub** tab. A standalone hub lives at `/documentation-hub`. If the standalone placement is off, that address sends you to Documentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the sections">
    Featured shortcuts, if your MSP set any, come first. Open a section to see its resources. Resources your MSP has not placed in a section appear under **More resources**. Section headings organize resources and are not always links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and filter">
    Type in **Search resources**. Search needs at least two characters to look beyond the hub's own items. Use the type filters for **Documents**, **Folders**, or **Links**, and **Company scope** to narrow the list. Clear the filters if nothing matches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the result">
    Click a document to read it, a folder to browse its documents, or a link to open its destination in a new tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The hub shows only the resources your MSP shares with your company scope and role. A link to an outside service can ask for its own sign-in.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| There is no **Hub** tab | Your MSP controls whether the hub is on and where it appears. It can be a sidebar page, replace Overview, or be off. Ask your MSP. |
| **No items match your current search and filters.** | Clear the type and company filters, check the company selector, and try a shorter keyword. |
| A link opens another website | Hub links can point to outside services. They open in a new tab and may need their own sign-in. |
| A resource is missing | Your MSP may not have shared it with your role. Ask them to check. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I add or pin resources myself?">
    No. Your MSP manages the hub's layout and content in Documentation settings. Ask them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the hub have a different name?">
    Your MSP can rename it. Look for a page such as Important Info.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a hub link give me access to the outside service?">
    No. A hub link is a shortcut. The destination still needs your own account and permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are my search results empty?">
    Clear the filters, check your company selection, and try a short title or keyword. Ask your MSP to confirm the resource is shared with your role.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
