Use this page to look up and copy a password your MSP shared with you. It is for company users and MSP staff who were given access to shared documentation passwords.

## Before you start

- Your MSP must connect Hudu or IT Glue and turn on password sharing for your company. In Hudu, they do this in the integration settings.
- Your role needs Documentation access and the shared-password permission. Access to Documents alone does not show Passwords.
- Passwords are never stored in MSPortal. Each time you click **Reveal**, MSPortal fetches the value from Hudu or IT Glue.

## Find and use a shared password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Passwords">
    Open **Documentation > Passwords**. Check the company selector if you have access to more than one company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the entry">
    Use the **Name** and **Company** column filters. Check the **Username**, **Folder**, and **URL** columns to make sure it is the account you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reveal the password">
    Click **Reveal** and wait for the value to load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show or copy it">
    Use the eye icon (**Show password** or **Hide password**) to show or hide the value, and the copy icon (**Copy password**) to copy it. The username has its own copy icon (**Copy username**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the service">
    Click the login URL when one is shown, and sign in to the service. Copying a password does not sign you in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A revealed password hides itself after 90 seconds. Click **Reveal** again if you still need it. Hiding the value on screen does not clear what you already copied to your clipboard.

Some shared asset records also have a password field. Its **Reveal** control follows the same access checks and can show the linked username and login URL.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **Passwords** tab is missing | Your MSP needs to connect Hudu or IT Glue, turn on password sharing, and give your role the permission. Ask them to check all three. |
| **No Shared Passwords** | Password sharing is not set up for your company. Your MSP configures it per company in the Hudu integration settings. |
| **Failed to reveal password** | Tell your MSP the entry name and the error. Do not include the password itself. |
| **Failed to copy** | Your browser blocked clipboard access. Reveal the password and copy it by hand. |
| The password vanished | It hid itself after the timeout. Click **Reveal** again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I see an entry but not reveal it?">
    The entry and its live value can have different availability. Your MSP should check the sharing rules, your role and company scope, and the provider connection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change a shared password here?">
    No. This view is for reading and copying. Ask your MSP to update the password in the service and in their documentation provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding a password clear my clipboard?">
    No. The eye icon only changes what shows on screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Passwords to reset my own portal password?">
    No. These are credentials shared through your MSP's documentation system. They are separate from your portal sign-in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
