Use this page to read the IT records your MSP shares with you: assets, configurations, procedures, and inspection results. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Documentation read access.
- **Assets** and **Procedures** need Hudu or IT Glue. **Configurations** needs IT Glue. **Inspectors** needs Liongard. **Intune** needs CIPP with **Document Intune policies** turned on and, for company users, sharing turned on by the MSP. Your MSP controls connections, sharing, and company scope.
- These are records from your documentation tools. For device monitoring and device actions, use [Devices](/user-guides/devices).

## Browse assets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Assets">
    Open **Documentation > Assets** and check the company selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a layout">
    In the **Asset Types** list on the left, click a layout such as servers or laptops. Click **All Layouts** to clear it. Use **Search asset types...** to find a layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the asset">
    Use the column filters, such as **Name** and **Company**. Click the asset's name to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the details">
    An asset can show its company, layout, update time, manufacturer, model, serial number, and the fields your provider defines. Empty fields are left out. A protected password field has its own **Reveal** control, which needs [shared-password access](/user-guides/documentation/shared-passwords).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View IT Glue configurations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Configurations">
    Open **Documentation > Configurations**. The tab appears only when your MSP uses IT Glue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the table">
    Use the column filters. Columns include **Name**, **Company**, **Type**, **Status**, **Hostname**, **Primary IP**, **Serial Number**, **Manufacturer**, **Model**, **Operating System**, **MAC Address**, **Asset Tag**, **Location**, **Contact**, **Warranty Expires**, and **Updated**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the record">
    Click a linked name to open the record in IT Glue in a new tab. You need your own IT Glue access. A name without a link has no IT Glue URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Configurations are read-only here. You cannot edit them from this page.

## Read a procedure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Procedures">
    Open **Documentation > Procedures** and find the procedure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Click its name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the tasks">
    Read the description, the **Tasks** list, and the summary, such as **3 of 5 tasks completed**. A green check marks a done task, and an empty circle marks one that is not done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The task marks show the status recorded in your provider. You cannot tick tasks here. Ask your MSP to update or complete the procedure in the source system.

## Review Liongard inspectors

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Inspectors">
    Open **Documentation > Inspectors**. The tab appears when Liongard is connected and data is shared with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an inspector type">
    In **Inspector Types** on the left, click a type. The main area lists its systems with **System Name**, **Company**, **Last Inspected**, and **Status**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a system">
    Click a system. Check **Last inspected** before you read the results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the metrics">
    Click **Passing**, **Failing**, or **Warning** to filter the metrics. Click the active one again to remove the filter. Type in **Search metrics...** to search by name or value. **Expand** or **Collapse** shows or hides the detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    Read **Security**, **Inspected Metrics**, and **Recent Changes**. Use the back arrow beside the system title to return to the list when the inspector type has more than one system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Inspector types mapped to devices appear on the device pages instead. This tab covers service and environment inspectors.

## Review Intune configuration

<Note>Intune documentation is opt-in. MSP staff turn it on with **Document Intune policies** in the **Choose data** step of the [CIPP integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-cipp-integration#part-3-choose-data). It is off by default, and policies are documented during the nightly CIPP sync.</Note>

Open **Documentation > Intune** when your MSP manages Microsoft Intune through CIPP. The tab appears once **Document Intune policies** is on. The table lists each company's Intune policies with **Policy**, **Company**, **Type**, **Platform**, **Assigned to**, **Last modified** and **Changed**, which shows when the portal last detected a change. Use the company selector or the table filters to narrow the list.

Select a policy's name to open its details:

- **Overview** shows the description, template, the groups the policy is assigned to or excluded from, and when it was last modified.
- **Settings** lists the configured settings as readable names and values. Use **Filter settings** to find one. Secrets, certificates and scripts are never stored or shown; they appear as **Hidden (secret)**, **Hidden (certificate)** or **Hidden (script)**. For administrative templates and endpoint security intents, CIPP does not report individual settings, so only the name, assignments and dates are documented.
- **Changes** compares each documented version with the one before it and shows the old and new values.

To print or save one company's Intune documentation as a PDF, select exactly one company in the company selector and choose **Export**. The export is titled **Intune configuration:** followed by the company name, and lists the company's policies and the changes detected in the last 90 days. Until one company is selected, **Export** is unavailable and shows **Select one company to export its Intune documentation**.

Company users see this tab only when the MSP turns on **Show Intune documentation to company users** in **Settings > Documentation > Intune**, and then only for their own company. This sharing switch is separate from **Document Intune policies** and is off by default.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A tab is missing | The provider is not connected, sharing is off, or your role lacks access. Ask your MSP admin. |
| **No Asset Layouts** | Asset layouts must be turned on in the documentation provider before assets sync. |
| **No Configurations** | Configurations sync from IT Glue for companies mapped to an IT Glue organization. Ask your MSP to map yours. |
| **No Procedures** | Procedures appear after they sync from your provider. |
| **No Inspectors** or no inspectors for a company | Your MSP must map Liongard environments to your company and choose which inspectors to share. See [Liongard Documentation Visibility](/user-guides/settings/liongard-visibility). |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit an asset or configuration here?">
    No. Ask your MSP to correct the source record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I tick off a procedure task?">
    No. The page shows recorded status only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do two asset types show different fields?">
    Each provider layout defines its own fields. Only filled-in fields appear.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are inspection results live?">
    No. Use the last inspected and updated times to judge how fresh they are.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When does an Intune change appear?">
    When **Document Intune policies** is enabled for the CIPP integration, the portal documents policies during the nightly CIPP sync. A change made in Intune appears after the next sync, with the date the portal detected it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are workstation or server inspector details?">
    On the matching device in **Devices**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
