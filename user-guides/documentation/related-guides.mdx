Related guides are documents the AI Assistant offers while a ticket is being drafted, so the requester can try a known fix before the ticket is picked up. The ticket is still created. You import the documents that take part, AI writes a description, keywords and matching rules for each one, and a person reviews that text before any requester sees it.

## Before you start

- You need the **Manage** permission on **Documentation** in the **Settings** section of your role. Check it in **Settings > Users & Roles**.
- AI must be on for your portal (**Settings > Tenant > AI**). Without it, you can still edit, enable and review guides, but **Import** and **Regenerate metadata** stay off.
- The documents come from folders you already have in Documentation: portal pages, Hudu articles or IT Glue documents. Images inside IT Glue documents do not render in MSPortal.
- Company users only ever see guides whose document they can already open, following the same folder and document sharing rules as the **Documents** tab. Share the folder with the right client roles before expecting clients to see its guides.

## The Related guides table

Open **Settings > Documentation** and click **Related guides**. The tab is one table of your guides. Each row shows the document, its first three keywords, its review status and an **Enabled** switch. Filter any column from its header, and use **Columns** to add the **Description** column. When a guide has more than three keywords, a **+N** button follows them. Point at it to see the rest, or click it to open the guide.

## Import a folder's documents as guides

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Import">
    Click **Import** above the table. The **Import guides** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a folder">
    Pick a folder of shared documents in **Folder**. Only folders that contain documents are listed. The line below tells you how many documents the folder holds and how many are already imported. Documents that are already imported are always skipped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import the documents">
    Click **Import N documents**. AI reads each document and writes a description of one to three sentences, up to fifteen keywords, and a **When to suggest** rule. The button shows progress while it works through the folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    The dialog closes when every document is imported, and each one appears in the table with **Needs review**. A document that could not be processed is listed in the dialog so you can import the folder again to retry it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A guide is suggested to requesters only once it is both reviewed and enabled.

## Review and edit a guide

Requesters never see a guide until a person has approved it. Click a guide's row to open it in a pane beside the table; the open row is highlighted. On a phone, the pane replaces the list, and **Back to list** returns to it.

The top of the pane shows the document's name, its folder and company, and badges for its review status and whether it is enabled. Use the arrows beside the position, such as **2 of 3**, to move through the guides in the table's current filter and sort order. **Open document** opens the document in a new tab so you can compare it with the text.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the description">
    Under **Shown to requesters**, **Description** is required. Requesters see it with the guide, and the AI also reads it to decide whether the document fits a request. Keep it to the problem the document solves and the product it covers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune the keywords">
    Under **Used only by the AI to match requests**, **Keywords** are the words a requester would type: symptoms, product names, error text. A request that contains two keywords, or one multi-word phrase, always shows the guide; otherwise the AI picks from the best keyword matches. Add a keyword by typing it and pressing Enter; remove one with its ×.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set when to suggest it">
    **When to suggest** tells the AI which requests the guide fits and which it does not, for example "Sign-in errors in Procore only; not for other apps." Requesters never see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve it">
    Leave **Show as a related guide** on. A guide that needs review shows the hint **Our AI wrote this metadata. Check it against the document, then approve it.** Click **Approve and next** once the text matches the document. That saves your edits, marks the guide reviewed and opens the next guide that still needs review. When no other guide needs review, the button reads **Approve**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Save** keeps your edits without changing the review status. A reviewed guide shows only **Save**.

The pane's **⋯** menu holds:

- **Regenerate metadata**: has AI rewrite the guide from the current document and sets it back to **Needs review**. It is off while you have unsaved edits or when AI is off.
- **Mark as needs review**: for a reviewed guide, saves your edits and moves it back to **Needs review**, which stops it being suggested.
- **Delete**: removes the guide. The document itself is kept.

Use the **Enabled** switch in the table to pause a guide without losing its text; the switch saves immediately and keeps the review.

### Move through guides quickly

While the pane is open and you are not typing in a field, press the Up and Down arrows (or **k** and **j**) to open the previous or next guide, and Esc to close the pane. If you switch guides with unsaved edits, MSPortal asks **Discard your unsaved changes?** Choose **Keep editing** to stay, or **Discard** to drop the edits and move on.

## Update several guides at once

Tick the rows you want, or the header checkbox for every row, then open **Actions (n)**.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Enable** | Turns on the selected guides. Their review status stays as it is. |
| **Disable** | Turns off the selected guides without losing their text or review. |
| **Mark reviewed** | Marks the selected guides reviewed by you. |
| **Regenerate metadata** | Asks you to confirm, then has AI rewrite the description, keywords and **When to suggest** rule from the current document, replacing any edits. Each guide goes back to **Needs review**. Needs AI to be on. |
| **Delete** | Asks you to confirm how many guides to remove. The documents themselves are kept. |

## What requesters see

When a requester describes a problem on the **New Ticket** page or to the AI Assistant, MSPortal shows up to three matching guides in a **Related guides** card with the line "These guides may solve this without waiting. Your ticket is still created." Each guide shows its reviewed description. Only reviewed, enabled guides whose document the requester may open are offered.

Clicking a guide opens the document inside MSPortal. On the **New Ticket** page it opens in a new tab, so the form keeps what the requester typed. The ticket draft continues as normal either way.

Guides are matched against the request text only. A request that contains two of a guide's keywords, or one of its multi-word phrases, always shows that guide. AI ranks the remaining guides on their description and **When to suggest** rule, so rely on keywords when a document must always appear for a known phrase.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings > Documentation** or its **Related guides** tab is missing | Your role lacks the **Manage** permission on **Documentation** settings. Ask an admin to update your role in **Settings > Users & Roles**. |
| **Import** in the dialog is disabled | Choose a folder, and check that AI is on for your portal. If the dialog says every document in the folder is already imported, use **Regenerate metadata** on those guides instead. |
| A guide never appears for requesters | It must be **Reviewed** and **Enabled**, and the requester must be able to open the document. Check the folder's sharing on the **Documents** tab and try a request containing two of its keywords. |
| A client sees a guide they should not | Guides follow document sharing. Remove the client role from the folder or document on the **Documents** tab. |
| The description is wrong | Edit it, or select the guide and choose **Regenerate metadata**, then review it again. Images in IT Glue documents are not read. |
| **Save** or **Approve** is disabled in the pane | Fill in **Description**. It is required. **Save** also stays off until you change something. |
