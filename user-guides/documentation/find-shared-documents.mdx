Use this page to find and read documents your MSP has shared with you. It is for company users and MSP staff.

The **Documents** tab can hold pages and uploaded files made in the portal, plus documents shared from Hudu or IT Glue. Portal documents do not need either integration.

## Before you start

- You need Documentation read access.
- Your MSP decides which documents you can open. A direct link does not give access to a restricted document.

## Find a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Documents">
    Open **Documentation > Documents**. If you can reach more than one company, check the company selector first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a folder">
    Click a folder in the folder list to narrow the results. **All Documents** clears the folder. **Unfiled Documents** shows documents with no folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by name">
    Type in the **Name** column filter. Check the other column filters if an expected document is missing. The table shows 50 documents per page. Changing the company resets the folder and filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the document">
    Click its name. The **Source** and **Type** columns tell a provider document from a portal **Page** or **File**. A paperclip means it has attachments. For an uploaded file, click **Preview** in the row for a quick look.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read and navigate

The document page shows its title and, when available, its company, folder, and last update. Long articles show a table of contents (**On this page**) on large screens. Click a heading to jump to it, and use the page's back control to return to Documents.

An uploaded file shows on its document page. PDF, Word, CSV, and Excel files are supported. The page can show only part of a large workbook or dataset, so click **Download** in the page header for the complete file with its original formatting.

## Open or download attachments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Attachments">
    On a large screen, look for **Attachments** beside the article. On a small screen, scroll below the content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open or download">
    Click an attachment's name to open it. Use its download control to save it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a file has no preview, use its download option when it is available and you have access.

IT Glue files open in IT Glue. MSPortal cannot preview or download them, because IT Glue only gives files to signed-in IT Glue users. A document that is only an IT Glue file shows **Open in IT Glue** in place of its content, and its attachments open IT Glue in a new tab. You need your own IT Glue sign-in.

## Export an article to PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Export PDF">
    If your MSP turned export on, the article header shows **Export PDF**. Click it to open a print view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print to PDF">
    Choose your browser's PDF destination or a printer. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for your portal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The export uses the article's text. **No article content to export** means the article has no text body. For an article that is mainly an attached file, download the original file instead.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A document is missing | Check the company selector, folder, and column filters. Then ask your MSP to share it with your role or company. |
| **No visible documents** | Nothing is shared for your scope. Ask your MSP to share provider documents or add portal pages and uploads. |
| A spreadsheet preview is incomplete | Preview can show one sheet or limited rows. Download the original. |
| An IT Glue attachment opens IT Glue | That is expected. Sign in to IT Glue with your own account, or ask your MSP for a copy. |
| **Export PDF** is missing or fails | Your MSP controls export. Allow pop-ups, or download the original file for a file-based document. |
| **File not available** | The attachment has no link. Ask your MSP for a copy. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do we need Hudu or IT Glue to use Documents?">
    No. Your MSP can create pages and upload files in the portal. Providers add records shared from those systems.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company users upload or edit portal documents?">
    No. Ask your MSP to add or correct content.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are attachments on a phone?">
    Below the article content. On large screens they sit in the side panel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send a portal document link to someone else?">
    The recipient needs access too. A portal link does not change permissions. Ask your MSP if you need an approved public link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
