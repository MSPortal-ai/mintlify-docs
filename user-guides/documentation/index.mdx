Documentation brings together the resources your MSP shares with you. It holds portal pages and uploaded files, records shared from Hudu or IT Glue, and an optional resource hub. Your role, your company scope, and your MSP's sharing settings decide what you see.

## Before you start

- You need Documentation read access. Passwords and Domains need their own permissions.
- Portal pages, uploaded files, and the Hub do not need Hudu or IT Glue. Assets, Configurations, Passwords, and Procedures need a connected provider.
- Your MSP chooses what is shared. See [Understand your role and company access](/user-guides/account/understand-your-access).

## Open Documentation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the module">
    Click **Documentation** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company selector">
    Choose the company whose documentation you want. Changing it resets the current folder and filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    Click a tab in the page header. Only the tabs that apply to you appear. See the table below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add files or link to files

Yes. You can add files or link to files in MSPortal. MSP staff use the **Documentation Hub** to give clients one place for files, documents, folders, and useful links.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the file">
    Open **Documentation > Documents**, click **Upload**, and add the file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to the Hub">
    In **Settings > Documentation > Hub**, click **+** in **Featured** or a section, choose **Document**, pick the file, and click **Add resource**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link to a file stored elsewhere instead">
    Choose **Link**, enter a **Link title**, paste the file's sharing URL, and click **Add resource**. The file stays in its own service, which still controls who can open it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The full steps are in [Add files and links to the Documentation Hub](/user-guides/documentation/add-files-and-links-to-hub).

## Tabs

| Tab | What it shows | When it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hub** | Curated documents, folders, and links | Your MSP turned the Hub on. It can also be a separate page or replace Overview. |
| **Documents** | Portal pages, uploaded files, and shared provider articles | You have Documentation access. |
| **Assets** | Shared asset records | Hudu or IT Glue is connected and you have Documentation access. |
| **Configurations** | IT Glue configuration inventory | IT Glue is connected and you have Documentation access. |
| **Passwords** | Credentials shared with your role and company | Hudu or IT Glue is connected, password sharing is on, and you have the password permission. |
| **Procedures** | Provider runbooks and their recorded task status | Hudu or IT Glue is connected and you have Documentation access. |
| **Inspectors** | Shared Liongard service and environment records | Liongard is connected and you have Documentation access. |
| **Domains** | Your company's domain inventory | You have the domain documentation permission. |

## Email security checks for client domains

This feature is **off by default**. An internal administrator enables **Email domain security checks** in Features for a tenant or a personal pilot. Until enabled, the email-security and **Problems** columns, row checkboxes, **Check now**, **Create Planner item**, the report block and the **Domain check** choice are hidden. Turning it off stops new checks and hides stored DNS results without deleting them.

<Note>
  Branch preview: the **Problems** column, bulk actions, Planner items and **Export CSV** described below, and their screenshots, show an upcoming Domains update. The table may differ from your live portal until it is released.
</Note>

### Read a domain's email security

Open **Documentation > Domains** and use the company selector to choose your scope. Checks run daily over public DNS without a security integration.

- **Email security** shows **Passing**, **Needs attention**, **Failing**, **Domain not found** or **Not checked yet**, with **SPF**, **DMARC** and **DKIM** badges beside it. Use the column filter to show one status.
- **Problems** lists what to fix, most serious first, for example **No DMARC record**, **DMARC not enforced (p=none)** or **SPF allows any sender (+all)**. It leaves out optional records most domains never publish (MTA-STS, TLS-RPT, DNSSEC and BIMI) and a DKIM key that could not be found.
- Click the status badge to open the detail sheet with every check: SPF, DMARC, DKIM, MTA-STS, TLS-RPT, DNSSEC and BIMI, each with its explanation and DNS record.
- A star beside the domain name marks the company's primary domain. **Sources** and **Primary** are hidden by default; turn them on under **Columns**.

DKIM can only be discovered at known mail-provider selectors. **Unknown** means no key was found at those selectors; it does not prove DKIM is absent or broken.

### Check domains again

To check one domain now, open its row's **…** menu and select **Check now**. To check several, select their checkboxes and choose **Actions (N) > Check email security**. One request covers up to 100 domains; with more selected, the action is unavailable.

Checking needs write or manage access to documentation domains and active billing. The checks are queued, and the list watches for updated results for two minutes. A failed DNS lookup keeps the previous result.

### Create Planner items from the results

You need **Write Planner** or **Manage Planner** and active billing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the domains">
    For one domain, open its row's **…** menu and select **Create Planner item**. For several, select their checkboxes and choose **Actions (N) > Create Planner items**. Up to 100 domains can be included.

    <Frame caption="Branch preview: the row menu has Check now and Create Planner item.">
      <img src="/images/product/domains-email-security-row-menu-preview-desktop.png" alt="Documentation Domains row menu open with Check now, Create Planner item and Set primary." width="2880" height="1920" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a quarter">
    The dialog lists each company with its selected domains. Choose the **Quarter** the work belongs in. The list offers the current quarter and the seven after it. An item in the current quarter is due today; a later quarter's item is due on that quarter's first day.

    <Frame caption="Branch preview: one Planner item for Scaling Ops covering its two selected domains.">
      <img src="/images/product/domains-email-security-planner-dialog-preview-desktop.png" alt="Create Planner item dialog with the Quarter set to Q4 2026 (Oct to Dec) and Scaling Ops listed with mspcheckmate.com and msportal.ai." width="2880" height="1920" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the items">
    Click **Create item** (or **Create N items**). A message confirms how many Planner items were created, and a second message says how many could not be created if any failed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each company gets one Planner item covering its selected domains. One domain gives an item titled **Email security: example.com**; several give **Email security for N domains**. Each domain's full check result goes in the item's private notes: its status, mail provider, DMARC enforcement, check date, and every check with its explanation and DNS record. The Planner assistant can read them when you plan the work. Company users cannot write private notes, so the items they create carry the results in the public notes instead.

The notes are a copy of the results when the item was created; later checks do not update them. Open the item in [Planner](/user-guides/planner/index) to review it or change its type, status and visibility.

### Export the domain list

Click **Export CSV** above the table. The file includes every domain that matches the company selector and your filters, across all pages, including hidden columns. When email security checks are enabled, it adds **Email security**, **SPF**, **DMARC**, **DKIM** and **Problems** columns. **Problems** lists the problems separated by semicolons.

<Accordion title="Before the feature is enabled">
  Domain records, **Export CSV** and the ordinary row actions remain available. Email security, **Problems**, the row checkboxes, **Check now** and **Create Planner item** appear only after the feature is enabled.
</Accordion>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A tab is missing | The integration is not connected, sharing is off, or your role lacks the permission. Ask your MSP admin. |
| A document or record is missing | Check the company selector, folder, and filters. Then ask your MSP to share it with your role or company. |
| **No visible documents** | Nothing is shared for your scope yet. Your MSP can share provider documents in **Settings > Documentation**, or create portal pages and uploads. |
| **Unable to load documentation** | Reload the page. If it keeps failing, report it through the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does my Documentation page have fewer tabs?">
    Tabs depend on connected providers, sharing settings, and your permissions. Ask your MSP about the resource you need.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Documentation access include passwords?">
    No. Passwords need a separate permission and password sharing turned on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company users manage the document library?">
    No. The MSP manages pages, uploads, folders, and sharing. Company users read what is shared with them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get an incorrect document fixed?">
    Give your MSP the document title, the company, and the correction. Provider records may need a change in Hudu, IT Glue, or Liongard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
