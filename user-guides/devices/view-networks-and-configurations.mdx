The **Devices** page can show **Networks** and **Configurations** tabs next to the device inventory. This guide is for company users and MSP staff who need to read them. MSP staff can also link a collector to a company location.

## Before you start

- **Networks** appears when your MSP has connected Domotz.
- **Configurations** appears when your MSP has connected IT Glue and there are IT Glue configurations in your company selection that are not linked to a device.
- You need access to Devices and to the company.
- Your MSP sets up integrations and company mappings. Company users can read these tabs but cannot change links.

## Check site networks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Devices > Networks">
    Click **Devices** in the sidebar, then the **Networks** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the site">
    Check the company selector. Use the table filters to find the **Site**, **Company**, or **Location** you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the measurements">
    Check **ISP**, **Download**, **Upload**, **Uptime**, **Devices online**, **Networks / VLANs**, and **Last synced**. A dash means the value is not available. It does not mean zero. A download average can appear under the latest value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Issues column">
    **Healthy** means no issues were counted. **Has issues** means some were. Point at the issue count to see a breakdown of **IP conflicts**, **Failing TCP sensors**, **Active alerts**, and **Devices with packet loss**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the devices at the site">
    Click the **Devices online** count. The inventory opens for the linked location, or for the company when no location is linked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Download and upload values are reported by Domotz. Opening the page does not run a speed test from your browser. **Healthy** only covers the issue types listed above, so check **Last synced** and tell your provider about readings that look wrong.

## Link a collector to a location (MSP staff)

You need manage access to Devices.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Edit in the Location column">
    Find the site row and click **Edit** in its **Location** cell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a location">
    Search and pick one of the company's locations. The link saves as soon as you pick it. There is no Save button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a link if needed">
    Open the same picker and choose **Remove link**. This also saves right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you close the picker without choosing, nothing changes. If the company has no locations, add them first. Company users ask their provider to change a link. If the picker says **Map to a company first**, the collector is not mapped to a company yet.

## Read IT Glue configurations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Devices > Configurations">
    Click **Devices**, then the **Configurations** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a configuration">
    Check the company selector, then filter by **Name**. A **Company** filter is available when you select several companies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the columns">
    Review **Type**, **Hostname**, **Primary IP**, **Serial Number**, and **Updated**. Use the column selector for more fields, such as **Manufacturer**, **Model**, and **Location**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page through the results">
    Use the pagination controls at the bottom of the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This tab only lists IT Glue configurations that are **not linked** to a device, so it is not a full copy of the inventory. For a configuration that is linked, open the device and use its **IT Glue** tab. MSP staff can open a configuration's IT Glue link when one exists. Company users see the name as text. The tab has no edit or import button.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Networks** tab | Ask your MSP to check that Domotz is connected. |
| No network rows | Check the company selector and that collectors are mapped to the company. |
| **Could not load network data** | This is a loading error, not an empty network. Reload, or ask your provider. |
| No **Configurations** tab | IT Glue may not be connected, or every configuration in this company selection is already linked to a device. |
| A configuration is missing | Check filters and the company selector, then check whether it is already linked to a device. |
| No **Duplicates** tab | Only MSP staff with manage access to Devices see it. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does another user see more tabs?">
    Tabs depend on integrations, data, company selection, and permissions. **Duplicates** is for MSP staff with manage access to Devices.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening Networks test my internet speed?">
    No. It shows speeds reported through Domotz.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Healthy mean the site has no problems?">
    No. It means none of the listed issue types were counted in the available data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Devices online open the whole company?">
    The collector is not linked to a location. Your MSP can link it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company admin change a collector's location?">
    No. Only MSP staff can. Ask your provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a linked device missing from Configurations?">
    The tab only lists configurations that are not linked. Open the device and check its **IT Glue** tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
