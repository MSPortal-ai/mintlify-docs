**Devices** brings together manual inventory records and equipment imported from connected tools. Use it to find a device, inspect the information reported for it, review warranty dates, and maintain supported inventory fields.

Company users see records permitted by their role and company scope. MSP staff can have additional management, import, duplicate-review, and provider controls. The MSP manages Company user permissions.

## Understand the views

| View | Availability and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Devices** | Inventory in your permitted company scope |
| **Networks** | Domotz-backed sites, measurements, reported issues, and location links |
| **Configurations** | Unlinked IT Glue configurations; the tab requires available rows in the current scope |
| **Duplicates** | MSP staff with device-management permission can review possible duplicate records |
| Device **Overview** | Available inventory and reported details |
| Device **Edit** | Device-management permission is required; supported saves also depend on the source |
| Device integration tabs | Cork/Liongard data or IT Glue integration availability |

If no optional destinations are available, the page can show only the inventory without a tab strip.

## Check the evidence behind a value

Use reported timestamps and source information when interpreting a device. Missing information does not mean a device is healthy, covered by warranty, or free of security issues. A warranty status depends on recorded dates; IP location is approximate and is not GPS tracking.

Some device details are source-controlled. Editing the portal does not necessarily change an external RMM/PSA record or the physical computer. After a save, reopen the record and verify the intended fields, especially after an error or later synchronization.

## Management actions

Authorized users can have row and selection actions such as adding inventory to a planner item or meeting, bulk updates, provider data refresh, and deletion. The available actions depend on both device permissions and access to the destination module. Check the selected count before applying a bulk action.

Duplicate merging is restricted to MSP staff. Removing an inventory record is different from wiping a device or uninstalling an agent. Provider synchronization and import settings should be reviewed when correcting integrated records.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can Company users see devices?">
    Yes, when their role and company scope permit access. The MSP decides their permission assignment. Viewing, creating, editing, and deletion are separately controlled by the available actions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I correct a serial number or warranty date?">
    Open the device and use **Edit** when available. Manual and integrated devices can support different updates. Follow the [editing guide](/user-guides/devices/add-and-edit-devices) and verify the stored result.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find equipment from a specific office?">
    Use the inventory's Location filter. In Networks, the Devices online link also opens inventory for the linked site, falling back to the company when no site is linked.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the inventory show live data from every provider?">
    It shows the data available from manual records and connected sources. Fields and freshness vary. Check timestamps and ask the MSP about missing or stale information.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company administrator merge duplicates?">
    No. Duplicate merging is an MSP staff operation requiring device-management access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does device access let me configure integrations?">
    No. MSP integration administration is separate from viewing inventory. Ask your provider to review mappings, imports, or synchronization.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
