**Devices** brings together manual inventory records and equipment imported from connected tools such as your RMM. Use it to find a device, read what is reported for it, check warranty dates, and keep supported fields up to date.

Company users see the devices their role and company allow. MSP staff can also manage devices, review duplicates, and import equipment.

## Before you start

- Click **Devices** in the sidebar. If it is missing, ask your MSP to check that the module is on and that your role includes it.
- Viewing, adding, editing, and deleting are separate. Adding, editing, and deleting need manage access to Devices.
- Use the company selector in the header to narrow the list to one company.

## The tabs on the Devices page

| Tab | When you see it | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Devices** | Always | Inventory for the companies you can see |
| **Networks** | Domotz is connected | Sites, speeds, uptime, and reported issues |
| **Configurations** | IT Glue is connected and some configurations are not linked to a device | Unlinked IT Glue configurations |
| **Duplicates** | MSP staff with manage access to Devices | Possible duplicate devices to review and merge |

If none of the optional tabs apply, the page shows only the inventory.

## Check a device

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Devices">
    Click **Devices** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the device">
    Use the column filters or the summary cards. See [Find and export devices](/user-guides/devices/find-devices).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the device Name">
    The panel opens on **Overview**. Tabs such as **Edit**, **Cork**, **Liongard**, **Backup**, and **IT Glue** appear when they apply. See [Understand device details](/user-guides/devices/understand-device-details).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check where the information came from">
    Look at the timestamps and the source. A blank value does not mean the device is healthy, covered by warranty, or free of security problems.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Some fields are controlled by the tool that imported the device. Editing them in MSPortal does not change the external tool or the physical computer. After you save, reopen the record and check the result.

## Use actions on several devices (MSP staff)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select devices">
    Check the boxes at the left of the rows. Checkboxes appear only if you have manage access to Devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Actions menu">
    It appears above the table and shows the selected count. Check the count before you continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    The menu can include **Bulk Update**, **Add to Planner**, **Add to Meeting**, **Merge Selected**, and **Delete Selected**. When all selected devices are Meraki devices it also offers **Refresh Meraki Status**. **Merge Selected** is for MSP staff only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A single device has the same kind of actions in its row menu: **Edit** (or **View Details** without manage access), **Add to Planner**, **Add to Meeting**, and **Delete**. Planner and meeting actions also need access to those modules. Deleting a device removes the portal record, not the computer or its agent.

## Warranty

The **Warranty End** column and the summary cards use the warranty dates on the record. A device with no dates has no warranty status. Use **Add to Planner** on a device to create a task for a renewal. See [Planner](/user-guides/planner/index).

## Custom fields

An MSP admin can add custom fields for devices in **Settings > Customization > Fields** by choosing the Device type. They then appear in the device's **Edit** tab.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No devices appear | Check the company selector and any column filters. Ask your MSP whether devices have been imported. |
| A device is missing | Search by **Serial Number**. Your MSP can check the integration's import settings. |
| A value looks old | Check **Last Seen** and the integration's last sync. Ask your MSP. |
| I cannot edit a device | You need manage access to Devices. Ask your MSP. |
| A tab is missing | Tabs depend on integrations, data, and permissions. See the table above. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can company users see devices?">
    Yes, when their role and company allow it. Editing and deleting are controlled separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I correct a serial number or warranty date?">
    Open the device and use the **Edit** tab if you have it. Follow [Add and edit device records](/user-guides/devices/add-and-edit-devices).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I find equipment from one office?">
    Use the **Location** column filter. In **Networks**, the **Devices online** count opens the inventory for the linked site.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the inventory live data from every provider?">
    No. It shows what manual records and connected tools have reported, as of their last sync.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company admin merge duplicates?">
    No. Merging is for MSP staff with manage access to Devices.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does device access let me change integrations?">
    No. Integration setup is separate. Ask your provider to review mappings, imports, or sync.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
