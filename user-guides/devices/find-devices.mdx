Use **Devices** to find equipment you can see, check its details, and review warranty dates. This guide is for company users and MSP staff with access to Devices.

## Before you start

- Your role and company selection decide which devices you see. Your MSP manages company user access.
- Seeing a device does not let you edit or delete it. Those actions need manage access to Devices.
- Devices come from manual records and from connected tools. Fields differ by source.

## Find a device

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the inventory">
    Click **Devices** in the sidebar. If tabs appear at the top, choose **Devices**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company selector">
    Company users see their own companies. MSP staff may be looking at several companies or all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Use the filters in the column headers. You can filter by **Name**, **Device Type**, **Manufacturer**, **Model**, **Serial Number**, **Location**, and more. Wait for the results to load. When you look for one device, filter on one identifying field at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the details">
    Click the device **Name**. The side panel opens on **Overview**. Check the company, identifying details, and dates before you use them in a support request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The table has pages. Use the controls at the bottom to move through them. If a column is missing, open the column selector, because some columns are hidden until you turn them on.

## Review warranty dates

The cards above the table are shortcuts.

| Card | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Devices** | Clears the warranty or device type shortcut |
| **Warranty Expired** | Shows devices whose recorded warranty end date has passed |
| **Expiring (90d)** | Shows devices whose recorded warranty ends within 90 days |
| **By Device Type** chips | Shows one device type. **Uncategorized** means no type is set |

Click an active card again to clear it. **Total Devices** does not clear your column filters or company selection. Clear those yourself if the list is still shorter than you expect. For other date ranges, use the **Warranty End** column filter.

A blank warranty date means none is recorded. It does not mean the device is covered or expired. Ask your IT provider to confirm warranty details.

MSP staff also see an **Unimported Devices** row under the cards when connected tools have devices waiting to be imported. Each chip opens that provider's import page.

## Export the inventory

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your scope and filters">
    Choose the company and filters that match the list you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export CSV">
    The button is in the table controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the file">
    Check your browser's downloads, open the file, and check the records before you share it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The export includes every matching device across all pages. It does not depend on which rows are checked or which page you are on. It uses its own set of columns, so hiding a column on screen does not remove it from the file. The columns include **Name**, **Company**, **Device Type**, **Manufacturer**, **Model**, **OS**, **Serial Number**, **IP addresses**, **Location**, **Last User**, **Assigned Owner**, **Last Seen**, **Integration**, **Warranty Start**, **Warranty End**, and **Device Age**.

If the file is empty or wrong, check your filters and try again after the list has finished loading. An empty file does not prove you have no devices.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Devices** is not in the sidebar | The module may be off or your role may not include it. Ask your MSP. |
| The list says **No devices found** | Check the company selector, column filters, and warranty or type card. If it is still empty, your provider may need to import devices or review your access. |
| An expired or expiring count looks low | These cards only count devices with a recorded warranty end date. |
| The CSV has a column you hid | The export uses its own column set. |
| The CSV is empty | Wait for the list to load, check filters, and try again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find the serial number or name for a support ticket?">
    Open **Devices**, click the device **Name**, and read **Overview**. You can also use the **Serial Number** column. Include the device name and the problem in your [support request](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Total Devices reset every filter?">
    No. It only clears the warranty or type shortcut.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Export CSV only download the rows I checked?">
    No. It downloads all devices that match your filters, across pages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit a device if I can view it?">
    Only with manage access to Devices. The **Edit** tab does not appear without it. Ask your provider to make the change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
