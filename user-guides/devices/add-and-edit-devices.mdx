Use the **Devices** inventory to record equipment and keep its details current. This guide is for people with manage access to Devices. Adding a record does not install an agent, find hardware, or connect the device to an RMM.

## Before you start

- You need manage access to Devices. Without it, the **Add Device** button is disabled and the **Edit** tab does not appear. Your MSP sets this.
- Adding, editing, and deleting also need an active MSPortal subscription.
- Search the inventory first. A connected tool may have already imported the device.

## Add a manual device

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check for an existing record">
    In **Devices**, filter by **Name** or **Serial Number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Device">
    The button is at the top right of the page. A panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Name">
    **Name** is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Company">
    **Company** is required. Check it even when you opened the form from a filtered list. Then choose a **Location** for that company if it fits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in what you know">
    Choose **Device Type** and **Manufacturer**. Add **OS**, **IP Address**, **MAC Address**, **Serial Number**, **Warranty Start**, and **Warranty End**. Leave unknown fields empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Status and Auto Apply Groups">
    **Status** is a text field. **Auto Apply Groups** is a switch. Use them the way your MSP uses them. They do not change the physical device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Device in the footer">
    A message confirms "Device created successfully." Find the record in the company's inventory and open it to check the values. Click **Cancel** to close the panel without saving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a device

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the device">
    Click the device **Name**. You can also open the row menu and choose **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Edit tab">
    Wait for the current values and custom fields to load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the fields">
    Update what you need. When you move a device, change **Company** and **Location** together. Fill in any custom fields your MSP marked as required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update Device">
    Nothing saves until you click it. Watch for a success or error message, then reopen the device to check the saved values.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Cancel** discards your changes and returns to **Overview**. If you close the panel with unsaved changes, MSPortal asks "Discard your unsaved changes to this device?"

Device fields and custom fields save separately. If an error appears, reopen the device and check both before you try again.

## Edit a device that came from an integration

Synced devices take their details from the connected tool.

- Meraki devices show some fields as read-only ("Some fields synced from Meraki"). You can still change warranty dates, company, location, and custom fields.
- A field the connected tool controls can be replaced at the next sync.
- **Update Device** changes the portal record. It does not change the physical computer or write the change back to your RMM or PSA.

For a correction in a source-controlled field, ask your MSP to fix it in the connected tool or its import settings.

## Delete a device record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    Click the actions button at the end of the device's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    This needs manage access to Devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Check the name in the **Delete Device** dialog, then click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To delete several devices, check their rows and choose **Delete Selected** from the **Actions** menu. The dialog says a device synced from an integration is not imported again on the next sync, and that this cannot be undone.

Deleting removes the portal record only. It does not wipe the computer or remove an agent.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add Device** is disabled | You need manage access to Devices. Ask your MSP. |
| No **Edit** tab | The same permission applies. Without it you see **Read-only mode**. |
| The change did not appear | Check for an error message, reopen the device, and look at both device fields and custom fields. |
| A synced field changed back | The connected tool controls it. Ask your MSP to correct it there. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Add Device install monitoring or antivirus?">
    No. It only creates an inventory record. Your MSP sets up agents and integrations elsewhere.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do I have to check Company when the list is already filtered?">
    The form has its own company field. Check it so the record lands in the right company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a field save as soon as I type?">
    No. Click **Add Device** for a new record or **Update Device** on the Edit tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Delete erase the physical device?">
    No. It removes the portal record. Wiping a device or removing an agent is done in your RMM.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
