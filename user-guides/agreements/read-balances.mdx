This guide is for company users and MSP staff. It shows how to read the balances on an agreement, and what the progress bar and messages mean.

## Before you start

- You need the **read agreements** permission.
- Balances are copied from the PSA. This view is read-only. You cannot edit or top up a balance here.
- Check the units first. An agreement can track hours, incidents, or money.

## Read the current balance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agreement">
    Click **Agreements** or **Contracts** in the sidebar, then click the agreement's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Balances tab">
    The tab name shows how many balance rows exist, for example **Balances (3)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Current section">
    Rows marked **Current** are the active period. Each shows its dates, an hourly rate when the PSA supplies one, and the remaining balance. **As of** at the top shows when the balance data last synced. It is not live usage tracking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the units and the bar">
    The text tells you what is left. When a purchased total exists, the filled part of the bar shows usage. Amber means less than 25% remaining. Red means the balance is below zero. A negative value is shown, not hidden.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list also shows an **Hours Remaining** column for current balances. The panel shows each period in full, so use it for the breakdown.

| What you see | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| "X of Y hrs remaining" | Hours left out of the purchased total |
| "X of Y incidents remaining" | Incidents left, not hours |
| "X remaining" (a money amount) | A money-based balance. Confirm the currency and terms with your MSP |
| "X remaining" with no bar | The PSA gave a balance but no purchased total |
| **Unlimited** | The PSA marks the balance unlimited. Check the agreement's actual scope |
| A dash, or **No hour balances found** | No balance data for this view. This is not zero |

## Read past periods

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scroll to History">
    Past rows are grouped by year, with the number of periods for each year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read each period">
    Each row is marked **Past** and has its own dates and balance. Do not add a past balance to the current one unless your MSP confirms the agreement carries it forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If **Some details could not be loaded** appears, the panel may show only the list's current summary. The history may be incomplete until that is fixed.

## Report a problem with a balance

Send your MSP the agreement name, the period, the units and balance you see, and the **As of** time. Ask them to check recent time entries and the PSA's balance calculation. Do not assume a missing row means unlimited coverage.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The balance is blank or a dash | The PSA did not supply it, or the details did not load. Ask your MSP. A dash is not zero. |
| The balance is negative | The PSA reports more usage than purchased. Ask your MSP to review the period and agreement terms. |
| The list shows incidents or an amount under **Hours Remaining** | The agreement tracks that unit. Read the units before you compare agreements. |
| The balance looks old | Check **As of**. Recent changes may not have synced yet. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does an empty balance mean I used all my hours?">
    No. Zero, missing data, and **Unlimited** are three different states.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the progress bar show hours remaining?">
    The filled part shows usage. The text beside it shows what remains.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I top up a balance here?">
    No. Ask your MSP to change it in its service or billing system.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
