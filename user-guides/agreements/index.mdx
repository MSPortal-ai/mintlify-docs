description: 'Review service agreements, current and historical balances, and PDFs shared by your MSP.'

---

Open **Agreements** or **Contracts** to review your service agreement records, available balances, and uploaded documents. Your MSP must enable this area and give you access.

The name depends on the connected PSA: ConnectWise uses **Agreements**; Halo and Autotask use **Contracts**. If ConnectWise is also enabled, the portal uses Agreements.

The list can also include your contracts with other vendors, such as software licenses or internet circuits, when your MSP tracks them in its PSA. The **Source** column tells the two apart: **MSP** for agreements with your MSP, **Vendor** for contracts with a third party.

Agreement terms and balances come from the connected provider. The portal’s document section holds the PDFs uploaded by your MSP. These screens do not create or edit the underlying PSA agreement, renew it, record a signature, or take a payment.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not find Agreements or Contracts?">
    Your MSP must enable the area and grant read access. Ask your IT provider to check availability and your role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Agreements and Contracts different portal modules?">
    They are the same portal area with PSA-dependent wording. Use the name shown in your sidebar.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I renew or sign my agreement here?">
    These screens let you review imported details and uploaded files. Contact your MSP for its renewal or signing process.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who uploads the agreement PDF?">
    MSP staff with the necessary write access manage the documents. Company users can open the documents they are allowed to read.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
