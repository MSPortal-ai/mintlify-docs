This guide is for company users and MSP staff. It shows how to find an agreement and read what the PSA reports for it.

## Before you start

- You need the **read agreements** permission. Your MSP controls it.
- The page is called **Agreements** or **Contracts** depending on your MSP's PSA.
- The details are copied from the PSA. You cannot edit them here.

## Find an agreement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Agreements or Contracts">
    Click it in the sidebar. If you have a company selector, choose the company first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Use the **Name**, **Type**, and **Status** filters. MSP staff also see a **Company** column with its own filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the row">
    Check **Name** (the contract number sits under it when there is one), **Type**, **Status**, **Start Date**, **End Date**, and **Billing**. **Hours Remaining** and **Documents** show only when your agreements have that data. Use the column toggle and the page controls. Page sizes are 25, 50, and 100.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the row">
    A panel opens on **Summary**. It shows **Status**, **Number**, **Start Date**, **End Date**, and **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the other tabs">
    Click **Balances** for hours, incidents, or money. Click **Documents** for uploaded PDFs. The numbers in the tab names are the counts. Close the panel to go back to the list. Changing the company selector also closes it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the Billing details

For Halo, the Summary also shows a **Recurring billing** section. It lists the recurring charge amounts and how often they bill, the **Next invoice** date, and the **Billing items** with quantity, unit price, discount, and amount before tax. The amounts are before tax and may differ from the final invoice, because usage, one-time charges, and adjustments can change it.

Halo billing can instead show a message such as **Billing details unavailable**, **No recurring charge linked**, or **Recurring billing inactive**. A message like that does not mean the agreement is free. Check with your MSP. For other PSAs, **Billing** shows the amount and cycle the PSA supplied.

## When details are incomplete

If **Some details could not be loaded** appears, the panel shows the summary from the list only. The balance history and document list may be incomplete. Close and reopen the panel later, or ask your MSP.

A dash means the value is not available. It does not mean zero. Check the source agreement with your MSP before you rely on a billing figure.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An agreement is missing | Check the company selector and filters. Then ask your MSP to check your permission, company mapping, and the PSA sync. |
| The list says **No agreements found** or **No contracts found** | Same checks. A load problem can also cause this. Use the retry option if it appears. |
| A value is wrong | Ask your MSP to correct it in the PSA. |
| I need a CSV | The table has no export. Ask your MSP for a report. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is it called Contracts in my portal?">
    Halo and Autotask use Contracts. ConnectWise uses Agreements.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit the billing amount or end date?">
    No. Ask your MSP to change it in the PSA.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an Active badge mean every service is covered?">
    No. It is the status the PSA reports. Read the agreement or ask your MSP which services it covers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are past balances and PDFs?">
    Click the row, then the **Balances** or **Documents** tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
