This guide is for company users and MSP staff. It shows how to open an agreement PDF. Uploading, replacing, and deleting PDFs is an MSP task, covered in [Upload and manage agreement PDFs](/user-guides/agreements/manage-documents).

## Before you start

- You need the **read agreements** permission.
- Your MSP must have uploaded a PDF to the agreement. If none is there, ask them.

## Open a PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agreement">
    Click **Agreements** or **Contracts** in the sidebar, then click the agreement's row. Check the agreement name and company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Documents tab">
    The tab name shows the count, for example **Documents (2)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the year and version">
    Documents are grouped by agreement year, with the highest version first. Each shows a title, a version badge such as **v2**, the upload date, and the size. **Latest** marks the first version in that year. It does not mean the file is signed, accepted, or the current legal agreement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Download icon">
    The PDF opens in a new browser tab through a temporary link. Depending on your browser settings, it shows there or downloads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the document">
    Confirm the year, parties, version, and contents. If pages are missing or it is the wrong file, tell your MSP.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The link is temporary. Do not bookmark it. Come back to the **Documents** tab and click **Download** again for a fresh link.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No documents have been uploaded yet** | Check for a **Some details could not be loaded** warning. If there is none, ask your MSP whether a PDF was uploaded. |
| **Failed to open document** | Click **Download** again. If it keeps failing, ask your MSP to check the file and your access. |
| Nothing opens | Your browser may have blocked the new tab. Allow it and try again. |
| A saved link no longer works | It expired. Click **Download** again. |
| An older version is gone | Your MSP may have replaced that year's files or deleted one. Ask for the version you need. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does opening a PDF sign or accept it?">
    No. It only opens the uploaded file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Latest mean the newest document across all years?">
    No. It marks the first version within its own year.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I upload a signed copy?">
    Not as a company user. Ask your MSP how to send it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do I see a document count but no files?">
    The list can load before the detail. Look for a **Some details could not be loaded** warning and ask your MSP if it persists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I restore a deleted or replaced version?">
    No. There is no restore. Ask your MSP for a copy.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
