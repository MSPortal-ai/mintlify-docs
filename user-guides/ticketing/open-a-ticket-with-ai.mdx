The AI Assistant reads your description, picks a ticket template, and fills in the New Ticket form. It does not submit anything. You review the form and click **Create Ticket** yourself.

<Tip>
You can also start a request from the AI Assistant on **Home**. See [How to open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket#start-with-the-home-ai-assistant). This guide covers the AI Assistant on the **New Ticket** page.
</Tip>

## Before you start

- Your account needs permission to create tickets. If you cannot see **New Ticket**, ask your IT provider.
- Your IT provider must have at least one request type set up for your company. If none exist, the page shows **No Templates Available**.
- AI must be turned on for your portal. If you do not see **Open AI Assistant** or the floating AI button, AI is off. You can still [pick a request type yourself](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket#use-the-ticket-form-instead).

## Create a ticket with AI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tickets">
    Click **Tickets** in the sidebar.

    ![Tickets page](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-09-02/5afd802a-aae0-46c4-9a79-eb774ab5da44/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=93,121&br_px=1813,1083&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click New Ticket">
    Click **New Ticket** at the top right. The AI Assistant panel may open on its own, because the default setting opens it with the page.

    If the panel did not open, click **Open AI Assistant** on the **Create a Ticket with AI** card, or click the floating AI button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe the request">
    Type your request in the assistant's message box. Include what the problem is, when it started, who is affected, any error message, and what you already tried.

    Example descriptions:

    - "I need a new employee setup for John Smith starting Monday in the Sales department"
    - "My email isn't syncing on my phone since yesterday afternoon"
    - "Request for Adobe Creative Suite license for the marketing team"

    Do not include passwords or Social Security numbers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the message">
    Click the send button (**Send message**) or press **Enter**. The assistant picks the best request type from the ones available to you and prepares the answers it can infer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply the suggested changes">
    A **Suggested Changes** card appears in the assistant panel with the **Template**, the **Form content** it will fill in, and any **Questions to complete the draft**. Read it, then click **Apply to draft**. Click **Discard** to throw the suggestion away.

    Nothing is written to the form until you click **Apply to draft**. After you apply it, a "Template selected and fields populated" message appears and the **Template Selected** badge shows at the top of the page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the AI Generation Summary">
    The form shows an **AI Generation Summary** with three lists. **Fields we filled for you** shows what the AI completed. **Required fields you need to complete** shows what is still empty. **Optional fields available** shows the rest. Click a required field in the list to jump to it.

    <img src="/images/product/ticket-ai-generation-summary.png" alt="AI Generation Summary listing populated fields and the review reminder" width="1404" height="400" />

    On wide screens a **Required** bar also lists the empty required fields.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the template sections">
    Go through every section of the form. Multi-section templates show each section on its own tab, so visit each one. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).

    ![Hardware and peripherals section of the generated form](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-09-02/17632bf7-a316-4dc8-b9d6-4b77c5ccded0/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=187,0&br_px=1906,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=600,199)

    Check the **Subject**, names, selected dropdown options, and every date. The AI can misread dates such as "next Monday", so set each date with the calendar picker.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add attachments">
    If the template has a file-upload field, attach screenshots or documents there.

    ![Scheduling and attachments section](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-09-02/f5bf5e8f-495f-468c-aa2c-cb31f8dffd55/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=187,0&br_px=1906,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=721,204)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Create Ticket">
    Click **Create Ticket** at the top right and wait for the result. A message such as "Ticket #1234 created successfully!" confirms it.

    If required fields are missing, an error lists them (for example "Please fill in required fields: Job Title, Department"). Complete those fields and click **Create Ticket** again.

    ![Missing required fields error after clicking Create Ticket](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-09-02/a01d5a07-b6cf-4e5e-a6ae-d851292ed210/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=187,0&br_px=1906,961&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=968,33)

    See [understand the result](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket#understand-the-result) if the ticket is created but attachments or an appointment fail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change the AI result

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add detail in the assistant">
    Open the AI Assistant panel again and send another message that adds detail or describes the change. The assistant sees your current draft, so it works from what is already on the form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply the new suggestion">
    A new **Suggested Changes** card appears. Click **Apply to draft** to use it. Review the form again afterward, because a new suggestion can change the template or earlier answers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a small fix, such as a date or a dropdown choice, edit the form directly instead.

<Note>
If you edit the form after a suggestion appears, the card shows **Draft changed**. Discard it and ask the assistant again.
</Note>

## Pick a template yourself

<Steps>
  <Step title="Leave the assistant">
    On the New Ticket page, click **Edit form** in the assistant panel, or close the panel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the request type">
    Select a request card under **Request Service**, or open **Choose a template...** under **Select Template**. Which one you see depends on your portal's manual template selection setting. See [How to open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket#use-the-ticket-form-instead).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Embedded templates

Some templates contain another template inside them. For example, a **New Hire** template can include a **New Computer Request** template.

If your description covers the embedded template's questions, the AI fills those too. The summary lists them as **Parent Label > Inner Label**, such as **New Computer Request > Model**. Click a required one to jump to it inside the embedded card.

Each embedded template still creates its own PSA ticket with its own board, priority, and status when you submit. AI only helps fill the questions.

## Write a good description

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Name the people, dates, and locations. | Write "it's broken" or "new person needs stuff". |
| Include any error message and who it affects. | Paste passwords or other secrets. |
| Describe the impact on work. | Use abbreviations or slang the template does not use. |

**Good:** "I need to set up a new employee named Sarah Johnson who starts on January 15th in the Marketing department. She'll need access to Adobe Creative Suite, Slack, and our project management tools."

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Open AI Assistant** button or floating AI button | AI is off for your portal. Pick a request type yourself, or ask your IT provider to turn AI on. |
| **No Templates Available** shows on the page | Your IT provider has not set up request types for your company yet. Contact them. |
| The AI chose the wrong template | Send a more specific description, or click the back arrow in the page header and pick a template by hand. Going back asks you to confirm because it clears the form. |
| A field is empty | The AI only fills what your description supports. Fill it in yourself. |
| A date is wrong | Reset it with the calendar picker. |
| The embedded template's fields are blank | Expand the embedded card and fill the questions. It creates its own ticket either way. |
| The assistant says the page or draft changed | You edited the form while it was working. Send your request again. |
| The message says the ticket was sent by email | Your request reached your IT provider by email instead. No action is needed. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the assistant submit the ticket?">
    No. Applying the suggestion only fills the form. The ticket is created when you click **Create Ticket**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the AI use a request type I cannot normally see?">
    No. It chooses from the request types available to you. It does not change your permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use AI when no request types exist?">
    No. The assistant needs at least one request type. Ask your IT provider to set one up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I trust every generated answer?">
    Review them all. Check dates, names, and selected options before you submit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I continue by hand after using AI?">
    Yes. Edit the form directly, or click the back arrow in the page header to pick another template. Going back clears the current answers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
