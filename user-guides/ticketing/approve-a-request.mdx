title: 'Approve a request'
description: 'Review requests waiting for your approval, follow your own requests, forward a rejected request by email, and see what happened after a decision.'

Some requests need sign-off from people at your company before your IT provider starts work on them. They wait on the **Approvals** page until every step of their approval chain approves.

**Audience:** Company users. Your IT provider decides which requests need approval, who approves them, and whether the ticket is created before or after approval.

## Approve or reject a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Approvals">
    Open **Approvals** in the sidebar. **Waiting for me** lists the requests you can decide now. You also get an email and a notification when a request needs you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the request">
    Select a request to see its details, who asked for it, each approval step and its history. If the ticket was already created, its number and waiting status are shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Select **Approve** or **Reject** and add an optional note. The requester and the other approvers can read it. When your step has enough approvals, the next step's approvers are asked. One rejection ends the request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You only see requests from your own company that you asked for or are asked to approve.

## Forward a rejected request

When your IT provider allows it, the reject dialog offers **Email me a copy of this request** and, if your company set them up, fixed addresses such as an internal IT mailbox. Tick the ones you want. Each address receives the full request, the requester, your note, and a link back, so your company can handle it internally. The request's history records where it was forwarded.

## Follow your own requests

When you submit a request that needs approval, MSPortal opens it on the **Approvals** page. Depending on how your IT provider set the chain up, the ticket is created only after approval, or right away in a waiting status. Find your requests later under **My requests**. You are told when a request is approved, not approved, or expires.

Select **Withdraw request** while it is still waiting if you no longer need it.

## After a decision

When the last step approves, the ticket is created and its number appears on the request, or the ticket that already existed moves to the approved status. A rejection moves an existing ticket to the not-approved status. In both cases the ticket's details show an **Approval** section with who approved or rejected it and a link to the request.

Your IT provider can also approve or reject a request for the approvers, for example when an approver is away. Those decisions are marked as made by your IT provider and include their reason.
