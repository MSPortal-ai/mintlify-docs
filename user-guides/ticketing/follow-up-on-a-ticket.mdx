This guide is for company users. Open **Tickets** to find a support request, check its status, and continue the conversation with your IT provider.

## Before you start

- Your IT provider controls which tickets you can see and which actions you can take. You may see only your own requests or all tickets for your company.
- Adding a note and changing a status are separate permissions from viewing. If you cannot do either, ask your IT provider.

## Find a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tickets">
    Click **Tickets** in the sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Open, Closed, or All">
    The list starts on **Open**. Click **Closed** to find completed requests, or **All** to see both.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Use the filter under a column heading, such as **Summary** or **Status**, to search. Which columns show depends on your IT provider's display settings. If you see a company selector at the top, check that the right company is selected. Use the page controls at the bottom to see more results.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the ticket">
    Click the ticket's row. The details window shows the description, status, priority, and activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a note to a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ticket">
    Click the ticket's row in the list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Add Note">
    Find the **Add Note** box in the ticket details. If it is collapsed, click **Expand Add Note section**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write your update">
    Describe what changed or answer the technician's question. Include what you tried and any new error message. Add the note to the existing ticket when it is about the same issue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Attach files if you need to">
    Click **Add attachment** and choose up to 5 files. Wait for them to attach before you send. Some ticketing systems, such as Syncro, do not accept attachments here. In that case, ask your IT provider how to send the file.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the note">
    Click **Add Note**. A message such as "Note added successfully" confirms it, and the note appears in the activity list. Typing a note does not send it.

    If the note saved but an attachment failed, keep the note and send only the missing file again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change a ticket's status

If your role can update statuses, open the ticket and pick a new status from the status selector in the details. Wait for "Ticket status updated successfully" before you rely on the change.

If the status has no selector, add a note asking your IT provider to change it. The status names come from your IT provider's ticketing system, so there is no one "close ticket" option for every portal.

## Download an attachment

Open the ticket and look for the attachments button with a file count in the ticket header. Click it, then click the file you want. The button only shows when the ticket has attachments and its ticketing system supports them (ConnectWise, Halo, or Autotask).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A finished ticket disappeared | Click **Closed** or **All**. The default view shows only **Open** tickets. |
| A coworker sees a ticket you cannot | Your role may be limited to your own tickets. Ask your IT provider. |
| A ticket is missing right after you sent it | Check the confirmation message, the company selector, and your filters. Some setups send the request by email instead of to the ticket list. Give the confirmation to your IT provider. |
| A note or status change failed | Note the error and ask your IT provider to check your permissions and their ticketing connection. |
| The list and the details show different information | Tell your IT provider the ticket number. The list and details can update at different times. |
| An attachment will not download | Ask your IT provider to confirm the file still exists in their ticketing system. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see closed tickets?">
    In **Tickets**, click **Closed** or **All**. **Open** is the default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see every ticket for my company?">
    Only if your IT provider allows it. Some roles see only their own tickets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I reply without opening a new ticket?">
    Yes, if your role can add notes. Open the existing ticket and use **Add Note**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I close or reopen a ticket myself?">
    Only if your role can update statuses and the status you need is available. Otherwise, add a note asking your IT provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see technicians' internal notes?">
    Company users do not see internal notes. You see the public conversation only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I log time on a ticket?">
    No. Time entries are for your IT provider's technicians. Use **Add Note** to reply.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
