This guide is for company users. A request template is the form behind a support request. Your IT provider builds the templates. You choose one, answer its questions, and submit it.

## Before you start

- Your role needs permission to create tickets. See [How to open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket).
- Your IT provider must have made at least one active template available to your company.
- You do not need to design or configure a template to ask for help.

## Choose a request

The easiest way is the AI Assistant on **Home**. Describe what you need and the assistant picks a template for you. To choose one yourself, use the steps below.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New Ticket">
    Click **Tickets** in the sidebar, then **New Ticket**. If the AI Assistant panel opens, click **Edit form** or close the panel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the request">
    Under **Request Service**, type in **Search services or problems...** or click a category. Requests are sorted by how often they are used. Each card shows the request name, its category, and a short description. If your portal uses the list view, open **Choose a template...** under **Select Template** and search instead.

    <img src="/images/product/ticket-request-catalog.png" alt="Generic Issue request card in the request catalog" width="528" height="440" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the description">
    Read the card's description to be sure the request fits. Click the card to open its form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Answer the questions">
    Fill in the form and click **Create Ticket**. See [How to open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) for the full steps and for what the result messages mean.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If no request fits, describe the issue to the Home AI Assistant, or ask your IT provider which request to use.

## What the questions mean

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| A required question (marked with an asterisk) | You must answer it before you can create the ticket. |
| Help text under a question | Guidance from your IT provider on what to enter. |
| Gray example text in an empty box | A placeholder. It is not an answer. Type your own. |
| A question that appears after you change an answer | The form has a conditional question. Check the form again after each choice. |
| A **Subject** marked **Auto-generated** | Your IT provider fixed the subject. You cannot edit it. |
| A section inside your form with its own questions | Another request form is built in. Fill it in too. It can create its own ticket. |
| Different questions than a coworker at another company sees | Your IT provider can keep a separate version of a request for each company. |

## If a request is missing

First check the company you are working in, the category, and the search text. If it is still missing, ask your IT provider to check that:

- The template is active.
- It is turned on for your company.
- Your role is allowed to use it.
- Any company type or industry limits match your company.

A direct link to a template does not give you access to it. Company administrators cannot create templates or change who can use them. Your IT provider does that.

When you contact your IT provider, send the request name, your company, the category or search you used, and what you see on screen. Do not send passwords or integration credentials.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No Templates Available** shows | Your IT provider has no active template for you. Ask them to add one. |
| A request you expect is not listed | Follow [If a request is missing](#if-a-request-is-missing). |
| The subject is locked | Your IT provider locked it on purpose. Ask them to change it if it is wrong. |
| Another question appeared after you changed an answer | That question applies to your new choice. Answer it. |
| You are unsure the request was sent | Check **Tickets** before you send it again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to pick a template before using the Home assistant?">
    No. Describe what you need on Home. The assistant picks an available template and gathers the details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do I see fewer requests than my IT provider's staff?">
    Your list only shows active templates that are turned on for your company and role. Staff can see more in the template editor.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company administrator turn on a missing template?">
    No. Ask your IT provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does choosing a template create the ticket?">
    No. The ticket is created when you click **Create Ticket** and see the success message.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
