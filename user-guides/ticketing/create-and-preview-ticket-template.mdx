A ticket template defines the questions, defaults, and access rules for a repeatable support request. The Home AI Assistant uses available templates to prepare tickets, and users can also choose them on **Tickets > New Ticket**. This guide is for MSP staff. Company users submit requests and cannot create templates.

## Before you start

- Your role needs permission to write or manage ticket settings (**Settings > Tickets**).
- Connect your PSA integration if you want PSA defaults such as board, priority, and status.

## Start a draft template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New Template">
    Go to **Settings > Tickets > Templates** and click **New Template**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name the template">
    Under **Template Details**, enter a unique **Template Name**. Choose a **Category**, or keep **No Category**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe when to use it">
    Write a **Description** that explains when to use this request.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn off the active switch while you draft">
    New templates start active. Turn off **Template is active and available for use** if you do not want users to see the draft yet. You keep the setting by clicking **Save Template**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The new template can inherit PSA defaults from your ticket settings. Check them before you make the template available. Creating a template does not create a ticket.

## Help the Home assistant choose this request

Use **AI Description** to say what the template is for and when the assistant should pick it. For example, tell a new employee setup apart from a change to an existing employee's access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write or generate the text">
    Type the description yourself, or click **Generate**. **Generate** works after you enter a name and add at least one field. It writes a summary from the template's fields.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and edit">
    Read the result and edit it so it states clear selection criteria. Then save the template.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a section and a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a section">
    Under **Sections & Fields**, click **Add Section**. The new section gets a numbered title. Click the title to rename it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a field">
    Click **Add field** in the section. The field editor opens with a new optional **Text** field. Enter a clear **Field Label**, such as "Request summary".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the type and requirement">
    Keep **Text** for a short answer, or pick another **Type**. Set **Required** to **Optional**, **Required**, or **Conditional**. A conditional field needs a finished rule. See [Show or require a field conditionally](/user-guides/ticketing/how-to-create-a-ticket-template#show-or-require-a-field-conditionally).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add guidance">
    Use **Placeholder** for an example and **Help Text** for instructions. A placeholder is not a saved answer. Leave the generated **Field Key** alone unless you have a reason to change it. Keys must be unique in the template and cannot be edited after the first save.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the section">
    Collapse or expand sections and fields as you work. Adding, editing, removing, and reordering all change the draft. Nothing is kept until you click **Save Template**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Keep the first version small enough to test. For choice fields, conditional logic, embedded templates, and PSA routing, see [How to create a ticket template](/user-guides/ticketing/how-to-create-a-ticket-template).

## Preview the form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Preview">
    Click **Preview** in the page header. The **Form Preview** panel opens on the right.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Try the form">
    Enter sample answers. Check section navigation, labels, instructions, and required and conditional questions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reset or close">
    Click **Reset** to clear your sample answers. Click **Close**, or click outside the panel, to return to editing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Branch preview: configured defaults appear when Preview opens. **Reset** restores those defaults. Every field is available in the form and preview, including fields inside embedded templates, subject to its configured conditions.
</Note>

<img src="/images/product/ticket-template-checklist-desktop-preview.png" alt="Branch preview showing editable defaults and the Technician Checklist section with synthetic Demo fields" width="1440" height="960" />

Preview uses the current draft, so you can check changes before you save. It does not create a ticket. It does not test PSA creation, webhooks, embedded tickets, or scheduling. Linked-data pickers depend on your own permissions, so Preview also does not show exactly what a client sees.

## Review routing and access

Before you activate the template, open these sections of the editor.

| Section | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **PSA Integration Settings** | The board or queue, status, priority, and type defaults for your PSA. A status that is not active on the chosen board blocks saving. **Use Client's PSA Defaults** takes board, priority, and status from the company instead. |
| **Subject Configuration** | A default **Subject Template**, and **Lock subject (users cannot modify it)** if you want it fixed. |
| **Company Visibility** | The per-company switches and the **New Companies** default. When it is **Enabled**, companies added later get the template. |
| **Role Access** | Which company roles can use the template. With none selected, every role can. Branches inherit the parent's roles. |

Company Visibility and Role Access changes made in the editor are saved when you click **Save Template**. Company Access, Role Access, and Company Gating dialogs in the Templates list save as you change them.

<Warning>
Do not turn every role off to hide a template. With no roles selected, every role can use it. Turn off **Template is active and available for use** instead.
</Warning>

## Save and activate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save the draft">
    Click **Save Template** and wait for "Template created successfully". The editor returns to the Templates list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Answer the new-company prompt if it appears">
    If some existing companies are off while **New Companies** is on, a "New companies will receive this template" prompt appears. Click **Save Anyway** to accept it, or **Cancel** to go back and change the setting.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reopen and verify">
    Open the template again. Check the name, active state, fields, defaults, and access.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate">
    When it is ready, turn on **Template is active and available for use** and click **Save Template** again. You can also use **Set Active** in the Templates list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check availability">
    Open **Tickets > New Ticket** for the intended company and confirm the template appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If saving fails, the message names the problem and your draft stays open. Common causes are a missing or duplicate name, a duplicate field key, an invalid PSA status, a conditional field without a trigger, or an embedded field with no template.

## Cancel a draft

Click **Cancel** in the header. If you have unsaved changes, a confirmation asks whether to discard them. Click **Discard** to leave without saving, or **Keep editing** to stay. Your last saved version is unchanged.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Generate** is disabled | Enter a template name and add at least one field. |
| Save fails with a name or key error | Rename the template or field so it is unique. |
| Save fails with a status error | Pick a default status that is active on the selected board. |
| Save fails on a conditional field | Choose the trigger field and value, or remove the condition. |
| The template does not appear on New Ticket | Check that it is active and turned on for the company, role, and any gating. See [Understand your available request templates](/msp-guides/ticketing/understand-request-templates). |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I create a ticket template?">
    Go to **Settings > Tickets > Templates** and click **New Template**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a new template inactive by default?">
    No. New templates start active. Turn the switch off and save if you want an inactive draft.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a field save the template?">
    No. Field and section edits change the draft. Click **Save Template**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is AI Description used for?">
    It tells the Home assistant what the template is for and when to pick it. It does not grant access or create a ticket.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Preview create a ticket?">
    No. It only shows the form.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is placeholder text a default answer?">
    No. A placeholder is an example in an empty box. Use **Default Value** for a pre-filled answer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I edit a field key?">
    Keys are locked after the first save. Change the label or help text instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the new-company warning mean?">
    Some existing companies are off while **New Companies** is on, so companies you add later will get the template automatically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
