This guide is for company users who want to ask their IT provider for help or request a service. Start with the AI Assistant on **Home**. If AI is not available in your portal, or you prefer a form, use [Tickets > New Ticket](#use-the-ticket-form-instead).

## Before you start

- Sign in with the portal address your IT provider gave you.
- Your role needs permission to create tickets. Being able to see tickets does not mean you can create them. If **New Ticket** is missing, ask your IT provider to check your access.
- Your IT provider must have made at least one active request template available to your company.
- You do not need access to **Settings** to open a ticket. Your IT provider manages templates, users, and permissions.

## Start with the Home AI Assistant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Home">
    Click the logo at the top of the sidebar. Signing in also lands you on **Home**. The AI Assistant message box is on this page. You do not need to open **Tickets** or choose a template first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe what you need">
    Type your request in the message box, then click **Send message** or press **Enter**. For example:

    > I need to open a support ticket. My laptop cannot connect to the office Wi-Fi this morning. I restarted it, but the error still says "Cannot connect to this network."

    Say who is affected, when it started, and what you already tried. Do not include passwords or recovery codes.

    <img src="/images/product/home-ticket-prompt.png" alt="Home AI Assistant message box containing an unsent example Wi-Fi support request and the Send control" width="1344" height="248" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Answer the assistant's questions">
    The assistant picks a request template and asks for any details it still needs. Answer its questions and correct anything it got wrong, such as a date, a person, or the type of request.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the ticket draft">
    The assistant shows your ticket as a draft. Click **Edit form** to change any answer, and **Hide form** to close the editor again. If a required answer is missing, the draft lists it and **Review and submit** stays unavailable until you fill it in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and send the ticket">
    Click **Review and submit**. A **Review change** card shows the exact ticket that will be created. Click **Apply change** to create it, or **Discard** to cancel.

    Wait for the card to show **Applied**. The assistant then confirms the ticket, for example **Ticket #1234 created**. Nothing is sent to your IT provider until you click **Apply change**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Follow your ticket">
    Open **Tickets** to find the request, read replies, and add notes. See [Find and follow up on a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/follow-up-on-a-ticket).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Your IT provider controls whether AI is available on Home. If you see the standard Home page instead, use the ticket form below.
</Note>

## Use the ticket form instead

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New Ticket">
    Click **Tickets** in the sidebar, then click **New Ticket** at the top right. If your IT provider sent you a direct link to a request, open the link and the form is already selected.

    The AI Assistant panel can open first. To fill in the form yourself, click **Edit form** in the panel, or close the panel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the request type">
    Under **Request Service**, search **Search services or problems...** or browse the categories, then click the card that matches what you need.

    Some portals show a **Select Template** list instead of cards. Open **Choose a template...**, search, and pick the request.

    <img src="/images/product/ticket-request-catalog.png" alt="Generic Issue request card in the request catalog" width="528" height="440" />

    Your IT provider names these requests. A general support request might be called **Generic Issue**, and new equipment might have its own request.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the subject and answer the questions">
    Type a short **Subject**, such as "Unable to connect to Wi-Fi." Some requests set the subject for you and mark it **Auto-generated**. You cannot edit those.

    Answer the required questions. Say what happened, when it started, who is affected, the exact error message, and what you already tried. Do not include passwords or recovery codes.

    <img src="/images/product/ticket-request-form.png" alt="Request form with a sample Wi-Fi issue subject and description" width="1536" height="1964" />

    If the form has section tabs, click the round arrow at the bottom to move to the next section, and visit every tab. Some questions appear, or become required, after you change an earlier answer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add files and review">
    If the request has a file-upload field, attach screenshots or documents there. Check screenshots for unrelated personal information first.

    Review the subject, dates, selected people, and each required question. If your request contains another request form inside it, fill that in too. It creates its own ticket.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the ticket">
    Click **Create Ticket** at the top right, or use the round arrow on the last section. Wait for a message such as "Ticket #1234 created successfully!"

    If required questions are empty, the message lists them. Fill them in and click **Create Ticket** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change the request type

Click the back arrow in the page header, or click **Choose a Different Template** if it shows. If you already entered answers, a confirmation asks you to **Discard** them or choose **Keep editing**. Changing the template clears the form, so copy anything you want to keep first.

## Schedule an appointment

If the request shows **Schedule appointment**, turn it on before you create the ticket. The ticket is created first, and then you continue to TimeZest to pick a time.

Your IT provider must turn on scheduling for the request. If the ticket is created but the appointment cannot be prepared, contact your IT provider to book the time. You do not need to create the ticket again.

## Understand the result

| Result | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Ticket #1234 created successfully!" | Keep the number and follow the request in **Tickets**. |
| The ticket was created, but attachments failed to upload | Keep the ticket. Add the missing files from the ticket, or ask your IT provider how to send them. |
| An additional request inside the form failed | Tell your IT provider which one failed. The main ticket may already exist. |
| "Ticket sent via email" | Your IT provider's ticketing system was unavailable, so the request went to their email instead. Keep the confirmation and ask them for a reference. |
| Creation failed | Read the error, fix any highlighted answer, and try again. Check **Tickets** first so you do not create a duplicate. |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **New Ticket** is missing | You do not have permission to create tickets. Ask your IT provider. |
| No requests are listed | Your IT provider has not made a template available to your company. Ask them to check. |
| The AI Assistant does not appear | Your IT provider has turned AI off for your portal. Use the ticket form. |
| The form looks different from a coworker's | Templates differ by company and request type, and some questions depend on earlier answers. |
| You left the page and lost your answers | Unfinished tickets are not saved. Submit the request, or keep a copy of your answers. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I open a support ticket?">
    Start with the AI Assistant on **Home**. You can also click **Tickets**, then **New Ticket**, or open a direct request link from your IT provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does describing my problem on Home send a ticket?">
    No. The assistant prepares a draft. The ticket is created only after you click **Review and submit** and then **Apply change**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I view tickets but not create one?">
    Viewing and creating are separate permissions. Ask your IT provider to turn on creating for your role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to use AI?">
    No. Click **Tickets**, then **New Ticket**, and fill in the form.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did one request create more than one ticket?">
    A template can include another request form. Each one creates its own ticket. The completion messages name each one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my own permissions?">
    No. Your IT provider manages permissions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
