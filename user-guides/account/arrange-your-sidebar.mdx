**Audience:** Company users. Your IT provider manages shared settings, templates, and user permissions.

Open **Settings > User Settings** (`/settings/user`) and scroll to **Sidebar order**. The editor shows the pages available for your account. The MSP controls the organization's default layout and which modules are available.

## Keep the desktop sidebar expanded

<Note>
Branch preview for app PR #1605. These controls and screenshots show the upcoming sidebar update.

On desktop, move the pointer into the icon rail from the page, or tab into the navigation, to open it. Select **Keep sidebar expanded** beside the logo to keep the navigation visible. The page moves beside the expanded sidebar.

Select **Collapse sidebar** beside the logo to return to the icon rail immediately. Moving back into the rail opens it again. Your choice is remembered in this browser across page changes and reloads, and shared with other open tabs. If browser storage is unavailable, it lasts until the next full page load.

On phones, use **Toggle menu** in the top bar to open the navigation sheet. Docking is a desktop preference.

## Reorder pages and sections

<Steps>
  <Step title="Move pages">
    Drag the handle beside a page to move it within its section or into another section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize sections">
    Enter a **Category name** and select **Add category** to create a personal section. Drag pages into it. Select a custom section's name to rename it, and use the up and down controls to move a section.

    Deleting a custom section returns its pages to their default sections; it does not delete the pages or their records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply your layout">
    Select **Save order**. Until you save, the editor displays **Not applied until you save** and your changes are only staged.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a settings shortcut

In **Sidebar order**, find the **Settings** panel. Drag an individual settings page into the section where you want it, then select **Save order**. For example, staff with access to **Companies** can add **Settings: Companies** to Planning for direct access from the main sidebar.

Only settings pages available to your account appear. A shortcut does not grant additional access. You can reorder it like any other page, or use **Remove from navigation** and save to hide it. Pages you have placed stay listed in the **Settings** panel with an **In sidebar** or **Disabled** status.

## Hide or restore a page

Drag an unused page into **Disabled modules**, or use its **Remove from navigation** control. Select **Save order** to hide it from your sidebar.

To show it again, drag it from **Disabled modules** into a section and save. An item marked **Disabled by your administrator** is locked by the MSP and cannot be restored from your personal editor.

<Note>
Hiding a page changes navigation only. It does not revoke permissions, disable an integration, or delete any records. Ask your MSP to change access permissions.
</Note>

## Restore the default layout

Select **Reset my order** to load your company or organization's default into the editor. Review it, then select **Save order** to apply the reset. Reset alone does not save the change.

The personal editor lets you rearrange custom links and dashboard entries already supplied by your administrator. Ask your MSP to add new external links or configure shared navigation defaults.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why did the sidebar not change after I dragged a page?">
    The editor stages changes. Select **Save order** and wait for the saved message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will my changes affect other people?">
    Changes in **User Settings > Sidebar order** affect your personal layout. The MSP manages company and organization defaults separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not restore a disabled module?">
    A lock means your administrator disabled it at a higher level. Ask your MSP to change that setting. A module can also be unavailable because of permissions or feature availability.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does removing a page delete its data?">
    No. Removing it from navigation only hides its sidebar entry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I undo my personal layout?">
    Select **Reset my order**, review the staged default layout, then select **Save order**. This restores the applicable company or organization default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add my own external links from this screen?">
    New custom links are configured by the MSP in the shared Navigation settings. Your personal editor can reorder links already available to you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the editor cannot load?">
    Retry the page. If it still fails, give your MSP the error message. Do not assume an empty or failed editor means your access was removed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
