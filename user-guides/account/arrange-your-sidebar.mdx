Arrange the sidebar the way you work. Your changes affect only your own account. Your IT provider manages shared settings, templates, and user permissions.

## Before you start

- Open **Settings > User Settings** and scroll to the **Sidebar order** card. Every signed-in user can use it.
- The editor lists only the pages available to your account. Your MSP controls the default layout and which modules exist.
- Nothing changes until you click **Save order**.

## Keep the desktop sidebar expanded

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar">
    On desktop, move the pointer into the icon rail on the left, or tab into the navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dock it">
    Click **Keep sidebar expanded** beside the logo. The sidebar stays open and the page moves beside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collapse it again">
    Click **Collapse sidebar** beside the logo to return to the icon rail. Moving the pointer back into the rail opens it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your choice is remembered in this browser across page changes and reloads, and shared with other open tabs. If browser storage is unavailable, it lasts until the next full page load. On phones, click **Toggle menu** in the top bar to open the navigation. Docking is a desktop setting.

## Reorder pages and sections

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > User Settings">
    Scroll to the **Sidebar order** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move pages">
    Drag the handle beside a page to move it within its section or into another section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a section (optional)">
    Enter a **Category name** and click **Add category**. Drag pages into the new section. Click a custom section's name to rename it, and use **Move category up** and **Move category down** to reorder it.

    **Delete category** returns the section's pages to their default sections. It does not delete the pages or their records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save order**. Until you save, the editor shows **Not applied until you save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a settings shortcut

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Settings panel">
    In **Sidebar order**, find the **Settings** panel. It lists the settings pages your account can open, such as **Settings: Companies**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag a page into a section">
    Drag the settings page into the section where you want it. A page you have placed shows **In sidebar** in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save order**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A shortcut does not grant extra access. To remove one, click **Remove from navigation** and save.

## Hide or restore a page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hide the page">
    Drag an unused page into **Disabled modules**, or click **Remove from navigation** on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save order**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore it later (optional)">
    Drag the page from **Disabled modules** back into a section, then click **Save order**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An item marked **Disabled by your administrator** is locked by your MSP and cannot be restored here.

<Note>
Hiding a page changes navigation only. It does not revoke permissions, disable an integration, or delete any records.
</Note>

## Restore the default layout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset">
    In **Sidebar order**, click **Reset my order**. The editor loads your company or organization default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    Check the layout, then click **Save order**. Reset alone does not save the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Custom links and dashboards that your administrator placed in the sidebar can be reordered here. New links and shared defaults are set in shared navigation settings by your MSP.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| The sidebar did not change after I dragged a page | Click **Save order** and wait for the **Sidebar order saved** message. |
| I cannot restore a disabled module | A lock or **Disabled by your administrator** means a higher level disabled it. Ask your MSP. The page can also be missing because of your permissions or feature availability. |
| A page I expect is not in the editor | The editor lists only pages your account can open. Ask your MSP to check your role. |
| The editor will not load | Reload the page. If it still fails, send your MSP the error message. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will my changes affect other people?">
    No. **Sidebar order** is your personal layout. Your MSP manages company and organization defaults separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does removing a page delete its data?">
    No. Removing a page from navigation only hides its sidebar entry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I undo my personal layout?">
    Click **Reset my order**, review the staged default, then click **Save order**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add my own external links from this screen?">
    No. Your MSP adds custom links in shared Navigation settings. You can reorder links already available to you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
