See which role and companies your account has, and what to do when a page or action is missing. This page is for company users. Your IT provider manages permissions.

## Before you start

- Every signed-in user can open **Settings > User Settings** and see their own access.
- Your role decides which actions you can take. Your company access decides which companies' records you can work with.
- A menu item can be visible while its Create, Edit, Delete, or Export actions are not.

## Find your role and permissions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open User Settings">
    Go to **Settings > User Settings**. The profile summary near the top shows your assigned role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open permission details">
    In the **Access** card, click **View details** beside **Permissions**. The **Your Permissions** drawer opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read your permissions">
    The drawer groups permissions under **Main Navigation**, **MSP Tools**, **Settings**, and **Other**. Each module shows badges such as **Read**, **Write**, **Manage**, **Create**, **Edit**, **Delete**, or **Export**. Viewing and making changes can need different permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the drawer">
    Close it when you are done. This screen is read-only and does not edit your role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check your company access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company list">
    In the **Access** card, click the company count beside **Company access**, for example **3 companies**. The **Company Access** drawer opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your company">
    The drawer lists the companies available to you. If the company you need is missing, ask your MSP to review your company assignment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Where the portal shows a company selector, check the selected company before you open a record. A role can allow a module while your company assignment still limits the records you see.

## Request a permission change

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect the details">
    Note the module, the action you need, the affected company, and what happens now. For example, "I can view tickets for our company, but I need permission to create a new ticket." Add the page address or the exact error message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the request">
    Contact your MSP. If you can create tickets, [open a support request](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). If you cannot reach the portal or the ticket form, use your IT provider's usual support channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Client Health and scoring access

**Client Health** and **Health Score Configuration** are MSP tools. Company users, including Company administrators, cannot open the Client Health dashboard, set manual risk flags, or change scoring. Ask your MSP to explain a health assessment.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A page is missing from the sidebar | Your personal or MSP navigation layout may hide it, or your role may not include it. Check **Settings > User Settings > Sidebar order** and **View details**. |
| A page opens but **Create** or **Edit** is missing | Your role may allow viewing without that action. Check the badges in **Your Permissions**. |
| A company or its records are missing | Open **Company Access** and check the company is listed, then check the selected company. |
| An integration tab is missing | The feature may not be enabled, or the integration or its data may not be available yet. Ask your MSP. |
| A page shows an access-denied message | Ask your MSP to check your role and company assignment. Reordering the sidebar does not grant access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my own permissions?">
    No. **Access > View details** is a read-only summary. Ask your MSP to change your role, permissions, or company access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I open Client Health or change its scoring as a Company user?">
    No. Client Health, manual risk flags, and scoring configuration are MSP tools. Company administrator status does not grant access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company administrator create users or change their roles?">
    No. The MSP manages company users. Ask your MSP to add a user, change a role, or adjust company access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can a colleague do something that I cannot?">
    Your colleague may have a different role or company assignment. Features, integrations, and navigation settings can also change what appears.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does showing a hidden page in my sidebar give me access?">
    No. Sidebar layout and permissions are separate. [Arrange your sidebar](/user-guides/account/arrange-your-sidebar) to organize available pages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see which companies I can access?">
    Go to **Settings > User Settings** and click the company count in the **Access** card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to save anything after viewing permissions?">
    No. Both drawers are read-only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
