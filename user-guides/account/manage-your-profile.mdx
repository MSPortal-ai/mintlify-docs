Update your own name, timezone, profile picture, and email preference. This page is for company users. Your IT provider manages shared settings, templates, and user permissions.

## Before you start

- Every signed-in user can edit their own profile.
- Open **Settings > User Settings**. This page shows your personal information, sign-in method, access summary, and email preferences.
- You cannot edit other people's accounts here. Your MSP manages roles and access.
- During impersonation, User Settings is read-only. Sign in as yourself to make changes.

## Edit your personal information

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the profile editor">
    Go to **Settings > User Settings** and click **Edit profile**. The **Personal information** drawer opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your details">
    Enter your **Full name**. You can also set an optional **Display name (optional)**, **Title (optional)**, and **Department (optional)**.

    Choose your **Timezone**, or select **Use Organization Default** to follow the organization's timezone. This is your personal preference. It does not change the organization's default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the changes">
    Click **Save changes** and wait for **Profile updated successfully**. Closing the drawer discards unsaved edits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your profile picture

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an image">
    On **Settings > User Settings**, click **Change Avatar** under your profile picture. Choose a PNG or JPEG image under 5 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Crop it">
    In **Crop Avatar**, adjust the crop, zoom, and rotation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click **Save & Apply** and wait for **Profile picture updated successfully!** The avatar saves separately from the **Personal information** drawer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review how you sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in card">
    On **Settings > User Settings**, click **Manage sign-in**. The page scrolls to the **Sign-in method** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read your sign-in details">
    The card shows your sign-in email and whether you use email and password or a Microsoft or Google workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage a Microsoft or Google account (optional)">
    If you sign in with Microsoft or Google, click **Manage in Microsoft** or **Manage in Google**. That opens the provider's account security page in a new tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The profile editor does not change your sign-in email or password.

## Turn product update emails on or off

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the card">
    On **Settings > User Settings**, find the **Email Notifications** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch the preference">
    Turn **Product update emails** on or off. It saves immediately, and there is no Save button. Wait for the confirmation message before you leave.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product/account-product-update-emails.png" alt="Product update emails switch and the message explaining the role default." width="1856" height="408" />

When the card says **Currently using your role's default setting**, your role controls the default. Changing the switch records your own preference.

<Note>
This switch controls product update emails about MSPortal features. It does not control ticket replies, security messages, or other notifications from your IT provider.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| My profile changes disappeared | Closing the drawer discards unsaved edits. Reopen it, make the changes, and click **Save changes**. |
| **Save changes** fails | Check that **Full name** is filled in and retry. If it keeps failing, send your MSP the error message. |
| My profile picture will not upload | Use a PNG or JPEG under 5 MB. Finish the crop and click **Save & Apply**. |
| I cannot see **Edit profile** | You are probably viewing the page while impersonating another user. Sign in as yourself. |
| I need to change my sign-in email | The profile editor has no sign-in email field. Ask your MSP. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does changing my display name change my permissions?">
    No. Names and personal preferences do not change your role or company access. Your MSP manages access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will turning off product update emails stop ticket emails?">
    No. It only controls product update emails. Ask your MSP about ticket and other notification settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the timezone change affect everyone?">
    No. **Timezone** is your personal preference. Choose **Use Organization Default** to follow the organization's timezone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
