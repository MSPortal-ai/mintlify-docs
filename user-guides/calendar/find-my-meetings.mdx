Use My Meetings to see the meetings where you are a participant, split into upcoming and past. It is for MSP staff and for company users with meeting access.

## Before you start

- Check the company selector at the top of the app. My Meetings follows it and does not include meetings from every company.
- The page lists meetings where your account is a participant, including a meeting that has your attendee email. Creating a meeting does not by itself put it here. If a meeting is missing, ask your MSP to check the attendee and your company access.

## Find a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Meetings">
    Go to **Calendar**, open **Calendar Menu** and choose **My Meetings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    Choose **Upcoming Meetings** or **Past Meetings** in the header.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/calendar-my-meetings-tabs.png" alt="My Meetings tabs with Upcoming Meetings selected and Past Meetings available." width="548" height="74" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type in **Search meetings...**. Search matches the meeting title and the company name only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Click the filter button next to the search box. The drawer has **Companies**, **Attendees**, **Event Types** and **Status**. Your choices apply as you make them. Click **Apply Filters** to close the drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a meeting">
    Click the meeting title to open the meeting dialog. Use the **...** menu at the end of a row for more actions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove filters, click the chips under the search box, click the clear option next to them, or click **Reset** in the drawer. These do not change the company selector.

**Status** offers **Scheduled**, **Confirmed**, **Completed**, **Canceled**, **Rescheduled** and **Pending Notes**. Canceled meetings are not hidden here.

An event is **Upcoming** until its start time. From its start time on, it is **Past**, even while it is running or still marked **Scheduled**. The tab does not record attendance or completion.

The table shows 10 meetings per page. Use the page controls under the table. On a small screen, scroll the table sideways to reach the other columns.

Changing the company selector returns you to **Upcoming Meetings** and the first page and closes an open dialog. Your search and filters can stay, so check them if the new view looks empty.

## Read the summary counts

| Summary | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **All Events** | All loaded meetings and busy blocks, before your search and filters. |
| **Upcoming** and **Past** | Events that match your search and filters, split by start time. |
| **Rescheduled** and **Canceled** | Meetings with that status, before your search and filters. Busy blocks are not counted. |
| **Missing Notes** | Past meetings with no notes, capped at three. It is not a full backlog count. |

The counts use different filters, so a search can change **Upcoming** and **Past** without changing **All Events**. None of them confirm attendance or that an invitation was delivered.

## Read meetings and busy blocks

- A meeting row shows the **Meeting Title**, **Date & Time**, **Client Company**, **Attendees**, **Type** and **Status**. Attendee shows the first name and a **+ more** count. You appear as **You**. **Unknown** means a name could not be resolved.
- A row with the type **Calendar Block** is synced busy time from a calendar. It cannot be opened, and its company shows **N/A**. A **Companies** filter hides these rows. To change the busy time, use its source calendar.
- A recurring series is one row. Use the [main Calendar](/user-guides/calendar/find-calendar-events) to see each occurrence.
- Times use your MSP's calendar time zone.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A meeting I organized is missing | You may not be a participant. Add yourself as an attendee, or ask your MSP to check your access. |
| A running meeting is under **Past Meetings** | Past starts at the start time. |
| The list looks empty | Check the company selector, the chips under the search box, and the **Upcoming Meetings** or **Past Meetings** tab. |
| Busy blocks disappeared | A **Companies** filter excludes them. Clear it. |
| I cannot edit a meeting | Editing needs Write or Manage access to Calendar or Meetings. Opening a meeting does not need it. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Past mean I attended the meeting?">
    No. It only means the start time has passed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are canceled meetings hidden?">
    No. Use **Status** to show or find them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Apply Filters change a meeting?">
    No. It closes the drawer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my search not change All Events or Canceled?">
    Those counts use the loaded meetings before your search and filters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Missing Notes the total of meetings that need notes?">
    No. It shows at most three past meetings without notes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export My Meetings to CSV?">
    No. This page has no CSV export.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
