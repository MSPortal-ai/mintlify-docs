The wrap-up survey is a quick internal rating of how a meeting went. Answers are only visible to your team, never to the client. This page is for MSP staff. It is separate from the client-facing feedback surveys.

## Before you start

- You need Write access to Calendar or Meetings, and an active subscription.
- The wrap-up must be on for the meeting's type in **Settings > Operations > Calendar > Post-Meeting**.
- Your administrator controls the questions and which are required. See [Manage survey questions](/user-guides/calendar/manage-meeting-survey-questions).

## Answer the survey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wrap-up">
    Open the meeting and click **Wrap-Up**, **Start wrap-up** or **Continue wrap-up**. You can open it before the meeting is marked **Completed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Survey">
    Click **Survey** in the step bar. The page is titled **How did the meeting go?**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer each question">
    Click a number for a rating or scale, **Yes** or **No**, pick an option from a list, or type text. Required questions have a red asterisk.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/meeting-wrap-up-rating-questions.png" alt="Example engagement and meeting-value questions with one-to-five rating buttons" width="590" height="300" />
    </Frame>
    Your MSP's questions can differ from this example.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Continue">
    This saves your answers and opens **Transcript & AI**. If a required question is empty, **Please answer the required questions before continuing.** appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Clicking an answer does not save it. Only **Continue** does. **Save & exit**, and switching to another step, do not save unsent answers.

## Revise saved answers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Return to Survey">
    Click **Survey** in the step bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your answers">
    Edit them as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Continue">
    This replaces the saved answers. The survey is one record for the meeting, so check with colleagues before you replace it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Skip the survey

Click **Skip this step** to mark the survey skipped and go to **Transcript & AI**. Skipping clears any answers already saved for the meeting. To keep saved answers, click **Transcript & AI** in the step bar or **Save & exit** instead.

The check mark on **Survey** means the survey was submitted. It does not mean the meeting is complete or a summary was sent.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The survey says **No survey questions are configured** | Every question is off. Ask an administrator to turn one on, or skip the step. |
| I see questions I did not create | Your MSP has no questions yet, so the built-in defaults show. |
| My answers are gone | You left the step without clicking **Continue**, or someone skipped the survey. |
| **Please answer the required questions before continuing.** | Fill in every question marked with an asterisk. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can company users fill in this survey?">
    No. It is for MSP staff. Client feedback surveys are separate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is No the same as leaving a Yes / No question blank?">
    No. **No** is an answer. Blank is unanswered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does each technician get their own response?">
    No. The meeting has one survey response.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does submitting the survey finish the meeting or send email?">
    No. It saves the survey and opens **Transcript & AI**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
