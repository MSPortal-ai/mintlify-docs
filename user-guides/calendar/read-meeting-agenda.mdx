Use the Meeting Agenda to see the budgets, tickets, reports and other items your MSP prepared for a meeting. Company users and MSP staff can read it. MSP staff with meeting write access can also build it.

## Before you start

- You need access to the meeting and its company. Reading the agenda does not let you edit the meeting or the linked records.
- To add, reorder, hide or remove items, you need Write access to Calendar or Meetings. Ask your MSP if the **Add Item** button is missing.

## Read an agenda item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting page">
    Open [List View](/user-guides/calendar/find-meetings-list) or [My Meetings](/user-guides/calendar/find-my-meetings), click the **...** menu on the meeting and choose **Open Meeting Page**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to Meeting Agenda">
    The top of the card shows the item count and how many are visible in presentation, for example **8 items** and **6 visible in presentation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand an item">
    Click the item's title row to show its details, and click again to collapse it. Click the title, not the drag handle, eye or trash icons.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/calendar/meeting-page-agenda.png" alt="Meeting Agenda card on the meeting page with agenda items" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch views (staff)">
    With more than three items, MSP staff see a **Group View** or **List View** button. Group View puts similar items together. The meeting also groups items automatically when it has several of one type, or tickets from more than one provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Grouping only changes how items are laid out. It does not add or remove records. An expanded row can show a short summary, such as a budget's fiscal year, and not the whole record. Use the source module for the full record.

## Add items to the agenda (MSP staff)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Item">
    The button is in the Meeting Agenda header. On an empty agenda, click **Add First Item**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a category or search">
    Type in **Search for items to add to agenda...**, or pick a category. The categories are **Budget**, **Planner Item**, **Report**, **Ticket**, **Compliance Check**, **Goal**, **Project**, **Device**, **Training**, **Invoice**, **License** and **Quote**. Use **Back to categories** to go back.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/meeting-agenda-add-types.png" alt="Excerpt of the MSP Add Items to Agenda chooser showing Budget, Planner Item, Report, Ticket, Compliance Check, Goal, Project and Device." width="520" height="800" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the items">
    Tick each item to add. The button shows how many you picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Selected">
    The items are added right away and **Item added to agenda** confirms it. Opening the chooser or searching adds nothing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For your own content, open **Custom Content** and choose **Text Block** or **Divider**. A text block is added at once with the title **New Text Block**. Edit it from its row.

## Organize the agenda (MSP staff)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reorder">
    In List View, drag an item by its handle. The new order saves at once. Items cannot be dragged in Group View.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show or hide in presentation">
    Click **Hide from presentation** (the eye icon) on an item. Click **Show in presentation** to bring it back. A hidden item stays in the workspace, dimmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set presentation settings">
    Click **Presentation Settings** on an item. The **General** tab has **Show in overview slide**, **Show as detail slide**, **Slide Template** and **Custom Title (optional)**. **Display Fields** picks which fields show. Click **Save Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a text block or notes">
    Click **Edit** on the item to change its title, content or **Presenter notes (not shown in presentation)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove an item">
    Click **Remove item** (the trash icon). **Item removed from agenda** confirms it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Removing an item removes it from the meeting only. The budget, ticket or other source record stays. Editing an agenda title or notes does not change the source record.

<Warning>
The eye icon controls the presentation only. It is not a privacy setting. Anyone who can open the meeting can still see a hidden item.
</Warning>

## What the agenda can show

The agenda can show budgets, Planner items, reports, tickets, compliance checks, goals, projects, devices, invoices, licenses, training, quotes, text blocks and dividers. Each item is a link to its record, not a copy of everything in the source module.

Items show the data of the linked record when you open the page. They do not refresh while the page stays open. Missing detail or an error does not mean the source record is healthy, approved or paid. Ask your MSP to check the source and your access.

### Quote providers

Agenda **Quote** items use **Quoter** quotes. The separate [Quotes module](/user-guides/quotes/index) supports Salesbuildr, QuoteWerks and Quoter. A missing Salesbuildr or QuoteWerks quote in the agenda does not mean MSPortal does not support them.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I do not see **Add Item** | You need Write access to Calendar or Meetings. Ask your MSP. |
| An expanded item shows little detail | Some types show a summary. Open the source module for more. |
| An item is dimmed | It is hidden from the presentation. Click **Show in presentation** to include it. |
| I cannot drag items | You are in Group View or you lack write access. Switch to **List View**. |
| A quote I want is not in the picker | The agenda picker only lists Quoter quotes. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does expanding an agenda row save a change?">
    No. Dragging, the eye icon, edits and removal are the actions that save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are there more items than visible-in-presentation items?">
    The total includes items hidden from the presentation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding an item make it confidential?">
    No. Presentation visibility is not an access control.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does removing an item delete the original record?">
    No. It removes the meeting link. Removing a text block deletes that text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Add Item wait for a final Save?">
    No. Clicking **Add Selected**, or choosing a text block or divider, adds the item at once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company administrators add or reorder items?">
    No. That needs Write access to Calendar or Meetings, which your MSP assigns.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
