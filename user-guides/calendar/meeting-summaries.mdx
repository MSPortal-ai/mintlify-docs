After a meeting, the wrap-up walks your team through three steps: an internal survey, the transcript with AI notes, and the client summary email. You can stop at any point and continue later. You can also open **Send Summary** alone when all you need is the email. This page is for MSP staff.

Company recipients can [understand a meeting summary](/user-guides/calendar/understand-meeting-summary).

## Before you start

- You need Write access to Calendar or Meetings, and an active subscription.
- The wrap-up must be on for the meeting's type. An administrator sets this in **Settings > Operations > Calendar > Post-Meeting**. See [Configure the post-meeting workflow](/user-guides/calendar/configure-post-meeting).
- **Send Summary** needs a meeting with a company set.

## Start the wrap-up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mark the meeting Completed, or open Wrap-Up">
    On the meeting page, set **Status** to **Completed**. The **Wrap up this meeting?** prompt appears when **Prompt when a meeting is completed** is on. You can also click **Wrap-Up** in the page header at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the prompt">
    Click **Start wrap-up**. **Not now** closes the prompt and leaves **Wrap-Up** available. **Don't ask for this meeting** stops the prompt for this meeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through the three steps">
    The steps are **Survey**, **Transcript & AI** and **Send Summary**. Click a step at the top of the page to jump to it. **Back**, **Skip this step** and **Continue** are at the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue later if needed">
    Click **Save & exit** in the page header. A meeting with a wrap-up in progress shows **Post-meeting wrap-up in progress** with a **Continue wrap-up** button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 1: Survey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer the questions">
    Rate how the meeting went. Answers are only visible to your team. See [Complete the internal survey](/user-guides/calendar/complete-meeting-survey).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Continue">
    This saves the answers and opens **Transcript & AI**. **Skip this step** records the survey as skipped and clears any saved answers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Transcript & AI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the transcript">
    Upload a **.txt**, **.vtt**, **.srt** or **.md** file up to 5 MB, or paste text and click **Use pasted text**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate notes & action items">
    AI drafts notes and action items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and click Add to meeting">
    Nothing reaches the meeting until you click **Add to meeting**. See [Add a meeting transcript](/user-guides/calendar/add-meeting-transcript).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Send Summary

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for the AI draft">
    When AI is available, the step drafts the email on its own. Under **Based on** you can see which sources it used. On the standalone **Send Summary** page, click **Generate with AI** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check recipients and subject">
    Review **To**, **CC** and **Subject**. See [Draft and send a meeting summary](/user-guides/calendar/send-meeting-summary).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send Email">
    The wrap-up is marked complete. Click **Finish without sending** to complete it with no email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send only the summary

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting page">
    Go to **Calendar**, open the meeting, then click **Send Summary** in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write or generate the email">
    Click **Generate with AI**, or type your own in **Email Content**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send Email">
    The page opens empty. Nothing is generated or sent until you ask.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What is saved where

- **Survey** answers save when you click **Continue**. Leaving the step without it loses unsent answers.
- A transcript file saves when you choose it. Pasted text needs **Use pasted text**. Generated notes save in the wrap-up and reach the meeting only through **Add to meeting**.
- The unsent email lives only in the open page. **Save & exit** does not keep it, so copy it first if you need to leave.

## After you send

- The wrap-up is marked complete. You can reopen any step.
- If **Update the QBR log automatically** is on, MSPortal writes a QBR entry for the company and opens the meeting's **QBR Log** tab. See [Account Notes and QBR Log](/user-guides/calendar/account-notes).
- Check the email, the optional PSA ticket note and the QBR entry separately. Each can succeed or fail on its own.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| There is no **Wrap-Up** button | The wrap-up is off for this meeting type, or you are a company user. Check the Post-Meeting settings. |
| No completion prompt appeared | **Prompt when a meeting is completed** is off, or the prompt was dismissed for this meeting. Click **Wrap-Up**. |
| **Send Summary** does not open | The meeting needs a company. Set one and try again. |
| My unsent email is gone | The email is not saved. Write it again. |
| No QBR entry appeared | See the troubleshooting in [Account Notes and QBR Log](/user-guides/calendar/account-notes). |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need the whole wrap-up to send a summary?">
    No. Use **Send Summary** on the meeting page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Save & exit save everything?">
    No. It keeps your place. Unsent survey answers and the unsent email are lost.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening Wrap-Up only read the meeting?">
    No. The first visit creates a progress record, and opening **Send Summary** can start an AI draft.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Finish without sending send an email?">
    No. It completes the wrap-up only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do company users fill in the wrap-up survey?">
    No. It is for MSP staff.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
