Use this page to attach a transcript to a meeting and, if you want, have AI draft meeting notes and action items from it. It is for MSP staff. The transcript step is part of the internal post-meeting wrap-up, and company users never see it.

## Before you start

- You need Write access to Calendar or Meetings, and an active subscription.
- The wrap-up must be on for the meeting's type. Check **Settings > Operations > Calendar > Post-Meeting**. See [Configure post-meeting settings](/user-guides/calendar/configure-post-meeting).
- AI generation also needs **AI transcript processing** turned on in that tab, and AI must be available for your account. You can still save a transcript without AI.

## Open the transcript step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting">
    Go to **Calendar** and open the meeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Wrap-Up">
    The button is in the page header. If a wrap-up is already started, click **Continue wrap-up** in the banner instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Transcript & AI">
    Finish or skip **Survey** first, or click the **Transcript & AI** step at the top of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Paste transcript text

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste the conversation">
    Click **Or paste the transcript** and paste the text. Typing alone does not save it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Use pasted text">
    This button appears once there is text. Wait for the **Transcript saved** message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the saved row">
    A row with the character count replaces the paste box. This confirms the transcript is stored. It does not run AI or change the meeting notes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload a transcript file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the file">
    Click **Upload transcript file**, or drag a file onto that area. Supported types are **.txt**, **.vtt**, **.srt** and **.md**, up to 5 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to save">
    The file saves as soon as you choose it. There is no separate Save button. A **Transcript saved** message confirms it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

MSPortal keeps the conversation text, so VTT and SRT timestamp lines and caption tags are removed and the character count can be lower than the file size. The stored text can be up to 500,000 characters. An empty file shows **The file appears to be empty**. Keep your original file, because this step has no download button.

## Replace or remove a transcript

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product/meeting-transcript-replace-controls.png" alt="Replace transcript button and red trash control for a saved transcript" width="370" height="72" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Replace with a file">
    Click **Replace transcript**, pick the new file and wait for **Transcript saved**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or replace with pasted text">
    Click the red trash button, confirm **Remove the transcript and any AI output generated from it?**, then paste the new text and click **Use pasted text**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Replacing or removing a transcript clears the AI notes and action items drafted from it. Notes and action items you already added to the meeting stay.
</Warning>

You cannot edit saved transcript text in place. Correct the text in your source and replace it.

## Generate notes and action items with AI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Generate notes & action items">
    The button shows under the saved transcript when AI is available. If AI is off, the page says **AI is not available for your account, but the transcript is saved with the meeting.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    Under **Review AI output**, edit the notes. For each action item, you can change the description, **Assignee**, priority and due date. Clear the box next to an action item to leave it out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to add">
    Tick **Add these notes to the meeting notes** to append the notes. Leave it unticked to add only action items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add to meeting">
    Nothing is saved to the meeting until you click this. A message such as **Added notes and 3 action items to the meeting** confirms it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After a successful add, the output shows **Applied to meeting**. Generate again if you replace the transcript and want a fresh draft.

Review names and dates before you add them. If an error appears, check the meeting before you retry, because notes and action items are saved separately.

## Finish the step

- Click **Continue** to go to **Send Summary**. The summary step can draft an email with AI right away.
- Click **Skip this step** when there is no transcript.
- Click **Save & exit** in the page header to leave the wrap-up. Text left in the paste box is not saved, but a saved transcript stays.

## What each button does

| Action | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Use pasted text** or choosing a file | Stores the transcript with the meeting. |
| **Generate notes & action items** | Creates an AI draft for you to review. |
| **Add to meeting** | Saves the reviewed notes and action items to the meeting. |
| **Send Email** on the summary step | Sends the summary email. |

## Capture a transcript from a meeting (branch preview)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on recording for the meeting">
    In Calendar, enable **Record and coach this meeting** below **Teams meeting**, confirm recording permission and save. The feature is off by default and needs administrator setup. It is part of the US staff pilot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the meeting">
    Wait for capture and transcript recovery to finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Use in wrap-up">
    In Meeting Coach, click **Use in wrap-up** to add the saved transcript here. An existing wrap-up transcript is kept. Replacing it needs a separate, explicit action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Meeting Coach pilot](/user-guides/calendar/meetings#meeting-coach-pilot-branch-preview) for recording, scheduling and cancellation.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I do not see a **Wrap-Up** button | The wrap-up is off, or it is off for this meeting type. Check **Settings > Operations > Calendar > Post-Meeting**. |
| **File is too large (max 5 MB)** | Trim the file or paste the text instead. |
| **The file appears to be empty** | The file had no conversation text after cleanup. Check the file or paste the text. |
| **Could not read the file** | Save the transcript as .txt, .vtt, .srt or .md and upload it again. |
| The paste box shows again after I saved | Reload the page. The saved transcript shows once the page loads fresh. If it is still missing, ask your administrator to check. |
| **Generate notes & action items** is missing | AI is off for your account or **AI transcript processing** is off in **Settings > Operations > Calendar > Post-Meeting**. |
| **Failed to apply to the meeting** | Open the meeting and check what saved before retrying. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does pasting save the transcript?">
    No. Click **Use pasted text** and wait for **Transcript saved**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which transcript files work?">
    TXT, VTT, SRT and Markdown files up to 5 MB.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I download the original transcript here?">
    No. Keep your original file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does saving a transcript create notes or action items?">
    No. Generate the AI draft, review it, then click **Add to meeting**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I save a transcript when AI is off?">
    Yes. Saving is separate from AI generation. The summary step can still use the saved text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to the AI draft if I replace the transcript?">
    The draft is cleared. Notes and action items already added to the meeting stay.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
