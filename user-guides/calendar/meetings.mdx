Each meeting has its own page with its details, attendees, notes, agenda, action items and files. It is for MSP staff and for company users who can access the meeting. Your MSP sets what you can change.

## Before you start

- You need access to the meeting and its company.
- Saving notes, changing the status, building the agenda and adding action items need Write access to Calendar or Meetings. Without it, the controls are read-only or rejected.
- **Wrap-Up** and the **Private Notes** and **QBR Log** tabs are for MSP staff.

## Open a meeting page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the meeting">
    Open [List View](/user-guides/calendar/find-meetings-list) or [My Meetings](/user-guides/calendar/find-my-meetings) under **Calendar > Calendar Menu**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Click the **...** menu at the end of the row and choose **Open Meeting Page**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The header shows the title, date and time. Its buttons are **Present**, **Wrap-Up** (staff, when on for the meeting type), **Send Summary** and **Edit Meeting Details**. **Edit Meeting Details** opens the meeting in the Calendar so you can change its schedule, attendees and invitations.

## Read the details and attendees

**Meeting Details** shows **Company**, **Meeting Type**, **Status**, **Location** and **Purpose / Agenda**. **Attendees** lists everyone on the meeting with their email. The panel only displays attendees. To add or remove people, use **Edit Meeting Details**.

Change **Status** with the **Status** list: **Scheduled**, **Confirmed**, **Completed**, **Canceled**, **Rescheduled** or **Pending Notes**. It saves as soon as you pick it. Choosing **Canceled** asks you to confirm first.

## Take meeting notes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a notes tab">
    Under **Meeting Notes**, choose **Public Notes**. Staff also have **Private Notes**, which client users never see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the notes">
    The editor supports formatting, lists and links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save Notes">
    Notes do not save automatically. **Save Notes** saves the tab you are on, so save each tab you edit. **Saved!** confirms it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For an on-site tech visit, the card is **Visit Notes** and has **Save Draft** and **Submit Visit Notes**. Submitting marks the visit **Completed**.

## Build the agenda

Click **Add Item** in **Meeting Agenda**, pick the items and click **Add Selected**. You can drag items to reorder them and hide any from the presentation. See [Read meeting agenda items](/user-guides/calendar/read-meeting-agenda) for every step.

## Track action items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Action">
    The button is in the **Action Items** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the dialog">
    Enter a **Description** (required), a **Priority** and a **Due Date**, and optional **Notes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Action Item">
    The item appears in the list. Use the status list on each item to set **Pending**, **In Progress**, **Completed** or **Cancelled**. Use the pencil to edit and the trash can to delete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Attach files

In **File Attachments**, click to upload or drop a file. The upload starts at once and does not need **Save Notes**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The page shows **Meeting Not Found** | Check the company selector and ask your MSP to check your access. |
| **Save Notes** does nothing or errors | You may lack Write access to Calendar or Meetings. |
| Text I typed in the other notes tab is gone | Each tab saves separately. Save before you switch. |
| There is no **Wrap-Up** button | The wrap-up is off for this meeting type, or you are a company user. |
| The **QBR Log** tab is missing | It needs a staff account and a meeting with a company. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are notes saved automatically?">
    No. Click **Save Notes**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does one button save everything on the page?">
    No. Status, agenda changes, action items and files each save on their own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does reading the page send a meeting summary?">
    No. **Send Summary** is a separate step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do all quote providers work in the agenda?">
    No. The agenda's Quote item uses Quoter. The Quotes module supports Salesbuildr, QuoteWerks and Quoter.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Meeting Coach pilot (branch preview)

Meeting Coach is a private MSP staff feature in the US pilot. Administrators must
first enable **Meeting Coach** for the pilot tenant and complete the capture
setup. It is off by default; clients and EU tenants cannot enable recording.

1. Open the meeting in **Calendar** and enable **Teams meeting**.
2. Enable **Record and coach this meeting** directly below Teams.
3. Confirm that participants have been informed and the required permission to
   record and transcribe has been obtained, then save the meeting.
4. Publish the calendar invitation through the existing **Save & send** workflow.
   Capture waits for the generated Teams link and follows schedule changes.
5. Open **Meeting coach** from the meeting page for the live transcript and
   private guidance. The company comes from the meeting record.

Each choice applies to one occurrence. For a series-level edit it covers only
the first occurrence; enable later occurrences separately. To cancel capture,
turn the same switch off and save, or use **Stop capture** in the coach.
Connecting a calendar alone never opts meetings into recording.

Recall recovery data is kept for seven days. Saved transcripts remain attached to
the MSPortal meeting. When capture has finished, **Use in wrap-up** copies the
transcript into the existing review workflow; it does not automatically publish
notes or approve client work.
