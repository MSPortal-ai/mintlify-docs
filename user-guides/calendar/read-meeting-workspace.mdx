Use this page to read a meeting's page without changing anything. It is for company users who can access the meeting and for MSP staff. To edit, see [Meeting Pages](/user-guides/calendar/meetings).

## Before you start

- You need access to the meeting and its company. A visible control does not mean you may save a change.
- The meeting page and the meeting dialog in the Calendar are different views. The page has the notes, agenda, action items and files.

## Open the meeting page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the meeting">
    Open [List View](/user-guides/calendar/find-meetings-list) or [My Meetings](/user-guides/calendar/find-my-meetings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open its page">
    Click the **...** menu on the meeting row and choose **Open Meeting Page**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll through the cards">
    Read **Meeting Details**, **Attendees**, **Meeting Notes**, **Meeting Agenda**, **Action Items** and **File Attachments**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you see **Meeting Not Found**, check the company selector and ask your MSP to check the meeting and your access. It does not always mean the meeting was deleted.

## Read the details and attendees

- The header shows the date and the start and end times in your MSP's calendar time zone. Open the meeting in the Calendar for the end date of a multi-day meeting.
- **Meeting Details** shows company, meeting type, status, location and **Purpose / Agenda**. When the page has no location, **Location** shows the company's office. Check the invitation for the real venue. **Purpose / Agenda** shows the meeting notes when there is no separate description.
- **Attendees** lists each person by name and email. It does not show who accepted or attended. MSPortal users are listed under **Internal**. Guests invited by email are listed under **External**, or **No external attendees** appears when there are none.

## Read the notes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meeting Notes">
    Company users see **Public Notes**. MSP staff also see **Private Notes**, and **QBR Log** when the meeting has a company.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/meeting-notes-tabs.png" alt="MSP meeting notes tabs showing Public Notes, Private Notes and QBR Log." width="614" height="72" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read, and save only if you edited">
    **Save Notes** saves the tab you are on. It is needed only after you type. Notes do not save automatically, and leaving with unsaved text loses it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The [QBR log](/user-guides/calendar/read-account-notes) is an internal company record and is separate from public notes. On a technician visit, **Save Draft** and **Submit Visit Notes** replace **Save Notes**. Submitting also marks the visit **Completed**, so avoid it when you are only reading.

## Read the agenda and action items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expand agenda items">
    In **Meeting Agenda**, click an item's title row. See [Read meeting agenda items](/user-guides/calendar/read-meeting-agenda).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Action Items">
    Each item shows its description, priority, due date, assignee and status. Completed items have a crossed-out description.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An action item's status is separate from the meeting status and from any ticket or budget it mentions. A completed action does not mean a budget was approved or a ticket was resolved. The status list on an action item saves as soon as you change it, so leave it alone when you are only reading.

## Download files

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open File Attachments">
    Find the card at the bottom of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Download">
    Click **Download** next to the file. It downloads that file only, not a PDF or CSV of the whole meeting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choosing or dropping a file in the upload area starts an upload at once. If the list is empty when you expect a file, ask your MSP to check the attachment and your access.

## What the header buttons do

**Present**, **Wrap-Up**, **Send Summary** and **Edit Meeting Details** open separate pages. You do not need them to read the workspace. **Edit Meeting Details** opens the meeting in the Calendar, where changes need the right access.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Meeting Not Found** | Check the company selector, then ask your MSP to check your access. |
| **Location** shows an office name | The meeting has no location. Check the invitation. |
| A file is missing | Ask your MSP to check the attachment and your access. |
| I cannot save notes | You need Write access to Calendar or Meetings. Ask your MSP. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the Calendar meeting dialog the same as Meeting Details?">
    No. The dialog and the page are separate views. Only the page has notes, agenda, action items and files.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do attendee names confirm who accepted?">
    No. The list shows identities only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are notes saved automatically?">
    No. Click **Save Notes** on the tab you edited.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Save Notes save both tabs?">
    No. It saves the active tab only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the meeting Status wait for Save Notes?">
    No. It saves as soon as you pick it, when you have access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Present, Wrap-Up or Send Summary to read the agenda?">
    No. Read it on the page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
