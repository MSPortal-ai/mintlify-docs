Use **Send Summary** to email a follow-up to the people who attended a meeting. You can write it yourself or have AI draft it. This page is for MSP staff. Company recipients can [understand a meeting summary](/user-guides/calendar/understand-meeting-summary) and ask their MSP for corrections.

## Before you start

- You need Write access to Calendar or Meetings, and an active subscription.
- The meeting must have a company set.
- To add the summary to a ticket, a default PSA must be configured and the company's tickets synced.

## Open the composer and write the email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting page">
    Go to **Calendar > Calendar Menu > List View** or **My Meetings**. Click the **...** menu on the meeting and choose **Open Meeting Page**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send Summary">
    The button is in the page header. The page opens with **To**, **Subject** and **Email Content**. Nothing is generated or sent yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick length and tone (optional)">
    Under **AI draft**, set **Length** to **Brief**, **Standard** or **Detailed**, and **Tone** to **Friendly**, **Neutral** or **Executive**. The defaults are **Standard** and **Friendly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate with AI (optional)">
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/meeting-summary-generate-button.png" alt="Generate with AI button for requesting a meeting summary draft" width="320" height="80" />
    </Frame>
    Wait for **Draft ready. Review it before sending.** Or skip this step and type your own text in **Email Content**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    Check dates, amounts, names and commitments against the meeting. Remove internal details that should not be emailed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
The unsent email is not saved. Cancel, leaving or reloading the page discards it. **Save & exit** in the wrap-up does not keep it either. Copy the text first if you need to come back.
</Warning>

Once the editor has text, the button reads **Regenerate**. A successful regeneration replaces the whole editor, including your edits. Changing length, tone or recipients does not redraft on its own.

### What Based on shows

After a draft, **Based on** lists the sources used. They are **Meeting notes**, **Agenda**, **Action items** and **Transcript**. **Transcript (shortened)** means a long transcript was cut for drafting. If the meeting has none of these, a message says the draft will be thin. The draft uses public notes, not **Private Notes**. Agenda items hidden from the presentation can still feed the draft.

Generating does not save notes, create action items or send anything.

## Set recipients and subject

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review To">
    **To** starts with the meeting participants' email addresses. That can include staff and other guests, so check every address. At least one recipient is required, up to 50.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people">
    Type a name or email in **Search people or type an email**. Pick a match with the arrow keys and **Enter** or **Tab**, or type a full address. The search covers meeting participants, assigned staff and the company's contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add CC (optional)">
    Open **CC**, up to 50 addresses. Click **Quick add assigned staff** names to add them. Remove a recipient with the **x** on its chip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the subject">
    It starts as **Meeting Summary: [meeting title]**. The limit is 500 characters.
  </Step>
</Steps>

These recipients only affect this email. The meeting's invitation list does not change. If you edit **To** after generating, update the greeting yourself or regenerate.

## Add the summary to a PSA ticket (optional)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the ticket option">
    Switch on **Add as note to [provider] ticket**. It shows when a default PSA is set. The provider is your configured PSA, such as ConnectWise, Autotask, Halo or Syncro.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the ticket">
    Type a ticket number or summary in **Search Ticket**. Results are limited to the meeting's company and the tickets you can see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the ticket">
    Check the number, title and company before you continue. If you turn the option on and pick no ticket, sending shows **Please select a ticket to link or uncheck the link option**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The note is added after the email is sent. It is a public ticket note, so write for the client. Adding the note needs ticket-note permission, which is separate from meeting write access. The email sends even if the note fails. If **No tickets found** shows, check that the company's tickets are synced from your default PSA.

## Send the email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the footer">
    The footer shows **Sending from your MSPortal account** and the recipient count, such as **To 3, CC 1**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send Email">
    There is no second review screen. Wait for the result. The email is sent from your MSP's configured MSPortal sending address, not your personal mailbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Result | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Please add at least one recipient** | Add a To address. |
| **Please enter a subject line** | Fill in **Subject**. |
| **Please generate or enter email content** | Write or generate the body. |
| **Meeting summary sent successfully via MSPortal!** | The sending service accepted the email. It does not prove delivery or that it was read. |
| **Email sent via MSPortal, but failed to link to ticket** | The email was sent. Fix the ticket note separately and do not resend. |
| An error with no clear result | Check for an existing email or ticket note before you retry. |

Sending marks an open wrap-up complete. It does not change the meeting's own status.

## Check the QBR follow-up

When **Update the QBR log automatically** is on, sending can start a QBR entry for the company and add new commitments as action items. A QBR sync to Autotask is separate. Email success does not mean any of these worked. Open the [QBR log](/user-guides/calendar/account-notes) and the meeting's action items to check. After sending from the wrap-up, the **QBR Log** tab opens. From the standalone page you return to the meeting page.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Send Summary** does not open | The meeting needs a company. Check your access with an administrator. |
| **Generate with AI** is missing | AI is not available for your account. Write the summary yourself. |
| A ticket does not show in the search | Check the default PSA, the meeting company and your ticket visibility. Then check sync. |
| I resent and now have two emails | Do not resend because of a failed ticket note. Fix the note separately. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does opening Send Summary send an email or generate a draft?">
    No. On the standalone page you click **Generate with AI** and **Send Email**. The wrap-up's summary step starts an AI draft on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Regenerate keep my edits?">
    No. It replaces the editor content.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are all To recipients company contacts?">
    No. They come from the meeting participants and can include staff.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a recipient invite them to the meeting?">
    No. It only changes who gets this email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the email sent from my Microsoft 365 mailbox?">
    No. Sending from a personal Microsoft 365 mailbox was retired. The email goes out from your MSP's configured sending address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Based on prove the draft is accurate?">
    No. It lists the sources only. Check the facts yourself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does sending complete the meeting?">
    No. It completes an open wrap-up, not the meeting status.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
