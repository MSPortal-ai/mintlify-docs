Use this page to mark periods when changes are restricted, such as month-end or a client's busy season. It is for MSP administrators. Company users can [understand freeze windows and report conflicts](/user-guides/calendar/understand-change-freezes).

A freeze window warns people about restricted periods. It does not cancel meetings, move provider events or block every booking.

## Before you start

- You need Calendar settings access and permission to manage Calendar settings (Manage access to Calendar).
- Tenant-wide windows can only be created or edited from **Global View**. Set the company selector at the top of the app to Global View first.
- A Specific site window needs an existing site (location) on the company. This editor does not create sites.

## Choose the scope

| Scope | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Tenant-wide** | A period that applies to your whole MSP. Needs Global View. |
| **Company-wide** | A period for one company. |
| **Specific site** | A period for one site of one company. |

In Global View a new window starts as **Tenant-wide**. In a company view it starts as **Company-wide** with that company selected. In a company view, tenant-wide windows still show in the list, but their edit and delete buttons are disabled.

## Create a freeze window

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Operations > Calendar">
    Select the **Freeze Windows** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add freeze window">
    The **Add freeze window** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the window">
    Enter a **Name** of 2 to 160 characters. Add a **Description** if you like, up to 2,000 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the scope">
    Open **Scope** and pick **Tenant-wide**, **Company-wide** or **Specific site**. Company-wide needs a **Company**. Specific site needs a **Company** and then a **Site**. Changing the company clears the site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the dates">
    Enter **Starts at** and **Ends at**. The end must be after the start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the recurrence">
    Open **Recurrence** and pick **One time**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Annual**. For a repeating window you can set **Repeat until**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Active">
    **Active** is on for a new window. Turn it off to save a window without showing it in the Calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Wait for **Freeze window saved**. The **Save** button stays disabled until the name, dates and scope fields are valid. **Cancel** closes the dialog without saving. This tab has no page-header Save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How repeating windows work

The start date anchors the repeat, and **Starts at** and **Ends at** define each occurrence.

| Recurrence | Schedule |
| --- | --- |
| **One time** | One occurrence at the dates you entered. |
| **Daily** | Every day from the start date. |
| **Weekly** | Every seven days, on the start date's weekday. |
| **Monthly** | The start date's day each month. In a shorter month it uses the last day. |
| **Annual** | The same month and day each year. It uses the month's last day when needed. |

**Repeat until** includes occurrences that start on that date. It does not cut an occurrence short. Leaving it blank repeats without an end date, but occurrences are only generated for a window of roughly the next 18 months. Open the Calendar to confirm the dates you need.

To turn a repeating window into **One time**, clear **Repeat until** first. A hidden old cutoff can stay saved and leave no visible occurrence.

## Check time zones

The dialog uses your browser's time zone and saves it with the window. There is no time zone picker. The list shows each window in its saved zone, while the Calendar shows your viewing zone. When you edit a window from a different zone, check the dates before you click **Save**. Saving applies your current browser zone.

## Edit, switch off or delete a window

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit">
    Click the pencil on the window, change the fields and click **Save**. **Cancel** keeps the saved version. Editing a repeating window changes every occurrence. You cannot edit one occurrence on its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch off">
    Edit the window, turn **Active** off and click **Save**. The window stays in the list with an **Inactive** badge, and its Calendar occurrences are removed. Turn **Active** back on and save to bring them back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    Click the trash can. The prompt **Delete freeze window "name"?** asks you to confirm. Confirm to delete the window and its occurrences, or cancel to keep it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deleting a freeze does not delete the meetings, changes or provider appointments that overlapped it.

## Check the result in the Calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Calendar">
    Go to **Calendar** and set the company selector and date range you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Changes">
    Open the layers menu and tick **Changes**. You need access to view changes, otherwise this layer is missing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Calendar Blocks">
    Tick **Calendar Blocks** in the same menu. Freeze windows draw as blocks in the Day, Week and Month views.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the block">
    Check the freeze name and its start and end. A change or appointment that overlaps a freeze shows a snowflake icon, and its details panel says **This event overlaps a freeze window.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Save** is disabled | Give the window a name of at least 2 characters, valid dates with the end after the start, and the Company or Site that the scope needs. |
| I can open settings but cannot manage freezes | Viewing settings and managing freezes are separate permissions. Ask an administrator to check your role. |
| A tenant-wide window is read-only | Switch to Global View. You need permission to manage tenant-wide settings. |
| The **Site** list is empty | Pick the **Company** first. The list shows that company's existing locations. |
| A saved window is not in the Calendar | Check **Active**, the scope, the date range and **Repeat until**. Turn on **Changes** and **Calendar Blocks**. An empty grid does not mean it is safe to schedule work. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a freeze stop meetings from being booked?">
    No. It shows restricted periods and flags overlaps. Follow your own scheduling and approval process.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I see a tenant-wide freeze but not edit it?">
    Tenant-wide windows stay visible in a company view. Switch to Global View with the right permission to edit or delete them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing Company keep the old Site?">
    No. The Site is cleared so you can pick a site of the new company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I prepare a freeze without turning it on?">
    Yes. Turn **Active** off before you save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does a monthly freeze starting on the 31st do?">
    It runs on the last day of any shorter month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my repeating freeze disappear after I edited its dates?">
    Check **Active**, the scope and **Repeat until**. An old cutoff before the new start date leaves no occurrences.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting a freeze delete overlapping meetings?">
    No. It removes the freeze and its occurrences only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
