Use this page to find and read the notes, QBR entries and Autotask notes saved for a company. It is for MSP staff. Company users cannot see account notes. They can read [shared meeting information](/user-guides/calendar/read-meeting-workspace) and [meeting summaries](/user-guides/calendar/understand-meeting-summary) instead.

## Before you start

- You need access to the company. Staff with Read access to Calendar, Meetings or Companies settings for that company can read its notes.
- To see Autotask notes, the company must be linked to an Autotask integration that has synced.

## Open a company's account notes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Companies">
    Select the company you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Notes tab">
    The tab lists account notes, QBR log entries and Autotask notes together. Each card shows a badge for its kind, **Account note** or **QBR log**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also open history from two other places.

- **A meeting's QBR Log tab.** Open a meeting that has a company set and select **QBR Log**. It lists the company's QBR entries. This meeting's entry is first and carries a **This meeting** badge.
- **The company overview's Account Notes card.** It shows the three most recent entries. Click **View all** to open the company's **Notes** tab. Click a row linked to a meeting to open that meeting.

## Search and filter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type in **Search account notes**. Search matches the title, author, note type, note text, **Discussed** text, decisions, and commitment descriptions and owners. Click **Clear search** to empty the box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by kind">
    Open **Filter by kind** and choose **All notes**, **Account notes** or **QBR log entries**. This filter is on the company **Notes** tab. A meeting's QBR Log shows QBR entries only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by note type">
    Open **Filter by note type** and pick a type such as an Autotask note type. The list is empty and the filter is disabled when no entry has a type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort">
    Open **Sort account notes** and choose **Newest first** or **Oldest first**. The current meeting's entry always stays first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset">
    Click **Clear** to remove the search, kind and note type filters. It keeps your sort choice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The line under the filters reads **Showing** and a count. The panel loads the 500 most recent entries. Search, filters and sorting work on those 500 only. There is no next page or export.

## Read an entry

- **Account notes** show a title, a date, the note text and the author. Click **Show full note** to expand a long note and **Show less** to collapse it. Neither changes the saved note.
- **QBR entries** show **Discussed**, **Agreed** and **Actioned**. Each commitment can show an owner and a due date. A count such as **2 of 5 open** counts that entry's commitments only.
- Click **Open meeting** to jump to the meeting a QBR entry is linked to. An entry made in company settings may not be linked to a meeting.

<Warning>
Clicking **Done** on a QBR commitment saves right away. It does not change the matching meeting action item or the Autotask copy.
</Warning>

## Understand the labels

| Label or button | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **From Autotask** | A note synced from Autotask. It is read-only here. Change it in Autotask. |
| **AI generated** | AI wrote the entry. It may have been edited since. |
| **Synced to Autotask** | The note was pushed to Autotask. Later edits do not update that copy unless you turn on the update option when you save. |
| **Autotask sync failed** | The note saved in MSPortal but the push to Autotask failed. Hover the badge to see the error, then click **Sync to Autotask** after you fix the cause. |
| **Edit** and **Delete** | Shown on notes written in MSPortal. Delete needs Manage access. |
| **Edited** | The date and time of the last manual edit. |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I cannot find the Notes tab | Open **Settings > Companies**, then open the company. The **Notes** tab sits in the tab row at the top. You need access to that company. |
| An old note is missing | The panel loads the 500 most recent entries. Older entries are not shown. |
| The note type filter is disabled | None of the loaded entries has a note type. Portal notes have a type only when you chose an Autotask note type. |
| Autotask notes are missing | Link the company to Autotask and let the integration sync. See [Autotask integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-autotask-integration). |
| **Edit** is missing on a note | Notes marked **From Autotask** cannot be edited here. You also need Write access to edit portal notes. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can company users read account notes or the QBR Log?">
    No. These are MSP staff views. Company users can read shared meeting information and meeting summaries.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the meeting's QBR Log show only that meeting?">
    No. It shows all QBR entries for the company, with this meeting's entry first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the overview card count every open commitment?">
    No. It shows the latest three entries. Each QBR row shows its own open-commitment count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Clear reset the sort?">
    No. It clears the search, kind and note type filters and keeps your sort.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does ticking Done update the meeting action item?">
    No. It changes the QBR commitment only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a note I pushed to Autotask listed once?">
    MSPortal hides the synced Autotask copy of a note it pushed, so you see the MSPortal note only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
