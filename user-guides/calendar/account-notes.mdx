Account Notes give each company one running record of the relationship. Notes you write in MSPortal, QBR log entries and notes synced from Autotask all appear in the same list. This page is for MSP staff.

A QBR log entry is a structured record of a business review. It lists what was **Discussed**, what was **Agreed** and what was **Actioned**. Each commitment under **Actioned** has an optional owner, an optional due date and a **Done** tick.

<Note>
Account notes are internal. Company users never see them. Company users can read [shared meeting information](/user-guides/calendar/read-meeting-workspace) and [meeting summaries](/user-guides/calendar/understand-meeting-summary) instead.
</Note>

## Before you start

- You need Write access to Calendar, Meetings or Companies settings for the company to add or edit entries. Deleting needs Manage access.
- The **QBR Log** tab only appears on meetings that have a company set.
- Generating a QBR entry with AI needs Write access to Calendar or Meetings, and an active subscription.
- Pushing notes to Autotask needs an active Autotask integration with the company linked. See [Autotask integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-autotask-integration).

## Where to find account history

| Where | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings > Companies**, open the company, then the **Notes** tab | The full history. It includes account notes, QBR log entries and Autotask notes. |
| A meeting's **QBR Log** tab | The company's QBR entries, with this meeting's entry first. |
| The company overview's **Account Notes** card | The three most recent entries. **View all** opens the **Notes** tab. |

## Generate a QBR entry from a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting">
    Go to **Calendar**, open a meeting that has a company set, and select the **QBR Log** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate from this meeting">
    MSPortal builds the entry from the meeting notes, agenda, action items and transcript. It also reads the company's recent QBR entries, so follow-ups on earlier commitments are picked up. The entry is saved as soon as it is generated. A **QBR log entry generated** message confirms it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the entry">
    Check **Discussed**, **Agreed** and **Actioned**. Click **Edit** to fix anything that is wrong, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Commitments from the entry are also added to the meeting's action items. An owner whose name matches one staff member is assigned automatically. Any other owner, such as a client contact, is left unassigned and the name is kept in the item's notes. Commitments that match an existing action item are skipped. Later QBR edits and **Done** ticks do not change the action items.

## Regenerate an entry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the QBR Log tab">
    Open the meeting and select **QBR Log**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Regenerate with AI">
    This button replaces **Generate from this meeting** once the meeting has an entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The **Regenerate this entry?** dialog warns that the entry will be replaced. Click **Regenerate with AI** to continue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Regenerating replaces handwritten edits. A commitment keeps its **Done** tick only when the new commitment has the same description, ignoring capital letters. If the entry was already pushed to Autotask, the Autotask copy is updated too.
</Warning>

## Write entries automatically after a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Operations > Calendar">
    Select the **Post-Meeting** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Update the QBR log automatically">
    This setting is on by default. After a meeting summary is sent, MSPortal writes the QBR entry for the meeting's company with AI. The meeting's **QBR Log** tab shows **Writing the log entry** until it lands, usually within a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether to push to Autotask (optional)">
    If Autotask is connected, set **Push QBR log entries to Autotask as** to a note type. Leave it on **Do not push to Autotask** to keep automatic entries in MSPortal only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    The **Save** button is in the page header.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Automatic entries are skipped for:

- Meetings with no company.
- Meetings of type **On-site Tech Visit**, **Service Call** or **Internal Meeting**.
- Entries you already edited by hand, including a saved **Done** tick.
- All meetings, while **Update the QBR log automatically** is off.

You can always write an entry yourself with **Generate from this meeting**.

## Track commitments

Each QBR entry shows a count such as **2 of 5 open** next to **Actioned**. Tick **Done** on a saved entry and the change saves immediately. The count belongs to that entry only. The company overview card shows each recent QBR entry's own open count, not a company-wide total.

## Save notes into Autotask

This applies to companies linked to an Autotask integration. Other companies keep notes in MSPortal.

1. Open the note dialog and choose an **Autotask note type**.
2. Turn on **Push to Autotask when saved**.
3. Click **Save**.

Editing a pushed note and saving with **Update the Autotask copy when saved** turned on updates the same Autotask note. The full steps and failure handling are in [Create and update account notes](/user-guides/calendar/write-account-notes).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **QBR Log** tab is missing on my meeting | Set a company on the meeting and reopen it. The tab is staff-only. |
| No entry appeared after I sent a summary | Check that **Update the QBR log automatically** is on in **Settings > Operations > Calendar > Post-Meeting**. Check the meeting type and that the entry was not edited by hand. Then click **Generate from this meeting**. |
| A message says the entry was edited by hand and left unchanged | Automatic generation never overwrites an edited entry. Click **Regenerate with AI** to replace it on purpose. |
| The Autotask note type list is empty | The company must be linked to Autotask and the integration must have synced its note types. Types refresh with the daily sync. |
| A regenerated entry lost my edits | Regenerating replaces the whole entry. Only matching **Done** commitments keep their tick. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are Account Notes the same as meeting notes?">
    No. Account Notes and the QBR Log are internal company history. Public meeting notes, meeting action items and the summary email are separate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need AI to record a QBR?">
    No. In the company **Notes** tab click **Add note** and choose **QBR log**. On a meeting's **QBR Log** tab click **Add entry** when no entry exists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Generate from this meeting only a preview?">
    No. It generates and saves the entry in one step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does sending a summary always create a QBR entry?">
    No. It depends on the setting, the meeting type, the company and any earlier edits. Background work can also fail, so open the **QBR Log** tab to confirm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a manual QBR entry create meeting action items?">
    No. Only AI generation adds action items. Add them yourself on the meeting for a manual entry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting a note delete its Autotask copy?">
    No. Deleting removes the MSPortal note only. See [Create and update account notes](/user-guides/calendar/write-account-notes).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
