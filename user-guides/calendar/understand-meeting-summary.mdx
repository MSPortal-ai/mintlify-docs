Your MSP can email you a recap of a meeting. It can describe the discussion, decisions and follow-up actions. This page is for company users who receive a summary. The sender can write the email or edit an AI draft.

## Before you start

- Receiving the email does not give you portal access or make you a meeting participant. Your MSP manages your portal account.

## Review the recap

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the email">
    Check the meeting title, date and company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the details">
    Compare decisions, amounts, owners and due dates with what was agreed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for corrections">
    Reply through your usual support channel with the meeting and the correction. Keep the email for reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the meeting in the portal (optional)">
    If you have portal access, go to **Calendar > Calendar Menu > List View** or **My Meetings**. Click the **...** menu on the meeting and choose **Open Meeting Page** to read its public notes and action items.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The email and the meeting page are separate. The email does not prove every commitment was added to the action list.

## Follow up on an action

Use the owner and due date to agree the next step with your MSP. A recap does not approve a quote, pay an invoice, open a ticket or mark work complete. To open a support request, see [How to open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). If your MSP added the recap to a ticket as a note, continue the discussion on that ticket.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I did not get a summary | Ask your MSP whether one was sent and to which address. A completed meeting or a finished presentation does not mean a summary was sent. |
| The recap is wrong | Ask your MSP to correct it. Editing a portal note does not change an email already sent. |
| I cannot open the meeting page | Ask your MSP to check your access to the meeting. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I read a summary?">
    In the email your MSP sent. With portal access you can also read the meeting's public notes and actions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a summary mean the actions are finished?">
    No. Check each commitment and its status with the owner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see the internal survey or transcript?">
    No. The wrap-up is an MSP staff workflow. Ask your MSP for what you need.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
