A freeze window marks a period when changes are restricted, such as a busy trading period or a reporting close. The Calendar shows the window and flags changes that overlap it. This page is for company users and MSP staff who can see the Calendar and the **Changes** layer. Your MSP sets up freeze windows. Company users cannot create or edit them. MSP staff can follow [Manage freeze windows](/user-guides/calendar/manage-freeze-windows).

A freeze warning does not cancel an event, block every booking or grant an exception. Follow your organization's process.

## Before you start

- You need access to the **Changes** layer. If **Changes** is missing from the layers menu, your role does not include it. Ask your MSP.
- Freeze windows can apply to the whole MSP, one company or one site. Ask your MSP when the scope is unclear.

## Find a freeze window

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Calendar">
    Check the company selector and the date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the layers menu">
    Click the stacked-layers icon in the toolbar.
    <Frame caption="The layers menu. Calendar Blocks is off in this screenshot, and the own-appointments item is shown under its former name, My Blocks.">
      <img src="/images/product/calendar-layers-menu.png" alt="Calendar layers menu with Changes enabled and Calendar Blocks off" width="480" height="294" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Changes and Calendar Blocks">
    Keep **Changes** ticked. In the Day, Week and Month views, also tick **Calendar Blocks** to draw the freeze on the grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the freeze block">
    Check the name and the start and end times. A repeating freeze can show on several dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check overlapping work">
    A change or appointment that overlaps a freeze shows a snowflake icon. Its details say **This event overlaps a freeze window.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Mine**, **Site** or **All** beside a layer shows the access your MSP gave you. It is not a setting you can change.

## Handle a conflict

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the times">
    Compare the freeze and the planned work, including the Calendar time zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your MSP">
    Send the company and site, the freeze name, the planned work and the date and time. Ask whether to reschedule or follow an exception process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turning a layer off only hides the freeze from your view. Your MSP must change the freeze or move the work.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A freeze is missing from the grid | Check the company, the date range and your access. Tick **Changes** and **Calendar Blocks**. The freeze must be active and have an occurrence in that range. |
| I see no warning on my change | A missing warning is not approval. Check the freeze dates and ask your MSP. |
| The same freeze shows on many dates | It repeats daily, weekly, monthly or yearly. Check the date you need. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can company users create freeze windows?">
    No. Ask your MSP to add one under **Settings > Operations > Calendar > Freeze Windows**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a freeze stop meetings from being booked?">
    No. Treat it as a warning and follow your approval process.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does turning off Changes remove the freeze?">
    No. It only hides it for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a freeze at my site apply to every site?">
    Not necessarily. A freeze can be tenant-wide, company-wide or for one site. Ask your MSP.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
