Use this page to narrow the Calendar to the events you care about and to switch layers on and off. It is for MSP staff and for company users who have Calendar access. Filters only change what you see. They do not change permissions, cancel events or edit meetings.

## Before you start

- Filters work on the **Day**, **Week** and **Month** views. The **Year** view follows the company selector and the layer toggles, but it does not apply search, staff, status or the other filters.
- The company selector at the top of the app sets which companies the Calendar loads. See [Find events in Calendar](/user-guides/calendar/find-calendar-events).

## Search and filter events

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Calendar">
    Go to **Calendar** and pick **Day**, **Week** or **Month**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type in **Search events...**. It matches the title, notes, location and company name of events in the dates you are viewing. It does not search other dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick staff">
    Open **Staff** in the toolbar and tick one or more people. Events show when any ticked person is assigned. The list comes from the people on the events and blocks that are loaded, so it can be short.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the filter drawer">
    Click the **Filter** icon at the right of the toolbar. A number on the icon counts the filter groups that are active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose filters">
    The drawer has **Event Types**, **Companies**, **Staff** and **Status** sections. In **Week** view it also has an option to enable **Saturday / Sunday**, because Week view shows weekdays only by default. When a role list is available, **Filter by Role** is at the bottom of the drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Apply Filters">
    This closes the drawer. Your choices already took effect as you made them, so closing the drawer does not undo them. Click **Reset** to clear them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choices inside one section are alternatives, so ticking two statuses shows events with either one. Different sections narrow the results together.

**Status** offers **Scheduled**, **Confirmed**, **In Progress**, **Completed** and **Canceled**. The Calendar hides **Canceled** meetings until you tick **Canceled**.

**Filter by Role** shows events for companies where a ticked person holds the chosen company role. It does not mean that person attends the event.

Changing the company selector at the top clears your company chips and closes any open event dialog. Search, staff, role, status and hidden titles stay, so check them if the Calendar looks empty after a switch.

## Turn layers on or off

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the layers menu">
    Click the stacked-layers icon in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the layers you want">
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/calendar-layers-menu.png" alt="Calendar layers menu with Appointments and Changes ticked at All access, Calendar Blocks and the own-appointments item unticked." width="480" height="294" />
    </Frame>
    The menu can include **Appointments**, **Changes**, **My Outlook appointments** and **Calendar Blocks**. The screenshot is from an older version and shows **My Outlook appointments** under its former name, **My Blocks**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Layer | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Appointments** | Meetings, service calls and appointments from your PSA. On by default. |
| **Changes** | Scheduled changes and freeze windows. On by default. |
| **My Outlook appointments** | Your own busy blocks from your Microsoft 365 calendar. On by default. |
| **Calendar Blocks** | Other people's busy blocks, and freeze windows drawn on the grid. Off by default. |

**Appointments** and **Changes** each show **Mine**, **Site** or **All** on the right. That label is the access your MSP gave you for the layer. You cannot change it here. A layer you have no access to is not listed. A Site role with no site assigned can show **Mine**.

Turning a layer off hides it only. It does not delete an appointment, cancel an invitation or change a provider calendar.

- To see freeze windows in the Day, Week and Month grids, tick both **Changes** and **Calendar Blocks**. See [Manage freeze windows](/user-guides/calendar/manage-freeze-windows).
- Busy blocks follow **Appointments**, so keep **Appointments** on to see them.
- Busy block subjects can be hidden by your MSP's settings. See [Configure Calendar defaults](/user-guides/calendar/configure-calendar-defaults).
- If you pick staff or roles while **Calendar Blocks** is on, only those people's blocks show.

## Hide repeated titles and all-day events

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Hide items">
    Click **Hide items** in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hide all-day events (optional)">
    Tick **Hide all-day events** at the top of the list to hide all-day and multi-day all-day events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick titles to hide">
    Tick the event titles you do not want to see. A title match ignores capital letters and spaces around the title. It hides every event with that exact title, on every day and for every company.
  </Step>
</Steps>

These choices are saved in your browser for this Calendar page, so they stay after you reload. A project schedule view keeps its own list. To show a title again, open **Hide items** and untick it. **Reset** does not clear hidden titles. Hiding an event never deletes it or changes anyone else's view.

## Reset the filters

Click **Reset** in the filter drawer. It clears search, **Event Types**, **Companies**, **Staff**, **Status** and role filters. It turns **Appointments** and **Changes** back on, shows all-day events and returns Week view to weekdays only. It does not clear hidden titles, **Calendar Blocks**, **My Outlook appointments** or the company selector.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The Calendar looks empty | Check the company selector, then open the filter drawer and click **Reset**. Check **Hide items** for hidden titles. |
| A canceled meeting is missing | Open **Filter**, expand **Status** and tick **Canceled**. |
| **Appointments** or **Changes** is not in the layers menu | Your role has no access to that layer. Ask your MSP. |
| Freeze windows do not show | Tick **Changes** and **Calendar Blocks**. You also need access to view changes. |
| Year counts ignore my search | The Year view does not apply the detailed filters. Open a day, week or month. |
| Another person's blocks show with mine | Staff and role filters apply to busy blocks. Clear them to see only your own. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Apply Filters save anything to the meeting records?">
    No. Filters only change your view. **Apply Filters** closes the drawer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Filter by Role show only meetings that role holder attends?">
    No. It matches companies where the person holds that role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the company selector clear every filter?">
    No. It clears company chips and closes open event dialogs. Other filters and hidden titles stay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do Mine, Site and All mean?">
    They are the access level your MSP assigned for that layer. They are labels, not options.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does turning Appointments off cancel invitations?">
    No. It only hides them from your view.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Hide items hide only one occurrence?">
    No. It hides every event with the same title.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a zero on the Filter icon mean nothing is hidden?">
    No. The number counts active filter groups. Hidden titles and layer toggles are not counted, except that hiding all-day events counts as one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
