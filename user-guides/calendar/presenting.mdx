Use **Present** on a meeting page to read its agenda as slides. The presentation uses your portal access and the meeting's saved agenda settings.

This page is for company users who can access the meeting and its linked records, and for MSP staff. Your MSP manages company access. Adding an action item is a separate write operation and requires permission.

## Before you start

- You need access to the meeting and its linked records. MSP staff present with their own staff view.
- Add agenda items first. See [Read meeting agenda items](/user-guides/calendar/read-meeting-agenda).
- Adding an action item needs Write access to Calendar or Meetings.

## Open the presentation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting page">
    In **Calendar > List View** or **My Meetings**, open the meeting's **…** menu and choose **Open Meeting Page**. Check the meeting title and company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Present">
    Choose **Present** in the meeting page header. Wait for the agenda and slide controls to load. The first slide is normally **Meeting Agenda**, but saved presentation settings can omit the overview.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/calendar/meeting-present-overview-prod.png" alt="Presentation overview slide listing agenda items grouped by type, with the timer, Fullscreen and Exit controls" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before sharing">
    Move through the slides before sharing your screen. An MSP presentation uses the staff member's view; it does not switch to Company permissions. Linked records can contain staff-visible information, including ticket work history. Review the actual content and ask the meeting owner to adjust it when needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you see **No Presentation Content**, use **Go Back**. The meeting may have no agenda items enabled for presentation. Ask the meeting owner to check item visibility and overview/detail settings. A failed load also needs investigation; this message alone does not prove the agenda was deleted.

## Navigate the slides

Each agenda item becomes a detail slide. For example, a Planner item shows its status, start and due dates, estimated hours, cost estimate, business value, description and public notes.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/calendar/meeting-present-slide-prod.png" alt="Detail slide for a Planner item with dates, hours, cost, business value, description and public notes" />
</Frame>

The bottom bar shows the current slide and total, such as **1 / 3**. Its dots select individual slides. An overview item is a summary row; clicking the row does not jump to its detail slide.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product/meeting-presentation-navigation.png" alt="Presentation footer with Previous, slide dots, a slide counter and Next" width="624" height="80" />
</Frame>

| Control | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Next** or **Right Arrow** | Move one slide forward. **Space** also advances when you are not typing. |
| **Previous** or **Left Arrow** | Move one slide back. |
| A slide dot | Jump to that slide. Hovering a dot shows its slide title. |
| **Overview** or **Home** | Return to the overview when included; otherwise go to the first slide. |
| **Last** or **End** | Go to the last slide. |
| **Exit** | Leave the presentation and return to the meeting page. |

Previous is unavailable on the first slide and Next on the last. Reaching the final slide does not mark the meeting completed. Some button labels collapse to icons on smaller screens.

## Use fullscreen and the timer

Choose **Fullscreen** to expand the presentation, or press **F**. Choose **Minimize** to leave fullscreen while staying in the presentation. Browser fullscreen support and keyboard handling can vary.

**Escape** first leaves fullscreen when fullscreen is active. Outside fullscreen, Escape returns to the meeting page. Use the visible **Exit** button when you want to leave the presentation directly.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product/meeting-presentation-timer.png" alt="Presentation timer with start and reset controls showing zero elapsed time" width="214" height="86" />
</Frame>

The timer starts at **0:00** and is initially stopped. Use the play icon to start, pause to stop counting temporarily, and reset to return to zero and stop. Moving between slides keeps the current timer running. Leaving or reloading the presentation starts a new timer session.

The timer does not record billable time, save a timesheet, change the meeting's scheduled duration, or complete the meeting.

## Understand what appears

Presentation content depends on the saved agenda, linked record, your access, and the item's display settings. It is not a complete copy of every source record.

- An item hidden from presentation is excluded from both the overview and detail slides. It can still exist in the meeting workspace.
- An item can appear in the overview without having a detail slide, or have a detail slide without appearing in the overview.
- The overview's **Items** count is the number included in that overview. The footer counts slides, including the overview itself when present. These counts can differ.
- The overview may group items by type or ticket provider. Detail slides follow the saved item order, so their sequence can differ from the grouped overview.
- The overview's **Date** is the current date when rendered, not the meeting's scheduled date. Check the meeting workspace for the scheduled date and time.

Budgets and devices can show dedicated detail views. Other linked items can show different fields or only limited content. Do not assume every report becomes an embedded chart or every quote becomes a complete proposal. Use the source module for the complete record; see [quote documents](/user-guides/quotes/download-quotes) for Quoter, QuoteWerks and Salesbuildr document routes.

Some display choices do not apply consistently to every type of slide. After the meeting owner changes fields, a custom title or a template, reopen the presentation and inspect the result before sharing. Hiding a slide or a display field does not change permissions on its source record.

## Handle stale or unavailable content

The presentation loads the saved agenda when opened. Some linked detail views load separately and can reuse recently loaded data. The deck does not continuously update as another person edits the meeting or source record.

If something changed, reload the presentation and review it again. If a linked item still cannot load, check that it is available in its source module and ask your MSP to verify your access. Missing details do not mean the underlying work is complete or that a budget is approved.

## Capture a follow-up action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Action Item">
    The button is at the right of the bottom bar. The **Quick Add Action Item** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the description">
    Press **Ctrl+Enter**, or **Command+Enter** on a Mac, to save from the keyboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Action Item">
    Wait for **Action item added successfully**. **Cancel** closes the dialog without saving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The quick dialog has no priority, due date or notes field. Open the meeting page and click the pencil on the action item to set them. Adding an action does not create a ticket, send a summary or complete the meeting. Company users who cannot save an action should ask their MSP to capture it.

## Finish the presentation

Select **Exit** to return to the meeting workspace. Save any meeting notes deliberately with **Save Notes**; exiting the deck is not a notes save. Meeting status, follow-up actions and sending a summary are separate workflows.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No Presentation Content** appears | Click **Go Back**. Ask the meeting owner to turn on presentation visibility for at least one agenda item. |
| A slide shows old data | Reload the presentation. It does not update while it is open. |
| **Add Action Item** fails | Saving needs Write access to Calendar or Meetings. Ask your MSP to add the action. |
| Escape closed the presentation | Outside fullscreen, **Escape** returns to the meeting page. Use **F** or **Minimize** to leave fullscreen only. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I start a meeting presentation?">
    Open the full meeting page and select Present in its header. List View and My Meetings offer Open Meeting Page in the meeting's row menu.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Company users present an accessible meeting?">
    The meeting page offers Present. You still need access to the meeting and its linked records; Company administrator status does not grant extra access or meeting write permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does presentation mode switch an MSP user to the Company's view?">
    No. It uses the presenter's current access. Review each slide before screen sharing, especially ticket work history and other staff-visible details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I click an overview row to jump to its slide?">
    No. Use the slide dots, Next, Previous, Overview or Last controls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the item count differ from the slide count?">
    The overview counts items included there. The footer counts generated slides, including the overview when present. Overview visibility and detail-slide inclusion can be set separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is there no overview slide?">
    Saved settings can omit it, and no overview is generated when no visible item is included there. Overview or Home then takes you to the first available slide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the overview Date the scheduled meeting date?">
    No. It shows the current date when rendered. Check the meeting workspace for the scheduled date and time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding an agenda item make it private?">
    No. Hiding it affects presentation inclusion. The item can remain in the workspace, and its source record retains its own access rules.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Escape always leave the presentation?">
    In fullscreen, Escape first leaves fullscreen. Outside fullscreen it returns to the meeting page. The visible Exit button leaves the presentation directly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the timer save billable time or complete the meeting?">
    No. It is a temporary elapsed-time display for the current presentation session. Reset stops it at zero; leaving or reloading starts a new session.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do slides update while another person edits the meeting?">
    Not continuously. Reload and review the presentation after changes; linked details may also use recently loaded data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does every field or template setting affect every slide type?">
    Do not assume so. Some dedicated detail views do not apply all those choices. Inspect the resulting slide before sharing it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does No Presentation Content mean the meeting was deleted?">
    No. There may be no enabled slides, or the content may not have loaded. Go Back and check the agenda and your access with the meeting owner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening Quick Add Action Item save anything?">
    No. Submit Add Action Item to create it; Cancel creates nothing. The quick dialog captures a description, and saving requires meeting write permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does reaching the last slide or selecting Exit send a summary?">
    No. Neither action sends a summary, changes meeting status, nor saves notes. Return to the workspace for those separate workflows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I download the presentation as a PDF or PowerPoint?">
    This presentation screen has no PDF or PowerPoint export control. Use the relevant source module's document options when you need a record document.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
