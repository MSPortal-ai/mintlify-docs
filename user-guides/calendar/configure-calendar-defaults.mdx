Use this page to set the Calendar defaults that apply to everyone at your MSP. It is for MSP administrators. Company users can [read and filter the Calendar](/user-guides/calendar/filter-calendar-events), but they do not change these settings.

## Before you start

- You need Calendar settings access and permission to manage Calendar settings.
- These settings apply to your whole MSP, not to one person or company. A viewer's own filters are separate.
- The [Freeze Windows](/user-guides/calendar/manage-freeze-windows) and [Post-Meeting](/user-guides/calendar/configure-post-meeting) tabs sit beside General Settings and save separately.

## Change the general defaults

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Operations > Calendar">
    Select the **General Settings** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how busy blocks look">
    Turn **Show event details** on to show an available subject instead of **Busy** on calendar busy blocks. A block still shows **Busy** when it has no subject or you lack access to its details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the default meeting length">
    Open **Default Meeting Duration** and choose **15 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **45 minutes**, **1 hour**, **1.5 hours** or **2 hours**. New meetings start with this length.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the start of the week">
    Set **Week Start Day** to **Sunday** or **Monday**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    The **Save** button is in the page header. A **Your calendar settings have been updated successfully.** message confirms it. The button is disabled until you change something.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changing these defaults does not change existing meetings, send invitations or change Microsoft 365 or PSA sync settings. Switching tabs does not save your changes, so click **Save** first.

## Set Regular Hours and half-day ranges

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Work Hours card">
    It sits beside the general settings. **Regular Hours** and the **Half Day Settings** ranges are below the card description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Regular Hours">
    Pick a **Start** and an **End** time under **Regular Hours**.
    <Frame caption="Regular Hours set to 8 AM to 5 PM. Your MSP can choose different values.">
      <img src="/images/product/calendar-regular-hours.png" alt="Regular Hours showing Start at 8 AM and End at 5 PM" width="772" height="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the half-day ranges">
    Under **Half Day Settings**, set a **Start** and **End** for **Morning** and for **Afternoon**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Use the **Save** button in the page header.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New installs start with Regular Hours 08:00 to 17:00, Morning 08:00 to 12:00 and Afternoon 13:00 to 17:00.

| Range | Used by |
| --- | --- |
| **Regular Hours** | The **Full-day** shortcut in the meeting editor. |
| **Morning** | The first click on the **Half-day** shortcut. |
| **Afternoon** | The second click on **Half-day**, which switches to the afternoon range. |

Each range must end after it starts on the same day. An overnight range such as 10 PM to 6 AM is not accepted. For an invalid range the page shows **Enter valid start and end times, with the end after the start.** and **Save** stays disabled.

Work hours are defaults for the shortcuts. They do not stop anyone from booking a meeting outside those hours.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Save** is disabled | You have no changes, a save is running, or a Start and End pair is invalid. |
| **Save** fails | Viewing settings and managing them are separate permissions. Ask an administrator to check your role. |
| Busy blocks still say **Busy** | The block has no subject, or you cannot see its details. Check that **Show event details** is on and saved. |
| Existing meetings did not change length | Defaults only apply to new meetings and shortcut clicks. |
| I need to connect a calendar | General Settings does not connect a calendar. See the [integrations guides](/user-guides/integrations/index). |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can company administrators change these defaults?">
    No. These are MSP settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are these personal preferences?">
    No. They apply to everyone at your MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the default duration resize existing meetings?">
    No. It sets the starting length of new meetings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do Work Hours block meetings outside those hours?">
    No. They only fill in times for the **Full-day** and **Half-day** shortcuts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Full-day always mean 8 hours?">
    No. It uses your Regular Hours range. A 9 AM to 6 PM range gives nine hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does switching tabs save my changes?">
    No. Click **Save** in the page header before you leave the tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
