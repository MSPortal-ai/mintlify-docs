Use List View to see your meetings in one table and filter them by column. It is for MSP staff and for company users with meeting access. The list shows only meetings in the companies and with the visibility your account allows.

## Before you start

- Check the company selector at the top of the app. Global View still shows only the companies you can access.
- List View shows meeting records. It does not show busy blocks, changes or freeze windows. Use the main Calendar for those.

## Find a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open List View">
    Go to **Calendar**, open **Calendar Menu** and choose **List View**. The page is titled **Meetings List**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the filters under the column headings">
    Type in the **Title** filter, or open the **Date & Time**, **Company**, **Participants**, **Type** or **Status** filter. Filters combine, so each one narrows the list further.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/calendar-list-date-filter.png" alt="Date and Time filter showing All and relative day, week, month and quarter periods, with more options below." width="316" height="640" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the meeting">
    Click a row to open the meeting dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the row menu for more">
    Click the **...** button at the end of the row. Depending on the meeting and your access, it offers **Open Meeting Page**, **Present**, **Wrap-Up** and **Edit**.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/calendar-list-actions.png" alt="MSP meeting row menu with Open Meeting Page, Present, Wrap-Up and Edit." width="348" height="294" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove a filter, set it back to **All** or clear the text. Changing the company selector resets the filters and sorting and closes an open meeting dialog.

## What each filter does

| Filter | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Date & Time** | The meeting's start date. Pick **Last 30 Days**, **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This Week**, **Last Week**, **This Month**, **Last Month**, **This Quarter**, **Last Quarter**, **This Year** or **Last Year**. |
| **Title** | Words in the meeting title. The location under the title is not searched. |
| **Company** | One or more company names in the list. |
| **Participants** | People shown in the table, including external email addresses. |
| **Type** | One meeting type. |
| **Status** | One meeting status. |

**Date & Time** has fixed periods only, with no custom date range. Use the [Calendar's Day, Week and Month views](/user-guides/calendar/find-calendar-events) to look at a specific date. Filter choices come from the loaded meetings, so an option only appears when a meeting uses it.

## Read the rows

- **Date & Time** shows the start date and the start and end times in your MSP's calendar time zone. Open the meeting for the end date of a multi-day meeting.
- Each recurring series is one row. The list does not expand every occurrence.
- Participants show as initials, up to three, then **+** a count. A guest count such as **(2 external)** follows. A name shows as **Unknown** when it cannot be resolved. Initials do not mean the person accepted or attended.
- **Company** shows a dash when the meeting has no company.
- Canceled meetings are included. Pick **Canceled** in **Status** to find them.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A meeting is missing | Check the company selector and the filters. The list covers only your accessible companies. |
| A person is not in the **Participants** filter | The filter lists names found in the loaded meetings. Check the company selector. |
| I cannot find a future meeting by date | The **Date & Time** filter has fixed periods such as **This Week**. Use the main Calendar to browse a specific date. |
| I do not see busy blocks or changes | They are not in this table. Use the main Calendar. |
| A row menu option is missing | **Present**, **Wrap-Up** and **Edit** depend on the meeting type, your access and the wrap-up settings. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is List View different from My Meetings?">
    List View shows every accessible meeting for the selected company scope. [My Meetings](/user-guides/calendar/find-my-meetings) shows meetings where you are a participant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Global View show every company's meetings?">
    No. It shows the companies you can access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the date filter include meetings that overlap the period?">
    No. It filters on the start date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are canceled meetings hidden?">
    No. They show in List View. The main Calendar hides them by default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening the row menu change anything?">
    No. Nothing is sent or saved until you finish an action.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
