Use this page to set the questions your staff answer in the wrap-up survey. It is for MSP administrators. The questions are for internal staff, not for company survey invitations. The list applies to your whole MSP. To answer the survey, see [Complete the internal meeting survey](/user-guides/calendar/complete-meeting-survey).

## Before you start

- You need access to Calendar settings, plus Manage access to Calendar or Meetings to change questions.
- Question changes save at once. They do not use the page-header **Save**.

## Add a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Operations > Calendar">
    Select the **Post-Meeting** tab and find the **Post-meeting survey questions** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add question">
    The **Add question** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the question">
    Enter the **Question** (up to 500 characters). Add a **Description (optional)** of up to 2,000 characters if it helps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the answer type">
    Open **Answer type** and pick **1-5 rating**, **1-10 scale**, **Yes / No**, **Choice list** or **Free text**. For **Choice list**, enter one option per line in **Options (one per line)**. Use at least one option, up to 20, each up to 200 characters.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/post-meeting-answer-types.png" alt="Answer-type menu with 1-5 rating, 1-10 scale, Yes / No, Choice list and Free text" width="360" height="332" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Required and Active">
    Turn on **Required** to make staff answer before **Continue**. **Active** puts the question in the survey. New questions start active and not required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    **Question saved** confirms it. **Cancel** drops the dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Answer type | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **1-5 rating** | A short rating such as engagement. |
| **1-10 scale** | A broader score. |
| **Yes / No** | A binary answer, such as whether follow-up is needed. |
| **Choice list** | One answer from your options. |
| **Free text** | Comments. |

## Add the default questions

When the list is empty, click **Add default questions**. It creates editable questions for client engagement, meeting value, client sentiment, follow-up needed and additional comments. They start active and not required. The button does not show when any question exists, even if all are off.

## Edit a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the pencil">
    It is labeled **Edit question** on the question's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the fields">
    Update the question, description, answer type, options, **Required** or **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Changing a question away from **Choice list** removes its options. Answers already submitted keep the question as it was when they were saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switch a question on or off

Use the switch on the question's row. It saves at once. A question that is off stays in the list but leaves the survey. Turn off every question to leave the survey with no questions. Deleting every question is different, because staff then see the built-in defaults.

## Change the order

Click **Move up** or **Move down** on a row. Each move saves at once. Wait for one move to finish before the next. Reload the page to check the order after an error.

## Delete a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the trash can">
    A prompt reads **Delete the question "name"? Existing answers keep a snapshot of it.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Cancel to keep the question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Answers already submitted keep their snapshot. Re-submitting a survey uses the current active questions, so switch a question off instead of deleting it if you may want it back.

## Which Save to use

| Change | When it saves |
| --- | --- |
| Add or edit a question | **Save** in the question dialog. |
| Active switch | At once. |
| **Move up** and **Move down** | At once. |
| Delete | After you confirm. |
| Post-Meeting Flow settings | The page-header **Save**. |

**Required** only checks the survey's **Continue** button. Staff can still skip the survey.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Enter a question** | The **Question** field is empty. |
| **Add at least one option** | A **Choice list** needs at least one option. |
| I cannot change questions | You need Manage access to Calendar or Meetings, as well as settings access. Ask an administrator. |
| A saved question is missing from the wrap-up | Check that **Active** is on, the flow is on, and the meeting type is selected. Reopen the wrap-up. |
| The order looks wrong after an error | Reload the page and check the saved order. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do company users answer these questions?">
    No. They are for MSP staff.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are the questions shared across the MSP?">
    Yes. One list applies to everyone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need the page-header Save after editing a question?">
    No. The dialog **Save** is enough.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I hide a question without deleting it?">
    Turn off its **Active** switch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting all questions leave an empty survey?">
    No. Staff then see the built-in defaults. Turn the questions off instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Required stop staff from skipping?">
    No. It only checks **Continue**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
