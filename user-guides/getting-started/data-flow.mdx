This page maps how information moves through MSPortal, so you know which module to open next. It is for MSP staff planning a client workflow. The map shows possible flows. One record does not automatically create every downstream record, so you choose each next step.

## Plan a client workflow from evidence to presentation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect the evidence">
    Complete a survey, connect Microsoft 365, and import devices from your RMM. See [Surveys](/user-guides/surveys/index), [Microsoft 365](/user-guides/microsoft-365/index), and [Devices](/user-guides/devices/index).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn findings into goals">
    Create goals from survey responses, or add them by hand. Review compliance failures and Secure Score recommendations. See [Goals](/user-guides/goals/index) and [Compliance](/user-guides/compliance/index).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Planner items">
    Create Planner items from goals, compliance findings, and standards gaps. Attach devices, then set hours and costs. See [Planner](/user-guides/planner/index).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Roll costs into a budget">
    Add the Planner items you intend to pursue to a budget and review the forecast. See [Budgets](/user-guides/budgets/index).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Present to the client">
    Build a QBR agenda from goals, Planner items, compliance, and budgets. Present it live, then send a report. See [Meetings](/user-guides/calendar/meetings) and [Presenting meetings](/user-guides/calendar/presenting).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat each quarter">
    Send a follow-up survey, close finished Planner items, and open new goals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Before you present, check the company and provider instance, the latest sync result, the report filters and date range, and who can see the result. An MSP preview does not prove a company user sees the same thing.

## What the Planner is for

The Planner is your roadmap for each client. A vCIO or account manager lays out the next several years of IT work in plain, estimated form, so the client can react to it before anyone writes a formal quote.

- **Estimates, not quotes.** Hours and costs on Planner items are directional. They feed [Budgets](/user-guides/budgets/index) and the forecast. A Planner estimate does not issue a quote, approve spending, or create a PSA project.
- **Years in advance.** Add work well ahead of time, such as a firewall refresh or a server upgrade.
- **Owned by the account owner.** Technicians work in the PSA. The Planner is the account strategy layer above it.
- **Traceable.** A linked source on each item shows which goal, control, standard, or survey put it on the roadmap.

PSA tickets and projects are not Planner items. They show up in reports without passing through the Planner.

## Writebacks to your source systems

Some actions create or update something in a connected system. They appear as dashed teal arrows in the diagram.

- **Onboarding to projects.** An onboarding portal links to a project. Depending on your setup, that is an MSPortal project or a project in your PSA.
- **Devices to your PSA and documentation tool.** Warranty dates can write back to Autotask, ConnectWise, and Halo assets and to IT Glue configurations.

Writeback depends on the provider and the action. Do not assume every integration writes back, or that every device field updates your PSA. Check the provider's guide and verify the result in the external system.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Data in a report is older than the provider | Check the latest successful sync, the company mapping, the report period, and the filters. Opening a report does not request fresh provider data. |
| A finding did not create a Planner item | Findings do not create Planner items automatically. Use the creation or linking action in the module. |
| A record is missing for one company | Check the company mapping in **Settings > Integrations** and confirm the sync finished. |
| A provider record did not update after a change | Writeback is provider- and action-specific. Check the provider guide, then the external record. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does every finding automatically create a Planner item?">
    No. Review the finding, then use the supported create or link action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a Planner estimate create a quote or approve spending?">
    No. Quoting, budget approval, and PSA project creation are separate workflows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does every integration write changes back to its source?">
    No. Writeback depends on the provider and the action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do PSA tickets appear in the Planner?">
    No. Tickets and projects flow to reporting without becoming Planner items.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
