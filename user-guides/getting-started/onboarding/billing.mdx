Confirm your plan and payment details before you roll out to clients, so nothing about the subscription surprises you mid-launch. This page is for MSP staff with billing access.

## Before you start

- Primary admins can always open Billing. Other staff need a role with manage access to billing settings.
- Billing is an optional step in the Launch Center plan. A launch can finish without it.
- Payment and plan changes happen in a secure hosted billing portal that opens from the Billing page.

## Review your plan in Launch Center

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the step">
    Go to **Launch Center** and select **Billing** in **Setup guide**. It is in the **Foundation** phase. You can also go to **Settings > Administration > Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the subscription status">
    Check the subscription or trial status near the top. If you see **Billing Information Unavailable**, click **Retry**. Missing numbers in that state do not mean you have no subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check usage and the next invoice">
    Read the **Usage Summary** and **Subscription** cards. They show your companies, the next billing date, and the estimated invoice. If a **Pending sync** badge shows, click **Sync billing status**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change anything in the hosted portal">
    To change a plan or payment method, click the button the page offers, such as **Manage subscription**, **Manage payment methods**, **Add payment method**, or **Choose a plan**. Make the change in the hosted billing portal and confirm it there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return and sync">
    Go back to **Billing** and click **Sync billing status**. Check the status and the **Billing history** table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Billing page has no Save button. Closing the hosted portal is not a confirmation, so check the current subscription before you repeat a change. Check the billing email in **Settings > Administration > Tenant** too. See [Billing settings](/user-guides/settings/billing) for every card and button.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Billing Information Unavailable** | Click **Retry**. Do not repeat a purchase until the page loads. |
| The hosted portal does not open | Click the button again. If it still fails, send support the error message. |
| A change looks unconfirmed | Click **Sync billing status**, then check the subscription and invoices. Do not submit it again. |
| You cannot open Billing | Ask a primary admin for billing manage access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a table of company limits per plan?">
    No. Use the plan, usage, and terms shown for your own account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does returning from the billing portal prove my change worked?">
    No. Click **Sync billing status** and check the subscription and payment details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does completing this step confirm a payment?">
    No. Launch progress and billing transactions are separate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
