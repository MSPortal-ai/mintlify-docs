Bring devices from your RMM into the portal's inventory for each rollout company. This page is for MSP staff with integration-management access.

## Before you start

- Finish the [RMM connection](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/rmm-setup) and map your sites or companies first. Devices from unmapped sites do not appear.
- Check that at least one device sync finished. The first sync can take a while for a large account.
- Importing only the pilot companies first keeps the first pass simple.
- You can also use Addigy for this step when you chose it without a primary RMM.

## Import devices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the device page">
    In **Launch Center**, select **Client devices** in **Setup guide**, then click **Open Device Import**. You can also open **Settings > Integrations**, click your integration, and open its **Devices** tab. ConnectWise Manage calls it **Configurations**, and Halo PSA and Syncro call it **Assets**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map the device types">
    In the **Device Type Mappings** table, click **Quick Match** to map provider types to your device types by name. Click **Map with AI** for types name matching missed, and **Quick Create** for any type that still has no match. Or choose a type yourself in the **Maps To** dropdown. **Map with AI** saves right away, so review the result before you import.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ignore what you do not track">
    Check **Ignore** on categories you do not want, such as printers or sensors. Ignored types are hidden from pending imports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Click **Import All Mapped** to import every unimported device of a mapped type. Or click **Import All** on one type row. Or tick devices in **Staged Devices** and click **Import Selected**. Wait for the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on auto-sync (optional)">
    Check **Auto-Sync** on a mapped type to import new devices of that type on every sync. Check the mapping first, because those devices are imported without review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the inventory">
    Open **Devices** in the sidebar, choose the company in the company selector, and confirm the imported devices and their source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Launch Center">
    In **Launch Center**, open **Live verification** and check **Devices imported for the rollout audience**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Import Devices](/user-guides/integrations/import-devices) for every control on the device page.

## What counts as ready

The check looks for real devices in the Devices inventory for every rollout company, from the RMM you selected, or from Addigy when you added it. These do not count: staged rows, a device you added by hand, or a healthy connection alone. It is a blocking check when your plan includes client data and a device integration. One imported device does not cover every company.

If a device count looks low, check which categories you ignored. Excluded records stay out of the import on purpose.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The staged list is empty | Check the sync status, the API user's scope, and that the sites are mapped. |
| Devices are staged but not in inventory | Check the type mapping, ignored types, and the import result. |
| Inventory exists but the check fails | Check that the devices belong to the selected RMM and to every rollout company. Do not import duplicates. |
| A type cannot be imported | It needs a **Maps To** device type first. Ignored types cannot be imported. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do staged devices count as imported inventory?">
    No. Check the real **Devices** records for the company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does one imported device satisfy a wider rollout?">
    No. Each rollout company needs matching devices.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Map with AI a draft preview?">
    No. It saves the mappings it creates right away.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
