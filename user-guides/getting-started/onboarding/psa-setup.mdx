Connect your PSA so MSPortal can sync companies, contacts, and tickets. This page is for MSP staff with integration-management access. It covers Halo PSA, ConnectWise Manage, Autotask, and Syncro.

## Before you start

- Choose your PSA in the **Integrations** step first. See [Choose launch integrations](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/integration-selection).
- Have your PSA administrator create an API user and give you its credentials. The fields differ by provider, so keep the provider's setup guide open (see Related guides).
- Know which boards, queues, or teams hold your client tickets.

## Connect your PSA

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the step">
    In **Launch Center**, select **Connect your PSA** in **Setup guide**. The PSA setup wizard opens inside the plan. You can also use **Settings > Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    On the **Connect** step, enter your API credentials and click **Test Connection**. Wait for **Connection Verified**. If you see **Connection Failed**, check the credentials, region, or URL, then click **Retry Connection**. Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check permissions">
    The **Permissions** step checks what the API user can read. Required items show a **Required** badge. If any are **Not permitted**, grant them in your PSA, then click **Re-check**. If a permission is granted but returns no data, setup can continue, but nothing syncs until the API user can see records. Click **Next** when the required items pass.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to sync">
    On the **Sync** step, turn on the data to sync, such as **Sync Users**, **Sync Companies**, and **Enable Ticket Sync**. Pick the boards, queues, or teams to sync. Data starts syncing in the background when you continue. Use **Contact Auto-Import** only if you want active contacts created as portal users, and choose a **Default Role for Imported Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure tickets">
    On the **Configure** step, open **Ticket Settings** and set the fields it shows, such as **Closed Statuses**, **Client Comment Status**, and **Default Board for New Tickets**. In **Resolution Status Stepper**, click each stage and choose the PSA statuses that belong to it. Status choices appear only after you select boards, queues, or teams in the **Sync** step. Save the settings and wait for **Ticket settings saved**. Syncro configures tickets automatically, and Autotask shows its own fields such as **Fallback Resource**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import (optional)">
    On the **Import** step, import users and companies, or click **Skip**. You can import them later from **Settings > Users & Roles** and **Settings > Companies**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete setup">
    Click **Complete Setup**. Use **Save & Exit** if you need to stop and come back. Your progress is saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check the data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the sync status">
    Open the integration in **Settings > Integrations** and look at the last sync time. The first sync can take a while for a large account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map companies">
    Map your PSA companies to MSPortal companies. A similar name is not enough, so confirm each match. See [Integration company mapping](/user-guides/settings/integration-company-mapping).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Launch Center">
    In **Launch Center**, open **Live verification**. **A healthy PSA connection is available when required** is blocking when your plan needs a PSA. It checks the instance chosen in your plan. An unrelated active integration does not satisfy it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Next, set up [ticket delivery, visibility, and templates](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/ticket-settings).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Connection Failed** | Check the credentials, region or URL, and the API user's status in your PSA. Then click **Retry Connection**. |
| Required permissions are missing | Grant them in your PSA and click **Re-check**. |
| "Please complete the connection step first" | Finish **Connect** and **Test Connection** before the later steps. |
| Status choices are empty on **Configure** | Select boards, queues, or teams in the **Sync** step first. |
| The wrong connection is checked | Go back to **Integrations** and choose the intended instance. |
| No records arrive | Check the sync status, the API user's permissions, and the company mappings. Do not clear and resync until you know why. Clearing data can remove mapped records. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a successful credential test complete the rollout?">
    No. Check the sync, the company mappings, the imported records, and the remaining findings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can any active connection satisfy this step?">
    No. The check applies to the instance selected in your plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I set this up later from Settings?">
    Yes. Open your PSA in **Settings > Integrations**. The wizard resumes where you stopped.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
