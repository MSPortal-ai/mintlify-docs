Company access groups decide what your clients' users can see and do in the portal. This page is for MSP administrators who manage client access.

## Before you start

- You need role-management access to create or edit a group. You need write access to Company users to assign one.
- Company access groups are called **Company Roles** on the **Roles** tab. In the Company user editor they appear as **Permissions Group**.
- Decide what each kind of client user needs, for example a viewer who opens tickets, or a client lead who sees reports.

## Create a Company access group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Roles tab">
    Go to **Settings > Users & Roles** and open **Roles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the existing groups">
    In **Company Roles**, check whether an existing group fits. Presets include **Viewer**, **Finance**, and **Company Admin**. To start from one, open its row menu and click **Duplicate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a role">
    Click **Add Role** in **Company Roles**. Enter a **Name** and **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the permissions">
    Set **Read**, **Write**, or **Manage** for each resource. Use **Search permissions...** to find one. Check each module the client should see, and leave the rest off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a ticket display group (optional)">
    If you have ticket display groups, choose one in **Ticket Display Group** to control which tickets these users see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Role**. Use **Update Role** for later edits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assign the group to client users

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Company Users">
    Go to **Settings > Users & Roles** and open **Company Users**. Use the company selector and filters to find the person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the user">
    Click the user's name. The **Edit Company User** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the group">
    Under **Permissions**, open **Permissions Group** and pick the group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. The **Permissions Group** column shows the new group.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change many users at once, select them and use **Actions > Update Permissions**. See [Change a Company user's permissions](/user-guides/settings/change-company-user-permissions).

If a provider imports your client users, its contact-type rules and defaults can set the group too. Review those rules before you change the same user again.

## Check what clients can see

<Steps>
  <Step title="Test as a client">
    Use your approved client-review or impersonation process and sign in as a user with the new group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the key pages">
    Open the pages the client needs and check the data. A visible menu item does not prove the user can read every record behind it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Launch Center">
    In **Launch Center**, open **Live verification** and check the client-user finding for every rollout company.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Hiding a sidebar item is not a security rule. Use permissions, company scope, and [record visibility](/user-guides/getting-started/hiding-items-from-clients).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A client user sees too much or too little | Open the group in **Roles > Company Roles** and check the levels. Then check the user's company and group. |
| An imported user keeps getting a different group | Check the provider's contact-type rules and the company and integration defaults. |
| You cannot find **Company Roles** | You may lack role-management access. Ask an administrator. |
| The group exists but no user has it | Assign it in **Company Users**. A new role has no users. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I manage Company permission groups?">
    In **Settings > Users & Roles > Roles**, under **Company Roles**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding a sidebar item restrict access?">
    No. Use permissions, company scope, and record visibility.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Team Roles the same as access groups?">
    No. Team Roles assign MSP staff responsibilities and grant no client permissions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
