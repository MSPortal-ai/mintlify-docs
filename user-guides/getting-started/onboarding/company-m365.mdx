Connect each rollout company to its own Microsoft 365 tenant so MSPortal can sync users, licenses, and Secure Score. This page is for MSP staff with integration access, plus someone who can approve consent in the client's Microsoft tenant.

## Before you start

- You need permission to manage companies or integration company mappings.
- A Microsoft 365 administrator for each client tenant must be available to approve the permissions, or you must have admin credentials for that tenant.
- Your rollout companies must exist in MSPortal.
- Allow pop-ups for MSPortal. Consent opens in a pop-up window.

## Connect each company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the companies list">
    In **Launch Center**, open **Connect other tools**, tick **Microsoft 365** under **Productivity**, and click **Open** on its card. Then click **Open setup in Settings**. You can also go to **Settings > Integrations > Microsoft 365 > Companies**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the company">
    Use **Search companies...** or the filter to find the rollout company. Check that you are on the right company and Microsoft tenant before you connect. Check existing mappings before you create another connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect** on the company's row. A pop-up opens the Microsoft sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and approve">
    Sign in with an administrator account from the client's Microsoft 365 tenant. Read the permissions on the Microsoft consent screen and the target tenant, then approve. Finish any acknowledgment MSPortal shows first. The pop-up closes and the list refreshes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the connection">
    Confirm the row shows **Connected** with the right Microsoft tenant. A pop-up that closed is not proof of a healthy connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for each company">
    Work down your rollout companies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the client's admin cannot sign in from your browser, use **Actions > Generate Link** on the row and send them the link. It works once and expires in 7 days. See [Connecting Companies to Microsoft 365](/user-guides/microsoft-365/connecting-companies) for links, reconnecting, and disconnecting.

## Check the data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at the sync">
    Check **Last synced** on the row. It covers users and groups. For licenses and Secure Score, open the Cloud page for that company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up client users">
    Importing users and assigning access is separate from consent. See [Prepare Company users](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/company-users).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Launch Center">
    Back in **Launch Center**, check the **Microsoft 365** card on **Connect other tools**. It shows **Ready** once at least one company has a healthy connection, and **Needs attention** when connections exist but none is healthy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Match the rollout scope

Start with the intended pilot company, then review each additional company you plan to launch. Connecting one company does not connect the others, and the **Ready** status on the card does not replace a review of every company connection. Connect only the companies you intend to enable. Do not connect an unrelated tenant just to change a status.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The pop-up does not open | Allow pop-ups for MSPortal and click **Connect** again. |
| The pop-up closes but the company is not connected | Consent may have been canceled or failed. Fix the reported problem and start again. |
| Consent worked but the connection is unhealthy | Check the connection status and the tenant before you repeat consent. Use **Reconnect** if a token expired. |
| Users are missing | Check the sync, the company mapping, and whether the users can be imported. |
| A company shows more than once | Each connected tenant has its own row. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does one Microsoft 365 connection complete a wider rollout?">
    No. Every company you plan to enable needs its own healthy connection. Review the saved connection and expected data for each one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does consent give imported users portal access?">
    No. Import users, assign a company, and choose an access group separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the pop-up closing prove the connection works?">
    No. Check that the row shows **Connected**, then check the sync.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
