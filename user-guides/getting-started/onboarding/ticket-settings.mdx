Set up where client requests go, what clients can see, and which request forms they can use. This page is for MSP staff with Ticket settings access. Launch Center's **Set up tickets** step has **Statuses**, **Delivery** and **Display groups** tabs. Request templates are an item in the **Portal checklist**.

## Before you start

- You need write or manage access to Ticket settings in **Settings > Tickets**. Without it, the **Delivery** and **Display groups** tabs offer **Open ticket settings** instead.
- To deliver tickets to your PSA, connect it first. See [PSA connection](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/psa-setup). **Set up tickets** opens after **Connect your PSA**.
- Your rollout companies must exist.
- Have a monitored email address for fallback delivery.

## Set ticket statuses

On the **Statuses** tab, choose which PSA statuses close and resolve a ticket. When your PSA sets up inside Launch Center, this is the connection's **Configure tickets** task. See [PSA connection](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/psa-setup) for its fields.

## Set up ticket delivery

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    In **Launch Center**, open **Set up tickets** and click the **Delivery** tab. It shows the **Ticket Delivery & Defaults** card from **Settings > Tickets > General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the delivery method">
    Set **Delivery Method** to **PSA Integration** to create tickets in your PSA, or **Email (SMTP)** to send them to an email address. The change saves right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the destination">
    For PSA delivery, choose the **PSA Integration**, then enter a **Fallback Email Address** that gets the ticket if the PSA connection fails. For email delivery, enter the **Email Address (for email delivery)**. Each field saves when you click out of it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the ticket defaults">
    Under **Ticket Defaults**, pick the routing values for your PSA, such as board or queue, priority, status, and type. Click **Save Defaults**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Submit a test request and confirm it reaches the right place with the right defaults. A finished step does not prove a message arrived.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Set up tickets** turns done when delivery has a valid destination and the PSA's ticket configuration is done. For email delivery, that means a configured email address. For PSA delivery, it needs an active selected PSA. If the selected PSA is later disabled or paused, the step needs attention. See [Ticket settings](/user-guides/settings/tickets) for every card.

## Review ticket visibility

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    Click the **Display groups** tab. If you see **Choose your PSA for tickets first**, select the PSA on the **Delivery** tab. Display groups apply to your selected PSA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a display group">
    Click **Create Group**. Enter a **Group Name**, choose **Ticket Visibility** (**All Tickets**, **Same Location Only**, or **Own Tickets Only**), assign the client roles, and choose the boards, ticket types, fields, and the statuses portal users may set. Save the group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with client roles">
    Check a limited user and a client administrator against real tickets. Look at internal notes, closed work, and the statuses they can set. Do not assume one fixed set of status categories applies to every provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make a request template available

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the templates">
    In **Launch Center**, open **Portal checklist** and click **Open settings** on **Ticket request templates**. It opens **Settings > Tickets > Templates** in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or pick a template">
    Create a template, or open an existing one. Follow [How to create a ticket template](/user-guides/ticketing/how-to-create-a-ticket-template) for each field. Keep the switch labelled **Template is active and available for use** on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can use it">
    Open **Company Visibility** in the template. Turn the template on for your rollout companies. Under **Role Access**, check that client roles can use it. A template that only staff can see is not available to clients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit it as a client">
    Sign in as a client, or use your approved review process. Open Home or **Tickets**, pick the template, and send a request. A draft is not a submitted ticket.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The checklist item counts enabled request templates and ticks itself once there is at least one. That count does not check each company. Every rollout company needs an enabled client-visible template that applies to it. A company-wide template covers a company only where it is not switched off for that company.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Set up tickets** stays **To do** | Check the **Delivery Method**, and that the matching email address is filled in. For PSA delivery, check that the selected PSA is active and its ticket configuration is done. |
| **Choose your PSA for tickets first** | Select the PSA under **PSA Integration** on the **Delivery** tab. |
| A company has no template | Open the template's **Company Visibility** and turn it on for that company. Check **Role Access**. |
| A client cannot start a request | Check the client role's access, the template's visibility, and **Company Visibility**. |
| You changed three things and cannot tell which fixed it | Change one thing at a time and test again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does client ticketing need a PSA?">
    No. Delivery supports email and PSA. Set the method and its destination.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a finished step prove a ticket arrived?">
    No. It checks stored settings. Send a test request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a staff-only template count?">
    No. Each rollout company needs a client-visible template that is enabled for it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
