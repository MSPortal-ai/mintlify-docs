Set the company profile that the rest of the portal uses for names, schedules, and reports. This page is for MSP administrators.

## Before you start

- You need Launch Center manage access, or tenant settings access, to save the profile. Individual setup screens can need their own permissions.
- Decide the name clients should recognize, not an internal shorthand.
- Know the timezone your team works in.

## Enter the company profile in Launch Center

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the step">
    Go to **Launch Center** and select **Company profile** in **Setup guide**. It is in the **Foundation** phase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Enter your **Company name**. Choose your **Timezone**. Enter your **City** and **Country**. You need at least a city or a country.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save with Next">
    Click **Next**. It saves the profile and moves to the next step. If a field is highlighted, fix it and click **Next** again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Reopen the step and confirm the saved values. Then open **Live verification** and check that **Company profile has the required launch details** passes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Limits: the company name can be up to 200 characters, and city and country up to 120 characters.

## Edit the full tenant record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tenant settings">
    Go to **Settings > Administration > Tenant** and stay on the **Details** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the organization details">
    In **Tenant Details**, set **Company Name** and **Billing Email**. Choose the **Primary Admin** from your current staff users. Turn on **Restrict users to assigned companies only** if staff should only see their assigned companies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set locations and preferences">
    In **Locations & Preferences**, set **Timezone**, **Default Language**, and **Default Currency**. Click **Add Location** to add an office or site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Settings** in the page header. Adding or editing a location saves from its own dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choose the timezone before you schedule client work, because it sets the default for scheduling and reports. Each person can still choose a personal timezone in User Settings. Review [Billing](/user-guides/settings/billing) separately, since a billing email does not activate a subscription.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Next** shows a save error | Fix the highlighted field and click **Next** again. |
| You cannot edit the fields | Check that you have Launch Center manage access or tenant settings access. |
| "Setup changed in another session" | Another administrator updated the plan. Reopen the step and compare values before you save. |
| The profile check still fails | It needs a company name, a timezone, and a city or country. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a street address for the profile check?">
    No. The check needs a name, a timezone, and a city or country.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where did the Save Changes button go?">
    The Launch Center profile saves with **Next**. On the Tenant settings page, use **Save Settings** in the header.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does entering a billing email activate my subscription?">
    No. Review subscription and payment in Billing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
