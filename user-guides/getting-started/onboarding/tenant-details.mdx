Set the company profile that the rest of the portal uses for names, schedules, and reports. This page is for MSP administrators.

## Before you start

- You need Launch Center manage access and tenant settings write access to save the details in Launch Center. Individual setup screens can need their own permissions.
- Decide the name clients should recognize, not an internal shorthand.
- Know the timezone your team works in.

## Confirm your organization in Launch Center

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the step">
    Go to **Launch Center** and select **Organization and support** in **Launch steps**. It is in the **Portal setup** phase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Under **Organization**, enter your **Organization name**, choose the **Primary administrator** from your active staff, and choose your **Timezone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save with Continue">
    Click **Continue**. It saves the details, confirms the step, and moves on. A short **Saved** note appears. If a field is highlighted, fix it and click **Continue** again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Reopen the step and confirm the saved values.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The organization name can be up to 200 characters. The same step also holds the client-facing support details. See [Support identity and AI settings](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/ai-settings). To set your address, locale, and currency, click **More organization settings (address, locale, currency)**. It opens Tenant settings in a new tab.

This step records your review. It does not validate every profile field or check the billing address.

## Edit the full tenant record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tenant settings">
    Go to **Settings > Administration > Tenant** and stay on the **Details** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the organization details">
    In **Tenant Details**, set **Company Name** and **Billing Email**. Choose the **Primary Admin** from your current staff users. Turn on **Restrict users to assigned companies only** if staff should only see their assigned companies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set locations and preferences">
    In **Locations & Preferences**, set **Timezone**, **Default Language**, and **Default Currency**. Click **Add Location** to add an office or site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Settings** in the page header. Adding or editing a location saves from its own dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choose the timezone before you schedule client work, because it sets the default for scheduling and reports. Each person can still choose a personal timezone in User Settings. Review [Billing](/user-guides/settings/billing) separately, since a billing email does not activate a subscription.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Continue** shows a save error | Fix the highlighted field and click **Continue** again. |
| You cannot edit the fields | Check that you have Launch Center manage access and tenant settings access. Without them, the step shows the saved values read-only. |
| "Someone else changed the launch at the same time" | Another administrator updated Launch Center. Reopen the step and compare values before you save. |
| **Save Settings** fails | Correct the indicated field and save again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does confirming the Launch step validate my address?">
    No. Review and save your business and billing address in Tenant settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where did the Save Changes button go?">
    The Launch Center step saves with **Continue**. On the Tenant settings page, use **Save Settings** in the header.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does entering a billing email activate my subscription?">
    No. Review subscription and payment in Billing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
