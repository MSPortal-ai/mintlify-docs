Turn on the courses your clients should see and assign them to the right people. This page is for MSP staff with Training settings access. The Training step in Launch Center is optional.

## Before you start

- You need write access to Training settings to enable courses, and Training write or manage access to create learning paths.
- Premium courses use the Bigger Brains catalog. They are available only to companies that have Premium training turned on. See [Enable training](/user-guides/training/enabling-training).
- Know which companies and users should take training first.
- If your organization uses the direct Bigger Brains experience, **Settings > Training** does not appear and you manage training in the **Training** module.

## Enable courses

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the step">
    In **Launch Center**, select **Training foundation** in the **Training** phase of **Setup guide**. It shows the course table from **Settings > Training**. In this step, your change applies to your whole organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the courses">
    Use the search box and the column filters for **Type**, category, **Duration**, and **Enabled**. **Type** shows **Included**, **Premium**, or **Custom**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn them on">
    Click the **Enabled** switch on each course you want, or tick several rows and choose **Enable Selected**. The change saves right away and a message confirms it. There is no Save button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview a course (optional)">
    Open a row menu and choose **Preview Course**. The course opens in a new tab. A staff preview does not prove a client has access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To enable courses for a single company later, open **Settings > Training**, choose that company in the company selector, and turn courses on. See [Training settings](/user-guides/settings/training).

## Assign a learning path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Learning Paths">
    Go to **Training > Learning Paths**. Check the company selector first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the path">
    Click **Create Learning Path**. Enter a title and optional description, and choose the company, or the tenant-wide option when available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the courses">
    On **Courses**, tick the courses to include. Use only courses that are enabled for the companies you are assigning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the learners">
    On **Assignments**, choose **Users** or **User Groups**, select the targets, and add them to the assigned list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Learning Path**. Reopen the path and confirm the courses and the assigned learners.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Manage learning paths](/user-guides/training/learning-paths) for editing, templates, and groups.

## Check launch readiness

In **Launch Center**, open **Live verification**.

| Check | What it needs |
| --- | --- |
| **At least one training course is enabled** | A course available to every rollout company. |
| **Training has an audience assignment** | A learning path assigned to an active user or group at every rollout company, with a course they can access. |

Both are blocking when your plan includes training. Enabling a course in the catalog is not the same as assigning it, and a saved path is not the same as an assignment. These checks do not prove anyone watched a lesson, passed a quiz, or got a reminder.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A company has no course evidence | Check that the course is enabled for it, and that Premium access is on for Premium courses. |
| The assignment check fails | Check that the path is assigned to an active user or group at that company and holds a course they can reach. |
| A learner cannot see a course | Check training permissions and whether the course is enabled for their company before you build a duplicate path. |
| You do not see **Settings > Training** | You may use the direct Bigger Brains experience, or lack Training settings access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a course in the catalog mean clients can take it?">
    No. Turn it on with the **Enabled** switch, and for Premium courses turn on Premium training for the company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does creating a learning path assign it?">
    Only if you add learners on **Assignments** before you save. Reopen the path to confirm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does training readiness prove people finished a course?">
    No. It checks configuration and assignments, not completion or reminders.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
