Get the client contacts for your pilot companies into the portal with the right company and access group. This page is for MSP staff with Company-user settings access.

## Before you start

- You need write access to Company users in **Settings > Users & Roles**.
- Your rollout companies must exist and be mapped to your PSA first. See [Import companies](/user-guides/integrations/import-companies).
- A Company access group must exist for the users. See [Company access groups](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/company-permission-groups).
- Importing a user does not send an invitation.

## Import PSA contacts in the connection

If your PSA sets up inside Launch Center, you can import contacts there. After you import or match at least one company, open the PSA's **Import or match users** task under **Connect your PSA**. Other companies can wait, and you can return later to import more users.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select contacts">
    Use **Import** beside a contact, or tick contacts. **Select current page** selects the visible page, and you can then select all pages that match your filters. **Select all with email** selects every matching contact that has an email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import them">
    Click **Import selected**. Choose the Company **Role** and review the import and skipped counts before you confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Contacts without an email address are skipped. You can add an address in the dialog to include a contact, and correct or clear an invalid address before you confirm. A synced contact in staging is not a portal user until you import it. See [Import or match PSA contacts in the connection page](/user-guides/integrations/import-users#import-or-match-psa-contacts-in-the-connection-page).

## Review and add Company users

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Company Users">
    Go to **Settings > Users & Roles** and open **Company Users**. In **Launch Center**, **Import and invite client users** in the **Portal checklist** opens the same tab in a new browser tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review who is already there">
    Use the company selector and the table filters to look at the users for one company. Check each person's company, email, active state, and **Permissions Group** before you import again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import contacts from a provider">
    Click **Import**. The **Bulk Import Company Users** dialog lists your connected providers. Choose your PSA or Microsoft 365, then pick the contacts and the access group, and confirm. Bulk import creates users across all companies in your organization, so check your company mappings first. Follow [Import Users](/user-guides/integrations/import-users#import-company-users) for each field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or add one user by hand">
    Go to **Settings > Companies**, open the company, and open its **Users** tab. Click **Add User**, fill in the person's details, choose a **Permissions Group**, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the saved user">
    Click the user's name. In **Edit Company User**, confirm the company, email, status, and **Permissions Group**. Where a provider sync manages roles, also review its contact-type rules, company defaults and integration defaults.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for each rollout company">
    Do the same for every company you plan to launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send invitations when you are ready

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the users">
    On **Company Users**, tick the users you want to invite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the email">
    Open **Actions** and click **Send Welcome Email**, or open a user's row menu. Confirm in the dialog. This sends a real email, so do it only when the pilot is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check launch progress

In **Launch Center**, the **Portal checklist** item **Import and invite client users** counts client users who are active or invited, and ticks itself once there is at least one. That count does not prove every rollout company has a ready user. Check that each company you plan to launch has an active Company user with the right company and access group. A staged contact, an inactive user, or an unassigned user is not ready for access.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A user's role changed after a sync | Check the provider's contact-type rules and the company and integration defaults. Do not override the same field repeatedly. |
| A user is in the wrong company | Fix the source company mapping first, then check the affected records before you import more. |
| A user cannot sign in | Check their status and access separately from the integration. Check that an invitation was sent. |
| An imported contact is missing | Staged provider contacts are not portal users. Import them from **Company Users** or the PSA's **Import or match users** task. |
| A contact was skipped | It has no email address. Add one in the import dialog and import it again. |
| A company has no ready user | Open **Company Users**, filter to that company, and check that someone is active with an access group. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a staged provider contact count as a ready Company user?">
    No. You need an active portal Company user with a company and an access role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does one active user cover all rollout companies?">
    No. Review the users and access for each company you plan to launch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does importing a contact send an invitation?">
    No. Send invitations separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do Team Roles replace the access group?">
    No. The access group controls permissions. Team Roles assign MSP staff to a company.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
