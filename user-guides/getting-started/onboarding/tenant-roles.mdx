Create permission roles for your own team, then assign them to staff. This page is for MSP administrators with role-management access.

## Before you start

- You must be the Primary Admin or have role-management access in **Settings > Users & Roles**.
- Plan which modules each kind of staff member needs, for example help desk, account manager, or read-only.
- Tenant Roles are for MSP staff. Company Roles are for your clients' users. See [Company access groups](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/company-permission-groups).

## Create a staff role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Roles tab">
    In **Launch Center**, select **Access and permissions** in **Setup guide**. It opens **Users & Roles** on the **Roles** tab. You can also go to **Settings > Users & Roles** and open **Roles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new role">
    In **Tenant Roles**, click **Add Role**. To start from an existing role instead, open its row menu and click **Duplicate**, then edit the copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the role">
    Enter a **Name** such as Help Desk Technician, and a **Description**. Check **Default role for new Tenant users** only if this role should be the default for new staff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the permissions">
    Under **Permissions**, set **Read**, **Write**, or **Manage** for each resource in **Main Navigation**, **MSP Tools**, and **Settings**. Use **Search permissions...** to find a resource. The **All** checkbox on a section selects everything in it, so check before you save. You can only grant permissions you hold yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the role">
    Click **Create Role**. To edit a saved role later, click **Update Role**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assign the role to staff

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Users tab">
    Go to **Settings > Users & Roles** and open **Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the user">
    Open the row menu for the person and click **Edit**. Choose the role in **Role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. Role changes apply the next time the user signs in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the access">
    Ask the staff member to sign in and check that they can open what they need and nothing more. A role name alone does not prove what it permits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check launch readiness

In **Launch Center**, open **Live verification**. **At least one permission group beyond Administrator is configured** is a blocking check. It passes when your organization has at least one tenant or company group of its own other than **Administrator**. It does not check that each staff member holds the right role.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot see the **Roles** tab | Ask an administrator for role-management access. |
| A permission is greyed out | You can only grant permissions you hold. Ask the Primary Admin. |
| The readiness check still fails | Create or duplicate a tenant or company role of your own. The built-in Administrator group does not count. |
| A role change did not apply | Ask the user to sign out and back in. |
| You only want the check to pass | Create a role your team will use. Do not add duplicates just to clear the finding. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are access groups the same as Team Roles?">
    No. Roles grant permissions. Team Roles describe responsibilities and grant none.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does creating a role give it to anyone?">
    No. A new role has no users until you assign it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the launch check prove every staff permission is right?">
    No. Test a real staff account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
