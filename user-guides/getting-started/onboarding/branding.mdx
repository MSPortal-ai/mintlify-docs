Set your brand color and logos once. They appear in the sidebar, buttons, links, the favicon, and the reports and certificates MSPortal generates. This page is for MSP administrators with tenant branding access.

## Before you start

- Open branding in **Launch Center** (the **Branding** step) or in **Settings > Administration > Tenant** on the **Branding** tab. Both show the same editor.
- Have your logo files ready. Use a PNG, JPEG, or SVG file under 1.5 MB. A favicon must be under 256 KB.
- Have your primary brand color as a hex value, such as `#0f766e`.

## Set your brand color

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Colors tab">
    Open the **Colors** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the primary color">
    In **Primary brand color**, choose a swatch or type a 3 or 6 digit hex value. MSPortal builds light and dark variants for you. The live preview shows buttons, a badge, a sidebar item, and a link in light and dark mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the color">
    Click **Save color**. Wait for **Brand color saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Tenant branding shows light and dark sample previews. Save color is separate from Save Settings.">
  <img src="/images/product/tenant-branding-colors.png" alt="Tenant branding includes built-in light and dark sample previews. Save color is separate from header Save Settings." width="1800" height="1240" />
</Frame>

The color applies to every user in your organization. There is no per-user override.

## Upload logos

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Light Mode">
    Open the **Light Mode** tab. It has **Primary Logo (Light)**, shown in the expanded sidebar, and **Small Icon (Light)**, shown when the sidebar is collapsed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the primary logo">
    Click **Upload** under **Primary Logo (Light)** and choose a file. A wide logo around 140 x 32 px works best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Crop and apply">
    In the **Crop Logo** dialog, position and zoom the image, then click **Save & Apply**. Wait for **Logo updated successfully**. Closing the dialog without **Save & Apply** does not save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the small icon">
    Click **Upload** under **Small Icon (Light)**. Use a square image around 32 x 32 px, crop it, and click **Save & Apply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for dark mode">
    Open **Dark Mode** and upload **Primary Logo (Dark)** and **Small Icon (Dark)**. If you skip these, MSPortal uses the light versions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each logo saves when you apply the crop. You do not need another save click.

## Upload the favicon and the reports logo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the favicon">
    Open the **Favicon** tab and click **Upload**. Use an ICO or PNG file under 256 KB, around 32 x 32 px. It shows in browser tabs when people use your custom domain. There is no crop dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the reports logo">
    Open the **Reports & Certs** tab and click **Upload**. This logo appears on generated reports, documents, and training certificates. Any size works, and a PNG with a transparent background looks best.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Both uploads save as soon as you pick the file.

## Customize the sign-in page (optional)

The **Sign-in Page** tab changes how the sign-in page looks on your custom domain. You can set the logo size, a solid, gradient, or image background, a welcome title and subtitle, and help instructions. Click **Save sign-in page** to apply, or **Reset to default** to remove your changes.

## Check where the brand appears

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at both themes">
    Switch between light and dark mode and check the sidebar, the compact logo, and the browser tab icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a report">
    Open a generated report or a training certificate and check the reports logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check readiness">
    In **Launch Center**, open **Live verification**. **A portal logo is configured** and **Brand colors are configured** are recommended checks. They confirm the assets exist, not that they look right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reset branding

Click **Reset to Default** at the top right of the editor to clear all logos and the favicon. A confirmation asks first. This affects the whole organization.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Please upload a PNG, JPEG, SVG, or ICO image." | Use one of those file types. |
| "Logo must be under 1.5MB" or "Favicon must be under 256KB" | Compress the file and upload it again. |
| The logo is clipped | Pick a source image with space around the wordmark and crop again. |
| A logo disappears in dark mode | Upload a contrasting version under **Dark Mode**. |
| The favicon looks stale | Reload the page before you upload it again. |
| The upload buttons are disabled | You need tenant branding access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does closing the cropper save the logo?">
    No. Click **Save & Apply** and wait for the success message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Save Settings save my logos and color?">
    No. Logos, the favicon, and the reports logo save on upload. The color saves with **Save color**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a passing branding check prove my logos look right?">
    No. Check light, dark, compact, and report placements yourself.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
