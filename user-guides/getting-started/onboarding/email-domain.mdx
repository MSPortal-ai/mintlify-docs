Send portal notifications from your own domain, such as `support@yourdomain.com`, instead of the default MSPortal sender. This page is for MSP administrators with Email settings access and someone who manages the domain's DNS. It is not a Launch Center step, so do it when you are ready to email clients.

## Before you start

- You need write access to Email settings. Without it, you see **No sending domain configured. Contact an administrator to set one up.**
- Have access to your DNS provider, or ask the person who does.
- Pick the domain you want to send from, for example `yourdomain.com`.

## Add and verify the domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sending Domain">
    Go to **Settings > Email** and open the **Sending Domain** tab. If a domain is already listed, review it before you add another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the domain">
    Under **Custom Sending Domain**, enter your domain in **Domain Name** and click **Add Domain**. You see **Domain added successfully. Please add the DNS records to verify.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the DNS records">
    Copy each record shown for the domain, with its type, name, and value, into your DNS provider. Use exactly what the page shows. Example SPF and DKIM values from other guides will not work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check verification">
    When the records are in place, click **Check Verification**. The status shows **Pending DNS**, **Verifying**, **Verified**, or **Failed**. Pending does not mean verified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the status">
    Click **Refresh Status** to get the latest result. If it still says **Pending DNS** or **Failed**, compare each record with your DNS provider before you try again. Some providers add your domain name to the host name for you, so check for a doubled suffix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the sender address">
    Once the status is **Verified**, the page shows **Domain verified and ready to use** and the **Default From Address** field. Enter the address, such as `support@yourdomain.com`, and click **Save**. Wait for **Default from address updated**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

DNS changes can take time to spread. Your DNS provider and the status on the page tell you when it is ready. Verification improves sender trust but does not guarantee inbox placement.

## Remove a sending domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Sending Domain tab">
    Go to **Settings > Email > Sending Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Remove">
    Click **Remove**. A dialog explains what happens. MSPortal stops sending from the domain, goes back to the default msportal.ai sender, and needs a new verification if you add the domain again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Remove Domain**. This does not delete the DNS records at your provider, so clean those up yourself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What this setting does not do

A sending domain sets the sender of outbound messages. It does not set up ticket delivery, your support mailbox, or inbound email. Those are separate. Launch Center's support check needs only a **Support email**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Status stays **Pending DNS** | Compare each record's type, name, and value with your DNS provider. Check for a doubled domain suffix. Wait, then **Check Verification** again. |
| Status shows **Failed** | Fix the record the page points to, then check verification again. Ask the domain administrator to check for conflicting records. |
| No **Default From Address** field | It appears only after the domain is **Verified**. |
| **Save** is greyed out | The address field is empty. Enter an address on the verified domain. |
| You cannot add a domain | You need write access to Email settings. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I copy generic SPF and DKIM examples?">
    No. Use the exact records shown for your domain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Check Verification mean the domain is verified?">
    No. Read the status. It can stay pending.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a sending domain set up incoming support tickets?">
    No. Sending, receiving, and ticket delivery are separate settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
