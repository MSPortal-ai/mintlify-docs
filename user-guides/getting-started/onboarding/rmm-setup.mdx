Connect your primary RMM so MSPortal can pull in sites and devices. This page is for MSP staff with integration-management access. The same flow covers Datto RMM, NinjaOne, ConnectWise Asio, N-central, Addigy, Meraki, UniFi, and WatchGuard.

## Before you start

- Choose your RMM in the **Integrations** step first. See [Choose launch integrations](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/integration-selection).
- Have your RMM administrator create API credentials. The fields differ by provider, so keep the provider's setup guide open (see Related guides).
- Connect your PSA first and map your companies. RMM sites map to those companies. See [PSA connection](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/psa-setup).

## Connect your RMM

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the step">
    In **Launch Center**, select **Connect your RMM** in **Setup guide**. The RMM wizard opens inside the plan. You can also use **Settings > Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    On the **Connect** step, enter your API credentials, such as a key and secret, and a region or URL where the provider asks for one. Click **Test Connection**. When the test passes, click **Create Integration**, then **Next**. If you see **Connection failed**, check the credentials and the region, then retest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map sites or companies">
    On the **Map** step, wait for the first sync to finish. It can take a few minutes, and much longer for a large ConnectWise Asio account. If the list is empty, click the sync button or check for updates. For each provider site or company, click **Select company...** and choose the matching MSPortal company. **Quick Match** suggests matches by name, so check each one. Click **Create new company** if no match exists. Click **Import Selected** to save the mappings. You must map at least one before you continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map locations (optional)">
    Providers that support it show a **Map Locations** step. Map each location to a PSA location, then click **Save All**, or click **Skip**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open device import">
    On the **Import** step, click **Open Device Import**. You land on the integration's device page. Continue in [Import rollout devices](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/device-import).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete setup">
    Return to the wizard and click **Complete Setup** when you have finished.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check the connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at the sync status">
    Open the integration in **Settings > Integrations**. Check that the last sync finished and that your sites show the right device counts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Launch Center">
    In **Launch Center**, open **Live verification**. **A healthy RMM connection is available** is blocking when your plan needs an RMM. It checks the instance chosen in your plan. An unrelated active integration does not satisfy it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Staged devices alone do not satisfy device readiness. Import them next.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Connection failed** | Check the key, secret, region or URL, and that the API user is active in your RMM. |
| The **Map** list is empty | The first sync may still be running. Wait, then click the check or sync button. |
| "You must map at least one site before proceeding." | Map one site to a company and click **Import Selected**. |
| The wrong connection is checked | Go back to **Integrations** and choose the intended instance. |
| No devices show later | Check the sync status, the mapping, and the API user's permissions. Do not clear and resync before you know why. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a successful credential test complete the rollout?">
    No. Check the sync, the site mappings, the imported devices, and the remaining findings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can any active connection satisfy this step?">
    No. The check applies to the instance selected in your plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Addigy without another RMM?">
    Yes. Pick Addigy under **Other integrations**. Launch Center then uses it for the device step.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
