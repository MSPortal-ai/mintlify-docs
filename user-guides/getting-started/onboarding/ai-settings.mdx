Tell clients what your portal is called and where to get help, and decide whether the AI assistant is on. This page is for MSP administrators.

## Before you start

- You need Launch Center manage access, or tenant settings access.
- Have a monitored support email address ready. Clients will see it.
- Pick a short portal name your clients already associate with your MSP.

## Set the support identity in Launch Center

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the step">
    Go to **Launch Center** and select **Organization and support** in **Launch steps**. It is in the **Portal setup** phase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Under **Client-facing details**, enter your **Portal name**, **Support email**, **Support phone**, and **Website**. The portal name can be up to 100 characters. Enter the website as a full web address, such as `https://example.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save with Continue">
    Click **Continue**. It saves your changes and confirms the step. If a field shows an error, fix it and click **Continue** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The step records your review. Entering an address does not check that the mailbox receives mail. Send a test message to confirm. The same support email and portal name appear on the **Context** tab of the AI settings below.

## Set the AI context and access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the AI tab">
    Go to **Settings > Tenant** and click the **AI** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can use AI">
    On **General**, turn **Enable all AI-powered features** on or off. Turn **Enable AI features for client users** on or off to control what company users see. When it is off, company users see non-AI screens and your team keeps AI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your contact details">
    On **Context**, enter the **Portal name**, **Support Email**, **Support Phone**, **Sales Phone**, **Emergency Phone**, **Business Hours**, **Website URL**, and **General Notes**. The assistant can share these with users. Do not enter passwords or other secrets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Settings** in the page header.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Home page** tab customizes the AI Home welcome screen, and **Assistant panel** sets the starter questions people see. Enabling AI does not give anyone access to data their role and company scope do not allow.

## Check the client experience

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Home as a client">
    Use your approved client-review or impersonation process and open Home with a company user role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for the assistant">
    Check that the assistant and shortcuts you expect are there. If not, recheck the two AI switches and the user's role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The support email is separate from [ticket delivery](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/ticket-settings) and from [sending-domain verification](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/email-domain). Set each one on its own.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Continue** shows a save error | Fix the highlighted field. The portal name needs 100 characters or fewer, the support email must be valid, and the website must be a full web address. |
| The assistant is missing for clients | Check **Enable all AI-powered features** and **Enable AI features for client users**. Then check the company's AI setting and the user's role. |
| The step is read-only | You need Launch Center manage access and tenant settings access. Ask a teammate with access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do AI context notes complete support setup?">
    No. Save the support email and portal name, then confirm the step in Launch Center.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does enabling AI give clients access to all data?">
    No. Company scope and permissions still apply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the support email configure ticket delivery?">
    No. Set ticket delivery separately in ticket settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
