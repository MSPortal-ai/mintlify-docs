Team Roles name the responsibilities on a client account, such as account manager or technician. You define the roles, then assign your staff to each company. This page is for MSP staff with Company-user settings access.

## Before you start

- You need read access to **Company Users** or **Companies** settings to open the **Team Roles** tab. You need write access to either one to add and edit roles.
- Assigning staff in **Settings > Companies** needs write access to Companies settings.
- Team Roles do not grant permissions. Permissions come from [access groups](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/tenant-roles).
- An assignment can limit a staff member's company selector to their assigned companies, and it decides who appears in the client's team menu.
- Have your list of rollout companies and the staff for each.

## Define the roles

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Roles">
    Go to **Settings > Users & Roles** and open **Team Roles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a role">
    Click **Add Role**. Enter a **Role Name**, such as Account Manager, and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the role">
    Click **Save role**. A message confirms **Role created successfully**. To change a role later, use **Edit role**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A role definition alone does not assign anyone.

## Assign staff to a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company's actions">
    Go to **Settings > Companies** and open the company's row menu. In Launch Center, **Assign client team roles** in the **Portal checklist** opens this page in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Assign Teams">
    Click **Assign Teams**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the role and people">
    Choose the Team Role and the MSP staff members responsible for this company, then confirm. Repeat for each role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Wait for the success message. If only some assignments saved, the message says how many companies succeeded. Fix the rest and try again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To assign the same people to many companies, select the companies in **Settings > Companies**, open **Actions**, and click **Assign Teams to Selected**. Choose the role, the people, and the companies. Check the selected company count first, because bulk assignments apply to all of them at once.

## Show the team to clients (optional)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the client teams menu settings">
    On the **Team Roles** tab, find the **Client teams menu** card and click **Configure**. You need write access to roles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to show">
    Turn on **Show the teams menu in the client portal**. Choose which roles appear in **Team Roles shown**, and which details to share: name, email, phone, and profile photo. Select at least one role and one detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the client view">
    Sign in as a client user, or use your approved review process, and open the team menu in the top bar. Check the contact details before rollout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check launch progress

In **Launch Center**, the **Portal checklist** item **Assign client team roles** shows how many team roles are assigned and ticks itself once at least one exists. That count does not prove every rollout company has a team. Check the assignments for each company before you invite clients.

## Provider-managed teams

If your PSA role mappings are offered, review which responsibilities sync. For mapped ConnectWise roles, sync adds and removes members to match the PSA. Roles you did not map stay manual. If an assignment keeps changing, check the mapping before you edit it again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A company still has no team | Check that you assigned a person, not only created a role. |
| A staff member's company selector changed | Check their team assignments. Assignments can limit the selector. |
| A client cannot see an expected team member | Check the selected company, the role assignment, and **Client teams menu** settings. |
| An assignment keeps reverting | A mapped PSA role is syncing it. Change it in the PSA or the mapping. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are Team Roles job titles for client users?">
    No. They assign MSP staff responsibilities, can limit staff company scope, and control the client team menu.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a role finish account-team setup?">
    No. Each rollout company needs a real staff assignment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a ConnectWise team assignment change on its own?">
    Mapped ConnectWise roles sync membership from the PSA, including removals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
