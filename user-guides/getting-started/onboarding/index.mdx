Use this playbook to prepare your MSP's portal for clients. [Launch Center](/user-guides/getting-started/launch-center) builds a plan around your goals and data sources, so the steps you see depend on what you chose. This page lists the work in the order most MSPs do it, with a guide for each part. Client onboarding is a separate [workflow for individual companies](/user-guides/client-onboarding/index).

## Before you start

- Use an MSP account with access to the settings you will change. Launch Center manage access does not replace a setup screen's own permissions.
- Agree on a launch coordinator, a pilot company, and where your companies, users, and devices come from.
- Have these ready:
  - PSA API credentials, and RMM API credentials if you use one
  - A Microsoft 365 administrator for each client tenant you will connect
  - Your logo files and brand color
  - A domain you can add DNS records to, if you will send email from your own domain

## Work through the rollout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build your plan">
    Open **Launch Center**, click **Build my launch plan**, and choose your goals, data source, and rollout. See [Work through your portal launch plan](/user-guides/getting-started/launch-center).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the foundation">
    Save your [company profile](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/tenant-details), [support email and AI settings](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/ai-settings), [branding](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/branding), and review [billing](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/billing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up access">
    Create [staff access groups](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/tenant-roles) and [Company access groups](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/company-permission-groups). Then define [account teams and Team Roles](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/company-roles).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your systems">
    [Choose your integrations](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/integration-selection), then connect your [PSA](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/psa-setup), your [RMM](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/rmm-setup), and [Microsoft 365 for each company](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/company-m365).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in client data">
    Map your companies, then [prepare Company users](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/company-users) and [import devices](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/device-import). Staged provider records are not portal records until you import them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up client tasks">
    Configure [ticket delivery, visibility, and templates](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/ticket-settings) and [training](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/training). If you want email from your own domain, set up your [sending domain](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/email-domain).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check readiness and launch">
    Open **Live verification**, fix every **Blocking** finding, review the client experience, and click **Complete launch**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you only have time for a first pass, do the company profile, staff access, branding, and your PSA and RMM. Everything else can come later.

## Save and verify

Open each step from **Setup guide**. Each embedded screen has its own save behavior. **Next** saves the step and moves on, so wait for the result and check readiness. Moving to another step or clicking **Do this later** can also save pending changes, so neither is a way to discard a draft.

Readiness covers the rollout you chose. A pilot checks its company. A wider rollout can need evidence for every active client company. One connected provider or one imported user does not necessarily satisfy the plan. Recheck after you change the scope.

## Before you invite clients

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check access">
    Confirm each client user has the right company and access group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check content">
    Confirm their templates and training are enabled, and that records belong to the right company. See [Control what Company users can see](/user-guides/getting-started/hiding-items-from-clients).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check ticketing">
    Check ticket delivery separately from template visibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite when ready">
    Completing launch records setup. Invitations and other client messages are separate actions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A step is blocked | Follow its finding in **Live verification** to the step that fixes it. |
| Launch Center is missing | Check your permissions. Also check whether the launch was completed or an administrator dismissed it. |
| The plan has different steps than this page | Launch Center builds the plan from your goals, data sources, and rollout. |
