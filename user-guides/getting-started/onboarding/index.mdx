Use [Launch Center](/user-guides/getting-started/launch-center) to prepare your MSP's portal step by step. Everyone gets the same steps in the same order, and you defer the optional ones you do not need. This page lists the work in each phase, with a guide for each part. Client onboarding is a separate [workflow for individual companies](/user-guides/client-onboarding/index).

## Before you start

- Use an MSP account with access to the settings you will change. Launch Center manage access does not replace a setup screen's own permissions.
- Agree on a launch lead and where your companies, users, and devices come from.
- Have these ready:
  - PSA API credentials, and RMM API credentials if you use one
  - A Microsoft 365 administrator for each client tenant you will connect
  - Your logo files and brand color
  - A domain you can add DNS records to, if you will send email from your own domain

## Work through the setup

| Phase | Work and guides |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect** | [Choose integrations](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/integration-selection), configure the [PSA](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/psa-setup) and optional [RMM](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/rmm-setup), [import or match companies and users](/user-guides/integrations/import-companies), then set [ticket delivery and visibility](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/ticket-settings). Review security and other integrations even when you do not need any. Connect [Microsoft 365 for each company](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/company-m365) when you are ready. |
| **Standards** | [Discover standards and set plans](/user-guides/settings/standards), add-ons, billing evidence, plan contents, detection and company-type mappings. Review what clients should have against what they have. |
| **Roadmap** | Bring in existing client work. Review proposed PSA opportunity imports or [Standards sync](/user-guides/settings/sync-standards) before applying them. |
| **Portal setup** | Review [tenant details](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/tenant-details), [support](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/ai-settings), [branding](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/branding), [billing](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/billing), [staff access](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/tenant-roles), [client users](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/company-users), [devices](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/device-import) and [account teams](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/company-roles). Continue through request templates, [training](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/training), compliance playbooks, client review meetings and the client preview. If you want email from your own domain, set up your [sending domain](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/email-domain). |
| **Finish** | Resolve the remaining required steps and click **Finish launch**. Invitations remain separate actions. |

If you only have time for a first pass, do the nine required steps: connect your PSA, import or match your clients, set up tickets, and work through the required Standards steps. Everything else can come later.

## Save and review

Use **Launch steps** to choose a step. **Continue** saves what the step saves, then moves on. Complete a step's visible save, connect or import actions and check their results before you leave.

Review the **Portal setup** controls in place and wait for their save result. Where the **Portal checklist** opens a larger settings page in a new tab, save there, return to Launch Center and click **Confirm and continue**. This records your review. It is not an automatic check of every setting. Optional steps offer **Do this later** and **Resume**. **Do this later** does not save edits you have not saved, and previously saved configuration stays saved.

On **Find your standards**, **Anything else?** has a **Chat about my standards** button that opens the assistant when it is available. Use it to discuss missed tools and services, then review the suggested changes before saving.

## Before you invite clients

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check access">
    Confirm each client user has the right company and role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check content">
    Confirm their templates and training are enabled, and that imported records belong to the right company. See [Control what Company users can see](/user-guides/getting-started/hiding-items-from-clients).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check ticketing">
    Check ticket delivery separately from template visibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the client experience">
    If you use **Impersonate** to check a client user's view, exit impersonation before you return to Launch Center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite when ready">
    Finishing the launch records setup. Invitations and other client messages are separate actions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A step is waiting | Finish the earlier steps it names under **Opens after**. |
| A step needs attention | Open it and fix the connection or setting behind it. |
| Launch Center is missing | Check your permissions. Also check whether the launch was completed or an administrator dismissed it for the organization. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is this the client onboarding checklist?">
    No. This playbook prepares the MSP portal. Individual clients use the separate client onboarding workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Do this later discard my draft?">
    No, but it does not save it either. Use **Continue** to save a form first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does finishing the launch invite clients?">
    No. Client invitations are separate actions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
