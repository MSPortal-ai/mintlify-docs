Tell Launch Center which systems you use, then work through each connection. This page is for MSP staff managing Launch Center. Entering credentials also needs integration-management access.

## Before you start

- Know your PSA, your primary RMM, and any other tools you want in the launch, such as documentation, security, or backup.
- Know who owns the credentials for each system.
- Choosing a provider does not connect it. Credentials and consent come in the connection tasks.

## Choose your systems

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your PSA">
    Open **Connect your PSA** in **Launch steps** and select your PSA's logo card. A card shows **Connected** when your organization already has that integration. **Setup guide** on the card opens the provider's instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it">
    Complete the connection tasks the step offers. Some providers set up inside Launch Center. Others show **Open setup in Settings**. An existing healthy connection can complete the step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your primary RMM">
    In **Connect your RMM**, select your primary RMM, or **No RMM** if you do not use one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through clients and tickets">
    Complete **Import or match your clients** and **Set up tickets**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose security and other tools">
    In **Connect security tools** and **Connect other tools**, tick every provider you use. You can select several. Each ticked card shows its connection status and an **Open** action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish each connection">
    Open each chosen provider and complete its required setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The provider choices use the same logo cards across PSA, RMM, security and other tools. The first choice does not ask you to select a connection instance.

## Connect other tools

**Connect other tools** groups providers under **Productivity**, **Documentation**, **Distribution and quoting**, **Backup**, **Infrastructure**, and **Migration from other tools**. On desktop, scroll inside the right-hand provider panel to reach every group. The step title and **Back** / **Confirm and continue** controls stay visible. On a phone, scroll the page to reach the remaining providers and controls. **Migration from other tools** contains CloudRadial for importing its service catalog. It is separate from productivity connections.

## Make an explicit choice on optional tools

**Connect security tools** and **Connect other tools** stay **To do** until you review them. If you need none, click **Confirm and continue** with no providers selected. You can also click **Do this later**, then **Resume** when you are ready.

Confirming a selection does not make an unfinished connection ready. Read the connection status and finish its required tasks.

## Change a choice

Click **Change** on the PSA or RMM step to return to its provider choices, or **Keep current choice** to cancel. On the security and other tools steps, tick or untick cards. Changes save the setup choice. Removing a provider from this list does not disconnect it or delete imported records.

Use the provider's settings to inspect its existing connections. If a connection is missing or needs attention, fix that connection's setup. Another connection for the same provider is not interchangeable.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A provider is missing from the list | Only providers available to your organization show. Check your features and plan. |
| A provider is ticked but still **Not connected** | Click **Open** on its card and finish the connection tasks. |
| A step stays **To do** with no tools chosen | Click **Confirm and continue** to record that you need none. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does selecting a provider connect it?">
    No. Credentials, consent, company matching, and imports are separate steps. Opening the documentation does not connect a provider either.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does removing a provider from my choices disconnect it?">
    No. Launch Center choices and integration connections are separate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
