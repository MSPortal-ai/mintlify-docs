Tell Launch Center which systems you use, so the plan adds a connection step for each. This page is for MSP staff managing Launch Center. Entering credentials also needs integration-management access.

## Connect other tools (branch preview)

In the updated Launch Center, open **Connect other tools**. Choose providers under **Distribution and quoting**, **Backup**, or the other groups shown. On desktop, scroll inside the right-hand provider panel to reach every group; the step title and **Back** / **Confirm and continue** controls stay visible. On a phone, scroll the page to reach the remaining providers and controls. Each provider card shows its own logo, and **Setup guide** opens that provider's instructions. **Migration from other tools** contains CloudRadial for importing its service catalog; it is separate from productivity connections.

## Before you start

- Know your PSA, your primary RMM, and any other tools you want in the launch, such as documentation, security, or backup.
- Know who owns the credentials for each system.
- Choosing a provider here does not connect it. Credentials and consent come in the next steps.

## Choose your systems

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the step">
    In **Launch Center**, select **Integrations** in the **Connect** phase of **Setup guide**. The workspace is titled **Choose integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your PSA">
    Under **Main integrations**, turn on **We use a PSA**. Under **PSA platform**, choose your PSA from **Select your PSA**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your primary RMM">
    Turn on **We use an RMM**. Under **Primary RMM platform**, choose your RMM from **Select your primary RMM**. Addigy is chosen separately under **Other integrations**, so you can use it next to your RMM for Mac management.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add other integrations">
    Under **Other integrations**, pick any other services for this launch. They are grouped as **Productivity and service catalog**, **Documentation**, **Security**, **Backup**, **Commerce and quoting**, and **Device management, infrastructure and audit**. You only see providers available to your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the connection instance">
    If you have more than one connection for a provider, choose one in **{provider} instance**. The default, **Use the healthiest available connection**, picks the best one. Choose an instance yourself if you have separate test and production connections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save integrations**. Wait for **Integrations saved**, then click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the new steps">
    Look at **Setup guide**. A connect step now exists for each system you chose, and readiness findings follow them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you picked a PSA or Microsoft 365 as your client data source when you built the plan, it stays selected here. To change that, click **Edit launch plan**.

## Change the selection later

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reopen the step">
    Go back to **Integrations** in **Setup guide**, or click **Edit launch plan** to change the client data source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your choices">
    Change the providers or instances. If a chosen instance was removed, you see **The integration instance selected in your plan is no longer available.** Choose a replacement yourself. Another connection for the same provider is not interchangeable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and review">
    Click **Save integrations**, then check the regenerated steps and findings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Removing a provider from the plan does not disconnect or delete that integration.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Choose a provider for each enabled PSA or RMM connection." | Pick a provider under **PSA platform** or **Primary RMM platform**, or turn the switch off. |
| A provider is missing from the list | Only providers available to your organization show. Check your features and plan. |
| The wrong instance is checked | Choose the instance in **{provider} instance**. Do not rely on the default if you have several. |
| **Integrations saved** but no connect step appears | Reload the plan and open **Setup guide**. The step appears only for systems that need setup. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does selecting a provider connect it?">
    No. Credentials, consent, and imports are separate steps.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which connection is checked if I have several?">
    The instance you chose. Do not swap in another one just because it is healthy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does removing an integration from the plan disconnect it?">
    No. Plan choices and integration connections are separate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
