MSPortal support keeps the platform working for you and helps you use it. This page sets out what we handle, what stays with your team, and what belongs to another vendor. It applies to every MSPortal subscription.

*Last updated: November 18, 2026*

## The short version

- **We support MSPortal.** We fix what is broken, answer questions about how features work, and troubleshoot the integrations we provide.
- **You run your portal.** Your administrators make the changes inside it: users, roles, permissions groups, companies, templates, forms, reports and content.
- **Your clients come to you.** We work with your MSP's staff. Company users get help from you.
- **Other vendors support their own products.** We help you work out which side a problem is on.

## What support covers

| Area | What we do |
| --- | --- |
| **Problems with the platform** | Errors, pages that will not load, data that displays incorrectly, and features that do not behave the way these guides describe. We investigate, fix the cause and tell you when it is resolved. |
| **How-to questions** | How a feature works, where a setting lives, which permission controls it, and which guide covers it. |
| **Integrations and syncs** | Connection, mapping and sync problems in the integrations MSPortal provides: a sync that fails, records that are missing or out of date, a credential that is rejected. We find out whether the cause is in MSPortal or with the provider. |
| **Sign-in problems** | A staff member who cannot sign in, and administrators who are locked out of the account. |
| **Billing and subscription** | Questions about your plan, invoices and usage. |
| **Feedback and feature requests** | We read every item, reply to it, and email you when it moves. Planned work is on the [product roadmap](https://app.msportal.ai/product-roadmap). |
| **Security and privacy** | Security concerns and requests about your data. |

## What stays with your team

MSPortal is built so your administrators can run it without us. When a request asks us to make a change inside your portal, we reply with the guide that shows how to do it. There are three reasons.

1. **Access is a security decision, and it is yours.** Who can see which company's data is for your MSP to decide and to answer for. A change your administrator makes is made under their own sign-in, with the permissions you gave them. We will not grant or change access because an email asked us to.
2. **It is faster.** Most of these changes take a minute or two in **Settings**. Waiting for a reply from us takes longer.
3. **You know your clients.** We do not know who belongs in which group, which contacts are current, or what your client agreed to.

| Outside our scope | What we do instead |
| --- | --- |
| **User and access administration**<br />"Add this person to this security group." "Give Dana the admin role." "Sam left, please remove him." | Point you to [Users & Roles](/user-guides/settings/users-roles) and [Change a Company user's permissions](/user-guides/settings/change-company-user-permissions). If the control does not work as described, that is a bug and we fix it. |
| **Configuration and content**<br />"Build our onboarding template." "Set up our ticket forms." "Create this report for our business review." | Explain how the feature works and suggest an approach. You build it and you own it. |
| **Data entry and cleanup**<br />"Import these 200 contacts." "Update these device records." "Fix the company names." | Show you the tools in [Import and export data](/user-guides/settings/import-export). If a sync created the bad data, that one is ours to investigate. |
| **Changes in your other tools**<br />"Add the user in Microsoft 365." "Create the API member in ConnectWise." "Change our Autotask security level." | The [integration guides](/user-guides/integrations/index) list what to set up on the provider's side. We do not make changes in your PSA, RMM or Microsoft 365 tenant for you. |
| **Support for your clients**<br />A Company user writes to us about their ticket, their access or their portal. | We send them back to you. You are their first line of support. Bring us anything that turns out to be a platform problem. |
| **Other vendors' products**<br />"Our RMM agent is not reporting." "The PSA API is returning errors." | We help you work out which side the problem is on. When it is the provider's, their support team owns the fix. |
| **Custom development**<br />"Write a script against the API." "Build our MCP workflow." "Debug our automation in another tool." | We document the API and the [MCP Server](/user-guides/mcp-server/index), confirm they behave as documented, and fix them when they do not. |
| **Training and consulting**<br />"Train our new hire." "Run a session for our client." "Tell us how to structure our agreements." | These guides, the release notes and answers to specific questions. |

## When we step in

- **Something is broken.** If you follow a guide and the result is wrong, tell us. Fixing it is our job, and we will not ask you to work around it.
- **You are locked out.** If none of your administrators can sign in, we restore access after confirming the request with someone we can verify at your MSP.
- **The portal cannot do it.** If a change is impossible from your side, we treat the request as a feature request and tell you what we plan to do with it.
- **There is a security concern.** Report it straight away. See [How to reach us](#how-to-reach-us).

## Who can contact support

Support is for your MSP's staff: people with their own sign-in to your MSPortal account.

- Requests that affect access, billing or your data must come from a staff member we can confirm has the authority to ask. We may check with one of your administrators before acting.
- Company users, meaning your clients, contact you. We do not take instructions about your portal from them.

## How to reach us

| Channel | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Send Feedback** in the portal | Bugs, questions and feature ideas. It records the page you are on, which helps us find the problem faster. |
| **Microsoft Teams chat** | Quick questions, if your MSP has a Teams chat with us. For bugs and feature ideas, use **Send Feedback** so the request is tracked and you get an email when it moves. |
| [support@msportal.ai](mailto:support@msportal.ai) | Anything you cannot send from inside the portal, including sign-in problems. |
| [security@msportal.ai](mailto:security@msportal.ai) | Security concerns. |
| [status.msportal.ai](https://status.msportal.ai) | Current platform status. Check here first during an outage. |

We aim to reply within one business day. Outages and security concerns come first. The same scope applies in every channel, including chat.

### What to include

A complete request gets a faster answer. Tell us:

- The page you were on and the company or record involved.
- What you expected, and what happened.
- When it happened, and whether it happens every time.
- A screenshot, including any error message.
- For integration problems, the provider and the record in that system.

## Requests outside this scope

We will tell you promptly when a request is outside this scope, and point you to the guide or the vendor that can help. We will not leave a request unanswered.

Now and then we may help with something outside this scope as a courtesy. That does not change the scope for the next request.
