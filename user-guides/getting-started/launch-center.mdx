Launch Center walks you through setting up MSPortal for your team, one step at a time. It checks the connection and data steps against your real configuration as you go. This page is for MSP staff who set up the organization's portal. It is not the onboarding task list for a company user. For client onboarding, use the [client onboarding guides](/user-guides/client-onboarding/index).

## Before you start

- You need Launch Center read access (**read_launch_center**), Launch Center manage access (**manage_launch_center**), or the matching tenant settings access to open it. The primary administrator can always open it. Company users cannot, and anyone without access is sent back to Home.
- You need manage access (**manage_launch_center** or tenant settings management) to change anything. With read access only, you can look at every step but you cannot save, confirm, or defer one.
- Support staff who impersonate your organization can look at Launch Center but cannot change it.
- Individual steps can need their own settings permissions, such as Standards, ticket settings, branding, or users. The step tells you when one is missing.
- Connecting integrations, importing users, inviting staff, and changing branding or billing are real changes. Launch Center is not a sandbox.

## Open Launch Center

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the sidebar">
    Click **Launch Center** in the **Planning** section of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or use the Launch pill">
    Click the **Launch** pill in the top bar. An amber dot on the pill means a required step needs attention. New organizations land in Launch Center after signup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Launch Center is hidden from the sidebar and top bar once the launch is complete, once someone dismisses it, and for organizations that finished the previous onboarding process.

## Start setup

On a new organization, Launch Center opens on **Set up MSPortal for your team**. It lists the five parts of setup, each with its step count.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the five parts">
    The parts are **Connect your tools**, **Set your standards**, **Plan the roadmap**, **Make the portal yours**, and **Finish**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Start setup">
    You become the launch lead and the first step opens. You can hand the lead role to someone else at any time. If you only have read access, the screen says **Ask an administrator with Launch Center access to start setup.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

Launch Center no longer asks you to build a plan from a questionnaire. Everyone gets the same steps, in the same order, and you defer the optional ones you do not need. You can leave at any time and Launch Center reopens on the step you last visited.

## What the plan contains

| Phase | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect** | **Connect your PSA**, **Connect your RMM** (optional), **Match your clients**, **Set up tickets**, **Connect security tools** (optional), **Connect other tools** (optional), **Continue with MCP** (optional) |
| **Standards** | **Find your standards**, **Plans**, **Add-ons** (optional), **Billing** (optional), **Plan contents**, **Detection**, **Company types** (optional), **Review standards** |
| **Roadmap** | **Plan the roadmap** (optional) |
| **Portal setup** | **Organization and support**, **Brand your portal**, **Staff and access**, **Portal checklist** (all optional) |
| **Finish** | **Finish launch** |

Launch Center has 21 steps in total. Nine of them are required to finish: **Connect your PSA**, **Match your clients**, **Set up tickets**, **Find your standards**, **Plans**, **Plan contents**, **Detection**, **Review standards**, and **Finish launch**. Every other step is optional and never blocks the launch.

Some steps open only after others are settled. A waiting step shows **Opens after** and the step names, and you can still open it to read it.

- **Match your clients** and **Set up tickets** open after **Connect your PSA**.
- **Plans** and **Add-ons** open after **Find your standards**.
- **Billing**, **Plan contents** and **Company types** open after **Plans**.
- **Detection** opens after **Find your standards**.
- **Review standards** opens after **Plan contents** and **Detection**.
- **Plan the roadmap** opens after **Review standards**.
- **Finish launch** opens its button after every required step is done or skipped.

## Use the Launch steps rail

The **Launch steps** rail on the left lists every phase and step.

- A status icon shows whether a step is done, skipped, needs attention, is waiting, or is the current step. Optional steps show **Optional**. A step you deferred shows **Later**, and a step that does not apply shows **Not needed**.
- Click a step to open it. The step you are on is highlighted.
- Some steps list their tasks under them, for example the setup tasks of the PSA you chose. Click the arrow at the right of the step to show or hide its tasks.
- Click the collapse button at the top of the rail (**Collapse launch steps**) to shrink it to a strip of status icons. Click **Expand launch steps** to bring it back.
- On a phone or tablet, the rail becomes a bar above the step. It shows how many required steps are done, for example **3 of 9 required steps**, and the current step. Tap it to open **Launch steps** in a bottom sheet.

## Work through a step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a step">
    Pick a step in the rail, or keep clicking **Continue**. Each step panel shows the step title, a short description, and a status badge (**To do**, **Done**, **Skipped**, **Needs attention**, or **Waiting**). The footer shows your place, for example **Step 4 of 21**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the setup work">
    Most steps embed the real setting, so you change it in place. The sections below cover each phase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Continue">
    **Continue** first saves what the step saves, then moves to the next step that still needs work. If saving fails, you see a message and stay on the step. On a step you confirm yourself, the button reads **Confirm and continue** and marks the step done. On **Plan the roadmap** it reads **Mark done**. Inside a connection, it walks through the connection's tasks (**Continue to** the next task, then **Finish connection setup**) before it moves to the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Defer an optional step">
    On an optional step, click **Do this later**. The step shows **Later** in the rail and Launch Center moves on. **Do this later** records the deferral only. It does not save edits you have not saved. Click **Resume** on a deferred step to bring it back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back">
    Click **Back** to return to the previous step that is not waiting on another step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Connection and data steps finish on their own once the app can see the result, for example a healthy PSA connection or an active client company. Steps the app cannot detect, such as reviewing your standards or your portal branding, finish when you confirm them.

Without manage access, **Continue** only moves you on. It does not save or confirm anything, and **Do this later** is hidden.

## Connect your tools

### Connect your PSA and RMM

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your tool">
    Pick your PSA (or, on **Connect your RMM**, your RMM) from the provider cards. A card shows **Connected** when your organization already has that integration. On the RMM step you can choose **No RMM** (**We do not use one**), and Launch Center then marks the step **Not needed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    For tools that set up inside Launch Center, the setup tasks appear under the step in the rail and you work through them in the panel. For other tools, the panel shows **Set up** followed by the tool name and **in Settings**. Click **Open setup in Settings**, finish there, and you return to Launch Center. A tool that is already connected and syncing says so instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the connection">
    On the last task, click **Finish connection setup**. The step turns done once the connection is healthy and its required tasks are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To switch tools later, click **Change** (**Change PSA** or **Change RMM**) beside the chosen tool, or **Keep current choice** to cancel.

### Match your clients

**Match your clients** shows three counts. **Active companies** is how many clients MSPortal holds. **Company types found** is how many company types MSPortal sees in your PSA, and **Unmatched PSA companies** is how many PSA companies are not matched yet. When your PSA sets up inside Launch Center, the company matching is embedded in the panel. Otherwise, click **Open Companies** to match, import, or tidy up companies in Settings. If you have no companies yet, the panel says **No companies yet** and offers the same link.

### Set up tickets

**Set up tickets** has three tabs.

- **Statuses**: choose which PSA statuses close and resolve a ticket.
- **Delivery**: choose how new tickets reach you.
- **Display groups**: choose which tickets clients can see. This tab asks you to choose your PSA on the **Delivery** tab first.

Delivery and display groups need ticket settings access. Without it, the panel offers **Open ticket settings**.

### Connect security tools and other tools

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose every tool you use">
    Tick the cards for the tools you want connected. **Connect other tools** groups them as **Productivity**, **Documentation**, **Distribution and quoting**, **Backup**, **Infrastructure**, and **Migration from other tools**. Your choice saves as you tick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set each one up">
    A ticked card shows its status (**Ready**, **Needs attention**, **Setting up**, or **Not connected**). Click **Open** on a card to set that tool up. Click **All tools** to return to the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Click **Continue** when you are done. If you chose no tools, **Confirm and continue** marks the step done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Continue with MCP

After connecting your integrations, open **Continue with MCP**. This optional step gives your AI assistant the connection instructions and a setup brief.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your assistant">
    Under **1. Connect your assistant**, choose your assistant to open its setup dialog. If you prefer, skip ahead and copy the script.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the setup script">
    Under **2. Let your agent connect and help you set up**, click **Copy setup script** and paste the script into your agent. The script includes your portal's server address, preserves other MCP connections, and explains browser sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and approve access">
    Sign in to MSPortal and approve the areas the assistant needs. Ask it to verify the connected user and tenant before changing anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your preferences">
    Review each proposed change before it is applied, then let the agent verify the saved result and identify any remaining steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The setup brief covers your tenant details, branding, service plans, standards, client access, ticket templates and meetings. What your agent can do depends on your permissions, the access you approve, and the tools your server exposes, so have your agent inspect those first. Copying the script does not grant access or apply changes. You need MCP connection access to use the assistant buttons and the script. **Manage MCP connections** opens the connection settings in a new tab.

Provider sign-in, payment authorization, DNS changes and visual review may still need you in the browser. See [MCP Server](/user-guides/mcp-server/index) for client-specific connection steps and access management.

## Set your standards

The **Standards** phase defines what every client should have and how MSPortal checks it. Each step uses the guided Standards setup, so the controls match the Standards settings.

| Step | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Find your standards** | The tools and services every client should have. |
| **Plans** | Whether you sell clients a plan or tier. |
| **Add-ons** | Packages clients can add to their plan. |
| **Billing** | Which PSA products show a client is on each plan. Launch Center skips it when there is no billing catalog or no plan. |
| **Plan contents** | Which standards belong to which plan. |
| **Detection** | How MSPortal knows a client has each standard. |
| **Company types** | Which plan each PSA company type is on. Launch Center skips it when you do not use plans. |
| **Review standards** | What each client should have against what it has. |

Editing standards needs the **Standards settings** permission on top of Launch Center manage access. Without it, the footer says **Editing standards needs the Standards settings permission** and **Continue** stays disabled. See [Standards](/user-guides/msp-tools/standards) and [Manage the Standards catalog](/user-guides/settings/standards) for the detail behind each step.

## Plan the roadmap

**Plan the roadmap** brings roadmap items in and adds the standards each client is missing. It is optional.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask the roadmap assistant">
    Use a suggested prompt under **Try asking**, such as **Check what's already on the roadmap**, **Import PSA opportunities**, **Add incomplete standards to the roadmap**, or **How does the sync work?**. Nothing changes until you apply a proposal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review each proposal">
    Proposals appear under **Proposals to review**. Click **Apply** to accept one or **Dismiss** to drop it. Importing opportunities needs the **Manage planner** permission. Adding incomplete standards also needs **Manage Standards settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Mark done">
    Applying any proposal completes the step. You can also click **Mark done** without applying one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The standards sync creates one roadmap item for each client and standard. It runs for every client, not only the ones selected in the company selector, and it is safe to run again. If AI is turned off for your portal, the assistant is replaced by **Import PSA opportunities** and **Add incomplete standards** buttons that do the same work.

## Set up your portal

The **Portal setup** phase has four optional steps. The first three edit your settings in place. The fourth is a checklist that links to the larger settings areas.

### Organization and support

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your organization">
    Under **Organization**, check **Organization name**, **Primary administrator**, and **Timezone**. You pick the primary administrator from your active staff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set what clients see">
    Under **Client-facing details**, fill in **Portal name**, **Support email**, **Support phone**, and **Website**. Clients see the portal name when they sign in and in emails they receive, and they see the support email when they need help. Enter the website as a full web address, such as `https://example.com`. Clear a field to remove its stored value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Continue">
    **Continue** saves your changes, then confirms the step. A short **Saved** note appears. If a field is not valid, the form shows what to fix and you stay on the step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **More organization settings (address, locale, currency)** to open the rest of your organization settings in a new tab. Without manage access and tenant settings write access, the step shows the saved values read-only and tells you to ask a teammate with access.

### Brand your portal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    The step embeds the branding editor. Changes save as you make them, and clients see them on the portal and in reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through the tabs">
    Set your **Colors**, upload your logos on **Light Mode** and **Dark Mode**, add a **Favicon**, set the **Reports & Certs** logo, and customize the **Sign-in Page**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the step">
    Click **Confirm and continue**. Nothing else needs saving because the editor has already saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The custom domain is not part of the embedded editor. Click **Custom domain and sign-in options** to open the branding settings in a new tab. **Setup guide** opens the matching guide. If branding cannot load, the step says so and still offers those links. See [Configure portal branding](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/branding) for the detail.

### Staff and access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your staff">
    The **MSP staff** list shows each person's email, role, and a status of **Active**, **Invited**, or **Inactive**. It shows the first 50 people and then **and N more**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a teammate">
    Under **Invite a teammate**, enter **Email** and **Full name**, choose a **Role**, and click **Send invitation**. The person receives an email invitation to sign in. If no staff role exists yet, the form says to create one in Settings first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage users and roles">
    Click **Open** on **Manage all users** to edit, disable, or remove staff and client users. Click **Open** on **Review roles and permissions** to check what each role can do. Both open in a new tab. **Users and roles guide** opens the matching guide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the step">
    Click **Confirm and continue**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Inviting needs user management access. With read access only, you can see the list but not invite. Launch Center tells you when an email already belongs to someone on your team, or when you sent too many invitations and need to wait a few minutes.

### Portal checklist

**Portal checklist** is one page that lists the remaining settings for clients. Each row has a tick box, a short description, a status line, a **Guide** link, and an **Open** link that opens the settings page in a new tab. The header shows your progress, for example **4 of 9 done**.

| Item | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Review your subscription** | Confirm the subscription, payment details and billing contact for your MSP. |
| **Import and invite client users** | Check each user's company and role before sending invitations. |
| **Import client devices** | Open your connected RMM or device provider and import devices into their matched companies. |
| **Assign client team roles** | Open a company's actions and choose **Assign Teams** to assign MSP staff to its team roles. |
| **Ticket request templates** | Choose common request types, set the questions clients answer, and enable them for the right companies. |
| **Client training** | Review the course library and assign training to the right people or groups. |
| **Compliance playbooks** | Select the frameworks or best-practice playbooks your clients need. |
| **Schedule a client review** | Choose a client and create a QBR or review meeting to discuss standards and roadmap priorities. |
| **Review a client's portal access** | Select a client user, review their company and role, and use **Impersonate** if your role allows it. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an item">
    Click **Open** on a row, do the work in the new tab, and come back. Click **Guide** for a step-by-step guide. If you cannot open a page, the row says **Ask a teammate with access to these settings to complete this task.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the status line">
    Where the app can detect the work, the row shows a live count and **Detected**, for example **2 active subscriptions** or **No devices imported yet**. A detected item ticks itself and you cannot untick it. The app counts the subscription, client users, devices, team roles, ticket request templates, courses, playbooks and upcoming client meetings. It shows a count only for pages you are allowed to open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick what you finished">
    For an item the app cannot detect, tick the box when you finish it. The tick saves at once, and the row then says **Marked done by you**. **Review a client's portal access** has no count, so you always tick it yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Confirm and continue">
    **Confirm and continue** confirms the whole step. You do not need every box ticked. Click **Do this later** if the step does not apply yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check your progress

- The header shows **3 of 9 steps**, which counts the required steps that are done or skipped.
- The header shows **Complete** once the launch is finished, and **Needs attention** when a required step that used to pass now fails.
- The rail shows each step's status. Open any step marked **Needs attention** and fix the connection or setting behind it.
- The **Finish launch** step lists every required step that is still open, with the reason it is waiting.

Launch Center no longer has a separate **Live verification** list or an **Edit launch plan** button. The steps and their statuses are the readiness check.

## Review the client experience

The separate **Review the client experience** step with its **Inspect** preview is gone. Review the client side from the **Portal checklist** instead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    In **Portal checklist**, find **Review a client's portal access** and click **Open**. It opens the client users list in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a client user">
    Select a client user and review their company and role. Use **Impersonate** if your role allows it, to see the portal as that person sees it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exit impersonation">
    Finish the client check and exit impersonation before you return to Launch Center. Launch Center is read-only while you impersonate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the item">
    Back in Launch Center, tick **Review a client's portal access**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Complete the launch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Finish launch">
    Open **Finish launch** in the **Finish** phase. It shows how many required steps are left, for example **2 required steps are left**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the open steps">
    Click **Open** beside each listed step and finish it. A step that is waiting on another step has no **Open** button until the other step is settled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Finish launch">
    When the panel reads **Ready to launch**, click **Finish launch** in the footer. It is available only to people with manage access, and only when every required step is done or skipped.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you finish the launch:

- The **Launch** pill and sidebar entry disappear.
- Launch Center shows **Your portal is launched** with the date, four figures (**Required steps done**, **Connections ready**, **Standards**, **Roadmap items**), and the five parts of setup.
- Launch Center rechecks the required steps every night (04:20 UTC). If one fails later, for example an integration disconnects, the status becomes **Needs attention**. The **Launch** pill returns with an amber dot, Launch Center shows the failing step, and the launch lead receives a notification titled **Your portal launch needs attention**. It returns to **Complete** once the required steps pass again.

Completing the launch does not send client invitations.

To change an answer after launching, click **Reopen setup** (managers only). Your progress is kept.

## Change the launch lead

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the control">
    In the Launch Center header, find **Launch lead**. It shows the current lead, or **No lead yet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a person">
    Click the control and pick a person. You can search by name or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the result">
    Wait for the save, then check that the new name shows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only the current lead, the primary administrator, or the tenant owner can reassign, and not while impersonating. For everyone else, the header shows the lead's name as text. This hands over launch responsibility, not ownership of the tenant.

## Dismiss Launch Center for everyone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the confirmation">
    Click **Dismiss** in the Launch Center header. This is for managers only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Read the message, then click **Discard** to remove Launch Center. Click **Keep it** to cancel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This removes Launch Center from the sidebar and top bar for everyone in your organization. It does not delete your setup progress or disconnect any integration. To bring it back, open the Launch Center page again from a saved link and click **Restore Launch Center**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Launch Center is missing | Check your role. Then check that the launch was not already completed, dismissed, or replaced by the previous onboarding process. Company users do not see it. |
| Launch Center sends you to Home | You do not have Launch Center or tenant settings access. Ask an administrator. |
| The steps are read-only | You have read access only. Ask an administrator for **manage_launch_center** or tenant settings management. |
| **Continue** shows a save error | Stay on the step, check what saved, fix the fields (for example an invalid email address), and try again. |
| "Someone else changed the launch at the same time" | Another person updated Launch Center. Try again, and compare before you reapply your edits. |
| **Finish launch** is disabled | Open the **Finish launch** step and finish the required steps it lists. You also need manage access. |
| A step shows **Opens after** | Settle the steps it names first. You can still open and read the step. |
| **Continue** is disabled on a Standards step | Editing standards needs the **Standards settings** permission. Ask an administrator to grant it. |
| A step shows done without a click | Connection and data steps finish from live evidence once the app can see the result. |
| A finished step needs attention again | Check the connection or setting behind it. A later change can break an earlier pass. |
| A banner says **Finished in the previous Launch Center** | Your setup finished under the older checks. Click **Reopen setup** to restart it under the new checks. Your first change here does the same. |
| A checklist item will not untick | The app detected the work, so the item stays ticked. |
| A row says **Ask a teammate with access to these settings to complete this task** | Your role cannot open that settings page. Ask someone who can. |
| **Send invitation** fails | The email may already belong to your team, or you sent too many invitations. Wait a few minutes and try again. |
| The **Launch** pill is gone | The launch was completed or Launch Center was dismissed. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Launch Center the client onboarding portal?">
    No. It is an MSP setup workflow. Client onboarding has separate portals, tasks, and access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to set up the roadmap or portal steps before launching?">
    No. Only the nine required steps block the launch. Use **Do this later** on any optional step and return when you are ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Do this later save my edits?">
    No. It records the deferral and moves on. Use **Continue** to save a form first. Settings that save as you go, such as the branding editor, stay saved either way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a passing step prove every client can use the portal?">
    No. It proves the checked evidence is in place. Review a real client user in the portal checklist before you invite clients.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will completing launch email or invite my clients?">
    No. Invitations and client emails are separate actions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does removing Launch Center disconnect my integrations?">
    No. It only hides Launch Center. Saved configuration stays in place.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can someone with read access change anything?">
    No. Changing a step needs manage access, and each step can need more permissions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
