Launch Center builds a setup plan for your portal and checks it against your real configuration as you go. This page is for MSP staff who set up the organization's portal. It is not the onboarding task list for a company user. For client onboarding, use the [client onboarding guides](/user-guides/client-onboarding/index).

## Before you start

- You need Launch Center read access (**read_launch_center**), Launch Center manage access (**manage_launch_center**), or the matching tenant settings access to open it. The primary administrator can always open it. Company users cannot.
- You need manage access to change the plan. With read access only, the plan is read-only.
- Individual setup screens inside the plan can need their own settings permissions, such as integrations or branding.
- Connecting integrations, importing users, and changing branding or billing are real changes. The launch plan is not a sandbox.
- Know where your client data comes from (PSA integration, Microsoft 365, or CSV import).

## Open Launch Center

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the sidebar">
    Click **Launch Center** in the **Planning** section of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or use the Launch pill">
    Click the **Launch** pill in the top bar. It shows how many readiness checks have passed. New organizations land in Launch Center after signup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Launch Center is hidden once the launch is complete, once someone dismisses it, and for organizations that finished the previous onboarding process.

## Build your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a path">
    On the welcome screen, click **Build my launch plan** to answer a short questionnaire. Or click **Explore first** to skip it and get a lightweight plan. You can build a full plan later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose focus areas">
    On **What do you want to launch?**, select one or more of **Client ticketing**, **Self-service portal**, **Training**, **vCIO planning**, **Compliance**, and **Client data foundation**. **Complete portal rollout** includes every area and replaces individual choices. **Explore the platform** keeps the plan light.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the context questions">
    Under **A little context**, answer **Where will your client data come from?** (**PSA integration**, **Microsoft 365**, or **CSV import**) and **How do you want to roll out?** (**Start with one pilot client** or **Prepare all clients**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the plan">
    Click **Build my plan**. The plan shows the steps for your choices. Tracks you did not choose stay under **Explore later**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change your answers later, click **Edit launch plan** (managers only).

## What the plan contains

| Phase | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| **Foundation** | Company profile, Branding, Support experience, Access and permissions, Billing |
| **Connect** | Integrations, Connect your PSA, Connect your RMM, Connect Microsoft 365, and one step for each other integration you pick |
| **Client data** | Client companies, Client users, Client devices, Client teams |
| **Ticketing** | Ticket delivery and defaults, Ticket visibility, Ticket request template |
| **Training** | Training foundation |
| **vCIO** | vCIO foundation (define standards, review adoption, build the roadmap, schedule the QBR) |
| **Compliance** | Import a playbook, review imported checks, create checks with AI |
| **Review and launch** | Readiness review, Review the client experience, Complete launch |
| **Explore later** | Optional steps from tracks you did not choose |

Which steps are required depends on your answers. Branding and Billing are optional. Review the client experience is optional too.

## Work through a step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a step">
    Select a step in **Setup guide**. It explains why the step matters, what it affects, and what to confirm. Use **Collapse setup guide** and **Expand setup guide** to make room.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the setup work">
    Many steps embed the real setting. **Company profile** edits your company name, timezone, city, and country. **Support experience** edits your **Portal name** and **Support email**. **Integrations** lets you choose your PSA, your primary RMM, and other tools, then adds a connect step for each. **Ticket visibility** manages display groups and allowed statuses for your primary PSA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save in the screen's own control">
    Use the screen's own button, such as **Save changes** or **Save integrations**, and wait for the confirmation. Some controls save immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Next">
    Click **Next** to save and move on. If saving fails, you see a message and stay on the step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Defer an optional step">
    On a step that is not required, click **Do this later**. It saves the screen and moves to the next required step. It does not undo anything you already saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **Back** to return to the previous step. If you left unsaved changes in a workspace, you are asked to confirm before you discard them. Some steps need a permission beyond Launch Center access. In that case the step explains which permission is missing.

## Check readiness

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Live verification">
    Click **Live verification** in the plan footer. It shows how many checks pass, for example 3/12.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read each finding">
    Each finding has a severity of **Blocking**, **Recommended**, or **Optional**. Findings are grouped by the setup step that fixes them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix and recheck">
    Go to the step named in the finding, fix it, and look at **Live verification** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Checks cover facts such as a support email, a portal logo, brand colors, a permission group beyond Administrator, a healthy PSA, RMM and Microsoft 365 connection, an active client company, mapped client users, an active client request template, and an upcoming QBR. Integration and data steps complete on their own once their checks pass.

A later change can make a passing check fail. Check the time on the finding before you invite clients.

## Review the client experience

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the step">
    In **Review and launch**, select **Review the client experience**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a company and access group">
    Pick a **Client company** and an **Access group**, then click **Inspect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the preview">
    The preview shows the permissions that access group grants and the checks for that client. It is read-only, and it sends no invitations or tickets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This preview does not sign you in as a client. To test the real client experience, use your approved client-review or impersonation process.

## Complete the launch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear blocking findings">
    Open **Live verification** and fix every **Blocking** finding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete launch">
    Open the **Complete launch** step and click **Complete launch**. It is available only when all blocking checks pass.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you complete the launch:

- The **Launch** pill and sidebar entry disappear.
- The Launch Center page shows **Your portal setup is complete**.
- Readiness is rechecked every night (04:20 UTC). If a blocking check later fails, for example an integration disconnects, Launch Center shows **Needs attention** and an alert, until you fix it.

Completing the launch does not send client invitations.

## Assign a launch coordinator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the control">
    In Launch Center, find **Launch coordinator**. It shows the **Current launch coordinator**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a person">
    Click **Assign launch coordinator** (or **Reassign**) and pick a person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the result">
    Wait for the save, then check that the new name shows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only the current coordinator, the primary administrator, or the tenant owner can reassign. This hands over launch responsibility, not ownership of the tenant.

## Remove Launch Center for everyone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Click **Dismiss Launch Center**. This is for managers only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Read the message, then click **Remove for everyone**. Click **Keep Launch Center** to cancel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This removes Launch Center from the sidebar and top bar for everyone in your organization. It does not delete your setup progress or disconnect any integration.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Launch Center is missing | Check your role. Then check that the launch was not already completed, dismissed, or replaced by the previous onboarding process. Company users do not see it. |
| The plan is read-only | You have read access only. Ask an administrator for **manage_launch_center** or tenant settings management. |
| **Next** shows a save error | Stay on the step, check what saved, fix the fields, and try again. |
| "Setup changed in another session" | Another person updated the plan. Reload and compare before you reapply your edits. |
| **Complete launch** is disabled | Open **Live verification** and fix the blocking findings. |
| A step shows complete without a click | Integration and data steps complete from live evidence once their checks pass. |
| A completed step needs attention again | Check the connection or setting behind it. A later change can break an earlier pass. |
| The **Launch** pill is gone | The launch was completed or Launch Center was dismissed. |
| A step says another permission is needed | Launch Center access does not include that module. Ask an administrator to grant the listed permission. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Launch Center the client onboarding portal?">
    No. It is an MSP setup workflow. Client onboarding has separate portals, tasks, and access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to set up vCIO or training before launching?">
    No. Those tracks are optional. Use **Do this later** and return when you are ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Do this later discard my edits?">
    No. It saves the workspace first, then records the deferral.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a readiness pass prove every client can use the portal?">
    No. It proves the checked evidence meets the rule. Test the real client role and workflow before rollout.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will completing launch email or invite my clients?">
    No. Invitations and client emails are separate actions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does removing Launch Center disconnect my integrations?">
    No. It only hides Launch Center. Saved configuration stays in place.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can someone with read access change the plan?">
    No. Changing the plan needs manage access, and each setup screen can need more permissions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
