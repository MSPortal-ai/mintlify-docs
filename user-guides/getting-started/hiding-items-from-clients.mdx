title: "Control what Company users can see"
description: "Hide Planner items, goals, compliance checks, custom fields, dashboards, and onboarding items from company users."

MSPortal separates your MSP team from your client contacts (company users). Most records are visible to both. Use the controls on this page to keep internal records away from company users. This page is for MSP staff who can edit the record or setting.

## Before you start

- You need edit access to the record or setting you are changing.
- Company users never see these controls. Only MSP staff do.
- New records are visible to company users by default. Hiding is always an explicit choice.
- Hiding a record does not change what a company user's role allows. Roles control modules and actions. Visibility controls individual records.
- Hiding a sidebar item only changes navigation. See [Customize the sidebar](/user-guides/getting-started/customizing-the-sidebar).

## Choose the right control

| What you want to hide | Control |
| --- | --- |
| A Planner item, goal, or compliance check | **Hide from company users** in its edit dialog |
| A custom field | **Hide from company users** on the field definition |
| A dashboard | **Visibility** set to **Internal (MSP Team)** |
| An onboarding template task | **Visibility** switch set to **Internal** |
| A client onboarding documentation page | **Visible to client portal** switch |

## Hide a Planner item, goal, or compliance check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the record">
    Open the Planner item, goal, or compliance check in its edit dialog. Confirm the company and that you can edit it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Hide from company users">
    Check **Hide from company users**. It sits in the dialog footer, next to the save controls. If the checkbox is missing or disabled, the view is read-only or historical.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the dialog's save button. Checking the box only stages the change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it saved">
    Reopen the record and check that **Hide from company users** is still on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Hidden records disappear from every list, count, widget, and export that company users can reach. MSP staff still see them. To show the record again, clear **Hide from company users** and save.

## Hide a custom field

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the field">
    Go to **Settings > Customization > Fields** and edit the field definition. See [Custom fields](/user-guides/settings/custom-fields).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Hide from company users">
    Check **Hide from company users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the field">
    Save the definition.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Company users still see the company, device, or other record that owns the field. They do not see the field or its value.

## Hide a dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Go to **Settings > Dashboards** and create or edit the dashboard. See [Dashboard settings](/user-guides/dashboards/manage-dashboard-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Visibility">
    In **Visibility**, choose **Internal (MSP Team)** to hide it from company users. **Both** shows it to MSP staff and company users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Hide onboarding items from clients

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template">
    Go to **Settings > Client Onboarding** and open the onboarding template. This page appears only when client onboarding is enabled for your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the task visibility">
    On a task, use the **Visibility** switch. **Client** shows it in the client portal. **Internal** keeps it with your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a documentation page visibility">
    For documentation pages, use the **Visible to client portal** switch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A template sets the starting values for portals launched from it. Existing portals keep their own settings, so open the active portal to change a task that is already live. Hidden tasks show an internal-only marker in the portal.

## Before you share

A visibility change cannot recall a file someone already downloaded or information already sent outside the portal. After you hide something, check it from a company user's point of view. Use your approved client-review or impersonation process, because an MSP view has different access.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I do not see **Hide from company users** | You may lack edit access, or the view is read-only or historical. |
| A company user still sees the record | Confirm the change saved by reopening the record. Check for another copy or a shared export. Send support the page and the user if it persists. |
| Hiding a custom field did not hide the company | Field visibility is separate from the parent record. The parent stays visible. |
| A template change did not update a live portal | Templates only set the starting values. Change the active portal's task directly. |
| A hidden sidebar item still opens | Sidebar settings only change navigation. Use roles and record visibility for access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding a sidebar module make its records private?">
    No. Sidebar preferences change navigation. Use role permissions and record visibility.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the checkbox saved immediately?">
    No. In the Planner, goal, and compliance dialogs it is part of the form. Save the record, then reopen it to check.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding a custom field hide the whole record?">
    No. Only the field and its value are hidden.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do MSP staff still see hidden items?">
    Yes. Hidden items appear normally to your MSP team.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
