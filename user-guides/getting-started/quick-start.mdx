**Audience:** MSP staff. Company contacts should use their organization’s invitation and Company guides for the access their MSP provides.

## Sign in and choose your scope

Use the portal URL and sign-in method your administrator supplies. Complete the invitation or account setup instructions when required; available password, Microsoft and Google sign-in methods depend on the organization’s configuration.

Use the company selector to choose the client or portfolio view you intend to work with. Then inspect the page, selected record and any scope selector inside the editor. Some settings apply to the entire organization; other pages and dialogs have their own company or connection choice. Changing the top-bar company is not proof that every subsequent action is company-specific.

## Find your way around

- **Sidebar:** open the module you need. Its contents depend on permissions, enabled features, connected providers and navigation preferences.
- **Search:** use the top-bar search control or **Ctrl+K** on Windows/Linux or **Option+K** on Mac outside editable fields, then choose a result you can access.
- **Settings:** open administration and personal preferences. Check whether you are changing your account, a company or the organization.
- **Help:** use the current page’s Help entry where available to find its workflow guide.

A missing module does not identify one universal cause. Check the role, provider availability and sidebar settings before creating another connection.

On desktop, open the sidebar and select **Keep sidebar expanded** beside the logo to keep navigation visible. Select **Collapse sidebar** to return to the icon rail. See [Customize the sidebar](/user-guides/getting-started/customizing-the-sidebar#keep-the-desktop-sidebar-expanded) for the branch preview and saved preference behavior.

## Prepare your first rollout

1. Open [Launch Center](/user-guides/getting-started/launch-center) and choose the setup goals and rollout companies that match your plan.
2. Review organization details, access and branding in the [MSP Setup Playbook](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/index).
3. Connect the intended provider instances, review imported records and save company mappings. A successful connection test is separate from saving and synchronization.
4. Select a pilot company and inspect the records that actually loaded. Resolve missing mappings or stale sync results before inviting the wider audience.
5. Review client permissions, visibility and the selected rollout companies’ readiness. A completed setup task is not proof that every client can access the intended feature.

## Save deliberately

Follow the current form’s Save or apply action. Some actions save immediately, including certain row changes, uploads and creation controls. **Reset**, **Cancel**, and closing an editor do not have identical meanings everywhere. Read the task guide before assuming an already saved action will be undone.

Keyboard shortcuts are screen-specific. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/user-guides/getting-started/keyboard-shortcuts) for supported controls rather than assuming Ctrl+S or Ctrl+Enter saves every form.

## Start a client workflow

Use Surveys and Goals to capture priorities, Planner to organize work, Budgets to review planned spend, and Calendar or Reports to prepare the client discussion. Open the relevant module guide before importing, applying templates or using provider actions; those operations have different save boundaries.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is the onboarding wizard?">
    Use Launch Center and the MSP Setup Playbook. The current setup experience follows your selected goals and rollout audience; do not wait for the old floating first-sign-in helper.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can a colleague see a module I cannot?">
    Compare role permissions, enabled features, provider availability and sidebar preferences. An active provider connection alone does not grant access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does choosing a company scope every setting to that company?">
    No. Check the page or dialog’s own scope. Organization settings, personal preferences and individual integration instances can have separate scope.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does setup completion prove clients can use the portal?">
    No. Check readiness, saved mappings and the intended Company user’s role and access before expanding the rollout.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
