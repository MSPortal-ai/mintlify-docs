This page gets MSP staff signed in, oriented, and started on a first rollout. Company users should use their organization's invitation and the Company guides for the access their MSP provides.

## Before you start

- You need an invitation from an MSP administrator. You cannot sign in without a portal account.
- Have your portal address ready. Your administrator gives you the portal URL and tells you which sign-in method to use.
- Setup work needs the right role. An administrator can grant access in **Settings > Users & Roles**.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your portal">
    Go to the portal URL your administrator gave you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sign-in method">
    Click **Sign in with Microsoft** or **Sign in with Google**, or enter your email and password and click **Sign in**. Use the method your organization set up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish verification if asked">
    If your account uses multi-factor authentication, enter your code and click **Verify & Sign In**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you were invited by email, open the invitation first and follow its account setup link.

## Find your way around

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar">
    The sidebar on the left holds the modules, grouped as **Planning**, **Operations**, and **Protection**, with **MSP Tools** for MSP staff. **Settings** sits at the bottom. You see only the modules your role, enabled features, and connected integrations allow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dock the sidebar (optional)">
    On desktop, the sidebar opens when you move the pointer into the icon rail. Click **Keep sidebar expanded** beside the logo to keep it open, or **Collapse sidebar** to return to the icon rail. See [Customize the sidebar](/user-guides/getting-started/customizing-the-sidebar#keep-the-desktop-sidebar-expanded).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the top bar">
    The top bar holds the company selector, **Search**, the **Launch** pill, recent pages, the assistant, notifications, **Help**, language, the theme toggle, and **Sign out**. On a phone, click **Toggle menu** for the sidebar and **More** for the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for anything">
    Click **Search** or press **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux, or **Option+K** on Mac, outside any text field. See [Find pages and records with Search](/user-guides/getting-started/universal-search).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a page's guide">
    Click **Help** in the top bar to open the guide for the page you are on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A missing module usually has one of three causes. Your role may not include it, a feature may be off for your organization, or a needed integration is not connected yet. Check these before you add another connection.

## Choose a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company selector">
    Click the company selector at the left of the top bar, or press **Ctrl+J** (**Option+J** on Mac).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a company">
    Choose **Global View** for every company, **Assigned Companies** for the companies assigned to you, or a single company. Type in **Search companies...** to find one. Pages that show client data follow your choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it before you change anything">
    Look at the selector again before you save or import. Some settings apply to your whole organization, and some dialogs have their own company or connection picker.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prepare your first rollout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Launch Center">
    Click **Launch Center** in the **Planning** section, or the **Launch** pill in the top bar. Build your plan from your goals. See [Work through your portal launch plan](/user-guides/getting-started/launch-center).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your organization">
    Review your organization details, access, and branding. See the [MSP Setup Playbook](/user-guides/getting-started/onboarding/index).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your integrations">
    Connect your PSA and RMM, review the imported records, and save the company mappings. A successful connection test is separate from saving and from the first sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a pilot company">
    Select one company and open **Overview** to see what loaded. Fix missing mappings or stale sync results before you widen the rollout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check client access">
    Review client permissions and what is visible to company users. See [Control what Company users can see](/user-guides/getting-started/hiding-items-from-clients). A completed setup step does not prove clients can reach the feature, so test with a company user role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Save your work

Follow each form's own **Save** or apply button. Some actions save at once, such as certain row changes, uploads, and creation controls. **Reset**, **Cancel**, and closing an editor do not mean the same thing everywhere, so read the task guide first. Keyboard shortcuts differ by screen. See [Use keyboard shortcuts](/user-guides/getting-started/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Start a client workflow

Use Surveys and Goals to capture priorities, Planner to organize work, Budgets to review planned spend, and Calendar or Reports to prepare the client discussion. See [How data moves through MSPortal](/user-guides/getting-started/data-flow).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I cannot sign in | Use the sign-in method your administrator chose. If you have no account, ask an administrator to invite you. |
| A module is missing from the sidebar | Check your role, your organization's enabled features, connected integrations, and your sidebar layout in **Settings > User Settings > Sidebar order**. |
| I cannot find Launch Center | It needs Launch Center access. It is also hidden after the launch is complete or dismissed. |
| Pages show another company's data | Check the company selector in the top bar. |
| A colleague can see a module I cannot | Compare roles, enabled features, connected integrations, and sidebar layouts. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is the onboarding wizard?">
    Use Launch Center and the MSP Setup Playbook. They follow the goals you choose.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does choosing a company scope every setting to that company?">
    No. The selector filters company data. Organization settings and personal preferences are not company-specific, and each integration instance has its own scope.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does setup completion prove clients can use the portal?">
    No. Check readiness, saved mappings, and the access of a real company user before you widen the rollout.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
