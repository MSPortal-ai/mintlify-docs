Search opens a page or finds a record from one box. It is for company users and MSP staff. It shows a short set of matches, not a full list, so use a module's own filters when you need every record.

## Before you start

- You need to be signed in on a page that shows the top bar. Public links and embedded pages do not have Search.
- Results depend on your role, the selected company, and your connected integrations. Your MSP manages company access.
- Deep Search notes are for MSP staff only.

## Find a page or record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Search">
    Click **Search** in the top bar. Or press **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux, or **Option+K** on Mac, while your cursor is not in a text field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a specific term">
    Type a page name such as **User Settings**, or a record title or number. Results load as you type. If the whole phrase finds nothing, try a shorter, distinctive word. Ticket search needs at least 3 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the category and destination">
    Results are grouped by category. Read each title and subtitle so you pick the right page or record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the result">
    Click the result, or use the **Up** and **Down** arrow keys and press **Enter**. To keep your current page open, open the result in a new tab from your browser's right-click menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close Search without opening anything">
    Press **Esc**. Opening and closing Search does not save anything on the page underneath. Finish or cancel any open edit before you open a result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Search inside notes with Deep Search

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Search">
    Click **Search** in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Deep Search">
    Click **Deep Search** in the footer of the palette. The search box now reads **Search everything including notes...**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your term">
    Type or retype your term after you switch modes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a note result">
    Notes results show a snippet of the matching text. Click a result to open the ticket, meeting, Planner item, or project that holds the note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it off (optional)">
    Click **Deep Search** again. Your choice is remembered in this browser, so it stays on until you turn it off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deep Search adds indexed ticket, meeting, Planner, and project notes. It is a record search, not an AI request, and it does not search product guides.

## What Search can find

Depending on your access and integrations, results can include these categories.

| Category | Needs |
| --- | --- |
| Navigation | Always available. Jump to a page or settings screen by name. |
| Tickets | Ticket access. |
| Companies | Always available. |
| Devices | Device access. |
| Training | Training access. |
| Compliance | Compliance access. |
| Projects | Project access. |
| Budgets | Budget access. |
| Invoices | Invoice access. |
| Backups | Backup access and the Cove integration. |
| Security | Security access and the Cork integration. |
| Goals | Goal access. |
| Planner | Planner access. |
| Documentation | Documentation access and the Hudu or IT Glue integration. |
| Notes | Deep Search turned on, and an MSP staff account. |

Search normally returns up to five matches per category. The count shows what was returned, not how many records exist. A company selection narrows record searches but does not remove navigation links. A result does not grant extra access, because the page you open can still require permission.

**Documentation** results are records in your portal's Documentation area. For product how-to instructions, use the page's **Help** entry or ask the [Home assistant](/user-guides/ai-assistant/use-home-assistant).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No results | Try a shorter term, check the selected company, and open the module to use its own filters. No results does not prove a record is missing. |
| A category is missing | The category needs a permission and sometimes an integration (see the table above). Ask your MSP to check your role and connected services. |
| Tickets never appear | Type at least 3 characters. |
| Results do not show a new record | Close and reopen Search, retype the term, and check the module directly. |
| A result opens an unavailable page | Check that the record still exists and that your access did not change. Send your MSP the destination and the error. |
| Ctrl+K or Option+K does nothing | Click outside any text field first, or click **Search** in the top bar. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Search show every matching record?">
    No. It returns a limited set per category. Refine the term or use the module's own search and filters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does searching change records?">
    No. Typing a term only reads results. Opening a result navigates to it and does not submit a form you were editing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do I see different results from a colleague?">
    Permissions, company selection, connected services, and available records can differ between users.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Deep Search find product help articles?">
    No. It searches indexed record notes. Use **Help** or the Home assistant for product instructions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to turn on Deep Search every time?">
    No. The preference is remembered in the same browser. Another browser can have a different setting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
