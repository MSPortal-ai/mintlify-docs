**Audience:** Company users and MSP staff. Your role, selected company and connected services affect the results available to you. Your MSP manages Company access.

Search is a quick way to open an available page or find a matching record. It shows a short set of results, not a complete inventory or report.

## Open Search and follow a result

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the palette">
    Select the magnifying-glass **Search** button in the top bar. Outside editable fields, press **Ctrl+K** on Windows/Linux or **Option+K** on Mac.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a specific term">
    Type a page name, such as **User Settings**, or a recognizable record title or number. Pause briefly while results load. Use a shorter, distinctive term if the complete phrase does not match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the category and destination">
    Results are grouped by category. Read the title and subtitle so you choose the intended page or record. Select the result to open it. You can also use the arrow keys and Enter within the result list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close without navigating">
    Press **Escape** to dismiss Search. Opening and closing the palette does not save changes to the page underneath. Finish or cancel an open editor before navigating to a result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A result is a link. Use its browser context menu to open it in another tab when you want to keep the current page. Search is available where the signed-in portal top bar is present; public links and embedded pages can have different controls.

## Understand the results

Depending on access and integrations, result groups can include Navigation, Tickets, Companies, Devices, Training, Compliance, Projects, Budgets, Invoices, Backups, Security, Goals, Planner and Documentation. Deep Search can also add Notes.

The palette normally requests up to **five matches per category**. Matching fields vary by record type. If the record is not shown, refine the term or open that module and use its own filters. The palette's result count is the number returned, not the total number of matching records in your organization.

Company selection narrows applicable record searches. It does not remove general navigation links or necessarily hide shared catalogs. Confirm the company on the record you open. A result does not grant extra access; the destination can still require permission or an available integration.

**Documentation** results refer to records in your portal's Documentation area. For product how-to instructions, use page **Help** or ask the [Home assistant](/user-guides/ai-assistant/use-home-assistant) for a guide and check its citation.

## Use Deep Search for notes

1. Open Search and select **Deep Search** in the footer.
2. Check that the prompt changes to **Search everything including notes...**.
3. Enter or change your search term after switching the mode.
4. Review any Notes results and their matching snippets. Open the result to read its parent record and surrounding context.

Deep Search adds indexed ticket, meeting, Planner and project notes where your access permits. It does not guarantee every note, file attachment or recently changed record is searchable. It is a record-search mode, not an AI request or a search of every public product guide.

The mode is remembered in this browser. Select **Deep Search** again to turn it off. Another browser or a reset of browser storage can have a different preference.

## If you cannot find something

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No results | Try a shorter term, check company selection and open the relevant module. No results alone does not prove the record does not exist. |
| A missing category | Ask your MSP about role permissions and whether the service is connected. |
| Results do not reflect a new record or scope | Close and reopen Search, re-enter the term, and check the module directly. Report persistent mismatches. |
| A result opens an unavailable page | Check whether the record still exists and whether your access changed. Give your MSP the destination and error. |
| The keyboard shortcut conflicts with an editor | Use the top-bar Search button after finishing the edit. |

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Search show every matching record?">
    No. It returns a limited set per category. Refine the term or use the module's own search and filters for a fuller list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does searching change records?">
    Entering a term reads results. Opening a result navigates to that record; it does not submit the form you were editing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do I see different results from a colleague?">
    Your permissions, company selection, connected services and available records can differ. Ask the MSP to review access when needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will Deep Search search product help articles?">
    Deep Search adds indexed record notes. Portal Documentation records and public product guides are different sources. Use Help or ask the Home assistant for cited product instructions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to enable Deep Search every time?">
    No. The preference is remembered in the same browser. You can turn it off using the same footer control.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an empty result prove that a note or ticket is absent?">
    No. Permissions, company scope, supported fields, indexing, result limits or a loading failure can affect the result. Check the parent module before concluding that data is missing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
