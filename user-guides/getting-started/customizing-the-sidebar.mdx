Shape MSPortal's left-hand navigation to match how your team works. You can reorder pages, group them into your own sections, hide modules you do not use, add up to 20 custom links to outside tools, pin dashboards, and add settings shortcuts. This page is for MSP admins who set defaults for everyone. Each person can also arrange their own sidebar (see [Arrange your personal sidebar](/msp-guides/account/arrange-your-sidebar)).

## Before you start

- You need a role with **Navigation** settings permission to open **Settings > Customization > Navigation**. Without it, the page does not appear in the settings menu.
- Sidebar layouts cascade across three levels. A lower level overrides the one above it.

| Level | Where to edit | Who it affects |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| **Tenant default** | **Settings > Customization > Navigation** with **All companies (default)** selected | Every user, unless a company or personal layout overrides it |
| **Company override** | **Settings > Customization > Navigation**, then choose a company in **Applies to** | Users of that company |
| **Personal order** | **Settings > User Settings > Sidebar order** | Only that user |

- Custom links and the **Dashboards** panel appear in the tenant default and company editors. The personal editor covers reordering, custom sections, settings shortcuts, and hiding modules.

## Keep the desktop sidebar expanded

This setting is saved per browser and is available to every user.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar">
    On desktop, move the pointer into the icon rail on the left, or tab into the navigation. The sidebar opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dock it">
    Click **Keep sidebar expanded** beside the logo. The sidebar stays open and the page moves beside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collapse it again">
    Click **Collapse sidebar** beside the logo to return to the icon rail. Moving the pointer back into the rail opens it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your choice is remembered in this browser across page changes and reloads, and shared with other open tabs. If browser storage is unavailable, it lasts until the next full page load. On phones, click **Toggle menu** in the top bar to open the navigation. Docking is a desktop setting.

## Reorder, group and hide pages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Navigation editor">
    Go to **Settings > Customization > Navigation**. The left column mirrors your sidebar, with the built-in sections (Planning, Operations, Protection, and MSP Tools) and every page inside them. Check the **Applies to** selector at the top so you edit the layout you intend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder pages">
    Grab the handle next to a page and drag it up, down, or into a different section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your own section">
    Type a name in **Category name** and click **Add category**. Drag pages into the new section. Click a custom section's title to rename it, and use **Move category up** and **Move category down** to reorder it. **Delete category** returns its pages to their default sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hide a module">
    Drag a page into the **Disabled modules** panel, or click **Remove from navigation** on the page. Hidden modules disappear from the sidebar for the affected users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save order**. Until you save, the editor shows **Not applied until you save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To return to the baseline layout, click **Reset default** (tenant default) or **Reset company override** (company editor). Reset stages the baseline, so click **Save order** to apply it.

<Warning>
Disabling a module only hides it from the sidebar. It does not change what users can access. Manage access with user roles.
</Warning>

## Add custom links

Add your own links to the sidebar, such as documentation sites, vendor portals, or internal tools. Custom links always open in a new tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Navigation editor">
    Go to **Settings > Customization > Navigation**. The **Custom links** panel appears to the right of the sidebar layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a link">
    Enter a link name and a URL, then click **Add link**. Only HTTP and HTTPS URLs are accepted, and you can create up to 20 custom links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag it into the sidebar">
    Drag the new link from the Custom links panel into any sidebar section, including your own custom sections, and position it wherever you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save order** to apply the layout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Hover over a link in the Custom links panel to edit or delete it. Dragging a placed link back into the panel removes it from the sidebar without deleting the link, so you can place it again later.

## Pin a dashboard

Put any dashboard one click away by dragging it into the sidebar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the dashboard">
    In the Navigation editor, use the search box in the **Dashboards** panel to filter the list. The panel shows client-visible shared dashboards, including external dashboard links you have configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag it into a section">
    Drag the dashboard into any sidebar section. A dashboard already placed in the sidebar shows an **In sidebar** badge in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save order** to apply the layout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You can also pin a dashboard from **Settings > Dashboards** using its **Pin to Sidebar** option. Dashboards pinned that way appear in the Planning section by default, and the Navigation editor lets you move them anywhere.
</Tip>

## Add a settings shortcut

Place individual settings pages directly in the sidebar. The **Settings** panel in the Navigation editor lists the settings pages available to your account, such as **Settings: Companies** or **Settings: Integrations**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drag a settings page into a section">
    Drag the page from the **Settings** panel into any sidebar section. A page already placed shows **In sidebar** in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save order** to apply the layout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A shortcut does not grant access. Each user sees a settings shortcut only when they could open that page from the settings menu, so users without the required permission do not see it. To remove a shortcut, drag it back into the **Settings** panel or use **Remove from navigation**, then save. Users can also add settings shortcuts from their personal **Sidebar order**.

## Set a company override

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the company">
    In **Settings > Customization > Navigation**, open the **Applies to** selector and choose a company. Companies without a saved override use **All companies (default)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the layout">
    Reorder, group, hide, and add custom links as described above. Modules disabled at the tenant level appear locked here and cannot be re-enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save order**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the default">
    To edit the tenant default again, click **Edit all companies (default)**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove one company's override, click **Reset company override** and then **Save order** without editing the baseline. The company follows the all-companies default again. If you edit the staged baseline before saving, you create a customized layout instead.

**Reset all company overrides** is a separate action on the all-companies view. Its confirmation deletes every company's saved layout immediately, without waiting for **Save order**, and cannot be undone. Personal layouts still apply on top. Do not use it to reset just one company.

If a company's layout fails to load, retry before you edit it. Do not save over it with a guessed default.

## Set a personal sidebar order

Each user can rearrange their own sidebar without affecting anyone else. The personal editor does not have custom links or a dashboards panel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your preferences">
    Go to **Settings > User Settings** and scroll to **Sidebar order**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange your sidebar">
    Drag items between sections, create custom sections, and move anything you do not use into **Disabled modules**. Custom links and pinned dashboards placed by your administrator can be reordered here too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save order**. Click **Reset my order** to stage a return to your company or tenant default, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Modules your administrator disabled show **Disabled by your administrator** and cannot be re-enabled from the personal editor.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| My changes are not showing up | The editor stages changes. Click **Save order**. Check **Applies to**, because a company or personal layout can override the tenant default. |
| I cannot add more custom links | Each layout supports up to 20 custom links. Delete one you no longer need. |
| A dashboard is missing from the **Dashboards** panel | The panel lists client-visible shared dashboards. Private and internal-only dashboards are not offered. Check the dashboard's sharing and visibility under **Settings > Dashboards**. |
| A module in **Disabled modules** is locked | A higher level (tenant or company) disabled it. Change it at that level. |
| I do not see **Navigation** in Settings | Your role lacks navigation settings permission. Ask an admin to update your role. |
| The sidebar shows a settings shortcut for one user but not another | A shortcut only appears for users who could open that settings page. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are sidebar changes saved as I drag items?">
    No. The editor stages layout and custom-link changes until you click **Save order**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Reset all company overrides another staged reset?">
    No. Confirming it immediately removes all company layout overrides. **Reset default** and **Reset company override** stage a baseline until you click **Save order**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding a sidebar item remove access to its data?">
    No. Sidebar visibility is a navigation preference. Use roles and record visibility controls for access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I put a Settings page in the sidebar?">
    Yes. Use the **Settings** panel in the editor (see Add a settings shortcut above).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
