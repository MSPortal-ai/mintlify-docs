## How to Use These Guides

### Know who each guide is for

These guides are for MSP staff. Company users see a smaller portal that depends on their role and company access, so an MSP screenshot may show controls a company user does not have. Each guide says who it is for.

### Finding Information

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse by Module">
    Select the guide for the module you're working with from the sidebar or cards above
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Search">
    Press `Ctrl+K` (or `Option+K` on Mac) in the portal to jump to a page or record. See [Find pages and records with Search](/user-guides/getting-started/universal-search).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Prerequisites">
    Review any prerequisites at the start of each procedure
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow Along">
    Work through the numbered steps in order. Screenshots sit inside the step they show
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where should a new MSP administrator start?">
    Start with the Platform Quick Start, then open Launch Center and follow the MSP Setup Playbook.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are MSP screenshots the same as the client experience?">
    No. MSP staff can have administration controls and portfolio views unavailable to Company users. Check the audience and permission requirements in each guide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are provider setup instructions?">
    Open the Integration setup directory and choose your provider. Each guide lists what you need and the mapping steps.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
