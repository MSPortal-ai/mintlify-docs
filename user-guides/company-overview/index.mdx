The Company Overview puts a company's key numbers and short lists on one page. It pulls from modules such as Tickets, Devices, Training, Projects, Goals, Reports, and Meetings. Company users see the areas their MSP allows. MSP staff can review one company or a permitted group of companies.

## Open it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Overview">
    Click **Overview** in the **Planning** section of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a company (MSP staff)">
    Use the company selector in the top bar to pick **Global View**, **Assigned Companies**, or one company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow a card or widget">
    Click a summary card or **View All** on a widget to open the full module.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The overview is a starting point. Open the module to see full records, filter in detail, or make a change. A summary never grants extra permissions.

## MSP administration

Company user accounts, permissions, and provider setup are managed by the MSP. See [Change a Company user's permissions](/user-guides/settings/change-company-user-permissions) and [Users & Roles](/user-guides/settings/users-roles).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A widget is missing | Your role may not read that module, an integration may be off, or there is no data. Ask your MSP. |
| A number looks wrong | Open the module behind it and check the scope and filters. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do different users see different widgets?">
    Widgets depend on permissions, company scope, connected integrations, and data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the overview show every customer?">
    No. It shows the scope available to you. Company users do not gain access to other customers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change users or permissions from here?">
    No. Company user administration is an MSP task.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a zero prove there are no issues?">
    No. Check the module when a figure is unexpected. Missing or failed data can make the overview incomplete.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
