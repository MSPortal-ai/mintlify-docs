Use the Company Overview to see a company's tickets, devices, goals, meetings, and more on one page, and to spot what needs attention. It is for company users and MSP staff. What you see depends on your role and company access.

## Before you start

- Every signed-in user can open the overview. Each metric and widget appears only if your role can read that module.
- Widgets also depend on your connected integrations and on whether data exists.
- A company user sees only their own company. MSP staff can choose a company or an aggregate view.

## Open the overview and choose a scope

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Overview">
    Click **Overview** in the **Planning** section of the sidebar. The page title is **Company Overview**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the company selector">
    MSP staff: click the company selector at the left of the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a scope">
    Choose **Global View** for every company you can access, **Assigned Companies** for the companies assigned to you, or one company. Choosing one company opens that company's overview and puts its name in the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the scope before you read the numbers">
    Look at the selector and the page header. Parent-company users can combine a parent and its child companies in one view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a company you expect is missing, ask your MSP to check your company access. Changing the address in your browser does not grant access.

## Read the summary metrics

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the summary cards">
    At the top of the page, the cards are grouped as **Assets**, **Service**, and **People**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read each card">
    | Group | Cards |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Assets** | **Devices**, **Warranty Expired**, **Expiring (90d)** |
    | **Service** | **Open Tickets**, **Upcoming Meetings**, **CSAT** for the recent period, **Active Projects** |
    | **People** | **Training Progress**, **Users**, **Incomplete Goals** |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a card">
    Click a card to open its module. The module opens in your current company scope. It may not apply the card's exact condition, so set the filter you need there. For example, a warranty card opens **Devices**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use the widgets

Below the summary, widgets show a short preview of recent records. A widget's count can be larger than the rows it shows. Click **View All** to open the full module.

| Widget | What it shows and where it goes |
| --- | --- |
| **Open Tickets** | Open tickets. Open one to inspect it, or click **View All**. See [Follow up on a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/follow-up-on-a-ticket). |
| **Device Alerts** | Offline devices and warranty issues. See [Find devices](/user-guides/devices/find-devices). |
| **Recent Compliance** | Recently completed compliance checks. |
| **Planner Items** | Upcoming Planner items. |
| **Active Projects** | Active PSA projects. This widget is hidden when there are none. |
| **Strategic Goals** | Incomplete goals. A goal preview opens Goals, not always that exact goal. |
| **Training Progress** | Completion rate, completed, and overdue. For your own courses, use [My Training](/user-guides/training/take-a-course). |
| **Reports** | Reports for the company. |
| **Upcoming Meetings** | Upcoming meetings, with **Today** and **Tomorrow** labels. |
| **Documentation** | Articles, assets, and procedures. Shown only with a documentation integration. See [Read shared documentation](/user-guides/documentation/find-shared-documents). |
| **Recent CSAT** | Recent satisfaction responses. See [Read CSAT feedback](/user-guides/surveys/read-csat-feedback). |
| **Backup health** | Backup summary when a supported backup provider is connected. |

Widgets appear on their own, so the layout changes when some are missing.

## Read Account Notes (MSP staff)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose one company">
    Select a single company in the company selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Account Notes">
    Find the **Account Notes** widget. It shows recent notes and **QBR log** entries, with a count of open commitments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a note">
    Click **View All** to open the company's notes. A note linked to a meeting opens that meeting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Account Notes are internal staff content. Company users never see this widget.

## Check an unexpected result

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the scope">
    Check the company selector first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the module">
    Open the module behind the widget and check its filters and records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the data source">
    Check that the integration behind the data is connected and syncing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your MSP">
    If it still looks wrong, ask your MSP to check permissions or sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A zero or an empty widget does not prove everything is healthy. Data can be missing or failed to load.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A widget is missing | Your role may not read that module, the integration may not be connected, or there is no data. Ask your MSP. |
| **Active Projects** is missing | It hides itself when the company has no projects. |
| A card opens a list without my condition | Set the filter in the module. |
| Training Progress takes me to My Training | Aggregate training needs more permission, so you see your own training instead. |
| The page shows "Company not found" | The company is outside your access. Pick another company. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does a widget show fewer records than its count?">
    Widgets show a short preview. Click **View All** for the full list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Training Progress my own progress?">
    No. It summarizes the company in view. Use **Training > My Training** for your own courses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit a ticket or goal here?">
    No. The overview holds summaries and shortcuts. Open the module to make changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company administrator read Account Notes?">
    No. Use shared Documentation for client-facing material.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I start a new support request?">
    Use the AI Assistant on Home. See [How to open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
