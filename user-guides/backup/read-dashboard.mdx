## Review the summary

1. Open **Backup > Dashboard**.
2. Confirm the company selection before comparing numbers. When you select several companies, the tiles and cards add their results together.
3. Read the summary tiles across the top.
4. Scroll to the provider cards to see results for each connected backup provider.
5. Select a tile, a status segment, or a bar to open the matching records.

The dashboard summarizes Cove, Acronis, and NinjaOne device backups. It also shows Microsoft 365 backup health when that data is available.

## Read the summary tiles

| Tile | What it shows | Where it opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Protected devices** | Records the providers classify as protected, out of the total device count. The ring shows **Protection coverage** as a percentage. | Devices, filtered to Success |
| **Needs attention** | Records with a warning or a backup still in progress. | Devices, filtered to Warning and InProcess |
| **Failed backups** | Records whose latest result failed or that have no backup. | Devices, filtered to Failed and NoBackup |
| **Unprotected devices** | Records that are not protected, with their share of all devices. | Devices, with the default status filter |
| **Storage used** | Total storage the providers report. | Does not open a list |
| **Active alerts** | Active Acronis alerts. This tile appears only when Acronis is connected. | Alerts |

When Cove or Acronis has recent session history, **Needs attention** and **Failed backups** add a small trend line for the last 7 days. Their descriptions then count sessions instead of devices, for example "3 failed sessions in 7 days". Without session history, the descriptions show a percentage of devices instead.

A session count and a device count answer different questions. One device can fail several sessions in a week, and a device that failed yesterday can succeed today.

## Read a provider card

Each connected device-backup provider gets its own card, titled **Cove overview**, **Acronis overview**, or **NinjaOne overview**. Cards appear in that order.

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Device status** | A bar split into Protected, Warning, Failed, and Unprotected. Select a segment to open Devices filtered to that status and provider. |
| **By workload** | The most common workload types, such as Files and folders, Disk image, Server, or Workstation. Types the portal does not recognize appear as Other workload. |
| **Last backup** | Cove and Acronis only. Devices grouped by their latest backup: Under 24 hours, 1 to 3 days, 3 to 7 days, Over 7 days, or Never. |
| **Backup sessions per day** | Cove and Acronis only. Successful, Warnings, and Failed sessions for each of the last 7 days. |
| **Active alerts by severity** | Acronis only. Active alerts grouped as Critical, Error, Warning, and Information. Select a bar to open Alerts. |
| **Protection coverage** | The protected share for that provider, for example "12 of 14 devices protected", with the provider's reported **Storage**. |

Selecting a **By workload** or **Last backup** bar opens Devices filtered to that provider only. The list does not narrow to the workload type or age group, so review the **Type** and **Last Backup** columns yourself.

Acronis counts can include resources that are not single endpoints. See [Find device backups](/user-guides/backup/find-device-backups) for how to read those rows.

A card only shows the sections the provider has supplied data for in the selected scope. **Device status** and **Protection coverage** always appear. If **By workload**, **Last backup**, **Backup sessions per day**, or **Active alerts by severity** is missing from a card, the provider has not supplied that data. A missing section does not mean backups succeeded or failed.

## Review Microsoft 365 and alerts

Microsoft 365 cards share the grid with the provider cards when Microsoft 365 data is available:

- **Cove Microsoft 365** shows a **Backup status** bar and a **Workloads** breakdown for Exchange, OneDrive, SharePoint, and Teams. The card leaves out **Workloads** when Cove has not supplied workload data. Select a segment or bar to open the Cove Microsoft 365 report.
- Kaseya SaaS Protection and Spanning show their backup health widgets.
- Acronis Microsoft 365 appears as a link card with the provider name and **View Microsoft 365 backups**. Cove uses the same link card when its detailed summary is unavailable.

Open [Microsoft 365](/user-guides/backup/read-microsoft-365-backups) to investigate individual users, sites, or teams. A company with only Microsoft 365 data does not see the device tiles or provider cards.

For Acronis alerts, select **Active alerts** or a severity bar, then follow the [alerts guide](/user-guides/backup/review-alerts).

## Check freshness before relying on a result

A protected status describes the imported result. It does not prove that a backup ran recently or that a restore was tested. Use the **Last backup** groups on the Cove and Acronis cards to spot stale devices, then open a device to inspect its dates. NinjaOne cards do not show backup age.

If the dashboard shows **No Backup Data Yet**, zero totals, or missing cards, confirm company scope and ask the MSP to check mapping and synchronization. A trend or workload section that fails to load is left off the card. Do not treat zero failed backups as evidence that all systems are protected.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does clicking a tile show a different number of devices?">
    The device list applies its own status filters and pagination. Dashboard counts can also cover several selected companies. Check the filters and the company selection, and treat the shortcut as a starting point for investigation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Unprotected devices not show the devices I expected?">
    The tile opens Devices with the default status filter, which excludes Not Configured records. Choose **Not Configured** in the Status filter to review them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the trend period?">
    No. The trend lines and session counts always cover the last 7 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the NinjaOne card have fewer sections?">
    NinjaOne does not supply daily session history or a last-backup time to this dashboard, so its card omits **Last backup** and **Backup sessions per day**. Open a NinjaOne device row for its available dates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Protected mean I can restore successfully?">
    It reports a provider status. Backup freshness, coverage, retention, and restore testing need separate confirmation from your MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I report an old or failed backup?">
    Start with the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). Include the device, provider, status, and date, then confirm the request when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
