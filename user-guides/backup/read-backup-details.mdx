Selecting a row in Backup opens a side panel with that record's detail. What the panel shows depends on the provider. MSP staff and company users with Backup read access can use it, for the records in their company scope.

## Before you start

- You have Backup read access and the record is inside your selected companies.
- Panels are read-only. They never start a backup, change a plan, resolve an alert or restore data.

## Read Acronis activity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row">
    Go to **Backup > Devices**, or choose **Acronis** under **Backup > Microsoft 365**, then select the device, mailbox, drive, site or team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the resource">
    Check the resource name and company at the top of the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the activity cards">
    Each card can show the operation, policy, resource name, result, how long ago it finished and how long it took. Results read **OK**, **Failed**, **Warning** or **Running**. Other provider values appear unchanged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page through activity">
    The panel shows 15 activities per page. Use the arrows when more pages exist. Go back to page 1 when you open another resource.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Read the operation as well as the result. An OK discovery, retention cleanup, scan or deletion does not mean a backup completed. A card shows up to four nonzero result details, such as items, folders, files or channels. It is a summary, not a full log.

If the panel is empty, confirm you opened an Acronis resource and check the page number. An empty panel does not prove no backup has ever run.

## Read Cove Microsoft 365 history

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row">
    Go to **Backup > Microsoft 365**, choose **Cove** if a selector appears, and select a mailbox, drive, site or team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the daily status">
    The panel is titled **Backup history** and covers the last 28 days. **Daily backup status** shows each day for the workload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the runs">
    Each run shows its status plus **Started**, **Completed**, **Protected data**, **Processed size** and **Errors**. Use **Previous** and **Next** to page. A note says when only the most recent runs are shown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the panel says history will appear after the next Cove sync, wait for that sync. **History updated** shows when it last refreshed.

## Read NinjaOne details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row">
    Go to **Backup > Devices** and select a NinjaOne row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the storage">
    Check **Status** and **Total Storage**. Storage can split into **Cloud**, **Local** and **Revisions**, with breakdowns such as File/Folder, Image, Image v2, Network Share, and Current, Previous and Deleted revisions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the dates">
    Under **Details**, compare **Last Success**, **Last Failure** and **Last Synced**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Sections appear only when NinjaOne reports data for them. **Last Synced** is when MSPortal last imported the record. It is not a backup time, so a newer sync does not mean a newer successful backup.

## Read Datto Backup details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row">
    Go to **Backup > Devices** and select a Datto Backup row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary">
    The panel shows the asset's status (**Protected**, **Needs attention**, **Last backup failed** or **No backup yet**) with **Last snapshot**, **Latest offsite copy**, **Local recovery points**, **Screenshot verification**, **Volumes** and **Operating system**. A note appears when backups are paused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the history">
    **Recent backups** summarizes the latest attempts, and the **Timeline** lists each one as **Successful**, **Failed** or **Verification failed**, with any reported errors. A successful backup can still carry a verification warning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Backup history fills in after the next Datto sync. **Last synced** shows import freshness, not a new backup.

## Read Slide Backup details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row">
    Go to **Backup > Devices** and select a Slide Backup row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary">
    The panel shows the asset's status, **Open alerts**, **Last recovery point**, **Latest cloud copy**, **Backup schedule**, **Local retention**, **Boot verification**, **File system verification**, **Protected volumes**, **Operating system**, **IP addresses** and **Agent last seen**. Notes appear when backups are paused or the encryption key is sealed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the appliance">
    The **Slide appliance** section shows its name, model, serial number, service plan, software version, warranty expiry, storage and protected data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the history">
    **Recent backups** lists each run as **Successful**, **Failed**, **Canceled** or **In progress**, with boot, file system and service verification results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read NinjaOne SaaS Backup history

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row">
    Go to **Backup > Microsoft 365**, choose **NinjaOne SaaS Backup** and select a record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary">
    The panel shows the account's state (**Backing up**, **Backing up with errors**, **Never backed up** or **Deactivated**), **Last backup**, **Workload**, **Messages** or **Files**, and **Storage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read recent changes">
    **Recent changes** is a timeline of what MSPortal saw change, such as **Errors reported**, **Errors cleared**, **Backup completed**, **Deactivated**, **Reactivated** or **First recorded**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

MSPortal records an entry each time a sync sees the account change. This is not the provider's job log, and the oldest entry is not necessarily the first backup.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Cove device rows do not open a panel | Expected. Cove Microsoft 365 rows open the history panel instead. |
| The panel says the item is not available | The record is outside your selected company scope. Change the company selector. |
| **Backup details could not be loaded** | Try again. If it stays, ask your MSP to check the integration. |
| History is empty | Wait for the provider's next sync. Empty history does not prove no backup has run. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does OK always mean a successful backup?">
    No. Read the operation type. OK can describe discovery or retention cleanup too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are only a few result counts shown?">
    A card shows up to four nonzero result entries. It is a summary of that activity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Last Synced mean the device just backed up?">
    No. It shows when MSPortal imported the record. Check the last success and the reported status separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I restore from a panel?">
    No. Ask your MSP through the Home AI Assistant, as described in [Open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
