Backup shows what your backup providers report: protection status, storage, backup dates and alerts. This guide is for MSP staff and company users who have Backup read access. Your MSP controls which providers are connected, which companies they map to and who can see Backup.

These screens only report. They do not start backups, restore files, change retention or resolve provider alerts.

## Before you start

- You have the **Backup** read permission. Without it, MSPortal sends you to Home.
- Your MSP has connected a backup provider and mapped its customers to companies. See [Integrations](/user-guides/integrations/index).
- Check the company selector at the top of the page. Every Backup page follows it.

## Open Backup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Backup">
    Select **Backup** in the sidebar. The page opens on **Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the company">
    Use the company selector. MSP staff can choose any company they are allowed to see. Company users see the companies their MSP grants them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a tab">
    Select **Dashboard**, **Devices**, **Microsoft 365** or **Alerts**. A tab appears only when MSPortal finds eligible data for the selected companies. If you open a hidden tab by its address, MSPortal sends you back to **Dashboard**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each tab shows

| Tab | What it shows | Providers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Protection tiles, one card per device-backup provider and Microsoft 365 summaries. | All below |
| **Devices** | One row per backed-up device, workload or appliance asset. | Cove, Acronis, NinjaOne, Datto Backup, Slide Backup |
| **Microsoft 365** | Exchange, OneDrive, SharePoint and Teams backups, or provider health reports. | Cove, Acronis, Kaseya SaaS Protection, Spanning Backup, NinjaOne SaaS Backup |
| **Alerts** | Active and resolved backup alerts. | Acronis, Datto Backup, Slide Backup |

Inside **Microsoft 365**, a provider selector appears when more than one provider has data. Kaseya is labeled **SaaS Protection**. With one provider, its report opens directly.

<Note>
Company users see your MSP's managed service instead of vendor names. Cards and tabs read **Managed Device Backup** and **Managed Microsoft 365 Backup**, numbered when there are several. The **Provider** column and provider filters are for MSP staff only.
</Note>

## Report a concern (company users)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Note what you see">
    Write down the company, the device or workload, the status shown and the last backup date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your MSP">
    Start with the Home AI Assistant, as described in [Open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). Review the request and confirm it when prompted. Opening Backup does not submit a ticket.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check setup and access (MSP staff)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the provider connection">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations** and confirm the backup provider is connected and its last sync succeeded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check company mapping">
    Map each provider customer or organization to the right company. Unmapped customers do not appear in client views.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the user's access">
    Confirm the user's role has Backup read access. See [Change Company user permissions](/user-guides/settings/change-company-user-permissions). Kaseya SaaS Protection and Spanning Backup also need their integration feature turned on for your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Setup guides: [Cove](/user-guides/integrations/enable-cove-backup-integration), [Acronis](/user-guides/integrations/enable-acronis-integration), [NinjaOne](/user-guides/integrations/enable-ninjaone-integration), [Kaseya SaaS Protection](/user-guides/integrations/enable-kaseya-saas-protection-integration) and [Spanning Backup](/user-guides/integrations/enable-spanning-integration).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Backup** is missing from the sidebar, or you land on Home | Your role lacks Backup read access. Ask an MSP administrator. |
| A tab is missing | Nothing eligible exists for the selected companies. Change the company selection, or ask your MSP to check the connection and mapping. |
| The dashboard shows **No Backup Data Yet** | No provider has reported data for these companies yet. MSP staff check **Settings > Integrations** and company mapping. |
| A message says some backup data could not be loaded | One provider failed to read. Totals leave that provider out, so treat zeros as unknown. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Backup require Cove?">
    No. Cove, Acronis, NinjaOne, Datto Backup, Slide Backup, Kaseya SaaS Protection, Spanning Backup and NinjaOne SaaS Backup each feed different tabs, as shown above.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is the Sync button?">
    Backup has no Sync button. The Kaseya and Spanning reports have **Refresh**, which reloads data already imported. MSP staff check provider syncs under **Settings > Integrations**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an empty dashboard mean every backup is healthy?">
    No. Missing mappings, unavailable data or a load problem can show empty results or zero totals. Ask your MSP to confirm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I restore a file or change the schedule here?">
    No. Ask your MSP. Authorized MSP staff use the provider's own tools.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
