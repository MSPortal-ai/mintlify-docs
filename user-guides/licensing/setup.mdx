Use this guide to connect Pax8 so your subscriptions, products, and Azure usage appear in the Storefront. This page is for MSP staff. The full wizard guide is [Configure Pax8 Integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-pax8-integration).

## Before you start

- You need an active Pax8 partner account and access to integration management in MSPortal.
- You need your Pax8 sign-in and any MFA device, because Pax8 connects through OAuth.
- Create the MSPortal companies you want to map first.
- Connecting Pax8 does not let clients buy anything. Set up the catalog and self-service rules afterward.

## Connect Pax8

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Integrations">
    Click **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Integrations**. Find **Pax8** and open its setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect to Pax8**, sign in to Pax8, and approve the request. You return to MSPortal when it finishes.

    <Frame caption="The Connect step starts the Pax8 sign-in. Nothing is authorized until you approve it in Pax8.">
      <img src="/images/product/pax8-oauth-connect.png" alt="Pax8 connection card with the Connect to Pax8 button." width="1024" height="380" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose sync settings">
    On **Sync Settings**, decide whether to turn on **Pax8 Invoices**. The switch saves right away. Turning it on starts an invoice sync into the Invoices page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map Pax8 companies">
    On **Map Companies**, review the **Quick Match** suggestions or pick each MSPortal company by hand. Save the mappings with **Import Selected** or **Next**. Only mapped companies get subscriptions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync subscriptions">
    On **Complete**, click **Sync Subscriptions**. A message reads **Sync started**. Click **View Storefront** to open the Storefront.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check the result

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Storefront > Subscriptions">
    Click **Storefront** in the sidebar. The subscriptions for your mapped companies appear after the sync finishes. A started sync is not a finished import.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map any new companies">
    If you add companies in Pax8 later, open the Pax8 setup again and map them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the catalog and self-service rules">
    Go to **Settings > Storefront**. See [Licensing](/user-guides/licensing/index).
  </Step>
</Steps>

For Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or Adobe license counts and assignments, open **Cloud > Licenses**. See [Read cloud license usage](/user-guides/cloud/licenses).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The Pax8 sign-in does not finish | Check that your Pax8 account has admin access and that pop-ups are allowed. Try again. |
| No companies appear | Wait for the first sync, then reopen the setup. |
| No subscriptions appear | Check that the Pax8 company is mapped to an MSPortal company, then run **Sync Subscriptions** again. |
| Products are missing from the catalog | Open **Settings > Integrations**, open Pax8, and click **Sync product catalog**. |
| Invoices are missing | Check that **Pax8 Invoices** is on and wait for the sync to finish. |
| Data looks old | Pax8 data syncs once a day. Run **Sync Subscriptions** or **Sync product catalog** to refresh sooner. |
| Pax8 stops syncing and asks you to reconnect | Open **Settings > Integrations**, open Pax8, and use **Reconnect**. |
| Clients cannot buy | Connecting is not enough. Set catalog visibility and self-service rules in **Settings > Storefront**. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is setup a three-step wizard?">
    No. The steps are **Connect**, **Sync Settings**, **Map Companies**, and **Complete**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Quick Match save my mappings?">
    No. It only suggests matches. Save them with **Import Selected** or **Next**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to save the Pax8 Invoices switch?">
    No. It saves right away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I set approvals and self-service?">
    In **Settings > Storefront**. See [Licensing](/user-guides/licensing/index).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting Pax8 in MSPortal cancel the subscriptions?">
    No. It removes the MSPortal connection and the synced data. Subscriptions stay in Pax8.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
