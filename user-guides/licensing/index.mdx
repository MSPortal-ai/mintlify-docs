Use **Settings > Storefront** to control what your clients can see in the Storefront and which subscriptions they can ask to change. This page is for MSP staff with Manage access to the **Storefront** resource.

<Info>
  Looking for license counts or who holds a license? Open **Cloud > Licenses** and read [Read cloud license usage](/user-guides/cloud/licenses). The old `/licensing` page now opens Cloud Licenses.
</Info>

## Before you start

- Connect Pax8, Sherweb, or both in **Settings > Integrations**. Without one, **Settings > Storefront** shows **No Storefront Integration** with a link to **Integrations**. See [Set Up Pax8 for Subscriptions and Storefront](/user-guides/licensing/setup).
- Map each distributor company to an MSPortal company. Only mapped companies appear.
- You need Manage access to the **Storefront** resource (in the role editor, **Storefront** with **Manage** access) to change anything. Users with only settings read or write access can open the page but cannot save.
- The self-service rules and the **Apply to All** button are for Pax8. Sherweb has no per-subscription rules. Sherweb requests always start as **Pending approval**.
- The page has two tabs, **Self-Service** and **Catalog**.

## Choose who is notified about requests

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Storefront">
    Click **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Storefront**. The **Self-Service** tab opens first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Approver Role list">
    In the **Request Approvals** card, open the **Approver Role** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Pick a company role, or **No role set**. The change saves right away and a message reads **Approver role saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Members of this role, for the requesting company, are notified of every new request. They can also approve or reject it. Primary admins are always notified. The role applies to both Pax8 and Sherweb.

## Set self-service defaults (Pax8)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Self-Service tab">
    Go to **Settings > Storefront > Self-Service**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the defaults">
    Turn **Allow Self-Service by Default** on or off. Enter **Min Qty** (at least 1) and **Max Qty**. Leave **Max Qty** empty for no limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Defaults**. A message reads **Global settings saved**. The defaults apply to subscriptions that sync in after this point. They do not change subscriptions you already have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: apply to every active subscription">
    Click **Apply to All** to copy these three values onto every active Pax8 subscription. It also sets **Approval** to **Required** on all of them. This overwrites the rules you set on individual subscriptions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New subscriptions start with **Approval** set to **Required** unless a vendor override or a saved default says otherwise.

## Set rules for one subscription (Pax8)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the subscription">
    On the **Self-Service** tab, scroll to **Subscription-Level Settings**. Use the filters under **Product**, **Company**, **Self-Service**, and **Approval** to find it. The table shows 25 subscriptions per page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Edit Self-Service Settings">
    Click the row, or click the **...** menu and choose **Edit Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the rules">
    Turn **Allow Quantity Changes** on to let clients request changes. Turn **Require Approval** on to hold changes for review. Enter **Minimum Quantity** and **Maximum Quantity**. **Require Approval** and the limits are unavailable while **Allow Quantity Changes** is off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Settings**. A message reads **Subscription settings saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dialog shows the current quantity so you can pick sensible limits. The row menu also has **Change Quantity** for subscriptions that allow changes. It sends a request like a client would.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Product** | The product and vendor. |
| **Company** | The mapped company, or **Unmapped**. |
| **Qty** | The current quantity. |
| **Self-Service** | **Enabled** or **Disabled**. |
| **Approval** | **Required** or **Auto**. |
| **Limits** | The minimum and maximum, or **No max**. |

## Choose which products clients see

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Catalog tab">
    Go to **Settings > Storefront** and click **Catalog**. Pax8 and Sherweb each get their own table when both are connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the product">
    Type in the filter under **Product**, or use the filters under **Vendor**, **Category**, **Visible**, and **Featured**. The table shows 20 products per page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show or hide it">
    Turn the **Visible** switch on to show the product to clients, or off to hide it. Hidden products never appear in the client catalog. Existing subscriptions are not cancelled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Feature it">
    Turn the **Featured** switch on to put the product at the top of the client catalog. The switch is unavailable while the product is hidden.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change many products at once, check the boxes in the first column. A bulk **Actions** menu appears with **Show Selected**, **Hide Selected**, **Feature Selected**, and **Unfeature Selected**. The header checkbox can select every product that matches your filters.

The Pax8 table shows **Product**, **Vendor**, **Category**, **Retail Price**, **Visible**, and **Featured**. The Sherweb table shows **Product**, **SKU**, **Visible**, and **Featured**.

## Approve client requests

Requests appear on **Storefront > Requests**. See [Review requests (MSP staff)](/user-guides/storefront/index) for the full steps. Approving a request does not change the subscription. You still make the change with the distributor, then mark the request fulfilled after the next sync confirms it.

## How data syncs

- Pax8 data syncs automatically once a day, at 06:00 UTC. Sherweb syncs once a day at 07:00 UTC.
- Products are refreshed about once a week on the schedule. A manual sync refreshes them right away.
- Only subscriptions for mapped companies are imported.
- To refresh sooner, run **Sync Subscriptions** on the **Complete** step of the Pax8 setup, or click **Sync product catalog** on the Pax8 integration in **Settings > Integrations**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Storefront** is missing from **Settings** | Your role does not have Storefront settings access, or the Storefront feature is off. Ask an administrator. |
| You see **No Storefront Integration** | Connect Pax8 or Sherweb in **Settings > Integrations**. |
| You see **No Products Found** | Products appear after the next Pax8 sync. Run a sync from **Settings > Integrations**. |
| You see **No Active Subscriptions** | Subscriptions appear after the next sync. Check the company mappings. |
| A save fails | You need Manage access to the **Storefront** resource. |
| A client cannot change a quantity | Check that **Allow Quantity Changes** is on for the subscription and that the new quantity is inside the limits. |
| A product is missing from the client catalog | Check that **Visible** is on and the company is mapped. |
| **Featured** is unavailable | Turn **Visible** on first. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did the Approval tab and the approval threshold go?">
    **Settings > Storefront** has two tabs, **Self-Service** and **Catalog**. Approval is set per subscription with **Require Approval**, and the **Approver Role** decides who is notified and can approve.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding a product cancel subscriptions?">
    No. It only removes the product from the client catalog.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do the defaults change my existing subscriptions?">
    No. They apply to new subscriptions. Use **Apply to All** to overwrite existing ones.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does approving a request change the quantity in Pax8?">
    No. You make the change in Pax8, then mark the request fulfilled after the next sync.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see prices?">
    MSP staff with Manage access to the **Storefront** resource. Company users do not see prices on Pax8 rows.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
