Review goals for the companies you can access, open a goal to read its details, and export the list to CSV. This guide is for Company users and MSP staff with **Read** access to Goals.

## Before you start

- You need **Read** access to Goals. Company users see only goals shared with their company.
- The goal list follows the company selector at the top of the portal.

## Find a goal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Goals">
    Click **Goals** in the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the company">
    Use the company selector at the top. Pick the company or group you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the table">
    Use the column filters to narrow the list. You can filter by **Title**, **Company**, **Category**, **Status**, **Priority**, **Progress**, **Due Date** and **Created Date**. The choices come from your MSP's setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort and page">
    Click a column heading to sort. The table shows 20 goals per page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a goal">
    Click the goal's title. Hover over a title to see its description.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only the title opens a goal. Clicking blank space in the row does nothing.

## Read the summary

The summary at the top of the page shows three numbers for the goals in your company scope.

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Goals** | The number of goals, with the number of companies they belong to. |
| **Average progress** | The average of all progress percentages, rounded. A goal with no progress counts as 0. |
| **Past due** | Goals with a due date before today and progress below 100%. |

Table filters do not change these numbers. **Status** and **Progress** are separate fields. A **Completed** status does not set progress to 100%, and **Past due** looks at the due date and progress, not the status.

## Read a goal's details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the goal">
    Click its title. A dialog opens. It is titled **View Goal** if you have read access only, or **Edit Goal** if you can manage goals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the fields">
    Review the title, description, category, status, priority, due date, progress, quarter, year and any custom fields. The quarter and year are separate from the due date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the dialog">
    Click **Cancel**. With read access the fields are locked and there is no save button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See linked Planner items

If a goal is linked to Planner items and you can read Planner, a **Planner** link appears under its title. Open the row's **...** menu and use **View in Planner** to open the linked item. See [Connect goals to Planner and meetings](/user-guides/goals/link-to-planner-and-meetings).

## Export the goals to CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the scope and filters">
    Choose the company and apply any filters you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export CSV">
    Click **Export CSV** in the table toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the file">
    Open the download before you share it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The CSV holds all filtered goals across every page of the table, not only the rows you ticked. It has fixed columns: **Title**, **Company**, **Category**, **Status**, **Priority**, **Progress**, **Due Date** and **Created Date**. Hiding a column does not remove it from the file. Descriptions and custom fields are not included.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A goal is missing | Clear the filters and check the company selector. Ask your MSP if the goal is hidden from Company users or belongs to another company. |
| **Could not load goals. Please try again.** | Click **Retry**. An error does not mean there are no goals. |
| A saved link does not open a goal | A link opens only goals you can already see in the current company scope. |
| There is no **...** menu on a row | Read-only users get the menu only when the goal has a linked Planner item they can read. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does the total stay the same when I filter?">
    The summary covers all goals in your scope. Filters narrow only the table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a Completed goal still past due?">
    **Past due** checks the due date and whether progress is below 100%. An editor can update progress to match the status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I download only the selected goals?">
    No. **Export CSV** uses the filtered list, not the ticked rows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will hiding a column remove it from the CSV?">
    No. The export has a fixed set of columns.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my Company Overview show the same goal count?">
    Not always. The overview can use a different scope or summary. Check the scope and what each number measures.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
