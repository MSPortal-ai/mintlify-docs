Turn goals into Planner work or put them on a meeting agenda. The original goals stay as they are. This guide is for MSP staff and Company users whose role has **Manage** access to Goals.

## Before you start

- You need **Manage** access to Goals.
- **Add to Planner** also needs **Write** or **Manage** access to Planner. Without it the action is not shown.
- Goals must belong to one company. A goal with no company (an organization goal) cannot be added to Planner or a meeting.
- For a meeting, create the meeting in Calendar first.

## Add goals to Planner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the goals">
    For one goal, open its **...** menu and choose **Add to Planner**. For several, tick their checkboxes and choose **Actions > Add to Planner**. All goals must be from the same company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the prepared Planner item">
    The Planner item dialog opens with the company locked to the goals' company. One goal supplies its title and description. Several goals make one item with a combined description and links to each goal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the details">
    Review the type, status, dates, hours and cost, linked records, custom fields and Company visibility. The due date is the earliest due date among the goals, or today when none has one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Planner item">
    Use the dialog's save button and wait for **Goals added to planner**. Closing the dialog without saving creates nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it from the goal">
    Open the goal's **...** menu and choose **View in Planner**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This creates and links one Planner item. It does not replace the goals or mark them complete.

## See a goal's linked Planner items and budgets

Under each goal's title, the Goals table shows what the goal connects to.

- **Planner** opens the linked Planner item. With several items it shows a count and opens a list.
- **Budget** opens a budget that has a line linked to the goal. With several budgets it shows a count and opens a list.

Links open in a new tab. You see a link only if you can read that area. To link a budget line to a goal, open the budget editor and use the line's **Link to Planner Item or Goal** selector.

## Add goals to a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the goals">
    For one goal, open its **...** menu and choose **Add to Meeting**. For several, tick their checkboxes and choose **Actions > Add to Meeting**. All goals must be from the same company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the meeting">
    The **Add to Meeting Agenda** dialog lists the items to add. Under **Select Meeting**, choose an upcoming meeting for that company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the agenda">
    After you choose a meeting, **Current Agenda** shows what is already on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add to Agenda">
    Wait for **Added to meeting agenda**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in Calendar">
    Open the meeting and check the agenda items.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list shows only meetings that have not started yet. It does not create a meeting. If you see **No upcoming meetings found**, create the meeting in Calendar first. Adding an agenda item does not send an invitation or change the goal's progress or status.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add to Planner** is missing | You need **Manage** on Goals and **Write** or **Manage** on Planner. |
| **Add to Planner** or **Add to Meeting** is missing on one goal | The goal has no company. Only goals with a company have these actions. |
| **Please select items from the same company** | Select goals from one company. |
| No meeting appears | Check the company and that the meeting has not started. Create it in Calendar if needed. |
| **Could not save planner item** | Your entries stay in the dialog. Fix any field problems and save again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will three selected goals create three Planner items?">
    No. They create one Planner item linked to all three goals.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Add to Planner save as soon as I click it?">
    No. It opens a draft. Save the draft to create the item.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I combine goals from different companies?">
    No. Split the work by company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a goal with no company?">
    No. These actions need a company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a past meeting missing?">
    The list shows upcoming meetings only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does linking sync status and progress?">
    No. Update the goal, the Planner item and the meeting separately.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
