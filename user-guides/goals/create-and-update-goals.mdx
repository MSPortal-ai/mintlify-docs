Add a new goal or change an existing one. This guide is for MSP staff and for Company users whose role has **Manage** access to Goals.

## Before you start

- You need **Manage** access to Goals. With only **Read**, **Add Goal** is disabled and the goal dialog has no save button.
- **Hide from company users** is shown to MSP staff only.
- Check the company selector first. In a single-company view the new goal belongs to that company.

## Create a goal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Open **Goals** and click **Add Goal** in the page header. The **Create New Goal** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the company">
    In **Global View** or with several companies selected, pick a **Company**. You can also pick **Organization goal (no company)** for an internal goal. In a single-company view the form uses the selected company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the objective">
    Enter a **Title** (required, up to 255 characters) and an optional **Description**. Say what result you want and how you will measure it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set category, status and priority">
    Choose a **Category**, **Status** and **Priority**. The choices come from your MSP's setup. **Category** can be left as **None**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the dates and progress">
    Pick a **Due Date**. Enter **Progress (%)** from 0 to 100. Optionally choose a **Quarter** (Q1 to Q4) and a **Year** (2000 to 2100).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in custom fields">
    Complete any custom fields your MSP added, including required ones. Wait until the form has finished loading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility (MSP staff)">
    Tick **Hide from company users** to keep the goal internal. Leave it clear to let Company users who have access see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Goal">
    Wait for **Goal created successfully**. The dialog closes and the goal appears in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img src="/images/goals/goals-add-form.png" alt="Create New Goal form with Company, Title, Description, Category, Status, Priority, Due Date, Progress, Quarter and Year" width="2399" height="1203" />

Opening the form or typing text saves nothing. Click **Cancel** to leave without saving.

## Update a goal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the goal">
    Click its title, or open the row's **...** menu and choose **Edit Goal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the fields">
    Edit what you need. Status and progress are separate fields, so update both when both changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check custom fields and visibility">
    Review any required custom fields. MSP staff can change **Hide from company users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update Goal">
    Wait for **Goal updated successfully**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An existing goal has no company selector. You cannot move a goal to another company by editing it.

## If saving fails

If **Failed to save goal** appears, the form keeps what you typed. Check the title, the number ranges, any required custom fields and the options you chose. If the save button stays disabled, wait for the custom fields to load, or ask your MSP to check your **Manage** access. The goal and its custom fields are saved together, so do not assume the main fields saved after an error.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add Goal** is disabled | Ask your MSP for **Manage** access to Goals. The tooltip reads **You need manage permission for goals to save**. |
| The save button stays disabled | The custom fields are still loading, or you lack **Manage** access. |
| **Please fill in required custom fields** | Complete the fields named in the message. |
| **Failed to save goal** | Check the title and number ranges, then try again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the status without changing progress?">
    Yes. They are independent. Set both if you want them to agree.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are category, status and priority choices the same for everyone?">
    No. They come from your MSP's setup.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding a goal change its company?">
    No. **Hide from company users** only controls whether Company users can see it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company user create a hidden internal goal?">
    No. The Company form has no visibility control.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does choosing a quarter set the due date?">
    No. Quarter, year and due date are separate fields.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
