Use **Goals** to track objectives for your companies. Each goal has a title, an optional description, a category, status, priority, progress, target dates and any custom fields your MSP has set up.

<Note>
  Branch preview: creating and editing goals with **Write** access is planned
  for the upcoming app release.
</Note>

## Before you start

- Click **Goals** in the main navigation. The goals you see follow the company selector at the top of the portal.
- **Read** access to Goals lets you view goals. **Write** access lets you create and edit individual goals. Deleting goals, bulk operations and linking actions still need **Manage** access. A Company administrator title does not grant these permissions. Your MSP assigns them.
- Company users see only goals that are shared with their company. MSP staff can hide a goal from Company users.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem                               | What to do                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| You can open a goal but not change it | Your role has **Read** but not **Write** for Goals. Ask your MSP to review your role.                                    |
| A goal you expect is missing          | Check the company selector and table filters. Goals hidden from Company users and goals for other companies do not show. |
| **Add Goal** is missing               | It is hidden without **Write** access to Goals.                                                                          |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I view a goal but not edit it?">
    Viewing goals needs **Read** access; creating and editing them needs
    **Write** access. Ask your MSP to review your role. You cannot change your
    own permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are all goals visible to Company users?">
    No. Company users see goals for their own company that are not hidden from
    them. Organization goals with no company are not shown to them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a goal become a task automatically?">
    No. **Add to Planner** opens a Planner item for you to review and save. The
    goal stays as it is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a goal saved when I open the form?">
    No. A new goal is saved only when you click **Create Goal**. Changes to an
    existing goal are saved when you click **Update Goal**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
