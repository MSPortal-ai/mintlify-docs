Use **Goals** to track objectives for your companies. Each goal has a title, an optional description, a category, status, priority, progress, target dates and any custom fields your MSP has set up.

## Before you start

- Click **Goals** in the main navigation. The goals you see follow the company selector at the top of the portal.
- **Read** access to Goals lets you view goals. Changing, adding or deleting goals needs **Manage** access to Goals. A Company administrator title does not grant it. Your MSP assigns it.
- Company users see only goals that are shared with their company. MSP staff can hide a goal from Company users.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You can open a goal but not change it | Your role has **Read** but not **Manage** for Goals. Ask your MSP to review your role. |
| A goal you expect is missing | Check the company selector and table filters. Goals hidden from Company users and goals for other companies do not show. |
| **Add Goal** is disabled | It needs **Manage** access to Goals. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I view a goal but not edit it?">
    Reading and managing Goals are separate permissions. Ask your MSP to review your role. You cannot change your own permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are all goals visible to Company users?">
    No. Company users see goals for their own company that are not hidden from them. Organization goals with no company are not shown to them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a goal become a task automatically?">
    No. **Add to Planner** opens a Planner item for you to review and save. The goal stays as it is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a goal saved when I open the form?">
    No. A new goal is saved only when you click **Create Goal**. Changes to an existing goal are saved when you click **Update Goal**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
