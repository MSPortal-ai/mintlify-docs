Use the column filters on the **Cloud > Posture** overview to find companies by score. This page is for MSP staff and company users who see more than one company.

## Before you start

- You need Cloud read access (the **Cloud** resource with **Read** access in the role editor).
- The overview table appears only when more than one company is selected in the top bar. With one company selected, the page shows that company's details directly and has no filters.
- The table shows 20 snapshots per page.

## Narrow the overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Cloud > Posture">
    Click **Cloud** in the sidebar. The **Posture** tab opens first. Select **Global View** or **Assigned Companies** in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick companies">
    Open the filter under the **Company** column heading. Search for a company, then check the companies you want. Checking a company here keeps the overview table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a provider">
    If a **Provider** column is shown, open its filter and check **Microsoft 365** or **Google Workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a score band">
    Open the filter under the **Secure score** column heading and choose **80% and above**, **50-79%**, or **Below 50%**. Choose **All** to remove the filter.

    <Frame caption="Score bands narrow the stored percentages. Check the provider before you compare scores.">
      <img src="/images/product/cloud-posture-score-options.png" alt="Secure score filter choices All, 80% and above, 50-79%, and Below 50%." width="474" height="268" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    The table reloads from page 1. Use the page controls under the table to see more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Filters in different columns must all match. Within **Company** or **Provider**, a row matches any checked option. The **Provider** column disappears when the results hold only one provider. **Clear Filters** removes the column filters. It does not change the company selector or your access.

The table shows the score as a whole percentage, but the band filter uses the exact value. A score that shows 80% can sit in the **50-79%** band. Open the details to see the exact points.

## Open a result

- Click a **Google Workspace** row to open its checks in a dialog. Close it to return to the list.
- Click a **Microsoft 365** row, or its score bar, to open Secure Score for that company.
- Click an **MFA**, **Domains**, or **Devices** percentage to open that company's Current Posture.

## What the overview cannot do

- It has no filter for **Snapshot date**, **Checks**, **Last synced**, **MFA**, **Domains**, or **Devices**.
- It cannot be sorted and has no CSV export.
- **Columns > Save Changes** only changes which columns you see.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| There are no filters | One company is selected. Select **Global View** or **Assigned Companies**. |
| You see **No daily cloud posture snapshots match these filters.** | Click **Clear Filters** and check the company selector. |
| The **Provider** column disappeared | Only one provider is left in the results. Click **Clear Filters** to bring it back. |
| A company is missing | It has no stored snapshot. Ask your MSP to check the integration, the company mapping, and **Daily security posture** for Google Workspace. |
| The results stay narrow after you clear filters | Open **Cloud > Posture** again from the sidebar and check the company selector. A link that carried a search term can keep it. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why are there no filters when I select one company?">
    One company opens its details directly. Filters belong to the overview table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I search every posture field from one box?">
    No. Use the **Company**, **Provider**, and **Secure score** filters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a score of 80% land in the 50-79% band?">
    The table rounds the score. The filter uses the exact value, so 79.6 shows as 80% but counts as **50-79%**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a Company filter open the single-company layout?">
    No. Only selecting exactly one company in the top bar does that.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export or sort the overview?">
    No. Use the filters and the detail views.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
