**Cloud** shows synced data from the services your MSP has connected. Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace share the main tabs. **Licenses** also covers Adobe, and **Domains** also covers DNSimple registrar domains. This page is for MSP staff and company users.

## Before you start

- You need Cloud read access (the **Cloud** resource with **Read** access in the role editor) for **Posture**, **Users**, **Groups**, **Licenses**, and **Domains**.
- Adobe licenses need Storefront read access. A user with only Storefront read access sees the **Licenses** tab and no other Cloud tab.
- **Cloud** appears in the sidebar under **Protection** when at least one of Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, CIPP, Adobe, or DNSimple is connected. MSP staff connect services in **Settings > Integrations**. See [Integrations](/user-guides/integrations/index).
- Company users see only their own company.

## Open Cloud

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Cloud in the sidebar">
    Look under **Protection**. With Cloud read access, **Posture** opens first. With only Storefront read access, **Licenses** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the company">
    Use the company selector in the top bar. MSP staff can pick one company, **Global View**, or **Assigned Companies** if their access is limited. Every Cloud tab follows this selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a tab">
    Click **Posture**, **Users**, **Groups**, **Licenses**, or **Domains** in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the table">
    Use the filters under the column headings. The **Provider** column appears only when your scope has more than one provider. The **Company** column is hidden when one company is selected. Filters belong to each tab, so check them after you switch tabs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Cloud** is missing from the sidebar | Your role has neither Cloud read nor Storefront read access, or no supported service is connected. Ask your MSP. |
| A tab is missing | Your role has only Storefront read access, which shows **Licenses** only. Ask your MSP for Cloud read access. |
| A table is empty | Click **Clear Filters** and check the company selector. |
| Data is missing for one company | Ask your MSP to check the integration, the company mapping, and the sync. |
| You see **You do not have access to this Cloud data.** | Your role lacks the access that tab needs. |
| You see **Cloud data is temporarily unavailable. Try again shortly.** | Reload the page after a minute. |
| The data looks old | Show **Last synced** on the table with **Columns > Save Changes**. Ask your MSP if it is out of date. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Cloud live data?">
    No. Every tab shows synced data. The tabs have no **Sync now** button, and switching tabs does not start a sync.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which permission do I need?">
    Cloud read access for all five tabs, which covers Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. Adobe licenses need Storefront read access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an empty tab mean the company has no data?">
    Not always. Check the filters, the company selector, and your access. Then ask your MSP to check the integration and the sync.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change anything from Cloud?">
    Mostly no. The tabs are for reading. MSP staff can also hide groups from clients on **Groups** and add Microsoft 365 licenses to a meeting or the planner on **Licenses**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
