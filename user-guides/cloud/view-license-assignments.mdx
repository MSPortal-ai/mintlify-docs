Use **Cloud > Licenses** to see who holds a Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace license, and to compare assignments by email domain, office, or license combination. This page is for MSP staff and company users. The views are read only.

## Before you start

- You need Cloud read access (the **Cloud** resource with **Read** access in the role editor). Storefront read access alone shows the **Licenses** view and nothing else.
- The views cover Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. They do not show Adobe.
- The data is synced. It does not add or remove licenses.

## See who holds a license

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick one company">
    Use the company selector in the top bar to pick one company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Cloud > Licenses">
    Click **Cloud** in the sidebar, then click the **Licenses** tab. Keep the **Licenses** view selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the license row">
    Click a Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Assigned users list">
    A panel opens with the counts and the **Assigned users** list. It shows **Name**, **Email**, and **Status**, 100 users per page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the panel">
    Close the panel when you are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If several companies are selected, the user list covers all selected companies for that license, while the heading describes the row you clicked. Pick one company first for a company review.
</Note>

Adobe rows do not open this panel. Google shows a dash for purchased and available counts because Google does not report them. An empty panel does not prove nobody holds the license, so ask your MSP to check the sync if the count looks wrong.

## Compare assignments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the company and provider">
    Set the company selector in the top bar. If a **Provider** filter is shown, set it too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a view">
    Click **By domain**, **By office**, or **By combination** next to **Licenses** above the table.

    <Frame caption="The By office and By combination buttons sit next to the Licenses and By domain buttons.">
      <img src="/images/product/cloud-license-office-combination-views.png" alt="Cloud license view controls for By office and By combination." width="508" height="70" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the table">
    Use the filters under the column headings. You can sort the columns and use **Columns** to show or hide them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a combination">
    On **By combination**, click a row to open **Users with this combination**. It lists **Name**, **Email**, and **Company**, 100 users per page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| View | What it groups | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **By domain** | Assigned users by email domain and license. | Compare license holders across email domains. Users without an email domain are left out. |
| **By office** | Assigned users by office and license. | Compare locations. **(No Office)** means the office is missing or blank. |
| **By combination** | Users who hold the same set of licenses. | Spot people who have an unusual set of licenses. |

A combination is a set of licenses. It is not a Microsoft 365 or Google group. Directory groups are on the **Groups** tab.

## What changes the breakdowns

- The company selector, the **Provider** filter, and **Hide free & trial** change the breakdowns.
- **Hide large allocations**, **Only unused seats**, and the **License**, **Product**, and **Status** filters do not.
- **Hide free & trial** changes which licenses count toward a combination. Turning it off can split one combination into several.
- With several companies selected, matching domains, offices, and combinations are added together across those companies.
- The counts are numbers of users. They are not purchased seats.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **By domain**, **By office**, and **By combination** are missing | You have Storefront read access only. Ask your MSP for Cloud read access. |
| An Adobe row does not open | Adobe has no assigned users panel. |
| The panel lists more users than the row's count | Several companies are selected. Pick one company. |
| You see **No license assignments with an email domain in scope.** | No assigned users in scope have an email domain. Check the company selector and **Hide free & trial**. |
| You see **No license combinations in scope.** | Check the company selector, the **Provider** filter, and **Hide free & trial**. |
| A combination changed after you turned off **Hide free & trial** | Free and trial licenses now count toward the set, so it split. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see which users have a specific license?">
    Select one company, open **Cloud > Licenses**, and click a Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does By combination show Microsoft 365 groups?">
    No. It groups people by their set of licenses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does (No Office) mean?">
    The user's office is missing or blank in the directory. It does not mean the person works remotely.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I assign or remove licenses from these views?">
    No. Make license changes in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, or ask your MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do the counts here differ from the Licenses view?">
    These views count users by domain, office, or combination. The **Licenses** view counts assignments and purchased seats, and applies its own switches.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
