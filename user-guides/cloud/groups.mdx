Use **Cloud > Groups** to see the Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace groups synced for your companies, and to open a group to read its members. This page is for MSP staff and company users.

## Before you start

- You need Cloud read access (the **Cloud** resource with **Read** access in the role editor). It covers both Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace groups.
- Company users see only the groups their MSP has left visible to clients. MSP staff see every group, including hidden ones.
- Opening a group does not make you a member and does not give you access to anything the group controls.

## Open a group and read its members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Cloud > Groups">
    Click **Cloud** in the sidebar, then click the **Groups** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the company">
    Use the company selector in the top bar. MSP staff can pick one company, **Global View**, or **Assigned Companies** if their access is limited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the group">
    Type in the filter box under the **Group** or **Email** column heading. Use **Type** and **Company** to tell similar groups apart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the group row">
    A dialog opens with the group name, the company, and a provider badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the members">
    The dialog lists **Name**, **Email**, **Type**, **Role**, and **Status**. It shows 100 members per page. Use the page controls at the bottom to see more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the dialog">
    Close it with the close button or press Escape.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dialog is read only. It has no member search, no export, no add or remove member action, and no expansion of nested groups.

## Show more columns

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the column list">
    Click **Columns** above the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the columns you want">
    Check the columns to show, such as **Aliases** and **Last synced**. Drag a column to change its order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. Click **Cancel** to discard your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each column shows

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Group** | The group name, with its description underneath when there is one. |
| **Email** | The group email, or a dash. Some security groups have no email. |
| **Provider** and **Company** | The source and mapped company. **Provider** appears only when your scope has more than one provider. **Company** is hidden when one company is selected. |
| **Type** | **Distribution**, **Security**, **Microsoft 365**, **Mail-enabled security**, **Google group**, or **Other**. |
| **Members** | The member count, or **Not available** when the provider did not send one. **Not available** is not zero. |
| **Client visible** | A switch per group. MSP staff with write access see it. See [Manage client group visibility](/user-guides/cloud/manage-group-visibility). |
| **Aliases** | Hidden by default. Google group aliases, or **None**. Microsoft groups always show **None**. |
| **Last synced** | Hidden by default. When the group record last synced. |

## Read the member dialog

- **Type** says whether the member is a user, a group, or another kind of entry.
- Microsoft members show **Not available** for **Role** and **Status**. Google members show the role and status Google reports.
- A nested group appears as one member row. The dialog does not list the people inside it.
- The count in the dialog heading comes from the group record. The page controls count the synced member rows. The two can differ.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **Groups** tab is missing | Your role does not have Cloud read access. Ask your MSP to add it. |
| You see **No cloud groups match these filters.** | Click **Clear Filters** and check the company selector. |
| A group is missing for a company user | MSP staff may have hidden it from clients. Ask your MSP. |
| You see **No members were synchronized for this group.** | The group is empty, or its members have not synced. Ask your MSP to check the integration. |
| **Members** shows **Not available** | The provider did not send a count. Open the group to read its rows. |
| The member count does not match the list | The heading count and the list count come from different synced data. Ask your MSP to check the integration. |
| You see **Cloud data is temporarily unavailable. Try again shortly.** | Reload the page after a minute. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I add or remove a group member here?">
    No. This page is read only. MSP staff change membership in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. Company users can [open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) with the group, the person, and the change they need.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an empty dialog prove the group has no members?">
    No. Members may not have synced yet. Confirm in the provider before you rely on it for an access review or offboarding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I expand a nested group?">
    No. A nested group shows as one member row. Check the nested group in the provider to see its people.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do Microsoft members show Not available for Role and Status?">
    MSPortal does not sync those fields for Microsoft group members.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I sync a group now from this page?">
    No. Groups update on the integration's regular sync.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
