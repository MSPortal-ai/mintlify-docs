Use the column filters on **Cloud > Users** to find a Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace account. This page is for MSP staff and company users who can see the Users tab.

## Before you start

- You need Cloud read access (the **Cloud** resource with **Read** access in the role editor). It covers both Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace users.
- The list starts with **Status = Enabled**. Disabled accounts are hidden until you change the filter.
- The table shows 50 users per page.

## Find an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Cloud > Users">
    Click **Cloud** in the sidebar, then click the **Users** tab. Check the company selector in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a name or email">
    Type in the filter box under the **Name** column heading to match the display name. Use the box under **Email** to match an email address or sign-in name. Case does not matter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow by provider or company">
    If the **Provider** or **Company** columns are shown, open their filters and check the options you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    The table reloads from page 1. Use the next and previous page controls under the table to see more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the **Name** cell shows an email because the account has no display name, filter by **Email** instead.

## Show disabled accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Status filter">
    Click the filter under the **Status** column heading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Disabled or All">
    Choose **Disabled** to see only disabled accounts. Choose **All** to see both.

    <Frame caption="Status starts on Enabled.">
      <img src="/images/product/cloud-users-status-options.png" alt="Cloud Users Status options: All, Enabled, and Disabled." width="256" height="204" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your other filters">
    Leave the company, provider, and license filters as they are.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Clear Filters** removes every column filter, including **Status = Enabled**. It does not change the company selector. Select **Enabled** again if you want that view back.

## Combine filters

| Filter | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Matches the display name. |
| **Email** | Matches the email address or the sign-in name. A domain fragment such as `@contoso.com` works. |
| **Provider** | Check **Microsoft 365**, **Google Workspace**, or both. Shown only when your scope has more than one provider. |
| **Company** | Check one or more companies. Hidden when one company is selected. |
| **Office** | Pick from the offices found in your scope. |
| **Department** | Hidden by default. Show it with **Columns**, then use its filter. |
| **Job title** | Hidden by default. Show it with **Columns**, then type in its filter. |
| **Account type** | Pick a directory type. Shown only when your scope has more than one type. |
| **Status** | **Enabled**, **Disabled**, or **All**. |
| **Licenses** | Pick one or more license names. A user matches when they hold at least one of them. |

A row must match every active filter. Within one filter, a row can match any checked option. If you check two licenses, users with either one match. To see exact license sets, use [By combination](/user-guides/cloud/view-license-assignments).

**License count**, **2-step verification**, and the date columns have no filters. The table cannot be sorted. To sort, [export the users](/user-guides/cloud/export-users) and use a spreadsheet.

## If you cannot find someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Status to All">
    The person may be disabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the other filters">
    Click **Clear Filters**, then check the company selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try the other field">
    Search **Email** if **Name** finds nothing, or the reverse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your MSP">
    If the person is still missing, ask your MSP to check the provider connection, the company mapping, and the last sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Disabled accounts are missing | Set **Status** to **Disabled** or **All**. |
| An email typed in **Name** finds nothing | **Name** matches the display name only. Use the **Email** filter. |
| The **Provider** column is missing | Your scope has only one provider. |
| The **Company** column is missing | One company is selected in the top bar. |
| The **Account type** column is missing | Every user in scope has the same account type. |
| You see **One or more Cloud filters are invalid.** | Click **Clear Filters** and try again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why are disabled accounts missing when I open Users?">
    **Status** starts on **Enabled**. Change it to **Disabled** or **All**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Clear Filters go back to Enabled only?">
    No. It removes every column filter, so **Status** returns to showing all accounts. The company selector stays as it is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does picking two licenses find people who hold both?">
    No. It finds people who hold at least one. Use **Cloud > Licenses > By combination** to see exact license sets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I filter by department or job title?">
    Click **Columns**, turn on **Department** or **Job title**, click **Save Changes**, then use that column's filter.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an empty result mean the account was deleted?">
    No. Filters, access, company mapping, or sync timing can hide an account. Ask your MSP to confirm in the provider.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
