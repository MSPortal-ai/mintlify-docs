Use the column filters on **Cloud > Groups** to find a Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace group. This page is for MSP staff and company users who can see the Groups tab.

## Before you start

- You need Cloud read access (the **Cloud** resource with **Read** access in the role editor).
- Company users see only the groups their MSP has left visible to clients.
- The table shows 50 groups per page.

## Find a group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Cloud > Groups">
    Click **Cloud** in the sidebar, then click the **Groups** tab. Check the company selector in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a name or email">
    Type in the filter box under the **Group** column heading to match the group name. Use the box under **Email** to match a group email address or part of one. Case does not matter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow by company or provider">
    If the **Company** or **Provider** columns are shown, open their filters and check the options you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow by type">
    Open the **Type** filter and check one or more types. A group matches when it has any checked type.

    <Frame caption="The Type filter lists the group types found in your current scope.">
      <img src="/images/product/cloud-groups-filter-controls.png" alt="Cloud Groups Type filter with option search and selection controls." width="482" height="224" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the group">
    The table reloads from page 1. Click a row to read the group's members. See [Read cloud groups and members](/user-guides/cloud/groups).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Filters on different columns must all match. The **Group** and **Email** filters do not search member names, descriptions, aliases, or company names. The search box inside the **Type** filter searches the type options only.

Changing a filter closes an open member dialog. **Clear Filters** removes every column filter. It does not change the company selector or your access.

## What the Groups table cannot do

- It cannot be sorted.
- It has no **Members** range filter and no **Client visible** filter.
- It has no CSV export, and the member dialog has no search or export.

For wider analysis, ask your MSP for the provider records.

## If a group is missing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear the filters">
    Click **Clear Filters** and check the company selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check who can see it">
    Company users do not see groups hidden from clients. MSP staff can check the **Client visible** switch for the group. See [Manage client group visibility](/user-guides/cloud/manage-group-visibility).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the connection">
    MSP staff check the Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace integration, the company mapping, and the last sync in **Settings > Integrations**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You see **No cloud groups match these filters.** | Click **Clear Filters** and check the company selector. |
| A company user cannot see a group MSP staff can see | The group is hidden from clients. MSP staff can turn on **Client visible**. |
| The **Provider** column is missing | Your scope has only one provider. |
| The **Company** column is missing | One company is selected in the top bar. |
| A **Type** option finds no rows | Other filters or client visibility are removing every group of that type. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I search by a member name to find their groups?">
    No. There is no member lookup on this page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the search box in the Type filter search group names?">
    No. It searches the type options only. Use the **Group** filter for names.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do two checked types both have to match?">
    No. A group matches when it has any checked type. Other active filters must also match.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I sort by member count or export the list?">
    No. The Groups table has no sorting and no export.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a group in a ticket form mean I can open Cloud Groups?">
    No. Ticket form group pickers do not need Cloud read access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does filtering the table limit Hide all security groups?">
    No. That button uses the company selector in the top bar and ignores table filters.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
