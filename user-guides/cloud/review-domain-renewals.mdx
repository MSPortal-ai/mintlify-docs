Use **Cloud > Domains** to read the expiry date and auto-renew setting of domains registered through DNSimple. This page is for MSP staff and company users. Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace rows do not carry registrar dates.

## Before you start

- You need Cloud read access (the **Cloud** resource with **Read** access in the role editor).
- Your MSP must have DNSimple connected in **Settings > Integrations**, and each domain must be mapped to a company. Unmapped DNSimple domains do not appear.
- You cannot renew a domain, change auto-renew, or edit a domain from this page.

## Check expiry and auto-renew

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Cloud > Domains">
    Click **Cloud** in the sidebar, then click the **Domains** tab. Check the company selector in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show only DNSimple rows">
    If a **Provider** column is shown, open its filter and check **DNSimple**. If there is no **Provider** column, your scope has only one provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the registrar columns">
    Click **Columns**. Turn on **Auto-renew**, **Registrar status**, and **Last synced**. Click **Save Changes**. **Expires** is shown by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the domain">
    Type the name in the filter box under the **Domain** column. Scroll the table sideways if some columns are off screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the dates">
    Read **Expires**, **Auto-renew**, and **Last synced** together. If **Last synced** is old, ask your MSP to confirm the registrar details before you rely on them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Expires** | The expiry date from the last sync. A dash means no date was synced. It does not mean the domain never expires. |
| **Auto-renew** | **On** or **Off** as synced from DNSimple. A dash means no value was synced. |
| **Registrar status** | The state DNSimple reports for the domain. It is not a test of DNS, your website, or email. |
| **Last synced** | When MSPortal last fetched the registrar record. Later changes are not shown until the next sync. |

## Build a renewal review list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter to DNSimple">
    Set the **Provider** filter to **DNSimple**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the list">
    Click **Export CSV** above the table. The file includes every domain that matches your filters, across all pages.

    <Frame caption="Export CSV downloads every matching domain. It does not renew a domain.">
      <img src="/images/product/cloud-domain-export-control.png" alt="Export CSV button on Cloud Domains." width="244" height="64" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort in a spreadsheet">
    The table cannot be sorted or filtered by date. Open the CSV and sort **Expires** there. The file includes **Expires**, **Auto-renew**, **Registrar status**, and **Last synced** even if those columns are hidden.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Hand the renewal to your MSP

- **Company users:** [open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) with the domain, the expiry date you see, and your concern.
- **MSP staff:** check the expiry, renewal setting, and account status in DNSimple before you act. If a domain is missing here, check the DNSimple company mapping. See [DNSimple integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-dnsimple-integration).

<Note>
**Unverified** on a DNSimple row and **Secondary domain** under **Cloud presence** are not renewal warnings. **Unverified** is the label for every DNSimple row. **Secondary domain** means the name was not found in a synced Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace tenant.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Expires** is a dash | The date was not synced. Ask your MSP to check the DNSimple connection and the domain's company mapping. |
| A DNSimple domain is missing | Check the company selector and the **Provider** filter. MSP staff check the company mapping. |
| **Auto-renew** is not shown | Show it with **Columns**, then click **Save Changes**. |
| The date looks out of date | Check **Last synced**. The page shows the last sync, not a live lookup. |
| **Export CSV** is missing | Your role does not have Cloud read access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I renew a domain or turn on auto-renew here?">
    No. Your MSP manages renewals in DNSimple.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an empty Expires field mean the domain never expires?">
    No. The date was not synced, or you are looking at a Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Auto-renew On guarantee the renewal?">
    No. It is a synced flag. Your MSP must confirm the live registrar settings and payment details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Created the original registration date?">
    For DNSimple rows it is the registration date. For Google Workspace it is the domain creation time. For Microsoft 365 it is the date MSPortal stored the record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Secondary domain mean I can cancel the domain?">
    No. It only means the name was not found in a synced Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace tenant. Ask your MSP before you cancel anything.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
