This guide is for MSP staff. Company users cannot review or reopen findings, even when they can read Current Posture.

## Before you start

- You have Microsoft 365 management permission.
- To see reviewed rows and provider details, you also need integration-settings read access.
- You opened **Cloud > Posture > Current Posture** with one company selected. See [Find and read Current Posture findings](/user-guides/microsoft-365/find-posture-findings).

## Mark a finding as reviewed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the finding">
    Find the record under **Items needing attention**, then open its row or detail arrow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the source">
    Read its source context, timestamps and current configuration. Confirm the exception is expected or already handled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note (optional)">
    In **Review**, explain the decision in up to 500 characters. Say why the exception is expected and what decision is needed later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark it reviewed">
    Select **Mark as reviewed**. It saves right away, with no second confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the result">
    Wait for **Finding marked as reviewed**. The panel closes and the findings and scorecards refresh.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Reviewing is an acknowledgment in MSPortal. It does not fix Microsoft settings, close a provider incident or remove a risk. Company users no longer see the reviewed item, and the overview calculation leaves the matching resource out, so counts or coverage can change with no change in Microsoft. This screen has no expiry or reminder for a review.

## Find and reopen a reviewed item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the right access">
    Use an MSP account with Microsoft 365 management permission and integration-settings read access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the record">
    Select the company and find the row. Reviewed rows are muted and marked **Reviewed**. There is no separate Reviewed tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the note">
    Open the row and read the saved note under **Review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reopen it">
    Select **Reopen finding**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Wait for **Finding reopened**, then check the refreshed table and metrics.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Reopening removes the acknowledgment. The record must still exist and meet the finding criteria to show in the normal company view. It does not reopen a Microsoft incident or restore a deleted record.

If you reviewed a finding and cannot find it afterward, check that you have integration-settings read access. Reviewed rows need that permission. A source refresh can also remove a finding that no longer qualifies.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Check |
| --- | --- |
| No review controls | Confirm an MSP staff account and Microsoft 365 management permission. Company users use the reader only. |
| Save fails | Check the displayed error, company scope, feature availability and permissions. Do not assume the review was saved without success. |
| Score improves after review | The acknowledged resource was excluded from calculation. Verify provider remediation separately. |
| Reviewed item cannot be reopened here | Check integration-settings read access and whether the source record still qualifies for this table. |
| Finding returns after a later sync | A changed provider identity can be a different finding. Recheck the evidence instead of assuming the earlier acknowledgment covered it. |

For normal user support requests, direct Company users to [the Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). Use [finding search and details](/user-guides/microsoft-365/find-posture-findings) to gather context.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can Company users mark a finding as reviewed?">
    No. The action requires an MSP staff account and Microsoft 365 management permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Mark as reviewed fix the issue in Microsoft 365?">
    No. It saves a portal acknowledgment and excludes the matching resource from the normal reader and overview calculation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a note required and how long can it be?">
    The note is optional and limited to 500 characters. Explain why the exception is expected or already handled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there another confirmation after Mark as reviewed?">
    No. Clicking it saves the acknowledgment. Wait for the success message and refreshed results.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is the Reviewed tab?">
    There is no separate Reviewed tab. Eligible MSP readers see reviewed rows muted in the same findings table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I acknowledge a finding but not see it afterward?">
    Including reviewed rows also requires integration-settings read access. Ask the MSP administrator to check that separate permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will a review expire automatically?">
    This screen has no review-expiry or reminder control. Reopen the finding when it needs attention again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Reopen finding reopen an incident in Microsoft?">
    No. It removes the portal acknowledgment. The provider record must still exist and qualify to return to the normal findings list.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
