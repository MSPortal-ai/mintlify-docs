Microsoft Secure Score rates how well a company's Microsoft 365 tenant follows Microsoft's security recommendations. Open **Cloud > Posture > Secure Score** to read the latest stored score and its recommendations. From the multi-company **Posture** overview, opening a Microsoft row also opens that company's Secure Score. This guide is for company users with Microsoft 365 read access and for MSP staff.

## Before you start

- Your role has Microsoft 365 read access.
- The company has a connected Microsoft 365 tenant and a synced score. MSP staff connect tenants under [Connecting companies to Microsoft 365](/user-guides/microsoft-365/connecting-companies).
- Company users ask their MSP to change permissions or security settings.

## Open the score for a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Secure Score">
    Go to **Cloud > Posture**, then choose **Secure Score**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company">
    Look at the company selector in the top bar. MSP staff should select one company before they review a score or plan work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary">
    Read the cards at the top. See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose controls">
    Select **Needs attention**, **Addressed** or **All**, then click a control's **Title** to read its detail panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for help (company users)">
    [Start with the Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). Include the company, the control title and your concern.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An empty page can mean no stored score exists for the selected companies. It does not mean the company passed every check. Ask the MSP to check the connection and sync.

## Read the summary

| Card | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Secure Score** | Stored points achieved out of available points. |
| **Score Percentage** | The overall score as a whole percentage. |
| **Industry Comparison** | Microsoft's average for all tenants, with your difference from it. It is not matched to your industry or company size. A dash means no comparison is available. |
| **30-Day Trend** | The change in points between the oldest and newest stored scores in the last 30 days, with its percentage. It shows 0 when fewer than two scores fall in that window. |
| **Category cards** | Points and percentages for categories such as Apps, Data and Identity. Only categories in the loaded data appear. |

The summary is separate from the controls table. Changing a table filter does not recalculate these cards. Category totals and the overall score can come from different synced datasets, so their totals need not match. A score is configuration evidence. It does not guarantee security or show that an incident happened.

## Choose a control view

<Frame caption="Example counts depend on the company and synced controls.">
  <img src="/images/product/secure-score-view-controls.png" alt="Secure Score Needs attention, Addressed and All views." width="556" height="78" />
</Frame>

| View | Included statuses |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs attention** | To address, Planned and Unknown. This is the starting view. |
| **Addressed** | Risk accepted, Resolved externally, Reviewed and Completed. |
| **All** | Both groups. |

**Addressed does not mean Completed.** Read each control's status. Planning work in MSPortal does not change Microsoft's control status. Switching views clears checked rows and can close a detail panel when its control is outside the new view.

## Read the columns

- **Title** opens the control.
- **Category** and **Service** describe its area.
- **Max Points** is the control's maximum value, not the score you can still gain.
- **Status** is Microsoft's state for the control, with a score-based fallback when none is supplied. An unrecognized state can keep Microsoft's wording and counts as Unknown.
- **Severity** comes from the recommendation tier: Core is Critical, Advanced is High, Defense in depth is Medium, and an unknown tier is Informational. It does not show an active threat.
- **Planner** appears when you have Planner access and opens linked work. A blank cell does not show that nobody planned similar work.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The page is empty | Check the company selector. Ask your MSP to check the Microsoft 365 connection and sync. |
| A control is not in the table | Switch from **Needs attention** to **All**, then check the column filters. |
| **Industry Comparison** shows a dash | Microsoft supplied no comparison data. |
| The summary cards do not change when you filter | They summarize the stored score, not the filtered table. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is Microsoft Secure Score?">
    Open **Cloud > Posture > Secure Score**, or open a Microsoft row in the multi-company Posture overview. The old Microsoft 365 entry is no longer in the sidebar.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Addressed mean every recommendation was completed?">
    No. Addressed also includes Risk accepted, Resolved externally and Reviewed. Check each status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a Planned control still under Needs attention?">
    Planned counts as needing attention. Creating a Planner item does not change Microsoft's state or complete the control.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Industry Comparison cover companies in my industry?">
    No. It uses Microsoft's all-tenant average.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Critical mean an attack was detected?">
    No. Severity comes from the recommendation tier. Review the recommendation with your MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change these settings myself?">
    Ask your MSP. Company users start with the Home AI Assistant. Viewing a score does not apply a change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
