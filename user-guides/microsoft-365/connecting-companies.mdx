The **Company M365 Setup** page lets MSP staff connect each client company to its Microsoft 365 tenant in one place. Once connected, MSPortal syncs users, licenses, domains and Secure Score data for that company.

## Before you start

- You are MSP staff with permission to manage companies or integration company mappings. Opening Settings alone does not give you connection rights.
- An administrator of the client's Microsoft 365 tenant is available to approve permissions, or you have admin credentials for that tenant.

## Connect a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the M365 Companies page">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations > Microsoft 365 > Companies**. The list shows every company and its connection status. A progress bar at the top shows how many companies are connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Find the company and click **Connect** on its row. A popup opens with the Microsoft sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and grant permissions">
    Sign in with an **administrator account** from the client's Microsoft 365 tenant and approve the permissions. Complete any compliance acknowledgment MSPortal shows before consent. When it works, the popup closes and the list refreshes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the connection">
    Confirm the right company and Microsoft tenant now show as **Connected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for each company">
    Work down the list. Use **Search companies...** to find a company, or the filter to show **All companies**, **Unconfigured only** or **Connected only**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your browser blocks the popup, MSPortal shows an error. Allow popups for MSPortal and try again.
</Note>

## Send a connection link instead

Use **Generate Link** if you sign in to client tenants in a separate browser profile or a different browser, or if someone else must approve the connection.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate the link">
    Open the **Actions** menu on the company's row and choose **Generate Link**. A **Shareable connection link** dialog shows the link. MSPortal tries to copy it. If your browser blocks that, copy it from the dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it where the admin is signed in">
    Paste the link into the browser or profile where the client's Microsoft 365 admin account is signed in, and finish the sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the connection">
    Return to the Companies page and confirm the company shows as **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Each link **expires in 7 days** and works **once**. If it has expired or been used, select **Generate new link**. Share it only with the intended administrator.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Generate Link works well with Chrome profiles or Firefox containers that keep each client tenant separate.
</Tip>

## Read the companies table

The table has one row per Microsoft tenant connection. A company with several connected tenants appears several times. Use **Actions > Add Tenant** to add another connection for the company. Use **Reconnect** to renew the connection on that row.

**Connected** means a connection is saved. It does not mean the latest sync worked. **Last synced** shows the latest user or group sync, not every Microsoft dataset, so check the Cloud page for the data you need.

## Reconnect a company

Reconnect when a token expired or permissions changed. Click **Reconnect** on the company's row and sign in again the same way you did the first time.

## Mark a company as not on Microsoft 365

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Actions menu">
    On the row of an unconnected company, open **Actions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark it">
    Choose **Mark as not on Microsoft 365**. It saves right away and removes the company from the progress count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Undo it later">
    Filter by **Not applicable**, then click **Restore** on the row.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This only sorts the company in setup. It does not revoke any Microsoft consent.

## Disconnect a tenant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Actions menu">
    On the tenant's row, open **Actions** and choose **Disconnect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the confirmation">
    The dialog names the company and domain. Other tenant connections for the company are separate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm only if you want to remove that connection. Synced users and groups stay but stop updating.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Disconnecting in MSPortal does not remove consent on the Microsoft side. Reconnecting does not guarantee earlier imported records come back.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The popup does not open | Allow popups for MSPortal and click **Connect** again. |
| The popup closes but the company is not connected | Consent may have been canceled or failed. Fix the reported problem and start again. |
| **Generate Link** did not copy the link | Browser clipboard access was denied. Copy the link from the dialog. |
| The link no longer works | It expired after 7 days or was used. Select **Generate new link**. |
| A company appears more than once | Each connected tenant has its own row. Use the tenant domain and ID to tell them apart. |
| **Last synced** looks old for licenses or Secure Score | **Last synced** covers users and groups only. Check the relevant Cloud page. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the progress bar show sync health?">
    No. It counts applicable companies that have a connection. Companies marked not on Microsoft 365 are left out, and a company with several tenants counts once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a separate browser profile for consent?">
    Yes. Use **Generate Link** and open it in that profile.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does closing the popup mean the connection worked?">
    No. Check that the tenant shows after the list refreshes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I check after consent?">
    Confirm the company, domain and tenant ID, then check that the expected users and groups arrive.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
