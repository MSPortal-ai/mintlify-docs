Select a ticket summary card or a supported chart element to see the tickets behind that number. Opening the list does not change any ticket. This guide is for Company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need access to the dashboard and to tickets. Your ticket permissions and company scope still apply to the list.
- Not every chart supports a ticket list. Other widgets can open a different record type, or do nothing.

## Open a ticket list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the company">
    Open the dashboard and confirm the company selected at the top of the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a card or chart element">
    Click a card such as **Current Open Tickets**, or a chart segment that supports drilldown. A window opens with the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the heading and count">
    The window title names the metric. Under it, the count reads **N tickets found**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the columns">
    The list shows **ID**, **Summary**, **Status**, **Priority**, **Company** and **Created**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move between pages">
    The list holds 25 tickets per page. When there is more than one page, use **Previous** and **Next**. The footer shows **Page X of Y**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a ticket">
    Click a row to open its ticket details. To respond, follow [Find and follow up on a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/follow-up-on-a-ticket).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the summary cards

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Current Open Tickets** | Tickets that are open now, within your scope and visibility rules. |
| **Entered Prev Month** | Tickets created in the last month. |
| **Closed Prev Month** | Tickets that are closed now and were last updated in the last month. |
| **Entered Prev 3 Months** | Tickets created in the last 3 months. |
| **Closed Prev 3 Months** | Tickets that are closed now and were last updated in the last 3 months. |

The card windows are rolling periods, not named calendar months or quarters. The date menu in the page header does not change them. A closed count is not a history of every closure, because a later update to a closed ticket moves it.

## Investigate a mismatch

Compare the company, the card or chart label, the dates, the status, the ticket type and your ticket visibility. Your MSP can also set [dashboard exclusions](/user-guides/dashboards/understand-dashboard-exclusions), including a rule for recurring tickets. Other modules apply their own filters.

The card counts use calendar-month intervals. The list behind a card uses the last 30 or 90 days. The two can differ near month boundaries.

When you report a mismatch, give your MSP the card name, the count, the company and the date.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No tickets found matching this criteria.** | No tickets match this metric, company and period. This is a real zero for that filter. |
| **Failed to load tickets. Please try again.** | The request failed. Close the window and try again. If it repeats, send your MSP the dashboard, metric, company and message. |
| A row will not open | Check your ticket access. The ticket may also be gone from the synced source. |
| A ticket another user sees is missing | Ask your MSP to review your ticket visibility and company access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does opening a ticket list update tickets?">
    No. The window only reads matching records.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many tickets fit on a page?">
    Up to 25. Use **Next** and **Previous** for more.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does every chart open a ticket list?">
    No. Only supported cards and chart elements do.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does changing Last 30 days not change the cards?">
    Cards keep their own windows. The date menu changes date-aware charts only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can a card count differ from its list?">
    Check company, ticket visibility, exclusions and sync timing. The 30 and 90 day list windows can also differ from the card's calendar-month windows at the edges.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a dashboard show tickets outside my access?">
    No. Ticket permissions and company scope still apply. Ask your MSP if expected records are missing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
