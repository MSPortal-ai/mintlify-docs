Open **Settings > Dashboards > Exclusions** to manage **Widget Data Exclusions**. The settings apply to every user, including Company users. The company selector does not limit them. This guide is for MSP staff. Company readers should use [Understand dashboard exclusions](/user-guides/dashboards/understand-dashboard-exclusions).

## Before you start

- You need Dashboards access under Settings with **Write** or **Manage** to change exclusions. With **Read** only, the page shows the values and the message **You don't have permission to modify exclusions.**
- Exclusions hide values from supported widgets. They do not delete tickets, change your PSA or replace user permissions.
- They do not affect external dashboards.

## Choose the right exclusion

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Exclude Recurring Tickets** | Hides tickets created in bulk from PSA recurring ticket templates in supported ticket widgets and their drilldowns. Detection covers the Autotask recurring ticket number format. |
| **Excluded Submitters** | Hides exact submitter names from submitter widgets such as Top Ticket Submitters. |
| **Excluded Ticket Types** | Hides exact ticket type names from type breakdown widgets. It is not a filter on every ticket total. |

## Add a submitter or ticket type

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Exclusions tab">
    Go to **Settings > Dashboards** and click **Exclusions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add">
    Click **Add** under **Excluded Submitters** or **Excluded Ticket Types**. A search box opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the value">
    Type at least three characters in **Search values...** and wait for the results. Click the value to add it. The search shows up to 50 matches, so narrow it if you do not see yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or add a custom value">
    If the value is not listed, type it in **Custom value...** and click **Add**, or click **Add "your text" as custom**. Match the spelling, capitalization and spacing exactly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save Exclusions">
    The new badge is only a draft until you click **Save Exclusions**. Wait for **Exclusions saved. Dashboard widgets will now filter out excluded values.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a dashboard">
    Open an affected dashboard with the company and dates you care about and check the chart.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change or remove an exclusion

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove a badge">
    Click the **X** on the badge. To fix a typo, remove the old value and add the correct one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include recurring tickets again">
    Turn off **Exclude Recurring Tickets**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save Exclusions">
    Nothing changes for readers until you save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turning the recurring switch off does not override a Company role that hides recurring tickets in its own ticket view. Those roles keep their own setting.

## Discard unsaved changes

**Reset** appears once you have unsaved changes. It puts the page back to the last saved values. It does not clear saved exclusions. To remove a saved exclusion, remove it and click **Save Exclusions**. **Save Exclusions** is disabled until you change something.

Switching to another tab in Dashboard settings keeps your unsaved draft while the page stays open. Leaving the page does not save it.

## Matching and chart scope

- Matches are exact and case-sensitive. A partial name or wildcard does not work. Excluding **Unknown** does not exclude **Unknown User**.
- A submitter is matched against the ticket's contact name, or its reported-by name when there is no contact.
- Submitter exclusions affect submitter charts. Type exclusions affect type charts. Other ticket totals still count those tickets.
- The page has no company, contact, technician or subtype exclusions.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Save Exclusions** is disabled | Make a change. It is also disabled while a save is running. |
| No search results | Type at least three characters. Try a different part of the name, or add it as a custom value. |
| A custom exclusion has no visible effect | Check the exact spelling and case, the widget, the company, the dates and whether any tickets match. |
| A widget total differs from the Tickets list | Compare the dates, role access, company and exclusions. Widgets do not all use the same exclusions. |
| Recurring tickets still show | Detection covers only the Autotask recurring ticket format, and only supported widgets use it. |
| The controls are read-only | Ask an MSP admin to grant **Write** or **Manage** for Dashboards under Settings. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are exclusions limited to the selected company?">
    No. They apply tenant-wide to supported widgets for all users.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Add save the exclusion?">
    No. It adds a badge to the draft. Click **Save Exclusions**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add a value that is not in the search results?">
    Yes. Type it as a custom value, click **Add**, then **Save Exclusions**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Reset undo a saved change?">
    No. It restores the last saved values and discards your unsaved edits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do submitter and type exclusions change every ticket total?">
    No. They apply to submitter and type charts only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Exclude Recurring Tickets stop recurring tickets in Autotask?">
    No. It filters supported dashboard data. Schedules stay in your PSA.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I set exclusions per dashboard or per user?">
    No. They are tenant-wide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can exclusions replace ticket permissions?">
    No. They are display filters, not access rules.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do exclusions change an external dashboard?">
    No. The external service has its own filtering.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
