Create a personal dashboard from **Dashboards**, then add the charts and summary cards you need. Creating the dashboard saves it right away. Later editor changes need **Save** in the page header. This guide is for MSP staff with dashboard authoring access.

## Before you start

- You need **Write** or **Manage** access to Dashboards in your role. Seeing a dashboard does not mean you can edit it.
- Start with a custom dashboard. System dashboards such as **Ticketing Dashboard** have no **Edit** button in the reader.
- The widgets you see depend on your module permissions, connected integrations and company data.
- A personal dashboard belongs to you. To share it, an MSP admin uses [Settings > Dashboards](/user-guides/dashboards/manage-dashboard-settings).
- Company users should read [Read and filter a dashboard](/user-guides/dashboards/read-a-dashboard) and ask the MSP for changes.

## Create a personal dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Dashboards">
    Click **Dashboards** in the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the plus button">
    In the icon rail on the left, click the **+** button. Its tooltip reads **Create New Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Type a **Name** (up to 100 characters). Add an optional **Description** (up to 500 characters). Choose an **Icon**: Dashboard, Ticket, Bar Chart, Pie Chart, Trending or Users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Dashboard">
    The portal creates the dashboard and opens its editor. **Cancel** takes you back without creating anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img src="/images/product/dashboard-author-new.png" alt="New dashboard form with Name, Description, Icon, Cancel, and Create Dashboard" width="836" height="664" />

The dashboard now exists, even if it is empty. Leaving the editor does not delete it.

## Add widgets and summary cards

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Widget Library">
    On the left of the editor, the **Widget Library** lists widgets by category. On a phone, tap the **Widget Library** button to open it. Type in the search box to filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a widget to add it">
    The widget lands on the canvas in the first free spot. Add as many as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick summary cards">
    Under **Summary Cards**, tick the cards you want: **Current Open Tickets**, **Entered Prev Month**, **Closed Prev Month**, **Entered Prev 3 Months** and **Closed Prev 3 Months**. Clear a box to remove a card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange the widgets">
    Hover over a widget. Drag the grip to move it. Use the size button to change its size, the gear to edit its settings, and the **X** to remove it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Click **Save** in the page header. Wait for **Dashboard saved**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Click **Back** to open the saved dashboard. Choose the company and dates, then check each chart's labels and values.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Summary cards are saved with the layout, but the editor canvas does not show them. Look for them in the saved dashboard. They keep their own time windows. See [Inspect tickets behind dashboard metrics](/user-guides/dashboards/inspect-dashboard-tickets).

<Note>
The editor canvas previews data over a long date range with no company selected. After you save, use **Back** and check the dashboard with the company and dates your readers will use.
</Note>

## Add HaloPSA native CSAT widgets

HaloPSA native satisfaction responses can appear on custom dashboards.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the Halo import">
    Follow [enable Halo's satisfaction import](/user-guides/integrations/enable-halo-integration#import-halopsa-native-csat-for-dashboards-and-reports). Confirm the rating scale and company mapping, and give readers CSAT access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the widgets">
    In the **Widget Library**, add **CSAT Overview**, **CSAT Trend**, **CSAT by Company** or **Recent CSAT Feedback**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and check">
    Click **Save**, then open the dashboard and check the company and period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

These widgets show imported Halo feedback together with any other CSAT sources. They are not Halo-only widgets. If they are missing or empty, check the import setting, role access, sync, company links and dates. See [Read and export CSAT feedback](/user-guides/surveys/read-csat-feedback).

## Rename a dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Rename">
    Click **Rename** in the editor header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new name">
    Type up to 100 characters. The dialog shows a character count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Apply">
    **Apply** stages the new name in the editor. **Cancel** keeps the old name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Click **Save** in the page header to store the new name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a dashboard

Users with **Manage** access to Dashboards see a **Delete** button in the editor for custom dashboards.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Delete">
    Click **Delete** in the editor header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Read the **Delete Dashboard** message and click **Delete**. This cannot be undone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand Save and Back

<img src="/images/product/dashboard-editor-actions.png" alt="Dashboard editor controls for Rename, Delete, widget width, Back, and Save" width="906" height="66" />

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Create Dashboard** | Creates the dashboard immediately and opens its editor. |
| Add or remove a widget, change summary cards | Changes the editing session. Click **Save** to keep it. |
| **Apply** in Rename | Stages the name. **Save** is still needed. |
| **Save** in the page header | Saves the layout and any new name. Wait for **Dashboard saved**. |
| **Back** | Opens the saved dashboard. If you have unsaved changes it asks you to discard them first. |
| Width **½**, **⅓**, **¼** | Changes the editor's column count only. It is not a saved setting and does not enable **Save**. |

The page subtitle shows **Unsaved changes** while you have edits that are not saved. **Save** stays disabled until you change something.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **+** button or **Edit** is missing | Ask an MSP admin to check your Dashboards **Write** or **Manage** access and the dashboard type. |
| **Save** is disabled | Make a content or name change. The width button does not count. |
| A widget is missing from the library | Check the module permission, integration, company and available data. |
| A chart is empty | Check its company and dates in the saved dashboard. |
| **Failed to save dashboard** appears | Keep the editor open and try again. Reopen the editor to confirm what was saved before you retry a second time. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Create Dashboard save anything before I add widgets?">
    Yes. It creates the dashboard right away. An empty dashboard stays until you delete it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a new dashboard shared with Company users?">
    No. It is personal. An MSP admin shares it in **Settings > Dashboards**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Back save my edits?">
    No. Back asks you to discard unsaved changes. Click **Save** first and wait for **Dashboard saved**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do the editor and the saved dashboard show different values?">
    The editor preview uses a long date range and no company. The saved dashboard uses the company and dates you choose.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit a system dashboard?">
    Not from the reader. Create a custom dashboard instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do summary cards follow the dashboard date menu?">
    No. They keep their own current or rolling-month periods.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a widget change tickets or other records?">
    No. It saves dashboard configuration only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
