External dashboards show a service that your MSP has linked. They can appear inside MSPortal or need a separate browser tab. The service can require its own account. This guide is for Company users with dashboard access and for MSP staff.

## Before you start

- Your MSP must have linked the dashboard and given your Company a URL for it. Company users cannot see a dashboard that has no URL for their company.
- You may need your own sign-in for the external service. An MSPortal login does not create it.

## Open the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Dashboards">
    Click **Dashboards** in the main navigation. A dashboard pinned by your MSP can also have its own link in the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it in the icon rail">
    External dashboards sit below the divider in the icon rail on the left. Hover over an icon to see its name and the label **External**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the company (MSP staff)">
    Choose one company in the company selector to open that company's URL. In **Global View**, or with several companies selected, MSPortal opens the default URL. Selecting several companies does not combine their dashboards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the dashboard">
    Wait for it to load, then sign in to the external service if it asks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it in a new tab if needed">
    If MSPortal cannot show it inside the page, click **Open in New Tab**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A dashboard pinned to the sidebar or set as default still needs the access above.

## What you see

| What you see | What it means and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| The embedded dashboard | Use the external service's own filters. Check the company and dates it shows. |
| **Frame policy check pending. Please contact your administrator.** | The embedding check has not run. Click **Open in New Tab**, or ask your MSP to run **Check Frame Policy**. |
| A message that the content cannot be embedded, with **Open in New Tab** | The site forbids display inside another site. Click **Open in New Tab**. |
| A sign-in page | Sign in with your account for the external service. |
| **Error Loading Content** with **Retry** and **Open in New Tab** | The embed failed. Click **Retry**, or open it in a new tab. If it keeps failing, ask your MSP to check the URL. |
| A blank frame | The site may be blocking the page. Click **Open in New Tab**, and ask your MSP to check the URL. |
| The dashboard is not listed, or a link shows a not found page | Ask your MSP to check that it is active, that your company has a URL, and that your role is allowed. Do not use another company's link. |

## Filters and exports

The date menu, width button and **Export PDF** button of internal dashboards do not apply to external dashboards. Use the filters, print and download options of the external service.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You never see the dashboard | Ask your MSP to confirm it is active, your company has a URL and your role is allowed. |
| It asks you to sign in again | Sign in with your account for that service. |
| It stays blank after loading | Click **Open in New Tab** and ask your MSP to check the URL. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I open an external dashboard?">
    In **Dashboards**, below the divider in the icon rail, or from its pinned link in the main navigation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the default URL work for every company?">
    No. Company users need their own company URL. MSP staff use the default URL in **Global View**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company Admin assign dashboards or change permissions?">
    No. Ask the MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why must I sign in again?">
    The external service has its own login. Signing in to MSPortal does not replace it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Force Allow Embedding get around the other site's restrictions?">
    No. It only changes MSPortal's decision. The other site can still refuse to be framed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will selecting several companies combine their dashboards?">
    No. Select one company to open its dashboard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Export PDF here?">
    No. Use the export options of the external service.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
