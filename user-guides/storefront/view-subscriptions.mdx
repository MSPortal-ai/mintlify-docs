Use **Storefront > Subscriptions** to see the products your company has through Pax8 and Sherweb. This page is for company users and MSP staff with Storefront access.

## Before you start

- You need Storefront read access. In the role editor this is the **Storefront** resource with **Read** access.
- Your MSP must have Pax8 or Sherweb connected, and your company must be mapped.
- To ask for a quantity change you need Storefront write access.
- If your MSP uses both Pax8 and Sherweb, each provider has its own section with its own table.
- Prices and costs are shown to MSP staff with **Manage** access to the **Storefront** resource. Company users do not see them on Pax8 rows.

## Find a subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Storefront > Subscriptions">
    Click **Storefront** in the sidebar. **Subscriptions** opens first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the company">
    Use the company selector in the top bar. Company users see only their own company. With several companies selected, a **Company** column appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the Pax8 table">
    Type a name in the filter under **Product**. Use the filters under **Vendor** and **Status**. **Status** starts on **Active**. Change it or clear it to see other statuses, such as cancelled or trial subscriptions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the Sherweb table">
    Type a name in the filter under **Product** or pick a company in the **Company** filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the row">
    Check the product and SKU, quantity, status, and billing. The table shows 25 subscriptions per page. Use the page controls under the table to see more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open the details (Pax8)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click a Pax8 row">
    You can also click the **...** menu on the row and choose **View Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the dialog">
    The dialog shows the status, company, quantity, billing term, commitment, start and end dates, and **Last synced**. If self-service is on, it also shows the minimum, maximum, and increment. MSP staff with **Manage** access also see pricing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the dialog">
    Click **Close**. If self-service is on, click **Edit** to open the quantity dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ask for a quantity change

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the subscription">
    A **Self-service** badge next to the quantity means changes are allowed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the quantity dialog">
    For Pax8, click the **...** menu and choose **Change Quantity**. For Sherweb, click **Request Change**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new total">
    Enter the new total quantity, not the number to add. To go from 10 to 12, enter 12. The Pax8 dialog shows the minimum, maximum, and increment, plus a summary of the change. Add notes if you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit Request** (Pax8) or **Send Request** (Sherweb). Your MSP is notified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Submitting records a request. It does not change the subscription. The quantity changes after your MSP fulfills the request with the distributor and the next sync confirms it. See [Storefront](/user-guides/storefront/index).

## Read the summary

For Pax8, cards above the table show **Total Subscriptions**, **Total Licenses**, **Companies** (when several companies are selected), and **Pending Requests** (when there are some). MSP staff with **Manage** access also see a monthly cost total per currency.

The cards follow the company selector. They do not follow the **Product**, **Vendor**, or **Status** filters, so a card can show more subscriptions than the table.

The quantity is the subscription total from the distributor. It is not a count of people who have a license assigned. **Last synced** shows when the data was last imported.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The table is empty | Clear the **Status** filter and check the company selector. Then ask your MSP to check the integration and the company mapping. |
| A cancelled or trial subscription is missing | **Status** starts on **Active**. Change or clear it. |
| **Change Quantity** is missing | Self-service is off for that subscription. Ask your MSP. |
| **Request Change** is disabled | You need Storefront write access, and the row must belong to a company. |
| You see an error about quantity limits | The quantity is outside the minimum, maximum, or increment in the dialog. |
| A request is refused because one is already open | An earlier request for this subscription is still open. Ask your MSP. |
| You see **Failed to load data** | Reload the page. If it persists, tell your MSP. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does quantity mean the number of licensed employees?">
    No. It is the subscription quantity from the provider. Assigning licenses to people is separate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I enter 2 or 12 to go from 10 to 12?">
    Enter 12, the new total.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel a subscription by entering zero?">
    No. The quantity must be at least 1 and can have a higher minimum. Ask your MSP about cancellation and commitment terms.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export the table to CSV?">
    No. These tables have no export. Ask your MSP for a distributor report.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do the cards show more subscriptions than the table?">
    The cards follow the company selector only. The table also follows its filters.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
