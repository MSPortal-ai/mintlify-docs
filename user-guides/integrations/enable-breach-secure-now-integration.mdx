This guide walks you through connecting Breach Secure Now (BSN) to MSPortal.ai. Setup is for **MSP staff with integration management permission**. On the BSN connection page, the **Setup guide** has three tasks: **Connect**, **Import or match companies** and **Review**.

## Overview

Breach Secure Now is a security awareness training and dark web monitoring platform for MSPs. By integrating BSN with MSPortal.ai, you can:

- View security awareness training progress for every client in one place
- Track Employee Secure Score (ESS), phishing fail rates, and micro training completion
- Monitor dark web breach exposure per client and per user
- Include security awareness metrics in client reports and QBRs

Mapping assigns BSN clients to MSPortal companies. It does not invite users or change their portal roles.

## Prerequisites

- An active Breach Secure Now partner account
- The MSPortal integration enabled for your partner account in Breach Secure Now
- An MSPortal.ai account with integration management permissions

## Part 1: Enable the Integration in Breach Secure Now

Before connecting, you need your **Partner Integration Key** from Breach Secure Now.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log into the Breach Secure Now Partner Portal">
    Sign in to your Breach Secure Now (PII Protect) partner portal with a partner administrator account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Integrations Section">
    Navigate to the integrations area of your partner configuration and locate **MSPortal** in the list of available integrations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable MSPortal">
    Enable the MSPortal integration for your partner account.

    <Note>
      If MSPortal does not appear in your integrations list, contact Breach Secure Now support and ask them to enable the MSPortal API integration for your partner account.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy Your Partner Integration Key">
    Copy the **Partner Integration Key**, the secret key generated when the integration is enabled. This is the only value you enter in MSPortal.

    <Note>
      You do not need your Partner ID. MSPortal identifies your partner account from the Partner Integration Key automatically.
    </Note>

    <Warning>
      Treat the Partner Integration Key like a password. Anyone with the key can read your client and user training data.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Part 2: Connect in MSPortal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Breach Secure Now">
    In MSPortal.ai, go to **Settings > Integrations**, choose **Directory**, find **Breach Secure Now**, and choose **Connect**. To maintain an existing connection, open it from your active integrations instead.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Your Partner Integration Key">
    Fill in the connection fields:

    | Field | Value |
    |-------|-------|
    | **Partner integration key** | The integration key from the BSN portal |
    | **API base URL** | Pre-filled with `https://api.pii-protect.com/securedapiaccess`. Leave the default unless Breach Secure Now has given you a different endpoint. |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test Connection">
    Choose **Test connection** to verify your key.

    When the test passes, a confirmation shows your partner name and a client count. This confirms MSPortal matched your key to the right partner account.

    <Note>
      The test reads only the first five clients, so the count tops out at 5. Your full client list appears on **Import or match companies**.
    </Note>

    If the connection fails:
    - Verify the Partner Integration Key was copied in full, without extra spaces
    - Confirm the MSPortal integration is enabled for your partner account in the BSN portal
    - If you see "That Partner Integration Key was not recognized by Breach Secure Now," return to Part 1, enable MSPortal for that partner account, copy the key again, then test again
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the connection">
    Once the test passes, choose **Save and continue**. This saves the connection and starts client discovery in the background. A successful test alone does not save anything.

    <Note>
      Your credentials are stored with encryption and are never exposed in logs or API responses.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 3: Import or Match Companies

Map your Breach Secure Now clients to MSPortal companies. This determines which data syncs to which company.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sync Your Clients">
    Open **Import or match companies**. If no clients have synced yet, choose **Sync clients** to pull your client list from Breach Secure Now. The sync usually takes less than a minute, and new clients appear as they arrive.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Match Clients">
    For each BSN client, accept the suggested company or choose the correct MSPortal company. **Accept strong matches** accepts every suggestion at 90% confidence or more after you review them. **Ignore** hides a BSN client you do not want to map, and you can restore it later.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import Missing Companies">
    If a BSN client does not have a corresponding MSPortal company yet, use **Import company** on its row. MSPortal creates the company right away and matches it to the BSN client. Check for an existing company first to avoid duplicates.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the Saved Matches">
    Each accepted or chosen match saves immediately and starts a user training sync for that client. Removing a saved match also takes effect immediately.

    <Warning>
      Only data from mapped clients syncs to MSPortal. Map at least one client before you finish setup.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Part 4: Review and Finish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the Sync">
    Use **Overview** and **Sync activity** to check the connection and the results of each sync. **Run sync** starts a sync in the background; the first sync can take several minutes, depending on how many clients and users you have.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish Setup">
    Open **Review** and choose **Finish setup**. Finishing setup does not wait for the background sync, which keeps running after you leave.
  </Step>
</Steps>

User-level metrics stay empty until the first user sync finishes. Missing values, zero counts and a connected status do not prove that everyone is trained or free of exposure. Check the company match and when the data last synced.

## What Gets Synced

The Breach Secure Now integration syncs the following data for mapped clients:

### Client Metrics

- Average Employee Secure Score (ESS)
- Average phishing fail rate
- Dark web breach counts (breached employees and total breaches)
- Active user count and product type

### User Training Data

Per-user security awareness details, including:

- Security training courses available and completed
- Micro training completion percentage and average score
- Employee Secure Score (ESS)
- Phishing simulation results (emails sent, clicked, captured data, phish caught)
- Dark web breach count and last breach date
- Last login and enrollment status

---

## Synchronization Schedule

After the initial setup, data syncs automatically once per day. Clients sync first, followed by user training data.

<Note>
You can trigger a manual sync at any time: open the Breach Secure Now connection from **Settings > Integrations** and choose **Run sync**. Check **Sync activity** for the result before relying on new data.
</Note>

## Managing Your Integration

After setup, open the Breach Secure Now connection from **Settings > Integrations**:

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Connect** | Test the saved key without re-entering it, or enter a new Partner Integration Key and choose **Save changes** to replace it. Testing alone does not save it. Leave the masked key unchanged to keep it. Changing the API base URL requires entering the key again. |
| **Import or match companies** | Match clients you onboarded in Breach Secure Now since the last visit |
| **Run sync** | Start a manual data refresh |
| **Disconnect…** | Under **More actions**. Removes the connection, synced BSN data, and stored credentials. It does not revoke the key in Breach Secure Now. |

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Connection test fails | Re-copy the Partner Integration Key from the BSN portal, checking for extra spaces |
| "That Partner Integration Key was not recognized" | Confirm the key was copied in full, and that the MSPortal integration is enabled for your partner account in the Breach Secure Now portal, then test again |
| "Breach Secure Now returned a web page instead of API data" | The API base URL points at the BSN portal website. Set it back to `https://api.pii-protect.com/securedapiaccess` |
| "The Breach Secure Now API base URL must be a pii-protect.com address" | Only `pii-protect.com` addresses are accepted. Restore the default API base URL |
| Setup reports a service configuration problem | Ask MSPortal support to investigate the reported error; a new Partner ID is not required |
| No clients appear on **Import or match companies** | Choose **Sync clients**, wait a moment for the background sync, then check the list again |
| No user metrics | Confirm the client is matched, run a sync, and check its result in **Sync activity** |
| A client's data is missing | Confirm the client is mapped to a company; only mapped clients sync |
| Counts still look old | Check **Sync activity** after the background work finishes; the sync started message only confirms the request |

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Partner ID for Breach Secure Now?">
    No. Enter the **Partner integration key** from the BSN MSPortal integration; MSPortal resolves the partner account from it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the BSN test count prove that every client was imported?">
    No. The connection test reads only a sample of clients. Review the client list on **Import or match companies** and the saved matches for actual coverage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Test connection replace the stored BSN key?">
    No. After entering the replacement, choose **Save changes**. A test only checks credentials.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does zero BSN breach exposure mean a client is safe?">
    No. A zero only describes the synced data. Check the company match, how recently the data synced, and provider coverage before drawing conclusions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company users connect their own BSN account?">
    No. The connection requires MSP integration management access. Mapping BSN users does not invite them or grant portal roles.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
