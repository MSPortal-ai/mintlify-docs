Use **Import or match companies** on the Salesbuildr connection page to link each imported Salesbuildr company to the right MSPortal company. Salesbuildr quotes are imported only for matched companies. Matching is separate from each company user's permission to see those quotes.

## Before you start

- You are MSP staff with integration-company mapping access. Company users ask their MSP to fix a match or quote permission.
- Salesbuildr is connected. See [Connect and manage Salesbuildr](/user-guides/integrations/enable-salesbuildr-integration).
- To import a new portal company from this page you also need company-write access and active billing.

## Match a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the matching task">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations**, open the intended **Salesbuildr** connection from the **Active** tab, and select **Import or match companies** in the **Setup guide**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the source company">
    The workspace opens on **Import**, which lists every company that still needs a match. Use **Suggested**, **Matched** and **Ignored** to switch views, and **Search companies** to find the record. If no companies have synced yet, select **Sync companies**. For a single missing company, select **Run sync > Companies** in the page header, wait for it to finish and check **Sync activity** for connection errors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the portal company">
    For a company with a suggestion, check the proposed company and select **Accept**, or select **Change** to pick another. For a company without one, select **Choose company…** and pick the existing MSPortal company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Each match saves immediately. Wait for the success message and find the company in **Matched**. There is no separate save step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import its quotes">
    Matching does not import quotes. Select **Run sync > Quotes** in the page header, let the sync finish, then open a known quote under the company to confirm it arrived.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Several Salesbuildr companies can match one portal company. Confirm their quotes should appear under it first.

## Accept strong matches in bulk

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the review">
    Select **Accept N strong matches**. Only pairs with 90% confidence or more are included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the pairs">
    Check each Salesbuildr company and portal company pair, and untick any you want to review yourself. A name score is not proof of the same organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept">
    Confirm the review. A batch can partly succeed. Check **Matched** and review only the companies still waiting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Accepting matches does not import quotes. Run a quote sync afterwards.

## Import a portal company

Search existing companies before importing. On an unmatched row, select **Import** to create a portal company from the Salesbuildr record and match it right away. To import several at once, use **Import companies** and review the selection. Existing companies with the same name are matched instead of duplicated.

If the company is imported but matching fails, choose that company from the row on retry. Leaving setup does not delete it.

**Ignore** sets a record aside from matching; it does not delete the Salesbuildr company. **Restore** in **Ignored** returns it for review.

## Remove or correct a match

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the matched company">
    In **Matched**, find the company and select **Change**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the correct company, or remove the match">
    Pick the right existing company to move the match, or select **Remove match**. Either change saves right away, with no confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resync quotes if you removed a match">
    After you match a previously unmatched company again, select **Run sync > Quotes** and check that the quotes appear under the right company.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Removing a match deletes the company's imported Salesbuildr quotes and line items from MSPortal. QuoteWerks behaves differently and keeps its quotes unassigned. The original quotes in Salesbuildr and the portal company are not deleted. Matching the company again does not bring the deleted rows back, so run a quote sync after you rematch. Changing a match to a different company moves its imported quotes to the new company.
</Warning>

## Finish setup

Select **Continue to Review**, then **Finish setup**. This records that setup is complete. It does not certify every match or start a quote sync. Connect form changes are separate: select **Test connection** and **Save changes** on **Connect** before you leave it.

A paused integration keeps its saved matches but refuses to sync. Ask an MSP administrator to restore the connection, then request the quote import again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A company is missing from the list | Select **Run sync > Companies**, wait, and check **Sync activity** for connection errors. Supplier, manufacturer, vendor and distributor companies are never listed. |
| Matched but no quotes appear | Select **Run sync > Quotes** and check the integration is not paused. |
| Quotes vanished after removing a match | Match the company again, then run a quote sync. |
| You cannot import a company | Importing a company needs company-write access and active billing. Ask an MSP administrator. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does choosing a portal company save it?">
    Yes. Accepting a suggestion, choosing a company or importing a company saves right away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does matching import quotes?">
    No. Quotes are imported for matched companies on the next quote sync. Select **Run sync > Quotes** to start one now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Remove match do?">
    It removes the match and the company's imported quotes and line items right away. The original Salesbuildr quotes and the portal company stay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will matching again restore deleted quotes?">
    Matching makes the company eligible for import again. Run a quote sync and check that the quotes reappear.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did only part of my batch save?">
    A batch can fail after earlier pairs saved. Check **Matched** and retry only the rest.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
