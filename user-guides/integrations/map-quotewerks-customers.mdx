title: 'Map QuoteWerks customers to companies'
description: 'Match imported QuoteWerks customers to MSPortal companies, accept suggestions, and import new companies.'
icon: 'file-invoice-dollar'
---

Use **Import or match companies** on the QuoteWerks connection page to link each imported QuoteWerks customer to the right MSPortal company. A saved match decides which company a customer's quotes and line items belong to. Matching is separate from each user's permission to read quotes.

## Before you start

- You are MSP staff with integration-company mapping access. Company users ask their MSP to fix a match.
- QuoteWerks is connected and its customers have been imported. See [Connect and manage QuoteWerks](/user-guides/integrations/enable-quotewerks-integration).
- To import a new portal company from this page you also need company-write access and active billing.

## Find a customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the matching task">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations**, open the intended **QuoteWerks** connection from the **Active** tab, and select **Import or match companies** in the **Setup guide**. The workspace is titled **Import or match QuoteWerks customers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a view">
    The workspace opens on **Import**, which lists every customer that still needs a company. Use **Suggested** for customers with a proposed company, **Matched** to review saved matches, and **Ignored** for customers you set aside.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Use **Search customers** to find the customer and check its identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import missing customers">
    If no customers have synced yet, select **Sync customers** and wait for them to arrive. For a single missing customer on an existing connection, select **Run sync > Customers** in the page header, then check again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Match a customer to a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept or choose">
    For a customer with a suggestion, check the proposed company and its confidence, then select **Accept**. To pick a different company, select **Change**. For a customer without a suggestion, select **Choose company…** and search for the MSPortal company. Check the name carefully when several companies look alike.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Each match saves immediately. Wait for the success message, then find the customer in **Matched**. Navigation does not submit a separate set of pending links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a quote">
    Open a quote for that customer under **Quotes**. Saving a match also moves the customer's already imported quotes and line items to the company, so you do not need to reimport.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Several customers can match one portal company. Confirm their quotes belong under that company before combining them.

## Accept strong matches in bulk

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the review">
    Select **Accept N strong matches**. Only pairs with 90% confidence or more are included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review every pair">
    Untick any pair you want to review yourself. A high score is only a name match, so confirm the two records are the same organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept">
    Confirm the review. A batch can partly succeed. Check **Matched** and review only the customers still waiting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Import a company when none exists

Search existing companies before importing. On an unmatched row, select **Import** to create a portal company from the customer and match it right away. To import several at once, use **Import companies** and review the selection. Existing companies with the same name are matched instead of duplicated.

If the company is imported but matching fails, choose that company from the row on retry. Leaving setup does not delete it.

## Ignore or restore a customer

Select **Ignore** to set aside a customer you never want matched. It moves to **Ignored** and is not deleted from QuoteWerks. Select **Restore** in **Ignored** to return it for review.

## Remove or change a saved match

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the matched customer">
    In **Matched**, find the customer and select **Change**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the correct company, or remove the match">
    Choose the right company, or select **Remove match**. Either change saves immediately, with no confirmation, and leaving the page does not undo it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Removing a saved match leaves the quotes and line items already imported without a company, so the previous company's users can no longer see them. It does not delete the QuoteWerks customer, the quotes or the company. Saving a corrected match updates the company on already imported quotes and line items. A sync is only needed for new or changed QuoteWerks data.

## Finish the task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Select **Continue to Review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish setup">
    On **Review**, select **Finish setup**. This records that setup is complete. It does not certify every match or start a quote sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the quote sync">
    Select **Run sync > Quotes** in the page header to import quotes from QuoteWerks. Check **Sync activity**, then check a known quote's company and line items under **Quotes**, and confirm the intended company role has quote access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A customer is missing | Select **Run sync > Customers** (or **Sync customers** when the list is empty) and wait for the import to finish. |
| A company's quotes disappeared from its users | A saved match was removed. Match the customer again. |
| A customer's quotes appear under the wrong company | Open **Matched**, select **Change** and choose the correct company. |
| You cannot import a company | Importing a company needs company-write access and active billing. Ask an MSP administrator. |
| A sync is refused because the connection is paused | Saved matches stay in place. Resolve the connection problem before running the sync again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does choosing a company save the match?">
    Yes. Accepting a suggestion, choosing a company or importing a company saves right away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Accept strong matches save its suggestions?">
    Yes. Accepted pairs save when you confirm. Unticked pairs stay as suggestions for you to review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to sync quotes again after fixing a match?">
    No. Saving a match moves the quotes and line items already imported. A sync only brings in new or changed data from QuoteWerks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can several QuoteWerks customers map to one company?">
    Yes. Choose the same company for each customer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Ignore delete the customer?">
    No. It only sets the customer aside in MSPortal. Select **Restore** to bring it back.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
