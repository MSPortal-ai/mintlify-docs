Turn on Bigger Brains when your users should take your Bigger Brains training from the **Training** page instead of the MSPortal training library. This page is for MSP staff who manage integrations.

## How it works

- The **Training** page opens Bigger Brains and signs each person in with their email address.
- To sign each person in, MSPortal sends their email address to Bigger Brains. Each person needs a Bigger Brains account with the same email address.
- While Bigger Brains is on, **Settings > Training** does not appear. You manage courses in Bigger Brains.
- Bigger Brains is off until you turn it on.

## Before you start

- You need the **manage** level of Integrations settings access to turn Bigger Brains on or off.
- Make sure your users have Bigger Brains accounts with the same email addresses they use in MSPortal.

## Turn Bigger Brains on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Integrations">
    Search for **Bigger Brains** and select **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Open Bigger Brains from Training">
    The setting saves as soon as you switch it. A message confirms that Bigger Brains is turned on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Training page">
    Open **Training** from the main menu. It opens Bigger Brains for the signed-in person.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To turn it off, open **Settings > Integrations**, select **Manage** on **Bigger Brains** and turn the switch off. The **Training** page shows the MSPortal training library again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Training says the person has no Bigger Brains account | Ask Bigger Brains to add an account with the same email address the person uses in MSPortal. |
| The switch is greyed out | You need the manage level of Integrations settings access. |
| **Settings > Training** is missing | Bigger Brains is on. Turn it off to manage courses in MSPortal again. |
