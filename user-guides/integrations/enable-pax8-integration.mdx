This guide walks you through connecting your Pax8 distributor account to MSPortal.ai. Once connected, Pax8 powers the [Storefront](/user-guides/storefront/index) and [Licensing](/user-guides/licensing/index) modules. This is an MSP staff setup task; company users use the access and purchasing rules their MSP provides.

## Overview

Pax8 is a cloud distribution marketplace for MSPs. By integrating Pax8 with MSPortal.ai, you can:

- Sync subscriptions, products, and companies from the Pax8 Marketplace
- Manage active licenses and subscription quantities in the Storefront
- Offer clients a curated product catalog with approval workflows and self-service rules
- Monitor Azure usage by company
- Sync Pax8 invoices into the Invoices page (optional)

## Prerequisites

- An active [Pax8](https://www.pax8.com) partner account with admin access
- MSPortal.ai account with integration management permissions
- Companies set up in MSPortal to map Pax8 companies to

## Part 1: Prepare Your Pax8 Account

Pax8 connects through OAuth, so there are no API keys to create. You authorize MSPortal directly with your Pax8 login.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm Admin Access">
    Make sure you can sign in to Pax8 with an account that has admin access to your partner organization.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Have Your Login Ready">
    During setup you will be redirected to the Pax8 login page to approve the connection, so keep your Pax8 credentials (and any MFA device) at hand.

    <Note>
      MSPortal.ai never stores your Pax8 password. Only encrypted OAuth tokens are saved, in the vault.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2: Set Up Pax8 in MSPortal

Pax8 setup runs on its connection page. The **Setup guide** rail lists the tasks: **Connect**, **Choose data**, **Import or match companies** and **Review**.

### Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the connection">
    In MSPortal.ai, go to **Settings > Integrations**, open the **Directory** tab, find **Pax8**, and choose **Connect**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the OAuth Flow">
    On **Connect**, choose **Sign in with Pax8**. You are redirected to the Pax8 login page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorize Access">
    Sign in to Pax8, complete any MFA prompt, and approve the authorization request. Pax8 then redirects you back to the connection page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connection Confirmed">
    Back on the connection page, **Connect** shows that your Pax8 account is connected. MSPortal begins fetching your Pax8 companies in the background.

    If you need to switch to a different Pax8 account later, use **Reconnect** on **Connect**; it forces the Pax8 login screen so you can choose another account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Choose data

Open **Choose data** to choose which optional Pax8 data to sync.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Invoice Sync">
    Under **What gets synced?**, turn on **Pax8 Invoices** if you want your Pax8 invoices on the [Invoices page](/user-guides/invoices/view-pax8-invoices), broken down by mapped company with line items and upcoming draft charges. This setting is optional and off by default.

    The switch saves as soon as you change it. When you turn it on, the first invoice sync starts right away. Check the Invoices page after that sync finishes; the saved switch alone does not mean invoices have arrived.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sync Subscriptions">
    Choose **Sync Subscriptions** to pull active licenses from Pax8 into the Storefront module. Only matched companies have their subscriptions imported. The confirmation means the sync has started, not that every subscription and product has arrived.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Import or match companies

Open **Import or match companies** to match Pax8 companies to your MSPortal companies. This determines which subscriptions and usage data belong to which company.

- After connecting, MSPortal fetches your Pax8 companies automatically, typically in under a minute. If the list stays empty, start a sync from the empty list.
- Review the **Suggested** matches and choose **Accept**, or use **Accept N strong matches** to accept every suggestion at 90% confidence or more. Suggestions are not saved until you accept them.
- In the **Import** view, pick the correct MSPortal company from **Choose company…**, or use **Import company** to create one. **Import companies** creates several at once; an existing company with the same name is matched instead of duplicated.
- Use **Ignore** for a Pax8 company you don't want to match, and **Restore** to bring it back.

Matches, imports and **Remove match** save immediately. Only matched companies have their subscriptions imported. For the shared controls, see [Map and import companies](/user-guides/integrations/import-companies).

### Review

Open **Review** and choose **Finish setup** once the required tasks are complete. Background syncs keep running after setup finishes. You can access the Storefront module anytime from the sidebar.

## What Gets Synced

Pax8 integration syncs the following data types to MSPortal:

### Companies

Your Pax8 company list, used for matching Pax8 companies to MSPortal companies.

### Products

The Pax8 product catalog, which powers the Storefront catalog and the products your clients can browse and request.

### Subscriptions

Active subscriptions and license quantities for mapped companies, shown in the Storefront's Subscriptions section and used by the Licensing module's approval and self-service workflows.

### Azure Usage

Azure consumption data by company, shown in the Storefront's Azure Usage section.

### Invoices

Pax8 invoices for mapped companies, with line items and upcoming draft charges, shown on the [Invoices page](/user-guides/invoices/view-pax8-invoices). Invoices sync only when **Pax8 Invoices** is turned on in **Choose data**.

<Note>
  For how to use this data day to day, see the [Storefront guide](/user-guides/storefront/index) and the [Licensing module](/user-guides/licensing/index), which covers approval workflows, catalog visibility, and client self-service. Connecting Pax8 does not turn on client purchasing; configure catalog visibility, permissions and approval rules there.
</Note>

## Synchronization Schedule

After initial setup, data syncs automatically:

| Data Type | Sync Frequency |
|-----------|----------------|
| Companies | Daily (06:00 UTC) |
| Products | Every 7 days (06:00 UTC run) |
| Subscriptions | Daily (06:00 UTC) |
| Azure Usage | Daily (06:00 UTC) |
| Invoices | Daily (06:00 UTC), when **Pax8 Invoices** is on |

All Pax8 data types run in a single daily sync at 06:00 UTC. Products refresh every 7 days after the first sync. Data types sync sequentially for each integration to keep the Pax8 connection stable.

<Note>
You can start a sync at any time with **Run sync** on the connection page, or with **Sync Subscriptions** in **Choose data**.
</Note>

## Managing Your Integration

After setup, open the Pax8 connection from **Settings > Integrations**. The **Manage** group in the **Setup guide** holds **Overview** and **Sync activity** for connection status and background results.

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Run sync** | Start a full Pax8 sync now |
| **Reconnect** | Re-run the OAuth flow on **Connect**, with the option to switch Pax8 accounts |
| **Choose data** | Change invoice sync |
| **Import or match companies** | Adjust company matches |
| **More actions › Disconnect…** | Remove the connection |

### Reconnecting Your Account

If your Pax8 connection expires or you need to switch accounts:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the connection">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations** and open your Pax8 connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconnect">
    On **Connect**, choose **Reconnect** and sign in to Pax8 again. Reconnecting is a fresh authorization, so check that you used the intended account and review your company matches afterward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Disconnecting the Integration

Open **More actions** on the connection page and choose **Disconnect…**, then confirm. This removes the stored OAuth tokens, the company matches and the data synced from Pax8 (subscriptions, products, companies). Check that you selected the right connection before confirming. Disconnecting in MSPortal does not cancel any subscriptions in Pax8.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Authorization fails | Ensure you have admin access to your Pax8 account, complete its sign-in, MFA and consent, then retry **Sign in with Pax8** |
| No companies to match | The list refreshes automatically after connecting; if it stays empty, start a sync from the empty list or choose **Run sync** |
| Subscriptions missing in Storefront | Ensure the company is matched; only matched companies have subscriptions imported. Check **Sync activity** for the last subscription sync |
| Connection stops syncing | Open the connection and choose **Reconnect** on **Connect** to re-authorize |
| Invoices missing | Confirm **Pax8 Invoices** is on in **Choose data**, the company is matched, and the invoice sync has finished |
| Stale data | Data refreshes daily; use **Run sync** for an immediate refresh |

## Security & Privacy

- **Token Storage**: OAuth tokens are encrypted in the vault; your Pax8 password is never stored
- **Data Isolation**: All synced data is tenant-isolated and only visible to your organization
- **Protected Authorization**: The OAuth flow uses signed state tokens to prevent forged connection requests
