Connect your Pax8 account to bring distributor data into MSPortal. This is an MSP staff setup task; Company users use the access and purchasing rules their MSP provides.

## Before you start

Use an MSP account with integration management permission and a Pax8 account authorized to approve the connection. Have your Pax8 sign-in and MFA ready. Open **Settings > Integrations** and select the Pax8 connection you intend to configure, or start a new one.

## Connect and choose sync settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect to Pax8">
    On **Connect**, click **Connect to Pax8**. Sign in and approve the requested access on Pax8's page. Return to the setup wizard after authorization. Initial company discovery runs in the background.

    <Frame caption="MSP setup example; no credentials entered.">
      <img src="/images/product/pax8-oauth-connect.png" alt="Pax8 OAuth connection card with Connect to Pax8 button." width="1024" height="380" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose invoice synchronization">
    Click **Next** to open **Sync Settings**. **Pax8 Invoices** is optional. Changing its switch saves immediately; enabling it also requests the first invoice sync. Check the Invoices page after that sync rather than treating the saved switch as proof that invoices arrived.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map companies">
    On **Map Companies**, use **Quick Match** to propose links or choose an MSPortal company manually. Review every proposal. **Import Selected** saves pending mappings; **Next** also saves them before advancing and requests a data sync. Save at least one mapping before continuing through the normal setup sequence.

    **Create new company** creates the MSPortal company immediately, but its provider link is still pending until imported. Removing an existing link saves immediately. For the shared controls, see [company imports](/user-guides/integrations/import-companies).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the imported data">
    On **Complete**, **Sync Subscriptions** requests a background sync. **Sync started** acknowledges the request; it does not confirm that all subscriptions or products were imported. Use **View Storefront** to check subscriptions, then **Complete Setup** to leave the wizard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check the result

Confirm the intended companies are mapped and inspect their subscriptions in [Storefront](/user-guides/storefront/index). Products and Azure usage depend on available provider data; invoices additionally require the saved invoice-sync setting. Client catalog visibility, approvals and purchasing rules are separate from connecting the distributor.

Sync runs in the background. Check the latest successful data and errors for the specific connection rather than relying on a fixed completion time or the setup step's green indicator.

## Reconnect or remove a connection

Open the intended connection and use **Reconnect** on **Connect** to repeat Pax8 authorization, including choosing another account when needed. Recheck the company mappings afterward. The connected indicator describes the saved connection, not a fresh API test.

The wizard's **Delete** action requires confirmation and removes the MSPortal integration and associated synchronized data. It is not a cancellation of subscriptions in Pax8. Review which connection you selected before confirming.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Authorization does not finish | Confirm your Pax8 access and complete its sign-in/MFA/consent, then return to MSPortal. |
| No companies are listed | Allow discovery to finish; use **Sync Companies** if a new fetch is needed, then check for errors. |
| A subscription is missing | Confirm the source account, saved company mapping and successful subscription sync. |
| Invoice switch saved but nothing appeared | Check invoice sync outcome, source invoices and mapped company coverage. |

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Does Pax8 setup require an API key?">

No. Use **Connect to Pax8** and complete Pax8 OAuth authorization.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I enable Pax8 invoices?">

Open **Sync Settings** and enable **Pax8 Invoices**. The switch saves immediately and requests invoice synchronization.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Quick Match save Pax8 company links?">

It proposes pending links. Review and save them with **Import Selected** or **Next**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does creating a company also finish its Pax8 mapping?">

The company is created immediately; its pending provider link still needs to be saved.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Sync started mean Pax8 subscriptions are ready?">

No. Inspect the background result and actual Storefront subscription data.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Pax8 setup enable client purchasing automatically?">

No. Configure catalog visibility, permissions and purchasing or approval rules separately.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is Reconnect a fresh authorization?">

Yes. It starts Pax8 authorization again. Recheck the intended account and mappings afterward.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does deleting Pax8 in MSPortal cancel subscriptions?">

No. It removes the MSPortal connection and associated synchronized data; subscription cancellation is a separate provider operation.

</Accordion>
