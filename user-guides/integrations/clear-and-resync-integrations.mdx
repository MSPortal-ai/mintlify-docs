This guide explains how to resynchronize data between MSPortal and your PSA system when you need to refresh ticket data or resolve synchronization issues.

## Overview

The Clear and Resync feature allows you to:
- Perform a quick synchronization of recent changes
- Complete a full data refresh by clearing and reimporting all tickets
- Resolve synchronization conflicts or data inconsistencies
- Apply configuration changes that require a full resync

## When to Use Clear and Resync

### Quick Sync
Use Quick Sync when:
- Testing new configuration changes
- Updating recently modified tickets
- Resolving minor sync delays
- Refreshing data after brief connection issues

### Clear & Resync
Use Clear & Resync when:
- Initial integration setup is complete
- Major configuration changes have been made
- Data inconsistencies need to be resolved
- Switching between PSA systems
- Recovering from extended sync failures

## Prerequisites

- PSA integration must be configured and enabled
- You must have permission to manage integrations
- Ensure no critical ticket updates are in progress
- Back up any local ticket data if needed

## Steps to Resync

### 1. Navigate to Integration Settings

Go to [https://app.msportal.ai/settings/integrations](https://app.msportal.ai/settings/integrations)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-09-04/34208b96-e8a0-41b7-9a44-7d101fd5d6be/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=130,121&br_px=1850,1083&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)

### 2. Access Ticket Settings

Click **Configure** on your PSA integration to open its setup wizard. Then click the **Configure** step in the wizard's step bar. The **Ticket Settings** card is at the top of that step.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-09-04/34208b96-e8a0-41b7-9a44-7d101fd5d6be/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=260,173&br_px=1980,1134&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=665,277)

### 3. Locate Sync Actions

Scroll down to the **Sync Actions** section, below the Resolution Status Stepper. Here you'll find two synchronization options:

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-09-04/8de8e81e-c430-4df4-83c9-57c2590efb4e/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=260,179&br_px=1980,1140&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=662,276)

### 4. Choose Sync Method

#### Option A: Quick Sync
- Click the **Quick Sync** button
- Synchronizes only recent changes
- Preserves existing data

#### Option B: Clear & Resync
- Click the **Clear & Resync** button
- What happens next depends on your PSA:

| PSA | What Clear & Resync does |
|-----|--------------------------|
| ConnectWise Manage and Autotask PSA | Deletes this integration's synced tickets from MSPortal, then reimports all tickets from the PSA |
| Halo PSA | Runs a full ticket resync that reads the last 365 days of tickets from Halo again |

> **Warning:** Clear & Resync starts as soon as you click it. There is no confirmation dialog. On ConnectWise and Autotask it deletes all local ticket data for the integration and reimports everything from your PSA. Any unsynchronized local changes will be lost. Tickets in the PSA itself are never deleted.

### 5. Wait for Completion

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-09-04/ae262903-5a40-4d3d-9f75-879286501b65/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=260,243&br_px=1980,1205&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=545,394)

While the sync runs, the button shows a spinner, and a toast confirms the sync has been triggered. The sync continues in the background; refresh the Tickets page after a few minutes to see the reimported data.

## What Gets Synchronized

### Ticket Data
- Ticket details (title, description, status)
- Priority and categories
- Assigned technicians
- Company and contact associations
- Custom fields (if configured)
- Ticket notes

### Related Data
Quick Sync and Clear & Resync only cover tickets. Companies, contacts and time entries sync on their own schedules. To refresh boards, statuses, priorities and types, use **Sync PSA Data** on the integration instead. See [ConnectWise PSA Settings Resync](/user-guides/integrations/connectwise-resync-psa-settings).

## Post-Sync Verification

After synchronization completes:

1. **Check Ticket Count:** Verify the expected number of tickets appears
2. **Review Recent Tickets:** Confirm latest tickets are present
3. **Test Updates:** Make a small change to a test ticket to verify two-way sync
4. **Check Mappings:** Ensure status and priority mappings are correct
5. **Verify Permissions:** Confirm users can access appropriate tickets

## Troubleshooting

### Sync Takes Too Long
- Check your internet connection
- Verify PSA system is responsive
- Consider using Quick Sync for testing
- Contact support if sync exceeds 30 minutes

### Missing Tickets After Sync
- Verify board, queue or team selection in the wizard's **Sync** step
- Check excluded status configurations
- Ensure date range settings are correct
- Review PSA permissions

### Sync Fails to Complete
- Check PSA API credentials
- Verify integration is enabled
- Review error logs for specific issues
- Try Quick Sync before Clear & Resync

### Data Inconsistencies
- Perform a Clear & Resync
- Check field mapping configurations
- Verify time zone settings match
- Review custom field mappings

## Best Practices

- **Schedule Full Syncs:** Perform during off-hours to minimize disruption
- **Test First:** Use Quick Sync to test configuration changes
- **Document Changes:** Note what prompted the resync for future reference
- **Monitor First Sync:** Stay available during initial synchronization
- **Regular Maintenance:** Schedule periodic full syncs (monthly/quarterly)

## Performance Considerations

### Quick Sync Performance
- Minimal system impact
- Can be run during business hours

### Clear & Resync Performance
- Duration depends on ticket volume
- Best performed during maintenance windows
