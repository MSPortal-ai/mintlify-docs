Connect Sherweb to synchronize customers, subscriptions and per-customer catalogs. This beta integration is configured by MSP staff with integration management permission. Company users do not administer the distributor connection.

## Prepare the credentials

Open the **Partner Portal** link shown on Sherweb's Connect step. It opens Sherweb's **Security > APIs** page. Obtain the **Client ID**, **Client Secret** and **Subscription Key** for the Service Provider API. Keep secret values private.

## Connect and map customers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sherweb setup">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations** and select the intended Sherweb connection or start a new one. Setup has **Connect**, **Map Customers** and **Complete** steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and save">
    Enter all three credential values. **Test connection** checks them; **Save and connect** creates the connection. A passing test by itself does not save a new connection. Customer discovery starts in the background after connecting.

    <Frame caption="MSP setup example; no credentials entered.">
      <img src="/images/product/sherweb-credential-fields.png" alt="Blank Sherweb Client ID, Client Secret and Subscription Key fields." width="1020" height="424" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save customer mappings">
    Review **Quick Match** proposals or choose an MSPortal company for each customer. **Import Selected** saves pending links; **Next** also saves pending links before advancing and requests synchronization. The normal flow requires at least one saved mapping.

    **Create new company** immediately creates an MSPortal company and stages its link. Save that pending link too. Removing an existing mapping saves immediately. See [company imports](/user-guides/integrations/import-companies) for the shared controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check subscriptions and catalogs">
    On **Complete**, use **Run full sync** when a new sync is needed, then inspect [Storefront](/user-guides/storefront/index). **Complete Setup** leaves the wizard; it does not wait for every provider request to finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Customers must be mapped for their subscriptions and customer-specific catalog data to be useful in the correct company context. Check the selected distributor and company when comparing data. Catalog availability and prices can differ between customers; connecting Sherweb does not configure client ordering rules.

## Change or test saved credentials

Reopen the intended connection's **Connect** step. All three fields start blank so you can keep existing values.

- To test the saved credentials, leave **all three** fields blank and click **Test connection**.
- To test replacement credentials before saving, fill **all three** fields. A partially entered replacement cannot be tested as a mixed set.
- To replace only one or two values, enter those values and click **Update credentials**. Blank fields retain their saved values. The update validates the combined credentials before saving.

After updating, the form clears. Use **Test connection** with the blank form to check the saved set. Testing alone does not persist replacements.

## Remove a connection

Use **Delete** in the wizard footer and review the confirmation. This removes the MSPortal connection and associated synchronized data. It does not cancel Sherweb subscriptions or revoke the API application in Sherweb.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Test asks you to fill or clear fields | Either enter all three replacements or clear all three to test the stored set. |
| Customers remain empty | Allow discovery to finish, then use **Sync Customers** and inspect the connection error if needed. |
| Catalog or subscriptions are missing | Check the saved customer mapping, chosen distributor and successful sync. |
| Connection saved but data is stale | A save is not background-sync completion. Check the next successful result. |

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Which Sherweb credentials are required for a new connection?">

Client ID, Client Secret and Subscription Key are all required.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I test saved Sherweb credentials?">

Leave all three fields blank on the existing connection and click **Test connection**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I test a partial Sherweb credential replacement?">

No. Fill all three replacement fields to test them, or clear all three to test the saved set.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I update only the Sherweb subscription key?">

Yes. Enter that value and use **Update credentials**; blank fields keep their stored values. The update validates the combined set.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does testing Sherweb save the connection?">

No. Use **Save and connect** for a new connection or **Update credentials** for replacements.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are new Sherweb company mappings saved immediately?">

Manual and Quick Match links are pending until **Import Selected** or **Next** saves them. Unlinking an existing mapping saves immediately.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is a Sherweb customer catalog the same for every company?">

No. Catalogs are customer-specific. Check the intended company and distributor.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Complete Setup prove Sherweb sync finished?">

No. Check the actual subscriptions, catalog and sync outcome after the background work runs.

</Accordion>
