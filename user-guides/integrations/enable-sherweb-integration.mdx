This guide walks you through connecting your Sherweb distributor account to MSPortal.ai using our streamlined setup wizard. The Sherweb integration is currently in Beta.

## Overview

Sherweb is a cloud solutions distributor for MSPs. By integrating Sherweb with MSPortal.ai, you can:

- View every client's Sherweb subscriptions alongside the rest of their data
- Sync each customer's offer catalog, including pricing, from Sherweb
- Power the Storefront with a curated, client-facing product catalog
- Keep subscription and catalog data refreshed automatically every day

## Prerequisites

- Active Sherweb partner account with access to the Sherweb Partner Portal (cumulus.sherweb.com)
- MSPortal.ai account with integration management permissions
- Sherweb API credentials: a Client ID, Client Secret, and Subscription Key

## Part 1: Generate Sherweb API Credentials

Before connecting to MSPortal.ai, you need API credentials from the Sherweb Partner Portal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log into the Sherweb Partner Portal">
    Access the Sherweb Partner Portal at [cumulus.sherweb.com](https://cumulus.sherweb.com).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to API Management">
    Go to **Security > APIs** in the Partner Portal. The Connect step of the MSPortal wizard also links straight to this page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Service Provider API Credentials">
    Create credentials for the **Service Provider API**. Sherweb issues three values that MSPortal needs:

    | Value | Purpose |
    |-------|---------|
    | **Client ID** | Identifies your API client |
    | **Client Secret** | Authenticates your API client |
    | **Subscription Key** | The `Ocp-Apim-Subscription-Key` value that authorizes access to the Service Provider API |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy All Three Values">
    Copy the Client ID, Client Secret, and Subscription Key.

    <Warning>
      **Store the Client Secret securely.** You may not be able to view it again after leaving the page. If you lose it, generate new credentials in the Partner Portal.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2: MSPortal Setup Wizard

MSPortal.ai provides a guided 3-step wizard to connect and configure your Sherweb integration: **Connect**, **Map Customers**, and **Complete**.

### Starting the Wizard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Integrations">
    In MSPortal.ai, go to **Settings > Integrations**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find Sherweb">
    Find **Sherweb** in the list of available integrations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start Setup">
    Click **Connect** to launch the setup wizard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

### Step 1: Connect

Enter your Sherweb API credentials to establish the connection. All three values are required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Credentials">
    Fill in the three fields:

    - **Client ID**
    - **Client Secret**
    - **Subscription Key**

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/sherweb-credential-fields.png" alt="Sherweb Connect step with Client ID, Client Secret and Subscription Key fields" width="1020" height="424" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
      Your Sherweb credentials are stored securely using Supabase Vault encryption. They are never exposed in logs or API responses.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test Connection">
    Click **Test connection** to verify your credentials.

    If the test fails:
    - Verify you copied each value in full, without extra spaces
    - Confirm the values came from **Security > APIs** in the Sherweb Partner Portal
    - Ensure the credentials have not been revoked
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and Connect">
    Click **Save and connect** to create the integration. A passing test does not save anything on its own.

    MSPortal immediately starts fetching your Sherweb customer list in the background, so it is ready for the next step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

### Step 2: Map Customers

Map Sherweb customers to your MSPortal companies. This determines which data syncs to which company.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for Customers to Appear">
    The customer list fills in automatically as the initial sync completes; the page checks for new customers every few seconds. If the list stays empty, click **Sync Customers** to start the sync again.

    <Info>
      The initial customer sync usually finishes within a couple of minutes.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use Quick Match">
    Click **Quick Match** to automatically match Sherweb customers to MSPortal companies based on name similarity. Review the suggested matches and apply the ones that look right.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Manual Mapping">
    For customers that were not automatically matched:

    1. Find the customer in the list
    2. Click the **Select company** dropdown in the "Mapped" column
    3. Search for and select the correct MSPortal company
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create New Companies">
    If a Sherweb customer does not have a corresponding MSPortal company, use **Create new company** to add one. New companies are automatically mapped to their Sherweb customers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply and Proceed">
    Click **Import Selected** to apply your pending mappings, then click **Next**. Any remaining pending mappings are saved automatically when you advance.

    <Warning>
      You must map at least one customer before proceeding. Subscriptions and catalog data are only synced for mapped customers.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When you leave the Map Customers step, MSPortal automatically starts a sync so newly mapped customers get their subscriptions and catalog data right away.
</Note>

---

### Step 3: Complete

Confirm the integration and run your first full sync.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run a Full Sync">
    Click **Run full sync** to pull your Sherweb customers, then the subscriptions and offer catalog for every mapped customer.

    The sync runs in the background. You can click **View company mapping** to review your customer-to-company links while it runs.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete Setup">
    Click **Complete setup** to finish the wizard and return to the Integrations page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Gets Synced

The Sherweb integration syncs the following data:

### Customers

Every customer under your Sherweb account, ready to be mapped to MSPortal companies.

### Subscriptions

Subscription details and pricing for each **mapped** customer, including quantities, status, and billing information. Subscriptions appear in the [Storefront](/user-guides/storefront/index) under Subscriptions.

### Catalog Items

Each mapped customer's offer catalog from Sherweb, including product names, SKUs, and pricing. You control which catalog items clients can see: go to **Settings > Storefront** to mark Sherweb catalog items as **Visible** or **Featured**.

<Note>
  Sherweb subscriptions and catalogs are customer-scoped, so only data for customers mapped to an MSPortal company is stored.
</Note>

## Synchronization Schedule

After initial setup, data syncs automatically:

| Data Type | Sync Frequency |
|-----------|----------------|
| Customers | Daily at 07:00 UTC |
| Subscriptions | Daily at 07:00 UTC |
| Catalog Items | Daily at 07:00 UTC |

The daily sync runs customers first, then subscriptions, then catalogs, so subscription and catalog data always reflects your latest customer mappings.

<Note>
You can trigger a manual sync at any time: open the Sherweb wizard from **Settings > Integrations** and click **Run full sync** on the Complete step, or click **Resync** on the Sherweb row in the integrations list.
</Note>

## Managing Your Integration

After setup, manage your integration from **Settings > Integrations**:

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Manage** | Re-open the wizard to rotate credentials or adjust customer mappings |
| **Test connection** | On the Connect step, leave all three fields blank and click **Test connection** to verify your saved credentials |
| **Resync** | Trigger a full data refresh |
| **Delete** | Remove the integration (available in the wizard footer) |

### Rotating Your Credentials

If you need to update your Sherweb API credentials:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integration Settings">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations** and click **Manage** on your Sherweb connection to open the wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter New Values">
    On the Connect step, enter only the values you want to change. Leave a field blank to keep its current value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the New Values (Optional)">
    To test replacement credentials before saving, fill in **all three** fields and click **Test connection**. A partial set cannot be tested.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Credentials">
    Click **Update credentials** to save. MSPortal checks the combined credentials before saving them, then clears the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Saved Credentials">
    With all three fields blank, click **Test connection** to check the saved set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Managing Customer Mappings

Manage your Sherweb customer-to-company mappings at any time:

- Re-open the wizard and go to the **Map Customers** step, or
- Go to **Settings > Integrations > Company Mapping**

### Deleting the Integration

Click **Delete** in the wizard footer. This removes the integration, your stored credentials, and previously synced customer records. It does not cancel any subscriptions in Sherweb. You can reconnect at any time.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Connection test fails | Verify all three values (Client ID, Client Secret, Subscription Key) are correct and copied in full |
| Test asks you to fill in or clear the fields | Fill in all three fields to test new credentials, or clear all three to test the saved ones |
| No customers appear on the Map step | The initial sync can take a few minutes; the list refreshes automatically. Click "Sync Customers" to retry |
| Subscriptions or catalog missing | Only mapped customers sync; map the customer to a company, then run a full sync |
| Catalog items not visible to clients | Check item visibility under Settings > Storefront |
| Stale data | Run a full sync from the wizard's Complete step, or wait for the daily 07:00 UTC sync |

---

## Security & Privacy

- **Credential Storage**: Your Sherweb Client ID, Client Secret, and Subscription Key are encrypted using Supabase Vault and never exposed in logs or responses
- **Data Isolation**: All synced data is tenant-isolated and only visible to your organization
- **Read-Only Access**: MSPortal only reads data from Sherweb; it never modifies your Sherweb subscriptions or places orders
