This guide walks you through connecting your DNSimple account to MSPortal.ai using our streamlined setup wizard.

## Overview

DNSimple is a domain registration and DNS management service. By integrating DNSimple with MSPortal.ai, you can:

- Keep an up-to-date inventory of every domain registered or hosted in your DNSimple account
- Map each domain to the MSPortal company it belongs to
- Track expiration dates, auto-renew status, and registration details
- Show domain renewals in client reports with the DNSimple Domains reporting block
- Build automation rules that alert you before a domain expires

## Prerequisites

- Active DNSimple account
- DNSimple Account API token
- MSPortal.ai account with integration management permissions

## Part 1: Generate a DNSimple API Token

Before connecting to MSPortal.ai, you need an Account API token from DNSimple.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log into DNSimple">
    Access your account at [dnsimple.com](https://dnsimple.com).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Access Tokens">
    Go to your account page and open **Account > Access Tokens**.

    <Tip>
      Use an **account** token, not a user token. Account-scoped tokens are recommended, with one account per integration. If you manage multiple DNSimple accounts, connect the account that holds the domains you want to track.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the Token">
    Click to add a new access token and give it a recognizable name, such as `MSPortal Integration`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy the Token">
    Copy the token immediately.

    <Warning>
      **Store the token securely!** DNSimple only shows it once. If you lose it, create a new token.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2: MSPortal Setup Wizard

MSPortal.ai provides a guided 3-step wizard to connect DNSimple and map your domains.

### Starting the Wizard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Integrations">
    In MSPortal.ai, go to **Settings > Integrations**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find DNSimple">
    Locate the **DNSimple** row in the list of available integrations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start Setup">
    Click **Connect** to launch the setup wizard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

### Step 1: Connect

Enter your DNSimple Account API token to establish the connection.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the Token">
    Paste your token into the **Account API Token** field. Use the eye icon to show or hide the value while you paste.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/dnsimple-setup-controls.png" alt="Blank DNSimple account-token form with separate Test connection and Save and connect controls." width="1152" height="564" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
      Your token is stored securely using Supabase Vault encryption. It is never exposed in logs or API responses.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test the Connection">
    Click **Test connection**.

    When the test passes, you'll see a "Connection works" confirmation showing the authenticated account email and account number.

    If the test fails:
    - Verify you copied the full token without extra spaces
    - Confirm the token is an account token and has not been revoked
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and Connect">
    Click **Save and connect** to create the integration. MSPortal checks the token again, saves it, and starts an initial domain sync in the background.

    <Note>
      **Test connection** does not save anything. Only **Save and connect** creates the integration.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Continue to Mapping">
    Click **Next** to open the **Map Domains** step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

### Step 2: Map Domains

Map DNSimple domains to your MSPortal companies. Mapped domains are what power reporting and renewal alerts for each client.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for Domains to Appear">
    The initial sync usually completes in under a minute. If no domains are listed yet, click **Sync Domains** to fetch them from your DNSimple account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use Quick Match">
    Click **Quick Match** to automatically match domains to MSPortal companies based on name similarity. Review the suggested matches and adjust as needed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Map Manually">
    For domains that were not matched automatically:

    1. Find the domain in the list
    2. Click the **Select company...** dropdown in the **Mapped** column
    3. Search for and select the correct MSPortal company

    <Note>
      Domains can only be linked to companies that already exist. The mapping step does not create new companies, because a domain represents an asset of a client, not the client itself.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ignore Domains You Don't Need">
    Click **Ignore** on an unmapped domain to remove it from the mapping backlog. Click **Restore** to bring it back. Both save immediately. Ignoring a domain does not delete it or change anything in DNSimple.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply Your Mappings">
    New matches show as **Pending** until you save them. Click **Import Selected** to save, then click **Next** to continue. **Next** also saves any pending mappings and starts another domain sync.

    Removing a saved mapping takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

### Step 3: Complete

Confirm the integration and run the first full sync.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run a Full Sync">
    Click **Run full sync** to refresh the domain inventory. A full sync also purges any domains that have been transferred out of your DNSimple account since the last sync.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete Setup">
    Click **Complete setup** to finish the wizard and return to the Integrations page.

    You can revisit your mappings at any time under **Settings > Integrations > Company Mapping**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Gets Synced

The DNSimple integration keeps one record per domain in your account:

- Domain name
- State: registered, or hosted (DNS only)
- Expiration date (registered domains)
- Registration date
- Auto-renew status
- Private WHOIS status

Domains mapped to a company are also recorded as that company's domains, and appear in:

- The **DNSimple Domains** reporting block, showing renewal dates and auto-renew status
- Automation rules built on the **DNSimple Domain** entity, for example an alert 30 days before a domain expires

---

## Synchronization Schedule

After initial setup, data syncs automatically:

| Data Type | Sync Frequency |
|-----------|----------------|
| Domains | Daily at 6:30 AM UTC |

The daily sync refreshes every domain's details and removes domains that no longer exist in your DNSimple account.

<Note>
You can trigger a manual sync at any time from the wizard's Complete step with **Run full sync**, which is useful right after registering or transferring domains in DNSimple.
</Note>

## Managing Your Integration

After setup, manage your integration from **Settings > Integrations**:

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Manage** | Re-open the wizard to rotate the token, adjust mappings, or run a sync |
| **Company Mapping** | Manage domain-to-company links under Settings > Integrations > Company Mapping |
| **Delete** | Remove the integration (available in the wizard footer); stored credentials and synced domain records are deleted. Domains and DNS records in DNSimple are not touched |

### Rotating Your API Token

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Integration">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations** and click **Manage** on the DNSimple row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the New Token">
    On the Connect step, paste the new token. Leaving the field blank keeps the current token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and Update">
    Click **Test connection** to verify, then **Update credentials** to save. Clicking **Next** does not save a new token.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Use a token for the same DNSimple account. A token for a different account changes which domains sync.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Connection test fails | Verify the token is an account token, copied fully, and not revoked in DNSimple |
| No domains appear on the Map step | Click **Sync Domains** and wait a moment; the inventory sync usually completes in under a minute |
| A domain is missing an expiration date | Hosted (DNS-only) domains have no registration to expire; only registered domains carry expiration dates |
| Renewal data not showing for a client | Confirm the domain is mapped to that company on the Map step or the Company Mapping page |
| A transferred-out domain still shows | Run a full sync; it purges domains that left your DNSimple account |

---

## Security & Privacy

- **Token Storage**: Your DNSimple token is encrypted using Supabase Vault and never exposed in logs or responses
- **Data Isolation**: All synced data is tenant-isolated and only visible to your organization
- **Read-Only Access**: MSPortal only reads domain information; it never changes your DNSimple domains or DNS records
