Warranty writeback copies the warranty dates MSPortal tracks for your devices into **Hudu assets** or **IT Glue flexible assets**. MSP admins turn it on from the Hudu or IT Glue setup. It writes to your documentation platform, so turn it on only when you want MSPortal's dates to update those records.

<Note>
For IT Glue, configurations are the usual target and need no field mapping. See [Warranty writeback to IT Glue configurations](/user-guides/integrations/itglue-configuration-warranty-writeback). This guide covers Hudu assets and IT Glue flexible assets.
</Note>

## Before you start

- A connected Hudu or IT Glue integration, with companies mapped to MSPortal companies.
- An API key that can update the assets you want to change.
- Synchronized Hudu asset layouts or IT Glue flexible asset types that have a serial number field and a warranty date field.
- Devices in MSPortal with a serial number and a successful warranty check that found an expiration date. See [Understand device details](/user-guides/devices/understand-device-details).

## How devices are matched

MSPortal matches a device to an asset by serial number only. The serial number must be unique inside the mapped company and the asset type you choose.

- It never matches by device name or hostname.
- Assets with a missing, placeholder or duplicate serial number are skipped.
- An optional start date is written only when the device has one.
- A missing date in MSPortal never erases a date already in your documentation platform.

## Turn on warranty writeback

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the warranty settings">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations** and select **Configure** on your Hudu or IT Glue integration. In Hudu, open the **Documentation** step. In IT Glue, open **Sync & Sharing** and expand **Flexible assets (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map device types to asset types">
    Under **Device and warranty field mappings**, choose a Hudu asset layout or IT Glue flexible asset type for each MSPortal device type you want to write. Leave a device type on **Not mapped** to exclude it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the fields">
    For each mapped device type, choose a **Serial number field** and a **Warranty expiration field**. Both are required. Hudu also offers **Primary serial** for the serial number. **Warranty start field** is optional and must be a different field from the expiration field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the switch on">
    Turn on **Enable warranty writeback**. In IT Glue the switch is named **Write warranty dates to flexible assets**. You need at least one complete mapping to turn it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save warranty settings**. The switches and selectors do not take effect until the save succeeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Saving with writeback on starts an asset sync and a writeback right away, and it also turns on synchronization for the mapped asset types. Check your content sync and client visibility settings after you save.
</Warning>

If several device types use the same asset type, they share the same serial and warranty fields. Changing a field on one row changes the others, so review every row before you save.

## Check the result

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for the run to finish">
    The first writeback starts when you save. After that it runs daily while the switch is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a known asset">
    In Hudu or IT Glue, open an asset whose device has a warranty date in MSPortal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the dates">
    Check that the serial number, company and warranty dates match the device in MSPortal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Turn it off or change the scope

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the warranty settings">
    Open the same step you used to turn it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off or remove mappings">
    Turn off the switch to stop all future writes. To stop writing one device type, set it to **Not mapped**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save warranty settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Dates already written stay in Hudu or IT Glue. Turning writeback off does not roll them back. Saving a mapping also turns on sync for its asset type, and removing the mapping does not turn that sync off again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Save warranty settings** is disabled | Fill in the serial and expiration fields for every mapped device type, and use different fields for start and expiration. Enabling needs at least one mapping. |
| No asset types or fields in the list | Sync asset types from the section above first, then confirm the API key can read active layouts or types. |
| One asset never changes | Check that the device has a successful warranty check and an expiration date, a device type mapping, a mapped company, a unique serial number, and that the key can update the asset. |
| Dates are still old after saving | Wait for the background run to finish, then check the asset in Hudu or IT Glue. |
| Another row changed when you picked a field | Device types that share an asset type share its field choices. |
| Dates remain after turning it off | Turning writeback off stops future writes. It does not undo earlier ones. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the switch save right away?">
    No. The switch and mappings apply only after you select **Save warranty settings**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does writeback wait for the next daily run?">
    No. Saving with writeback on starts a sync and writeback immediately. It then runs daily.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it match by device name?">
    No. It uses a unique serial number inside the mapped company and asset type.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can IT Glue configurations get warranty dates?">
    Yes, and that is the usual choice. See [Warranty writeback to IT Glue configurations](/user-guides/integrations/itglue-configuration-warranty-writeback). Flexible asset mappings are optional.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which devices are written?">
    Devices with a successful warranty check and an expiration date, a mapped device type, a mapped company and a unique serial match.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does turning it off restore the old dates?">
    No. It stops future writes only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
