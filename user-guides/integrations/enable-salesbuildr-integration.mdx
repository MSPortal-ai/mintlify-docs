This guide walks you through connecting Salesbuildr to MSPortal.ai using the 3-step setup wizard.

## Overview

Salesbuildr is a quoting and sales platform for MSPs. By integrating Salesbuildr with MSPortal.ai, you can:

- Show Salesbuildr quotes, including line items and totals, on the MSPortal **Quotes** page for your team and for client portal users
- Map Salesbuildr companies to your MSPortal companies so each client only sees their own quotes
- Open any quote in the Salesbuildr customer quote portal, or download its PDF, straight from MSPortal
- Import Salesbuildr products into your budget catalog so budgets use your real sell prices and billing cadence

<Note>
Salesbuildr is in beta. If the integration is not available in **Settings > Integrations**, contact support@msportal.ai.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

- Salesbuildr administrator access, with the **Public API** feature enabled on your Salesbuildr account
- Your Salesbuildr account URL, which ends in `.salesbuildr.com` (for example `https://acme.salesbuildr.com`)
- Admin access to MSPortal.ai with the **manage_settings_integrations** permission
- Companies already set up in MSPortal (from your PSA integration or manually created)

## Part 1: Create an API Key in Salesbuildr

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open API settings">
    In Salesbuildr, go to **Settings > Integrations > API Key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the key">
    Create an API key named `MSPortal` and copy it to a secure location.

    <Warning>
      Treat this key like a password. Anyone with the key can read your Salesbuildr data.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the account URL">
    Note the `.salesbuildr.com` address you use to sign in. A custom quote portal domain does not serve the API and will not work here.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Part 2: MSPortal Setup Wizard

MSPortal provides a guided 3-step wizard to connect and configure your Salesbuildr integration.

### Step 1: Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the setup page">
    In MSPortal, go to **Settings > Integrations**, find **Salesbuildr**, and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the connection details">
    Enter the **Account URL** and the **API key**. You can paste the full URL from your browser; everything after the host name is ignored. Paste only the key value, without an `api-key:` prefix.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/salesbuildr-connection-fields.png" alt="Salesbuildr Connect step with Account URL and API key fields" width="1152" height="448" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Click **Test Connection**. MSPortal verifies the API key and reports the number of companies it can see. Testing does not save anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect Salesbuildr**. The API key is stored securely in Vault and MSPortal starts importing your Salesbuildr company list in the background.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2: Map

Link each Salesbuildr company to the matching MSPortal company.

- Use **Quick Match** to propose links by name, review the confidence scores, and apply the ones you agree with.
- Pick a company manually from the dropdown for anything Quick Match missed, or create a new MSPortal company from the Salesbuildr record.
- Click **Import Selected** to save pending mappings. **Next** also saves them before moving on.
- Only quotes for mapped companies are imported. Unmapped Salesbuildr companies are skipped until you map them.

Click **Next** when you are done. MSPortal queues a quote sync for the companies you just mapped. For more detail, see [Map Salesbuildr companies](/user-guides/integrations/map-salesbuildr-companies).

### Step 3: Complete

Click **Start quote sync** to pull quotes for the mapped companies now, then click **Open Quotes** to view them. **Complete Setup** also requests a quote sync before it closes the wizard. Quotes appear on the **Quotes** page as soon as the sync finishes.

## What Gets Synced

| Data | Schedule | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Companies | Nightly, plus after connect and from the mapping page | Source list for company mapping (customers, leads, and your own record; suppliers and manufacturers are skipped) |
| Quotes and line items | Every 4 hours, plus after mapping changes | Only for mapped companies; deleted quotes are removed on the next sync |
| Products | Nightly, plus from the Refresh action on the integration row | Available in **Budgets > Products > Import**; products removed in Salesbuildr stop being offered |

Each quote shows its number, title, status, the Salesbuildr contact and owner, and monthly, annual, and one-time totals calculated from its line items. **Open in Salesbuildr** opens the customer quote portal for that quote, and **Download PDF** fetches the current quote PDF from Salesbuildr.

## Using Salesbuildr Products in Budgets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the import dialog">
    Go to **Budgets**, open **Budgets Menu > Products**, and click **Import from Integrations**. Salesbuildr products appear alongside products from your PSA, with their name, SKU, category, vendor, and unit price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import or link">
    Select the products you want in your catalog and import them, or link a Salesbuildr product to a catalog product you already maintain. Imported products keep their billing cadence (monthly, annual, or one-time), setup price, unit, and product type from Salesbuildr.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to a budget">
    In the budget builder, the **Products and Services** panel lists your catalog. Adding a Salesbuildr product creates a line with the right recurrence; a product with a setup fee also adds a one-time setup line so onboarding costs land in the first month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing the Connection

- Click **Manage** on your Salesbuildr connection to test, rotate the API key, change the account URL, or adjust company mappings. Leave the API key blank to keep the stored key; the save button then reads **Save settings** instead of **Update credentials**. Save your Connect changes before you click **Next**, because **Next** does not save them.
- Mappings can also be managed on **Settings > Integrations > Company Mapping** alongside your other integrations. Unlinking a company removes its imported quotes; relinking moves them to the new company.
- To remove the integration, click **Delete** in the wizard footer and confirm. This deletes the stored connection and the imported Salesbuildr data from MSPortal. It does not change anything in Salesbuildr or delete the MSPortal companies you mapped to.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Account URL is rejected | Enter your `.salesbuildr.com` address. A custom quote portal domain does not serve the API. |
| API key is rejected | Confirm the **Public API** feature is enabled in Salesbuildr. Create a new key under **Settings > Integrations > API Key** and paste it without extra spaces, then click **Update credentials**. |
| A client sees no quotes | Confirm their Salesbuildr company is mapped to their MSPortal company and that a quote sync has run since the mapping was saved. |
| The integration shows as paused | The API key was revoked or Salesbuildr was unreachable for an extended period. Click **Manage**, enter a working key, and save to resume syncing. |
