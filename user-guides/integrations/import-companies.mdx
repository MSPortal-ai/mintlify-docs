After setting up an integration, importing companies is an essential next step to populate your MSPortal.ai instance with your customer data.

**Audience:** MSP staff with company-import access and a configured provider connection. Open a connection in **Settings > Integrations** for inline import, or use the separate **Settings > Companies > Import** screen. Available sources depend on the organization's integrations.

## Import or match companies inside an integration

Open a connection from **Settings > Integrations**, then select **Import or match companies**. The same task opens inline in Launch Center. The task label stays the same across providers; the workspace identifies the source records as companies, sites, organizations or the provider's equivalent.

1. The workspace opens on **Import**, showing records that are not yet linked, including records with suggested matches. Use **Suggested** when you want to review proposed links, or **Matched** and **Ignored** to revisit those records. Review the source records and search before importing. If a portal company already exists under a different name, choose that company or review and accept a suggested match.
2. Select **Import** beside a source record to open the import dialog with that record selected. For a batch, select **Import companies** and choose records in the dialog.
3. Review the names and selection count. The dialog loads unmatched records across provider pages for the current search, then displays up to 50 per page. Use **Select current page** for the displayed page or **Select all pages** for all records matching the dialog search. **Previous** and **Next** move between pages; **Clear** removes the selection. Records with suggested matches are included. Review the suggested destination before deciding whether to import or match an existing company.
4. Confirm with the dialog's **Import** button, which includes the selected company count (for example, **Import 3 companies**). Import creates missing portal companies and links the source records. Existing companies with the same name are matched instead of duplicated.
5. Read the result and verify the company links before importing users or devices. Importing or matching at least one company opens the related tasks for that company; you can leave other companies for later and return whenever you need more users or devices. For a partial result, review the remaining records and any companies already created before retrying.

Changing the dialog search returns to the first page and clears its all-pages selection. Individual and current-page selections are retained; a count identifies selected companies outside the search. Review that count before confirming.

Opening or closing the selection dialog does not import anything. Choosing an existing company, accepting a suggestion, unlinking and ignoring/restoring save when applied. Imported or matched records leave the waiting list; its count includes both suggested and unmatched records.

## Separate Companies import screen

The sections below describe **Settings > Companies > Import**. That separate workflow stages links and applies them through **Import Selected**; its selection and save behavior differs from the connection-page dialog above.

## Prerequisites

- You must have at least one PSA integration configured (ConnectWise Manage, Autotask, HaloPSA, or Syncro)
- You must have appropriate permissions to manage companies

## Steps to Import Companies

**Quick Match**, bulk **Assign**, filter counts and **Clear Filters** share one toolbar. Searching keeps the table header in place. The rows fill the dialog above its footer, and pagination stays visible when a search has no matches. On a phone, open **Filters** to search.

  <Step title="Access the Import Menu">
    1. On the Companies page, click the **Import** button
    2. The import menu lists every configured integration for your account. Choose **Import from** your PSA (for example, **Import from ConnectWise Manage**).
       - You'll see a list of all available companies from your PSA system
       - The list shows companies from the last sync with your PSA. If a company is missing, check the integration's sync status and company filters, then use the dialog's sync option to refresh the list.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Companies to Import">
    1. Review the list of available companies from your PSA
    2. Check the checkbox next to each company you want to import
       - You can select multiple companies at once
       - Use the search or filter options to find specific companies
    3. Instead of importing as new, you can also:
       - Link a PSA company to an existing MSPortal company using the **Select company...** dropdown in its row
       - Click **Quick Match** to automatically propose links to existing companies based on name similarity, then review and apply the matches
       - Choose **Create new company** in the dropdown to create and map a company on the spot

    <Note>
    **Create new company** saves the new company as soon as you click **Create & Map**. Its link to the PSA company is saved when you click **Import Selected**. Closing the dialog does not remove a company you already created.
    </Note>

  <Step title="Import Selected Companies">
    1. After selecting your desired companies, click the **Import Selected** button
       - The button shows the count of selected companies and pending links (for example, "Import Selected (2)")
    2. The import process will begin automatically

## Important Notes

- **Data Sync**: Imported companies maintain a connection to your PSA system for ongoing synchronization
- **Duplicate Prevention**: MSPortal.ai automatically prevents duplicate company imports. If a company already exists, link it with **Select company...** instead of creating a new one.
- **Bulk Operations**: You can import multiple companies at once to save time
- **Updates**: Company data can be refreshed from your PSA at any time using the sync features
- **Removing a link**: Removing a saved link with its unlink control takes effect immediately. Clearing a pending link only removes the unsaved suggestion.

## Troubleshooting

If you don't see any companies available for import:
1. Verify your integration is properly configured and connected
2. Check that you have the necessary permissions in both MSPortal.ai and your PSA system
3. Ensure your PSA contains active company records

If a company was created but its link failed, reuse that company when you retry. Pick it in the **Select company...** dropdown rather than creating another one.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does opening company import fetch every company live from the PSA?">
    No. It reads synchronized provider records; inspect sync status and source filters when data is missing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Create new company in the separate Companies import screen wait until Import Selected?">
    No. In that separate workflow, confirming creation saves the company immediately and stages its source link. The connection-page Import dialog instead imports and links its selected records when you confirm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does removing a saved company link wait for a final Save?">
    No. The saved-link removal action persists immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I create another company after its link fails?">
    No. Inspect and reuse the company that was already created before retrying the link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
