The Benji Pays integration can give your clients two things on the **Invoices** page. Set up either one, or both:

| Feature | What your clients see | What you copy from Benji Pays |
|---------|----------------------|-------------------------------|
| **Pay Now button** | A button on every unpaid invoice that opens a pre-filled Benji Pays checkout | One payment link, from the Benji Pays payment links page |
| **Billing portal sign-on** | An **Open billing portal** button that signs them in to Benji Pays with their MSPortal login | Five values from a Benji Pays **SSO Configuration**. No payment link is used. |

When you connect, you choose what clients get: **Pay Now button and billing portal sign-in**, **Pay Now button only**, or **Billing portal sign-in only**. You can turn either feature on or off later.

<Tip>
  **Which link do I copy?** For the Pay Now button, copy one **Default** link from the **PSA/ERP and other 3rd Party Payment Links** section, then swap in MSPortal's two placeholders ([Part 1](#part-1-pay-now-build-your-payment-link)). For billing portal sign-on, you don't copy any link from the payment links page. Those values come from **SSO Configuration** in Benji Pays ([Part 2](#part-2-billing-portal-sign-on-create-an-sso-configuration)).
</Tip>

## Prerequisites

- An active Benji Pays merchant account
- An MSPortal user with the **Manage Integrations** permission
- For the Pay Now button: at least one PSA integration (ConnectWise, Autotask, or Halo PSA) syncing invoices. Without invoices there is nowhere to show Pay Now buttons.
- For billing portal sign-on: each client's MSPortal email must belong to their Benji Pays billing account, because Benji Pays matches clients by email.

Do Part 1, Part 2, or both in Benji Pays, then connect in MSPortal (Part 3).

## Part 1 (Pay Now): Build your payment link

Skip this part if clients will get billing portal sign-on only.

Benji Pays doesn't show a link made for MSPortal. You copy a link made for a PSA and replace its two placeholders with MSPortal's.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment links page in Benji Pays">
    Sign in to Benji Pays and open the custom payment links page from **Settings**. Depending on your account it may be titled **QuickBooks Custom Payment Links**. Benji Pays' own guide is [Custom Payment Links for any PSA or ERP/CRM](https://support.benjipays.com/support/solutions/articles/150000181442-custom-payment-links-for-any-psa-or-erp-crm).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Skip the QuickBooks and Gateway Specific sections">
    The page opens with **QuickBooks Online Payment Link Templates** and **Gateway Specific Links**. Those links are for QuickBooks invoice emails. Don't use them for MSPortal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy a Default link">
    Scroll to **PSA/ERP and other 3rd Party Payment Links**. Each PSA (HaloPSA, ConnectWise, Autotask, Syncro and others) has a row labelled **Default**. Click **Copy** on the **Default** row of any PSA. They all open the same checkout; only the placeholder names differ.

    The other rows, such as **Benji Payments (Bank)** or **Benji Payments (CC) (Surcharging Enabled)**, send clients to one payment method only. Use one of those instead of **Default** only if you want to limit how clients can pay.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Replace the two placeholders">
    Paste the link into a text editor. Every Benji Pays link has an `invoiceNumber=` value and a `transactionAmount=` value. Replace each value with MSPortal's placeholder, exactly as written, square brackets included:

    | Replace the value after | With |
    |-------------------------|------|
    | `invoiceNumber=` | `[INVOICENUMBER]` |
    | `transactionAmount=` | `[AMOUNTDUE]` |

    For example, the ConnectWise **Default** link

    ```
    https://billing.yourmsp.com/portal/pay/?invoiceNumber=[invnumber]&transactionAmount=[invamount]
    ```

    becomes

    ```
    https://billing.yourmsp.com/portal/pay/?invoiceNumber=[INVOICENUMBER]&transactionAmount=[AMOUNTDUE]
    ```

    Keep everything else as Benji Pays shows it, including the address at the start. It may be your `yourshop.benjipays.com` address or your own billing domain, such as `billing.yourmsp.com`. Both work.

    <Warning>
      The placeholders are case-sensitive. `[invoicenumber]`, `{invoice}` or `[INVOICE_NUMBER]` won't work, and MSPortal won't save the link until both `[INVOICENUMBER]` and `[AMOUNTDUE]` are present.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2 (Billing portal sign-on): Create an SSO configuration

Skip this part if clients will get the Pay Now button only.

Billing portal sign-on adds an **Open billing portal** button to **Invoices** for your clients. It signs them in to your Benji Pays customer portal as themselves, using their MSPortal login, so they don't need a Benji Pays password. This part doesn't use any link from the payment links page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a JWT SSO configuration in Benji Pays">
    In Benji Pays, go to **Settings** > **Customer Portal Settings** > **SSO Configuration**, click **Add Configuration**, and choose **JWT**. Fill in:

    - **Description**: a name you'll recognize, for example `MSPortal`.
    - **Remote Login URL**: your MSPortal client portal address followed by `/invoices`, for example `https://portal.yourmsp.com/invoices`. Benji Pays sends clients here to sign in, and back here with a message if sign-in fails.
    - **ISS**: an issuer name, for example `msportal`.
    - **Login Button Text**: the text Benji Pays shows on its own login page.

    Save the configuration. Benji Pays then shows **AUDIENCE**, **ISSUER (ISS)**, **CLIENTID**, **CLIENTSECRET**, and a **Login Redirect URL** ending in `/callbackjwt`. Keep this page open. Benji Pays' own walkthrough is [Customer Portal SSO via JWT](https://support.benjipays.com/support/solutions/articles/150000196355-customer-portal-sso-via-jwt).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 3: Connect BenjiPays in MSPortal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the BenjiPays setup">
    In MSPortal, go to **Settings** > **Integrations**. Use the **Payments** filter or scroll to find **BenjiPays**, then click **Connect**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what clients get">
    Under **What should clients get?**, choose one:

    | Choice | What the form asks for |
    |--------|------------------------|
    | **Pay Now button and billing portal sign-in** (default) | The payment link from Part 1 and the sign-on values from Part 2 |
    | **Pay Now button only** | The payment link from Part 1 |
    | **Billing portal sign-in only** | The sign-on values from Part 2. No payment link is needed. |

    The fields below follow your choice.

    <img src="/images/product/benjipays-connect-choice.png" alt="The What should clients get? menu on the BenjiPays Connect form, with Pay Now button and billing portal sign-in, Pay Now button only, and Billing portal sign-in only" width="1200" height="590" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste the payment link (Pay Now)">
    Paste the link you built in Part 1 into **Payment link template**. MSPortal checks the link as you type and again when you save:

    | Problem | Fix |
    |---------|-----|
    | Not a valid URL | Re-copy the link from Benji Pays |
    | Missing `[INVOICENUMBER]` or `[AMOUNTDUE]` | Replace the PSA's placeholder values as shown in Part 1 |
    | Doesn't start with `https://` | Benji Pays links always use `https://`. Re-copy the link. |
    | Not a public web address, such as `localhost` or an IP address | Use the address Benji Pays shows on the payment links page |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the sign-on values (billing portal sign-on)">
    Copy these from the Benji Pays SSO configuration you saved in Part 2:

    | MSPortal field | Benji Pays value |
    |----------------|------------------|
    | **Callback URL** | **Login Redirect URL**, for example `https://yourshop.benjipays.com/callbackjwt`, or `https://billing.yourmsp.com/callbackjwt` if your portal uses your own domain |
    | **Issuer** | **ISSUER (ISS)** |
    | **Audience** | **AUDIENCE** |
    | **Client ID** | **CLIENTID** |
    | **Client secret** | **CLIENTSECRET** |

    Copy **Callback URL** exactly as Benji Pays shows it. It must start with `https://` and end in `/callbackjwt`, with nothing after it.

    The client secret must be at least 16 characters. MSPortal stores it encrypted and never shows it again. When you edit these settings later, leave **Client secret** as it is to keep the saved secret.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and continue">
    Click **Save and continue**. BenjiPays shows as **Connected** in **Settings** > **Integrations**, and what you chose appears on the **Invoices** page straight away. Saving with sign-on chosen turns sign-on on. MSPortal then opens **Configure BenjiPays**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 4: Finish in Configure BenjiPays

**Configure BenjiPays** has a section for each feature. Use the one for each feature you chose.

<Steps>
  <Step title="(Pay Now) Rename the button and check the preview">
    Under **Pay Now button**, **Button label** sets the text on the button. The default is **Pay Now**. You can use up to 40 characters, for example "Pay Online". Click **Save Pay Now settings**.

    **Live preview** shows the button and the link it would open for a sample invoice (invoice `INV-1024`, amount `499.00`). Click it to check Benji Pays opens the checkout.

    <img src="/images/product/benjipays-configure.png" alt="The Pay Now button section of Configure BenjiPays, with the Show the Pay Now button on unpaid invoices switch, Payment link template, Button label, Live preview and Save Pay Now settings" width="1964" height="985" />

    <Warning>
      The preview opens your real Benji Pays checkout, not a sandbox. Check the page loads, but don't submit a payment.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="(Billing portal sign-on) Check it as a client">
    Sign in as a client whose email is on a Benji Pays billing account, open **Invoices**, and click **Open billing portal**. The client lands in their Benji Pays portal without entering a Benji Pays password.

    The sign-on settings are under **Billing portal single sign-on**, with an **Enable billing portal sign-in** switch and **Save portal settings**, which saves only the portal settings.

    <img src="/images/product/benjipays-portal-sso-settings.png" alt="Billing portal single sign-on settings with the Enable billing portal sign-in switch, Callback URL, Issuer, Audience, Client ID and Client secret fields, and the Save portal settings button" width="1900" height="1076" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only clients see **Open billing portal**. MSP staff don't, and neither does anyone impersonating a client, because the portal always signs in the person who clicks. Clients whose MSPortal email isn't confirmed yet are asked by Benji Pays to verify it the first time.
</Note>

## Turning features on or off

Go to **Settings** > **Integrations**, open **BenjiPays**, and in the **Setup guide** choose **Configure BenjiPays**.

- **Pay Now button**: use **Show the Pay Now button on unpaid invoices**, then click **Save Pay Now settings**. Turning it off removes the payment link and button label; billing portal sign-on keeps working.
- **Billing portal sign-on**: use **Enable billing portal sign-in**, then click **Save portal settings**. Turning it off removes **Open billing portal** from **Invoices** straight away, and MSPortal refuses portal sign-in until you turn it back on. MSPortal keeps the saved values and client secret, so turning it back on doesn't need them again. Pay Now buttons aren't affected.

To switch between both features, Pay Now only and sign-on only in one step, change **What should clients get?** in **Connect** and click **Save changes**.

If both features are off, **Configure BenjiPays** shows **Clients see nothing from BenjiPays**. Turn one back on, or click **Disconnect BenjiPays** if you no longer use it.

## How Pay Now buttons work

- The button appears only when the invoice has a balance above zero. Voided, cancelled and fully paid invoices don't get one. If your PSA reports no balance, MSPortal uses the invoice total instead, unless the invoice is closed.
- Clicking it opens your Benji Pays checkout in a new tab with the invoice number and balance filled in. The amount is a plain number with two decimals, such as `499.00`.
- Clicking it doesn't mark the invoice paid in MSPortal. The status updates after your PSA's next invoice sync.
- Every client sees the same button label.

<Info>
  The balance comes from your PSA's invoice sync. If a payment hasn't posted in your PSA yet, the button uses the older balance. Run your PSA's invoice sync if amounts look wrong.
</Info>

## Disconnecting

1. Go to **Settings** > **Integrations** and open **BenjiPays**.
2. Open **More actions** in the connection header and choose **Disconnect…**.
3. Click **Disconnect** in the **Disconnect BenjiPays?** dialog, or **Cancel** to keep it.

Disconnecting removes the Pay Now buttons and the **Open billing portal** button straight away, and deletes the saved client secret. Your Benji Pays account isn't touched. To reconnect, choose what clients get again and re-enter the payment link or sign-on values.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| No link on the Benji Pays page contains `[INVOICENUMBER]` | That's expected. Copy a **Default** link and replace its placeholder values, as in [Part 1](#part-1-pay-now-build-your-payment-link). |
| "Payment link must include the [INVOICENUMBER] placeholder" | Replace the value after `invoiceNumber=` with `[INVOICENUMBER]`, brackets and capitals included. |
| "Payment link must include the [AMOUNTDUE] placeholder" | Replace the value after `transactionAmount=` with `[AMOUNTDUE]`, brackets and capitals included. |
| "Payment link must point to a public web address" | The link points at `localhost`, an IP address, or a single-word host name. Re-copy the link from Benji Pays. |
| "Payment link must use https://" | Benji Pays links always use `https://`. Re-copy the link. |
| Pay Now buttons not showing on **Invoices** | Check BenjiPays shows as **Connected** in **Settings** > **Integrations**, that **Show the Pay Now button on unpaid invoices** is on in **Configure BenjiPays**, and that the invoice has a balance above zero and isn't voided. Refresh **Invoices**. |
| Pay Now opens with the wrong amount | Your PSA's invoice balance is out of date. Run an invoice sync from your PSA integration. |
| Benji Pays says the invoice isn't found | The invoice number from your PSA doesn't exist in Benji Pays yet. Create the transaction in Benji Pays first. |
| "Clients see nothing from BenjiPays" | The Pay Now button and billing portal sign-on are both off. Turn one on in **Configure BenjiPays**, or disconnect BenjiPays. |
| "Use your BenjiPays payment link: an https:// address that includes [INVOICENUMBER] and [AMOUNTDUE]." | Build the link as in [Part 1](#part-1-pay-now-build-your-payment-link), then paste it again. |
| "Use the HTTPS callback URL from BenjiPays. It ends in /callbackjwt." | Copy the **Login Redirect URL** from Benji Pays exactly, with nothing after `/callbackjwt`. |
| "The client secret must be at least 16 characters" | Re-copy **CLIENTSECRET** from the Benji Pays SSO configuration in full. |
| **Save portal settings** stays greyed out | Fill in every field. **Callback URL** must be the **Login Redirect URL** from Benji Pays: `https://`, ending in `/callbackjwt`, with nothing after it. The first time you enable sign-in, **Client secret** is required. |
| No **Open billing portal** button for a client | Check **Enable billing portal sign-in** is on and saved in **Configure BenjiPays**. The button shows only for clients signed in as themselves, never for MSP staff or while impersonating. |
| Client sees "Your email is not linked to a BenjiPays billing account" | Benji Pays has no billing account with that client's MSPortal email. Add the email to the client's billing account in Benji Pays. |
| Client sees "Your billing account is disabled" | The account is disabled in Benji Pays. Re-enable it there. |
| Client sees "BenjiPays requires email verification" | The client follows the verification email from Benji Pays, then clicks **Open billing portal** again. |
| Client sees "Unable to open your billing portal" | Re-copy **Callback URL**, **Issuer**, **Audience**, **Client ID** and **Client secret** from the Benji Pays SSO configuration, then click **Save portal settings**. |

## Permissions

| Action | Permission required |
|--------|---------------------|
| View the BenjiPays setup | `read_settings_integrations` (Integrations settings access) |
| Save, change, turn features on or off, or disconnect BenjiPays | `manage_settings_integrations` |
| See Pay Now buttons on **Invoices** | `read_invoices` |
| Open the billing portal from **Invoices** | A client account with `read_invoices`, signed in as themselves |

Anyone who can view invoices can see the saved payment link, because it's the link behind the Pay Now button. Users without `manage_settings_integrations` see the setup as read-only.
