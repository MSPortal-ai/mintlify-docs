The Benji Pays integration adds a **Pay Now** button next to every unpaid invoice on the Invoices page. Click it, and your client lands on a pre-filled Benji Pays checkout for that specific invoice and balance, no typing required.

Unlike PSA, RMM, and backup connectors, Benji Pays has no data to sync. The Pay Now button needs only a payment-link template that MSPortal stores once and re-uses for every invoice. We swap `[INVOICENUMBER]` and `[AMOUNTDUE]` into your link at render time.

You can also let clients open their Benji Pays billing portal from **Invoices** with their MSPortal login, so they don't need a separate Benji Pays password. This is optional and is set up in [Part 3](#part-3-let-clients-open-their-billing-portal-optional).

On the BenjiPays connection page, the **Setup guide** has three tasks: **Connect**, **Configure Pay Now** (optional) and **Review**.

## Prerequisites

- Active Benji Pays merchant account
- A Benji Pays shop with an issued custom payment link
- MSPortal user with the **Manage Integrations** permission
- At least one PSA integration (ConnectWise, Autotask, or Halo PSA) syncing invoices, otherwise there are no invoices to render Pay Now buttons on

## Part 1: Get Your Payment Link from Benji Pays

The link MSPortal needs lives in your Benji Pays account. You're looking for the **Custom Payment Link** format, the one with the `[INVOICENUMBER]` and `[AMOUNTDUE]` tokens embedded.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Benji Pays">
    Log into your Benji Pays account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Payment Links page">
    Navigate to the **Payment Links** page for your shop / integration.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy the Custom Payment Link">
    Scroll to the very bottom of the page. The Custom Payment Link will look like this:

    ```
    https://yourshop.benjipays.com/portal/pay/?shortCode=!XYZ-&invoiceNumber=[INVOICENUMBER]&transactionAmount=[AMOUNTDUE]
    ```

    Copy the link exactly as Benji Pays displays it, including both `[INVOICENUMBER]` and `[AMOUNTDUE]` placeholders. The placeholders are case-sensitive. Do not replace the placeholders, MSPortal needs them to substitute the live invoice values per row.

    <Warning>
      If you remove the placeholders or rewrite them (for example to `{invoice}` or `[INVOICE_NUMBER]`), MSPortal will reject the link on save. Both literal tokens are required.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2: Configure Benji Pays in MSPortal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations settings">
    In MSPortal, go to **Settings** > **Integrations**. Use the **Payments** filter or scroll to find **BenjiPays**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Benji Pays setup page">
    Click **Connect** on the BenjiPays row. The setup page opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste the payment link template">
    In the **Payment link template** field, paste the URL you copied from Benji Pays.

    <img src="/images/product/benjipays-setup-controls.png" alt="BenjiPays setup page with the Payment link template field and the required [INVOICENUMBER] and [AMOUNTDUE] tokens" width="1404" height="260" />

    MSPortal checks the URL validity and both placeholders inline as you type, and enforces the remaining rules when you save:

    | Issue | Checked | Fix |
    |-------|---------|-----|
    | Not a valid URL, or not an http(s) URL | Inline, as you type | Re-copy the link from Benji Pays |
    | Missing `[INVOICENUMBER]` placeholder | Inline, as you type | Re-add the literal token, brackets and all |
    | Missing `[AMOUNTDUE]` placeholder | Inline, as you type | Re-add the literal token, brackets and all |
    | Not an `https://` URL | On save | Benji Pays links must use https |
    | Host is not benjipays.com or benjipays.io (or a subdomain of either) | On save | Pasted the wrong link, this is rejected to prevent phishing |
  </Step>

  <Step title="(Optional) Customize the button label">
    The **Button label** field controls the text on the button. The default is **Pay Now**. You can rename it to anything up to 40 characters, for example "Pay Online" or "Settle Invoice".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the live preview">
    The **Live preview** panel renders the button with your current label, then shows the URL it would generate for a sample invoice (invoice `INV-1024`, amount `499.00`).

    Click the preview button to open the generated link in a new tab. This is the same URL your clients will hit, just with sample values. Use it to confirm Benji Pays accepts the link before going live.

    <Warning>
      The preview opens your real Benji Pays checkout, not a sandbox. Check the page loads, but do not submit a payment.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save & enable">
    Click **Save & enable** at the bottom of the page. BenjiPays now shows as **Connected** in **Settings > Integrations**, and Pay Now buttons appear on the Invoices page immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 3: Let clients open their billing portal (optional)

Billing portal single sign-on adds an **Open billing portal** button to the **Invoices** page for your clients. Clicking it signs them in to your Benji Pays customer portal as themselves, using their MSPortal login. Benji Pays matches each client by email address, so a client's MSPortal email must belong to their Benji Pays billing account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a JWT SSO configuration in Benji Pays">
    In Benji Pays, go to **Settings** > **Customer Portal Settings** > **SSO Configuration**, click **Add Configuration**, and choose **JWT**. Fill in:

    - **Description**: a name you will recognize, for example `MSPortal`.
    - **Remote Login URL**: your MSPortal client portal address followed by `/invoices`, for example `https://portal.yourmsp.com/invoices`. Benji Pays sends clients here to sign in, and returns them here with an error message when sign-in fails.
    - **ISS**: an issuer name, for example `msportal`.
    - **Login Button Text**: the text Benji Pays shows on its own login page.

    Save the configuration. Benji Pays then shows the **AUDIENCE**, **ISSUER (ISS)**, **CLIENTID**, and **CLIENTSECRET** values, and a login redirect URL ending in `/callbackjwt`. Keep this page open. Benji Pays' own walkthrough is [Customer Portal SSO via JWT](https://support.benjipays.com/support/solutions/articles/150000196355-customer-portal-sso-via-jwt).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the BenjiPays configuration in MSPortal">
    Go to **Settings** > **Integrations** and open **BenjiPays**. In the **Setup guide**, choose **Configure Pay Now**, then scroll to **Billing portal single sign-on**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy the Benji Pays values into MSPortal">
    | MSPortal field | Benji Pays value |
    |----------------|------------------|
    | **Callback URL** | The login redirect URL, such as `https://yourshop.benjipays.com/callbackjwt` |
    | **Issuer** | **ISSUER (ISS)** |
    | **Audience** | **AUDIENCE** |
    | **Client ID** | **CLIENTID** |
    | **Client secret** | **CLIENTSECRET** |

    MSPortal stores the client secret encrypted and never shows it again. When you edit these settings later, leave **Client secret** blank to keep the saved secret.

    <img src="/images/product/benjipays-portal-sso-settings.png" alt="Billing portal single sign-on settings with the Enable billing portal sign-in switch, Callback URL, Issuer, Audience, Client ID and Client secret fields, and the Save portal settings button" width="1900" height="1076" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn it on and save">
    Make sure **Enable billing portal sign-in** is on, then click **Save portal settings**. This button saves only the portal settings; your payment link and button label are kept.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check it as a client">
    Sign in as a client whose email is on a Benji Pays billing account, open **Invoices**, and click **Open billing portal**. The client lands in their Benji Pays portal without entering a Benji Pays password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only clients see **Open billing portal**. MSP staff don't, and neither does anyone impersonating a client, because the portal always signs in the person who clicks. Clients whose MSPortal email is not yet confirmed are asked by Benji Pays to verify it the first time.
</Note>

To stop clients from opening the portal, turn off **Enable billing portal sign-in** and click **Save portal settings**. The button disappears from **Invoices** straight away, and MSPortal refuses any portal sign-in until you turn it back on. Your Pay Now buttons are not affected.

## How Pay Now Buttons Render

Once enabled, every unpaid invoice on the **Invoices** page gets a Pay Now button in its row.

- The button only appears when the invoice has a balance greater than zero. Voided, cancelled, or fully paid invoices don't get a button. If your PSA reports no balance for an invoice, MSPortal uses the invoice total instead, unless the invoice is closed.
- Clicking the button opens your Benji Pays checkout in a new tab with the invoice number and balance pre-filled. The amount is sent as a plain number with two decimal places, such as `499.00`.
- Clicking the button does not mark the invoice paid in MSPortal. The paid status updates after your PSA's next invoice sync.
- The button uses the label you configured, the same way for every client

<Info>
  Balance comes from your PSA's invoice sync. If your PSA reports the wrong balance (for example because a payment hasn't posted yet), the Pay Now button will use that stale figure. Refresh your PSA's invoice sync if amounts look wrong.
</Info>

## Editing or Disconnecting

To change your template or label after enabling:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Integrations**
2. Click **Configure** on the BenjiPays row
3. Edit any field, then click **Save changes**

To disconnect Benji Pays entirely:

1. Open the Benji Pays setup page
2. Click **Disconnect** in the bottom-left corner
3. Click **Disconnect** in the **Disconnect BenjiPays?** dialog, or click **Keep BenjiPays** to cancel

Disconnecting immediately removes Pay Now buttons from every invoice and the **Open billing portal** button for clients, and deletes the saved client secret. Your Benji Pays account is not touched, only the MSPortal-side settings are forgotten. You can reconnect at any time by pasting the link again and re-entering the portal settings.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "Payment link must include the [INVOICENUMBER] placeholder" | The URL you pasted doesn't contain the literal text `[INVOICENUMBER]`. Re-copy from Benji Pays, the placeholder is required. |
| "Payment link must include the [AMOUNTDUE] placeholder" | Same as above, but for `[AMOUNTDUE]`. |
| "Payment link must point to a benjipays.com or benjipays.io address" | The URL points somewhere else (a typo, a redirect, or the wrong link). Re-copy the Custom Payment Link from Benji Pays. |
| "Payment link must use https://" | Benji Pays issues https URLs only. If you have an http link, contact Benji Pays support. |
| Pay Now buttons not showing on the Invoices page | Confirm BenjiPays shows as **Connected** in Settings > Integrations. Confirm the invoice has a balance greater than zero and is not voided. Refresh the Invoices page. |
| Pay Now opens to the wrong amount | Your PSA's invoice balance is stale. Trigger an invoice sync from your PSA integration. |
| Pay Now opens to "Invoice not found" on Benji Pays | The invoice number in your PSA doesn't exist as a transaction in Benji Pays yet. Create the corresponding transaction in Benji Pays first. |
| **Save portal settings** stays greyed out | Fill in every field. **Callback URL** must be the `https://` Benji Pays address ending in `/callbackjwt`, with no extra text after it. When sign-in is enabled for the first time, **Client secret** is required. |
| No **Open billing portal** button for a client | Confirm **Enable billing portal sign-in** is on and saved. The button shows only for clients signed in as themselves, never for MSP staff or while impersonating. |
| Client sees "Your email is not linked to a BenjiPays billing account" | Benji Pays has no billing account with that client's MSPortal email. Add the email to the client's billing account in Benji Pays. |
| Client sees "Your billing account is disabled" | The account is disabled in Benji Pays. Re-enable it there. |
| Client sees "BenjiPays requires email verification" | The client must follow the verification email Benji Pays sent, then click **Open billing portal** again. |
| Client sees "Unable to open your billing portal" | Re-copy **Issuer**, **Audience**, **Client ID**, and **Client secret** from the Benji Pays SSO configuration, then click **Save portal settings**. |

## Permissions

| Action | Permission Required |
|--------|---------------------|
| View BenjiPays setup page | `read_settings_integrations` (Integrations settings access) |
| Save, change, or disconnect BenjiPays | `manage_settings_integrations` |
| See Pay Now buttons on Invoices | `read_invoices` |
| Open the billing portal from Invoices | A client account with `read_invoices`, signed in as themselves |

Anyone who can view invoices can read the saved payment link, because the link renders as the Pay Now button on the Invoices page. Users without `manage_settings_integrations` see the setup page as read-only.
