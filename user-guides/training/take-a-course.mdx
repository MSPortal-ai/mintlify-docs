This guide is for company learners and MSP staff completing their own training in the portal's course player.

## Before you start

- You need Training access and an enrollment in the course. See [Find and enroll in a course](/user-guides/training/find-and-enroll).
- Training delivered through another provider has its own player. For Huntress tiles, follow [Open your Huntress training](/user-guides/training/open-huntress-training).

## Start or resume a course

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your course">
    Open **Training Menu > Course Library** and select **My Courses** to show your enrollments. If you have not enrolled yet, [find and enroll in the course](/user-guides/training/find-and-enroll).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the player">
    Click **Start**, or **Continue** if you already have progress. A direct course link still needs access and an enrollment. Ask your MSP if the course does not open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through the content">
    Follow the course's instructions. Video, document, and interactive courses work differently. Use the player's fullscreen button for more room.

    Finish all the content. When the content is done, an **Assessment** section unlocks if the course has one. Click **Submit assessment** when you finish it. A preview or time spent on the page does not complete the course. A document course can ask you to click **Confirm reading** after you select **I have read and understood this document**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    The player shows whether you passed and your score. If it says your progress could not be saved, keep the page open while it retries. Report repeated errors to your MSP with the course title and the message.

    When a certificate is available, click **View Certificate** or **View & Share** to open it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Follow an assigned learning path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Training in the sidebar">
    The landing page shows each learning path assigned to you, with completed courses, remaining work, and overall progress.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the next course">
    Click **Start Learning Path**, or **Resume Next** once you have started. You can also click **Start** or **Continue** on a course inside the path. **Show more** expands a course description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review finished courses">
    A completed course shows **Review** or **View Certificate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you have no learning path assigned, Training shows the course library instead. Your individual enrollments are still there.

## Check your progress and history

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Training">
    Click **Training Menu > My Training**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary">
    The summary shows **Enrolled Courses**, **Completed**, **Learning Hours**, and **Average Score**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check an enrollment">
    The table lists each **Course / Learning Path** with its **Status**, **Progress**, **Score**, and **Due Date**. Use the column filters to narrow the list. To open a course, go back to its library card or learning path.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| The player sends you back to Training | Check that you enrolled and still have access to the course. |
| The content is blank or unavailable | Check your connection and report the course title and visible error to your MSP. |
| Progress has not reached Completed | Finish all sections and any required quiz; check for a save or submission error. |
| A quiz or retry is unavailable | Check the course's instructions and attempt limits; ask your MSP for help. |
| A due date or assignment is wrong | Ask your MSP to correct it. Learner progress controls do not edit assignments. |
| The course is missing from the library after completion | Enable **Filters > Visibility > Show completed courses**. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I resume training?">
    Use **Continue** on the course's library card, or **Resume Next** on your assigned learning path. Resume behavior within the content depends on the course type and the progress successfully saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I manually mark any course complete?">
    No. Follow the course's required content and assessment. A document course may provide a reading confirmation; that does not apply to every course type.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why am I not complete after watching the video?">
    The course may include more content or a required assessment. Check the player and any error message. Ask your MSP if you completed the requirements but the enrollment still shows unfinished.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Training show learning paths for one user and a library for another?">
    Users can have different assignments. The landing page shows assigned learning paths when available and can show the library when no paths are assigned.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change an assigned due date?">
    Ask your MSP to review the assignment or due date. Your personal training page is for completing and tracking learning.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I do if progress does not save?">
    Check your connection, retain the exact error and course title, and contact your MSP if the problem continues. Avoid unenrolling or creating a duplicate enrollment to resolve a progress problem.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
