**Audience:** Company users and MSP staff with Security Awareness read access. Available views depend on your MSP's connected providers, company mappings, and your permitted company scope. Your MSP manages users, permissions, and provider setup.

## Choose the right view

| What you want to do | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Review awareness information for your company | **Security > Security Awareness** |
| Find a learner's assignment completion and phishing metrics | [Read learner status](/user-guides/training/read-awareness-status) |
| Inspect a Breach Secure Now learner's risk details | [Read BSN risk details](/user-guides/training/read-bsn-risk) |
| Compare CyberHoot company compliance and Hoot Score | [Read CyberHoot company summaries](/user-guides/security/read-cyberhoot-companies) |
| Find a HootPhish challenge | [Review HootPhish challenges](/user-guides/security/read-hootphish-challenges) |
| Read the selected challenge's participant results | [Review participant results](/user-guides/security/read-hootphish-participants) |
| Take your own portal courses | [Find and start training](/user-guides/training/index) |

Security Awareness shows CyberHoot in dedicated company and challenge sections. Other connected providers—Breach Secure Now, Phin, and Huntress—use the learner status table below. That table can be absent when there are no results for those providers.

The alternate **Training > Training Menu > Security Training** entry opens a learner table that can include all four providers, including CyberHoot. It is a different presentation from the CyberHoot company and challenge sections. Some tenants use a different Training experience; use the Security entry if the Training menu is unavailable.

## Phished.io

The [Phished.io integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-phished-integration) adds **Learners** and **Insights** views under its provider selection. Learners uses the shared training status table; Insights shows current training-session totals and simulation events from the last 30 days. Phished also becomes an available provider in the combined Security Training learner table.

Training is a current snapshot. Simulation event counts preserve the source event types and do not provide a calculated click rate. Confirm company mapping, permissions and the last successful sync before interpreting the results.

## Company users: review and ask for help

Select your company if a company selector is available, wait for the relevant view to load, then check the provider and metric before interpreting the result. Missing data, an empty filtered list, or **Not provided** does not prove that training is complete or that there is no risk.

These readers do not assign training, launch simulations, send reminders, unlock provider accounts, or change your permissions. To request help, start with the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). Include the provider, course or challenge name, and the issue; review and confirm the prepared request. Your MSP handles account and assignment changes.

## MSP staff: investigate missing or unexpected data

1. Confirm the selected company and your Security Awareness read permission.
2. Check that the appropriate integration is active and its customer or organization is mapped to the correct company.
3. Review the integration's sync information and the source provider when figures appear stale or incomplete.
4. Use the provider's supported administration workflow for assignment, campaign, or account changes. The readers described here display imported results.

Provider setup guides: [Breach Secure Now](/user-guides/integrations/enable-breach-secure-now-integration), [Phin](/user-guides/integrations/enable-phin-integration), [Huntress](/user-guides/integrations/enable-huntress-integration), and [CyberHoot](/user-guides/integrations/enable-cyberhoot-integration).

Huntress **Assignments** is a separate tab under Security Training when Huntress SAT is available and your role also has Training read access. See [Find Huntress training assignments](/user-guides/training/read-huntress-assignments) and [Open your Huntress training](/user-guides/training/open-huntress-training). The learner status reader is not the assignment manager or an episode player. Use your assigned training entry for completion tracking; viewing a summary does not complete a course.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does Security Awareness look different from Security Training?">
    Security Awareness separates CyberHoot company and HootPhish results from the other providers' learner table. Security Training is the combined learner status view.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company administrator assign courses or manage users here?">
    These views are readers. Your MSP manages account permissions and provider administration. Start a request through the Home AI Assistant when you need a change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export these awareness tables to CSV?">
    The learner status table and CyberHoot sections described here do not have a configured CSV export control.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an empty page mean everyone has finished training?">
    No. Check company scope, filters, integration mapping, sync status, and any load error with your MSP. An absent table or missing value is not a completion result.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
