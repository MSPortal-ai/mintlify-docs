Training brings your available courses, assignments, progress, and certificates into one place in the portal. Your MSP controls who has access, which courses are available, and which training provider is connected.

## Before you start

- Open **Training** in the sidebar. You need Training read access to see it.
- Company users see the courses their MSP has made available to their company.
- Admin pages (**All Enrollments**, **Learning Paths**, **Reports & Analytics**) need the **Manage Training** permission.

## Open the Training Menu

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Training in the sidebar">
    The landing page shows your assigned learning paths. If you have none, it shows the course library instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Training Menu at the top right">
    The menu lists the training pages you can use.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/training/training-menu-dropdown.png" alt="Training Menu open on the My Training page, listing Course Library, My Training, All Enrollments, Learning Paths, and Reports & Analytics" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a page">
    Use the table below to pick the page you need.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Page | What it is for | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Course Library | Browse courses and enroll | Everyone with Training access |
| My Training | Your enrollments, progress, and scores | Everyone with Training access |
| All Enrollments | Enroll users, send reminders, remove enrollments | **Manage Training** |
| Learning Paths | Create paths, groups, and templates | **Manage Training** |
| Reports & Analytics | Completion and compliance reports | **Manage Training** |
| Security Training | Security awareness status from connected providers | Security Awareness read access and a connected Breach Secure Now, Phin, Huntress, or CyberHoot integration |

Some MSPs use a provider-hosted training experience. In that case, **Training** opens the provider's learning portal and some pages above do not appear.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Training** is not in the sidebar | Ask your MSP to check your Training permission and that training is enabled. |
| A menu item is missing | **All Enrollments**, **Learning Paths**, and **Reports & Analytics** need **Manage Training**. **Security Training** needs Security Awareness access and a connected provider. |
| The course library is empty | Clear the search and filters, switch **My Courses** to **Show All**, and check the company selector. Then ask your MSP to enable courses. |
| Training opens a different site | Your MSP uses a provider-hosted training portal. Use that portal's catalog and assignments. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can every portal user open every training page?">
    No. Your MSP controls training permissions, company scope, course availability, and Premium access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who assigns training or changes a due date?">
    Your MSP. Company users complete the training made available to them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does my Training page look different from a colleague's?">
    Assignments, available courses, and permissions can differ between users. Ask your MSP to check the course or page you need.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who do I contact about a training problem?">
    Contact your MSP with the course title, the page you were on, and the exact error message.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
