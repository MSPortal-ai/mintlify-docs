This guide is for company learners and MSP staff who completed their own training. A certificate is issued when you finish a course's required content and pass any assessment.

## Before you start

- Complete the course, including any quiz. A progress percentage or a preview does not issue a certificate.
- Completed courses are hidden from the library by default, so you turn on a filter to see them.

## Open your certificate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the course library">
    Open **Training > Training Menu > Course Library**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show completed courses">
    Click **Filters**. Under **Visibility**, select **Show completed courses**, then click **Apply Filters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click View Certificate">
    Find the course and click **View Certificate** on its card. In the small grid view the button reads **Certificate**. On an assigned learning path, **View Certificate** also appears beside each completed course.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the details">
    The certificate shows your name, the course, the completion date, the certificate number, and your final score when the course has one. If something is wrong, ask your MSP to check your training record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Right after you finish a course, the result dialog can also offer **View & Share**, which opens the same certificate.

The name and logo at the top come from your MSP's certificate branding setting. The certificate shows either your MSP's branding or your own company's name and logo.

## Download a copy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the certificate">
    Use the steps above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Download">
    Find **Download** in the **Share your achievement** area. The file is a PNG image named `certificate-` followed by the certificate number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the file to check it">
    If the download fails, try again later. If it keeps failing, send your MSP the certificate number and the error message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share a certificate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    In **Share your achievement**, click **Copy** to copy the certificate link. Paste it where you want to send it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or share to a service">
    Click **LinkedIn**, **Facebook**, **X (Twitter)**, or **Email**. Social services may ask you to sign in first. **Email** opens your email app with a prepared message, so check the recipient before you send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The certificate link opens a public page. Anyone with the link can see your name, the course, and the completion details without signing in. Share it only with people you want to see it.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **View Certificate** button | Check the course is completed and any quiz was passed. Turn on **Show completed courses** in **Filters > Visibility**. If the course is complete but no certificate exists, ask your MSP to check it. |
| The download is not a PDF | Certificates download as PNG images. Keep that file as your copy. |
| The certificate image looks out of date | Click **Regenerate Certificate Image** below the share buttons. |
| The link does not open for someone | Copy the link again from the certificate page and resend it. If it still fails, tell your MSP the course title and certificate number. |
| The name on the certificate is wrong | Ask your MSP to check your profile and training record. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to share my certificate?">
    No. Sharing is optional and separate from completing the course.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Copy send my certificate to anyone?">
    No. **Copy** only puts the link on your clipboard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing my profile name change a certificate I already have?">
    Do not count on it. Ask your MSP to check the certificate if the name is wrong.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who sets the logo on my certificate?">
    Your MSP chooses **MSP branding** or **Client branding** in Settings > Training. Client branding uses your company's name and logo.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
