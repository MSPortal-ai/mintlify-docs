This guide is for company learners. MSP staff can also browse the library and use **Self Enroll** for their own learning. Enrolling other people is an MSP task done from **All Enrollments**.

## Before you start

- Your MSP must turn training on for you and make courses available to your company.
- Premium courses also need Premium training turned on for you.
- For Huntress tiles, use [Open your Huntress training](/user-guides/training/open-huntress-training). Huntress tiles are existing assignments and have no enroll button.

## Find a course

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the library">
    Click **Training** in the sidebar, then **Training Menu > Course Library**.

    If Training opens a provider's learning portal instead, use that portal's catalog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or filter">
    Type in the search box to search by course title, description, or category. Click **Filters** to narrow by **Category**, **Tags**, **Course Type**, and **Difficulty Level**, then click **Apply Filters**.

    Use the small grid, large grid, or list buttons to change the layout. **My Courses** shows only your enrollments. Click **Show All** to remove that filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the course card">
    Check the description and any **Required**, **Included**, or **Premium** label. If the card has a **Preview** button, click it to watch a preview. A preview does not enroll you or complete the course.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enroll in a course

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Enroll">
    Click **Enroll** on the course card (**Enroll Now** in list view). Wait for the confirmation message.

    MSP staff see **Self Enroll** to enroll themselves and **Enroll Users** to enroll others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Start">
    After you enroll, the card shows **Start**. After you have made progress, it shows **Continue**. See [Take a course and track progress](/user-guides/training/take-a-course).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find a course you already completed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Filters">
    In the course library, click **Filters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show completed courses">
    Under **Visibility**, select **Show completed courses**, then click **Apply Filters**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/training-library-filters.png" alt="Visibility section with the Show completed courses checkbox." width="702" height="194" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the certificate">
    On a completed course with a certificate, click **View Certificate**. You can also check status in **Training Menu > My Training**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove an enrollment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the course">
    Click **My Courses** to show your enrollments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Unenroll">
    Click **Unenroll** on the card. This button appears only on a course you have not finished and that has no certificate. Learners see it only on courses they have not started and that are not required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Unenrolling changes your training record. To change what you see in the library, use filters instead. If **Unenroll** is missing or removal is refused, the course is required, part of an assigned learning path, started, or completed. Ask your MSP.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The library is empty | Clear the search and filter chips, click **Show All**, and check the company selector. If it stays empty, ask your MSP to enable courses or your training access. |
| A completed course is missing | Turn on **Show completed courses** in **Filters > Visibility**. |
| **Enroll** is disabled | Select a company in the company selector. If it stays disabled, ask your MSP to check your course access. |
| A coworker sees a course you cannot | Availability depends on company settings, permissions, and Premium access. Ask your MSP to check your account. |
| **Unenroll** is missing | The course is required, started, completed, or in an assigned learning path. Ask your MSP to correct the assignment. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I enroll a coworker?">
    No. Company learners enroll themselves. Ask your MSP to assign training to another user or group.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Preview count as completing the course?">
    No. Enroll, then use **Start** or **Continue** and finish the content and any quiz.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Enroll start the course right away?">
    No. It adds the course to your training. Click **Start** to open it. Starting a course inside an assigned learning path can enroll and open it in one step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did the course disappear when I finished it?">
    The library hides completed courses by default. Turn on **Show completed courses**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
