This guide is for MSP staff. It covers three separate decisions: which companies and users get Premium training, which courses are available, and who is enrolled. Doing one does not do the others. Company users who cannot see training should contact their MSP.

## Before you start

- You need the **Manage Training** permission to turn Premium training on or off for companies and users.
- To change the **Enabled** switches on the courses list, you also need write access to Training settings.
- To see the **Actions** menu on the **Companies** page, you also need write access to Companies.
- Premium training uses the Bigger Brains catalog. It is billed per enabled user, based on the highest number of enabled users in the billing period.
- Standard training is the set of included MSPortal courses at no extra training cost.

## Turn Premium training on or off for a company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Companies">
    Find the company. The **Training** column shows **Premium** or **Standard**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/training/company-row-menu-enable-training.png" alt="Company row menu showing Enable Premium Training" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the company's row menu">
    Choose **Enable Premium Training**. To turn it off, choose **Disable Premium Training** from the same menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the result">
    A message confirms the change. It saves right away, so there is no Save button. The **Training** column updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change several companies at once, select their rows, open **Actions**, and choose **Enable Premium Training** or **Disable Premium Training**.

<Note>
Turning Premium on for a company gives its users Premium access unless a user has their own setting (see below). Review [Billing](/user-guides/settings/billing) before you enable it for many companies.
</Note>

## Set Premium training for one user

A user can follow the company default or have their own On or Off setting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Users & Roles > Company Users">
    Find the user. The **Premium Training** column shows **On** or **Off**. Under it, **Company default** means the user follows the company, and **User override** means the user has their own setting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the user's row menu">
    Choose **Enable Premium Training** or **Disable Premium Training**. The change saves right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to the company default if needed">
    On a user with a **User override**, choose **Use Company Default** from the same menu. That user then follows future company changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change several users, select their rows and choose **Enable Premium Training**, **Disable Premium Training**, or **Use Company Default** from the bulk actions menu.

Changing the company setting does not remove user overrides. If a user's access does not match the company, check the **Company default** or **User override** label under their status.

## Choose which courses are available

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Training">
    The table lists every course with **Enabled**, **Course Title**, **Type**, **Category**, **Tags**, **Duration**, and **Enrollments** columns.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/training/settings-training-overview.png" alt="Training settings page showing the course list" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn courses on">
    Use the **Enabled** switch on each course. To change several at once, select rows and choose **Enable Selected** or **Disable Selected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company scope">
    The page follows the company selector. Premium courses are only enabled for companies that have Premium training turned on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Training settings](/user-guides/settings/training) for course previews, custom courses, and certificate branding.

## Enroll learners

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Training > Training Menu > All Enrollments">
    This menu item needs the **Manage Training** permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Enroll Users">
    Choose the courses, optionally set a **Due Date**, select the users, and click the enroll button, which reads **Enroll 3 User(s)** when three users are selected. Users get an email about the new course.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send reminders when needed">
    Select enrollments and use **Send Reminders** in the bulk actions menu. A single enrollment also has **Send Reminder** in its row.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To assign a set of courses as a program, use [Learning paths](/user-guides/training/learning-paths).

## Check that it works

Ask a real learner to open **Training > Training Menu > Course Library** and start a course. A staff preview only shows the content. It does not prove the learner's Premium access, company scope, or enrollment.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Premium courses are missing for a user | Check the company shows **Premium**, the course is **Enabled** in **Settings > Training**, and the user's **Premium Training** column shows **On**. |
| A user is still **Off** after you enabled the company | The user has a **User override**. Choose **Use Company Default** on that user. |
| No **Enable Premium Training** option | You need the **Manage Training** permission. |
| No **Actions** menu on the Companies page | Select at least one company row, and check you have write access to Companies. |
| A learner cannot see a course | Check the course is enabled, the learner is enrolled, and the company selector matches. |
| Billing looks higher than the users enabled today | Billing uses the highest number of enabled users in the billing period. See [Billing](/user-guides/settings/billing). |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does enabling Premium assign every course?">
    No. Premium access, course availability, and enrollment are separate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to click Save after changing Premium training?">
    No. Company and user Premium changes save as soon as you choose them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Off and Use Company Default?">
    **Off** is a setting for that user and stays Off if the company turns Premium on. **Use Company Default** removes the user's own setting so they follow the company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does turning Premium off remove billed usage already recorded?">
    No. Billing uses the highest number of enabled users in the billing period, so earlier days in the period still count.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
