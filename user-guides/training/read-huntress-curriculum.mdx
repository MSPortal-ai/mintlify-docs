This guide is for MSP staff and company users who can open the Huntress **Assignments** tab. The curriculum panel is read-only.

## Before you start

- You need the access listed in [Find Huntress training assignments](/user-guides/training/read-huntress-assignments).
- Your MSP manages assignments and Huntress settings.

## Open a curriculum

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assignment list">
    Open **Training > Training Menu > Security Training > Assignments**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click View curriculum">
    Use the button in the row's **Actions** column. Clicking the assignment name does not open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the top of the panel">
    Read the company, assignment name, description, and status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the schedule and requirements">
    Review **Starts**, **Ends**, **Passing score**, **Attempts**, and **Learner progress**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll through Episodes">
    Each episode shows its title, description, learning objectives, and an **Unlocks on** date when it has one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the panel">
    Close it when you are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The panel uses the assignment already loaded in the list, so opening it does not start a sync. **Unlimited** means no attempt limit. **Not provided** means Huntress did not send a value. The passing score and attempts apply to the assignment, so do not assume each episode has its own.

**Learner progress** is a completed-of-assigned count. The panel has no list of learner names. Episode numbers follow the order imported from Huntress.

## Preview an episode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Preview episode">
    Use the button on the episode. It opens the Huntress link in a new browser tab. Huntress may ask you to sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A preview may not record learner completion. For tracked learning, use the Huntress training link assigned to your account. Opening a preview does not complete the assignment.
</Warning>

**Under maintenance** turns the preview button off. **Preview unavailable** means the link is missing or unusable, or the episode is a draft. **Unlocks on** is for information in this panel. The preview can still open before that date. Your own library tiles do check the unlock date. To find your own episodes, see [Open your Huntress training](/user-guides/training/open-huntress-training).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Huntress has not returned any episodes for this assignment yet** | Ask your MSP to check the company mapping and the assignment content sync. |
| A description or passing score is missing | Huntress did not send it. It does not remove the training requirement. |
| Details look wrong | Close the panel, reload the list, and check the right company and assignment. Then ask for help through the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) and name the assignment and episode. |

You cannot edit dates, attempt limits, passing scores, or learners here.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Preview episode record completion?">
    It may not. Use your assigned Huntress link, then check the result in Huntress and the later synced status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is an episode preview disabled?">
    The episode is under maintenance, a draft, or has no usable link. Ask your MSP to check it in Huntress.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the passing score or attempt limit?">
    No. They are imported, read-only values.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an available preview mean the unlock date has passed?">
    No. The curriculum panel shows the date but does not enforce it on previews.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
