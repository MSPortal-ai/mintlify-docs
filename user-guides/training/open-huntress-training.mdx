This guide is for company learners and MSP staff who take Huntress security awareness episodes. Your assigned episodes appear as tiles in the Course Library.

## Before you start

- You need Training read access.
- Your portal email must match a Huntress learner that is still active.
- You must be in a Huntress assignment that has synced episodes. Draft and canceled assignments do not show.
- Your MSP must turn on and map the Huntress SAT feed. MSP staff who only manage Huntress see company-wide assignments in [Find Huntress training assignments](/user-guides/training/read-huntress-assignments), not personal tiles.

## Find your episodes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the course library">
    Open **Training > Training Menu > Course Library**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click My Courses">
    This shows your enrollments. Personally assigned Huntress episodes are already included. You do not enroll separately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Search by episode title, description, company, or assignment name. Huntress tiles carry a **Huntress** badge and the assignment name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear filters if an episode is missing">
    Huntress episodes use the **Security Awareness** category and have no difficulty level, so a **Difficulty Level** or **Course Type** filter can hide them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find completed episodes">
    Click **Filters**, select **Show completed courses** under **Visibility**, then click **Apply Filters**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/product/training-library-filters.png" alt="Course library Visibility filter with Show completed courses" width="702" height="194" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

The library shows one tile per episode, even when the episode is in more than one of your assignments. The assignment name and progress on a tile come from one of those assignments.

## Open an episode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the button on the tile">
    The button reads **Start** when you have no progress, **Continue** when you have some, and **Review** when the episode is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the button">
    The episode opens in a new browser tab on Huntress. Huntress may ask you to sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
These buttons open Huntress's preview link, not the portal course player. A preview may not record your completion. To get tracked completion, use the Huntress training link assigned to you and follow Huntress's instructions. Ask your MSP if you did not receive that link.
</Warning>

Tile messages:

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Locked** | The episode's unlock date is still in the future. |
| **Under maintenance** | The preview cannot be opened right now. |
| **Preview unavailable** | The preview link is missing or cannot be used. |

Larger grid and list views show more detail, such as the unlock date and passing score.

## Check your progress

Progress on Huntress tiles comes from synced assignment activity. A completion date, or progress of 100% or more, changes the button to **Review**. Several episodes in one assignment can show the same progress or score, because the numbers belong to the assignment and not to each episode.

Huntress tiles count toward the library's **Enrolled Courses** and **Completed** totals and its average score. Those totals also include portal courses. Tiles have no certificate, **Unenroll**, or **Enroll Users** buttons.

Finishing an episode in Huntress does not update the portal right away. Check the result in Huntress first. If the portal still looks out of date later, ask your MSP to check the Huntress sync.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No Huntress tiles | Ask your MSP to check that your portal email matches an active Huntress learner, the account is mapped to your company, and you are in an assignment with synced episodes. Also check the company selector and clear filters. |
| A tile is missing after you finished | Turn on **Show completed courses** in **Filters > Visibility**. |
| Several episodes show the same score | The score is for the assignment, not each episode. |
| Progress did not update | Check the result in Huntress, then ask your MSP to check the sync. |
| Tiles do not load | Reload the page later. If it keeps failing, tell your MSP. A load error is not the same as having no assignments. |

To ask for help, start with the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). Include the assignment, the episode, and the result you expected.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Start guarantee my completion is tracked?">
    No. It opens a preview link. Use your assigned Huntress link for tracked learning and check the result in Huntress.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can an MSP admin see assignments but no personal tiles?">
    Personal tiles need a matching learner email and membership in an assignment with synced episodes. Admin access does not assign training.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to enroll in a Huntress episode?">
    No. Assigned episodes already appear under **My Courses**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
