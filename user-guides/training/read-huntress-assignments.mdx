This guide is for MSP staff and company users who need to see which Huntress training assignments exist and how far along they are. The page is read-only.

## Before you start

- You need both Training read access and Security Awareness read access.
- Your MSP must turn on Huntress SAT assignment sync and map the Huntress accounts to companies you can access.
- For your own episodes, use [Open your Huntress training](/user-guides/training/open-huntress-training).

## Find an assignment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security Training">
    Open **Training > Training Menu > Security Training**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Assignments tab">
    **Training Status** is the other tab. It shows [learner status](/user-guides/training/read-awareness-status).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company selector">
    Wait for the list to load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and filter">
    Type in **Search assignments, episodes, or companies...**. The search also covers assignment descriptions. Use the status filter to choose **All statuses**, **Draft**, **Staged**, **Launching**, **In progress**, **Completed**, or **Canceled**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page through the list">
    Click **Previous** or **Next** to browse 25 rows at a time. Click **View curriculum** on a row to [read its episodes](/user-guides/training/read-huntress-curriculum).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Searching or changing the status returns you to page 1. Changing the company selector resets the search, status, page, and any open curriculum panel. The list is ordered by start date, newest first. It has no column sorting or date filter.

## Read the summary and rows

| Summary box | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Assignments** | All synced assignments in your selected companies. |
| **Active assignments** | Assignments that are **Launching** or **In progress**. **Staged** is not counted. |
| **Episodes** | Distinct episodes in those assignments. |
| **Learner assignments** | Assigned learners added up across assignments. A person in two assignments counts twice. |
| **Completion** | Completed learner assignments divided by assigned learner assignments. It shows 0% when nobody is assigned. |

The summary covers your whole company selection. Search and status filters change the table and its count, but not the summary.

Each row shows **Assignment**, **Company**, **Status**, **Episodes**, **Learner progress**, and **Schedule**. **Learner progress** is a count and percentage of learners who completed, based on a completion date or progress of 100% or more. The page does not list individual learners.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Assignments** tab | You need both permissions, and your MSP must have Huntress SAT assignment sync on. Huntress security access alone is not enough. Some MSPs use a different Training experience. |
| **No assignments match these filters** | Clear the search and choose **All statuses**. |
| **No Huntress assignments yet** | Ask your MSP to check company mappings and the assignment sync. |
| **Huntress training could not be loaded** | Reload later. If it keeps happening, report the error. There is no retry button. |
| Data looks old | Synced data stays visible while an integration is paused. Ask your MSP to confirm it is current. |

This page does not run a sync, create or cancel assignments, enroll learners, send reminders, or export CSV. Company users can ask for changes through the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). MSP staff make changes in Huntress. See the [Huntress integration guide](/user-guides/integrations/enable-huntress-integration).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do the summary numbers stay the same when I filter?">
    They cover your whole company selection. Search and status only filter the table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Learner assignments count unique people?">
    No. A person counts once for each assignment they are in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I create, cancel, or send a Huntress assignment here?">
    No. This page only reads synced assignments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does View curriculum show which learners finished?">
    No. It shows the combined progress and the episode list. Use the learner status table for individuals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
