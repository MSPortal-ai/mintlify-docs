This guide is for company users and MSP staff who need a closer look at one Breach Secure Now learner. The panel is read-only and shows data already imported from Breach Secure Now.

## Before you start

- You need Security Awareness read access to the learner's company.
- Breach Secure Now must be connected and the learner mapped to a company in your scope.
- Your MSP handles user administration and changes in Breach Secure Now.

## Open the detail panel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the learner table">
    Open [the learner table](/user-guides/training/read-awareness-status). On **Security > Security Awareness**, click the **BSN** tab. On **Training > Training Menu > Security Training**, set the **Provider** filter to **Breach Secure Now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the learner">
    Use the **User** filter and the company selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the learner's row">
    A panel opens. Check the name, email, and company at the top before you read the figures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll through the sections and close the panel">
    The panel has five sections, listed below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Opening the panel does not refresh data from Breach Secure Now. If you change the company selector, close the panel and select the learner again.

## What the five sections show

| Section | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Security posture** | **Employee Secure Score**, **Security training score**, **Micro-training score**, and policy acknowledgment dates. |
| **Training progress** | **Security courses**, **Micro trainings**, and **Additional courses**, each as completed versus available, plus the last activity date for each. |
| **Phishing simulations** | **Emails sent**, **Emails clicked**, **Fail rate**, **Credentials captured**, and **Phish caught**. |
| **Dark web exposure** | **Breaches**, **Last breach**, **Breaches remediated**, and **Remediation training completed**. |
| **Account and delivery** | **Role**, **Tag**, **Product**, **Created**, **Last login**, **Welcome message received**, **Receiving micro training**, and **Positive opt-in**. |

Badges at the top can show **Locked**, **Email bouncing**, **Micro-training opt-out**, and **Active phishing campaign**. **Active** appears unless Breach Secure Now reports the account as inactive, so it is not a sign-in test.

A dash means the field was not supplied. Scores, phishing results, policy acknowledgments, and breach remediation are separate measures.

## If you see a concern

When the learner has breach or phishing activity, the panel tells you to review the latest event in Breach Secure Now before you act. The panel does not show the event history or offer a remediation button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Company users">
    Start with the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). Name the provider and learner, describe the concern, then review and confirm the request. Do not include passwords or captured credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="MSP staff">
    Check the company mapping and the source event in Breach Secure Now. Unlock an account, change delivery settings, assign training, or remediate in Breach Secure Now itself. Nothing in this panel saves a change or marks an exposure resolved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Clicking a row does nothing | Only Breach Secure Now rows open this panel. |
| Values look old | The panel uses the data already loaded. Ask your MSP to check the Breach Secure Now sync. |
| The wrong learner opened after changing companies | Close the panel and click the learner again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I unlock the learner or change their role here?">
    No. The panel only displays imported information. Your MSP makes changes in Breach Secure Now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the overall completion cover only security courses?">
    No. The learner table adds security courses, micro training, and additional courses together. The panel shows them separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a phishing click mean the account was compromised?">
    No. These are simulation results. Review the event with your MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening the panel refresh Breach Secure Now?">
    No. It shows the record already loaded in the table.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
