## Unified Projects navigation

For the unified experience, open **Projects** and select the **Onboarding projects** quick filter to find a portal's linked project. Under **Overview > Dashboard**, the staff-only **MSP Dashboard** view provides the onboarding overview and operational metrics; **Overview > Client Portal** contains **Tasks**, client **Documents**, **Pages**, **Activity**, **Key dates**, and **Portal settings**. Client uploads are separate from the project's staff-only **Files & Forms > Documents** area.

Reusable settings now live in **Settings > Projects**: **Templates** includes templates, checklists, and forms, and **Client Portals** holds portals, client roles, pages, and PSA defaults. Existing company and settings onboarding links redirect when the unified experience is available; references to the earlier Onboarding workspace below apply to portals still using that experience.

## Before you begin

You need onboarding read access to view this area and onboarding write access with active billing to save changes. Company scope and enabled features affect the available data. External documentation actions also require the corresponding provider connection and permissions.

Onboarding Forms are reusable, structured data-collection definitions you build once and attach to onboarding tasks. Unlike a free-text task, a form has typed fields (text, select, date, email, and more) so the answers you collect are clean and consistent. You can also **map** form fields to your documentation platform for use when reviewing and creating or updating assets in Hudu or IT Glue. Saving a form response and writing an external asset are separate outcomes.

<Note>
Forms are the structured successor to linking a Survey on a task. When a form task has a form attached, clients fill out the form; if no form is attached, the task falls back to the older inline survey experience.
</Note>

## Fill forms from a project

Forms can be used on regular projects without creating an onboarding portal. Open a project's **Files & Forms > Forms** area, choose an active form, fill its fields, and choose **Save progress** or **Submit**. The **Responses** table shows the saved entry; use **Edit** to correct a permitted response while its form is active.

Staff with **Share forms with clients** permission can select a form and switch on **Share this form with clients**. Clients with project access and form permissions can then fill it in. Clients see only shared forms and their own responses. Internal staff responses stay private, including responses saved before sharing was enabled. Turn the sharing switch off to revoke access; an archived shared form remains available to staff for unsharing.

**View forms and permitted responses** allows reading. **Submit and edit form responses** allows saving. A single-entry form keeps separate staff and individual-client responses; a repeatable form accepts additional entries. Staff can also review linked-portal responses when they hold onboarding read access.

After the archived-response update is rolled out, **View** opens permitted saved project responses to archived forms as read-only. Editing, new entries, **Save progress**, and **Submit** remain unavailable while the form is archived. The same project access, form sharing, and response ownership rules still apply.

Onboarding portal tasks keep their existing form workflow. Project response saves do not send data to Hudu or IT Glue automatically.

A form's repeat-entry setting determines whether multiple entries are allowed. After saving, check the responses table before submitting again. When you switch forms or leave with unsaved answers, review the unsaved-changes prompt before discarding them. These entries are separate from creating or updating Hudu or IT Glue assets.

See [Project Forms](/user-guides/projects/forms) for draft status, sharing, response links, and resuming an entry.

## Why Use Forms

- **Collect structured data** — typed fields instead of free text, so answers are consistent across clients
- **Reuse across portals** — build a form once and attach it to any template's tasks
- **Map to documentation** — prepare answers for reviewed Hudu asset or IT Glue flexible-asset creation
- **Allow repeat entries** — let clients submit multiple records (e.g. one entry per location or per admin account)

## Building a Form

Forms are managed in **Settings > Projects > Templates > Forms**, a workspace with a list of forms on the left and the selected form's details on the right.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Forms tab">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Templates > Forms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a form">
    Click **New Form**. A draft form named "New Form" is immediately saved and selected; closing the workspace does not undo its creation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set form details">
    In **Form Settings**, set:
    - **Name** — the form's display name
    - **Repeat Entries** — toggle on (**+ One More**) to let clients submit multiple entries, or off (**Single**) for one
    - **Description** — optional context
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add fields">
    Click **Add Field** for each piece of information you want to collect. Adding a field saves a new default field immediately. Edit its label, type, required setting, and other details, then use the form header **Save** to persist your edits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the form">
    Click the header **Save** to save form details, field edits, field ordering, and mapping edits. Resolve incomplete-row warnings or errors, then use **Activate** when the form is ready. Activation changes status separately; save edits before using it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Save behavior and lifecycle

| Action | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| **New Form** | Creates a saved draft form |
| **Add Field** or add a provider mapping | Creates the default field or mapping immediately |
| Edit form details, field settings, order, or mapping targets | Held for the form header **Save** |
| **Save** | Saves form details and valid field/mapping edits; incomplete rows are reported |
| **Activate**, **Revert to Draft**, **Archive**, **Restore** | Saves the status change immediately |
| Delete a field or mapping | Deletes that saved row immediately |

Save before adding or deleting rows, changing status, or switching forms: those actions can reload the stored form. Save is not an all-or-nothing transaction. If it reports an error, inspect the reloaded fields and mappings to see what persisted, then correct the remaining work. Do not assume a failed save restored the previous form.

Use the archive visibility control to find archived forms. **Restore** returns a form to Draft; review and activate it separately. Archiving a definition is not deletion of submitted client entries. Check existing portal tasks before changing a shared definition.

### Field Types

Each field has a **Type** that controls how clients answer it:

| Type | Use for |
|------|---------|
| **Short Text** | Names, short identifiers |
| **Long Text** | Notes, descriptions |
| **Single Select** | One choice from a list |
| **Multi Select** | Multiple choices from a list |
| **Yes / No** | Boolean answers |
| **Date** | Dates |
| **Email** | Email addresses |
| **Phone** | Phone numbers |
| **Number** | Numeric values |
| **URL** | Web links |
| **Location** | Select a location from the portal company |
| **Device** | Select devices from the portal company |
| **Contact** | Select a contact from the portal company |

Location, Device, and Contact choices come from company data. Do not enter their options manually. If a picker is empty, check the company and available records.

### Field Settings

Each field supports:

- **Label** — what the client sees
- **Required** — whether an answer is mandatory
- **Placeholder** — example text inside the input
- **Help Text** — guidance shown under the field
- **Options** — for select fields, one option per line or comma-separated
Field keys are generated from labels. Give fields clear, distinct labels, and check existing submissions before changing the structure of a shared form.

<Tip>
Write labels and help text from the client's perspective. "Microsoft 365 admin email" is clearer than "M365 UPN".
</Tip>

## Mapping Fields to Hudu or IT Glue

Mapping lets a field's answers populate your documentation platform. Each field has a **Field Mappings** subsection.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a mapping">
    Under a field, click **Hudu** or **IT Glue** to create a mapping. This saves a default mapping immediately; review the chosen target before using it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the target">
    Select the **Asset layout** (Hudu) or **Flexible asset type** (IT Glue), then pick the **Field Name** to map to. These options come from your synced documentation data — you pick from dropdowns, never type IDs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the form header **Save** after editing the mapping. Complete both the asset format and target field; an incomplete mapping can be skipped with a warning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Mapping requires synced documentation data. If you haven't run a Hudu or IT Glue sync yet, you'll see a prompt to run the documentation integration sync first, then return to map fields.
</Note>

## Create a form from an asset format

Use **From asset** to choose a synced Hudu asset layout or IT Glue flexible asset type. Review the field count and any unsupported-field warning, then click **Create form**. This creates a draft form with supported fields, provider mappings, and an asset-name field. Review required settings and omitted fields before activation. It creates a form definition, not an external asset or a copy of every provider record.

If you use the workspace's AI authoring assistant, review the generated forms before applying them. Applying creates saved forms; it is not merely a preview. Check the list after a partial failure before repeating the application.

## Review a submitted entry before creating an asset

On the portal **Tasks** tab, open a form task and the relevant saved entry. Use **Create asset** (or the existing-asset management action) when offered. Review the provider, mapped asset format, asset name, and field values, then choose **Create in Hudu** or **Create in IT Glue**. An already created asset offers the corresponding **Update in …** action.

Edits in this review dialog change what is sent to the provider; they do not rewrite the saved client form entry. Read the result and any unresolved-field warning. A **Saved** form badge is not proof that an external asset exists or was updated.

If the target is missing, check the active integration, company mapping, and synced asset schema. Resyncing the provider and correcting a stale mapping may be necessary. Avoid repeatedly creating assets after an ambiguous result; first check whether the prior action succeeded.

## Attaching a Form to a Task

Forms attach to tasks in the **template builder**. When editing a task with the **Form** evidence type, select the form to attach. You can also override whether that specific task allows multiple entries.

See [Onboarding Templates](/user-guides/client-onboarding/templates) for task configuration details.

## What the Client Sees

When a client opens a form task in their portal, the form renders inline with all its fields laid out by type — dropdowns for selects, toggles for Yes/No, date pickers, and so on. Required fields are marked.

- Clients fill out the fields and click **Save Form**
- Wait for the save result; a previously displayed **Saved** badge can remain while the client edits, so changed values need another **Save Form**
- If the form allows repeat entries, submitted entries appear as chips (Entry 1, Entry 2, …) and a **One More** button lets clients add another

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does New Form only open an unsaved form?">
    No. New Form immediately creates and selects a saved draft. Archive it if you created it by mistake and no longer need it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to save every field separately?">
    Use the form header Save for form details, field edits, ordering, and mapping edits. Adding or deleting a field or mapping is a separate immediate change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Activate also save the edits I was typing?">
    Save edits first. Activate changes status separately and reloads the stored form; it is not a substitute for Save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does an incomplete-row warning mean?">
    The valid work may have saved while incomplete fields or mappings were skipped. Review the reloaded form, complete the missing labels or mapping targets, and save again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does saving a client response create a Hudu or IT Glue asset?">
    A saved response alone does not prove that. Review the entry through Create asset or Manage assets, select the provider action, and verify its result. Configured automation is a separate path.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does From asset copy real client assets?">
    It creates a draft form from the synced asset format, with supported fields and mappings. It does not import every external record or create an external asset.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are Location, Device, or Contact choices empty?">
    These fields load company records. Check the portal company, available records, and access; manually authored select options do not supply these pickers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Restore make an archived form active?">
    No. Restore returns it to Draft. Review the form and use Activate separately when ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing values in Create asset alter the client response?">
    No. Those edits affect the provider payload. The submitted form entry remains unchanged.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
