Client Onboarding lets MSP staff build reusable onboarding templates, launch a client portal for each company, and track every step from discovery through go-live. This page is for MSP staff. Company users should start with [Open your onboarding portal](/user-guides/client-onboarding/open-your-onboarding).

<Note>
These guides describe the unified Projects experience, where each onboarding portal is linked to a project. If your organization still uses the earlier experience, onboarding settings are under **Settings > Client Onboarding** and each portal is under the company's **Onboarding** tab. Those links open the matching Projects page once your organization moves to the unified experience.
</Note>

## Before you start

- Client Onboarding must be turned on for your organization. Ask your MSPortal administrator to enable it.
- You need onboarding access in your role. See [Permissions](#permissions).
- Creating or changing templates and portals also needs an active subscription.

## How onboarding works

Client onboarding has three layers.

1. **Templates** are reusable blueprints with phases, tasks, documents, forms, and approvals.
2. **Portals** are copies of a template made for one company. Each portal has its own contacts, progress, and key dates.
3. **The client portal** is the secure page where clients complete tasks, upload files, sign agreements, and follow progress. Clients open it from an emailed link and do not need an account.

## Where things live

| What you need | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Templates, checklists, and forms | **Settings > Projects > Templates** |
| Portal directory, client roles, reusable pages, PSA defaults | **Settings > Projects > Client Portals** |
| Which project types run onboarding | **Settings > Projects > Project Settings > Project Types** |
| Find an onboarding project | **Projects**, then **Project category > Onboarding projects** |
| MSP overview of one onboarding | Open the project, then **Overview > MSP Dashboard** |
| Tasks and their status | **Project Plan > Tasks** |
| Client uploads and forms | **Files & Forms > Documents** and **Files & Forms > Forms** |
| Client contacts and invitations | **Team & Contacts** |
| Pages, activity, key dates, portal settings | **Client Portal** |

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Client Onboarding">
    Ask your MSPortal administrator to enable **Client Onboarding** for your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a template">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Templates > Templates** and select **New Template**. See [Templates](/user-guides/client-onboarding/templates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a portal">
    Go to **Projects**, select **New project**, choose an onboarding template, and finish the wizard. This creates a draft portal and its linked project. See [Managing Portals](/user-guides/client-onboarding/managing-portals#start-from-projects).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and start the portal">
    Use **View as** to check what the client sees, then select **Start Portal** on **Overview > MSP Dashboard**. See [Quick Start](/user-guides/client-onboarding/quick-start).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress">
    Follow tasks under **Project Plan > Tasks**, review uploads under **Files & Forms > Documents**, and watch the **Active Onboarding** widget on your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Permissions

| Permission | What it allows |
|------------|----------------|
| `read_settings_onboarding` | View onboarding templates and portals |
| `write_settings_onboarding` | Create and manage templates, launch and manage portals, use **View as** |
| `manage_settings_onboarding` | Full administrative access, including **Convert to Client** |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which guide should I read first?">
    Start with Quick Start. Then use Managing Portals for day-to-day work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is this the same as Launch Center?">
    No. Launch Center helps MSP staff set up their own MSPortal rollout. Client Onboarding manages onboarding for your clients.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do clients build templates or manage contacts?">
    No. Clients use the portal you share with them. MSP staff manage templates, contacts, and access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a PSA to use onboarding?">
    No. When you link a project, choose **MSPortal Projects** to track the work in MSPortal. PSA options appear when the company is mapped to an active PSA integration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does View as prove a client can open the portal?">
    No. **View as** shows the portal as a contact or role would see it, and nothing you enter is saved. To test real access, start the portal and use a real invitation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
