## Unified Projects navigation

For the unified experience, open **Projects** and select the **Onboarding projects** quick filter to find a portal's linked project. Under **Overview**, the staff-only **MSP Dashboard** view provides the onboarding overview and operational metrics; **Project Plan > Tasks** contains the delivery and onboarding work, **Files & Forms > Documents** brings project files and client uploads together, and **Client Portal** contains **Pages**, **Activity**, **Key dates**, and **Portal settings**. Access and sharing still follow your permissions.

Reusable settings now live in **Settings > Projects**: **Templates** includes templates, checklists, and forms, and **Client Portals** holds portals, client roles, pages, and PSA defaults. Existing company and settings onboarding links redirect when the unified experience is available; references to the earlier Onboarding workspace below apply to portals still using that experience.

## Before you begin

Your organization must have Client Onboarding enabled. Viewing requires onboarding read access; creating or changing templates and portals requires onboarding write access and active billing. **Convert to Client** requires onboarding management access. Your role and company scope can limit what appears.

The Client Onboarding module provides a workspace for onboarding new clients to your MSP. Build reusable templates, launch branded portals for each company, and track every step of the process — from initial discovery through full deployment.

## Overview

Client onboarding in MSPortal works in three layers:

1. **Templates** — Reusable blueprints that define your onboarding process (phases, tasks, documents, surveys, approvals)
2. **Portals** — Draft or active instances of templates created for a specific company, with their own contacts, progress, and timeline
3. **Client Portal** — A branded, secure experience where clients complete tasks, upload documents, sign agreements, and track progress

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Client Onboarding">
    Contact your account administrator to enable **Client Onboarding** for your tenant. Once enabled, the onboarding settings and features become available across your tenant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Template">
    Go to **Settings > Projects > Templates > Templates** and click **New Template**. Define your phases, tasks, and evidence requirements. See the [Templates guide](/user-guides/client-onboarding/templates) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch a Portal">
    Choose **Projects > New project**, select an onboarding template, and complete the Client portal step to create a draft. The earlier company experience uses **Onboarding > Start Onboarding**. Review project linking, configuration, and the client preview before using Start Portal. See [Managing Portals](/user-guides/client-onboarding/managing-portals).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Progress">
    Monitor task completion, review documents, and communicate with clients from the company's onboarding tab. Your dashboard also shows active portals and assigned tasks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How changes take effect

Templates define future portals; portal phases and tasks are copied at creation. Template detail edits use **Save Changes**, while many builder item controls save separately. Portal creation saves a draft before optional project linking. **Start & Send Invites** activates the portal and queues invitations; **View as** is a separate MSP preview that does not save client responses.

Review automation and email/report timing before creating a portal. Draft status prevents normal client entry but does not turn configured automation or explicit project actions into previews.

## Permissions

| Permission | Capability |
|------------|------------|
| `read_settings_onboarding` | View onboarding templates and portals |
| `write_settings_onboarding` | Create and manage templates, launch and manage portals |
| `manage_settings_onboarding` | Full administrative access to onboarding settings |

<Note>
Client onboarding must be enabled at the tenant level before the feature appears in settings and company pages. Contact your account administrator to enable it.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which guide should an MSP use first?">
    Use Quick Start to prepare a template and draft portal, then Managing Portals for day-to-day work and lifecycle actions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is this the same as Launch Center?">
    No. Launch Center helps MSP staff configure their own portal rollout. Client Onboarding manages onboarding workspaces for individual companies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do clients build templates or manage portal contacts?">
    These guides describe MSP administration. Clients use their assigned portal to complete tasks and provide information according to their access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a PSA to use onboarding?">
    No. Project linking offers MSPortal Projects for native tracking. External PSA options depend on the connected provider and company mapping.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an MSP preview prove the portal is ready for clients?">
    It verifies presentation in a selected contact or role context. Real invitation access, email delivery, and saved responses require separate verification.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
