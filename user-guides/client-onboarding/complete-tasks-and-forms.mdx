This guide helps Company contacts find their assigned onboarding work, complete checklists, provide requested information, and save form entries.

## Before you start

- You need access to an active onboarding portal. See [Open your onboarding portal](/user-guides/client-onboarding/open-your-onboarding).
- Your IT provider decides which tasks, phases, and forms you can see. Your portal separates work assigned to you from work your provider is doing.

## Find your next task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your portal">
    [Open your onboarding portal](/user-guides/client-onboarding/open-your-onboarding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at Needs your attention">
    This list at the top shows what is waiting on you. Select an item to open it. When nothing is waiting, it says **You are caught up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or open Your Tasks">
    Open **Your Tasks** to see everything assigned to you, grouped by phase. Select a task to open it. Depending on how your provider set up the portal, details open in a pop-up or expand in place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read before you act">
    Read the instructions, the due date, and any work that must be finished first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="An example task card in an MSP preview of the client portal. Select a card to open the item. Preview actions are not saved.">
  <img src="/images/product/onboarding-next-task.png" alt="Secure file request task card with its phase and an open arrow in an MSP preview" width="888" height="168" />
</Frame>

A task marked **Blocked** is waiting on other work. The portal shows the items that must be done first. Marking unrelated tasks complete does not unlock it. If the status looks wrong, ask your provider.

## Complete a checklist or simple task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the instructions">
    Open the task and check what it asks for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check off the checklist">
    If the task has a checklist, select each item after you do the work. Every checkbox saves right away, and you can change it while the task is still open. Finish all items before the completion button turns on. The portal says **Complete all items to enable task completion.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the task">
    Select **Mark Complete** and wait for the success message. Checking every item is separate from marking the task complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a simple task with no checklist, do the work and select **Mark Complete**. If you cannot finish a task, ask your provider for help instead of marking it complete.

## Provide requested information

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your answer">
    Some tasks have a text box that says **Provide the requested information...**. Enter a useful answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit it">
    Select **Mark Complete**. Typing alone does not submit anything, and an empty answer cannot complete a task that needs information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When a task asks for an upload, a credential, a signature, a survey, a meeting, or training, use that task's own controls. A general comment does not replace required evidence. See the guides in [Related guides](#related-guides).

## Save a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields">
    Open the form task and complete the required fields. When a field offers company records, pick from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the entry">
    Select **Save Form**. Fix any validation message and select it again. Wait for **Saved** before you leave. Fields do not save as you type. If you edit a saved entry, select **Save Form** again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or edit entries">
    If the form allows several entries, select **One More** to start another, fill it in, and select **Save Form**. Select an **Entry** badge to open an existing entry for editing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A form that allows only one entry has no **One More** button. Ask your provider if you need to submit more records.

If a list says no options are available for your company, ask your provider to prepare those records. Do not type an unrelated value into another field.

## Discuss a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the comments">
    Open the task and find its comments area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and send">
    Select **Add comment**, write your message, and select **Send comment**. Wait for it to show in the thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Comments save on their own. They do not complete a task or submit a form. Use a comment for task-specific questions, or **Ask a Question** at the top for a general question. Do not put passwords in comments. Your provider's internal notes are not shown to you.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Mark Complete** is not available | Finish every checklist item first. Check that the task is not **Blocked**. |
| You can read the portal but cannot save | The portal may be completed, your session may have expired, or the task may be blocked. Read the message on screen and contact your provider. |
| A form answer did not save | Select **Save Form** and wait for **Saved**. Typing does not save. |
| There is no **One More** button | The form allows one entry. Ask your provider. |
| A list has no options | Your provider needs to add the records. Ask them. |
| You marked a task complete by mistake | Ask your provider to reopen it. You cannot undo it yourself. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does checking the last checklist item complete the task?">
    No. Check every item, then select **Mark Complete**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are my form answers saved automatically?">
    No. Select **Save Form**, including after you edit a saved entry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I submit more than one form entry?">
    Only when your provider turned that on. Use **One More** when it shows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I complete my provider's tasks?">
    No. Complete the tasks assigned to you. Your provider handles its own work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a comment submit the form?">
    No. Comments, **Save Form**, and **Mark Complete** are separate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I finished my tasks. Is the onboarding finished?">
    Not necessarily. Your provider may still have work, reviews, or dependencies to finish before it closes the portal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
