This guide is for Company contacts invited to an onboarding portal. It shows how to open your invitation, verify your email, and come back later from your regular portal account.

## Before you start

- Your IT service provider creates the onboarding portal, adds you as a contact, and starts it. Nothing opens until they start it.
- You need the invitation email sent to you. Use your own invitation, not a colleague's.
- Access to your regular Company portal does not give you access to every onboarding portal.

## Open an emailed invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation">
    Select the link in the onboarding invitation email from your IT service provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    On **Verify Your Email**, enter the email address the invitation was sent to. The page shows a masked hint. Select **Send Verification Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Open the verification email and follow its link. The link expires after 30 minutes. If it has not arrived, check your junk folder. When the countdown ends, select **Resend Verification Email**. You can request up to 5 emails.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to the portal">
    On **Complete Verification**, select **Continue to Portal**. Opening the email link alone does not finish signing in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your workspace">
    Check the portal and company name. Then review the tasks and information your provider made available. It can include documents, forms, approvals, and training.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You do not need to create a regular portal account first. Your session lasts 30 days.

## Return from your Company account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in to your normal Company portal with the email your provider added to the onboarding contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open My onboarding">
    Select **My onboarding** in the sidebar, next to **Company**. It shows when your account has an onboarding portal. The page is titled **Your onboarding**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Find the active portal and select **Continue**. You skip the invitation email steps when your signed-in email and company match an active contact.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the page says **No onboarding portal is linked to this account**, ask your IT provider to add the email you sign in with. Your invitation may have gone to a different address, or the portal may no longer be active.

## Ask your provider a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ask a Question">
    Inside the portal, select **Ask a Question**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your question">
    Type it in the box. Questions go to the team managing your onboarding, and they can reply to your email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Select **Send Question** and wait for **Question sent!**. Select **Cancel** to close without sending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the portal says it could not send, or that no team members are assigned to receive questions, contact your provider through your usual support channel. Do not type passwords in a question. Use the credential task instead.

## Troubleshooting

| Message or situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Portal Not Started** | Ask your provider to start the portal. An invitation does not start a draft portal. |
| "That email doesn't match the one on file for this invitation." | Use the address the invitation was sent to. If it is wrong, ask your provider to update your contact. |
| "This verification has expired." | Go back to your original invitation link and start verification again. Ask for a new invitation if needed. |
| **Invalid Link** | Request a new verification link from the verification page. |
| "Too many failed attempts" or "Too many attempts" | Contact your provider for a new invite link. Do not keep trying addresses. |
| **Invitation Link Unavailable** | The link is invalid, expired, or revoked. Open the original invitation email, or ask your provider to resend it. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a regular portal account to use my invitation?">
    No. The invitation flow verifies your email and opens the onboarding portal. A regular Company account is useful for coming back through **My onboarding** when your provider has linked the same email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I sign in but not see my onboarding?">
    Your signed-in account must match an available contact on an active portal for the same company. Ask your provider to check your email, company, contact access, and the portal status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I forward my invitation to a colleague?">
    No. Ask your provider to add that person as a contact so they get their own link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company administrator add onboarding contacts?">
    No. Your MSP manages onboarding contacts and access. Ask them to add or update a contact.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Portal Not Started mean I typed something wrong?">
    No. Your provider has not started the portal yet. Ask them to start it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are my tasks submitted just because I opened the portal?">
    No. Each task has its own completion or submit button. Wait for a confirmation after you submit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where does Ask a Question go?">
    It goes to the team your provider set up to receive questions. If no one is set up, the portal tells you. Use your normal support channel then.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
