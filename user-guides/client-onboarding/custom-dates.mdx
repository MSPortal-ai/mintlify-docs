**Audience:** MSP staff setting portal milestones. You need onboarding read access to view the workspace and onboarding write access with active billing to add, edit, or delete dates. Clients can view the displayed countdown; they do not edit these dates.

Custom dates let you capture the key milestones for an onboarding portal, such as the kickoff, hardware delivery, go-live, or the end of a trial period. Mark one date as the countdown and the portal progress summary can show days remaining for a valid target date. Confirm the client presentation through the relevant preview and display settings.

## Unified Projects navigation

For the unified experience, open **Projects** and select the **Onboarding projects** quick filter to find a portal's linked project. Under **Overview**, the staff-only **MSP Dashboard** view provides the onboarding overview and operational metrics; **Project Plan > Tasks** contains the delivery and onboarding work, **Files & Forms > Documents** brings project files and client uploads together, and **Client Portal** contains **Pages**, **Activity**, **Key dates**, and **Portal settings**. Access and sharing still follow your permissions.

Reusable settings now live in **Settings > Projects**: **Templates** includes templates, checklists, and forms, and **Client Portals** holds portals, client roles, pages, and PSA defaults. Existing company and settings onboarding links redirect when the unified experience is available; references to the earlier Onboarding workspace below apply to portals still using that experience.

## Why Use Custom Dates

- **Anchor the onboarding to a deadline** so clients know when it must be complete
- **Track milestones** beyond the built-in portal status
- **Set expectations** with a visible days-remaining counter
- **Pre-populate standard milestones** from your onboarding templates

## Opening the Dates Manager

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Company Onboarding Tab">
    Open the linked project and choose **Client Portal > Key dates**. In the earlier company workspace, open **Onboarding**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Manage Dates">
    On the **Overview** tab, click **Manage dates** in the action bar. This opens the dates manager dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dates manager lists every custom date on the portal, with the countdown-marked date highlighted by a **Countdown** badge.

## Adding a Date

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Date">
    Click **Add Date** in the dates manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the Fields">
    - **Label** (required), the display name, for example "Go-Live" or "Hardware Delivery"
    - **Description** (optional), additional context for your team and the client
    - **Target Date** (optional), leave empty if you have not committed to a date yet
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Show Countdown">
    Turn on **Show countdown** to display this date as the active countdown at the top of the portal. Only one date can be the countdown at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Date** to create it, or **Save Changes** when editing an existing date. Wait for success, then check the saved row and portal summary. **Cancel** discards the unsaved edit. There is no separate global save for the dates dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the Countdown Displays

When a date has **Show countdown** enabled and a valid target date, the progress summary can display:

- The milestone label
- The target date
- A live calculation of days remaining, or **Today** on the target date, or **X days overdue** once it has passed

The countdown is a calendar-day indicator, not a continuously ticking deadline clock. A blank target can be saved as a milestone, but cannot produce a countdown. An overdue date does not automatically complete, block, or reschedule tasks.

## Template-Level Dates

You can also define custom dates on an onboarding **template**. Template dates use an **offset in days** from the portal start date rather than an absolute target date.

Review the dates copied into the new draft: offsets are resolved when the portal is created. Do not assume keeping a draft for several days and starting it later will shift its milestone dates automatically. If a template date has no offset, the target date is left empty for you to fill in from the dates manager.

This lets you set up a standard sequence once, for example "Kickoff = day 0, Hardware = day 14, Go-Live = day 45", and have every new portal start with those milestones pre-populated.

## Editing and Deleting Dates

- Use the row **Edit** control to change its label, description, target date, or countdown setting, then **Save Changes**.
- Enabling countdown on a different date and saving clears the previous selection. If saving fails, recheck both dates rather than assuming the old countdown remains selected.
- Use the row **Delete** control and confirm to remove a date. Removing a milestone does not delete the portal tasks or phases.

## What the Client Sees

The portal summary shows the selected countdown milestone when it has a valid target date. Do not treat the MSP dates manager as a complete preview of a client-visible milestone list. Check the actual client presentation before telling a contact where to find a date.

Clients do not see internal fields like template references, created timestamps, or edit controls.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I save a milestone without choosing its date?">
    Yes. Leave Target Date empty until agreed. It cannot display a countdown until it has a valid target date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does closing Manage dates save my edit?">
    No. Use Add Date or Save Changes inside the editor and wait for success. Cancel discards that unsaved edit; earlier saved dates remain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will starting a draft later move its template dates?">
    Do not assume that. Review the dates copied when the draft was created, then edit actual targets before activation if the schedule changed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does moving a milestone reschedule the tasks?">
    The milestone is a separate date record. Review task due dates and dependencies separately instead of assuming that changing the countdown moved the work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is no countdown visible?">
    Check Show countdown, a valid Target Date, and the portal presentation. A blank date can exist in the MSP manager without a client countdown.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I choose another countdown date?">
    Saving the new countdown selection clears the old selection. If a save fails, inspect both rows and restore the intended choice if needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an overdue countdown mean onboarding failed or stopped?">
    No. It shows elapsed calendar days relative to the milestone. Review actual task progress and blockers separately.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
