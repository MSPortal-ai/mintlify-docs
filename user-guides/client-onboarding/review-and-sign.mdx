Your IT provider can ask for an approval or a signature in your [onboarding portal](/user-guides/client-onboarding/open-your-onboarding). An approval records a decision. A signature task asks you to review content and draw your signature. This guide is for Company contacts.

## Before you start

- You need access to the assigned item. Your provider handles provider-owned approvals.
- These steps cover onboarding requests. They are not the same as the [Notifications approval queue](/user-guides/notifications/review-approvals).
- A **Blocked** item needs its prerequisites finished first.

## Approve or reject a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the approval item">
    Find the pending item in your onboarding workspace and read its summary and description. Already decided items display their status instead of decision buttons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the supporting document">
    If the request includes a document, review it in the document area. If it reports **Unable to load document**, ask your provider to resolve that before you make a decision that depends on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add any explanation">
    Select **Add notes (optional)** and enter the context you want to submit with your decision. Typing notes alone does not send them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your decision">
    Select **Approve** or **Reject**. This submits the decision directly; there is no second confirmation. Wait for **Approved** or **Rejected** and the decision date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The completed item does not offer an undo or change-decision button. Contact your provider if you selected the wrong decision or the request needs to change.

## Sign a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the document">
    Open the signature task and read the document and any **Additional notes**. Some tasks show text to sign instead of an attached PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign on the line">
    Draw your signature on the line marked **Sign here**. To start again, select **Clear**: the ink is taken back stroke by stroke until the pad is empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Signed pill">
    Pause after your last stroke and the pad folds into a **Signed** pill. To add to or change your signature, select the pill. The pad opens again with your signature still on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Select **I agree and sign**. The button needs a drawn signature. Drawing alone does not submit it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Wait for **Signature submitted**. The task then shows your signature and the signer details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You cannot edit or replace a signature after you submit it. Until then, you can reopen the **Signed** pill or select **Clear** as often as you need.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **I agree and sign** is not available | Draw a signature first. If you selected **Clear**, the pad is empty again, so sign once more. Check that the task is not **Blocked**, already signed, or in a completed portal. |
| The pad shrank to a **Signed** pill | That is expected after a pause. Select the pill to reopen the pad with your signature kept. Nothing is submitted until you select **I agree and sign**. |
| **Unable to load document** | Reopen the item and check your access. If it still fails, tell your provider the item name and the message. |
| You chose the wrong decision | Ask your provider to arrange a corrected request. There is no undo. |
| You signed the wrong document | Ask your provider for a new request. A submitted signature cannot be changed. |
| The item shows a result but no buttons | It was already decided. Your provider can reopen it if needed. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I approve an onboarding request without signing it?">
    An approval and a signature are separate item types. Use the action requested on the item; approving one item does not sign a separate document task.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Approve open a confirmation first?">
    No. In an onboarding approval item, **Approve** and **Reject** submit directly, including any optional notes you entered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are approval notes saved automatically?">
    No. They are included when you submit the decision. Leaving the item before deciding does not submit those notes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my decision or signature?">
    The Company view has no undo decision or edit submitted signature control. Ask your provider to arrange a corrected request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the signing button disabled?">
    Draw a signature first. Also check whether the task is blocked, already signed, or in a workspace that is no longer writable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the supporting document will not load?">
    Reopen the item and check your access. If it still fails, contact your provider with the item name and displayed error. Do not infer the document's content from its filename.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does approval finish the entire onboarding?">
    No. It completes that decision. Other tasks, documents, approvals, and provider work can still be outstanding.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
