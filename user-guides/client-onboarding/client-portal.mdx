The client onboarding portal is the workspace your IT provider shares for one onboarding. Company contacts use it to complete assigned work, send information, review requests, and follow progress. MSP staff can use this page to understand and explain what clients see.

<Note>
Company contacts follow the task guides below. MSP staff create templates, set up access, and manage submissions in the [MSP guides](#msp-setup-guides).
</Note>

## How clients get in

Clients open the portal from an emailed invitation, with no account or password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation link">
    Each contact has their own link. Links should not be shared between people.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the email address">
    The portal asks the client to confirm the invited email address and emails a verification link. The link expires after 30 minutes. A client can request up to 5 verification emails.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to the portal">
    The client selects **Continue to Portal**. A session starts and lasts 30 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Invitation links expire after 30 days. MSP staff can send, replace, or revoke a link under **Team & Contacts**. See [Managing Portals](/user-guides/client-onboarding/managing-portals#manage-contacts). A portal that is still a draft shows **Portal Not Started** until you start it.

## What appears in the portal

Your provider chooses the visible phases, tasks, documents, approvals, and pages. The role assigned to a contact can change what that contact sees, so another contact may see a different set.

The header shows the company, portal name, and who is signed in. It also has a language switcher, a light and dark theme switch, and **Ask a Question**. Below it, the overview shows overall progress as a ring, counts for **Your items** and **Our team**, **Overdue** items, progress through each phase, and up to three key dates. **Needs your attention** lists what is waiting on the client. When nothing is, it shows **You are caught up**.

The page then has these sections, also shown in a side menu.

- **Pages** are read-only reference articles. Reading a page does not sign off a task.
- **Your Tasks** holds work assigned to the client, grouped by phase.
- **Documents** holds upload requests and reference material. Submitting a file and getting approval are separate events.
- **Approvals** asks the client for a decision. Items already decided show their result.
- **Our Progress** shows the provider's own work that you chose to make visible.

Depending on the portal's **Client task editing** setting, an item opens in a pop-up or expands in place. A **Blocked** item names the prerequisites that must be done first.

## What saves and when

| Action | When it saves |
| --- | --- |
| Change a checklist checkbox | Right away. Overall task completion is separate |
| Type an information response | When the client selects **Mark Complete** |
| Fill in an onboarding form | When the client selects **Save Form**, including edits to a saved entry |
| Answer a linked survey | When the client selects **Submit Form** |
| Select files for a document request | Uploading starts right away |
| Choose a secure file | After the client confirms **Encrypt & Upload** |
| Enter credentials or choose a Bulk attachment | After **Submit Credentials**, then **Submit** in the confirmation |
| Draw a signature | When the client selects **I agree and sign** |
| Add approval notes | With **Approve** or **Reject** |
| Read a page, open a video, or download a calendar file | Does not complete a task |

A client should wait for the result on the item before leaving. If the result is unclear, check the item's status before repeating the action.

## Questions and progress

Clients use **Ask a Question** for a general question, and the comment thread on a task for task-specific discussion. Sending a comment does not submit a form or complete the task. Your internal notes never show to clients.

Finishing all visible work does not finish the onboarding. Other contacts, provider tasks, reviews, and dependencies may still be open. A completed portal is read-only.

## Security

| Protection | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Token-based access | Each contact has a unique link, stored hashed |
| Email verification | Contacts verify their email before they can open the portal |
| Sessions | A session lasts 30 days |
| No account needed | Clients do not create an account or password |
| Encrypted credentials | Credentials and secure files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and you can reveal or download each one once |
| Expiry | Credentials and secure files expire after 30 days if unused |
| Signatures | A signature records the signer's name, email, IP address, time, and a content hash |
| Access tracking | The portal records last access and visit count for each contact |
| Revocable access | You can revoke a contact's access at any time |

## Preview as a client

MSP staff with onboarding write access can use **View as** in the page header to see a contact's or role's view. A preview does not save anything and does not prove a real client can sign in. See [Managing Portals](/user-guides/client-onboarding/managing-portals#previewing-as-a-client).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a Company administrator manage onboarding contacts?">
    No. The MSP manages onboarding contacts and their access. Ask your provider to add a contact or change access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can everyone at my company use my invitation?">
    No. Each contact needs their own invitation. Forwarding a link does not change whose it is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does another contact see different tasks?">
    Your provider controls visibility and contact roles. Ask them to check the missing item and your contact's role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I finish my provider's work?">
    No. Complete the items assigned to you. The provider handles its own work, shown under **Our Progress**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a successful MSP preview submit a client response?">
    No. Preview entries are not saved and do not prove a contact has opened the portal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I read a completed portal but not save?">
    A completed portal stays readable and turns off submissions. Contact your provider if more action is needed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
