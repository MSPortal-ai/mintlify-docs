This guide helps Company contacts join a scheduled onboarding meeting, open training, submit a linked survey, and read reference pages.

## Before you start

- Open the item in your [onboarding portal](/user-guides/client-onboarding/open-your-onboarding).
- Your provider chooses which meetings, training, surveys, and pages you see.

## Join a scheduled meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting task">
    Check the date, time, and duration it shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your calendar">
    Select **Add to Calendar** to download a calendar file. Open or import the file in your calendar app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join">
    When it is time, select **Join Meeting** if your provider added a meeting link. It opens the meeting service in a new tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Downloading the calendar file does not send an RSVP, reschedule the meeting, or complete the task. If the task says your provider will schedule the meeting, there is no date yet. If a date shows without **Join Meeting**, ask your provider for the location or joining instructions.

## Open training content

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the training task">
    Read what it asks you to do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch or attend">
    For video training, watch the embedded video, or select **Open Video** to open the original link. YouTube, Vimeo, and Loom links can show an embedded player. Other links open on the video's own page. For **In-Person Training**, read the instructions and attend the session your provider arranged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check completion">
    If the task stays open and has no completion button, ask your provider to confirm it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the task says "Training content will be provided by your service provider," ask when it will be ready. Watching a video does not complete the task. Onboarding training is separate from the portal's [course library and My Training](/user-guides/training/index).

## Submit a linked survey

Some form tasks contain a linked survey instead of an onboarding form with a **Save Form** button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read and answer the questions">
    Open the task and complete its displayed questions. Required questions must have an answer; select all applicable choices where multiple selections are supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the response">
    Select **Submit Form**. Answers are not submitted as you type. Correct any required-field error and submit again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check completion">
    Wait for **Form completed** or **Form completed on** with a date. The completed view does not expose an edit or resubmit control.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the task says no form is linked, your provider needs to finish its setup. For a form with **Save Form**, **Entry** badges, or **One More**, follow [complete tasks and forms](/user-guides/client-onboarding/complete-tasks-and-forms).

## Read reference pages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the pages">
    Open the **Pages** section of your portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read a page">
    Select a page title to expand its article. Select it again to collapse it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Pages hold reference information such as a welcome guide or support instructions. Reading a page does not submit a response, sign a document, or complete a task. You cannot edit pages. Tell your provider if one needs updating.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Join Meeting** button | Your provider may not have scheduled the meeting or added a link. Ask for the joining details. |
| **Add to Calendar** did nothing visible | It downloads a file. Open the file from your downloads, or import it in your calendar app. |
| "No form is currently linked to this task." | Your provider needs to finish setting up the task. Contact them. |
| "Please answer all required questions." | Answer every required question, then select **Submit Form** again. |
| A training task will not complete | Ask your provider to confirm it. Some training tasks have no completion button. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I reschedule an onboarding meeting?">
    The Company meeting task displays the schedule and joining details. It does not provide a reschedule action. Contact your provider to request a change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Add to Calendar put the event into my calendar automatically?">
    It downloads a calendar file. Your calendar application handles opening or importing it; check that the event was added there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is there no Join Meeting button?">
    The meeting may not be scheduled, or the provider may not have supplied a meeting URL. Ask for the joining instructions or physical location.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will watching onboarding training award a course certificate?">
    The onboarding video task does not provide a course-certificate action. Certificates for eligible courses belong to the separate [training course workflow](/user-guides/training/certificates).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Save Form and Submit Form?">
    **Save Form** saves an onboarding form and can support editing entries or adding more. A linked survey uses **Submit Form** and then displays a completed message. Follow the controls on your task.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit a survey after submitting?">
    The completed survey view does not offer an edit button. Ask your provider how to correct a response.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to mark each reference page as read?">
    No. Reference pages are read-only articles without an acknowledgement action. Complete any separate task or approval that your provider requests.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
