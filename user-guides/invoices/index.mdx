**Invoices** shows invoice records copied from your MSP's PSA. Company users see the invoices their role and company allow. MSP staff also set up the integrations that supply these records.

## Before you start

- Open **Invoices** in the sidebar. You need invoice-viewing access, which your MSP assigns. If **Invoices** is missing, ask your MSP.
- The list works with ConnectWise, Autotask, and Halo. Columns, statuses, PDF availability, and update timing depend on that PSA and your MSP's settings.
- MSP staff also see a **Pax8** tab when Pax8 is connected. Company users use the PSA list only.

## Open the invoice list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Invoices in the sidebar">
    The page lists invoices for your company selection. The default order is invoice number, highest first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company selector">
    If you can switch companies, confirm the selection before you look for an invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click an Invoice # to read it">
    The PDF opens in a viewer. See [Download and export invoices](/user-guides/invoices/download-invoices).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The list is a view of copied records. Reading or downloading an invoice does not edit it, mark it paid, or make a payment. To fix a wrong value, ask your MSP to correct it in the PSA.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Invoices** is not in the sidebar | Ask your MSP to check your invoice access. |
| **No Invoices Found** | Clear filters and check the company selector. Then ask your MSP to check the PSA connection and sync. |
| A PDF will not open | Click **Try Again**. If it fails again, send your MSP the invoice number and the error. |
| An invoice is missing | Give your MSP the invoice number and company so they can check status settings, mapping, and sync. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a company admin grant invoice access?">
    No. Your MSP manages users and permissions. Ask them to check your invoice access and company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I create or edit an invoice here?">
    No. The list has no invoice form or amount editor. Contact your MSP about a correction.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening an invoice mark it as paid?">
    No. A payment button opens your MSP's payment provider. See [Use an invoice payment link](/user-guides/invoices/pay-an-invoice).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add an invoice to Planner from its row menu?">
    No. The row menu has **View PDF** and **Add to Meeting** only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
