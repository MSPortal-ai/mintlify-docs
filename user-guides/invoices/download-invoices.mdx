This guide is for company users and MSP staff who need invoice documents or data. PDFs come from your PSA, so a visible invoice row does not guarantee its PDF can be retrieved.

## Before you start

- You need invoice-viewing access from your MSP.
- Find the invoices first. See [Find and review invoices](/user-guides/invoices/view-invoices).

## Open and download one PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invoice">
    Click the **Invoice #**, or open the row menu and choose **View PDF**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the viewer to load">
    Check the invoice number and contents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Download">
    The button is in the viewer header. Check your browser's downloads for the PDF.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the document does not load, click **Try Again**. If it still fails, send your MSP the invoice number and the error. The PSA connection or credentials may need attention. If the viewer says the PDF is no longer available in your PSA, the invoice may have been deleted or voided.

## Download several PDFs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select invoices">
    Check the boxes on the rows you want. Select 50 invoices or fewer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Actions > Download PDFs">
    Wait while the PDFs are fetched and the ZIP is prepared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the ZIP">
    Compare the PDFs inside with the invoices you selected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A batch can partly succeed. MSPortal downloads the PDFs it can get and reports how many failed, so a ZIP does not prove every invoice is inside. Retry the missing ones one at a time. The row selection clears after the download, including when some failed.

## Export invoice data to CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your view">
    Choose the company, filters, sort, and page you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to export">
    To export the whole page, clear any row selection. To export some invoices, check those rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Actions > Export CSV">
    The file is named `invoices-` and today's date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the file">
    Compare its rows with the page and selection you meant to export.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Export CSV uses the page that is currently loaded. It does not fetch other pages. With rows checked, it exports the checked rows on that page. Repeat the export for each page you need, or raise the page size to 250 first.
</Note>

The CSV always has the same columns: **Invoice #**, **Reference**, **Apply To**, **Company**, **Type**, **Status**, **Date**, **Due Date**, **Total**, **Subtotal**, **Balance**, **Payments**, **Sales Tax**, **Terms**, **Bill To**, **PO #**, and **Sent**. Hiding a column on screen does not remove it. The file has no currency symbol, and a missing amount is written as 0, so check the PDF when an amount matters. The CSV is data only. It has no PDF and does not show whether an invoice is paid.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I can see the invoice but not its PDF | The PDF comes from the PSA. Click **Try Again**, or ask your MSP to check the PSA connection. |
| **Download PDFs** is greyed out | Select at least one invoice row. |
| The ZIP is missing a PDF | Retry that invoice alone and tell your MSP if it fails. |
| The CSV only has some invoices | It exports the loaded page. Raise the page size or export each page. |
| A CSV amount shows 0 | The value may be missing. Check the invoice PDF. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many PDFs can I download at once?">
    Up to 50 selected invoices per batch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Export CSV include every filtered page?">
    No. It exports the loaded page, or the checked rows on it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does downloading an invoice pay it?">
    No. Payment is separate. See [Use an invoice payment link](/user-guides/invoices/pay-an-invoice).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
