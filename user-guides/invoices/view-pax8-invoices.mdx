This guide is for MSP staff. The **Pax8** tab on **Invoices** splits each Pax8 partner invoice into one row per mapped company, so you can see who is charged for what.

## Before you start

- Pax8 must be connected, and its companies mapped to MSPortal companies. See [Pax8 setup and company mapping](/user-guides/integrations/enable-pax8-integration).
- Pax8 invoice sync must be turned on in the Pax8 integration settings. It is off by default and runs daily.
- You need invoice-read access. Company users do not see this tab. They use the PSA invoice list.

## Open the Pax8 tab

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Invoices in the sidebar">
    A **PSA Invoices** tab and a **Pax8** tab appear when Pax8 is connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Pax8 tab">
    The page is titled **Pax8 Invoices**. If it says **No Pax8 invoices yet**, turn on invoice sync in the Pax8 settings. The **Open Pax8 settings** button takes you there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the company selector">
    The list follows your company selection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the rows

Each row is one company's share of a Pax8 partner invoice, based on that company's mapped invoice items. The same partner invoice can show on several rows, one for each company.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Invoice Date** | Date of the Pax8 invoice. Click it to open the line items |
| **Company** | The mapped company |
| **Reference** | The Pax8 invoice reference |
| **Status** | **Unpaid**, **Paid**, **Void**, **Carried**, **Nothing Due**, or **Credited** |
| **Due Date** | Due date from the Pax8 invoice |
| **Subtotal**, **Total** | This company's charges only |
| **Invoice Total** | The whole partner invoice. Hidden until you turn it on in the column toggle. It repeats on every company row, so do not add those values together |
| **Items** | How many invoice items are in this row. Click it to open the line items |

A company with no mapped items can show an empty list even when others are charged on the same partner invoice.

## Find and inspect charges

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Use the **Invoice Date**, **Company**, and **Status** filters. The default order is invoice date, newest first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the line items">
    Click the invoice date or the **Items** count. The **Invoice line items** dialog opens for that row's company only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the items">
    Review **Product**, **Vendor**, **Type**, **Period**, **Qty**, **Price**, **Subtotal**, **Sales Tax**, and **Total**. **Term** and **Sales Tax** may be hidden at first. Use the column toggle to show them. Filters and pages work inside the dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the dialog">
    Close it when you are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review draft charges

When draft items exist, an **Upcoming draft charges** card appears above the list. It is a mid-month snapshot of charges that are not final yet. It shows subtotals by currency and the item count.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click View draft items">
    The **Draft charges** panel opens with the current draft items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare with care">
    The card follows your company selection only. It ignores the table's **Invoice Date**, **Status**, and **Company** filters. Check your selection before comparing it with the filtered rows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Draft charges are not invoices and can change before final billing. If **View draft items** opens nothing, tell your administrator. That is not the same as having no draft items.

## What this tab does not do

It is a read-only view. It has no invoice editing, charge approval, payment, PDF download, or CSV export. Use Pax8 for those.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No Pax8 tab | Pax8 must be connected, and you must be MSP staff. |
| **No Pax8 invoices yet** | Turn on invoice sync in the Pax8 settings and wait for the next daily sync. |
| A company's list is empty | Check the company mapping, the company selector, and your filters. An empty list does not prove Pax8 has no charges for that company. |
| The totals look doubled | **Invoice Total** repeats on every company row. Use **Total** for one company's share. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can company users see the Pax8 tab?">
    No. It is for MSP staff only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does one partner invoice appear on several rows?">
    Each row is one company's share of it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Total and Invoice Total?">
    **Total** is the row company's charges. **Invoice Total** is the whole partner invoice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are draft charges final?">
    No. They are a separate snapshot and may change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
