When your MSP connects BenjiPays, unpaid invoices show a **Pay Now** button. This guide is for company users and MSP staff who want to pay from the invoice list.

## Before you start

- Your MSP must have an active BenjiPays integration. Without it, no payment button appears.
- The button only shows on invoices with money due. See [Why the button is missing](#why-the-button-is-missing).
- Your MSP can rename the button, so yours may not say **Pay Now**.

## Pay an invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the invoice">
    Open **Invoices** and find the right invoice and company. Open the PDF if you want to check the original.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the amount">
    Read the invoice number and **Balance**. The link carries the balance, or the invoice total when no balance is supplied.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Pay Now">
    Click the payment button at the end of the row. The payment page opens in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the page and pay">
    Confirm the invoice and amount on the payment provider's page, then follow its steps. The portal does not collect payment details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the receipt">
    Use the payment provider's confirmation or receipt as proof of payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the portal later">
    The invoice updates after the next sync from your PSA, so the balance can still show for a while.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Clicking the button does not charge you. If the amount looks wrong, stop and ask your MSP to check the invoice first.

## Why the button is missing

- Your MSP has not turned on a working payment-link integration.
- The invoice has no balance due, or its status is void or cancelled.
- The invoice is closed and no balance is supplied.

A missing button does not prove an invoice is paid. A button that stays after you paid does not prove the payment failed. The portal may not have synced yet.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No payment button | Ask your MSP whether payment links are set up and how to pay this invoice. |
| The amount differs from what I expect | Ask your MSP to confirm the invoice before you pay. |
| I paid but the portal still shows a balance | Check the payment provider's confirmation and contact your MSP. Do not pay again because of an old portal balance. |
| The link does not work | Send your MSP the invoice number and what happened. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I pay every invoice from the portal?">
    Only invoices that show a payment button. Ask your MSP how to pay the others.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Pay Now mark the invoice paid?">
    No. It opens the payment page. The invoice changes after you pay and the PSA syncs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the button named something else?">
    Your MSP can set its own label. It still opens the payment page for that invoice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a company admin set up the payment integration?">
    No. Your MSP does. Ask them about missing or wrong payment links.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
