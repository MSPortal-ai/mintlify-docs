title: Adding CIS Benchmarks
description: Learn how to add CIS (Center for Internet Security) benchmarks to your MSPortal tenant for compliance tracking

---

MSPortal.ai comes with many built-in checks from CIS, the Center for Internet Security. Add them to your tenant from the Playbooks catalog, then find them in your compliance checks and groups.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the CIS Benchmarks catalog">
    Go to **Settings** > **Compliance** > **Playbooks**. Click **Security Frameworks**, then **CIS Benchmarks**. Search for the product you need, or browse the operating systems, browsers, cloud and infrastructure sections. You can select one benchmark or several.

    ![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-29/206f39ba-2c12-4fae-8fd6-96ae34c0a168/stack_animation.webp)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the levels you want">
    Most benchmarks come as **L1** (Level 1) and **L2** (Level 2) entries, and some offer a **Full** entry. Level 1 is essential hardening with little impact on day-to-day use. Level 2 adds stricter settings for high-security environments. These are CIS Benchmark profiles, not CIS Controls Implementation Groups (IG1 and IG2).

    To add both levels, select both entries and click **Add to Tenant** in the header. To add a single entry, click **Add** on its row. An **Imported** badge means the benchmark is already in your tenant.

    <Frame caption="One product with separate L1 and L2 entries.">
      <img src="/images/product/cis-benchmark-levels.png" alt="CIS benchmark catalog showing one product with separate L1 and L2 entries" width="916" height="310" />
    </Frame>

    ![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-29/7dd264c3-4f1e-4899-868d-8981b871e58f/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=1040,0&br_px=2416,769&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=957,159)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a status list and add to tenant">
    The **Add Playbooks to Tenant** dialog opens. For each selected benchmark:

    1. Review the **Folder** the checks will be added to.
    2. Choose a **Status list**. If your tenant has only one status list, it is already selected.
    3. Review the suggested **Device types**. Selected device types receive the imported check group automatically. Remove any type you don't want to target.

    Click **Add to Tenant**.

    <Warning>
    If a benchmark is already in your tenant, adding it again creates a second copy of its check group. It does not update the existing copy.
    </Warning>

    ![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-29/0323daf9-cbae-4a4b-b923-032258d0f476/stack_animation.webp)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to Compliance">
    Go to **Settings** > **Compliance**.

    ![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-29/3b9a47ed-123f-4ca1-8837-efdd1acad8ae/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,112&br_px=1376,881&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=373,277)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Groups">
    The checks have all been added. Open the **Groups** tab to see the new check groups.

    ![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-29/7ba4d031-f4bb-4e50-a968-1dfc1f852a86/stack_animation.webp)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open a group to see its checks">
    Click a group to see which checks were added.

    ![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-29/0f53e86a-ce1d-4656-a795-8ee72b11ed62/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=283,132&br_px=1659,901&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,277)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the added checks">
    In this example, many checks were added for CIS Apple iOS 18.

    ![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-29/50529a3e-d1de-4ede-9063-170bcf42004d/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=937,115&br_px=2313,884&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=525,436)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Adding a benchmark imports its checks. It does not change device settings or mark a company compliant. Set up groups, runs and company scope in [Compliance settings](/user-guides/settings/compliance).
</Note>
