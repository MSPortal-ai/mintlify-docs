Compliance AI is the assistant on the Compliance page. It answers questions about your compliance data, drafts check notes, plans updates, and prepares runs, always for the companies you have selected. Every change it suggests arrives as a proposal you review first.

## Before you start

- AI features must be enabled for your tenant, and you need Compliance access.
- Read-only users can ask questions and get summaries. Users with Compliance write access can also apply proposals.
- Drafting public notes and risk assessments are MSP staff tasks. Company users stay limited by their role and company scope.

## Ask the assistant a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assistant">
    On the **Compliance** page, click **Open assistant** at the right edge of the screen. The **Assistant** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the scope">
    The panel shows the current view (**Tree view**, **List view**, or **Risk view**) and the company scope, such as **All companies** or a company count. Change the company selector first if the scope is wrong. Changing the company clears the current conversation and any pending proposals. Changes you already applied stay saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a starter or type a question">
    Under **Start with**, click a starter button, or type your own request in the message box. A starter button sends its question right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the answer">
    Check the records, dates, and company scope behind the answer before you act on it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Starter buttons on the Tree and List views:

- **Summarize compliance** summarizes failed, overdue, and high-priority checks for the scope.
- **Draft check notes** drafts public notes for the most important failed checks. This needs write access.
- **Plan check updates** proposes status, priority, flag, or due-date updates the data supports. This needs write access.
- **Prepare a run** drafts a compliance run for the scope. This needs write access.

On the Risk view, with risk tracking enabled, the starters are **Find risk candidates**, **Complete risk assessments**, **Explain scoring**, and **Draft top risk**.

When you ask about a specific check or risk, name it, so the assistant does not guess which record you mean.

## Review and apply a proposal

When the assistant wants to change something, it shows a proposal card and does not act on its own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the pending proposals">
    Proposals collect at the bottom of the panel under **Pending proposals — review before applying**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the proposal">
    Each card shows what it would change and a before-and-after view of each field. The card type is **Check updates**, **New run**, **Run update**, **Draft notes**, or **Risk assessment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or dismiss">
    Click **Apply** to save the change, or **Dismiss** to discard it. Applying **Draft notes** saves the notes right away. There is no separate save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    MSPortal shows **Compliance changes applied**, or **Updated N checks; M could not be updated** when a batch only partly worked. Open the checks and confirm what saved before you retry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Dismissing a proposal does not undo changes you already applied. To correct an applied change, edit the record normally.

## Turn an AI assessment into a risk

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assessment">
    On a **Risk assessment** card, click **Use this assessment**. The standard risk editor opens, marked **AI-assisted assessment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review every field">
    Check the risk statement, category, likelihood, impact, score, owner, and visibility. The editor says **Review and edit every field below.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the risk">
    Click **Add risk**. Nothing is added to the register until you do. For a proposal about an existing risk, the card button reads **Edit risk**, and you click **Save changes** in the editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When several new risk proposals are pending, **Add all (N)** creates them all at once, without opening each editor. Review every proposal first. Some can fail, and the result reads **Added: X · Failed: Y**. Check the register before you retry.

AI-written assessment text does not approve a risk or complete remediation. See [Risk Management](/user-guides/compliance/risk-management).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I cannot find the assistant | AI features may be off for your tenant, or you lack Compliance access. Ask your MSP admin. |
| I cannot apply a proposal | Applying changes needs Compliance write access. |
| Answers cover the wrong companies | The assistant follows the company selector. Change the selection and ask again. |
| An apply only partly worked | Read the updated and failed counts, check the saved checks, and retry only what failed. |
| **I couldn't complete that request. Please try again.** | Send the question again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does clicking a starter button change anything?">
    It sends the question at once. Questions only read data. Changes happen only when you click **Apply** or **Add risk**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company users draft notes from private check answers?">
    No. Public-note drafting is an MSP staff task, and company users cannot see private answers through the assistant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Apply on Draft notes save the notes?">
    Yes. Review the proposed public notes first. Apply saves them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Use this assessment create a risk?">
    No. It opens the risk editor. **Add risk** creates it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Dismiss undo an applied change?">
    No. It only discards a pending proposal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an AI risk assessment approve the risk?">
    No. Approval and remediation are separate steps in [Risk Management](/user-guides/compliance/risk-management).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
