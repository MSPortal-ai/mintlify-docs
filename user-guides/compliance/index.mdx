**Compliance** brings together the checks your MSP has assigned to companies, locations, and devices. A check describes a requirement; its assigned assessment records the result for a particular scope and run.

**Company users:** review the checks and supporting information available to your company. Reading does not give you permission to change assessment results. Your MSP manages user access and which assessments are shared.

**MSP staff:** configure checks, groups, templates, statuses, and scoring; assign assessment runs; maintain findings and evidence; and track remediation when permitted.

## Other Compliance areas

Use **Compliance Menu** to open **Dashboard**, **Runs**, or **History**. The optional **Risks** tab appears when your MSP has enabled risk tracking. See [Risk Management](/user-guides/compliance/risk-management) and [Compliance AI](/user-guides/compliance/compliance-ai).

Checks and framework content depend on your MSP’s configuration. An empty dashboard does not install a framework or create assessment records. [Adding CIS benchmarks](/user-guides/compliance/adding-cis-benchmarks) describes a configuration workflow for MSP staff.

## Before changing an assessment

Users with Compliance write or management permission can see assessment editing controls. Changes to notes and assessment fields can **autosave**; **Save Changes** also exists. Closing a dialog does not undo changes already saved.

**AI Draft** in a check’s public-notes area appends generated content and saves it. Review what is appropriate for Company readers before invoking it, and verify the resulting content afterward. Do not treat this action as an unsaved private preview.

To draft public notes for several checks at once, select them in **Tree** or **List** and choose **Draft Public Notes with AI** from **Actions**. Each draft is written from the check's private notes and saved only when you approve it; denied drafts leave the check unchanged. Checks with empty private notes are skipped. See [Drafting public notes for selected checks](/user-guides/compliance/compliance-ai#drafting-public-notes-for-selected-checks). MSP staff only; branch preview.

## Adding selected checks to the Planner

Select checks in **Tree** or **List**, open **Actions**, and point to **Add to Planner**. Two options appear:

- **As one item** opens the Planner item editor with every selected check linked to a single item.
- **As separate items** creates one Planner item per check, each linked to its own check.

<img
  src="/images/product/compliance-bulk-planner-menu.png"
  alt="Compliance list with three checks selected and the Actions menu showing Add to Planner with As one item and As separate items"
  width="1440"
  height="900"
/>

**As separate items** opens a list of the selected checks:

1. For each check, choose a **Quarter**. The item is due on the first day of that quarter, so it lands in that quarter on the Planner timeline.
2. For each check, keep **Default** or pick a Planner template.
3. Select **Create**.

**Quarter for all rows** and **Template for all rows** set every row at once. They show **Mixed** when rows differ.

A **Default** item is titled "Resolve:" plus the check name, uses your default Planner type, and copies the check's public and private notes. A templated item takes its title, type, status, business value, hours, costs, probability, notes and client visibility from the template. Any compliance checks the template names are also linked when that company has them. Up to 100 checks are created per batch. Templates are listed for MSP staff only. Branch preview.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find my company\u2019s compliance results?">
    Open **Compliance**, check the company scope, and use **Tree** or **List**. Your MSP determines the checks and access available to you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I see a result but not change it?">
    Read access is separate from Compliance write or management permission. Ask your MSP to review access if your responsibilities require editing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a high score certify my organization?">
    The score summarizes the configured assessments and recorded results. It is not a certification or proof that every requirement has been assessed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are framework checks automatically included?">
    Do not assume they are. Your MSP configures the checks, groups, and templates used for your assessments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Close discard my changes?">
    It cannot undo changes that have already autosaved. Watch the save result and check the recorded values.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
