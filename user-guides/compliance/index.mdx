**Compliance** brings together the checks your MSP assigns to companies, locations, and devices. A check describes a requirement. Each assigned check records the result for one target in one run.

**Company users** read the checks and supporting information shared with their company. **MSP staff** set up checks, groups, templates, and scoring, start runs, and record findings, evidence, and follow-up work.

## Before you start

- You need Compliance read access to look. You need write or manage access to run checks, change a check, or add notes.
- Checks, groups, and templates come from your MSP's library. Nothing is included automatically. See [Compliance settings](/user-guides/settings/compliance) and [Playbooks](/user-guides/playbooks/index).
- An empty dashboard does not mean you are compliant. It usually means no run has been started for your scope.

## Open Compliance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Click **Compliance** in the sidebar. The page is titled **Compliance Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a view">
    Use the tabs in the page header. **Tree** groups checks by company, run, and group. **List** shows one row per check. **Risks** appears when your MSP has turned on risk tracking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open another area">
    Click **Compliance Menu** and choose **Dashboard**, **Runs**, or **History**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Compliance AI">
    If AI is on for your tenant, click **Open assistant** at the right edge of the page. See [Compliance AI](/user-guides/compliance/compliance-ai).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Run compliance checks

MSP staff with write access start a run here. A run creates an assigned check for each target you choose.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Run dialog">
    On the dashboard, click **Run Checks** in the page header. The **Run Compliance Checks** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the run">
    Type a **Name**, or click the clock icon to use today's date. Add optional **Notes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a due date">
    Pick a **Due date**, or click **1 week**, **2 weeks**, **30 days**, **60 days**, or **1 year**. Tick **Hide from company users** to keep the run internal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to assess">
    In the left panel, pick the **Companies**, **Locations**, **Devices**, or **Device Groups** to assess. Use **Select all**, or search each list. A device group applies the checks to every device of that type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the checks">
    In the right panel, pick **Templates**, **Groups**, or **Individual** checks. A template includes its groups and checks, and a group includes its checks. The totals show how many checks you picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the run">
    Click **Run compliance checks**. MSPortal shows **Compliance run queued successfully. Processing N companies in background.** The new run appears in the tree when it finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only checks with an **Approved** status are created. Pending or rejected checks are skipped, even when they are in a selected template or group. The dialog warns you when some checks in a group are not approved.

MSPortal needs a name, at least one target, and at least one template, group, or check. Otherwise it shows a message such as **Please enter a name for the compliance run**.

## Work on a check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the check">
    Click a check title in **Tree** or **List**. See [Read compliance guidance, evidence, and history](/user-guides/compliance/read-evidence-and-history) for a tour of the window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set status, priority, and due date">
    In the middle column, choose **Status** from the check's status list, **Priority** (**High**, **Medium**, or **Low**), and a **Due Date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the notes">
    Type in **Public Notes (Client Visible)** for text company users can read. Type in **Private Notes (Internal Only)** for your team. Click **Add Template** to insert saved note text. Type `@` to mention a teammate. They get a notification that opens the check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach evidence">
    On a wide screen, open **Uploads** in the right column and drop in files. On a phone, use the **Uploads** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Changes autosave, and the header shows **Saving**, **Saved**, or **Error**. Click **Save Changes** to save and close. If you close with unsaved changes, MSPortal asks **You have unsaved changes. Are you sure you want to close?**
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choosing a status can add the note template linked to that status to your public notes.

### Draft public notes with AI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write private notes">
    Type your raw observations in **Private Notes (Internal Only)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click AI Draft">
    Click **AI Draft** in the **Public Notes** header. The button is off until private notes exist, and it only appears when AI is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    AI adds a client-ready draft with **Summary**, **Business Impact**, and **Remediation** sections to the end of your public notes and saves it. MSPortal shows **AI draft saved successfully**. Edit the text, and remove anything you do not want company users to read.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Flag a check, add it to Planner, or create a ticket

- **Flag for Review** marks the check for your team. Click **Flagged** again to remove the flag.
- **Add to Planner** opens a Planner item for the check. It needs Compliance write access and Planner write access. When the check already has linked work, an **Open in Planner** link also appears.
- **Create Ticket** opens **Create Support Ticket**, where you type **Why are you creating this ticket?** and click **Create Ticket**. It needs a PSA integration mapped to the company and Compliance write access. Once a ticket exists the button reads **View Ticket**. Your PSA's default board and status must be set in **Settings > Tickets**.
- **Add to risk register** appears on a failed, completed check when risk tracking is on. See [Risk Management](/user-guides/compliance/risk-management).

## Change several checks at once

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select checks">
    In **Tree** or **List**, tick the checks you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Actions">
    Click **Actions (N)** in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Pick **Set Status**, **Set Priority**, **Add Note**, **Add to Planner**, or **Add to Meeting**. **Remove from Run** appears for users with Compliance manage access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Follow the prompt. **Remove from Run** permanently removes the checks and their history, notes, and statuses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Select checks from one company for **Add to Meeting**.

## Manage runs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Runs">
    Click **Compliance Menu** and choose **Runs**. The page is titled **Compliance Runs**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the list">
    Each run shows **Name**, **Status**, **Progress**, **Notes**, **Started By**, **Date**, and **Completed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update a run">
    Edit notes, or use **Mark company complete** or **Mark all complete** to close out a run. Users without write access see **View only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open or delete a run">
    Click a run to see its details, with **Overview**, **Compliance Checks**, and **History** tabs. Users with manage access can use **Delete Run**, which permanently deletes the run and all its check data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read statuses and scores

Statuses come from the status list on each check. Your MSP names them and decides which count as passed. Tree rows count **Past due**, **Passed**, **Failed**, and **In progress**.

The **Compliance Score** is a weighted percentage with a letter grade. Each check has a **Score Weight**, shown in its guidance with the formula `scope × (1 + crit × mult) × priority`. See [Find compliance checks and understand results](/user-guides/compliance/find-and-read-checks) and [Compliance scoring](/user-guides/settings/compliance-scoring).

A high score is not a certification. It summarizes the checks you have assessed.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Run Checks** is missing | You lack Compliance write access. Ask your MSP admin. |
| The tree is empty | Switch the run filter to **All**, check the company selector, and make sure a run exists. |
| A run does not include some checks | Only **Approved** checks are created. Approve them in the check library, then run again. |
| **Create Ticket** is missing | The company is not mapped to a PSA, or you lack write access. |
| **AI Draft** is missing or off | AI is off for your tenant, or the private notes are empty. |
| Status options say **Using fallback options** | The check has no status list. Ask your MSP admin to set one in Compliance settings. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find my company's compliance results?">
    Open **Compliance**, check the company selector, and use **Tree** or **List**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I see a result but not change it?">
    Reading and editing are separate permissions. Ask your MSP if you need write access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a high score certify my organization?">
    No. The score summarizes the checks that were assessed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are framework checks included automatically?">
    No. Your MSP builds the library or imports playbooks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Close discard my changes?">
    No. Changes that already autosaved stay saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does AI Draft save right away?">
    Yes. It adds to your public notes and saves. Review and edit afterward.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
