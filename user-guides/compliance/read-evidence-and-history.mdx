Use this page to read everything attached to one compliance check. It is for company users and MSP staff with access to the assessment.

## Before you start

- You need Compliance read access. Users with Compliance write or manage access also see editing controls.
- If you can edit, do not change controls while you only read. Changes to status, priority, due date, notes, and flags can autosave.
- Find the check first. See [Find compliance checks and understand results](/user-guides/compliance/find-and-read-checks).

## Open the right assessment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the check">
    Open **Compliance** and find the check in **Tree** or **List**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Click the check title. The check opens in a large window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the target">
    Check the company, run, group, and target in the breadcrumb under the title. Use the arrows in the header to move to the previous or next check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the main fields">
    In the middle column, read **Status**, **Priority**, **Due Date**, and the notes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On a wide screen, **Guidance** is on the left, the assessment is in the middle, and **Uploads**, **Custom Fields**, and **History** are on the right. On a phone, use the **Guidance**, **Details**, **Uploads**, and **History** tabs. On a screen of medium width, the right column is hidden. Widen the window or use a phone layout to reach those tabs.

## Read Guidance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Guidance">
    On a wide screen, read the left column. On a phone, tap **Guidance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the sections">
    Sections can include **Criticality**, **Frequency**, **Score Weight**, **Description**, **Why It's Important**, **How to Check**, **Remediation**, **Remediation Impact**, **References**, and **Default Value**. Sections without content are left out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Guidance describes how the check is defined. It does not show that the requirement was tested or fixed. Use the status, notes, and uploads for that.

Reference custom fields on the check definition appear with guidance and are read-only in the assessment. Custom fields for the assessment itself are separate.

## Read the notes

**Public Notes (Client Visible)** are meant for company readers. MSP staff also see **Private Notes (Internal Only)**. Company users cannot see private notes. If the public findings are unclear, ask your MSP for a clearer explanation.

Readers cannot edit status, priority, due date, or notes.

## Open supporting files

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Uploads">
    Click **Uploads** in the right column, or the **Uploads** tab on a phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the file">
    Look for the file name in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download it">
    Click the file's download icon, wait for the download, and open it in a suitable app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An upload is supporting material. A file on its own does not mean the check passed. Readers see the download icon without upload or delete controls. If a download fails, ask your MSP to check your access or the file.

## Review custom fields

On a wide screen, click **Custom Fields** in the right column. On a phone, the fields appear below the entries under **History**. Your MSP decides which fields exist and which you can see.

## Review earlier completed assessments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open History">
    Click **History** in the right column, or the **History** tab on a phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the entries">
    Each entry is an earlier completed instance of the same check for the same company. It shows the completion date, the recorded status, who completed it, and the **Public:** and **Private:** notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an entry">
    Click **View this completed instance** to open it as a read-only record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the run and target">
    Check the run and target before you compare notes or results with the current assessment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This list leaves out the assessment you have open and shows other completed instances. It is not a log of every edit. **No History Yet** means no other completed instance exists.

For a list of completed assessments across all checks, see [Review completed compliance assessments](/user-guides/compliance/view-check-history).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I see no **Uploads**, **Custom Fields**, or **History** tabs | The window is too narrow for the right column. Widen it, or use the phone layout. |
| **No History Yet** | No other completed instance of this check exists for the company. |
| A file will not download | Ask your MSP to check your access to the file. |
| A field I expect is missing | Your MSP controls which fields exist and who can see them. |
| I cannot edit anything | Read access does not allow edits. Ask your MSP if you need write access. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does History show every change to the current check?">
    No. It lists other completed instances of the check for the company. It is not an edit-by-edit log.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my current result missing from History?">
    The list leaves out the assessment you already have open.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are custom fields on a phone?">
    Assessment-level custom fields appear below the History entries. Definition-level reference fields appear with Guidance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an attached PDF mean the check passed?">
    No. Read the status, public notes, date, and the file itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I read files if I cannot upload?">
    Yes. Readers can download files. Upload and delete controls are hidden.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
