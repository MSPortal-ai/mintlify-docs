Use this page to look back at completed compliance checks across all runs. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Compliance read access. Your MSP controls which assessments are shared with company users.
- This page shows only completed assessments. It is separate from the **History** tab inside a check, which lists earlier completed instances of that one check for the company.
- The page loads the 100 most recently updated completed assessments. See [the history limit](#account-for-the-history-limit).

## Open Check History

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Compliance">
    Click **Compliance** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the History page">
    Click **Compliance Menu** in the page header and choose **History**. The page is titled **Check History**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company scope">
    Look at the company selector. The table follows the company you select.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the table">
    Use the column filters on **Company**, **Check**, **Entity**, **Run**, **Status**, **Completed By**, **Notes**, and **Date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an instance">
    Click a row to open the check as a read-only historical record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand the columns

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Company** | The company the assessment belongs to. |
| **Check** | The requirement assessed. **Flagged** appears under the title when the check is flagged, and a paperclip with a file count appears when files are attached. |
| **Entity** | The assessed company, location, or device, labeled **(Company)**, **(Location)**, or **(Device)**. |
| **Run** | The assessment run that holds the instance. |
| **Status** | The recorded status label. A completed assessment can still have an unsuccessful status. |
| **Completed By** | The user who completed it. If none is recorded, MSPortal shows the last user who updated it. |
| **Notes** | A preview of the **Public:** note. MSP staff with write access also see the **Private:** note. |
| **Date** | The last-updated time, not always the completion time. |

A paperclip shows the number of attached files. It does not show their contents.

## Account for the history limit

The page loads **up to 100 completed assessments**, newest update first. Filters, sorting, company selection, and paging work only on those loaded records. They do not fetch older assessments.

A company can show few or no rows even when older history exists. To find an older instance, open the check from **Compliance > Tree** or **List** and use its **History** tab, as described in [Read compliance guidance, evidence, and history](/user-guides/compliance/read-evidence-and-history#review-earlier-completed-assessments). You can also ask your MSP.

## Read a completed instance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row">
    Click the row. The check opens with a **Historical Record** notice: **You are viewing a completed instance of this check. All fields are read-only.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the record">
    Read the run, target, status, public notes, and any uploads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the record">
    Click **Close** to return to the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A completed instance is a snapshot. It does not describe the environment today. Open the latest assessment for the current result.

## Export the loaded history

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the filters">
    Choose the company and table filters you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Click **Export CSV** in the table toolbar. The file is named `compliance-history`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the file">
    Open the CSV and check its dates and rows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The CSV holds the filtered records from the loaded set, across all table pages, and never more than the 100 loaded assessments. It always has the same columns: Company, Check, Entity, Run, Status, Completed By, Date, Priority, Flagged, Public Notes, Files Attached, and Private Notes for users who may see them. Notes are plain text. Download attached files from the assessment.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No check history found matching your filters** | Clear the column filters and check the company selector. Remember that only the latest 100 completed assessments load. |
| An older assessment is missing | It is outside the 100 loaded. Use the check's **History** tab, or ask your MSP. |
| The table is empty after a load error | Reload the page. If it keeps failing, ask your MSP to investigate. An empty table does not prove nothing was completed. |
| I cannot edit the record | Historical records are read-only. Open the current assessment to make changes. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Check History show every assessment ever completed?">
    No. It shows the 100 most recently updated completed assessments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is an older company assessment missing after I pick the company?">
    The company filter works on the loaded records. The older assessment can be outside them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Date the date the check was completed?">
    No. It is the last-updated time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Completed By always name the person who completed it?">
    Not always. When no completing user is recorded, it shows the last user who updated the record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the CSV contain all older history?">
    No. It exports the filtered records from the 100 loaded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is this the same as the History tab inside a check?">
    No. This page lists completed instances across checks and runs. The check's tab lists completed instances of that check for the company.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
