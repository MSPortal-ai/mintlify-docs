Risk Management adds a risk register to Compliance. Failed checks appear as gaps you can promote into tracked risks. Each risk has a likelihood, an impact, an owner, a treatment plan, and a target date. This page is for MSP staff who manage risks and for company users who read the risks shared with them.

## Before you start

- Risk tracking must be on. An MSP admin turns it on under **Settings > Compliance > Risk**. See [Configure risk tracking and categories](/user-guides/settings/compliance-risk). If it is off, you see **Risk tracking is disabled** with an **Open Risk settings** button.
- You need Compliance read access to view risks.
- Only internal MSP users with Compliance write or manage access can create, edit, approve, or dismiss. Company users do not see **Unaddressed gaps**, and they see only risks shared with them.

## Find and read a risk

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Risks">
    Click **Compliance** in the sidebar, then the **Risks** tab. The page is titled **Risk Register**.

    <img src="/images/product/risk-header-tabs-preview.png" alt="MSP Demo risk header with Risk register and Unaddressed gaps tabs and the gap count" width="1280" height="853" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    Choose **Risk register** or **Unaddressed gaps** in the page header. The count beside **Unaddressed gaps** is the number of gaps waiting for review. Company users see only **Risk register**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary cards">
    The cards show **Active risks**, **High and critical**, **Overdue treatment**, and **Accepted risks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the register">
    Use **Search risks…** and the filters for **Category**, **Severity**, **Status**, **Owner**, **Target from**, and **Target to**. Click **Clear filters** to reset. The table is paged, so one page is not the whole register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a risk">
    Click a row, or open its **Risk actions** menu and choose **View risk**. The details show the statement, owner, target date, likelihood, impact, treatment plan, and visibility.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The risk details can also show:

- **Source compliance finding**, with **Open source** to jump to the check. A strategic risk has no source.
- **Linked remediation work**: the PSA ticket, Planner items, and evidence files attached to the source finding.
- **Failure occurrences**: how often the source check failed. A repeat failure adds to the count and does not create a duplicate risk.
- **Risk dependencies**: **Depends on** and **Required by**.
- **Risk history**: a log of edits.
- A **Source is now compliant** badge once the check passes. The badge does not review the risk, finish treatment, or close it.

Company users only open linked records they already have access to.

## Promote a compliance gap

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Unaddressed gaps">
    On **Compliance > Risks**, click **Unaddressed gaps**. Each row is a failed check with its company and **Failed on** date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to the register">
    Open the row menu and choose **Add to risk register**. The **Add compliance gap to risk register** editor opens, filled in from the check. Nothing is saved yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the fields">
    Check **Risk title**, **Risk statement**, **Category**, **Likelihood**, **Impact**, **Owner**, **Target date**, and **Treatment plan**. Criticality hints at impact, priority describes how urgent the fix is, and only likelihood times impact sets the **Risk score**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Check **Visible to company users**. A promoted gap can carry over the visibility of its source finding, so do not assume the switch starts off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the default category (optional)">
    Turn on **Use this category for future failures** to save the category as this check's default. This changes the check, not only this risk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the risk">
    Click **Add risk**. MSPortal shows **Risk added to the register**. Open the saved risk and check the assessment before you approve it for company users.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To skip a gap, open its row menu, choose **Dismiss**, type a **Reason** of at least three characters, and click **Dismiss gap**. Dismissing does not pass the check. If the check fails again later, a new gap appears.

## Add a strategic risk

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the risk">
    In the register header, click **Add strategic risk**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    Choose the **Company**, enter the **Risk title** and **Risk statement**, and choose a **Category**. Set **Likelihood** and **Impact**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add ownership and treatment">
    Choose an **Owner**, a **Target date**, and a **Treatment plan**. Under **Dependencies**, pick the upstream risks this one depends on. The list holds only risks for the selected company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**. A strategic risk starts internal-only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Score and track a risk

Likelihood and impact each run from 1 to 5. The score is their product.

| Severity | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Low | 1–4 |
| Medium | 5–9 |
| High | 10–16 |
| Critical | 17–25 |

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | **Open**, **Approved**, **Mitigating**, **Accepted**, or **Closed**. New risks start **Open**. |
| **Owner** | The person accountable. It does not create a ticket, create Planner work, or notify them. |
| **Target date** | When treatment should be done. |
| **Treatment plan** | How you will address the risk. |
| **Visible to company users** | Controls whether company users can see the risk. |

To edit a risk, open its **Risk actions** menu, choose **Edit risk**, change the fields, and click **Save changes**. Closed risks are read-only. If the check fails again later, a new gap appears.

## Share a risk with company users

A company user sees a risk only when **Visible to company users** is on, an MSP user has set the status to **Approved**, and the user has Compliance access to that company.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review internally">
    Create the risk and check it as MSP staff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and share">
    Edit the risk, turn on **Visible to company users**, set **Status** to **Approved**, and click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify access">
    Check with the intended company role. An MSP preview does not prove what that user sees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw sharing">
    To make a risk private again, turn **Visible to company users** off and save. Changing the status alone does not do this.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The approval is recorded the first time an MSP user sets the status to **Approved**. Moving the status to **Mitigating** or another value later does not clear it.

## Categories and AI help

Categories apply to the whole tenant. Add, rename, recolor, reorder, and archive them in [Risk settings](/user-guides/settings/compliance-risk). Archiving keeps existing risks. Category colors do not change severity.

Compliance AI can rank gaps worth reviewing and draft a risk assessment. **Use this assessment** opens the standard risk editor with the draft. Nothing is saved until you click **Add risk**. See [Compliance AI](/user-guides/compliance/compliance-ai).

## Report on risks

When risk tracking is on, the report builder offers five risk blocks. Dashboards support all but Risk Register.

| Block | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Risk Summary** | Active, high and critical, overdue, and accepted risk numbers. |
| **Risks by Category** | Active risks grouped by category and severity. |
| **Risk Matrix** | A five-by-five likelihood and impact heatmap. |
| **Risk Exposure Trend** | Active risk exposure over the report period. The metric can be active risks, total exposure, or high and critical risks. |
| **Risk Register** | A prioritized table of active risks with owners and target dates. Reports only. |

Check the company, period, and scope before you share a report. A summary is not the full list.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| There is no **Risks** tab | Risk tracking is off or you lack Compliance access. An MSP admin can turn it on in **Settings > Compliance > Risk**. |
| I cannot edit a risk | The risk is **Closed**, or you are not an MSP user with Compliance write access. |
| A company user cannot see a risk | Check that **Visible to company users** is on, the status was set to **Approved**, and the user has access to that company. |
| The dependency list is empty | It shows only other risks for the same company. |
| A save fails | Reopen the risk and check what saved before you try again. |
| Risk blocks are missing from the report builder | Risk tracking is off for your tenant. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I create a risk without a failed compliance check?">
    Yes. MSP writers use **Add strategic risk**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company users create or approve risks?">
    No. They read shared risks. MSP writers create, edit, and approve.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Visible to company users alone share a risk?">
    No. The risk also needs an **Approved** status and the user needs Compliance access to the company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will changing Approved to Mitigating make the risk private?">
    No. Turn **Visible to company users** off and save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are promoted gaps always internal-only?">
    No. They can carry over the source finding's visibility. Check the switch before you save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What sets risk severity?">
    Likelihood times impact, each 1 to 5. Priority and category color do not.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Use this category for future failures change only this risk?">
    No. It also changes the source check's default category.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does dismissing a gap pass its check?">
    No. It records why the gap is not a risk. The check result stays the same.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a compliant source close the risk?">
    No. Read the **Source is now compliant** badge separately from the risk status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does assigning an owner notify them?">
    No. It records ownership only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
