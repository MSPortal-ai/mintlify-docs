Use this page to browse the NIS2 playbooks and import them into your tenant as compliance checks. It is for MSP staff.

NIS2 is Directive (EU) 2022/2555. It applies to essential and important entities in the sectors listed in its annexes, and it reaches their suppliers through contract terms. Each EU Member State applies it through national law, so confirm the national rules for each client.

## Before you start

- You need Playbooks management permission and active billing to import. Read access lets you look at the catalog.
- Import permission is separate from read access. Without it, you see **You do not have permission to add playbooks.**

## Find the NIS2 playbooks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Playbooks">
    Open **Settings > Compliance > Playbooks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Security Frameworks">
    Choose **Security Frameworks**, then **NIS2**. The page groups ten playbooks by article.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start with scope and governance">
    Open the scope and governance playbook first. It records whether the client is an essential entity, an important entity, or a supplier to one. That answer decides how the other playbooks apply.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review the checks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a playbook">
    Click a playbook title to read its check list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read a check">
    Every check title starts with its article reference, such as **Art. 21(2)(c)** or **Art. 23(4)(a)**. Each check includes how to check, why it matters, remediation, expected evidence, and cross-references to the implementing regulation, ISO/IEC 27001, CIS Controls, and NIST CSF where they apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the details">
    Close the details when you finish. The **Copy** action in the details copies the playbook right away. Use **Add** on the playbook's card when you want the import dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Import playbooks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what to import">
    Click **Add** on one playbook. To import several, tick them and click **Add to Tenant (N)** in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the destination">
    In **Add Playbooks to Tenant**, review the **Folder** for each playbook. The default is a **NIS2** folder with one subfolder per playbook.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a status list">
    Pick a **Status list** for each selected playbook.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read any duplicate warning">
    A playbook you already imported shows **Already in your tenant. Importing again creates a second copy of this check group.** Cancel if you do not want a copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Click **Add to Tenant**. MSPortal shows **Playbook(s) added**, or **{name} imported. Find its checks in Compliance under "{folder}".**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Open **Settings > Compliance > Checks** and look in the destination folder. Open **Groups** to find the new group.
  </Step>
</Steps>

NIS2 checks are company-level checks, so the dialog suggests no device types.

## Prepare an assessment

Tailor the imported checks to the client's Member State. Replace the generic references to the national authority and CSIRT with the client's real bodies and portal, and adjust thresholds where national law sets them. Then set up groups, runs, and company scope in [Compliance settings](/user-guides/settings/compliance).

The playbooks give structure to a review. They do not decide a client's legal status, replace legal advice, or certify compliance.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Access restricted** or no **Add** button | You lack Playbooks import permission or an active subscription. Ask your MSP admin. |
| **Failed to add playbook(s)** | Check **Settings > Compliance > Checks** for a partial import before you try again. |
| A playbook appears twice | It was imported twice. Remove the extra group and its checks in Compliance settings. |
| The NIS2 page lists fewer than ten playbooks | An unmapped playbook appears under **Other NIS2 playbooks**. Look there. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do the playbooks cover national transposition laws?">
    No. They follow the directive text. Add or adjust checks for national rules after import.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use them for a client that is out of scope?">
    Yes. The scope playbook includes a check for suppliers to NIS2 entities, and the Article 21 playbooks work as a general security review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is NIS2 the same as NIST CSF 2.0?">
    No. NIS2 is EU law. NIST CSF 2.0 is a voluntary US framework. Many NIS2 checks cross-reference NIST CSF 2.0 categories.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I import a playbook again to update it?">
    Importing again makes another group and another set of checks. Review what you already have first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
