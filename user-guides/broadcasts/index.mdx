Emergency Broadcast lets your team email urgent messages to groups of users, such as security incidents, outages, planned maintenance and important announcements. An AI writer helps you draft the message, and the history records every send and each recipient's result. You can also post a **portal status banner** that appears at the top of your clients' portal home page. It is for MSP staff.

Open it from **MSP Tools > Emergency Broadcast**. The page has four tabs.

## Before you start

- Company users cannot use Broadcasts.
- Viewing needs read access to broadcasts. Sending, testing and saving drafts need write or manage access. Creating groups and deleting drafts need manage access. Ask an administrator if you do not see the Compose tools or **Create Group**.
- Set up a verified sending domain under [Email settings](/user-guides/settings/email) so broadcasts come from your own address. Without one, they are sent from MSPortal's default no-reply address.
- Create at least one broadcast group before you send. See [Create a recipient group](#create-a-recipient-group).

## Sending a broadcast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Compose tab">
    Go to **MSP Tools > Emergency Broadcast** and click the **Compose** tab. Saved drafts show under **Saved Drafts** at the top. Click one to load it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the subject and message">
    Enter a **Subject** and write your **Message** in the rich-text editor (bold, lists, links, and more are supported).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose recipients">
    Click **Select groups** to open the **Select Groups** dialog. Search, use **Select All / Deselect All**, and tick the groups that should receive the message. Each group shows its member count, and a running **unique recipients** total is displayed. Selected groups appear as removable chips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a theme">
    Choose a **Dark** or **Light** email theme (Dark is the default). This controls how the email looks in recipients' inboxes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and test (optional)">
    Click **Preview** to see the email as recipients will, and toggle between light and dark. Click **Send Test to Me** to send a copy to your own inbox first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send Broadcast**. A confirmation dialog tells you how many unique recipients across how many groups will receive it. Confirm to send. You're taken to the **History** tab to watch progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Sending cannot be undone, and broadcast sending is queued immediately (there is no email-send scheduling). Always use **Send Test to Me** or **Preview** before sending to a large audience.
</Warning>

### Saving drafts

Click **Save Draft** to store the current subject, message, selected groups, and theme. Drafts are personal to you and appear at the top of the Compose tab so you can pick up where you left off.

## Using the AI Broadcast Writer

The **AI Broadcast Writer** drafts both the subject line and the message body for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the writer">
    Click the floating **AI Broadcast Writer** button on the Compose tab to open the writer panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the message, or pick a quick template">
    Type what you want to communicate, or choose a **Quick template** to get started:
    - **Security Incident**: alert users about a threat with actionable steps
    - **Scheduled Maintenance**: notify users about planned downtime and impact
    - **Service Outage**: communicate an ongoing disruption
    - **General Announcement**: share an IT policy change or update
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply and refine">
    The assistant proposes subject and message changes. Review and apply the proposal to update the Compose form; applying it does not save a draft or send the broadcast. Keep chatting to refine it, for example *"make it shorter"* or *"add more urgency."* The writer is aware of which groups you've selected and how many people will receive the broadcast.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recipient groups

Broadcasts go to **broadcast groups**. A group is a reusable list of recipients. It can include users from one or several companies. A person in several selected groups gets one email.

Which groups you can use depends on your company scope. Tenant-wide access can use groups across all companies. Single or assigned-company access can use only groups for those companies.

### Create a recipient group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Groups tab">
    Go to **MSP Tools > Emergency Broadcast** and click the **Groups** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Group">
    The button is in the page header. You need manage access. The **Create User Group** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the group">
    On **Details**, enter a **Group Name** and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the scope">
    Turn on **Tenant-Wide (available to all companies)**, or leave it off and pick a **Company**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add membership rules (optional)">
    On **Rules**, choose **Match ALL rules (AND)** or **Match ANY rule (OR)**, then click **Add Rule**. Rules add users automatically by attributes such as job title, company, company type or Microsoft 365 group. Click **Preview Matching Users** to see who matches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Group">
    The group appears in the table. To pick individual people, open the group again and use the **Manual Members** tab. Group membership is the rule matches plus the manual members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use the actions menu on a group row to edit its details, manage members or delete it. Deleting a group removes all its members from it and cannot be undone.

## Track delivery

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the History tab">
    The table lists each broadcast with its **Subject**, **Groups**, **Recipients**, **Status**, **Sent By** and **Date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the status">
    See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a broadcast">
    Click a row. The **Recipients** tab shows each person's name, email, company and **Sent**, **Pending** or **Failed** status, with the error for failures. The **Message** tab shows what was sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Sending** | The broadcast is being delivered. |
| **Completed** | Processing finished. Check for failed recipients, because a Completed broadcast can still have failures. |
| **Failed** | Delivery failed. |
| **Test** | A test you sent to yourself. |

## Portal status banners

The **Portal Banner** tab posts an outage or maintenance notice to the portal home page, with no email. Banners appear at the top of the home page for your clients, styled by severity. You can post updates as the situation changes, like a status page. Viewing the tab needs read access to broadcasts. Creating, editing, switching on or off, posting updates and deleting need write or manage access.

### Create a banner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Portal Banner tab">
    Go to **MSP Tools > Emergency Broadcast** and click **Portal Banner**. Existing banners show their severity, companies, schedule and update count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New banner">
    Enter a **Title** (required) and a **Message**, for example "Email delivery delays" with a short explanation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a severity">
    Pick **Info**, **Warning** or **Critical**. Severity sets the banner's icon and color.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the companies">
    Use **Companies** to limit the banner to certain companies. Leave it empty to show it to all clients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it (optional)">
    Set **Show from (optional)** and **Show until (optional)** to show the banner only in a window, such as planned maintenance. Leave both empty to show it right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Dismissible and Active">
    Turn on **Dismissible** to let users hide the banner after reading it. Turn on **Active** to show it. Only active banners show on the portal home page. The **Preview** at the bottom shows how it will look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. An active banner inside its schedule window appears on the portal home page right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Post an update

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the Post update icon">
    Each banner row has a **Post update** icon button next to its **Active** switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the update">
    Enter a short status, such as "Microsoft has identified the cause and a fix is rolling out." Click **Post update**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a mistaken update (optional)">
    The **Update history** list in the dialog lets you remove an update that was posted in error.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Updates show on the banner as a timeline, newest first. The **Latest** update is highlighted and earlier ones collapse under **Show earlier updates**. Posting an update brings the banner back for users who dismissed it. Editing the banner does the same.

### Turn off, edit or delete a banner

- Use the **Active** switch on the banner row to turn it off or on without editing it.
- Click the pencil icon to edit it.
- Click the trash icon to delete it. Deleting removes the banner from the portal for good.

### What clients see

Active banners show at the top of the portal home page, on both the classic and the AI home. When several are active, the selected one shows its full message and timeline and the others collapse to one row. Dismissible banners have a close button. Banners that are not dismissible stay until you turn them off or delete them.

Active banners are also shared with the portal's AI assistant. When a client asks something like "is email down?" and it matches a banner, the assistant answers from it and says it is already being worked on. It still offers to create a ticket if the client wants individual follow-up.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Send Broadcast** is disabled | Enter a subject and a message, and select at least one group. |
| A broadcast failed | Open it in **History** and read each failed recipient's error. Check your sending domain under [Email settings](/user-guides/settings/email). |
| You cannot see the Compose tools or **Create Group** | Sending needs write or manage access. **Create Group** needs manage access. Ask an administrator. |
| You need to send later | Broadcast emails send as soon as you confirm. Use **Save Draft** to prepare one. Only portal banners can be scheduled, with **Show from** and **Show until**. |
| A client does not see the banner | Check that **Active** is on, the current time is inside the schedule window, and the client's company is in **Companies** (empty means all). The client may also have dismissed it. Post an update to bring it back. |
| The send count differs from the group sizes | People in several selected groups get one email. Check the unique recipient count and the History details. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Completed mean everyone got the message?">
    No. A broadcast with failed recipients can still be Completed. Check the Sent, Failed and Pending counts. A sent email does not mean the person read it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Preview or applying AI text send anything?">
    No. Preview only shows the email, and applying AI text only fills in Compose. **Send Test to Me** emails only you. **Send Broadcast** emails the selected groups after you confirm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the banner dates schedule an email broadcast?">
    No. **Show from** and **Show until** only control the portal banner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Save Draft publish a banner?">
    No. It saves your broadcast email. A banner shows as soon as you save it as active inside its window.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does turning a banner off delete its updates?">
    No. Turning it off only hides it. Deleting the banner removes it for good.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I resend after some recipients failed?">
    Check who failed and who got the message first. A new broadcast can email people who already received it, so choose the follow-up audience carefully.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does posting a banner update send an email?">
    No. Banner updates and broadcast emails are separate. Send a broadcast too if you want an email.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
