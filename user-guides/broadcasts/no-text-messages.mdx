Emergency Broadcast sends email only. Sending broadcasts by SMS text message is not planned. This page explains why, and what to use instead when email may not reach people. It is for MSP staff.

## Why we do not send text messages

Texting someone is a different privacy and consent problem from emailing them.

- **PSA phone numbers are often personal.** The mobile numbers in a PSA contact record are frequently people's own phones, not phones their employer owns. Sending texts to them means MSPortal would store and process personal data that was never collected for that purpose.
- **Doing it properly needs consent or proof of ownership.** To text those numbers responsibly, each person would need to opt in to receiving texts, or each number would need to be confirmed as owned by the client company and not a personal phone. Neither is something a PSA sync can tell us.
- **The risk is shared.** If MSPs passed those numbers to MSPortal and let us text them, the privacy and consent risk would fall on the MSP, on the MSP's clients and on MSPortal.

Email to work addresses does not carry the same problem, so broadcasts stay on email.

This page explains our product decision. It is not legal advice. If you text your clients' users yourself, check the consent rules that apply to you.

## What to use instead

### Emergency Broadcast email

Send the message to your [recipient groups](/user-guides/broadcasts/index#recipient-groups) from **MSP Tools > Emergency Broadcast > Compose**. People in several selected groups get one email, and **History** shows each recipient's result. See [Sending a broadcast](/user-guides/broadcasts/index#sending-a-broadcast).

### Portal Banner

Post a notice at the top of your clients' portal home page from the **Portal Banner** tab. Choose a severity, limit it to certain companies, and post updates as the incident changes. The portal's AI assistant also answers from active banners when a client asks about the problem. See [Portal status banners](/user-guides/broadcasts/index#portal-status-banners).

### Your RMM

When email itself is down, use your RMM to put a message on users' screens. Most RMM tools can run a script on managed devices that shows a pop-up notification. For an example, see NinjaOne's [How to send message notifications to users](https://www.ninjaone.com/script-hub/how-to-send-message-notifications-to-users/).
