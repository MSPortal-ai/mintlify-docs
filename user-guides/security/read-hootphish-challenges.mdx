Use this page to review HootPhish phishing challenges from CyberHoot. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need the Security Awareness read permission.
- Your MSP must connect CyberHoot and map its customers to companies. See [Enable the CyberHoot integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-cyberhoot-integration).
- If you have more than one awareness integration, choose the **CyberHoot** tab on **Security Awareness**.

## Find a challenge

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the challenge list">
    Open **Security > Security Awareness**, check the company selector, and click **HootPhish challenges** above the company table. A window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type in **Search challenges**. It matches the challenge name, the company name, or the CyberHoot challenge ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by status">
    Use the status dropdown to choose **All challenges**, **Active**, or **Completed**. Choose **All challenges** to see challenges in any other status.

    <img src="/images/product/security-awareness-challenge-status.png" alt="HootPhish challenge status filter: All challenges, Active, and Completed" width="356" height="204" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page through the list">
    Use **Previous page** and **Next page**. Each page holds up to 25 challenges, newest start date first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a challenge">
    Click the challenge name in the table, or tap its card on a small screen. Its [participant results](/user-guides/security/read-hootphish-participants) appear below the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The first challenge in the list is selected for you when your previous choice is not on the page. Searching, changing the status, changing the company, or paging can change the selected challenge. Check the name above the participant results.

## Read the challenge columns

The table shows **HootPhish challenges** (the name), **Mapped companies**, the status, **Dates**, **Participation**, **Success**, **Challenge score**, and **Challenge rank**. Cards on small screens also show **Avg. participant score**.

- **Participation** and **Success** are different percentages.
- A status of **Completed** does not mean every participant passed.
- A dash or **Not provided** means CyberHoot supplied no value.

Selecting a challenge only changes what you view. It does not enroll learners, send emails, or complete anything.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No HootPhish challenges found** | Clear **Search challenges**, choose **All challenges**, and check the company selector. Your MSP can confirm the CyberHoot sync. |
| The window shows an error | Close it and open it again. If it keeps failing, report it through the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). |
| I cannot find an upcoming or inactive challenge | The status dropdown only offers All challenges, Active, and Completed. Choose **All challenges**. |
| The participant section changed after I searched | The first challenge on the new page was selected. Check the name above the results. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does selecting a challenge send anything to learners?">
    No. It only shows the imported results.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Completed mean everyone passed?">
    No. Check **Succeeded** and **Failed** in the participant results.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I create or export challenges here?">
    No. Manage campaigns in CyberHoot.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
