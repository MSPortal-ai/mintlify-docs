Use this page to read the Security Dashboard and jump to the records behind each number. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Security read access.
- At least one of Cork, Acronis, Huntress, Avanan, Petra Security, or Fortinet must be connected. The **Dashboard** tab appears only then. See [Security](/user-guides/security/index) for the integration guides.
- The dashboard shows imported data. Opening it does not run a scan.

## Review the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Dashboard">
    Open **Security > Dashboard** and check the company selector. A selection of several companies adds them together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the tiles">
    The tiles at the top show the key numbers for each connected provider. Colors mark tiles that need attention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the cards">
    Below the tiles, one card per provider gives more detail. Only connected providers appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow a number">
    Click a tile or a count on a card to open the matching list. Check the filters already set on that list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the tiles

| Provider | Tiles |
| --- | --- |
| Acronis | **Open Incidents** and **Endpoints Online**. |
| Huntress | **Open escalations**, **Open incident reports**, and **Active agents**. |
| Cork | **At-risk compliance events** and **Critical vulnerabilities**, with the known exploited count underneath. |
| Avanan | **Open threats** and **Phishing (30 days)**. |
| Petra Security | **Open identity incidents** and **Failed sign-in attacks (30 days)**, with the number of tenants attacked underneath. |
| Fortinet | **FortiGates connected**, out of all FortiGates, and **FortiGates behind on firmware**, with how many are past end of life underneath. The firmware tile turns red when any FortiGate runs a FortiOS branch past end of life. |

Tiles show how many items were new in the last 30 days when that history is available.

## Read the cards

### Cork

- **Compliance Overview** shows **At Risk** and **Resolved** counts, **At-risk events by control**, and **New events per day**. At Risk and Resolved are separate properties and need not add up to the total.
- **Vulnerabilities** shows **Total**, **By priority**, **Known Exploited**, and **Monitored Endpoints**. **Critical** and **Known Exploited** can overlap, so do not add them.
- Counts link to [Security > Compliance](/user-guides/security/read-cork-events) or [Security > Vulnerabilities](/user-guides/security/read-vulnerabilities), with the matching filter set.

### Acronis

- **EDR Overview** shows **Open Incidents**, **Investigating**, and **Closed**, plus **Open by severity**, **New incidents per day**, a **high severity** count, and **Not Mitigated**.
- **Endpoint Health** shows **Online**, **Protected**, **Offline**, and **Unprotected**. Online and Protected are separate.
- **All addressed** can appear when the open count is zero. Check the other states and **Not Mitigated** before you rely on it.
- Counts link to [EDR Incidents](/user-guides/security/read-edr-incidents) and [Endpoints](/user-guides/security/find-endpoints).

### Huntress

- **Huntress overview** shows escalations, incident reports, and remediations with open, resolved, and pending counts, **Open by severity**, **Detections per day**, **Agent coverage**, and **SAT learners** and **SAT accounts**.
- Every link opens [Security > Huntress](/user-guides/security/read-huntress). It does not preselect a type.

### Avanan

- **Email security overview** shows **Threats by type, last 30 days**, a threat trend, **SAT-covered tenants**, **Spam (30 days)**, and **Graymail (30 days)**.
- Links open **Security > Email Security**.

### Petra Security

- **Identity threats** shows Microsoft 365 account compromises detected by Petra. **Open incidents by company** lists the companies with unremediated incidents, and **No open incidents** appears when there are none.
- Underneath, it shows **Live incidents (30 days)**, **Historical incidents**, **Failed sign-in attacks (30 days)**, and **Average posture score**. Historical incidents are compromises Petra found in past sign-in data when it onboarded a tenant, not new attacks.
- The tiles and each company link open **Security > Identity Threats**, filtered to open incidents when there are any.

### Fortinet

- **Fortinet firewalls** shows FortiGate connection, FortiOS firmware, and FortiCare and FortiGuard coverage from the last sync, with **FortiGates**, **Connected**, **Firmware behind**, and **Renewals due in 90 days**.
- **Coverage ending soonest** lists the FortiGate coverage lines that end first, and **Issues by company** shows where FortiGates need attention. **No FortiGate needs attention.** appears when nothing does.
- The card appears when Fortinet is connected and at least one FortiGate is in the selected companies. Renewal dates need Asset Management access on the Fortinet connection. See [Configure Fortinet Integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-fortinet-integration).
- The Fortinet tiles and card do not open a list. Open a FortiGate in **Devices** to see its firmware and coverage.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No Security Data Yet** | No security integration is connected or synced. An admin can connect one from **Settings > Integrations**. |
| A provider card is missing | That provider is not connected, you lack access, or its data failed to load. An Avanan load error shows as a red notice with the provider name. |
| Numbers look old | The dashboard shows synced data. Ask your MSP to check the integration sync. |
| A list is empty after you click a number | Clear the filter the dashboard set on that list. |
| Zero counts everywhere | Check the company selector. Zero counts do not prove you are safe, so ask your MSP to verify the sync. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I add Critical and Known Exploited to get a total?">
    No. A vulnerability can be both. Use **Total** for the overall count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does At Risk plus Resolved equal every Cork event?">
    Not necessarily. They are separate properties.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Online mean an endpoint is protected?">
    No. Connectivity and protection are reported separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a Huntress link open a broader list?">
    Huntress links open the whole Huntress tab. Use the **Type** filter there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I am a company user. How do I ask about a number?">
    Tell the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) which tile or card you mean and the company, then confirm the request when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
