Use this page to add Cork compliance events, vulnerabilities, or endpoints to the agenda of an upcoming meeting. It is for MSP staff who prepare meetings. EDR incidents use a [separate flow](/user-guides/security/follow-up-edr-incidents).

## Before you start

- You need Security read access and Calendar write access (one of the manage or write permissions for meetings or the calendar). Your MSP manages roles.
- The meeting must already exist and start in the future. This flow does not create meetings.
- The findings must belong to one company.

## Add findings to a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the findings">
    Open **Security > Compliance**, **Security > Vulnerabilities**, or **Security > Endpoints**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select rows from one company">
    Check the box beside each finding you want to discuss. If the selection mixes companies, MSPortal shows **Please select items from the same company**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Add to Meeting">
    Open **Actions (N)** in the page header and choose **Add to Meeting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the items">
    In the **Add to Meeting Agenda** dialog, review **Items to add** and the company name beside the title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the meeting">
    Open **Select Meeting** and pick an upcoming meeting. The list is sorted by start time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the agenda">
    Read **Current Agenda** to see what is already on the meeting. It shows **No items in agenda yet** when the agenda is empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add to Agenda**. MSPortal shows **Added to meeting agenda** and clears your selection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each selected finding becomes one agenda entry. Adding a finding does not resolve it or change anything in Cork.

On **Security > Endpoints**, a Cork row is added as a Cork endpoint entry. An Acronis row is added as a text entry with its name and source, because Acronis rows are not Cork endpoints.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add to Meeting** is not in the Actions menu | Select at least one row first. The menu appears in the page header only when rows are selected. |
| **No upcoming meetings found** for the company | Create a meeting for that company in Calendar first. Past meetings are not offered. |
| **Please select a company first** | Pick a company in the company selector, or select rows that carry a company. |
| **Failed to add items to meeting** | Check **Current Agenda** on the meeting before you retry, so you do not add the same finding twice. Ask your MSP to confirm you have Calendar write access. |
| A finding shows no company | The record is not mapped to a company. Ask your MSP to fix the company mapping, then select it again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not choose a past meeting?">
    The list only offers meetings whose start time is in the future.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Add to Meeting save the findings right away?">
    No. Opening the dialog and picking a meeting save nothing. **Add to Agenda** saves the entries.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I combine findings from different companies?">
    No. Select one company at a time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding an agenda item open a ticket or fix the issue?">
    No. It adds an entry to the meeting agenda only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I am a company user. How do I get a finding reviewed?">
    Ask your MSP to include it in a review meeting, or describe it to the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) and confirm the request when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
