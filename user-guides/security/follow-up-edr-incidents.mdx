Use this page to act on Acronis EDR incidents after you have read them. Company users start with the AI Assistant. MSP staff can use the ticket, meeting, and Planner shortcuts on **Security > EDR Incidents**.

## Before you start

- You need Security read access and an active Acronis integration. See [Read Acronis EDR incidents](/user-guides/security/read-edr-incidents).
- **Add to Meeting** needs Calendar write access. **Add to Planner** needs Planner write or manage access. The Planner item is created only if your role allows it.
- Select incidents from one company at a time. MSPortal shows **Please select items from the same company** otherwise.

## Request an investigation as a company user

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Home">
    Open **Home** and find the AI Assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the incident">
    Include the company, incident number, host, severity, and the date shown in the **Detected** column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer any questions">
    Reply to anything the assistant asks, then review the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the request">
    Confirm when prompted, then check the ticket confirmation. Viewing an incident does not create a ticket.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [How to open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) for the full flow.

## Create a ticket draft

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Create Ticket">
    On **Security > EDR Incidents**, open the three-dot **Actions** menu on one row and choose **Create Ticket**. For several incidents, check their rows and choose **Create Ticket** from **Actions (N)** in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the details">
    MSPortal shows **Incident details copied to clipboard** and opens the new ticket page. Paste the copied text into the ticket description. It lists host, severity, verdict, category, state, detected date, and the Acronis link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete and submit">
    Choose the company, fill in the required fields, check every host in the pasted text, and submit the ticket.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This shortcut does not submit a ticket or fill the form for you. If the paste is empty, copy the incident details by hand from the side panel.

## Add incidents to a meeting agenda

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Add to Meeting">
    Open the row's **Actions** menu and choose **Add to Meeting**, or check several rows and choose **Add to Meeting** from **Actions (N)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the items">
    In **Add to Meeting Agenda**, review **Items to add** and the company name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the meeting">
    Open **Select Meeting**, pick an upcoming meeting, and read **Current Agenda**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add to Agenda**. MSPortal shows a confirmation and clears your selection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each incident becomes one text entry titled like **EDR #12 - hostname**. It does not update when Acronis changes the incident.

## Create a Planner item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Add to Planner">
    Open the row's **Actions** menu and choose **Add to Planner**, or check several rows and use **Actions (N)**. The option is missing if your role lacks Planner access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    The dialog opens with a title, **Description**, **Business Value**, **Due Date**, and **Private Notes**. The status starts as Not Started, and the due date starts as today. Business Value starts at **High** when any selected incident has High severity or is Not Mitigated, and **Medium** otherwise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust and save">
    Change **Type**, **Status**, **Due Date**, or the notes if needed, then click **Save**. MSPortal shows **Added to planner**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the item">
    Open **Planner** and confirm the item and its notes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Several selected incidents become one Planner item titled **EDR Incidents (N items)**. The incident details are copied into Private Notes. They are a snapshot and do not update when Acronis changes.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Please select items from the same company** | Clear the selection and pick incidents from one company. |
| **Please select a company first** | Pick a company in the company selector. |
| **Add to Planner** is missing | Your role lacks Planner write or manage access. Ask your MSP admin. |
| **Failed to add items to meeting** | Check the meeting's agenda before you retry. Ask your MSP admin to confirm Calendar write access. |
| **Failed to create planner item** | Check **Planner** for a partial item, then retry. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Create Ticket submit a ticket?">
    No. It copies the details and opens the new ticket page. You paste, complete, and submit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do several incidents create several Planner items?">
    No. They create one Planner item. A meeting agenda gets one entry per incident.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the Planner notes update when the incident changes in Acronis?">
    No. They are a copy of the details at the time you saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a Planner item or agenda entry close the incident?">
    No. Remediate the incident in Acronis.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
