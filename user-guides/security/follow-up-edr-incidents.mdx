Use this page to act on EDR detections after you have read them. Company users start with the AI Assistant. MSP staff can use the ticket, meeting, task list, and Planner shortcuts on **Security > EDR > Detections**.

## Before you start

- You need Security read access and an active Acronis or Huntress integration. See [Read EDR detections](/user-guides/security/read-edr-incidents).
- **Add to Meeting** needs Calendar write access. **Add to Planner** needs Planner write or manage access. **Add to task list** is for MSP staff.
- Select detections from one company at a time for **Add to Meeting** and **Add to Planner**. MSPortal shows **Please select items from the same company** otherwise.

## Request an investigation as a company user

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Home">
    Open **Home** and find the AI Assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the detection">
    Include the company, the detection title or reference, host, severity, and the date shown in the **Detected** column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer any questions">
    Reply to anything the assistant asks, then review the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the request">
    Confirm when prompted, then check the ticket confirmation. Viewing a detection does not create a ticket.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [How to open a ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) for the full flow.

## Create a ticket draft

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Create Ticket">
    On **Security > EDR > Detections**, open the three-dot **Actions** menu on one row and choose **Create Ticket**. For several detections, check their rows and choose **Create Ticket** from **Actions (N)** in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the details">
    MSPortal shows **Incident details copied to clipboard** and opens the new ticket page. Paste the copied text into the ticket description. It lists each detection's host, type, title, severity, status, mitigation when Acronis reports one, detected date, and link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete and submit">
    Choose the company, fill in the required fields, check every host in the pasted text, and submit the ticket.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This shortcut does not submit a ticket or fill the form for you. If the paste is empty, copy the details by hand from the side panel.

<Note>
If you change the company or leave the EDR tab while the clipboard request is pending, its delayed result does not open a ticket page or show a confirmation. Start **Create Ticket** again from the detection you want to use. If copying fails while you are still on the same view, MSPortal keeps that view open and shows an error.
</Note>

## Add detections to a meeting agenda

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Add to Meeting">
    Open the row's **Actions** menu and choose **Add to Meeting**, or check several rows and choose **Add to Meeting** from **Actions (N)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the items">
    In **Add to Meeting Agenda**, review **Items to add** and the company name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the meeting">
    Open **Select Meeting**, pick an upcoming meeting, and read **Current Agenda**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add to Agenda**. MSPortal shows a confirmation and clears your selection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each detection becomes one text entry titled like **[EDR #12] Ransomware on hostname**. It does not update when the detection changes at the vendor.

## Add detections to a task list

Open the row's **Actions** menu and choose **Add to task list**, or check several rows and choose it from **Actions (N)**. Review the proposed tasks and save. Each detection becomes one task that links back to **Security > EDR > Detections**, and detections from different companies can go in one batch. See [Import and add linked tasks](/user-guides/tasks/import-tasks#add-work-from-an-existing-record) for the dialog.

## Create a Planner item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Add to Planner">
    Open the row's **Actions** menu and choose **Add to Planner**, or check several rows and use **Actions (N)**. The option is missing if your role lacks Planner access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    The dialog opens with a title, **Description**, **Business Value**, **Due Date**, and **Private Notes**. The status starts as Not Started, and the due date starts as today. Business Value starts at **High** when any selected detection has Critical or High severity or is Not Mitigated, and **Medium** otherwise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust and save">
    Change **Type**, **Status**, **Assignee**, the dates, the costs, **Hide from company users**, or the notes if needed, then click **Save**. Every field in the dialog is saved. MSPortal shows **Added to planner**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the item">
    Open **Planner** and confirm the item and its notes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Several selected detections become one Planner item titled **EDR detections (N items)**. The detection details are copied into Private Notes. They are a snapshot and do not update when the detection changes at the vendor.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Please select items from the same company** | Clear the selection and pick detections from one company. |
| **Please select a company first** | Pick a company in the company selector. |
| **Add to Planner** is missing | Your role lacks Planner write or manage access. Ask your MSP admin. |
| **Add to task list** is missing | Task lists are for MSP staff. |
| **Failed to add items to meeting** | Check the meeting's agenda before you retry. Ask your MSP admin to confirm Calendar write access. |
| **Failed to create planner item** | Nothing was saved and the dialog stays open. Click **Save** again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Create Ticket submit a ticket?">
    No. It copies the details and opens the new ticket page. You paste, complete, and submit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do several detections create several Planner items?">
    No. They create one Planner item. A meeting agenda gets one entry per detection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the Planner notes update when the detection changes?">
    No. They are a copy of the details at the time you saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a Planner item or agenda entry close the detection?">
    No. Remediate the detection in Acronis or Huntress.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
