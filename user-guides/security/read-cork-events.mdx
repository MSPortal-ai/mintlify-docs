Use this page to find and read Cork security compliance events. It is for company users and MSP staff. This is the **Compliance** tab inside Security, not the separate [Compliance assessment module](/user-guides/compliance/index).

## Before you start

- You need Security read access.
- Cork must be connected and its companies mapped. Without Cork, the **Compliance** tab does not appear. See [Enable the Cork integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-cork-integration).

## Find an event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Compliance">
    Open **Security > Compliance** and check the company selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Use the column filters. **Event Type** picks one event type. **Entity** searches the affected entity across all pages. **Company** picks companies. **Status** offers **At Risk** and **Not At Risk**. **Resolved** offers **Resolved** and **Unresolved**. **Created** takes a date range.

    <img src="/images/product/security-event-risk-filter.png" alt="Security Compliance Status filter with All, At Risk, and Not At Risk options" width="318" height="204" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort and page">
    Click a column header to sort. The table shows 20 events per page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an event">
    Click the **Event Type** link in a row to open the side panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A link from the Security Dashboard can open this list already filtered to **At Risk** or **Resolved**. Clear the filter to see every event.

## Read the table

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Event Type** | The kind of Cork finding. |
| **Entity** | The affected device, mailbox, or domain, when Cork supplies one. A dash means none. |
| **Company** | The mapped company. A dash means the record is not mapped. |
| **Status** | **At Risk**, or **OK** for Not At Risk events. |
| **Resolved** | **Resolved**, or **Open** while unresolved. |
| **Created** | When Cork recorded the event. |

Risk and resolution are separate. **OK** does not mean **Resolved**, and **Silenced** does not mean the issue was fixed.

## Read the event panel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the header">
    The title is the event type. The line below shows the entity and company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the badges">
    Badges show **At Risk** or **OK**, plus **Resolved** and **Silenced** when they apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the dates">
    **Created** and **Resolved** show the times Cork supplied. **Cure Period** shows the allowed time in hours, when Cork supplies it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the evidence">
    **Evidence** lists the reason and details Cork supplied. **No evidence details available** means Cork sent none for this event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The panel has no resolve or silence action. Handle the finding in Cork, and the result appears here after the next sync.

## Follow up

Select rows from one company, then open **Actions (N)** in the page header.

- **Add to Planner** creates one Planner item. See [Add security findings to Planner](/user-guides/security/add-to-planner).
- **Add to Meeting** adds the events to a meeting agenda. See [Add Cork findings to a meeting agenda](/user-guides/security/add-to-meeting).

These actions do not change the Cork event.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An event does not show when you type in **Entity** | **Entity** searches all pages of events, not only the page you see. Check the spelling, then clear the other filters, including **Company**, **Event Type**, **Status** and **Created**. A dash under **Entity** means Cork supplied none, so a search cannot match it. |
| The list is empty | Clear every filter, including any set by a dashboard link, and check the company selector. |
| **No compliance events found** for a company you expect to have data | Ask your MSP to check the Cork sync and company mapping. |
| A row shows a dash under **Company** | The record is not mapped to a company. Ask your MSP to fix the mapping. |
| **Security integration unavailable** | Cork is not connected, or you lack Security read access. Ask your MSP admin. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does Not At Risk show as OK in the table?">
    The filter says **Not At Risk** and the row badge says **OK**. They are the same value. Resolution is shown separately as **Open** or **Resolved**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Silenced mean resolved?">
    No. They are separate flags in Cork.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change an event or download a CSV here?">
    No. The table has no resolve, silence, or export action. It does offer Add to Planner and Add to Meeting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I am a company user. How do I ask about an event?">
    Tell the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) the event type, entity, company, and date, then confirm the request when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
