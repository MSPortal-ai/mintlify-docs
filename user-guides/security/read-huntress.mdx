Use this page to review Huntress security items for your companies. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Security read access.
- Huntress must be connected and its organizations mapped to companies. Unmapped organizations are left out. Without Huntress, the **Huntress** tab does not appear. See [Enable the Huntress integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-huntress-integration).
- Opening a record in Huntress needs your own Huntress sign-in.

## Find a security item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Huntress">
    Open **Security > Huntress** and check the company selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary cards">
    The cards at the top cover your selected companies. See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the items">
    Use the column filters on the **Huntress security items** table. **Item** searches by title. **Type** picks **Escalation**, **Incident**, or **Remediation**. **Status** and **Severity** take text. **Company** searches companies. Filtering returns you to page 1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read a row">
    Each row shows **Item** (with its category and organization underneath), **Type**, **Status**, **Severity**, **Company**, and **Detected**. Choose 25, 50, or 100 rows per page at the bottom of the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the record in Huntress">
    Click the link icon in the **Open** column (**Open in Huntress**). It opens in a new tab. A dash means Huntress supplied no link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also use the column menu to show or hide columns. You cannot approve a remediation or close an escalation here.

## Read the summary cards

The cards follow your company selection. They do not change when you filter the table.

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Protected agents** | Agents imported from Huntress, with the **active** count underneath. |
| **Open escalations** | Escalations not marked closed, resolved, complete, or completed, with the total underneath. |
| **Open incidents** | Incident reports not marked closed, resolved, complete, or completed, with the total underneath. |
| **Pending remediations** | Remediations not marked closed, resolved, complete, completed, or approved, with the total underneath. |
| **SAT learners** | Security awareness learners, with the number of SAT accounts underneath. It is not a completion rate. |

An agent counts as active when its status is active, online, healthy, or installed, when it is flagged online, or when it called back within the last 30 days. Active does not mean online right now. An item with no status counts as open or pending.

## Read the dates and related records

**Detected** means different things by type. For an escalation it is the occurrence time. For an incident report it is the report time. Either can fall back to the created date. For a remediation it can be the last sync date. It is not always the time of the original incident.

One escalation can involve several organizations. In a multi-company view it can show several company names.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No Huntress security items found** | Clear every filter and check the company selector. Then ask your MSP to check Huntress organization mapping and the integration sync. |
| Zero counts on every card | The integration may not be synced, or the organizations are unmapped. Zero counts do not prove you are safe. |
| The table is empty after a company switch | Change a filter to return to page 1. |
| **Security integration unavailable** | Huntress is not connected, or you lack Security read access. Ask your MSP admin. |
| **Open in Huntress** shows a sign-in page | You need your own Huntress access. Company users can ask their MSP to investigate. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do the cards stay the same when I filter the table?">
    The cards summarize your selected companies. Table filters only narrow the rows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Active mean the device is online right now?">
    No. An agent also counts as active when it called back in the last 30 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I approve or run a remediation here?">
    No. Use Huntress.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Planner and meeting actions available on Huntress items?">
    No. Those actions exist for Cork findings and EDR incidents only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I am a company user. How do I ask about an item?">
    Tell the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) the item title, company, type, status, and date, then confirm the request when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
