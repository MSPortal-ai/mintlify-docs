Use this page to read the learner results for one HootPhish challenge. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need the Security Awareness read permission and a connected CyberHoot integration. See [Enable the CyberHoot integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-cyberhoot-integration).
- Pick a challenge first. See [Review HootPhish challenges](/user-guides/security/read-hootphish-challenges).

## Find a participant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a challenge">
    Open **Security > Security Awareness**, click **HootPhish challenges**, and click a challenge name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the challenge">
    Scroll below the challenge list. Confirm the challenge name and the **Started** date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary cards">
    **Participants**, **Completed**, **Succeeded**, and **Failed** show the totals for the whole challenge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search participants">
    Type in **Search participants**. It matches the person's name, leaderboard name, email, or CyberHoot participant ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    The table lists **Participant**, **Result**, **Score**, **Rank**, and **Time**. Use **Previous page** and **Next page** for more. Each page holds up to 25 results, ordered by rank, then score, then name. Small screens show cards instead of a table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Searching changes the rows and their count. It does not change the four summary cards.

## Read a result

- **Completed**, **Succeeded**, and **Failed** are separate counts. Completed does not equal Succeeded, and the three need not add up to the total.
- **Result** shows the result CyberHoot supplied, or the participant status if there is no result. Recognized pass and fail values get a green or red badge. Other values get a gray badge, so read the text.
- **Score** is the participant's score. **Rank** shows with a number sign, like **#3**. **Time** shows minutes and seconds, like 2:05.
- **Not provided** means CyberHoot sent no value. It appears in **Result** when the participant has neither a result nor a status.

Rows are read-only. You cannot change a result, resend a challenge, or export a CSV here.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No participant results found** | Clear **Search participants** and check the selected challenge and company. Your MSP can confirm the CyberHoot sync. |
| **Participant results could not be loaded.** | Click **Try again** in the participant section. |
| Results disappeared after I changed the company | The selected challenge may have changed. Check the challenge name and clear **Search participants**. |
| A participant is missing | The search text may hide them, or CyberHoot has no result for them yet. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't the summary cards change when I search for one learner?">
    They show totals for the whole challenge. Search only filters the rows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a gray Result badge mean the learner has not started?">
    Not necessarily. It can be any CyberHoot result or status MSPortal does not color. Read the text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change a failed result or resend the challenge here?">
    No. Use CyberHoot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I am a company user. How do I ask about a result?">
    Tell the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) the company, challenge, and participant, then confirm the request when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
