Use this page to find and read Acronis endpoint detection and response (EDR) incidents. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Security read access.
- Acronis must be connected and its endpoints mapped to companies. Without Acronis, the **EDR Incidents** tab does not appear. See [Enable the Acronis integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-acronis-integration).
- Huntress incident reports are on the separate **Huntress** tab. See [Read Huntress security items](/user-guides/security/read-huntress).

## Find an incident

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open EDR Incidents">
    Open **Security > EDR Incidents** and check the company selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by host">
    Type in the **Host** column filter. It matches the host name or the host domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Use **Company** to pick companies, **Severity** to pick High, Medium, or Low, and **State** to pick Not Started, Investigating, or Closed. Click a column header to sort by it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the row">
    Each row shows **#**, **Host**, **Company**, **Severity**, **Category**, **State**, **Mitigation**, and **Detected**. The table shows 20 incidents per page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the details">
    Click a row, away from its checkbox and its three-dot menu, to open the side panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A link from the Security Dashboard can open this list with **State** already set. Clear the filter to see every incident.

## Understand the fields

- **Severity** is the severity Acronis reports. **Category** names the kind of detection.
- **State** tracks the investigation: **Not Started**, **Investigating**, or **Closed**.
- **Mitigation** is separate: **Auto Mitigated**, **Mitigated**, or **Not Mitigated**. A Closed incident can still be Not Mitigated, and a Mitigated incident can still be open.
- A badge can simplify a more specific Acronis value. Open the details panel to read the full Mitigation text.

## Read incident details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the side panel">
    Click the incident row. The title reads **Incident #** followed by the incident number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the badges and categories">
    The top of the panel shows the State and Mitigation badges and the **Categories**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Host">
    **Host** lists **Host Name**, **Domain**, **IP Address**, **Agent Version**, and **Company**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Incident Details">
    **Incident Details** lists **Severity**, **Verdict**, **State**, **Mitigation**, **Detected**, **Created**, and **Updated**. Fields without a value are left out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Acronis if you need more">
    Click **Open in Acronis Console** to open the incident in Acronis in a new tab. This needs your own Acronis sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The bottom of the panel lists **Internal ID**, **Acronis Incident ID**, and **Acronis Customer ID**, which your MSP can use to match the record.

This page is read-only. You cannot change an incident state, start remediation, or export a CSV here. To follow up, see [Follow up on EDR incidents](/user-guides/security/follow-up-edr-incidents).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The list is already filtered | Clear the **State** filter. A dashboard link can set it. |
| **No EDR incidents found** | Clear all filters and check the company selector. Ask your MSP to confirm the Acronis sync and company mapping. |
| **Security integration unavailable** | Acronis is not connected, or you lack Security read access. Ask your MSP admin. |
| **Failed to load EDR incidents** | Reload the page. If it keeps failing, report it through the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). |
| **Open in Acronis Console** is missing | Acronis did not supply a link for that incident. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Closed mean the incident is mitigated?">
    No. State and Mitigation are separate. Read both.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening the Acronis link give me access to Acronis?">
    No. You need your own Acronis access. Company users can ask their MSP to investigate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I resolve an incident or start a scan here?">
    No. Use Acronis for remediation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I am a company user. How do I ask for an investigation?">
    Tell the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) the incident number, host, company, severity, and date, then confirm the request when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
