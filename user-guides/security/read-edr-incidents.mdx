Use this page to review endpoint detection and response (EDR) detections from Acronis and Huntress on the **Security > EDR** tab. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Security read access.
- Acronis or Huntress must be connected, with its endpoints or organizations mapped to companies. Unmapped records are left out. Without either one, the **EDR** tab does not appear. See [Enable the Acronis integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-acronis-integration) and [Enable the Huntress integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-huntress-integration).
- Your MSP admin can rename the tab in **Settings > Navigation**, so it can show a different name.

The **EDR** tab has two views, **Overview** and **Detections**. It opens on **Overview**. Both follow your company selection.

## Read the Overview

Open **Security > EDR** and check the company selector.

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Open detections** | Detections nobody has started on, with a sparkline of new detections per week. Click it to open **Detections** filtered to open incidents, escalations, and incident reports. |
| **Resolved** | The share of all detections that are resolved, with the count underneath. |
| **Active agents** | Active agents, out of all agents. It appears when Acronis or Huntress is connected, and reads **No agents synced** until an agent is imported. |
| **Pending remediations** | Huntress remediations waiting for approval. It appears when Huntress is connected. Click it to open **Detections** filtered to open remediations. |
| **Not mitigated** | Acronis incidents the agent could not contain. It appears when Acronis is connected. |
| **Detections per week** | New detections per week over the last 12 weeks. Each point covers seven days, and the last one ends today. |
| **Open by severity** | Open detections by severity. |
| **Detection status** | Every detection split into **Open**, **In progress**, and **Resolved**. |

The tiles and charts count Acronis incidents and Huntress escalations and incident reports. Huntress remediations count only in **Pending remediations**.

Agent counts have no history in MSPortal, so **Active agents** shows today's value only. It counts Acronis agents that are online now and Huntress agents seen in the last 30 days. Huntress does not report whether an agent is connected right now.

If the data from Acronis or Huntress fails to load, the Overview shows **Could not load detections.** instead of totals or trends that leave that source out.

## Find a detection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Detections">
    Open **Security > EDR**, check the company selector, and click **Detections**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the table">
    Every column has a filter. **Detection** searches the title, host, reference, and company. **Type** picks **Incident**, **Escalation**, **Incident report**, or **Remediation**; it lists only the types your connected integrations produce. **Host** matches the host name. **Company**, **Severity**, and **Status** pick from a list. **Detected** takes a date range. Filtering returns you to page 1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read a row">
    Each row shows **Detection** (the title, with its reference underneath), **Type**, **Host**, **Company**, **Severity**, **Status**, and **Detected**. The table shows 25 detections per page, newest first. Click a column header to sort by it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the details">
    Click a row, away from its checkbox and its three-dot menu, to open the side panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

MSP staff also see a **Source** column, naming Acronis or Huntress, when both are connected. It can be filtered but not sorted.

A link from the Security Dashboard or the Overview can open **Detections** with **Status** and **Type** already set. Detection counts set **Type** to **Incident**, **Escalation**, and **Incident report**; **Pending remediations** sets it to **Remediation**. Clear the filters to see every detection.

## Understand the fields

**Type** names the kind of record:

- **Incident**: an Acronis EDR incident.
- **Escalation**: a Huntress escalation. Escalations have no host.
- **Incident report**: a Huntress incident report.
- **Remediation**: a Huntress remediation step attached to an incident report.

**Status** puts every source on one scale:

| Status | Acronis incident | Huntress escalation or incident report | Huntress remediation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** | Not Started | Not closed or resolved | Not approved yet |
| **In progress** | Investigating | Not used | Approved, even after its incident report closes |
| **Resolved** | Closed or False positive | Closed or resolved | Completed, cancelled, or not approved when its incident report closed |

**Severity** is the severity the source reports: **Critical**, **High**, **Medium**, **Low**, or **Info**. **Unrated** means the source sent none.

**Detected** means different things by type. For an Acronis incident it is the incident time. For an escalation it is the occurrence time, and for an incident report the report time; either can fall back to the created date. For a remediation it is when Huntress completed or approved it, or its incident report's time when it was never approved.

## Read the details

Click an Acronis incident to open its full panel. The title reads **Incident #** followed by the incident number.

- The top shows the State and Mitigation badges and the **Categories**.
- **Host** lists **Host Name**, **Domain**, **IP Address**, **Agent Version**, and **Company**.
- **Incident Details** lists **Severity**, **Verdict**, **State**, **Mitigation**, **Detected**, **Created**, and **Updated**. Fields without a value are left out.
- MSP staff also see **Open in Acronis Console** and the **Identifiers** your MSP can use to match the record.

State and Mitigation are separate in Acronis. A closed incident can still be **Not Mitigated**, and a **Mitigated** incident can still be open. Read both.

Click any other row to open a shorter panel with **Status**, **Severity**, **Host Name**, **Company**, **Detected**, and **Reference**. MSP staff also see **Open in vendor console**.

The vendor console needs your own sign-in to that vendor. Company users do not see vendor names or vendor console links on the **EDR** tab.

This tab is read-only for the detections themselves. You cannot change a status, approve a remediation, or export a CSV here. To follow up, see [Follow up on EDR detections](/user-guides/security/follow-up-edr-incidents).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The list is already filtered | Clear the **Status** and **Type** filters. A dashboard or Overview link can set them. |
| **No detections** | Clear all filters and check the company selector. Then ask your MSP to check the Acronis or Huntress sync and company mapping. |
| **No detections in the last 12 weeks** | Nothing new was detected for your selected companies in that time. Check the company selector and the integration sync. |
| Zero counts on every tile | The integration may not be synced, or its records are unmapped. Zero counts do not prove you are safe. |
| **Could not load detections.** | Reload the page. If it keeps failing, report it through the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). |
| **Security integration unavailable** | Neither Acronis nor Huntress is connected, or you lack Security read access. Ask your MSP admin. |
| **Open in vendor console** is missing | The vendor did not supply a link for that detection, or you are a company user. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did the Huntress tab go?">
    Huntress escalations, incident reports, and remediations are now on **Security > EDR > Detections**, in the same table as Acronis incidents. Old Huntress links open that view.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Resolved mean an Acronis incident is mitigated?">
    No. Status follows the Acronis state. Open the incident and read **Mitigation** too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Active agents mean the device is online right now?">
    Only for Acronis. An Acronis agent counts when it is online now. Huntress does not report live connectivity, so a Huntress agent counts when it was seen in the last 30 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I approve a remediation or close a detection here?">
    No. Use Acronis or Huntress.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I am a company user. How do I ask for an investigation?">
    Tell the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) the detection title or reference, host, company, severity, and date, then confirm the request when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
