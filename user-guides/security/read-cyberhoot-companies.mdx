Use this page to read CyberHoot results for your companies. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need the Security Awareness read permission. It is separate from Security read access.
- Your MSP must connect CyberHoot and map its customers to companies. See [Enable the CyberHoot integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-cyberhoot-integration).
- If you have more than one awareness integration, **Security Awareness** shows a tab for each. Choose **CyberHoot**.

## Review your companies

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security Awareness">
    Open **Security > Security Awareness** and check the company selector. If a **CyberHoot** tab is shown, select it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary cards">
    The cards at the top cover every mapped company in your selection. See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your company in the table">
    Each row shows the company name, with the CyberHoot customer name underneath, then **Hoot Score**, **HootRank**, and the compliance percentages. Use the column filters to narrow the list, and choose 25, 50, or 100 rows per page at the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show or hide columns">
    Use the column menu above the table to show or hide columns when the table is too wide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the sync time">
    **Last synced** above the table shows the latest sync time among the companies shown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the summary cards

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Mapped companies** | The number of mapped CyberHoot customer entries. One company can have more than one entry. |
| **Active learners** | The sum of the active-user counts. It shows **Not provided** if any entry has no count. |
| **Average Hoot Score** | The simple average of the company scores, leaving out missing scores. It is not weighted by learners. |
| **Phishing compliance** | The simple average of the company phishing percentages, leaving out missing values. It is not the share of all learners who passed. |

The cards cover every mapped company in your selection and do not change when you filter the table.

## Read the compliance columns

The table separates **Policy compliance**, **Video compliance**, **Survey compliance**, **Phishing compliance**, **ZAC foundational compliance**, **ZAC training compliance**, **Required CustomHoot compliance**, and **Optional CustomHoot compliance**. Read the column name and company before you compare values.

**Hoot Score** is a score. **HootRank** is the rank label from CyberHoot. A dash means CyberHoot supplied no value. It does not mean 0%.

To look at a campaign, see [Review HootPhish challenges](/user-guides/security/read-hootphish-challenges). This view has no date range, export, learner editing, or reminder action.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No mapped CyberHoot companies** | Nothing is mapped for your selection. Ask your MSP to map CyberHoot customers to companies and finish a sync. |
| **CyberHoot data could not be loaded** | Click **Try again**. It reloads the page data and does not start a CyberHoot sync. If it keeps failing, report it through the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). |
| **Active learners** shows **Not provided** | At least one entry has no active-user count. This is not zero. |
| The **Security Awareness** tab is missing | You lack the Security Awareness read permission, or no awareness integration is connected. Ask your MSP admin. |
| A value is a dash | CyberHoot supplied no value for that column. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Phishing compliance weighted by company size?">
    No. It averages the company percentages equally and leaves out missing values.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Last synced prove every company is current?">
    No. It is the latest sync time among the companies shown. Others can be older.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Try again sync CyberHoot?">
    No. It reloads the data MSPortal already has. Your MSP manages the CyberHoot sync.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
