Use this page to read the details of one endpoint from Cork or Acronis. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Security read access and an active Cork or Acronis integration.
- Find the device first. See [Find security endpoints](/user-guides/security/find-endpoints).
- The panel shows the row already loaded in the table. It does not scan the device or refresh data from the provider.

## Open the detail panel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Endpoints">
    Open **Security > Endpoints** and find the device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the row">
    Click the row away from its checkbox. A panel opens on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the device">
    Check the device name, company, and source at the top. Badges beside them show **Online** or **Offline**, the protection status, and the source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the sections">
    Scroll through the sections described below. Close the panel when you finish. Closing it changes nothing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Fields without a value are left out of the panel. Which fields appear depends on the source.

## Read the General section

**General** can show **Device Name**, **Hostname**, **Company**, **OS**, **OS Version**, **Architecture**, **IP Addresses**, **Source**, and **Last Seen**.

## Read an Acronis record

- **Acronis Agent** can show **Agent Version**, **Protection Status**, **Auto Update** (Enabled or Disabled), and **Registered**. These are reported values. You cannot change them here.
- Online and Protected are separate. A device can be online and unprotected.
- For Acronis records, **OS Version** and **Last Seen** are blank, because Acronis does not report them. The agent version and the registration date are in the **Acronis Agent** section, under **Agent Version** and **Registered**. Use **Status** in the list for the connection state.
- **Backup** shows **Last Backup Status**, **Last Backup Time**, and **Backup Size**. This section appears only when Acronis supplies a backup status or a backup size above zero.

## Read a Cork record

**Integration** can show **Integration Source**, **Integration Identifier**, and **External Device ID**. Cork records do not have an Online status or Acronis agent details.

## Identifiers

**Identifiers** lists **Internal ID**, **Integration ID**, and **External ID**. Your MSP can use them to match the record in the provider. You do not need to enter them anywhere.

The panel has no restore, backup now, protection, or update action. For backup history, see [Find device backups](/user-guides/backup/find-device-backups) and [Read backup activity and details](/user-guides/backup/read-backup-details).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The panel does not open | Click the row text, not the checkbox. |
| A field is missing | The provider did not supply it. Missing fields are left out. **OS Version** and **Last Seen** are always blank for Acronis. |
| No **Backup** section | Acronis sent no backup status or size for this device. It does not prove there is no backup. |
| Data looks old | The panel shows the last synced data. Ask your MSP to check the integration sync. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change protection or Auto Update here?">
    No. These are read-only. Your MSP uses the provider to change them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are OS Version and Last Seen blank for an Acronis device?">
    Acronis does not report them. Find the agent version and registration date in the **Acronis Agent** section. Check Acronis for the operating-system version.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening the panel refresh the data?">
    No. It shows the row already loaded in the table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I am a company user. How do I ask about a device?">
    Tell the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) the device, company, source, and the value you are asking about, then confirm the request when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
