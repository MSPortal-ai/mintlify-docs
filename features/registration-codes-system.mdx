description: "Use your intended account setup path and verify the resulting access."

If you received a registration or promotional code, use the account-registration flow and review the terms displayed for that code. Do not assume a code guarantees a specific trial, price or payment exemption. Joining an existing client portal and creating a new MSP account are different tasks; follow the invitation or setup instructions for your role.
