description: "Find the current calendar, meeting and recurring-booking workflows."

Use Calendar to review appointments and change windows in your permitted company scope. Meeting workspaces handle agendas, notes and follow-ups. Recurring bookings have their own host settings, client confirmation and publication lifecycle; a booking preview is not a confirmed calendar series.
