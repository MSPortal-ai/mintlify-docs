title: "Report Block Availability"
description: "Understand why a report block may be unavailable or empty."
---

The report palette depends on the company context, supported integrations, module data and your permissions. A visible block is not proof that it contains data for every reporting period or filter. If a block is empty, check company selection, its filters and dates, the relevant provider sync and access. Review generated pages before sharing.

## Blocks your role can't read

Each data block needs read access to its data. A device block needs Devices access, a compliance or risk block needs Compliance access, and a project block needs Projects access. When your role lacks that access, the block shows its title and **Not available** instead of its data. The rest of the report still loads normally.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/reporting/block-not-available.png" alt="A Huntress Security Overview block showing Not available" />
</Frame>

To see the block, ask an admin to add the matching read permission to your role in **Settings > Users & Roles**. See [Users & Roles settings](/user-guides/settings/users-roles). Text, headings, logos and images never need extra access.
