title: "AI Text Improvement"
description: "Review AI-assisted wording before saving or sending it."
---

Where the rich-text editor offers AI improvement, it can revise the current text using your instruction. Review the result and any changes to meaning, facts, links or formatting. Applying text to an editor is separate from the surrounding page’s save or send action; pages with autosave can persist edits without a final Save click.
