Use this guide to ask the Home AI Assistant for help, prepare a support request, and find an earlier conversation. It is for MSP staff. Company users have a similar page with their own controls.

## Before you start

- AI must be available for your account. If you see no assistant, ask your MSP administrator.
- The assistant has your access and no more. It cannot give you permissions.
- Select the company you mean before you start. The assistant works within your company selection, and changing it can start a new conversation or hide an older one. Your staff permissions differ from what a company user sees.
- Keep passwords, recovery codes, API keys and unrelated personal information out of the chat.

## Start a conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Home">
    Click **Home** in the sidebar. When your portal uses the AI Home, you see a greeting and a message box. On other pages, or when AI Home is off, click the sparkle tab on the right edge, labeled **Open assistant**. Your portal settings control the greeting, shortcuts and branding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your question">
    Click the message box and describe what you need. For example, "How do I find a completed support ticket?" Name the feature or task so the assistant can find the right help.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Press **Enter** or click **Send message**. Press **Shift+Enter** for a new line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the answer and its sources">
    For how-to questions, ask for the product guide and open its link. For questions about your records, check that the assistant found the record you meant. A guide describes a feature. Your role decides whether you can use it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a follow-up">
    Reply in the same conversation. Wait for the current answer to finish before you send another message. Say so when you mean a different ticket, company or record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also use the microphone button, labeled **Talk to the assistant**, when voice is turned on for your portal.


## Ask for help or share feedback

<Note>Branch preview: feedback and the assistant now share one conversation when AI is available.</Note>

Select **Send Feedback** in the top bar (under **More** on a phone), or use **Help & Support > Send Feedback**. This opens the shared assistant and starts an empty message with **I have feedback:**. Your existing draft is preserved. Nothing is sent until you finish the message and send it. On AI Home this focuses the Home conversation; on other pages it opens the assistant beside your work. You can also select **Open assistant** to start a normal conversation.

Describe your question, problem or idea. The assistant checks the product guides before sending feedback. If the feature already exists, it explains the steps and links to the guide. Reply if those steps do not solve your problem; unresolved bugs and missing capabilities can then be sent as feedback. You do not need to choose a feedback category.

Use **Capture this page** or **Upload image** to attach screenshots for a possible feedback report. You can also paste or drop images into the composer. Describe the issue in words: the assistant cannot read these images. Wait for the assistant to confirm that feedback was submitted. If AI is unavailable, **Send Feedback** keeps the direct feedback form.

If the assistant cannot finish a response, select **Send Feedback** beside **Try again** to open the direct feedback form. Describe the problem, choose its type and send it without waiting for chat to recover. The topbar **Send Feedback** button also opens this form after a chat failure. Closing and reopening the form preserves an unfinished draft.

## Follow a response

<Note>Branch preview: live progress stays inside the current reply, directly below your request. The finished answer replaces the progress display.</Note>

Select **Stop response** to end a running reply. Text already received stays visible with a **Stopped** status. If no answer text has arrived, the reply shows **Stopped**. Stopping a response does not undo a change that has already been applied.

## Use a shortcut

The default shortcuts are **Find my Account Manager**, **Open a support ticket**, **Check training status** and **View open tickets**. Your portal settings can change them.

Click a shortcut to send its question at once. It does not wait for you to edit it. To check a question first, type your own.

## Open a support ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    On **Home**, type what is wrong, for example "My laptop will not connect to the VPN."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the assistant's questions">
    The assistant asks for anything it is missing and prepares a ticket draft. Click **Edit form** if you want to change the draft by hand, and **Hide form** to close it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit">
    Click **Review and submit**. A **Review change** card shows the exact ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or discard">
    Click **Apply change** to create the ticket, or **Discard** to stop. Wait for the confirmation. See [How to open a ticket](/msp-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) for what happens next.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also use **Tickets > New Ticket**. When your role allows it, a **New Ticket** button also shows at the top right of Home.

## Review a proposed change

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the card">
    When the assistant proposes a change, a **Review change** card shows the target record and the exact values.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or discard">
    Click **Apply change** if everything is right. Click **Discard** if not. Typing "yes" in the chat does not apply the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Wait for **Applied**. A draft, an explanation or a ticket preview does not mean anything was saved. If a page editor receives a draft, save it with that page's own Save button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the result is unclear, check the record before you ask again. A failed attachment does not always mean the ticket was not created.

## Return to a conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open recent conversations">
    Click the history icon, labeled **Recent conversations**. On Home it is at the top left. Inside a conversation it is in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one">
    Click an entry to reopen it. Press **Escape** or click outside the list to close it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new topic">
    Click **New conversation** in the toolbar to begin a separate topic. It does not undo a change or close a ticket.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list depends on your account, company selection and access. If an older conversation is gone after an access or company change, start a new one. A missing entry does not mean its ticket was deleted.

## Troubleshooting

| What happened | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The assistant is not available or an answer fails | Keep your question, check your connection and try again. For urgent help, use **Tickets > New Ticket** or your usual support channel. |
| A guide mentions a control you do not see | Check the guide's audience and prerequisites, your role and the selected company. |
| The assistant picked the wrong record | Name the right one before you apply anything, and open the record to check it. |
| A review card says it expired or is out of date | Ask the assistant to prepare the change again, then review the new card. |
| Recent conversations will not load | Close the list and open it again. If it keeps failing, contact your MSP administrator. |
| The answer has no guide link | Ask for the product guide, or use the documentation. Do not assume a step exists. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does clicking a shortcut send the question?">
    Yes. Shortcuts send their preset question at once. Type your own question when you want to check it first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I start a support request?">
    Start with the assistant on Home. **Tickets > New Ticket** is the manual form.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a how-to question create a ticket?">
    No. Ask for instructions when you want guidance. Preparing a ticket and creating it are separate steps, and nothing is created until you click **Apply change**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the assistant grant me permissions?">
    No. Roles and company access are managed in **Settings > Users & Roles**, not through chat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does starting a new conversation undo an action?">
    No. It starts a new topic. Saved changes and created tickets stay as they are.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a guide link prove I have access?">
    No. Read the guide's prerequisites. Documentation does not give you access.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
