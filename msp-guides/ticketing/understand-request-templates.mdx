This guide is for MSP staff. A request template is the form behind a support request. It decides which templates a company sees on **Tickets > New Ticket** and what the Home AI Assistant can choose from.

## Before you start

- To change templates, your role needs permission to write or manage ticket settings (**Settings > Tickets**).
- To check what a client sees, you only need to create tickets.

## Check which templates a company sees

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the company">
    Choose the company in the company selector at the top. The template list follows the selected company and includes that company's branch versions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open New Ticket">
    Click **Tickets**, then **New Ticket**. Click **Edit form** in the assistant panel, or close the panel, to see the list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse the requests">
    Under **Request Service**, search **Search services or problems...** or pick a category. Cards are sorted by how often each template is used. In the list view, open **Choose a template...** under **Select Template**.

    <img src="/images/product/ticket-request-catalog.png" alt="Generic Issue request card in the request catalog" width="528" height="440" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compare with the template list">
    Open **Settings > Tickets > Templates**. The list shows each template's **Companies**, **Roles**, and **Groups** columns, which control who sees it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix a missing template

Work through these checks in order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check that it is active">
    In **Settings > Tickets > Templates**, check the **Status** column. Turn the template on if it is off.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Companies">
    Click the **Companies** value for the template (for example "12 of 40"). Make sure the company is turned on. A company with its own branch uses the branch instead of the parent.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Roles">
    Click the **Roles** value. If specific roles are selected, the user's role must be one of them. **All** means no restriction.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Groups">
    Click the **Groups** value to open **Company Gating**. If **Types** or **Industries** are turned on, the company must match both lists. See [Company Gating](/user-guides/ticketing/how-to-create-a-ticket-template#company-gating-types-and-industries).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check ticket permission">
    The user's role needs permission to create tickets. A direct link to a template does not bypass any of these checks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What clients see on the form

| Template setting | What the requester sees |
| --- | --- |
| **Required** field | A question marked with an asterisk that must be answered. |
| **Placeholder** and **Help Text** | Gray example text and guidance. A placeholder is not an answer. |
| **Conditional** field | A question that appears, or becomes required, after an earlier answer. |
| **Lock subject** | A **Subject** marked **Auto-generated** that cannot be edited. |
| **Embedded Template** | A sub-form that creates its own ticket on submit. |
| Company branch | A company-specific version with its own questions or routing. |

To build or change any of these, see [How to create a ticket template](/user-guides/ticketing/how-to-create-a-ticket-template).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No Templates Available** shows on New Ticket | Create or activate a template. The page offers **Create Template**. |
| A template shows for you but not for a client | You see more in the editor. Check **Companies**, **Roles**, and **Groups** for that template. |
| A client's form differs from another client's | The company may have a branch. Open **Branches** on the template. |
| The Home assistant does not pick the right template | Improve the template's **AI Description** so it says when to use the template. |
| Unsure whether a ticket was created | Check **Tickets** before you submit again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do I see more templates than my client?">
    The client list only shows active templates turned on for their company, role, type, and industry. Staff see more in **Settings > Tickets > Templates**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a client's company administrator enable a template?">
    No. Only MSP staff with ticket settings access change template availability.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does one company have different questions?">
    The company may have a branch of the template. Edit the branch from the template's **Branches** section.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a direct template link bypass restrictions?">
    No. The template must still be available to the company and role.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
