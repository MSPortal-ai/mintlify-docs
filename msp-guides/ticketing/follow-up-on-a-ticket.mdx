This guide is for MSP staff. Open **Tickets** to find a ticket from your connected PSA, read its activity, and reply.

## Before you start

- A PSA integration (ConnectWise, Halo, Syncro, or Autotask) must be connected, or **Tickets** does not show in the sidebar.
- Your role needs the **Tickets** read permission to see tickets. Adding notes needs **Add notes**, and changing a status needs **Update statuses**. Both are sub-permissions of Write on Tickets in the role editor.
- Pick the company you want in the company selector at the top. The list follows the selector.

## Find a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tickets">
    Click **Tickets** in the sidebar. The subtitle under the page title shows which companies the list covers and how many tickets match.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Open, Closed, or All">
    The list starts on **Open**. Click **Closed** for finished tickets, or **All** for both.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Use the filter under a column heading. **Ticket ID**, **Summary**, **Status**, **Assigned To**, **Opened By**, **Type**, **Category** and similar columns take text. **Priority** and **Source** use a dropdown. **Company** and **Location** let you pick several. **Created At**, **Last Updated** and **Closed Date** take a date range.

    Use the column picker to show or hide columns. Which columns exist depends on your ticketing provider and your ticket display settings under **Settings > Tickets**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Page through results">
    Use the page controls at the bottom. You can show 25, 50, 100, or 250 tickets per page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the ticket">
    Click a row to open the ticket details. The header shows the ticket number, subject, status, and priority. The activity list shows notes and time entries.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The ticket header stays visible while the request loads. Details
  and activity appear together when the ticket is ready. If the ticketing system
  takes longer to list attachments, their file count appears afterward. You can
  close the ticket while it is loading.
</Note>

## Add a note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add Note">
    In the ticket details, find the **Add Note** box. If it is collapsed, click **Expand Add Note section**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the note type">
    - **ConnectWise tickets:** pick **Discussion**, **Internal**, **Resolution**, or **Private**.
    - **Other providers:** pick **Ticket note** (sent to the ticket) or **Private**. Autotask notes are plain text only.

    A **Private** note is visible only to you. It is saved in MSPortal and never sent to your PSA. It cannot have attachments.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write the note">
    Type the update in the box.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Email the note (optional)">
    On a public note, add people under **Email to** to send them the note by email. See [Email a note to people](#email-a-note-to-people).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Attach files (optional)">
    Click **Add attachment** and choose up to 5 files. Wait for them to attach before you send. Syncro tickets do not accept attachments here.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log time (optional)">
    On ConnectWise and Autotask tickets, if you are an agent in the PSA, turn on **Log Time**. Set the **Date**, **Start Time**, and **End Time**, and mark **Billable** if needed. End time must be after start time. The button changes to **Add Note & Log Time**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the note">
    Click **Add Note** (or **Add Note & Log Time**). A message such as "Note added successfully" confirms it. If the note saved but an attachment failed, keep the note and resend only the missing file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If **Add Note** is greyed out, you need write permission for tickets.

## Understand Autotask client reply statuses

<Note>
  **Branch preview:** Automatic Autotask reply statuses and status-only retries
  are part of an unreleased update.
</Note>

You can [configure a reply status](/user-guides/integrations/enable-autotask-integration#set-the-status-after-a-client-comment) under **Settings > Integrations > Autotask PSA > Configure tickets**. It applies after a company user saves a public note. Your staff notes, internal notes and private notes do not trigger it. Closed or resolved tickets stay unchanged unless **Replies on closed tickets** is explicitly set to **Change to reply status**.

If a client reports that their note saved but the status failed, ask them to use **Retry status update** when offered. The portal and extension retry only the status, without posting another note. Retries expire after 24 hours, recheck access, and can leave the status unchanged if the ticket status or configured target has changed. The portal notice is limited to the current ticket view; it is not a durable inbox item.

If retry is unavailable or keeps failing, check the ticket in Autotask, the API user's write access and the configured reply status. Do not ask the client to resend the saved note. This setting does not write the assigned technician, queue or priority, though Autotask workflows can react to a status change.

## Edit or delete a private note

You can change or remove your own private notes at any time. This works on ticket private notes and on private notes on project ticket checklist items.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    On the private note, click the **Private note actions** button (⋯).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit the note">
    Choose **Edit**. Change the text, then click **Save**, or press Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac). "Private note updated" confirms it. Click **Cancel** to keep the original.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Or delete the note">
    Choose **Delete** and confirm. "Private note deleted" confirms it. A deleted private note cannot be recovered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Email a note to people

When your MSP turns on **Allow emailing ticket notes** under **Settings > Tickets > General**, public notes on HaloPSA, ConnectWise, Autotask and Syncro service tickets show an **Email to** field under the editor. See [Let users email ticket notes](/user-guides/settings/tickets#let-users-email-ticket-notes) to set it up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a public note">
    **Email to** appears on public notes only. On ConnectWise, pick **Discussion**. On other PSAs, pick **Ticket note** and keep the note **Public**. Internal notes, private notes, and ConnectWise **Internal** and **Resolution** notes are never emailed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add people">
    In **Email to**, type a name and pick a contact. Contacts of the ticket's company are suggested, and the ticket's own contact is marked **Ticket contact**. To add anyone else, type their full email address and press Enter. A note can go to up to 10 people.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the note">
    Click **Add Note**. A message such as "Note added. Emailing 2 people." confirms it. A line under the note shows **Emailing**, then **Emailed to** with the names. **Could not email** means a message failed. Hover over the line to see the addresses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Replies reach the ticket. HaloPSA sends the email itself. For ConnectWise, Autotask and Syncro, replies go to the PSA's **Reply mailbox** with the ticket number in the subject. The people you add stay in **Email to** for the ticket's next notes until you remove them, so check the list before you send.

If the composer shows "To email notes, set a reply mailbox in this PSA connection's ticket settings." where **Email to** would be, ask an admin to set the **Reply mailbox** on the PSA connection.

## Change a ticket's status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ticket">
    Click the ticket's row in the list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the new status">
    Use the status selector in the details panel. The choices come from the statuses your PSA has for the ticket's board.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the change">
    Wait for "Ticket status updated successfully" before you rely on it. The selector is missing if you lack **Update statuses**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Summarize a ticket

On ConnectWise, Halo, Syncro, and Autotask service tickets, click **Summary** in the ticket header. Choose **Email**, **Bullets**, or **Paragraph**. Click **Copy summary** to copy the text, or **Regenerate summary** to write it again.

## Add a ticket to a meeting

If your role can write to the calendar, click the actions menu at the end of a ticket row and choose **Add to Meeting**. The row needs a company.

## Download an attachment

Open the ticket and click the attachments button with a file count in the header, then click the file. The button shows only when the ticket has attachments and the provider is ConnectWise, Halo, or Autotask.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Tickets** is missing from the sidebar | Connect a PSA integration, and check that your role can read tickets. |
| A finished ticket disappeared | Click **Closed** or **All**. The default view shows only **Open**. |
| A ticket is missing | Check the company selector, then clear filters. Tickets sync from your PSA, so a very recent change can take a little while to appear. |
| The status selector is missing | You need **Update statuses**. |
| A note or status change failed | Check that your role has write access and that the integration is connected. |
| An attachment will not download | Confirm the file still exists in the PSA. |
| **Add Note & Log Time** is not offered | Time entry is limited to agents on ConnectWise and Autotask tickets. |
| **Email to** is missing | Check that **Allow emailing ticket notes** is on under **Settings > Tickets > General** and that you picked a public note type. |
| A note shows **Could not email** | Hover over the line to see the address and error. Check the address and send a new note to that person. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see closed tickets?">
    Click **Closed** or **All** above the table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do clients see my internal notes?">
    No. Company users do not see internal notes. On ConnectWise, choose **Internal** to keep a note internal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see a private note?">
    Only the person who wrote it. Private notes stay in MSPortal and are never sent to your PSA.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change a private note after I save it?">
    Yes. Open the **Private note actions** menu on the note and choose **Edit** or **Delete**. See [Edit or delete a private note](#edit-or-delete-a-private-note).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I reply without opening a new ticket?">
    Yes. Open the existing ticket and use **Add Note**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who receives a note I email?">
    Only the people in **Email to** when you send it. They stay in **Email to** for the ticket's next notes until someone removes them. Replies to the email are added to the ticket.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can company users escalate their open tickets?">
    Yes, through a status change. Give their Company role **Update statuses** and limit their display group's **Allowed Ticket Statuses** to your escalation statuses, such as **Escalated**. See [let company users escalate open tickets](/user-guides/settings/tickets#example-let-company-users-escalate-open-tickets).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
