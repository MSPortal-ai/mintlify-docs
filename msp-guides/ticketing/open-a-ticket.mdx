This guide is for MSP staff who create tickets for client companies. Start with the AI Assistant, or use the **New Ticket** form. Both create the ticket in your connected PSA.

## Before you start

- Your role needs permission to create tickets (the **Create tickets** permission on the Tickets resource in the role editor).
- A PSA integration (ConnectWise, Halo, Syncro, or Autotask) must be connected. **Tickets** does not show in the sidebar without one.
- At least one active ticket template must be available. If none exist, create one under **Settings > Tickets > Templates**. See [How to create a ticket template](/user-guides/ticketing/how-to-create-a-ticket-template).
- Pick the client company in the company selector at the top of the page before you start. The ticket is created for that company, and the company must be linked to your PSA integration. Templates can be limited to certain companies, so a template may not appear for every company.

## Start with the Home AI Assistant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the company">
    Choose the client in the company selector at the top of the page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the assistant">
    On any page, open the AI Assistant from its button and click **Open on home** in the panel header to use the full Home page. Home shows the assistant's message box. The assistant is off when AI is turned off for your portal (see **Settings > Tenant > AI**).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe the request">
    Type the request in the message box, then click **Send message** or press **Enter**. For example:

    > I need to open a support ticket. The accounting office laptop cannot connect to the office Wi-Fi this morning. We restarted it, but the error still says "Cannot connect to this network."

    Say who is affected, when it started, and what was tried. Do not include passwords or recovery codes.

    <img src="/images/product/home-ticket-prompt.png" alt="Home AI Assistant message box containing an unsent example Wi-Fi support request and the Send control" width="1344" height="248" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Answer the assistant's questions">
    The assistant picks a ticket template from those available for the company and asks for any details it still needs. Answer its questions and correct any wrong date, person, or request type. If the template has an additional form inside it, such as a hardware order inside a new-hire request, the assistant asks those questions too.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the ticket draft">
    The assistant shows the ticket as a draft. Click **Edit form** to change any answer, and **Hide form** to close the editor. If a required answer is missing, the draft lists it and **Review and submit** stays unavailable until you fill it in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and create the ticket">
    Click **Review and submit**. A **Review change** card shows the exact ticket that will be created. Click **Apply change** to create it, or **Discard** to cancel.

    Wait for the card to show **Applied**. The assistant then confirms the ticket, for example **Ticket #1234 created**. Nothing reaches your PSA until you click **Apply change**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Follow the ticket">
    Open **Tickets** to find it. See [Find and follow up on a ticket](/msp-guides/ticketing/follow-up-on-a-ticket).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Your tenant admin controls AI Home under **Settings > Tenant > AI > Home page**. Individual companies can also be switched off under **Settings > Companies**. If a company sees the standard Home page, use the ticket form below.
</Note>

## Use the ticket form instead

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the company">
    Choose the client in the company selector at the top of the page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open New Ticket">
    Click **Tickets** in the sidebar, then click **New Ticket** at the top right. A direct link to a template opens the form with that template already selected.

    The AI Assistant panel can open first, because **Settings > Tickets > General > New Ticket View** can default to the AI sidebar. To fill in the form yourself, click **Edit form** in the panel, or close the panel. You can also [use AI to prepare the request](/msp-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket-with-ai).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the request type">
    Under **Request Service**, search **Search services or problems...** or browse the categories, then click the card that matches the request.

    If your portal uses the list view, open **Choose a template...** under **Select Template**, search, and pick the template.

    <img src="/images/product/ticket-request-catalog.png" alt="Generic Issue request card in the request catalog" width="528" height="440" />

    Card or list view is set in **Settings > Tickets > General > New Ticket View**, under **Manual template selection**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the subject and answer the questions">
    Type a short **Subject**, such as "Unable to connect to Wi-Fi." Some templates set the subject for you and mark it **Auto-generated**. You cannot edit those.

    Answer the required questions. Include what happened, when it started, who is affected, the exact error message, and what was tried. Do not include passwords or recovery codes.

    <img src="/images/product/ticket-request-form.png" alt="Request form with a sample Wi-Fi issue subject and description" width="1536" height="1964" />

    If the form has section tabs, click the round arrow at the bottom to move to the next section, and visit every tab. The **Form section layout** setting can instead show all sections together. Some questions appear, or become required, after you change an earlier answer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add files and review">
    If the template has a file-upload field, attach screenshots or documents there. Check screenshots for unrelated personal information first.

    Review the subject, dates, selected people, and each required question. If the template contains another template inside it, fill that in too. Each embedded template creates its own ticket.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the ticket">
    Click **Create Ticket** at the top right, or use the round arrow on the last section. Wait for a message such as "Ticket #1234 created successfully!"

    If required questions are empty, the message lists them. Fill them in and click **Create Ticket** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change the request type

Click the back arrow in the page header, or click **Choose a Different Template** if it shows. If you already entered answers, a confirmation asks you to **Discard** them or choose **Keep editing**. Changing the template clears the form, so copy anything you want to keep first.

## Schedule an appointment

If the template shows **Schedule appointment**, turn it on before you create the ticket. The ticket is created first, and then you continue to TimeZest to pick a time. Scheduling must be turned on for the template.

If the ticket is created but the appointment cannot be prepared, a message tells you to contact your service provider. You do not need to create the ticket again.

## Understand the result

| Result | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Ticket #1234 created successfully!" | Keep the number and follow the ticket in **Tickets**. |
| "Ticket #1234 created with 2 of 3 attachment(s). 1 failed to upload." | The ticket exists. Add the missing file with a note on the ticket. |
| "N embedded tickets also created." | The main ticket and each embedded template's ticket were created. |
| "Ticket sent via email to ... PSA was unavailable - please create manually." | Your PSA did not respond, so the request went to the fallback email address. Create the ticket in your PSA by hand. |
| "No company is selected for this ticket." | Choose a company in the company selector at the top of the page, then submit again. |
| "The selected company isn't linked to your PSA integration." | Link the company under **Settings > Integrations > Companies**, or choose a different company. |
| "Template not allowed for this company" | Pick a template that is enabled for the company, or enable it under **Settings > Tickets > Templates**. |
| Creation failed | Read the error and fix any highlighted answer. Check **Tickets** before you resubmit so you do not create a duplicate. |

For Autotask, an MSP staff member who submits with no company selected creates the ticket under your MSP's own organization. Other PSAs require a company.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **New Ticket** is missing | Your role lacks permission to create tickets. Update the role under **Settings > Users & Roles**. |
| **No Templates Available** shows | Create an active template with **Create Template**, or check which companies it is enabled for. |
| No **Open AI Assistant** button | AI is off for your portal. Turn it on under **Settings > Tenant > AI > General**, or pick a template yourself. |
| The wrong company's templates show | Change the company selector. The page reloads the templates for that company. |
| You left the page and lost your answers | Unfinished tickets are not saved. Create the ticket, or keep a copy of your answers. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I create a ticket for a client?">
    Pick the company in the company selector. Then use the AI Assistant, or click **Tickets** and **New Ticket**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does describing the problem to the assistant create a ticket?">
    No. The assistant prepares a draft. The ticket is created only after you click **Review and submit** and then **Apply change**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can some users see tickets but not create them?">
    Viewing and creating are separate permissions. Give the role **Create tickets** under **Settings > Users & Roles**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did one request create more than one ticket?">
    A template can include embedded templates. Each one creates its own ticket with its own board, priority, and status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to use AI?">
    No. Click **Tickets**, then **New Ticket**, and fill in the form.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
