**Audience:** MSP staff preparing a request or explaining the Company request experience. Select the intended company before preparing a ticket. Your staff role can expose additional actions; it does not prove which controls a Company user can access. Use [ticket template settings](/user-guides/ticketing/create-and-preview-ticket-template) to configure requests.

Start on **Home** with the **AI Assistant** to ask your IT provider for help or request a service. Describe what you need in your own words, answer the assistant’s follow-up questions, and review the request before sending it.

This is the primary way to open a ticket. If you prefer to complete a form, or AI is unavailable in your portal, use [Tickets > New Ticket](#use-the-ticket-form-instead).


## Before you start

- Sign in using the portal address supplied by your IT provider. Your portal may use your provider's name, logo, and colors.
- Your MSP must give you access to Tickets and permission to create tickets. Seeing existing tickets does not necessarily mean you can create them.
- Your MSP must make at least one active request template available to you. The requests and questions you see depend on your company and the MSP's configuration.

<Note>
Your MSP manages ticket templates, user accounts, and permissions. You do not need access to **Settings > Tickets** to submit a request. If a request or button is missing, ask your IT provider to check your access.
</Note>

## Start with the Home AI Assistant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Home">
    Select **Home** in the sidebar and use the AI Assistant’s message box. You can start your request here without first opening Tickets or choosing a template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the issue or service you need">
    Type your request and select **Send message**, or press **Enter**. For example:

    > I need to open a support ticket. My laptop cannot connect to the office Wi-Fi this morning. I restarted it, but the error still says “Cannot connect to this network.”

    Include who is affected, when the issue started, and what you have already tried. Do not include passwords or recovery codes.

    <img src="/images/product/home-ticket-prompt.png" alt="Home AI Assistant message box containing an unsent example Wi-Fi support request and the Send control" width="1344" height="248" />

    This image shows an unsent example in the MSP Demo Home assistant. Your portal’s appearance and AI availability depend on your MSP’s settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the assistant’s questions">
    The assistant uses an available request template and gathers the details needed for your ticket. Answer any missing questions and correct assumptions, dates, people, or request details that are wrong.

    Asking for a ticket and preparing a draft are not confirmation that your IT provider has received it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send the request">
    Check the request details before using the submission control presented by the assistant. If it presents a **Review** card, read the proposed request and select **Apply** when you are ready to submit it. If it takes you to the request form, complete the required questions and select **Create Ticket** there.

    Wait for the submission result. Keep the ticket number or link when one is provided. If the result is unclear, check **Tickets** before starting another request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow your ticket">
    Open **Tickets** to find the request, read replies, and follow up. See [find and follow up on a ticket](/msp-guides/ticketing/follow-up-on-a-ticket).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Your MSP controls whether AI is available on Home. If you see the standard Home dashboard instead, or cannot use the assistant, use the ticket form below and ask your MSP about AI access.
</Note>

## Use the ticket form instead

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New Ticket">
    Select **Tickets** in the sidebar, then **New Ticket**. If your IT provider gave you a direct link to a request, open that link to start with its form already selected.

    If the AI Assistant opens first and you prefer to fill in the form yourself, close the assistant panel and choose **Select Template Manually** if that option is shown. You can also [use AI to prepare the request](/msp-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket-with-ai).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the request type">
    In **Request Service**, search **Search services or problems...** or browse the categories. Select the card that best matches what you need.

    Some portals use a compact **Select Template** list instead of cards. Open **Choose a template...**, search for the request, and select it.

    <img src="/images/product/ticket-request-catalog.png" alt="Generic Issue request card in the MSP Demo request catalog" width="528" height="440" />

    Your provider chooses these names. For example, a general support request might be called **Generic Issue**, while new equipment may have a separate request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the subject and questions">
    Enter a short, specific **Subject**, such as “Unable to connect to Wi-Fi.” Some requests generate the subject automatically; an **Auto-generated** subject is not editable.

    Complete the required questions. Include what happened, when it started, who is affected, the exact error message, and anything you have already tried. Do not include passwords or recovery codes.

    <img src="/images/product/ticket-request-form.png" alt="MSP Demo request form with a sample Wi-Fi issue subject and description" width="1536" height="1964" />

    The image shows the shared request form in an MSP Demo session. Your provider chooses your company’s fields and available requests.

    If the form has section tabs, visit every section. Other forms display all sections together. Questions can appear or become required after you change an earlier answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add supporting files and review">
    If the request includes a file-upload field, attach relevant screenshots or documents. Follow the file types and size limits shown by that field. Check screenshots for unrelated personal information before uploading them.

    Review the subject, dates, selected people, and every required question. If your request contains an additional request form inside it, complete that form too; it can create a separate ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the ticket">
    Select **Create Ticket** and wait for the result. Selecting a request or typing into the form does not submit it.

    A missing-field message lists the questions you still need to complete. Correct them and submit again. When creation succeeds, note the ticket number if one is shown, then return to **Tickets** to follow the request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change the request type

Select **Choose a Different Template**, or use the page's back control when a request is selected. Choosing a different template clears the current form answers. Copy any information you want to keep before switching.

## Schedule an appointment

If your request includes **Schedule appointment**, select it before creating the ticket. The ticket is created first; you then continue to TimeZest to choose a time.

This option requires your MSP to enable scheduling for that request. If the ticket succeeds but appointment preparation fails, contact your IT provider to arrange the appointment. You do not need to create the ticket again.

## Understand the result

| Result | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A ticket number is shown | Keep it for reference and follow the request in Tickets. |
| The ticket was created, but some attachments failed | Keep the existing ticket. Follow up with the missing files through an available attachment option or ask your IT provider how to send them. |
| An additional request inside the form failed | Tell your IT provider which additional request failed. The main ticket may already exist. |
| The request was sent by email or completed without a ticket number | Your provider's delivery setup may send requests outside the normal ticket list. Keep the confirmation and contact your provider if you need a reference. |
| Creation failed with no success confirmation | Read the error, correct any highlighted information, and try again. If the result is unclear, check Tickets before resubmitting to avoid duplicates. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I open a support ticket?">
    Start with the **AI Assistant on Home**: describe your issue, answer its questions, and review the request before submitting it. **Tickets > New Ticket** is the alternative for filling in a form. A direct request link supplied by your MSP can also open that form.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does describing my problem on Home immediately submit a ticket?">
    Describing the problem starts the conversation and preparation of your request. Answer any follow-up questions and review the details. Use the submission control shown by the assistant, then wait for a creation result. A draft or a review card is not a submitted ticket.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I view tickets but not create one?">
    Reading tickets and creating them are separate permissions. Your MSP must enable creation for your role. Ask your IT provider to check your permissions if **New Ticket** is missing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are there no templates, or why is a request missing?">
    Your MSP controls which active templates are available to your company and role. Check that you opened the correct portal and request link, then ask your provider to check the template's availability. Creating or publishing templates is an MSP task.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to use AI?">
    Home’s AI Assistant is the primary starting point, but you can also use **Tickets > New Ticket**, select a request manually, and complete the form yourself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do my form fields look different from another user's?">
    Templates can differ by company, request type, and configuration. Conditional questions also depend on earlier answers. Follow the labels and required-field messages in your own form.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I save an unfinished request by leaving the page?">
    Do not rely on leaving the page to preserve your answers. Complete and submit the request, or keep a separate copy of information you need to enter later. Changing templates clears the current answers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did one request create more than one ticket?">
    Your MSP can include additional request forms inside a template. Each applicable embedded request can create its own ticket. Read the completion messages for the main request and its additional requests.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my own permissions to open tickets?">
    Your MSP manages portal user permissions. Ask your IT provider for the access you need; the ticket form does not change your role or grant additional access.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
