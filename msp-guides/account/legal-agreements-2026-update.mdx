## The Data Processing Agreement

There was no published DPA before. DPA v1.3 is new, so everything in it is a new commitment. The main points:

- **Roles.** You are the controller, or a processor for your own clients. MSPortal is your processor, or sub-processor, for Customer Data (Sections 3 and 4).
- **Sub-processors.** The full list is in Section 6.2, with a dated change log. We give 30 days' notice of a new or replacement sub-processor, and you can object. An urgent replacement to protect the service can have shorter notice, but where the EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply, the full 30 days still applies (Section 6.4).
- **Security.** The technical and organizational measures are listed in Section 7 and Annex II.
- **Incidents.** We notify you without undue delay, and no later than 48 hours after we become aware of an incident (Section 8).
- **Deletion.** You can export for 30 days after termination, then we delete and send a certificate. Backups expire within 35 days (Section 9).
- **Data subject requests.** We forward any request we receive about your data within 5 business days, and help you answer it (Section 10).
- **International transfers.** EU-region data stays in the EEA except for the transfers listed in Section 11.2. The EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum and Swiss terms apply to transfers that need them (Section 11).
- **Governing law.** Irish law for customers established in the EEA (Section 17).

## What you need to do

1. Read the [Terms of Service](https://www.msportal.ai/terms) and the [DPA](https://trust.msportal.ai) before December 22, 2026.
2. Check your integrations, including ticket sync, for protected health information. If a source can send PHI and you can't reliably exclude it, keep it disconnected unless a written Business Associate Agreement with us covers that use.
3. To have the DPA apply before December 22, 2026, an authorized person can email [privacy@msportal.ai](mailto:privacy@msportal.ai) with your organization's legal name and this statement: "I am authorized to bind [legal name] and accept MSPortal's Customer Data Processing Agreement v1.3 on its behalf." We confirm the date it took effect.
4. If you don't agree to the updated Terms, email [support@msportal.ai](mailto:support@msportal.ai) before December 22, 2026 to cancel. We refund prepaid fees for the period after your cancellation takes effect.
