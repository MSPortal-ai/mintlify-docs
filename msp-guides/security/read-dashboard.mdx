Use this page to read the Security Dashboard and jump to the records behind each number. It is for company users and MSP staff.

## Before you start

- You need Security read access.
- At least one of Cork, Acronis, Huntress, Avanan, Petra Security, Fortinet, or Cyrisma must be connected. The **Dashboard** tab appears only then. See [Security](/msp-guides/security/index) for the integration guides.
- The dashboard shows imported data. Opening it does not run a scan.

## Review the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Dashboard">
    Open **Security > Dashboard** and check the company selector. A selection of several companies adds them together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the tiles">
    The tiles at the top show the key numbers for each connected provider. Colors mark tiles that need attention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the cards">
    Below the tiles, one card per provider gives more detail. Only connected providers appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow a number">
    Click a tile or a count on a card to open the matching list. Check the filters already set on that list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the tiles

| Provider | Tiles |
| --- | --- |
| Acronis and Huntress | **Open detections**, with a daily trend, and **Active agents**, out of all agents. One pair of tiles covers both providers. **Active agents** reads **No agents synced** until an agent is imported. |
| Cork | **At-risk compliance events** and **Critical vulnerabilities**, with the known exploited count underneath. |
| Avanan | **Open threats** and **Phishing (30 days)**. |
| Petra Security | **Open identity incidents** and **Failed sign-in attacks (30 days)**, with the number of tenants attacked underneath. |
| Fortinet | **FortiGates connected**, out of all FortiGates, and **FortiGates behind on firmware**, with how many are past end of life underneath. The firmware tile turns red when any FortiGate runs a FortiOS branch past end of life. |
| Cyrisma | **Critical findings**, with all open findings underneath, **Known exploited CVEs**, and **Remediated (30 days)**, with a daily trend. |

Tiles show how many items were new in the last 30 days when that history is available.

## Read the cards

### Cork

- **Compliance Overview** shows **At Risk** and **Resolved** counts, **At-risk events by control**, and **New events per day**. At Risk and Resolved are separate properties and need not add up to the total.
- **Vulnerabilities** shows **Total**, **By priority**, **Known Exploited**, and **Monitored Endpoints**. **Critical** and **Known Exploited** can overlap, so do not add them.
- Counts link to [Security > Compliance](/msp-guides/security/read-cork-events) or [Security > Vulnerabilities](/msp-guides/security/read-vulnerabilities), with the matching filter set.

### EDR (Acronis and Huntress)

- One **EDR** card covers Acronis incidents and Huntress escalations and incident reports. Huntress remediations count only in **Pending remediations**.
- **Detection status** is a bar of **Open**, **In progress**, and **Resolved**. Each part opens [Security > EDR > Detections](/msp-guides/security/read-edr-incidents) filtered to that status and to the **Incident**, **Escalation**, and **Incident report** types. The **Open detections** tile and the severity bars open the same view.
- **Open by severity** splits the open detections, and **Detections per day** shows new detections over the last 30 days.
- The bottom row shows **Active agents** and, when Acronis is connected, **Protected** as rings. **Active agents** counts Acronis agents online now and Huntress agents seen in the last 30 days. **Active agents** and **Protected** are separate.
- **Protected** opens [Security > Endpoints](/msp-guides/security/find-endpoints), which lists Acronis agents only. **Active agents** opens it too when Acronis is connected and Huntress is not; otherwise it is not a link.
- **Pending remediations** appears when Huntress is connected, and **Not mitigated** when Acronis is connected. A resolved Acronis incident can still be not mitigated.
- If the data from Acronis or Huntress fails to load, the card and the EDR tiles show the error instead of totals or trends that leave that source out.

### Avanan

- **Email security overview** shows **Threats by type, last 30 days**, a threat trend, **SAT-covered tenants**, **Spam (30 days)**, and **Graymail (30 days)**.
- Links open **Security > Email Security**.

### Petra Security

- **Identity threats** shows Microsoft 365 account compromises detected by Petra. **Open incidents by company** lists the companies with unremediated incidents, and **No open incidents** appears when there are none.
- Underneath, it shows **Live incidents (30 days)**, **Historical incidents**, **Failed sign-in attacks (30 days)**, and **Average posture score**. Historical incidents are compromises Petra found in past sign-in data when it onboarded a tenant, not new attacks.
- The tiles and each company link open **Security > Identity Threats**, filtered to open incidents when there are any.

### Fortinet

- **Fortinet firewalls** shows FortiGate connection, FortiOS firmware, and FortiCare and FortiGuard coverage from the last sync, with **FortiGates**, **Connected**, **Firmware behind**, and **Renewals due in 90 days**.
- **Coverage ending soonest** lists the FortiGate coverage lines that end first, and **Issues by company** shows where FortiGates need attention. **No FortiGate needs attention.** appears when nothing does.
- The card appears when Fortinet is connected and at least one FortiGate is in the selected companies. Renewal dates need Asset Management access on the Fortinet connection. See [Configure Fortinet Integration](/user-guides/integrations/enable-fortinet-integration).
- The Fortinet tiles and card do not open a list. Open a FortiGate in **Devices** to see its firmware and coverage.

### Cyrisma

- **Cyrisma vulnerability posture** covers the Cyrisma tenants mapped to the selected companies. It shows the **Average score** (or the company's **Overall grade** when one company is selected), **Clients graded**, **Assets scanned**, and **Distinct CVEs**.
- **Open findings by severity** splits the open findings into Critical, High, Medium, and Low. A finding is one CVE on one asset, so one CVE on ten assets counts ten times.
- **Findings remediated per day** draws the daily remediation counts Cyrisma reports for the last 31 days, today included. Its total matches the **Remediated (30 days)** tile. The chart appears after the first daily Cyrisma sync.
- **Lowest grades** lists the five companies with the weakest Cyrisma grade, with their open critical findings and findings remediated in the last 30 days. Grades are Cyrisma's own letters, from A+ to F.
- The tiles and each company open **Security > Vulnerabilities** filtered to Cyrisma, and to that company or to known exploited CVEs where it applies.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No Security Data Yet** | No security integration is connected or synced. An admin can connect one from **Settings > Integrations**. |
| A provider card is missing | That provider is not connected, you lack access, or its data failed to load. An Avanan or Cyrisma load error shows as a red notice with the provider name. The Cyrisma card also needs at least one Cyrisma tenant mapped to a selected company. |
| The EDR card or tiles show an error | Data from Acronis or Huntress failed to load, so MSPortal shows no partial totals. Reload the page. If it keeps failing, report it through the [Home AI Assistant](/msp-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket). |
| **Active agents** is not a link | It opens **Security > Endpoints** only when Acronis is connected and Huntress is not, because that list shows Acronis agents only. |
| Numbers look old | The dashboard shows synced data. Ask your MSP to check the integration sync. |
| A list is empty after you click a number | Clear the filter the dashboard set on that list. |
| Zero counts everywhere | Check the company selector. Zero counts do not prove you are safe, so ask your MSP to verify the sync. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I add Critical and Known Exploited to get a total?">
    No. A vulnerability can be both. Use **Total** for the overall count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does At Risk plus Resolved equal every Cork event?">
    Not necessarily. They are separate properties.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Active agents mean an endpoint is protected?">
    No. Activity and protection are reported separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are the Huntress cards and tiles?">
    Huntress and Acronis now share the **EDR** card and the **Open detections** and **Active agents** tiles. Huntress items are on **Security > EDR > Detections**. Use the **Type** filter there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I am a company user. How do I ask about a number?">
    Tell the [Home AI Assistant](/msp-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) which tile or card you mean and the company, then confirm the request when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
