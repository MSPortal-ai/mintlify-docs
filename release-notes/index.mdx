## September 8, 2026

### WatchGuard Cloud Integration

Bring WatchGuard hardware into your client device inventory. Connect **WatchGuard Cloud** in **Settings > Integrations**, map its accounts to your MSPortal companies, and import Fireboxes and access points.

- **Keep hardware with the right client**: Map managed accounts to companies and match imported hardware with existing device records.
- **Review device details**: See model, serial number, license expiry, allocation information, and the latest sync details from the device panel.
- **Plan renewals**: WatchGuard license expiry feeds device warranty and support dates, helping you track upcoming renewals alongside your other hardware.

This connection provides hardware inventory and licensing information. Live Firebox online/offline status, firmware, and WAN addresses are not available through this integration.

### Full Reports: Better Context, Clearer Conclusions

Full reports have received a substantial update to help you turn client data into useful QBRs, executive summaries, and project reviews. Generation now puts more emphasis on the evidence behind the report, the business outcomes it supports, and the decisions that need attention.

- **Review the evidence**: Inspect the source records and metrics used during generation. Review flags identify missing sections, selected sources without evidence, and unrecognized citations before you share the report. This internal review panel stays out of the client document and exports.
- **Start with relevant sections**: Default sections follow the report type and available data. Sections with confirmed missing source data are left out of the defaults, and you can customize the selection.
- **Keep setup manageable**: Section choices, additional instructions, and appearance controls are grouped under **Advanced options** when you need more control.
- **Focus the conversation**: Executive summaries give more attention to supported business outcomes, priorities, decisions, and next steps, with more consistent KPI card layouts.

[Explore Reporting](/user-guides/reporting/index)

### A Smoother Reporting Workspace

- **Delete reports in bulk**: Select multiple reports from the list and remove them together after confirming your selection.
- **Cleaner printed reports**: Improved page breaks, cover positioning, and margins make full reports easier to share. Reporting blocks also retain their colors in print output.
- **More reliable editing and scheduling**: Report builder fixes improve everyday workflows, one-time schedule edits preserve unchanged run dates, and approval emails link to the correct tenant portal.

### Faster Page Loads

- **Projects, Tickets, and Training**: Pages load their initial content more efficiently, reducing the wait before you can start working.
- **Project details and courses**: Improved data loading helps project views and training content open more smoothly.
- **Steadier loading states**: Placeholders more closely match the finished page, reducing movement as content arrives, including on smaller screens.

### Better Mobile Views

A broad mobile pass improves the way MSPortal fits and scrolls on smaller screens.

- **Tables and filters**: Better handling of wide content and touch scrolling makes lists easier to navigate.
- **Dialogs and forms**: Improved sizing and scrolling keep longer content and controls within reach.
- **Everyday pages**: Layout refinements across settings, onboarding, compliance, notifications, and the roadmap make better use of the available space.

### MSPortal Projects: Run Delivery Without a PSA

You can now create and manage projects directly in MSPortal, including phases, tasks, notes, and time tracking. Your team runs the work in MSPortal while clients follow the updates and deliverables you share through their portal.

<Note>
**To use MSPortal Projects, disable your PSA project management integration's project sync.** In **Settings > Integrations**, configure the connected PSA, turn off **Enable Project Sync** in its sync settings, and save. If you have more than one PSA configured for projects, disable project sync on each. While PSA project sync is enabled, new projects use the configured PSA. With no PSA project sync configured, new projects are created in MSPortal.

Existing PSA projects remain associated with their original provider; this setting does not convert them into MSPortal projects.
</Note>

- **Build the plan**: Create phases and tasks with descriptions, dates, estimated hours, priorities, statuses, and assignees.
- **Track time as you work**: Start a timer or enter time manually. A floating timer stays with you as you navigate, and project and phase totals reflect saved time entries.
- **Use familiar project views**: Review work through the project overview, phases, ticket board, schedule, portfolio health, and reporting. Edit native task status, assignment, and priority from the Phases view.
- **Keep people and progress together**: Maintain project contacts, choose a sponsor from the client's portal users, and share updates, milestones, decisions, action items, and deliverables.
- **Finish and reopen work**: Close completed projects and reopen them when more work is needed.

### User Statistics

See what your team actually did. The new **User Statistics** tool in MSP Tools counts the records each person created and the tickets they worked, so accountability reporting is backed by evidence rather than estimates. Three tabs share one date range and the company selector.

**Key Features:**
- **Team Leaderboard**: One row per person and a column per metric, plus total actions, active days, clients touched, last activity, and last sign-in. People with no activity in the range are included on purpose, because "who did nothing" is the question an accountability report exists to answer.
- **MSPortal Stats**: 37 metrics across 13 areas, including compliance checks completed, Planner items created, budgets, meetings, goals, reports, onboarding, client notes, documentation, tickets, surveys, training, and automation, with a trend chart grouped by day, week, or month.
- **Every number is evidence**: Click any count to open the individual records behind it, with links back to the source Planner item, budget, or report.
- **Scoped and exportable**: Filter by date range and the company selector, choose which metrics to show, and export any view to CSV.
- **Per-tab permissions**: Grant Team Leaderboard, MSPortal Stats, and Service Stats separately by role. The module appears in the sidebar for anyone holding at least one of them, and shows only the tabs they hold.

#### Service Stats

The third tab is a full ticket scorecard for every technician in your PSA, built from the tickets they were assigned rather than from anything anyone types in.

- **Per-technician scorecard**: Tickets assigned, tickets closed, open backlog, average resolution time in hours, first-response attainment with the median minutes to respond and the sample it was measured on, escalation rate with the escalated count, hours logged from PSA time entries, and CSAT with the response count and how many of those were low scores.
- **Attention flags, not a ranking**: Each technician is compared against the team average for the same period and company scope, and flagged for **Low CSAT**, **Missed first response**, **Slow resolution**, **High escalations**, or **Backlog building**. Everyone else reads **On track**. Flagged people sort to the top so the exceptions are the first thing on the page.
- **Thresholds you can see**: The flag rules are written out in a help popover on the table: more than 10 points below the team on CSAT or first response, resolution over 1.5x the team average, escalations more than 5 points above, or a backlog over 1.5x the team average. Anyone with fewer than 5 tickets or 3 CSAT responses is never flagged, so a light week does not look like a problem.
- **Team totals up top**: A summary rail carries technician count, tickets assigned and closed, open backlog, average resolution, first-response attainment, team CSAT, and how many people need attention.
- **Coverage guidance built in**: Every metric names the source it reads, so the page points you at the connection that fills a column: Simplesat or the built-in surveys to score CSAT per technician, PSA time entries for hours logged, ConnectWise board statuses for escalations, and ticket response timestamps from ConnectWise, Halo, or Autotask for first-response attainment.
- **Technicians without a portal account**: PSA technicians who are not linked to an MSPortal user still appear, marked **Not linked to a portal user**, so nobody drops out of the scorecard.
- **Filter, sort, and export**: Every column filters and sorts, including a filter on the attention flags themselves, and the filtered view exports to CSV.

### Scheduled Report Approvals

Hold a scheduled report for human review before it reaches a client.

**Key Features:**
- **Require approval on a schedule**: Turn on **Require approval before sending** in a schedule's Delivery step. The report still generates on cadence, but it is saved as a draft and nothing is emailed until someone approves it.
- **Approvals tab**: **Reports > Approvals** lists every report waiting for review with its company, template type, period, how long it has waited, and who will receive it. The tab shows a live count of pending reports.
- **Read before you release**: Open the report from the queue, then approve to publish and email the recipients, or reject with a note. A rejected report stays as an unsent draft and the schedule continues to its next run.
- **Nothing slips through**: Reviewers are notified when a report is waiting and reminded if it sits unreviewed for two days. A report is never sent or expired automatically.
- **Works with every template**: Approval applies to Report Builder templates and AI narrative reports alike.

### Simplesat

Bring your Simplesat survey results into MSPortal so client satisfaction sits next to the tickets and technicians that earned it. Simplesat is in beta.

**Key Features:**
- **Four-step setup**: Connect with an API key from your Simplesat account settings, choose what to sync, map your Simplesat customers to MSPortal companies, and review coverage before you finish. The key is stored in Vault, and the connection test reports how many customers, surveys, and responses it found.
- **Surveys, questions, and responses**: The survey catalog, its questions, and every CSAT and NPS response sync into MSPortal's existing satisfaction reporting, so your Simplesat scores show up everywhere CSAT and NPS already appear.
- **Scores land on the technician**: Each response attaches to the technician who worked the ticket by email address. That is what fills the CSAT column on the new Service Stats scorecard, so satisfaction is measured per person alongside their tickets and SLA numbers.
- **Company mapping with quick match**: Quick match proposes matches across your full Simplesat customer list and your full company list. Saving a mapping also attaches responses that already synced, so nothing stays stranded on an unmapped name.
- **Sync only what you want**: Company discovery and response sync are separate toggles, and Sync now in the wizard or Refresh on the integrations card runs either on demand.

Automation rules with a date-threshold trigger can now **bundle** every matching record into a single grouped output instead of creating one ticket or planner item per match. Warranty expirations, contract renewals, license expirations, compliance deadlines, any recurring date-driven scan benefits.

Turn on bundling in the rule's trigger settings, pick **Monthly** or **Quarterly** grouping, and the automation will:

- **Collect all matching records** for a group period (e.g. every laptop whose warranty expires in May) before firing
- **Create one ticket or planner item** for each group, populated with the list of affected entities via `{{entity_list}}`, `{{entity_count}}`, and `{{group_period}}` template variables
- **Dedupe safely**: dedup only marks records as processed after the bundle action succeeds, so a PSA outage does not silently drop a month of expirations
- **Work across entities**: currently supported for Open Ticket and Add to Planner actions on warranty-style rules; other bundle targets are on the list

Without bundling, an MSP running a single warranty rule across 300 clients could wake up to 100 open tickets on the first of the month. With bundling enabled, that is one grouped ticket per month, ready to triage.

[Documentation: Automation Bundling](/user-guides/settings/automation/bundling)

**Survey ↔ Ticket Loop**

A new **Survey Response Received** trigger type completes the survey-to-ticket loop. You could already send a survey from an automation rule; now the rest of the loop is wired end to end.

- **Trigger on submission**: fires the moment a recipient finishes a survey
- **Per-survey filter**: scope the rule to a single survey, or leave it open to react to any response
- **Branch on numeric answers**: NPS scores, quiz results, and any numeric answer are exposed as conditions so you can route based on the value (e.g. a low rating opens a ticket assigned to the Customer Success team)
- **Branch on text answers**: the answer text is available in conditions so you can look for specific keywords in open-ended replies
- **Ticket linkage both directions**: When a Send Survey action fires from a ticket-based rule (e.g. ticket-closed → send satisfaction survey), an internal note is posted on the source PSA ticket listing who received the survey, so technicians can see the dispatch without leaving the ticket. When the client responds, the follow-up rule has full context to open a new ticket, comment on the original, or alert an account manager.
- **Composes with existing actions**: pair it with Open Ticket, Add to Planner, In-App Alert, Send Email, or AI Transform

**PSA User Defined Fields (Including Ticket UDFs) Are Now First-Class Data**

MSPortal now pulls **User Defined Fields (UDFs) directly from your PSA** and makes them actionable across the app. Most importantly, **ticket UDFs from Autotask, Halo, and ConnectWise are now fully supported**, something we have been asked for repeatedly, alongside UDFs on projects, quotes, configurations, opportunities, companies, and contacts.

A new background task syncs every UDF definition from your PSA into a dedicated **Synced UDFs** tab on each integration, and the automation rule editor surfaces them automatically wherever they apply.

- **Ticket UDFs, finally**: every UDF you have defined on tickets in Autotask, Halo, or ConnectWise is synced into MSPortal and available as a condition in automation rules. Route tickets by "Severity Tier", "Escalation Contact", "Contract SKU", or any custom field your techs already rely on in the PSA
- **Covers all the other PSA entities too**: projects, quotes/opportunities, configurations, companies, and contacts also have their UDFs synced and exposed in the rule builder, tagged `[Autotask UDF]`, `[Halo UDF]`, or `[ConnectWise UDF]` so the source is obvious
- **Typed operators**: UDFs are synced with their data type (string, number, boolean, date, datetime, enum, multiselect) and the condition builder picks the right operator set automatically. Picklist UDFs get a dropdown populated with the real values from your PSA
- **Drive email recipients from a ticket UDF**: the new **Ticket UDF Email** recipient strategy resolves an email address stored on a specific ticket UDF at runtime (e.g. the "Escalation Contact" email on a managed client's ticket), so notifications go to the right person without hard-coding them per rule
